## eBay's other records

- Financial statement: Your financial statement provides a summary of your monthly account activity. It's in PDF format. Financial statements are available to download for 10 years from the date of issue, and only show details for payments processed by eBay.
- Tax invoice: Your tax invoice is issued for services rendered and includes all applicable seller fees and other costs.
- 1099-K detailed report: Your 1099-K detailed report provides a detailed breakdown of all unadjusted gross payment transactions on your Form 1099-K.

For month-end work on processor payouts, see [Month-end close for an online store's payouts](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payout-reconciliation/guides/online-store-month-end-checklist/).

## Steps

1. **Add the columns.** In Seller Hub, Reports, Transaction report, Customize column settings: turn on the fee and item columns before you create the report.
2. **Create the year.** Create the transaction report from January 1 to December 31 (or month by month) and download each CSV eBay emails you.
3. **Check every month is there.** Look at the monthly totals: a month with no sales is either quiet or missing.
4. **Flatten.** One row per sale, with every fee, the label, ad fees, refunds and what you kept; costs and outside labels added.
5. **Check the proof.** Every row's Net amount should be in a sale, on a row with no order number, or in money moved, to the cent.
6. **Compare with eBay's records.** Keep eBay's monthly financial statements and Form 1099-K beside your totals.
7. **Send it on.** Send the yearly totals and the one-row-per-sale file to whoever prepares your return.
