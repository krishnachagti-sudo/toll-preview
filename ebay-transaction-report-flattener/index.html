How it works

Every row that carries a sale's order number is folded into that sale's line, and what you kept is the sum of eBay's own Net amount on those rows. Every row's Net amount ends up in a sale, in the rows without an order number, or in money moved, to the cent.

What it does not cover

  • Labels bought outside eBay, unless you list them under "More".
  • What to report or deduct: this is arithmetic, not tax advice.
  • Payouts and transfers: they move money you already earned, so they are listed apart.
Read how sales are folded, proved and found

Folded by order number

Every row that carries a sale's order number is folded into that sale's line: the sale row, its item rows (eBay lists a multi-item order's items on the rows after the first), its shipping label, its other fees such as ad fees, refunds, claims and disputes. Payouts, transfers, holds and charges recouped from your payment method move money you already earned, so they are listed apart.

eBay's own numbers, proved

What you kept on a sale is the sum of eBay's Net amount on its rows, not an estimate. The fee columns are kept by their own names and checked against Gross minus Net on the sale row; a difference is shown, never absorbed. Every row's Net amount ends up in a sale, in the rows without an order number, or in money moved, to the cent.

Columns found, never guessed

eBay's help names the details a report holds but does not publish the CSV headings, and you can add or remove columns in Seller Hub. So columns are found by eBay's help names, then by word, and shown with how each was found. Any column can be named in the settings.

Please read

Files are processed in memory and not kept. The preview is free and runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded until you ask for the full file. The full flattened report (every sale, by month, as CSV files) is $2.00 per 2,500 sales in the report, and the same file again within 7 days is free.

Read the notes, the prices and the sources
  • Files are processed in memory and not kept.
  • This is arithmetic on eBay's own transaction report. It is not tax or accounting advice: ask a tax professional what to report and what you can deduct.
  • eBay's help names the details a transaction report holds but does not publish the CSV's exact column headings. Columns are found by eBay's help names, then by word (any heading with "fee" is a fee column, kept by its own name), and you can name any column yourself. Every column is shown with how it was found.
  • A sale's "kept" is the sum of eBay's own Net amount on every row with its order number. Label and fee rows with no order number are listed apart unless you ask to spread them over the sales by gross; a spread share is an estimate and is marked.

Prices

  • Preview (the totals by year, the proof and the first sales): free.
  • Flatten (every sale on one row, the months and the CSV files): $2.00 per 2,500 sales; a repeat for the same report is free for 7 days, and a larger one pays only the difference.
  • At most $9.00 a calendar month for this tool; after that it is free until the month ends.
