eBay seller calendar, November 2026 to December 2027

From December 2026 to December 2027 there are 19 dates: 13 monthly eBay statements and reports, the full-year 2026 report, and 5 IRS dates, the first estimated tax payment on January 15, 2027 (a Friday) and the 2026 return on April 15, 2027 (a Thursday).

Where the dates come from

  • eBay's help: At the start of each month, you'll receive an email to let you know that your financial statement is ready to download.
  • IRS estimated tax schedule: Jan. 1 to March 31: April 15; April 1 to May 31: June 15; June 1 to Aug. 31: Sept. 15; Sept. 1 to Dec. 31: Jan. 15 of the following year; the payment will be on time if you make it on the next day that's not a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday.
  • IRS filing: the individual return is due April 15. If day 15 falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, the due date is delayed until the next business day. Extension: an automatic 6-month extension of time to file (Form 4868, filed by the original due date).
  • The IRS pages give the rule, not each year's date: the 2027 dates here apply it, and none falls on a weekend.

Sources

  • eBay Help (US): Reconciling your eBay sales transactions (read November 8, 2026)ebay.com
  • IRS: Estimated tax FAQ (individuals) (read November 8, 2026)irs.gov
  • IRS: When to file (read November 8, 2026)irs.gov

Not tax or accounting advice: this is arithmetic on eBay's own transaction report. The sample reports are made up; their headings are the names eBay's help uses for these details, and your own report's headings may differ (the tool shows how it found each column).
