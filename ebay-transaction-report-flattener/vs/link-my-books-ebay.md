# Link My Books compared, for eBay

Link My Books connects eBay to Xero or QuickBooks Online on a monthly plan priced through a calculator (no plan price is shown at rest; trial 14 days with 90 days of history); here a year in one report of up to 2,500 sales is $2.00 once.

## Run this example in the flattener

[Open this report in the flattener](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/ebay-transaction-report-flattener/?sample=us)

## What Link My Books says

- Start a free 14-day trial, no card required; the trial includes 90 days of historical data.
- History: paid plans go back up to 24 months, depending on the plan.
- eBay: Each eBay store counts as 1 sales channel. All eBay regions supported.
- Ledgers: Xero (all regions, all plans except Cashbook) and QuickBooks Online.
- Prices: plan prices are shown only through a calculator; none is shown at rest.

## When Link My Books is better

- You keep your books in Xero or QuickBooks Online and want each payout posted as it happens.
- You sell on several channels and want them in one connection.
- You want someone else's team to set up the posting rules.

## When a flattened report is enough

- You need one clean file for the year, not a subscription.
- Your records are a spreadsheet, not a ledger.
- You want every fee by name for each sale, checked to the cent.
