# eBay's Earnings report compared

eBay's Earnings report shows each order's earnings after expenses and refunds, with your cost, free, for eligible sellers, but as one Expenses total and without labels bought outside eBay funds; flattened, the sample Lego order shows each fee by name and keeps $30.75 after the $8.75 label bought elsewhere.

## Side by side

From eBay's help (read November 8, 2026)

|  | Earnings report | Flattened transaction report |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who | Eligible sellers can view and download the Earnings report in either Seller Hub or My eBay | Anyone who can create a transaction report |
| Dates | Order creation date; later expenses and refunds land on the order | Each row on its own date; folded by order number |
| Fees | Expenses: your total fees and fee credits tied to an order, as well as the amount you've paid for shipping labels and donations | Each fee column by name; labels and ad fees apart |
| Labels bought elsewhere | Charges and credits for shipping labels purchased with methods other than eBay funds, such as PayPal, aren't included in this report | Added by order number |
| Your cost | Yes: the per-unit cost of acquiring the item. It should not include any general business costs | Yes, by Item ID or SKU |
| Rows with no order number | Not described | Listed, or spread by gross |
| Price | Free | Preview free; $2.00 per 2,500 sales, at most $9.00 a month |

## When the Earnings report is better

- You are eligible and one Expenses figure per order is enough.
- You want no upload at all: eBay builds it.
- You want a past month's orders to show their later refunds (if you set an order period for October 2025, the Order earnings in the report will reflect any expenses and refunds associated with those orders that were incurred in the month of October 2025 or later).

## When the flattened report is better

- You need each fee by name, labels from another label service, or ad fees with no order number.
- Your account does not have the Earnings report.
- Your totals must be by the date each thing happened, for the year.
