# eBay export extensions compared

Spreadsheetic, an eBay data exporter on the Chrome Web Store, exports eBay seller data into a spreadsheet: inventory, monthly sales tabs with fee breakdowns, net profit and ROI formulas, an expenses and mileage tab and a P&L tab; download or Google Sheets; its listing shows 10 users, no ratings and no price shown on the store page. The flattener needs no install and reads only the file you choose, in your browser for the preview.

## What the store page says

- What it does: exports eBay seller data into a spreadsheet: inventory, monthly sales tabs with fee breakdowns, net profit and ROI formulas, an expenses and mileage tab and a P&L tab; download or Google Sheets.
- Data: the listing says data is pulled from eBay's official APIs.
- Users and ratings: 10 users, no ratings.
- Updated: February 21, 2026 (version 1.0.0).
- Price: no price shown on the store page.

## When an extension is better

- You want data pulled from your account while signed in, without downloading a report.
- You want inventory, mileage and a P&L tab in one workbook.

## When the file-only flattener is better

- You do not want to install anything or give a browser add-on access to your eBay account.
- You want each sale proved against eBay's own Net amount, row by row.
- You want the same result from code (REST or MCP).
