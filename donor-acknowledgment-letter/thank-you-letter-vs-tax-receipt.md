# Thank-you letter or tax receipt?

A thank-you letter is also the tax receipt when it has the required information; by amount: under $250 the donor's bank record or a simple receipt is enough, $250 or more needs the written acknowledgment, and a payment over $75 that bought something also needs the disclosure, as in the $150.00 gala letter below.

## By amount

IRS Publication 1771.

| Gift | What the donor needs from you |
| --- | --- |
| Money, under $250 | Nothing required beyond a record such as a bank statement; a thank-you with the date and amount serves |
| Money, $250 or more | The written acknowledgment: amount, date and the goods-or-services sentence |
| Payment over $75, partly for a benefit | The disclosure with a good faith estimate, given when the payment is asked for or received |
| Goods or stock | A description, no value |

## A ticket letter

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: November 12, 2026
- To: Tom Becker, 3 Elm Court, Kettering, OH 45429

Dear Tom Becker,

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received your payment of $150.00 on November 7, 2026 for Fall gala.

**In exchange for your payment, we provided goods or services: Gala dinner ticket. Our good faith estimate of their fair market value is $60.00.**

**The amount of your contribution that is deductible for federal income tax purposes is limited to the excess of the money you gave ($150.00) over the value of the goods or services we provided ($60.00), that is $90.00.**

Riverside Food Bank (sample) is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 12-3456789).

Please keep this letter with your tax records.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## Wording to avoid in thank-you copy

- A dollar value for goods or stock.
- Tax-deductible language on a DAF grant thank-you.
- Telling a donor what they can deduct beyond the disclosure.

## Questions

### Is a thank-you letter the same as a receipt?

It can be: "Letters, postcards, or computer-generated forms with the above information are acceptable." (IRS Publication 1771).

### Does a $50 gift need a receipt?

Not a written acknowledgment. The donor needs a record: A donor cannot claim a federal income tax deduction for any monetary contribution unless the donor maintains a record of the contribution or a timely written communication from the charitable organization showing the name of the organization, the date of the contribution, and the amount of the contribution.
