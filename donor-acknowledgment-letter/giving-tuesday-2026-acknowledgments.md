# GivingTuesday 2026 acknowledgment letters

GivingTuesday 2026 is Tuesday, December 1, 2026; a gift made that day is a 2026 gift, and one of $250 or more needs a written acknowledgment, like the filled receipt below for $250.00 given on December 1. GivingTuesday 2027 falls on Tuesday, November 30, 2027.

## The dates

The Tuesday after the fourth Thursday of November, worked out; 2026 matches GivingTuesday's own site.

| Year | GivingTuesday | Thanksgiving |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 | Tuesday, December 1, 2026 | Thursday, November 26, 2026 |
| 2027 | Tuesday, November 30, 2027 | Thursday, November 25, 2027 |

## A receipt for a gift made that day

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: December 4, 2026
- To: Jordan Ellis, 77 Cedar Avenue, Dayton, OH 45406

Dear Jordan Ellis,

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received your contribution of $250.00 on December 1, 2026 for Annual fund (Online).

**No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution.**

Riverside Food Bank (sample) is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 12-3456789).

Please keep this letter with your tax records.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## What the day's gifts need

- Money gifts: the no-goods-or-services sentence (or the church sentence).
- Matched gifts and DAF grants: the DAF thank-you without receipt wording for a DAF grant.
- Online event tickets: a good faith estimate, and over $75 the disclosure.
- First-time donors: the first-gift opening line, worked out from your file.

## Questions

### When is GivingTuesday 2026?

Tuesday, December 1, 2026: the Tuesday after US Thanksgiving. GivingTuesday's site lists "GivingTuesday – Dec 1, 2026".

### When is GivingTuesday 2027?

Tuesday, November 30, 2027, the Tuesday after Thanksgiving on November 25, 2027. Worked out from that rule; GivingTuesday's site did not list 2027 when read.
