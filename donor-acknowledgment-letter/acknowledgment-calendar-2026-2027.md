# Acknowledgment calendar, November 2026 to December 2027

For 2026 gifts: GivingTuesday is Tuesday, December 1, 2026, the last day to give is Thursday, December 31, 2026, letters usually go out by January 31, 2027 (a Sunday, so Friday, January 29, 2027 in practice), and the donor must have them by Thursday, April 15, 2027 or, with an extension, Friday, October 15, 2027.

## The calendar

Weekdays and business days computed with the US federal holiday calendar.

| Date | What | The rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday, December 1, 2026 | GivingTuesday 2026 | Gifts made that day are 2026 gifts; acknowledge the $250-and-over ones like any other. |
| Thursday, December 31, 2026 | Last day for a 2026 gift | A gift counts in the year it is made; a mailed check counts on the day it is mailed (IRS Pub 526). |
| Friday, January 29, 2027 | Last business day before January 31, 2027 | Practice: letters and statements for 2026 gifts out by now. |
| Sunday, January 31, 2027 | January 31, 2027 | IRS Pub 1771: "Charities typically send written acknowledgments to donors no later than January 31 of the year following the donation." |
| Thursday, April 15, 2027 | Usual due date of a 2026 individual return | The donor must have the acknowledgment before filing, and by this date at the latest without an extension. |
| Friday, October 15, 2027 | Extended due date of a 2026 return | The outer limit for a donor who extended: the acknowledgment must arrive by the due date including extensions. |
| Tuesday, November 30, 2027 | GivingTuesday 2027 | Gifts made that day are 2027 gifts. |
| Friday, December 31, 2027 | Last day for a 2027 gift | The same rule as for 2026. |

An organization that does not acknowledge a contribution incurs no penalty; but, without a contemporaneous written acknowledgment, the donor cannot claim the federal income tax deduction. A late letter matters only if the donor has already filed.

## Questions

### Is January 31, 2027 an IRS deadline for acknowledgment letters?

No. "Charities typically send written acknowledgments to donors no later than January 31 of the year following the donation." It is a Sunday in 2027.

### What is the latest a donor can get the letter for a 2026 gift?

A donor must receive the acknowledgment on or before the earlier of: the date on which the donor files the donor's individual federal income tax return for the year of the contribution; or the due date (including extensions) of such return. For most 2026 returns that is Thursday, April 15, 2027, or Friday, October 15, 2027 with an extension.
