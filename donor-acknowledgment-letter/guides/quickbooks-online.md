# Donation receipt letters from QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks Online records a donation as a sales receipt or a bank deposit and can email the donor a receipt with "Save and send"; the 3 IRS wordings a mixed batch needs (no goods or services, a ticket's good faith estimate, a description without a value for goods) are text you add yourself, or an export of the gifts can be turned into letters that carry them.

## What QuickBooks Online does

- Nonprofit setup: Settings \> Account and settings, then "From the Tax form dropdown, select Nonprofit organization."
- A donation is a sales receipt (with a donation template and donation item) or a bank deposit.
- "Select Save and close or Save and send if you want to email your donor a receipt of their donation."
- The form can be renamed "Donation" in Custom form styles, but menus still say Sales receipt.

## From an export to letters

QuickBooks report headings such as Customer, Date, Amount and Product/Service are read as donor, date, amount and fund without renaming. Add a Type column for anything that is not a money gift, and a Benefit value and Benefit description for tickets.

## When QuickBooks alone is enough

- Every gift is money with nothing given back, and an emailed sales receipt with your wording is fine.

## When the letter maker helps

- Gala tickets, goods, stock, DAF grants or memorial gifts in the batch.
- Printed letters with one PDF each.

## Steps

1. **Run a report of the gifts.** A sales or transaction report for the period with customer, date, amount and product or service columns.
2. **Export to Excel or CSV.** Add a Type column for gifts that are not money (In-kind, Stock, DAF grant, Pledge).
3. **Paste or open it.** Customer is read as the donor, Product/Service as the fund. The preview shows each gift's letter, free.
4. **Make the letters.** One PDF per letter, a print file and an email list.

## Questions

### Can QuickBooks Online send a donation receipt?

Intuit's help says to record the donation as a sales receipt and "Select Save and close or Save and send if you want to email your donor a receipt of their donation."

### Does QuickBooks know the IRS wording?

Its receipt is a sales form you can rename and customize; the goods-or-services sentence and the $75 disclosure are text you add yourself.
