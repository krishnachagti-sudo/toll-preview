# Donation acknowledgment letter examples

Each gift type gets its own sentence: a money gift says "No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution."; a ticket gets a description and good faith estimate (and, over $75, the disclosure); goods and stock are described without a value; a DAF grant, a pledge and volunteer time are thanked without receipt wording.

## At a glance

Written by the letter maker from made-up samples.

| Gift type | What the letter says it received | IRS sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Gala ticket (payment partly for a benefit) | We received your payment of $150.00 on November 7, 2026 for Fall gala. | In exchange for your payment, we provided goods or services: Gala dinner ticket. Our good faith estimate of their fair market value is $60.00. The amount of your contribution that is deductible for federal income tax purposes is limited to the excess of the money you gave ($150.00) over the value of the goods or services we provided ($60.00), that is $90.00. |
| Donated goods | We received your gift of Two pallets of canned vegetables (about 1,200 cans) on November 14, 2026. This letter describes your gift; it does not state its value. | No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution. |
| Stock | We received your gift of 50 shares of Example Corp common stock on December 15, 2026. This letter describes your gift; it does not state its value. | No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution. |
| Donor-advised fund grant | We received a grant of $2,500.00 on December 16, 2026 from Fidelity Charitable (Lee Family Fund), made on your recommendation for Annual fund. | No goods or services were provided in exchange for this grant. |

## Money gift

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: December 4, 2026
- To: Jordan Ellis, 77 Cedar Avenue, Dayton, OH 45406

Dear Jordan Ellis,

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received your contribution of $250.00 on December 1, 2026 for Annual fund (Online).

**No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution.**

Riverside Food Bank (sample) is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 12-3456789).

Please keep this letter with your tax records.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## Gala ticket (payment partly for a benefit)

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: November 12, 2026
- To: Tom Becker, 3 Elm Court, Kettering, OH 45429

Dear Tom Becker,

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received your payment of $150.00 on November 7, 2026 for Fall gala.

**In exchange for your payment, we provided goods or services: Gala dinner ticket. Our good faith estimate of their fair market value is $60.00.**

**The amount of your contribution that is deductible for federal income tax purposes is limited to the excess of the money you gave ($150.00) over the value of the goods or services we provided ($60.00), that is $90.00.**

Riverside Food Bank (sample) is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 12-3456789).

Please keep this letter with your tax records.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## Donor-advised fund grant

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: December 21, 2026
- To: The Lee Family, 8 Park Lane, Oakwood, OH 45419

Dear Lee Family,

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received a grant of $2,500.00 on December 16, 2026 from Fidelity Charitable (Lee Family Fund), made on your recommendation for Annual fund.

Because Fidelity Charitable (Lee Family Fund) made the grant, this letter is a thank-you and not a receipt for a tax deduction.

**No goods or services were provided in exchange for this grant.**

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## Pledge

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789

Dear Sam Patel,

Thank you for your pledge of $1,000.00 to Riverside Food Bank (sample), made on November 21, 2026: $250 a quarter, January to October 2027.

This letter confirms your pledge; it is not a receipt. We will acknowledge each payment when we receive it.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)
