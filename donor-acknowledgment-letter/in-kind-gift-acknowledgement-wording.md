# What an in-kind acknowledgment says, by kind of gift

The charity describes and the donor values: goods and stock are described without a dollar value (IRS Publication 1771), a vehicle, boat or airplane claimed at over $500 follows Form 1098-C rules instead, and volunteer time gets a thank-you that is not a receipt, because "you can't deduct the value of your time or services" (Publication 526).

## The wording, by kind of gift

Written by the letter maker from made-up gifts.

| Gift | What the letter says | IRS sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goods | We received your gift of Two pallets of canned vegetables (about 1,200 cans) on November 14, 2026. This letter describes your gift; it does not state its value. | No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution. |
| Stock | We received your gift of 50 shares of Example Corp common stock on December 15, 2026. This letter describes your gift; it does not state its value. | No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution. |
| Vehicle | We received your gift of 2014 Honda Civic sedan on November 16, 2026. This letter describes your gift; it does not state its value. | No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution. (flagged: vehicle) |
| Volunteer services | Thank you for giving your time and skill to Riverside Food Bank (sample): Pro bono bookkeeping, 20 hours, on November 22, 2026. This letter thanks you for your service; it is not a receipt for a contribution of money or property. | None: not a receipt |

## Vehicles, boats and airplanes

The rules in this publication do not apply to a donated motor vehicle, boat, or airplane if the claimed value exceeds $500. If you donate a qualified vehicle with a claimed value of more than $500, you can't claim a deduction unless you attach to Form 8283 a copy of the CWA you received from the donee organization. The letter maker flags any description that names a vehicle so you can use the Form 1098-C route.

## Services and volunteer time

You can't deduct the value of your time or services, including: Blood donations to the American Red Cross or to blood banks, and The value of income lost while you work as an unpaid volunteer for a qualified organization. The letter maker's services letter says: "This letter thanks you for your service; it is not a receipt for a contribution of money or property."

## The donor's forms

If your total deduction for all noncash contributions for the year is over $500, you must complete Form 8283 and attach it to your Form 1040. Your letter describes the gift; the donor's appraisal and forms are theirs.

## Questions

### Who values an in-kind gift?

The donor. The charity describes it: Publication 1771 asks for "a description (but not the fair market value) of any contribution of property".

### Can we receipt volunteer hours?

No. IRS Publication 526: "You can't deduct the value of your time or services, including: Blood donations to the American Red Cross or to blood banks, and The value of income lost while you work as an unpaid volunteer for a qualified organization."

### What does a donor with large noncash gifts need?

IRS Publication 526: "If your total deduction for all noncash contributions for the year is over $500, you must complete Form 8283 and attach it to your Form 1040." The form is the donor's to file.
