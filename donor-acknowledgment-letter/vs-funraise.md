# Funraise's free receipt generator or the letter maker

Funraise's generator is free with "No sign-up, no watermarks, no per-receipt fees", takes pasted rows and handles goods-or-services values, in-kind and stock; the letter maker adds letters it does not mention (DAF grants, memorial family notices, pledges, volunteer services), segments, one PDF per letter and an API, at $0.05 a letter.

## What Funraise's receipt generator does

- "No sign-up, no watermarks, no per-receipt fees."
- Paste one gift per line straight from Google Sheets or Excel.
- An optional "Goods or services received" value is subtracted; "In-kind (non-cash)" and "Stock / securities" payment methods.
- Per-gift receipts or annual statements, printed or saved as PDF from the browser; a "Copy link" option shares the set.

> From Funraise's receipt generator's own page, read November 8, 2026. Plans and features change; check their page.

## What Endwise does

- Works from a gift list you already have (pasted cells or a CSV); no account holding your donors.
- Chooses the letter per row: money, ticket (with the $75 disclosure), goods, stock, DAF grant, pledge, volunteer services; memorial gifts add a family notice without the amount.
- Segments (first, repeat, lapsed, major, monthly) choose the opening line and can filter the batch.
- Free preview of every row; $0.05 a letter, never more than $9.00 in a month. Also over a REST API and MCP.

## When Funraise's receipt generator is the better choice

- Your gifts are money, tickets, goods and stock only, and a browser print is fine.
- You want it free.

## When the letter maker is the better choice

- DAF grants, memorial gifts with family notices, pledges or volunteer services in the batch.
- Opening lines by segment (first, lapsed, major, monthly).
- One PDF per letter, a print file, letter texts and an email list, or an API.
