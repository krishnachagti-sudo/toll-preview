# Zeffy receipts or the acknowledgment letter maker

Zeffy charges the organization $0 in platform fees ("Zeffy relies entirely on optional tips from your donors") and lists receipts among its tools, so for gifts taken through Zeffy's own forms it is the simplest route; gifts that arrive another way, such as checks, stock transfers, DAF grants and in-kind goods, need letters from a list, which is what the letter maker does at $0.05 a letter.

## What Zeffy does

- Pricing page: $0 platform fees on its Free, Basic and Premium plans.
- "Zeffy relies entirely on optional tips from your donors."
- Receipts are listed among the tools in the free platform.

> From Zeffy's own page, read November 8, 2026. Plans and features change; check their page.

## What Endwise does

- Works from a gift list you already have (pasted cells or a CSV); no account holding your donors.
- Chooses the letter per row: money, ticket (with the $75 disclosure), goods, stock, DAF grant, pledge, volunteer services; memorial gifts add a family notice without the amount.
- Segments (first, repeat, lapsed, major, monthly) choose the opening line and can filter the batch.
- Free preview of every row; $0.05 a letter, never more than $9.00 in a month. Also over a REST API and MCP.

## When Zeffy is the better choice

- You collect gifts through Zeffy forms anyway: receipts come with the payment, at no cost to you.
- You want a whole fundraising platform, not only letters.

## When the letter maker is the better choice

- Gifts arrive outside the platform: checks, stock, DAF grants, in-kind goods.
- A batch needs different IRS wording per gift, or memorial family notices.
- You do not want donors asked for a tip.
