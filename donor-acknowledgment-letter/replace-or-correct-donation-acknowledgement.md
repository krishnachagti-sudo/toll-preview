# Replacing or correcting an acknowledgment letter

Send the letter again for the same gift, opening with "This letter replaces our earlier acknowledgment of the same gift."; a late letter still counts if the donor has it before filing their return (or by its due date including extensions), and with the letter maker a reprint of the same gift within 180 days is free.

## A replacement, filled

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: March 2, 2027
- To: Jordan Ellis, 77 Cedar Avenue, Dayton, OH 45406

Dear Jordan Ellis,

This letter replaces our earlier acknowledgment of the same gift.

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received your contribution of $250.00 on December 1, 2026 for Annual fund (Online).

**No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution.**

Riverside Food Bank (sample) is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 12-3456789).

Please keep this letter with your tax records.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## Lost, wrong, late

What to send in each case.

| Case | What to do | Free repeat? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lost letter | Make the same gift's letter again, marked as a replacement | Yes, within 180 days |
| Wrong amount or date | Fix the row, then send a corrected letter marked as a replacement | No: a changed gift is a new letter |
| Never sent | Send it now; it counts if the donor has not filed yet | n/a |

An organization that does not acknowledge a contribution incurs no penalty; but, without a contemporaneous written acknowledgment, the donor cannot claim the federal income tax deduction.

## Steps

1. **Fix the gift row first.** Correct the amount or date in your records so the letter and the books agree.
2. **Tick "These replace letters already sent".** Every letter in the batch then opens with the replacement line.
3. **Make it again.** The same gift (donor, date, amount, type) within 180 days is not charged again.
4. **Keep both.** Keep the corrected row and the new PDF with the original.

## Questions

### Can we send a second copy of an acknowledgment?

Yes. Nothing in Publication 1771 limits copies, and "There are no IRS forms for the contemporaneous written acknowledgment."

### Is a letter sent in March too late?

Not if the donor has not filed yet: a donor must receive the acknowledgment on or before the earlier of: the date on which the donor files the donor's individual federal income tax return for the year of the contribution; or the due date (including extensions) of such return.

### Does a corrected amount cost again?

A changed amount is a different gift to the letter maker, so it is a new letter. A reprint of the same gift within 180 days is free.
