# In-kind donation letter template

An in-kind donation letter describes the goods and states no value, because IRS Publication 1771 asks for "a description (but not the fair market value) of any contribution of property"; the sample thanks Hardware Depot Inc. for "Two pallets of canned vegetables (about 1,200 cans)" received November 14, 2026.

## The filled letter

A business gives two pallets of canned goods to a food bank. Made up for this page.

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: November 20, 2026
- To: Hardware Depot Inc., 900 Industrial Way, Moraine, OH 45439

Dear Hardware Depot Inc.,

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received your gift of Two pallets of canned vegetables (about 1,200 cans) on November 14, 2026. This letter describes your gift; it does not state its value.

**No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution.**

Riverside Food Bank (sample) is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 12-3456789).

Please keep this letter with your tax records.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## Questions

### Do I put a dollar value on an in-kind donation letter?

No. Publication 1771 asks the charity for "a description (but not the fair market value) of any contribution of property". The donor values it.

### What if our sheet has a value for the goods?

The letter maker reads it but never prints it, and lists the row so you can see it was left out.

### What about a donated car?

The rules in this publication do not apply to a donated motor vehicle, boat, or airplane if the claimed value exceeds $500. The letter maker flags a description that names a vehicle, boat or airplane.

### Can I give a receipt for volunteer time?

IRS Publication 526: "You can't deduct the value of your time or services, including: Blood donations to the American Red Cross or to blood banks, and The value of income lost while you work as an unpaid volunteer for a qualified organization." The letter maker writes a thank-you for services that is not a receipt.
