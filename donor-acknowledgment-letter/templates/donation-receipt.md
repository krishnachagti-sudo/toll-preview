# Donation receipt template

A donation receipt for a money gift names the organization, gives the amount and date, and says whether anything was given in return; the filled sample below thanks Jordan Ellis for $250.00 received December 1, 2026 and says "No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution." (IRS Publication 1771).

## The filled letter

A $250 online gift to a food bank, made up for this page. The letter maker writes the same letter for every money gift in a list.

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: December 4, 2026
- To: Jordan Ellis, 77 Cedar Avenue, Dayton, OH 45406

Dear Jordan Ellis,

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received your contribution of $250.00 on December 1, 2026 for Annual fund (Online).

**No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution.**

Riverside Food Bank (sample) is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 12-3456789).

Please keep this letter with your tax records.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## What each line does

IRS Publication 1771, page 3, and where the sample letter meets it.

| Pub 1771 asks for | In the letter |
| --- | --- |
| the name of the organization | The letterhead and the opening line |
| the amount of any monetary contribution | We received your contribution of $250.00 on December 1, 2026 |
| a statement that no goods or services were provided by the organization in return for the contribution, if that was the case | The bold sentence |

The 501(c)(3) line and the EIN are not on the IRS list; donors find them useful, so the letter maker adds them when you tick the box. For gifts of goods or stock, see [the in-kind letter](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/donor-acknowledgment-letter/templates/in-kind-donation-letter/) and [the stock gift letter](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/donor-acknowledgment-letter/templates/stock-gift/).

## Questions

### What does a donation receipt need for the IRS?

For a gift of $250 or more, Publication 1771 lists the name of the organization and the amount of any monetary contribution, a description (not the value) of any property, and a statement about goods or services.

### Does the receipt need the donor's Social Security number?

No. It isn't necessary to include either the donor's Social Security number or tax identification number on the contemporaneous written acknowledgment.

### Is a receipt needed for a gift under $250?

The written acknowledgment rule applies to single gifts of $250 or more. A donor cannot claim a federal income tax deduction for any monetary contribution unless the donor maintains a record of the contribution or a timely written communication from the charitable organization showing the name of the organization, the date of the contribution, and the amount of the contribution. So a bank record or a receipt like this one serves the donor either way.

### Can I email the receipt?

Yes: An organization can provide the acknowledgment electronically, such as via an email addressed to the donor.
