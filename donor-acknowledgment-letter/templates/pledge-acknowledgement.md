# Pledge acknowledgment letter

A pledge letter confirms the commitment and is not a receipt: the sample thanks Sam Patel for a $1,000.00 pledge ($250 a quarter, January to October 2027) and says each payment will be acknowledged when received, since a promised gift "isn't a contribution until you make the note payments" (IRS Publication 526, on promissory notes).

## The filled letter

A donor pledges $1,000 to a building fund, paid in four quarterly payments. Made up for this page.

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: November 25, 2026
- To: Sam Patel, 55 Lake Drive, Centerville, OH 45459

Dear Sam Patel,

Thank you for your pledge of $1,000.00 to Riverside Food Bank (sample), made on November 21, 2026: $250 a quarter, January to October 2027.

This letter confirms your pledge; it is not a receipt. We will acknowledge each payment when we receive it.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

Each payment is then an ordinary gift: put it in the next batch and it gets [the donation receipt](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/donor-acknowledgment-letter/templates/donation-receipt/).

## Questions

### Is a pledge letter a tax receipt?

No. It confirms the pledge and schedule; the receipts come with each payment.

### When does a pledge count as a gift?

When it is paid. IRS Publication 526, on a promissory note: "If you issue and deliver a promissory note to a charity as a contribution, it isn't a contribution until you make the note payments."
