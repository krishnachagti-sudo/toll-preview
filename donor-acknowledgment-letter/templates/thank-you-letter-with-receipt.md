# Thank-you letter with the receipt wording

One letter can thank and receipt at once if it carries the required information ("Letters, postcards, or computer-generated forms with the above information are acceptable.", IRS Publication 1771): the sample thanks Chris Walker for a first gift of $100.00, adds the organization's own sentence, and closes with "No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution."

## The filled letter

A first-time donor gives $100 to a winter meals appeal. The organization adds one sentence of its own to every letter. Made up for this page.

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: December 4, 2026
- To: Chris Walker, 19 Maple Street, Dayton, OH 45405

Dear Chris Walker,

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received your contribution of $100.00 on December 2, 2026 for Winter meals (Card).

Your gift will put about 300 meals on our neighbors' tables this winter. We could not do this work without you.

**No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution.**

Riverside Food Bank (sample) is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 12-3456789).

Please keep this letter with your tax records.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## Opening lines by donor segment

The letter maker picks the first line from the gift's segment (worked from your file). Your own sentence follows it.

| Segment | How it is found | Opening line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First gift | No earlier gift for the donor in the file, or the First gift date | Thank you for your first gift to (organization). |
| Lapsed | Previous gift more than 12 months before (you can change the months) | Thank you for giving to (organization) again; it is good to have you back. |
| Major | Amount of $1,000 or more (you can change it) | Thank you for your generous gift to (organization). |
| Monthly | "Monthly" or "recurring" in the type, method or fund | Thank you for your monthly gift to (organization). |
| Repeat | Anyone else | Thank you for your continued support of (organization). |

## Questions

### Is a thank-you letter the same as a tax receipt?

It can be, if it has the required information: "Letters, postcards, or computer-generated forms with the above information are acceptable." (Publication 1771).

### How does the letter know it is a first gift?

From your file: a donor's earliest gift in the list, or a gift whose date matches a First gift date column, is a first gift. A Previous gift date column also marks lapsed donors.
