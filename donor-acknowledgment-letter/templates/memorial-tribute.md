# Memorial gift letters: the donor's receipt and the family's notice

A memorial gift needs two letters: the donor's acknowledgment, with the amount and "No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution.", and a notice to the family that names who gave but never how much; the sample makes 2 donor letters and 2 family notices for gifts in memory of Robert Gomez.

## The filled letter

Two neighbors give in memory of the same man, and his family is named to be told. Made up for this page.

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
- Date: November 20, 2026
- To: Maria Gomez, 40 Hill Street, Dayton, OH 45403

Dear Maria Gomez,

Thank you for your first gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample).

We received your contribution of $100.00 on November 12, 2026 (Online).

Your gift was made in memory of Robert Gomez.

**No goods or services were provided in exchange for your contribution.**

Riverside Food Bank (sample) is a tax-exempt organization under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 12-3456789).

Please keep this letter with your tax records.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## The notice to the family

The family is told who gave, never how much. One notice is made for each gift that names a family to notify.

- From: Riverside Food Bank (sample), 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402
- Date: November 20, 2026
- To: The Gomez family, 40 Hill Street, Dayton, OH 45403

Dear Gomez family,

We are writing to let you know that Maria Gomez has made a gift to Riverside Food Bank (sample) in memory of Robert Gomez.

We are grateful for this tribute, and we send our sympathy. Robert Gomez will be remembered through the work this gift supports.

With gratitude, Dana Lee, Executive Director, Riverside Food Bank (sample)

## Questions

### Does the family see the amount of a memorial gift?

Not in these notices. The IRS rules cover the donor's acknowledgment only; keeping amounts out of the family notice is common practice.

### Is the donor's memorial letter a tax receipt?

Yes, it is an ordinary acknowledgment with the amount and the goods-or-services sentence (IRS Publication 1771), plus a line naming the person remembered.

### What about a gift in honor of someone living?

The same: put the name in an "In honor of" column. The donor's letter says "in honor of", and the notice tells the person or family named to notify.
