Acknowledgment calendar, November 2026 to December 2027

For 2026 gifts: GivingTuesday is Tuesday, December 1, 2026, the last day to give is Thursday, December 31, 2026, letters usually go out by January 31, 2027 (a Sunday, so Friday, January 29, 2027 in practice), and the donor must have them by Thursday, April 15, 2027 or, with an extension, Friday, October 15, 2027.

Questions

Is January 31, 2027 an IRS deadline for acknowledgment letters?

No. "Charities typically send written acknowledgments to donors no later than January 31 of the year following the donation." It is a Sunday in 2027.

What is the latest a donor can get the letter for a 2026 gift?

A donor must receive the acknowledgment on or before the earlier of: the date on which the donor files the donor's individual federal income tax return for the year of the contribution; or the due date (including extensions) of such return. For most 2026 returns that is Thursday, April 15, 2027, or Friday, October 15, 2027 with an extension.
