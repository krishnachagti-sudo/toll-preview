We may change prices for future use. A price change never applies to a call already made. Taxes are added where the law requires them and shown at checkout.

Every API key has a monthly spending limit, and you can set one for the whole account. A call that would pass a limit is refused and charges nothing. Limits count from the first of each month, in UTC.
