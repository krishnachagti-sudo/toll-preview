# Virginia wage garnishment limit

In Virginia, a creditor garnishment may take the lesser of 25% of disposable earnings or the amount above 40 times the higher of the federal and Virginia minimum wage; for a weekly paycheck with $1,000.00 of disposable earnings on November 13, 2026 that is $250.00, against $250.00 under the federal rule.

- Weekly, $1,000 disposable: $250.00
- Every two weeks, $2,000 disposable: $500.00
- Federal limit on the same weekly pay: $250.00
- Virginia minimum wage used: $12.77 an hour

## The Virginia rule

Va. Code 34-29, as read on November 8, 2026:

"the maximum part of the aggregate disposable earnings of an individual for any workweek that is subjected to garnishment may not exceed the lesser of the following amounts: 1. Twenty-five percent of his disposable earnings for that week; or 2. The amount by which his disposable earnings for that week exceed 40 times the federal minimum hourly wage prescribed by 29 U.S.C. § 206(a)(1) or the Virginia minimum hourly wage prescribed by § 40.1-28.10, whichever is greater, in effect at the time earnings are payable."

- For other pay periods the Commissioner of Labor and Industry sets the equivalent multiple by regulation; that regulation was not read, so the tool scales by weeks in the period as Fact Sheet #30 does.
- A version effective July 1, 2027 changes only the tax exception (federal tax only); the limit is the same.

## Questions

### How much can be garnished from a paycheck in Virginia?

For a creditor judgment, the lesser of 25% of disposable earnings or the amount above 40 times the higher of the federal and Virginia minimum wage. On $1,000.00 of weekly disposable earnings that is $250.00; on $2,000.00 every two weeks it is $500.00.

### Is the limit taken from gross or net pay?

From disposable earnings: gross pay minus only the deductions the law requires, such as income taxes, Social Security and Medicare. Voluntary deductions such as health premiums and 401(k) stay in.

### What if the employee already has child support withheld?

Support withheld this pay period counts against the same limit, so the creditor gets only what is left of $250.00 on $1,000.00 a week, which can be nothing.
