# Wage garnishment limits by state

Federal law caps creditor garnishment at the lesser of 25% of disposable earnings or the amount above $217.50 a week, which is $250.00 on $1,000.00 of weekly disposable earnings; of the 19 jurisdictions whose statutes we have read, 8 allow less on that paycheck, shown below with the dollar figure.

- Federal limit, $1,000 a week: $250.00
- Jurisdictions read: 19
- Lower than federal on that paycheck: 8

## Questions

### Which limit applies if my state's is lower?

The lower one. If a state law gives a smaller garnishment, the employer follows it (15 U.S.C. 1677; DOL Fact Sheet #30).

### Which states do not allow wage garnishment for ordinary debts?

Of the states read here, Pennsylvania: 42 Pa.C.S. 8127 exempts wages in the employer's hands except for support, divorce, board, residential lease judgments, the Higher Education Assistance Agency and criminal restitution. States not yet read are not listed.
