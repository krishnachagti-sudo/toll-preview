IRS levy exempt amount for 2026

For 2026, a single employee paid weekly with three dependents has $615.38 of take-home pay exempt from an IRS wage levy (Publication 1494); everything above it goes to the IRS each payday until the levy is released.

The examples in Publication 1494

  • A single taxpayer who is paid weekly and claims three dependents has $615.38 exempt from levy. The calculator gives $615.38.
  • If that taxpayer is over 65 and writes 1 in the ADDITIONAL STANDARD DEDUCTION space, $654.80 is exempt ($615.38 plus $39.42). The calculator gives $654.80.
  • A taxpayer who is married, files jointly, is paid bi-weekly, and claims two dependents has $1,646.16 exempt from levy. The calculator gives $1,646.16.
  • If that taxpayer is over 65 and has a blind spouse and writes 2 in the ADDITIONAL STANDARD DEDUCTION space, $1,773.08 is exempt ($1,646.16 plus $126.92). The calculator gives $1,773.08.

If the employee does not return the statement of exemptions, the levy form says to figure the exempt amount as married filing separately with no dependents.

Questions

How much of a paycheck is exempt from an IRS levy in 2026?

It depends on filing status, dependents and pay frequency (Publication 1494, Rev. 12-2025); a single employee paid weekly with three dependents has $615.38 exempt.

Do the CCPA limits apply to IRS levies?

No. The creditor limit does not apply to any debt due for state or federal tax (15 U.S.C. 1673(b)(1)(C)).
