Garnishment limits from January 1, 2027

On January 1, 2027 the state minimum wage rises in Maine, Minnesota, South Dakota, Virginia and Washington, which lowers the garnishment limit where the state formula uses it: in Maine, $700.00 of weekly disposable earnings allows $96.00 in 2026 and $72.00 from January 1, 2027.

New York's rates stay the same on January 1, 2027 (New York State Department of Labor). The federal minimum wage stays $7.25 unless Congress changes it, so the federal limit does not move.

California's and Maryland's 2027 rates were not on the state pages read, and the District of Columbia changes on July 1: the calculator asks for the rate on pay dates it does not hold.
