# DOL Fact Sheet #30 and this calculator

DOL Fact Sheet #30 gives the federal rule and its table (nothing garnished at or below $217.50 a week, 25% from $290.00); this calculator applies the same rule to one paycheck and adds the state's own limit where its statute has been read, such as California's $0.00 against $400.00 federal on $1,600.00 every two weeks.

## Side by side

Fact Sheet #30 revised December 2024, read November 8, 2026.

|  | DOL Fact Sheet #30 | This calculator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Federal creditor limit | Stated, with a table for four pay periods | Computed for your paycheck, both tests shown |
| Support limits | 50% or 60%, plus 5% in arrears | Same percentages, in dollars, and what is left for a creditor |
| State limits | Says the law giving the smaller garnishment applies | Applies the state statute for the 18 jurisdictions read, with the citation |
| Priority of orders | Says the CCPA has none; state or other federal law decides | Takes amounts already withheld off the limit; does not rank orders for you |
| Official text | Yes: it is the agency's own statement | No: it quotes the sources and dates them |

## When the fact sheet is better

When you need the official wording of the federal rule, to cite to a court, a creditor or an employee, read the fact sheet itself. It is also the place to check that the federal minimum wage has not changed.
