# PayCheckCity and wage garnishments

PayCheckCity's calculator list (read November 8, 2026) has calculators for paychecks, bonuses, 401(k) and Form W-4 but no garnishment calculator; it can give the tax withholding you subtract, and this calculator turns that into the garnishment limit, $470.00 on $1,880.00 of biweekly disposable earnings under the federal rule.

## Your garnishment limit

[Work out this paycheck](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/garnishment-calculator/?state=TX&freq=biweekly&gross=2400&req=520&support=0)

## What we found on PayCheckCity

- Its calculator list at paycheckcity.com/calculator, read November 8, 2026, links to salary, hourly, bonus (flat and aggregate), gross-up, 401(k), W-4, dual salary and hourly, marriage, divorce, children and tip calculators.
- No garnishment calculator is listed, and the address paycheckcity.com/calculator/garnishment returned "not found" that day.
- The calculators are free to use on the site.

## When PayCheckCity is better

For the tax side of a paycheck: federal and state income tax withholding, Social Security and Medicare for a given salary and state. Those are the required deductions that come off gross pay before any garnishment limit, so it is a good first step when your payroll system is not to hand.
