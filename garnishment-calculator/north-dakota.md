# North Dakota wage garnishment limit

In North Dakota, a creditor garnishment may take the lesser of 25% of disposable earnings or the amount above 40 times the federal minimum wage, less $20 a week for each dependent; for a weekly paycheck with $1,000.00 of disposable earnings on November 13, 2026 that is $250.00, against $250.00 under the federal rule.

- Weekly, $1,000 disposable: $250.00
- Every two weeks, $2,000 disposable: $500.00
- Federal limit on the same weekly pay: $250.00

## The North Dakota rule

N.D. Cent. Code 32-09.1-03, as read on November 8, 2026:

"The maximum part of the aggregate disposable earnings of an individual for any workweek which is subject to garnishment may not exceed the lesser of: a. Twenty-five percent of disposable earnings for that week. b. The amount by which disposable earnings for that week exceed forty times the federal minimum hourly wage ... The maximum amount subject to garnishment under subsection 1 for any workweek must be reduced by twenty dollars for each dependent family member residing with the garnishment debtor."

- The employee gives the employer a signed list of dependents within ten days of the garnishment summons; without it, no dependents are presumed (32-09.1-03(2)).

## Questions

### How much can be garnished from a paycheck in North Dakota?

For a creditor judgment, the lesser of 25% of disposable earnings or the amount above 40 times the federal minimum wage, less $20 a week for each dependent. On $1,000.00 of weekly disposable earnings that is $250.00; on $2,000.00 every two weeks it is $500.00.

### Is the limit taken from gross or net pay?

From disposable earnings: gross pay minus only the deductions the law requires, such as income taxes, Social Security and Medicare. Voluntary deductions such as health premiums and 401(k) stay in.

### What if the employee already has child support withheld?

Support withheld this pay period counts against the same limit, so the creditor gets only what is left of $250.00 on $1,000.00 a week, which can be nothing.
