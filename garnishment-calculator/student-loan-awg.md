# Student loan wage garnishment (AWG)

For defaulted federal student loans an administrative wage garnishment may take up to 15% of disposable earnings (DOL Fact Sheet #30); with $1,880.00 disposable this pay period that is at most $282.00, and less when other orders already use the CCPA limit.

- AWG limit: 15% of disposable earnings
- $1,880 biweekly disposable: $282.00
- Same, after $300 of support: $170.00

## With other orders

The CCPA limit covers everything garnished in the period. On $1,880.00 biweekly the CCPA limit is $470.00; with $300.00 of support already withheld, the AWG order gets $170.00.

## Questions

### How much can be garnished for a defaulted student loan?

Up to 15% of disposable earnings: DOL Fact Sheet #30 says Education Department guaranty agencies may garnish up to 15% for defaulted federal student loans, and the CCPA limits apply.

### Does my state's garnishment law apply to a federal AWG order?

No. Fact Sheet #30 says state garnishment laws do not apply to these federal garnishments; the CCPA limits do.
