# Several garnishments on one paycheck

The CCPA sets no order of priority, but the total it allows is shared: on $2,000.00 of biweekly disposable earnings the federal limit is $500.00, so after $300.00 of support the first creditor writ gets $200.00, and a second writ or a student-loan order gets $0.00.

## What the statutes read here say

- Federal: no priority rules in the CCPA (Fact Sheet #30). The creditor limit is on the aggregate garnished (15 U.S.C. 1673(a)).
- Pennsylvania: support has first priority and criminal restitution orders second (42 Pa.C.S. 8127(b)).
- District of Columbia: one attachment at a time; the first delivered to the marshal has priority (D.C. Code 16-572).
- Delaware: one attachment on any amount of wages due; the creditor who attached first keeps priority until paid (10 Del. C. 4913(b)).
- California: a support order is deducted from the amount for an earnings withholding order (form WG-002).
- Tax levies follow their own rules: an IRS levy takes take-home pay above the Publication 1494 amount.

When orders conflict, ask the court or agency that issued the later one before you pay it.

## Questions

### Does the CCPA say which garnishment comes first?

No. Fact Sheet #30 says the CCPA has no rules on priority; state or other federal law decides, and questions go to the court or agency that issued the order.
