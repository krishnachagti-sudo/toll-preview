## Side by side

Xenett facts from xenett.com/pricing, read November 8, 2026

|  | Xenett | This tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $7.5/month per client (AI Financials Review), lists Undeposited Funds breakdown, Uncategorized Entries, Duplicate Entries; Workflow $10/month per client | $3.00 per report; at most $19.00 a month |
| Clearing accounts | $15/month per client add-on; lists Automated Recs for Clearing Accounts; $0 for the first 60 days (firm level) | Aging, netting and exact deposit sets for any clearing account export |
| Connection | Works inside QuickBooks Online and Xero (its pricing page lists a browser extension for both) | An export you upload; processed in memory and not kept |
| Trial | 14-day free trial | The preview is free |

At those prices, one client reviewed every month costs $7.50 with Xenett; with this tool, one report a month for that client costs $3.00. For 20 clients, Xenett's review plan is $150.00 a month; this tool stops charging at $19.00 a month.

## When Xenett is better

Xenett is better for a firm that wants a connected review of every client every month, with its other review points, workflow and client portal in one place. This tool is better for a cleanup or a check you run when you need it, without a subscription and without connecting the client's books.
