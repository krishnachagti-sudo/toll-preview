# Month-end aging cut-off dates, 2026 to 2027

At the December 31, 2026 year-end close, an item is over 90 days old if it is dated on or before October 1, 2026, and over a year old if dated on or before December 30, 2025; the table gives the 30, 60, 90 and 365-day cut-offs for all 14 month ends from November 2026 to December 2027.

## How they are worked out

Age is the close date minus the item's date in calendar days, the same rule the tool uses. An item is over 30 days old at 31 days, so the 30-day cut-off is the close date minus 31 days; likewise 61, 91 and 366 days for the others. Quarter ends (December 31, March 31, June 30, September 30) and the calendar year end are rows like any other.
