Clearing account cleanup

Drop in a clearing account's export and see what is really still open, how old each item is, and which open payments add up exactly to each bank deposit, to the cent.

How it works

Net off, age, then match, to the cent.

Undeposited Funds with years of old payments, a suspense account that never clears, a new client's Ask My Accountant pile: the same three steps.

What it does not cover

Accounting or tax advice, choosing between sets for you, or posting anything to your books.

Read the full list
  • Not accounting or tax advice. The tool lists open items and the exact sets that fit each deposit; it never posts a deposit or an entry to your books.
  • When more than one set of payments fits a deposit, every set is shown and none is chosen for you. Confirm with payment IDs, names and memos before you record the deposit.
  • Have your accountant review any journal entry before you clear leftovers, especially into a year that is already closed or filed.
  • Files are processed in memory and not kept.

First, what cancels out

Each clearing line is paired with the item it cleared, one for one first, then as an exact set.

Read the full rules

An account report lists the payments and the deposits that cleared them side by side. Each line on the clearing side (a deposit out of Undeposited Funds, a reclass out of Ask My Accountant) is paired with the item it cleared: one for one by exact amount first (the same name first, then the closest earlier date), then as an exact set of items in the date window. A line two sets could explain is left open, with both sets listed.

Then, the age of what is left

Open items aged 0 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and over 90 days, tied out to the report's total.

Read the full rules

Every open item is aged 0 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and over 90 days as of the date you choose, with the items over a year old counted, possible duplicates (same date, amount, name and number) flagged, and totals by name. The open items always add up to the account's balance, and that balance is checked against the report's own total row or running balance.

Last, the exact match search

Every set of open payments that equals a deposit to the cent, closest dates first.

Read the full rules

For each bank deposit, every set of open payments dated from 31 days before to 3 days after it (you can change both) is searched, and every set whose total equals the deposit to the cent is counted and listed, closest dates first. One set: ready to record. Two or more: you choose, with payment IDs and memos. With a fee allowance, sets that exceed the deposit by up to that share are listed apart, with the fee.

Prices

The preview is free. The full report is $3.00 per ledger file, at most $19.00 a month.

Read the full prices

The preview runs in your browser and is free: aging totals, the tie-out to the report's total, and how many deposits match. The full report, with every item, every match set and two CSV working papers, is $3.00 per ledger file; running the same file again within 7 days, with other settings or another bank file, is free. Quick matches of a list of amounts are $0.10 per deposit. At most $19.00 a month.

  • Preview (aging totals, the tie-out and how many deposits match): free.
  • Cleanup report (every aged item, what nets off and every match set, with CSVs): $3.00 per ledger file; a repeat for the same ledger file is free for 7 days.
  • Match amounts to deposits: $0.10 per deposit.
  • At most $19.00 a calendar month for this tool; after that it is free until the month ends.
