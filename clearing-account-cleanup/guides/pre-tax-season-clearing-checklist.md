## Why this order

Recording deposits changes the bank account, so it goes before the bank reconciliation. Reclassifying Ask My Accountant changes income and expense, so it goes before the books are handed over. Journal entries for leftovers go last, so that they only cover what nothing else explained.

For the date cut-offs at each month end of the year, see [the aging cut-off calendar](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/clearing-account-cleanup/month-end-aging-cutoffs-2026-2027/).

## Steps

1. **Export each clearing account.** Undeposited Funds, Ask My Accountant, suspense, any uncategorized income or expense account: Transaction Detail by Account from the first transaction to December 31.
2. **Undeposited Funds first.** Match the payments to bank deposits and record the deposits. Deposits move money into the bank account, so this comes before the bank reconciliation is signed off.
3. **Then Ask My Accountant and suspense.** Net out reclasses already posted, sort what is left by name and size, and send the client one question list.
4. **Then the leftovers.** List what still has no match, with its age, for the accountant. Any journal entry into a filed year is the accountant's decision.
5. **Keep the evidence.** Keep the two CSVs from each report (open items, deposit matches) and the bank statements behind each match, with the client file.
6. **Check the totals.** Export again: each account's open total should equal its balance, and the balance should be what you expect to carry into the new year.
