# Undeposited Funds: what it is and how to clear it

In this worked Undeposited Funds export, 14 payments totaling $7,072.74 are still open once the 5 lines that cancel each other are taken out, and 2 of them ($1,285.00) are over a year old. Intuit describes the account as "a temporary account that holds payments you plan to deposit later at the bank", so a balance that stays is a list of payments never grouped into a bank deposit.

## How it clears

Each bank deposit becomes one Bank Deposit (QuickBooks Online) or Make Deposits entry (QuickBooks Desktop) with exactly the payments of its set ticked, dated like the bank statement. The steps for each ledger are on their own pages: [QuickBooks Online](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/clearing-account-cleanup/undeposited-funds/quickbooks-online/), [QuickBooks Desktop](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/clearing-account-cleanup/undeposited-funds/quickbooks-desktop/) and [Zoho Books](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/clearing-account-cleanup/undeposited-funds/zoho-books/). Payments too old to match are a separate job: [old Undeposited Funds](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/clearing-account-cleanup/undeposited-funds/old-years/).

## Questions

### What is Undeposited Funds in QuickBooks?

Intuit calls it "a temporary account that holds payments you plan to deposit later at the bank". All payments in the Undeposited Funds account automatically appear in the Bank Deposit window.

### My file calls it something else. Is it the same account?

Possibly. Intuit says: "This account can be renamed, so check the Detail type column for "Undeposited Funds."" The tool reads the account by the name in your export, so pick it by that name.

### What happens if I delete a bank deposit?

In QuickBooks Desktop, Intuit says: "All payments on the deposit go back to your Undeposited Funds account." The payments are then open again and show in the aging.
