## The rules

- Age = the as-of date minus the item's date, in days.
- Buckets: 0 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, over 90 days. Items over 365 days are flagged as well.
- Matched to deposit: only when exactly one set of open items fits that deposit. When several fit, the column stays empty until you choose.
- The open items must add up to the account balance on the report. A difference means the export missed lines, often a date range that starts too late.

Use the same template every month; the [month-end review](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/clearing-account-cleanup/guides/month-end-clearing-review/) says what to check in it.
