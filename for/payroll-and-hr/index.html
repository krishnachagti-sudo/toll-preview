Which rate applies when the IRS changes it mid-year?

For 2026, 72.5 cents a mile through June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. An allowance paid before July 1 stays at the old rate, as Announcement 2026-11 says; the tool applies that rule to each line.

Can we get an invoice in our company's name?

Yes. Ask for the Team plan: one yearly invoice, a shared amount of use and up to five members.

Do you keep employees' data?

The privacy page says exactly what is kept about accounts and runs, in plain words. There are no ad or analytics trackers on the site.
