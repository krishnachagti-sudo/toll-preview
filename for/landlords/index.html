FAQ

Common questions

Need a tool for a job that is not here? Tell us the job.

Which states does the deposit tool cover?

The states whose statute was read and quoted, listed on the tool's page. For any other state it says so and gives no deadline, rather than guessing.

Is the letter legal advice?

No. It applies the statute's deadline and letter rules to what you enter and shows the text it relied on. A lawyer can tell you how they apply to a dispute.

What is the 1099 line for 2026 payments?

For payments made in 2026, most boxes of Form 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC start at $2,000, up from $600 for 2025.
