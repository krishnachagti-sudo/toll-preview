FAQ

Common questions

Need a tool for a job that is not here? Tell us the job.

Is the tax set-aside tax advice?

No. It is a calculation from the published tax rules for the year, with every constant cited, so you can check it. Your accountant can use it as a starting point; it does not replace them.

Does the tax set-aside keep my income?

No. On the web, your running total for the year is kept in your own browser, not on our servers.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. You get free runs every day without an account, and each tool shows your answer before you pay for anything.
