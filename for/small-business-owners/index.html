FAQ

Common questions

Need a tool for a job that is not here? Tell us the job.

Do payments by Zelle or Venmo need a 1099 from me?

It depends on how you paid. The 1099 tool page quotes the IRS instructions on which payments go on your 1099-NEC and which the card company or payment app reports on a 1099-K.

What is the 1099 line for 2026 payments?

For payments made in 2026, most boxes of Form 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC start at $2,000, up from $600 for 2025.

Does it file the 1099s for me?

No. It gives you a file to check and upload to the IRS's free IRIS portal yourself, and sending copies to each contractor stays with you.
