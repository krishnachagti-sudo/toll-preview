# Maine teacher license renewal hours

In Maine, a Teacher, specialist or administrator certificate needs 6 semester credits or 90 clock hours (one of these) in each license term. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022 and ends June 30, 2027: with 3 semester hours logged, 3 semester hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 0.4 semester hours a month.

## The rule

Maine: what renewal needs

| License | Required | Cycle | Example: began, ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teacher, specialist or administrator certificate | 6 semester credits or 90 clock hours (one of these) | The dates on your license | 2022-07-01 to 2027-06-30 |
| Education technician | 3 semester credits or 45 clock hours (one of these) | The dates on your license | 2022-07-01 to 2027-06-30 |

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 clock hour = 1 semester credit

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Maine teaching license?

Teacher, specialist or administrator certificate: 6 semester credits or 90 clock hours (one of these); Education technician: 3 semester credits or 45 clock hours (one of these).

### When is my Maine renewal due?

The dates on your license; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
