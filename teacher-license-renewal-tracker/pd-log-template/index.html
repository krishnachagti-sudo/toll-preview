PD log template for license renewal

A renewal log needs one line per activity: the date, title, provider, amount and unit, the category it counts toward, and the certificate or transcript; this Texas example totals 109 CPE hours of 150 after converting a 3-credit course to 45 hours and applying the independent-study and mental-health caps.

How long to keep it

  • "Instead, educators attest that they have completed the required CPE hours when renewing their certificates and must retain supporting documentation for five years, providing it to TEA only if requested as part of a certificate renewal audit." Texas Education Agency, Standard Certificate Renewal and Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Information
  • "Educators must maintain personal records of completed continuing teacher and leader education (CTLE), including:" New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)

Rules quoted from each state's own education agency page, with the date we read it. Not legal advice: your state agency, LPDC or district decides what counts. Totals come from the activities you enter; this tool does not read PERMS, TEACH, ECOS or any state portal. States whose official pages we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.

  • Texas Education Agency, Standard Certificate Renewal and Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Information, read November 8, 2026
  • New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE), read November 8, 2026

Steps

  1. Log each activity when it ends. Date, title, provider, the amount and its unit (clock hours, semester hours, CEUs, PDPs), and where the certificate or transcript is.
  2. Mark what it counts toward. A required topic (New York language acquisition, North Carolina literacy) or a capped category (Texas independent study).
  3. Total it by your state's rules. The tracker converts each line into the state's unit, applies caps, and flags lines outside the cycle or missing a record.
  4. Export before you renew. Keep the CSV and the summary with your certificates: Texas asks for five years of records, New York for eight.
