# New York teacher license renewal hours

In New York, a Professional classroom teacher or school leader certificate needs 100 CTLE clock hours every 5 years, ending the last day of the month before your birth month. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends November 30, 2027: with 50 CTLE clock hours logged, 50 CTLE clock hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 4 CTLE clock hours a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 semester hour = 15 CTLE clock hours
- 1 quarter hour = 10 CTLE clock hours

## Required topics and caps

- Professional classroom teacher or school leader certificate: at least 15 CTLE clock hours in language acquisition (needs of english language learners)
- Professional ESOL certificate or Bilingual Education extension: at least 50 CTLE clock hours in language acquisition (needs of english language learners)
- Level III Teaching Assistant certificate: at least 15 CTLE clock hours in language acquisition (needs of english language learners)
- Mentoring a new classroom teacher: counts up to 30 CTLE clock hours
- Mentoring a teacher candidate: counts up to 25 CTLE clock hours

## Good to know

- There is one CTLE requirement per educator, whatever the number of certificates.
- Keep your own CTLE records (NYSED lists what they must show).

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a New York teaching license?

Professional classroom teacher or school leader certificate: 100 CTLE clock hours; Professional ESOL certificate or Bilingual Education extension: 100 CTLE clock hours; Level III Teaching Assistant certificate: 100 CTLE clock hours; Permanent classroom teacher or school leader certificate: no hours.

### When is my New York renewal due?

5 years, ending the last day of the month before your birth month; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends November 30, 2027.
