# Tennessee teacher license renewal hours

In Tennessee, a Professional license needs 60 PDPs in each license term. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022 and ends June 30, 2027: with 30 PDPs logged, 30 PDPs are left on November 8, 2026, about 4 PDPs a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 clock hour = 1 PDP
- 1 micro-credential = 6 PDPs
- 1 CEU = 5 PDPs
- 1 semester hour = 10 PDPs

- Or: two years of qualifying educator experience during the current validity period.

## Good to know

- Evaluation scores and National Board certification also earn PDPs (see the FAQ).
- Time spent presenting does not earn PDPs.

## Tennessee in its own words

- "To renew a professional license, educators must either earn 60 PDPs during the validity period of their license or complete 2 years of qualifying educator experience during the current validity period." [Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators](https://www.tn.gov/education/educators/licensing/licensing-faq/pdp-faq-for-educators.html)
- "1 clock hour = 1 PDP" [Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators](https://www.tn.gov/education/educators/licensing/licensing-faq/pdp-faq-for-educators.html)
- "1 micro-credential earned = 6 PDPs" [Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators](https://www.tn.gov/education/educators/licensing/licensing-faq/pdp-faq-for-educators.html)
- "1 continuing education unit (CEU) = 5 PDPs" [Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators](https://www.tn.gov/education/educators/licensing/licensing-faq/pdp-faq-for-educators.html)
- "1 semester hour credit = 10 PDPs or 1 semester credit hour audited = 5 PDPs" [Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators](https://www.tn.gov/education/educators/licensing/licensing-faq/pdp-faq-for-educators.html)
- "To be valid for renewal or advancement of a Tennessee license, PDPs must be earned during the validity period of the license." [Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators](https://www.tn.gov/education/educators/licensing/licensing-faq/pdp-faq-for-educators.html)
- "Educators submit their own PDPs into TNCompass." [Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators](https://www.tn.gov/education/educators/licensing/licensing-faq/pdp-faq-for-educators.html)

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Tennessee teaching license?

Professional license: 60 PDPs.

### When is my Tennessee renewal due?

The dates on your license; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
