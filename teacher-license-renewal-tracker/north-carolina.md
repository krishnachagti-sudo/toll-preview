# North Carolina teacher license renewal hours

In North Carolina, a Continuing Professional License, grades K-5 needs 8 renewal credits (CEU) or 80 clock hours, or a combination every 5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 4 renewal credits (CEU) logged, 6 renewal credits (CEU) are left on November 8, 2026 (none of the logged credits are in the required categories yet), about 0.8 renewal credits (CEU) a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 CEU = 1 renewal credit (CEU)
- 10 clock hours = 1 renewal credit (CEU)

## Required topics and caps

- Continuing Professional License, grades K-5: at least 3 renewal credits (CEU) in subject area
- Continuing Professional License, grades K-5: at least 3 renewal credits (CEU) in literacy
- Continuing Professional License, grades 6-12: at least 4 renewal credits (CEU) in subject area
- Continuing Professional License, student services personnel: at least 4 renewal credits (CEU) in professional discipline
- Continuing Professional License, administrators: at least 4 renewal credits (CEU) in executive's role

## Good to know

- The "General" credits are the rest of the 8: any acceptable professional development counts there.
- Employing school systems may assign literacy requirements to any educator (State Board policy LICN-005).

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a North Carolina teaching license?

Continuing Professional License, grades K-5: 8 renewal credits (CEU) or 80 clock hours, or a combination; Continuing Professional License, grades 6-12: 8 renewal credits (CEU) or 80 clock hours, or a combination; Continuing Professional License, student services personnel: 8 renewal credits (CEU) or 80 clock hours, or a combination; Continuing Professional License, administrators: 8 renewal credits (CEU) or 80 clock hours, or a combination.

### When is my North Carolina renewal due?

5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
