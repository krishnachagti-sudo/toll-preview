# Teacher license renewal deadlines, 2027

For licenses issued in 2022, the fixed renewal dates in 2027 are June 30 (North Carolina, South Dakota, Utah, Montana), with activities counting to August 31 in Montana and October 1 in South Dakota, and September 30 for Nebraska Initial and Standard certificates.

Licenses issued in 2022 (the five-year licenses with a fixed end day)

| State | License | Expires | Activities count through | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Montana | Class 1 license | June 30, 2027 | August 31, 2027 | 60 PDUs |
| Montana | Class 2 license | June 30, 2027 | August 31, 2027 | 60 PDUs |
| Montana | Class 3 license | June 30, 2027 | August 31, 2027 | 60 PDUs |
| North Carolina | Continuing Professional License, grades K-5 | June 30, 2027 | June 30, 2027 | 8 renewal credits (CEU) or 80 clock hours, or a combination |
| North Carolina | Continuing Professional License, grades 6-12 | June 30, 2027 | June 30, 2027 | 8 renewal credits (CEU) or 80 clock hours, or a combination |
| North Carolina | Continuing Professional License, student services personnel | June 30, 2027 | June 30, 2027 | 8 renewal credits (CEU) or 80 clock hours, or a combination |
| North Carolina | Continuing Professional License, administrators | June 30, 2027 | June 30, 2027 | 8 renewal credits (CEU) or 80 clock hours, or a combination |
| South Dakota | Teacher, administrator or education specialist certificate | June 30, 2027 | October 1, 2027 | 6 renewal credits |
| Utah | Professional Educator License (PEL) | June 30, 2027 | June 30, 2027 | 100 renewal hours |
| Nebraska | Initial or Standard teaching certificate | September 30, 2027 | September 30, 2027 | 6 credit hours |

Other states run the cycle from the issue date (Texas, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Colorado and others) or print the date on the license; their deadline is your own date.

Work out your own date in the [Teacher license renewal tracker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/teacher-license-renewal-tracker/).

> Rules quoted from each state's own education agency page, with the date we read it. Not legal advice: your state agency, LPDC or district decides what counts. Totals come from the activities you enter; this tool does not read PERMS, TEACH, ECOS or any state portal. States whose official pages we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.

Each state's own source is linked on its page and in the [renewal requirements by state table](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/teacher-license-renewal-tracker/requirements-by-state/) (read November 8, 2026).
