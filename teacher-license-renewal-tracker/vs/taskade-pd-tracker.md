# Taskade Teacher PD Tracker vs this tracker

Taskade's Teacher PD Tracker is a free-to-clone workspace with 7 projects, 2 AI agents and 3 automations that tracks PD hours against a yearly target; it holds no state renewal rules, where this tracker applies each state's unit, caps and cycle.

## What we read on their pages

- Taskade's page: "Track PD hours per teacher against the yearly target, required courses and certificate renewals, and find teachers short on hours. Free to clone."
- Automations listed include a monthly behind and at-risk email and a Monday PD digest.
- Taskade pricing page: a Free plan at $0 a month; paid plans from $6 a month billed yearly ($72 a year).

## When they are the better choice

Taskade is better for a coach or team who want a shared workspace with reminders and emails inside a tool they already use. This tracker is better for rule-aware totals by state, with each rule quoted and dated, and without an account for the free totals.

## What this tracker does

- Holds the rule for 20 states, each quoted from the state agency and dated November 8, 2026; other states are listed as not covered.
- Converts each activity to the state's unit, applies caps and required topics, and flags activities outside the cycle.
- Free totals on the web; the audit export is $0.99 for a whole renewal cycle.

Open the [Teacher license renewal tracker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/teacher-license-renewal-tracker/).

> Rules quoted from each state's own education agency page, with the date we read it. Not legal advice: your state agency, LPDC or district decides what counts. Totals come from the activities you enter; this tool does not read PERMS, TEACH, ECOS or any state portal. States whose official pages we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.
