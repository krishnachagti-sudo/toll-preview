# State portal vs a personal tracker

Your state portal is the official record, but it holds only what someone reported: Pennsylvania's PERMS shows hours providers reported (PDE writes at least 12 months before your 5-year period ends), while Texas counts nothing and asks you to keep records five years.

## What we read on their pages

- "It is the responsibility of the educator to monitor their record of credits or hours in PERMS. It is also the educator's responsibility to contact the provider(s) to correct any discrepancies." [Pennsylvania Department of Education, General Act 48 FAQ](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/education/programs-and-services/educators/continuing-education-and-professional-development/act-48-and-perms/frequently-asked-questions/general-act-48-faq)
- "At least 12 months prior to the end of the five-year period, PDE will send a notice regarding any remaining credits or hours still needed to fulfill requirements, if applicable." [Pennsylvania Department of Education, General Act 48 FAQ](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/education/programs-and-services/educators/continuing-education-and-professional-development/act-48-and-perms/frequently-asked-questions/general-act-48-faq)
- "Instead, educators attest that they have completed the required CPE hours when renewing their certificates and must retain supporting documentation for five years, providing it to TEA only if requested as part of a certificate renewal audit." [Texas Education Agency, Standard Certificate Renewal and Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Information](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/certification/certification-renewals/standard-certificate-renewal-and-continuing-professional-education-cpe-information)
- "TMT allows you to store your PDU certificates as you earn them." [Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License](https://opi.mt.gov/Educators/Licensure/Educator-Licensure/Renew-Update-Advance-License)

## When they are the better choice

The state portal is better for what the state officially counts: it is the record that decides your status. A personal tracker is better as a running tally to plan what is left and to catch missing provider reports before the deadline.
