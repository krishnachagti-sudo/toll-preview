# District PD system vs a personal tracker

A district PD system records hours toward a target such as the "125 hours required" Proserva shows in its help article, and in Ohio the LPDC must approve all CEUs or contact hours; a personal tracker is a portable copy that survives a job change.

## What we read on their pages

- Proserva's help center: in the "Renew" tab teachers see their Current Renewal Hours (its example: 125 hours required) and can upload external CEU certificates.
- "This is only for use with your Local Professional Development Committee (LPDC). The LPDC must approve all CEUs or contact hours." [Ohio State Board of Education, Professional Development Conversion Chart (LPDC), last updated May 2025](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/sboe.ohio.gov/LPDC/resources/CEU-Conversion-Chart.pdf)

## When they are the better choice

The district system is better because its approval is what counts (in Ohio, the LPDC's). A personal tracker is better as your own copy, with the state rule attached, that you keep when you change districts.
