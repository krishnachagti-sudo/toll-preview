# Iowa teacher license renewal hours

In Iowa, a Standard teaching license needs 6 renewal credits every 5 years. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 3 renewal credits logged, 3 renewal credits are left on November 8, 2026, about 0.4 renewal credits a month.

## The rule

Iowa: what renewal needs

| License | Required | Cycle | Example: began, ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard teaching license | 6 renewal credits | 5 years from issue | 2022-07-01 to 2027-06-30 |
| Master educator teaching license | 4 renewal credits | 5 years from issue | 2022-07-01 to 2027-06-30 |

## Required topics and caps

- Individual professional development plan (IPDP): counts up to 3 renewal credits

## Good to know

- Mandatory reporter training for child abuse is also required at renewal.
- Iowa accepts renewal credits from approved Iowa providers and semester hours of college credit; it does not accept "professional development units".

## Iowa in its own words

- "6 renewal credits and the mandatory reporter training for child abuse." [Iowa Board of Educational Examiners, Standard Teaching License](https://educate.iowa.gov/educator-licensure/licenses-authorizations/standard-teaching-license)

- "Credits need to be earned after the issue date of the current license, or be no more than five years old if the license is expired." [Iowa Board of Educational Examiners, More Renewal Information](https://educate.iowa.gov/educator-licensure/applyrenewconvert/more-renewal-information)
- "Converting the Standard Educator license, will change the license type to a Master Educator license, and renew the license for an additional five years." [Iowa Board of Educational Examiners, Standard Teaching License](https://educate.iowa.gov/educator-licensure/licenses-authorizations/standard-teaching-license)

[Iowa Board of Educational Examiners, More Renewal Information](https://educate.iowa.gov/educator-licensure/applyrenewconvert/more-renewal-information)

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Iowa teaching license?

Standard teaching license: 6 renewal credits; Master educator teaching license: 4 renewal credits.

### When is my Iowa renewal due?

5 years from issue; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
