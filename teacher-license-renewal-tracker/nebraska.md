# Nebraska teacher license renewal hours

In Nebraska, an Initial or Standard teaching certificate needs 6 credit hours every 5 years, ending September 30 of the expiration year. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends September 30, 2027: with 3 credit hours logged, 3 credit hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 0.3 credit hours a month.

## The rule

Nebraska: what renewal needs

| License | Required | Cycle | Example: began, ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initial or Standard teaching certificate | 6 credit hours | 5 years, ending September 30 of the expiration year | 2022-07-01 to 2027-09-30 |
| Professional teaching certificate | 6 credit hours | 10 years, ending September 30 of the expiration year | 2022-07-01 to 2032-09-30 |

- Or: half-time or more teaching (or a certificated position) for one school year within the past five years.

## Good to know

- Nebraska verifies Nebraska teaching experience itself (NSSRS); hours are the route for those without that experience.

## Nebraska in its own words

- "Standard Teaching Certificate– expires September 30th in the fifth (5th) year following issuance or renewal." [Nebraska Department of Education, Certificate Renewals](https://www.education.ne.gov/tcert/renewals/)
- "Initial Teaching Certificate– expires September 30th in the fifth (5th) year following issuance or renewal." [Nebraska Department of Education, Certificate Renewals](https://www.education.ne.gov/tcert/renewals/)
- "Have completed six (6) credit hours in education or your content area or equivalent professional development within the past five (5) years while holding or qualified (expired) to hold a Nebraska teaching certificate" [Nebraska Department of Education, Certificate Renewals](https://www.education.ne.gov/tcert/renewals/)
- "Have taught or held a position requiring a teaching certificate for half time or more for one (1) school year within the past five (5) years in a legally operate school system." [Nebraska Department of Education, Certificate Renewals](https://www.education.ne.gov/tcert/renewals/)
- "Professional Teaching Certificate– expires September 30th in the tenth (10th) year following issuance or renewal." [Nebraska Department of Education, Certificate Renewals](https://www.education.ne.gov/tcert/renewals/)

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Nebraska teaching license?

Initial or Standard teaching certificate: 6 credit hours; Professional teaching certificate: 6 credit hours.

### When is my Nebraska renewal due?

5 years, ending September 30 of the expiration year; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends September 30, 2027.
