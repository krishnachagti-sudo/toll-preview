# Vermont teacher license renewal hours

In Vermont, a Level II license (per endorsement) needs 6 credits or 90 clock hours, or a combination in each license term. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022 and ends June 30, 2027: with 3 credits logged, 3 credits are left on November 8, 2026, about 0.4 credits a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 15 clock hours = 1 credit

> 15 hours counted as one credit, as the page pairs them (six credits or 90 hours; two credits or 30 hours).

## Required topics and caps

- Level II license (per endorsement): at least 2 credits in knowledge and performance standards of the endorsement
- Three-year Level I license (per endorsement): at least 1 credit in knowledge and performance standards of the endorsement

## Good to know

- The rest of the credits must fall within the Core Teaching and Leadership Standards for Vermont Educators.
- The Level II license length is not stated on the renewal page: enter your expiration date.

## Vermont in its own words

- "each endorsement is required to have six credits or 90 hours of new professional learning. At least two credits (or 30 hours) of new learning must apply directly to the knowledge and performance standards of the endorsement to be renewed." [Vermont Agency of Education, Renew License](https://education.vermont.gov/educators/licensed-vermont-educators/renew-license)
- "For educators renewing a three-year Level I license, each endorsement is required to have three credits (or 45 hours) of new professional learning. At least one credit (or 15 hours) of new learning must apply directly to the knowledge and performance standards of the endorsement to be renewed." [Vermont Agency of Education, Renew License](https://education.vermont.gov/educators/licensed-vermont-educators/renew-license)

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Vermont teaching license?

Level II license (per endorsement): 6 credits or 90 clock hours, or a combination; Three-year Level I license (per endorsement): 3 credits or 45 clock hours, or a combination.

### When is my Vermont renewal due?

The dates on your license; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
