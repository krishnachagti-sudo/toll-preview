# Georgia teacher license renewal hours

In Georgia, a Standard or Performance-Based Professional certificate (not employed in a Georgia school system at renewal) needs 6 semester hours, 10 PLUs, 10 CEUs or 100 clock hours (one of these) in each license term. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022 and ends June 30, 2027: with 3 semester hours logged, 3 semester hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 0.4 semester hours a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 PLU = 1 semester hour
- 1 CEU = 1 semester hour
- 1 clock hour = 1 semester hour

- Other routes the page lists: retaking the GACE content assessment, a Georgia employer verifying professional learning, a year of out-of-state experience, or National Board certification.

## Georgia in its own words

- "Complete one of the following renewal requirements within the validity of your current certificate, or within the 5 preceding years of the date of the renewal application for expired certification:" [Georgia Professional Standards Commission, Certificate Renewal](https://www.gapsc.com/CurrentEducator/takeCareYourCertificate/RenewCertificate.aspx)
- "6 semester hours from an accredited college/university with a grade of "B" or better." [Georgia Professional Standards Commission, Certificate Renewal](https://www.gapsc.com/CurrentEducator/takeCareYourCertificate/RenewCertificate.aspx)

- "10 Continuing Education Units (CEUs) from an accredited college/university or a provider authorized by the International Association for Continuing Education and Training ( IACET)." ## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Georgia teaching license?

Standard or Performance-Based Professional certificate (not employed in a Georgia school system at renewal): 6 semester hours, 10 PLUs, 10 CEUs or 100 clock hours (one of these).

### When is my Georgia renewal due?

The dates on your license; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
