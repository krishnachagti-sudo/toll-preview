License renewal, state by state

Teacher license renewal hours tracker

Log your PD and see how many hours (or credits, CEUs, PDPs) are left and the date they are due, in your state's own unit, with its rule quoted from its own site.

  • Made by Conyso
  • Exact, no AI
  • Official sources, dated
  • Files you upload are not kept
  • SecurityTerms

Free on this pageAudit export $0.99 a renewal cycle$0.01 a lookup over the API or MCP

What you get

Pick your state and license, enter when your cycle began, and log your PD. You get the hours (or credits, CEUs, PDPs) you have in your state's own unit, how many are left, the date they are due and how much a month that is, with required topics and caps checked and the state's rule quoted from its own site.

Your totals on this page are free. The audit export (CSV log and printable summary) is $0.99 for a whole renewal cycle; over the API or MCP a lookup is $0.01.

1. Your license

Updates as you edit. Free on this page.

2. Your activities

Kept in this browser only. Each one is sent to work out the totals, and is not stored on the server.

ExampleTexas Education Agency rule

Example: Texas Classroom Teacher, cycle began August 15, 2022, 5 activities

41CPE hours left by August 14, 2027

You have 109 CPE hours of 150 for your Texas Classroom Teacher; 41 CPE hours left by August 14, 2027, about 4.5 CPE hours a month. Not counted over the caps: 10 of independent study, 2 of mental health first aid, trauma-informed care, grief-informed practices.

Five activities from a cycle that began August 15, 2022: a 6-hour in-service, a 3-credit graduate course (45 CPE hours at 15 a credit), 12 hours on dyslexia, 40 hours of professional reading (capped at 20 percent, 30 hours) and 18 hours of mental health first aid (capped at 16).

CSV of every activity with its converted value and flags, plus a printable summary with the rule's source. Exporting the same cycle again is free.

For HR

Who is due when, across your staff.

One educator a line: name, state, license id (optional), expiration date, hours logged (optional).

Example5 educators

Of 5 educators on November 8, 2026: 1 expired, 1 expiring within 6 months, 1 within 7 to 12 months, 1 within 13 to 24 months and 1 later.

Colorado (October 31, 2026) has expired; Texas (March 31, 2027, 38 of 150 CPE hours still needed) is next, then North Carolina (June 30, 2027), Ohio (2028) and Pennsylvania (2029).

The rules

Where the rules come from, word for word.

Each covered state's rule is quoted word for word from that state's own education agency or licensing board page, read on November 8, 2026; every answer shows the quote and the link.

What it does not cover

State portals (PERMS, TEACH, ECOS and the rest), and states whose official pages we could not read.

Read the full list
  • Rules quoted from each state's own education agency page, with the date we read it. Not legal advice: your state agency, LPDC or district decides what counts.
  • Totals come from the activities you enter; this tool does not read PERMS, TEACH, ECOS or any state portal.
  • States whose official pages we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.

Price

Free totals, a paid audit export.

This tool never costs more than $9.99 in a month.

  • Rules and your hours left, on this page: free.
  • Rules or hours left over the API or MCP: $0.01 per lookup.
  • Audit export (CSV log and printable summary) for one renewal cycle: $0.99 per renewal cycle; a repeat for the same renewal cycle is free for 72 months.
  • Staff expiry table: $0.05 per educator, at least $0.50 per table.
  • At most $9.99 a calendar month for this tool; after that it is free until the month ends.
