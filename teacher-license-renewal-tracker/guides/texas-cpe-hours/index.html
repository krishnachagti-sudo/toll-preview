Texas CPE hours: what the 150 must cover

A Texas classroom teacher needs 150 CPE hours every five years (200 for principals, counselors, librarians, diagnosticians, reading specialists, superintendents or several certificates); a college course counts 15 CPE hours a semester credit, independent study counts up to 30 of the 150, and a 3-credit course, 40 hours of reading and 20 of mentoring make 95 CPE hours.

  • Superintendents: at least 2.5 hours every five years on child abuse, human trafficking and maltreatment (TEA, Texas Education Code 21.054(h)).

Questions

How many CPE hours do Texas teachers need?

150 CPE hours every five years for a classroom teacher certificate; 200 for principal, superintendent, counselor, librarian, educational diagnostician and reading specialist certificates, and 200 in total for several certificates.
