# New York CTLE: 100 hours and what counts

New York Professional certificate holders need 100 CTLE clock hours in each five-year registration period, at least 15 of them in language acquisition (50 for ESOL and bilingual extension holders); with 80 hours logged, 10 of them on language acquisition, 20 CTLE clock hours are left, including 5 more on language acquisition.

## Mentoring and college credit

- 1 semester hour = 15 CTLE clock hours
- 1 quarter hour = 10 CTLE clock hours
- Mentoring a new teacher counts up to 30 hours and a teacher candidate up to 25, at the district's discretion.

## NYSED's words

- "100 hours of CTLE are required during every five-year registration period" [New York State Education Department, Registration and CTLE Chart](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/registration-and-ctle-chart)
- "All other Professional teacher, School Building Leader, and School District Leader certificate holders must complete a minimum of 15 percent of the required CTLE clock hours dedicated to language acquisition addressing the needs of ELLs, including a focus on best practices for co-teaching strategies and integrating language and content instruction for ELLs." [New York State Education Department, Language Acquisition (CTLE)](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/language-acquisition)
- "For credit-bearing university or college courses, each semester-hour of credit is equal to 15 clock hours of CTLE, and each quarter-hour of credit is equal to 10 clock hours of CTLE." [New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/continuing-teacher-and-leader-education-ctle)
- "After the first registration period, the length of subsequent registration periods is exactly five years. Therefore, the expiration date for future registration periods is always the last day of the month preceding the educator's month of birth." [New York State Education Department, CTLE Registration Information](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/ctle-registration-information)
- "The first registration period is five years and 0-11 months, with the expiration date on the last day of the month preceding the educator's month of birth." [New York State Education Department, CTLE Registration Information](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/ctle-registration-information)
- "credit up to 30 hours of such time towards their CTLE requirement in each five-year registration period." [New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/continuing-teacher-and-leader-education-ctle)
- "credit up to 25 hours of such time towards their CTLE requirement in each five-year registration period." [New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/continuing-teacher-and-leader-education-ctle)
- "PERMANENT Classroom Teacher/School Leader ONLY" [New York State Education Department, Registration and CTLE Chart](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/registration-and-ctle-chart)
- "NOT subject to CTLE" [New York State Education Department, Registration and CTLE Chart](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/registration-and-ctle-chart)

## Questions

### Do permanent New York certificate holders need CTLE?

No: permanent classroom teacher and school leader certificates are not subject to CTLE.
