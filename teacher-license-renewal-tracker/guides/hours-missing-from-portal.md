# Missing hours in your state portal

Find out who reports your hours first: in Pennsylvania the provider reports to PERMS and you must check it and ask the provider to fix gaps (PDE writes at least 12 months before your 5-year period ends); Texas does not count hours for you at all, and Tennessee and Montana have you enter your own PDPs or PDUs.

| State | Portal | Who records the hours | The agency's words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pennsylvania | PERMS | Approved providers report; you check and ask the provider to correct it | "It is the responsibility of the educator to monitor their record of credits or hours in PERMS. It is also the educator's responsibility to contact the provider(s) to correct any discrepancies." [Pennsylvania Department of Education, General Act 48 FAQ](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/education/programs-and-services/educators/continuing-education-and-professional-development/act-48-and-perms/frequently-asked-questions/general-act-48-faq) |
| Texas | ECOS | Nobody: you attest at renewal and keep records five years | "Instead, educators attest that they have completed the required CPE hours when renewing their certificates and must retain supporting documentation for five years, providing it to TEA only if requested as part of a certificate renewal audit." [Texas Education Agency, Standard Certificate Renewal and Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Information](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/certification/certification-renewals/standard-certificate-renewal-and-continuing-professional-education-cpe-information) |
| Tennessee | TNCompass | You enter your own PDPs; your district approves them | "Educators submit their own PDPs into TNCompass." | Montana | TeachMT | You enter PDUs and can store certificates | "TMT allows you to store your PDU certificates as you earn them." [Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License](https://opi.mt.gov/Educators/Licensure/Educator-Licensure/Renew-Update-Advance-License) |
| Wyoming | PTSB | The workshop facilitator enters renewal credits | "Workshop facilitators are responsible for entering your renewal credit(s). PTSB no longer enters renewal credits." ## Why a private log helps

A portal shows what was reported; your log shows what you did, with the evidence. The difference is the list of providers to call, and the date each hour was earned tells you whether it still falls inside the cycle.

> Rules quoted from each state's own education agency page, with the date we read it. Not legal advice: your state agency, LPDC or district decides what counts. Totals come from the activities you enter; this tool does not read PERMS, TEACH, ECOS or any state portal. States whose official pages we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.

## Steps

1. **Check who reports.** The provider (Pennsylvania), your district (Tennessee approvals), a workshop facilitator (Wyoming) or you (Texas, Montana).
2. **Collect the proof.** Certificate of completion, transcript or attendance record, with the date, hours and provider.
3. **Contact the provider, then the district, then the agency.** Keep the date and any ticket number of each request.
4. **Log it yourself meanwhile.** A private log with the evidence column shows exactly which line is missing and when it was earned.
