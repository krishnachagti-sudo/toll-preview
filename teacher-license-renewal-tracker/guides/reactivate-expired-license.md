# Reactivating an expired teaching license

Most states we read let a lapsed license be renewed by finishing the same hours: Texas makes it inactive and adds a late fee, Pennsylvania keeps it valid but inactive until the Act 48 hours are reported, Alaska allows a 12-month reinstatement period and Maine 6 months; South Dakota counts activities only to October 1 of the expiration year.

[Texas Education Agency, Standard Certificate Renewal and Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Information](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/certification/certification-renewals/standard-certificate-renewal-and-continuing-professional-education-cpe-information) |

[Alaska Department of Education and Early Development, Renewal & Reinstatement](https://education.alaska.gov/teachercertification/renewal-reinstatement) |

| North Carolina | The same 8 renewal credits; apply any time | "If your license expired in previous years, the renewal request can be submitted at any time." [North Carolina Department of Public Instruction, Renew or Update Your Professional Educator's License](https://www.dpi.nc.gov/educators/educators-licensure/renew-or-update-your-professional-educators-license) |
| South Dakota | Invalid after October 1 of the expiration year; renew with all requirements and the invalid-status fee | "In order to renew a certificate with an invalid status, an educator shall complete all renewal requirements and pay the invalid educator status fee." [South Dakota Administrative Rule 24:28:17:10, Renewal requirements for an invalid educator status](https://sdlegislature.gov/Rules/Administrative/24:28:17:10) |
| Colorado | Activities within the seven years before the application | "For those with expired licenses, applicable activities must be completed within the seven years immediately preceding the submission date of your application to renew." | Iowa | Credits no more than five years old | "Credits need to be earned after the issue date of the current license, or be no more than five years old if the license is expired." [Iowa Board of Educational Examiners, More Renewal Information](https://educate.iowa.gov/educator-licensure/applyrenewconvert/more-renewal-information) |
| Georgia | Requirements within the five years before the application | "Complete one of the following renewal requirements within the validity of your current certificate, or within the 5 preceding years of the date of the renewal application for expired certification:" [Georgia Professional Standards Commission, Certificate Renewal](https://www.gapsc.com/CurrentEducator/takeCareYourCertificate/RenewCertificate.aspx) |

## Before you apply

- Find the exact expiration date on your license or in the state portal.
- Count only activities inside the window your state gives (the tracker flags the rest).
- Fees and applications are on each agency's page; we do not list fees.

> Rules quoted from each state's own education agency page, with the date we read it. Not legal advice: your state agency, LPDC or district decides what counts. Totals come from the activities you enter; this tool does not read PERMS, TEACH, ECOS or any state portal. States whose official pages we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.

Each state's own source is linked on its page and in the [renewal requirements by state table](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/teacher-license-renewal-tracker/requirements-by-state/) (read November 8, 2026 to November 9, 2026).
