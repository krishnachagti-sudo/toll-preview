# Moving states: what counts toward renewal

Each state counts activities inside its own license cycle: in Iowa, credits must be earned after the current license's issue date, so with a license issued July 1, 2025 a course from March 2024 is flagged and only 2 renewal credits of the 6 count.

## Check your new cycle

[Work out my hours left](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/teacher-license-renewal-tracker/?state=IA&license=standard&start=2022-07-01&logged=2&unit=credit)

## The worked Iowa case

- Course taken in the old state (2024-03-10): before the cycle (July 1, 2025)
- Course after the Iowa license (2025-10-04): counts 2

## Keep both records

- Getting a license through reciprocity is decided by the new state's licensing office; this guide is only about renewing it later.
- Keep the old state's certificates: Texas asks for five years of records and New York eight.
- Start a new cycle in the tracker for the new license, and keep the old export.

Each state's window is in [Do extra hours carry over?](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/teacher-license-renewal-tracker/guides/carry-over-hours/).
