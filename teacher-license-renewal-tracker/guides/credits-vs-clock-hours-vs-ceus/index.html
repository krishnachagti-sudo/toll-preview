Semester credits, clock hours and CEUs: converting for renewal

It depends on the state: one semester hour counts as 15 hours in Texas, New York, Colorado and Montana, 30 contact hours in Ohio and Pennsylvania, 30 PDUs in Oregon and 10 PDPs in Tennessee; in Ohio a 1-credit course, 3 CEUs and 45 workshop hours make 105 contact hours of the 180 needed.

A worked Ohio mix

You have 105 contact hours of 180 for your Ohio Five-year professional or associate license; 75 contact hours left by June 30, 2027, about 9.8 contact hours a month.

Common mistakes

  • Adding CEUs to clock hours one for one: in Ohio a CEU is 10 contact hours, in Tennessee 5 PDPs, in Alaska one renewal credit (at least 15 contact hours).
  • Counting a college course in New York that the institution did not award as CTLE: NYSED counts it only from an approved CTLE sponsor.
  • Rounding each line: keep the decimals and total at the end (Alaska counts 1.5 quarter hours as one credit).

The states' words

  • "Typically, one CEU equals 10 contact hours, 3 CEUs equal one semester hour, and 30 contact hours equal one semester hour." Ohio State Board of Education, Professional Development Conversion Chart (LPDC), last updated May 2025
  • "College coursework (1 semester credit = 15 CPE hours)" Texas Education Agency, Standard Certificate Renewal and Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Information
  • "For credit-bearing university or college courses, each semester-hour of credit is equal to 15 clock hours of CTLE, and each quarter-hour of credit is equal to 10 clock hours of CTLE." New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)
  • "1 continuing education unit (CEU) = 5 PDPs" Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators
  • "One (1) semester credit= 30 PDUs" Oregon Teacher Standards and Practices Commission, Renewal Requirements by License Type (updated April 1, 2026)
  • "A unit of renewal credit (CEU) is equal to 10 clock hours of professional development" North Carolina Department of Public Instruction, Renew or Update Your Professional Educator's License

Rules quoted from each state's own education agency page, with the date we read it. Not legal advice: your state agency, LPDC or district decides what counts. Totals come from the activities you enter; this tool does not read PERMS, TEACH, ECOS or any state portal. States whose official pages we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.

Each state's own source is linked on its page and in the renewal requirements by state table (read November 8, 2026 to November 9, 2026).
