Reactivating an expired teaching license

Most states we read let a lapsed license be renewed by finishing the same hours: Texas makes it inactive and adds a late fee, Pennsylvania keeps it valid but inactive until the Act 48 hours are reported, Alaska allows a 12-month reinstatement period and Maine 6 months; South Dakota counts activities only to October 1 of the expiration year.

Before you apply

  • Find the exact expiration date on your license or in the state portal.
  • Count only activities inside the window your state gives (the tracker flags the rest).
  • Fees and applications are on each agency's page; we do not list fees.

Rules quoted from each state's own education agency page, with the date we read it. Not legal advice: your state agency, LPDC or district decides what counts. Totals come from the activities you enter; this tool does not read PERMS, TEACH, ECOS or any state portal. States whose official pages we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.

Each state's own source is linked on its page and in the renewal requirements by state table (read November 8, 2026).
