# Semester credits, clock hours and CEUs: converting for renewal

It depends on the state: one semester hour counts as 15 hours in Texas, New York, Colorado and Montana, 30 contact hours in Ohio and Pennsylvania, 30 PDUs in Oregon and 10 PDPs in Tennessee; in Ohio a 1-credit course, 3 CEUs and 45 workshop hours make 105 contact hours of the 180 needed.

You have 105 contact hours of 180 for your Ohio Five-year professional or associate license; 75 contact hours left by June 30, 2027, about 9.8 contact hours a month.

## Common mistakes

- Adding CEUs to clock hours one for one: in Ohio a CEU is 10 contact hours, in Tennessee 5 PDPs, in Alaska one renewal credit (at least 15 contact hours).
- Counting a college course in New York that the institution did not award as CTLE: NYSED counts it only from an approved CTLE sponsor.
- Rounding each line: keep the decimals and total at the end (Alaska counts 1.5 quarter hours as one credit).

## The states' words

- "Typically, one CEU equals 10 contact hours, 3 CEUs equal one semester hour, and 30 contact hours equal one semester hour." [Ohio State Board of Education, Professional Development Conversion Chart (LPDC), last updated May 2025](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/sboe.ohio.gov/LPDC/resources/CEU-Conversion-Chart.pdf)
- "College coursework (1 semester credit = 15 CPE hours)" [Texas Education Agency, Standard Certificate Renewal and Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Information](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/certification/certification-renewals/standard-certificate-renewal-and-continuing-professional-education-cpe-information)
- "For credit-bearing university or college courses, each semester-hour of credit is equal to 15 clock hours of CTLE, and each quarter-hour of credit is equal to 10 clock hours of CTLE." [New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)](https://www.nysed.gov/teaching-initiatives/continuing-teacher-and-leader-education-ctle)
- "1 continuing education unit (CEU) = 5 PDPs" [Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators](https://www.tn.gov/education/educators/licensing/licensing-faq/pdp-faq-for-educators.html)
- "One (1) semester credit= 30 PDUs" [Oregon Teacher Standards and Practices Commission, Renewal Requirements by License Type (updated April 1, 2026)](https://www.oregon.gov/tspc/LIC/Documents/Renewal%20Requirements%20by%20License%20Type%20updated%204-1-2026.pdf)
- "A unit of renewal credit (CEU) is equal to 10 clock hours of professional development" [North Carolina Department of Public Instruction, Renew or Update Your Professional Educator's License](https://www.dpi.nc.gov/educators/educators-licensure/renew-or-update-your-professional-educators-license)

> Rules quoted from each state's own education agency page, with the date we read it. Not legal advice: your state agency, LPDC or district decides what counts. Totals come from the activities you enter; this tool does not read PERMS, TEACH, ECOS or any state portal. States whose official pages we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.

### How many clock hours is one semester credit for teacher renewal?

It depends on the state: 15 in Texas, New York, Colorado and Montana; 30 contact hours in Ohio; Tennessee counts 10 PDPs and Oregon 30 PDUs.
