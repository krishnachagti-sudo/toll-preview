# South Dakota teacher license renewal hours

In South Dakota, a Teacher, administrator or education specialist certificate needs 6 renewal credits every 5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 3 renewal credits logged, 3 renewal credits are left on November 8, 2026, about 0.3 renewal credits a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 semester hour = 1 renewal credit
- 15 clock hours = 1 renewal credit

## Required topics and caps

- Teacher, administrator or education specialist certificate: at least 3 renewal credits in transcripted college credit

- Or: national board certification or recertification, or two years as a mentee or mentor in a state-approved mentor program.

## South Dakota in its own words

- "A teacher, administrator, or education specialist certificate is valid for five years." [South Dakota Administrative Rule 24:28:17:12, Renewal requirements for educator certification](https://sdlegislature.gov/Rules/Administrative/24:28:17:12)
- "Complete six education-related credits, three of which must be transcripted, pursuant to 24:28:17:07." [South Dakota Administrative Rule 24:28:17:12, Renewal requirements for educator certification](https://sdlegislature.gov/Rules/Administrative/24:28:17:12)
- "Fifteen continuing education contact hours for one renewal credit;" [South Dakota Administrative Rule 24:28:17:07, Renewal credits](https://sdlegislature.gov/Rules/Administrative/24:28:17:07)
- "The educator certificate is valid from the date of issuance until June 30 of the year of expiration." [South Dakota Administrative Rule 24:28:17:01, Renewal cycle](https://sdlegislature.gov/Rules/Administrative/24:28:17:01)
- "The certificate is invalid if renewal requirements are not complete by October 1 of the year of expiration." [South Dakota Administrative Rule 24:28:17:01, Renewal cycle](https://sdlegislature.gov/Rules/Administrative/24:28:17:01)
- "The applicant for renewed certification shall complete the renewal requirements for a valid certificate between the issue date of the previous certificate and October 1 of the expiration year." [South Dakota Administrative Rule 24:28:17:02, Recency of credits for renewal](https://sdlegislature.gov/Rules/Administrative/24:28:17:02)
- "In order to renew a certificate with an invalid status, an educator shall complete all renewal requirements and pay the invalid educator status fee." [South Dakota Administrative Rule 24:28:17:10, Renewal requirements for an invalid educator status](https://sdlegislature.gov/Rules/Administrative/24:28:17:10)
