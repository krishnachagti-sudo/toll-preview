Renewal by state

Montana teacher license renewal hours

In Montana, a Class 1 license needs 60 PDUs every 5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 30 PDUs logged, 30 PDUs are left on November 8, 2026, about 3.1 PDUs a month.

The rule

Converting credits and CEUs

  • 1 semester hour = 15 PDUs
  • 1 quarter hour = 10 PDUs

Hours left and the monthly pace

For a Class 1 license whose cycle began July 1, 2022, as of November 8, 2026 (9.72 months before activities stop counting on August 31, 2027).

Good to know

  • Class 4A first renewals also need PDUs in career and technical education curriculum and instruction and in safety and teacher liability.

Montana in its own words

  • "For my Class 1 license 60 professional development units (PDUs), or any combination of PDUs and semester/quarter college credits. Each semester credit is worth 15 PDUs, and each quarter credit is worth 10 PDUs." Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License
  • "the professional development units (PDUs) you submit must have been earned during the five years of validity of the license you are renewing, through August 31 of the year the license expires." Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License
  • "For example, if your license expired on 06/30/2026, then it was issued effective 07/01/2021. You may use PDUs earned between 07/01/2021 and 08/31/2026 toward your renewal." Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License
  • "For my Class 2 license 60 professional development units (PDUs), or any combination of PDUs and semester/quarter college credits. Each semester credit is worth 15 PDUs, and each quarter credit is worth 10 PDUs." Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License
  • "For my Class 3 license 60 professional development units (PDUs), or any combination of PDUs and semester/quarter college credits. Each semester credit is worth 15 PDUs, and each quarter credit is worth 10 PDUs." Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License
  • "TMT allows you to store your PDU certificates as you earn them." Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License

Where to check your record: TeachMT (TMT). Other states: renewal requirements by state.

Questions

How many hours do I need to renew a Montana teaching license?

Class 1 license: 60 PDUs; Class 2 license: 60 PDUs; Class 3 license: 60 PDUs.

When is my Montana renewal due?

5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
