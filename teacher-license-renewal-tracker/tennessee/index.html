Renewal by state

Tennessee teacher license renewal hours

In Tennessee, a Professional license needs 60 PDPs in each license term. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022 and ends June 30, 2027: with 30 PDPs logged, 30 PDPs are left on November 8, 2026, about 4 PDPs a month.

The rule

Converting credits and CEUs

  • 1 clock hour = 1 PDP
  • 1 micro-credential = 6 PDPs
  • 1 CEU = 5 PDPs
  • 1 semester hour = 10 PDPs
  • Or: two years of qualifying educator experience during the current validity period.

Hours left and the monthly pace

For a Professional license whose cycle began July 1, 2022 and ends June 30, 2027, as of November 8, 2026 (7.69 months before activities stop counting on June 30, 2027).

Good to know

  • Evaluation scores and National Board certification also earn PDPs (see the FAQ).
  • Time spent presenting does not earn PDPs.

Tennessee in its own words

  • "To renew a professional license, educators must either earn 60 PDPs during the validity period of their license or complete 2 years of qualifying educator experience during the current validity period." Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators
  • "1 clock hour = 1 PDP" Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators
  • "1 micro-credential earned = 6 PDPs" Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators
  • "1 continuing education unit (CEU) = 5 PDPs" Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators
  • "1 semester hour credit = 10 PDPs or 1 semester credit hour audited = 5 PDPs" Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators
  • "To be valid for renewal or advancement of a Tennessee license, PDPs must be earned during the validity period of the license." Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators
  • "Educators submit their own PDPs into TNCompass." Tennessee Department of Education, Professional Development Points (PDP) FAQ for Educators

Questions

How many hours do I need to renew a Tennessee teaching license?

Professional license: 60 PDPs.

When is my Tennessee renewal due?

The dates on your license; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
