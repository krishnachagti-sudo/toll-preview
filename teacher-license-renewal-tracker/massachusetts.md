# Massachusetts teacher license renewal hours

In Massachusetts, a Professional license needs 150 PDPs every 5 years. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 75 PDPs logged, 75 PDPs are left on November 8, 2026, about 9.8 PDPs a month.

## Required topics and caps

- Professional license: at least 15 PDPs in content (subject matter knowledge)
- Professional license: at least 15 PDPs in pedagogy (professional skills and knowledge)
- Professional license: at least 15 PDPs in sheltered english immersion (sei) or english as a second language (esl)
- Professional license: at least 15 PDPs in students with disabilities and diverse learning styles

## Massachusetts in its own words

- "Individual professional development plans must include at least 150 Professional Development Points PDPs that break down as follows:" [Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, Advance, Extend, or Renew a License](https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/advance-extend-renew-license.html)
- "At least 15 Professional Development Points PDPs in content (subject matter knowledge)" [Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, Advance, Extend, or Renew a License](https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/advance-extend-renew-license.html)
- "At least 15 Professional Development Points PDPs in pedagogy (professional skills and knowledge)" [Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, Advance, Extend, or Renew a License](https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/advance-extend-renew-license.html)
- "At least 15 Professional Development Points PDPs related to Sheltered English Immersion (SEI) or English as a Second Language (ESL)" [Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, Advance, Extend, or Renew a License](https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/advance-extend-renew-license.html)
- "At least 15 Professional Development Points PDPs related to training in strategies for effective schooling for students with disabilities and the instruction of students with diverse learning styles" [Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, Advance, Extend, or Renew a License](https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/advance-extend-renew-license.html)
- "A Professional license is valid for five (5) calendar years." [Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, Advance, Extend, or Renew a License](https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/advance-extend-renew-license.html)
- "Licenses at the Professional stage must be renewed every five years or else they become inactive/invalid." [Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, Advance, Extend, or Renew a License](https://www.doe.mass.edu/licensure/advance-extend-renew-license.html)
