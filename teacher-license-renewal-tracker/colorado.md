# Colorado teacher license renewal hours

In Colorado, a Professional teacher license needs 90 contact hours or 6 semester hours, or a combination every 7 years. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2029: with 45 contact hours logged, 45 contact hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 1.5 contact hours a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 semester hour = 15 contact hours
- 1 quarter hour = 10 contact hours
- 1 CEU = 10 contact hours

## Required topics and caps

- Professional teacher license: at least 1 contact hour in behavioral health (culturally responsive, trauma- and evidence-informed)
- Professional teacher license: at least 1 contact hour in educating students with disabilities (child find, inclusive learning)
- Professional teacher license: at least 45 contact hours in english learner professional learning (elementary, english language arts, math, science, social studies or middle-level endorsements, if the designation is not held yet) (only where it applies to your endorsements)

## Colorado in its own words

- "Completion of 90 contact hours (six (6) semester hours) of applicable professional development activities:" [Colorado Department of Education, Renew a Professional Teacher License](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/cdeprof/checklist-renewprofessionalteacher)
- "For active licensees, applicable activities must have been completed within the seven years of your active license." [Colorado Department of Education, Renew a Professional Teacher License](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/cdeprof/checklist-renewprofessionalteacher)
- "A minimum of one (1) contact hour related to behavioral health that is culturally responsive and trauma- and evidence-informed; and" [Colorado Department of Education, Renew a Professional Teacher License](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/cdeprof/checklist-renewprofessionalteacher)
- "A minimum of one (1) contact hour related to increasing awareness of laws and practices pertaining to educating students with disabilities in the classroom, including but not limited to Child Find and inclusive learning environments." [Colorado Department of Education, Renew a Professional Teacher License](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/cdeprof/checklist-renewprofessionalteacher)
- "A minimum of 45 hours is required to satisfy this requirement." [Colorado Department of Education, Renew a Professional Teacher License](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/cdeprof/checklist-renewprofessionalteacher)
- "These 45 hours indeed fulfill half of the 90 contact hours required for renewal." [Colorado Department of Education, Renew a Professional Teacher License](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/cdeprof/checklist-renewprofessionalteacher)
- "7 years from date of issue" [Colorado Department of Education, Renew a Professional Teacher License](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/cdeprof/checklist-renewprofessionalteacher)
- "1 semester hour = 15 contact hours \| 1 quarter hour = 10 contact hours \| 1 CEU = 10 contact hours" [Colorado Department of Education, Renew a Professional Teacher License](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/cdeprof/checklist-renewprofessionalteacher)
- "For those with expired licenses, applicable activities must be completed within the seven years immediately preceding the submission date of your application to renew." [Colorado Department of Education, Renew a Professional Teacher License](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/cdeprof/checklist-renewprofessionalteacher)
