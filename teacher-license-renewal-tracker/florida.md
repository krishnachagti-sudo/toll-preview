# Florida teacher license renewal hours

In Florida, a Professional Certificate needs 6 semester hours in each license term. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022 and ends June 30, 2027: with 3 semester hours logged, 3 semester hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 0.4 semester hours a month.

| Professional Certificate | 6 semester hours | The dates on your license | 2022-07-01 to 2027-06-30 |

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 20 inservice points = 1 semester hour

## Required topics and caps

- Professional Certificate: at least 1 semester hour in teaching students with disabilities (swd)

- A passing score on the Florida Subject Area Examination for the subject counts as 3 semester hours; a valid National Board certificate renews the subject it covers.

## Good to know

- The certificate length is on your certificate: enter its expiration date.
- Certificates with a beginning validity date of July 1, 2020 or later that include certain subjects also need 2 college credits or 40 inservice points in science of reading instruction; educational leadership certificates need 1 college credit or 20 inservice points in Florida's leadership standards. These are not checked here.

## Florida in its own words

- "Earn six (6) semester hours of college credit which must include at least one (1) semester hour of college credit in teaching students with disabilities (SWD)." [Florida Department of Education, Florida Educator Certification Renewal Requirements](https://www.fldoe.org/teaching/certification/renewal-requirements/)
- "All renewal credits must be earned during the validity period and prior to the expiration date of the current Professional Certificate." [Florida Department of Education, Florida Educator Certification Renewal Requirements](https://www.fldoe.org/teaching/certification/renewal-requirements/)
- "Twenty (20) inservice points in an approved Florida master inservice program are equivalent to one (1) semester hour of college credit." [Florida Department of Education, Florida Educator Certification Renewal Requirements](https://www.fldoe.org/teaching/certification/renewal-requirements/)
- "Any combination of equivalencies may be used to earn renewal credit, provided the total amount of renewal credit earned is equivalent to six semester hours of college credit." [Florida Department of Education, Florida Educator Certification Renewal Requirements](https://www.fldoe.org/teaching/certification/renewal-requirements/)
- "A passing score on the Florida Subject Area Examination specific to the subject to be renewed is equivalent to three (3) semester hours of college credit for renewal purposes only." [Florida Department of Education, Florida Educator Certification Renewal Requirements](https://www.fldoe.org/teaching/certification/renewal-requirements/)
- "For individuals who hold a certificate in educational leadership, the individual must earn a minimum of one college credit or 20 inservice points in Florida’s educational leadership standards to renew their professional certificate with a beginning validity date of July 1, 2020 or later." [Florida Department of Education, Florida Educator Certification Renewal Requirements](https://www.fldoe.org/teaching/certification/renewal-requirements/)

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Florida teaching license?

Professional Certificate: 6 semester hours.

### When is my Florida renewal due?

The dates on your license; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
