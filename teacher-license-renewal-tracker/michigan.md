# Michigan teacher license renewal hours

In Michigan, a Standard Teaching Certificate needs 150 professional learning hours every 5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 75 professional learning hours logged, 75 professional learning hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 9.8 professional learning hours a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 semester hour = 25 professional learning hours

- Or, once for this certificate: an education-related master's or higher degree, or a valid out-of-state teaching certificate.

## Good to know

- SCECHs and district-provided professional development (DPPD) hours count as clock hours, one for one.
- Hours must be entered in MOECS before you apply.

## Michigan in its own words

- "Teaching certificates are valid for 5 years and expire June 30." [Michigan Department of Education, Teacher Recertification](https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/cert-guidance/teacher-recertification)
- "150 hours of Education Related Professional Learning." [Michigan Department of Education, Standard Teaching Certificate Renewal](https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/cert-guidance/teacher-recertification/standard-renewal)
- "A total of 150 education-related professional learning hours must be earned since the issue date of the most recent certificate or renewal. This can be achieved through one of the following options:" [Michigan Department of Education, Standard Teaching Certificate Renewal](https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/cert-guidance/teacher-recertification/standard-renewal)
- "1 semester credit = 25 professional learning hours" [Michigan Department of Education, Standard Teaching Certificate Renewal](https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/cert-guidance/teacher-recertification/standard-renewal)
- "1 SCECH Hour = 1 professional learning hour" [Michigan Department of Education, Standard Teaching Certificate Renewal](https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/cert-guidance/teacher-recertification/standard-renewal)
- "1 DPPD hour = 1 professional learning hour" [Michigan Department of Education, Standard Teaching Certificate Renewal](https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/cert-guidance/teacher-recertification/standard-renewal)
- "3. Any combination of the above." [Michigan Department of Education, Standard Teaching Certificate Renewal](https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/cert-guidance/teacher-recertification/standard-renewal)
- "An education-related masters or higher degree earned at any time from a college or university recognized by Council for Higher Education (CHEA) or U.S. Department of Education (USDE) (may be used one time to renew the Standard Teaching Certificate)." [Michigan Department of Education, Standard Teaching Certificate Renewal](https://www.michigan.gov/mde/services/ed-serv/ed-cert/cert-guidance/teacher-recertification/standard-renewal)
