# Utah teacher license renewal hours

In Utah, a Professional Educator License (PEL) needs 100 renewal hours every 5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 50 renewal hours logged, 50 renewal hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 6.6 renewal hours a month.

## The rule

Utah: what renewal needs

| License | Required | Cycle | Example: began, ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Professional Educator License (PEL) | 100 renewal hours | 5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year | 2022-07-01 to 2027-06-30 |

- Or: working in a Utah educational agency or school for the entire past five years (your daily professional learning counts as the 100 hours, no form needed).
