# PD log template for license renewal

A renewal log needs one line per activity: the date, title, provider, amount and unit, the category it counts toward, and the certificate or transcript; this Texas example totals 109 CPE hours of 150 after converting a 3-credit course to 45 hours and applying the independent-study and mental-health caps.

## The columns

Template columns

| Column | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| date | The day the activity ended, like 2024-02-17 |
| title | Workshop, course or conference name |
| provider | Who ran it (district, college, approved provider) |
| category | A required topic or capped area, or blank for general |
| amount | The number on the certificate |
| unit | clock hours, semester hours, quarter hours, CEUs, PDPs, PDUs |
| evidence | Certificate number, transcript, or where the file is |

## How long to keep it

- "Instead, educators attest that they have completed the required CPE hours when renewing their certificates and must retain supporting documentation for five years, providing it to TEA only if requested as part of a certificate renewal audit." [Texas Education Agency, Standard Certificate Renewal and Continuing Professional Education (CPE) Information](https://tea.texas.gov/educators/certification/certification-renewals/standard-certificate-renewal-and-continuing-professional-education-cpe-information)

## Steps

1. **Log each activity when it ends.** Date, title, provider, the amount and its unit (clock hours, semester hours, CEUs, PDPs), and where the certificate or transcript is.
2. **Mark what it counts toward.** A required topic (New York language acquisition, North Carolina literacy) or a capped category (Texas independent study).
3. **Total it by your state's rules.** The tracker converts each line into the state's unit, applies caps, and flags lines outside the cycle or missing a record.
4. **Export before you renew.** Keep the CSV and the summary with your certificates: Texas asks for five years of records, New York for eight.
