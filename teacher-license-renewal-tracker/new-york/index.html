Renewal by state

New York teacher license renewal hours

In New York, a Professional classroom teacher or school leader certificate needs 100 CTLE clock hours every 5 years, ending the last day of the month before your birth month. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends November 30, 2027: with 50 CTLE clock hours logged, 50 CTLE clock hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 4 CTLE clock hours a month.

The rule

Converting credits and CEUs

  • 1 semester hour = 15 CTLE clock hours
  • 1 quarter hour = 10 CTLE clock hours

Required topics and caps

  • Professional classroom teacher or school leader certificate: at least 15 CTLE clock hours in language acquisition (needs of english language learners)
  • Professional ESOL certificate or Bilingual Education extension: at least 50 CTLE clock hours in language acquisition (needs of english language learners)
  • Level III Teaching Assistant certificate: at least 15 CTLE clock hours in language acquisition (needs of english language learners)
  • Mentoring a new classroom teacher: counts up to 30 CTLE clock hours
  • Mentoring a teacher candidate: counts up to 25 CTLE clock hours

Hours left and the monthly pace

For a Professional classroom teacher or school leader certificate whose cycle began July 1, 2022, as of November 8, 2026 (12.71 months before activities stop counting on November 30, 2027).

Good to know

  • There is one CTLE requirement per educator, whatever the number of certificates.
  • Keep your own CTLE records (NYSED lists what they must show).

New York in its own words

  • "100 hours of CTLE are required during every five-year registration period" New York State Education Department, Registration and CTLE Chart
  • "All other Professional teacher, School Building Leader, and School District Leader certificate holders must complete a minimum of 15 percent of the required CTLE clock hours dedicated to language acquisition addressing the needs of ELLs, including a focus on best practices for co-teaching strategies and integrating language and content instruction for ELLs." New York State Education Department, Language Acquisition (CTLE)
  • "For credit-bearing university or college courses, each semester-hour of credit is equal to 15 clock hours of CTLE, and each quarter-hour of credit is equal to 10 clock hours of CTLE." New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)
  • "After the first registration period, the length of subsequent registration periods is exactly five years. Therefore, the expiration date for future registration periods is always the last day of the month preceding the educator's month of birth." New York State Education Department, CTLE Registration Information
  • "The first registration period is five years and 0-11 months, with the expiration date on the last day of the month preceding the educator's month of birth." New York State Education Department, CTLE Registration Information
  • "credit up to 30 hours of such time towards their CTLE requirement in each five-year registration period." New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)
  • "credit up to 25 hours of such time towards their CTLE requirement in each five-year registration period." New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)
  • "Educators who are subject to CTLE, and hold a Professional English to Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL) certificate or Bilingual Education extension, must complete a minimum of 50 percent of their required CTLE clock hours in language acquisition aligned with the core content area of instruction taught, including a focus on best practices for co-teaching strategies, and integrating language and content instruction for ELLs." New York State Education Department, Language Acquisition (CTLE)
  • "Teaching Assistant Level III certificate holders must complete a minimum of 15 percent of the required CTLE clock hours dedicated to language acquisition, addressing the needs of ELLs and integrating language and content instruction for such ELLs." New York State Education Department, Language Acquisition (CTLE)
  • "PERMANENT Classroom Teacher/School Leader ONLY" New York State Education Department, Registration and CTLE Chart
  • "NOT subject to CTLE" New York State Education Department, Registration and CTLE Chart
  • "There is one CTLE requirement per educator (e.g., 100 clock hours), regardless of the number of certificates held that are subject to CTLE." New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)
  • "Educators must maintain personal records of completed continuing teacher and leader education (CTLE), including:" New York State Education Department, Continuing Teacher and Leader Education (CTLE)

Questions

How many hours do I need to renew a New York teaching license?

Professional classroom teacher or school leader certificate: 100 CTLE clock hours; Professional ESOL certificate or Bilingual Education extension: 100 CTLE clock hours; Level III Teaching Assistant certificate: 100 CTLE clock hours; Permanent classroom teacher or school leader certificate: no hours.

When is my New York renewal due?

5 years, ending the last day of the month before your birth month; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends November 30, 2027.
