# Montana teacher license renewal hours

In Montana, a Class 1 license needs 60 PDUs every 5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 30 PDUs logged, 30 PDUs are left on November 8, 2026, about 3.1 PDUs a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 semester hour = 15 PDUs
- 1 quarter hour = 10 PDUs

## Montana in its own words

- "For my Class 1 license 60 professional development units (PDUs), or any combination of PDUs and semester/quarter college credits. Each semester credit is worth 15 PDUs, and each quarter credit is worth 10 PDUs." [Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License](https://opi.mt.gov/Educators/Licensure/Educator-Licensure/Renew-Update-Advance-License)
- "the professional development units (PDUs) you submit must have been earned during the five years of validity of the license you are renewing, through August 31 of the year the license expires." [Montana Office of Public Instruction, Renew, Update, Advance License](https://opi.mt.gov/Educators/Licensure/Educator-Licensure/Renew-Update-Advance-License)

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Montana teaching license?

Class 1 license: 60 PDUs; Class 2 license: 60 PDUs; Class 3 license: 60 PDUs.

### When is my Montana renewal due?

5 years, ending June 30 of the expiration year; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
