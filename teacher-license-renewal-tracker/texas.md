# Texas teacher license renewal hours

In Texas, a Classroom Teacher needs 150 CPE hours every 5 years. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 75 CPE hours logged, 75 CPE hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 9.8 CPE hours a month.

## The rule

Texas: what renewal needs

| License | Required | Cycle | Example: began, ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classroom Teacher | 150 CPE hours | 5 years from issue | 2022-07-01 to 2027-06-30 |
| Principal, Superintendent, Counselor, Librarian, Educational Diagnostician or Reading Specialist | 200 CPE hours | 5 years from issue | 2022-07-01 to 2027-06-30 |
| Multiple certificates (classroom teacher, administrator and/or student services) | 200 CPE hours | 5 years from issue | 2022-07-01 to 2027-06-30 |

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 semester hour = 15 CPE hours

## Required topics and caps

- Independent study: counts up to 20 percent of the total (30 CPE hours)
- Teaching or presenting CPE activities: counts up to 10 percent of the total (15 CPE hours)
- Mentoring other educators: counts up to 30 percent of the total (45 CPE hours)
- Mental health first aid, trauma-informed care, grief-informed practices: counts up to 16 CPE hours

## Good to know

- TEA does not count your hours: educators attest at renewal and keep the records for five years for an audit.
- An expired certificate becomes inactive; it can still be renewed with the CPE, the renewal fee and a late fee, without retaking exams.

### How many hours do I need to renew a Texas teaching license?

### When is my Texas renewal due?

5 years from issue; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
