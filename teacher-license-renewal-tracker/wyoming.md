# Wyoming teacher license renewal hours

In Wyoming, a Teaching or administrative license (most fields) needs 5 PD/renewal credits in each license term. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022 and ends June 30, 2027: with 3 PD/renewal credits logged, 2 PD/renewal credits are left on November 8, 2026, about 0.3 PD/renewal credits a month.

## Good to know

- Wyoming prints the exact requirement on the back of each license; check yours.
- The pages we read do not convert clock hours to renewal credits, so only credits are counted.

## Wyoming in its own words

- "For most teaching & administrative fields, five (5) Professional Development/Renewal Credits will be required to renew." [Wyoming Professional Teaching Standards Board, Renewing a License or Permit](https://wyomingptsb.com/licensure/renewing-a-license-or-permit/)
- "The requirements for renewing your specific license or permit are listed on the reverse side of the license or permit itself." [Wyoming Professional Teaching Standards Board, Renewing a License or Permit](https://wyomingptsb.com/licensure/renewing-a-license-or-permit/)
- "Workshop facilitators are responsible for entering your renewal credit(s). PTSB no longer enters renewal credits." [Wyoming Professional Teaching Standards Board, Workshop or Renewal Credit Notice](https://wyomingptsb.com/professional-development/workshop-or-renewal-credit-notice/)
- "It is recommended that you verify your credits as soon as possible, rather than waiting until your license is up for renewal only to find that you do not have the necessary renewal credits." [Wyoming Professional Teaching Standards Board, Workshop or Renewal Credit Notice](https://wyomingptsb.com/professional-development/workshop-or-renewal-credit-notice/)

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Wyoming teaching license?

Teaching or administrative license (most fields): 5 PD/renewal credits.

### When is my Wyoming renewal due?

The dates on your license; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
