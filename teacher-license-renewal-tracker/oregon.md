# Oregon teacher license renewal hours

In Oregon, a Professional Teaching License needs 125 PDUs in each license term once the license expires on or after July 1, 2029; one that expires before then needs no PDUs reported. For a license expiring June 30, 2030 with 63 PDUs logged, 62 PDUs are left on November 8, 2026, about 1.5 PDUs a month.

## The rule

Oregon: what renewal needs

| License | Required | Cycle | Example: began, ends |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Professional Teaching License | 125 PDUs | The dates on your license | 2025-07-01 to 2030-06-30 |
| Preliminary Teaching License | 75 PDUs | The dates on your license | 2025-07-01 to 2030-06-30 |
| Professional Administrator License | 120 PDUs | The dates on your license | 2025-07-01 to 2030-06-30 |
| Principal License | 90 PDUs | The dates on your license | 2025-07-01 to 2030-06-30 |

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 clock hour = 1 PDU
- 1 semester hour = 30 PDUs
- 1 quarter hour = 20 PDUs

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Oregon teaching license?

Professional Teaching License: 125 PDUs; Preliminary Teaching License: 75 PDUs; Professional Administrator License: 120 PDUs; Principal License: 90 PDUs.

### When is my Oregon renewal due?

The dates on your license; for a cycle that began July 1, 2025 it ends June 30, 2030.
