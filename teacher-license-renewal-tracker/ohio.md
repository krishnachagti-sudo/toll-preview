# Ohio teacher license renewal hours

In Ohio, a Five-year professional or associate license needs 180 contact hours, 6 semester hours or 18 CEUs, or a combination every 5 years. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 90 contact hours logged, 90 contact hours are left on November 8, 2026, about 11.8 contact hours a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 semester hour = 30 contact hours
- 1 quarter hour = 20 contact hours
- 1 CEU = 10 contact hours

## Good to know

- Quarter hours: the chart sets 9 quarter hours equal to 6 semester hours and 180 contact hours (20 contact hours each).
- Ohio Administrative Code 3301-24-08 sets what happens after a late filing; we could not read it, so it is not used.

## Ohio in its own words

- "To renew or align a five-year professional or associate license in the state of Ohio, you must earn the equivalent of six semester hours of coursework related to classroom teaching or the area of licensure." [Ohio State Board of Education, Professional Development Conversion Chart (LPDC), last updated May 2025](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/sboe.ohio.gov/LPDC/resources/CEU-Conversion-Chart.pdf)
- "Six semester hours equal 18 continuing education units (CEUs), 180 contact hours, or a combination thereof." [Ohio State Board of Education, Professional Development Conversion Chart (LPDC), last updated May 2025](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/sboe.ohio.gov/LPDC/resources/CEU-Conversion-Chart.pdf)
- "Typically, one CEU equals 10 contact hours, 3 CEUs equal one semester hour, and 30 contact hours equal one semester hour." [Ohio State Board of Education, Professional Development Conversion Chart (LPDC), last updated May 2025](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/sboe.ohio.gov/LPDC/resources/CEU-Conversion-Chart.pdf)
- "The coursework (or professional development) must be completed after the ISSUE DATE of your expiring credential(s) and after the APPROVAL DATE of your Individual Professional Development Plan (IPDP)." [Ohio State Board of Education, Professional Development Conversion Chart (LPDC), last updated May 2025](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/sboe.ohio.gov/LPDC/resources/CEU-Conversion-Chart.pdf)
- "This is only for use with your Local Professional Development Committee (LPDC). The LPDC must approve all CEUs or contact hours." [Ohio State Board of Education, Professional Development Conversion Chart (LPDC), last updated May 2025](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/sboe.ohio.gov/LPDC/resources/CEU-Conversion-Chart.pdf)

## Questions

### How many hours do I need to renew a Ohio teaching license?

Five-year professional or associate license: 180 contact hours, 6 semester hours or 18 CEUs, or a combination.

### When is my Ohio renewal due?

5 years from issue; for a cycle that began July 1, 2022 it ends June 30, 2027.
