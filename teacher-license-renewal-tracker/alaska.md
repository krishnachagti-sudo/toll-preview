# Alaska teacher license renewal hours

In Alaska, a Regular five-year certificate needs 6 renewal credits every 5 years. For a cycle that began July 1, 2022, it ends June 30, 2027: with 3 renewal credits logged, 3 renewal credits are left on November 8, 2026, about 0.4 renewal credits a month.

## Converting credits and CEUs

- 1 semester hour = 1 renewal credit
- 1.5 quarter hours = 1 renewal credit
- 1 CEU = 1 renewal credit
- 1 micro-credential = 1 renewal credit
- 15 clock hours = 1 renewal credit

## Good to know

- Non-academic hours need a pre-approval form; mandatory training from the previous five years is also required.
- After the expiration date there is a 12-month reinstatement period in which you are not certified.

## Alaska in its own words

- "Six renewal credits are required to renew or reinstate a regular five-year certificate. Each of the following equals one renewal credit:" [Alaska Department of Education and Early Development, Renewal & Reinstatement](https://education.alaska.gov/teachercertification/renewal-reinstatement)
- "One semester hour of credit earned from a regionally or nationally accredited university or college" [Alaska Department of Education and Early Development, Renewal & Reinstatement](https://education.alaska.gov/teachercertification/renewal-reinstatement)
- "One and a half quarter hours of credit earned from a regionally or nationally accredited university or college" [Alaska Department of Education and Early Development, Renewal & Reinstatement](https://education.alaska.gov/teachercertification/renewal-reinstatement)
- "One continuing education unit, with a minimum of 15 contact hours, from an approved provider" [Alaska Department of Education and Early Development, Renewal & Reinstatement](https://education.alaska.gov/teachercertification/renewal-reinstatement)
- "15 hours of non-academic credit (Requires a pre-approval form)" [Alaska Department of Education and Early Development, Renewal & Reinstatement](https://education.alaska.gov/teachercertification/renewal-reinstatement)
- "To renew a five-year certificate, applicants must meet the following requirements prior to the expiration date of their certificate:" [Alaska Department of Education and Early Development, Renewal & Reinstatement](https://education.alaska.gov/teachercertification/renewal-reinstatement)
- "You have exactly 12 months beyond the expiration date on your certificate to submit your application for reinstatement, without having to complete any additional requirements." [Alaska Department of Education and Early Development, Renewal & Reinstatement](https://education.alaska.gov/teachercertification/renewal-reinstatement)
