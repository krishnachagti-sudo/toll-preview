# How many name tags per sheet

Avery name badge sheets hold 8, 6 or 2 badges, so 200 guests need 25 sheets at 8 per sheet (Avery 5395, 5895, 5390), 34 at 6 per sheet (Avery 5392) and 100 at 2 per sheet (Avery 5146, 5147).

## Spares and part-used sheets

Spare badges count like guests: 200 guests and 20 spares at 8 per sheet is 28 sheets. Starting on a part-used sheet moves every badge along: starting at label 5 of an 8-label sheet, 200 guests take 26 sheets.
