# Avery 5390 name badges from a guest list

Avery 5390 name badge inserts are 2-1/4 in x 3-1/2 in, 8 per sheet (2 across, 4 down) on an 8.5 x 11 in sheet; 100 names need 13 sheets, with 4 labels left blank.

- Badge size: 2-1/4 in x 3-1/2 in
- Per sheet: 8 (2 x 4)
- Sheet: 8.5 x 11 in
- 100 guests: 13 sheets

## How big the names print

With the big-first-name style, a first name prints at up to 47.75 pt on this badge. "Christopher" prints at 41.25 pt (made smaller to fit); each badge is fitted on its own, so short names stay at the largest size.

- Al: 47.75 pt
- Ana: 47.75 pt
- Grace: 47.75 pt
- Michael: 47.75 pt
- Christopher: 41.25 pt (made smaller to fit)
- Maximiliana: 41.25 pt (made smaller to fit)
