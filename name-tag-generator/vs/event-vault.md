## When Event Vault badge maker is the better choice

- You want QR contact codes on the badges.
- You print on plain paper or card and cut it yourself.
- You want it free with no account.

## When this tool is

- You bought Avery badge sheets and need the names on the labels.
- You print walk-ins on part-used sheets.
- You want the API or MCP.
