# Avery Design & Print vs this name tag generator

Avery Design & Print is free, designed for every Avery product and imports a spreadsheet (Avery, read November 8, 2026); this tool costs $0.01 a badge with a $0.99 minimum, and adds a size fitted to each name, a start label for part-used sheets, walk-in badges, a check-in list and an API.

## Side by side

Avery Design & Print: from its own pages, read November 8, 2026. This tool: from its own prices and rules.

|  | Avery Design & Print | This tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free software (Avery sells the badges) | $0.01 a badge, at least $0.99 an event; reprints and walk-ins for the event free for 30 days |
| Account | Optional ("creating an account is optional") | Needed to download badges; preview and test page without one |
| Templates | Every Avery product | 6 badge templates and the numbers Avery lists as compatible |
| Spreadsheet import | Yes (Import Data / mail merge) | Yes (CSV, Excel, or rows over the API) |
| Long names | Not described on the pages read | Each name sized to fit its own badge; cut names listed |
| Walk-ins on a used sheet | Not described on the pages read | Yes, at any label |
| Designs, images, logos | Yes | No: text badges in Helvetica |

## When Avery Design & Print is the better choice

- You want a designed badge: images, logos, colors or a ready-made design.
- Your product number is not one of the templates here.
- You want it free and do not mind fitting long names yourself.

## When this tool is

- Your list has long names and you want every badge as large as it can be.
- You print on part-used sheets or print walk-ins at the door.
- You want badges from code or an AI agent (REST and MCP).
