# Conference Badge pricing vs paying per event batch

Conference Badge charges $0.39 per print-yourself PDF badge (read November 8, 2026), so 100 badges are $39.00; here the same 100 cost $1.00, and 500 cost $5.00 against $195.00.

## When Conference Badge is the better choice

- You want badges printed and shipped, with holders or lanyards.
- You want attendees pulled from Eventbrite without exporting a file.
- You need a badge size that is not an Avery template here.

## When this tool is

- You print the badges yourself on Avery sheets.
- Your event has more than a handful of guests: the price per badge is lower.
- You want walk-ins and a check-in list.
