Name tags from Google Sheets

To make name tags from Google Sheets, use File > Download to save the guest list, open the file in the badge maker and choose the Avery number: 60 guests on Avery 5392 (3 x 4 in, 6 per sheet) is 10 sheets.

Why not an add-on

Label add-ons for Google Docs and Sheets work inside your Drive. The most used one, Label Maker by Quicklution, lists 5M+ users and 4.8 stars from 8,019 ratings on its Workspace Marketplace page (read November 8, 2026) and asks, among other permissions, to "see, edit, create and delete all your Google Docs documents" and Sheets spreadsheets. Downloading the sheet and opening it here needs no access to your Drive.

The add-on is the better choice when you want the labels as an editable Google Doc, or you print labels from Sheets every week.

Compatible with Avery products; not made or endorsed by Avery. Avery is a trademark of its owner. Sizes and positions are from each template's avery.com page and the blank PDF template it offers, read November 8, 2026.

  • Google Docs Editors Help: Download a file (read November 8, 2026)support.google.com
  • Google Workspace Marketplace: Label Maker (read November 8, 2026)workspace.google.com
  • Avery: Template 5392, Name Badges Insert Refills 3" x 4" (and its blank PDF template U-0114-01), read November 8, 2026avery.com

Steps

  1. Download the sheet. In Google Sheets: File > Download, then choose a file type. Comma-separated values (.csv) or Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) both open here.
  2. Open it in the badge maker. Open the downloaded file or paste the rows. The first worksheet of an .xlsx is read.
  3. Pick the Avery number. Choose the template on the box, check the preview and print the free test page at 100%.
  4. Print. Download the badges and print at 100% (Actual size).
