# Sales tax in a 1099-K

If your platform's Box 1a includes sales tax you collected from buyers and paid over, take it out: it is not gross receipts (Schedule C instructions), so a $64,800.00 form with $4,620.00 of sales tax gives $60,180.00 of gross receipts.

- Box 1a: $64,800.00
- Sales tax out: $4,620.00
- Gross receipts: $60,180.00

Sales tax imposed on the buyer that you were required to collect and pay over is not in gross receipts and is not a deductible expense (line 23); sales tax imposed on you as the seller and collected from the buyer is in gross receipts and deductible on line 23.

## Questions

### Does the 1099-K include sales tax?

The IRS definition does not name sales tax; Box 1a is gross without adjustment for "any other amounts". eBay says it excludes sales tax; Square says it includes taxes collected on card transactions; Shopify's page does not say.

### Is sales tax I collected income?

Not when the tax is imposed on the buyer and you were required to collect and pay it over: the Schedule C instructions say it is not in gross receipts and not deductible. If the state let you keep part of it, that part is income on line 6.

### What if the tax is imposed on me as the seller?

Then the Schedule C instructions say the amount collected is in gross receipts on line 1 and the tax is deductible on line 23.
