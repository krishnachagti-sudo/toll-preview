# What Box 1a of Form 1099-K includes

Box 1a is the year's gross: the IRS says it is not adjusted for credits, cash equivalents, discounts, fees, refunds, shipping or any other amounts, so $3,000 of sales with $120 refunded and $87 of fees still shows $3,000.00, and you kept $2,793.00.

- Box 1a: $3,000.00
- Refunds: $120.00
- Fees: $87.00
- Kept: $2,793.00

The Instructions for Form 1099-K (Rev. 12/2026) define the gross amount as the total dollar amount of reportable payment transactions for each payee "without regard to any adjustments" for those items. Each transaction counts on its own date. The IRS page on what to do with the form adds that fees, credits, refunds, shipping, cash equivalents and discounts are not taxable income and can be deducted from the gross.

## The other boxes

- **Box 2:** Merchant category code (payment card filers; TPSOs leave it blank).
- **Box 3:** Number of payment transactions, not counting refund transactions.
- **Box 4:** Federal income tax withheld (backup withholding).
- **Box 1a:** Gross amount of the year's reportable payment card or third party network transactions, without any adjustment for credits, cash equivalents, discount amounts, fees, refunded amounts, shipping amounts or any other amounts; each transaction's dollar amount is set on the date of the transaction.
- **Box 1b:** The part of the gross where the card was not present (online, phone or mail-order sales, or a keyed-in card number).
- **Box 1c:** Cash tips included in Box 1a (new for 2026, P.L. 119-21 section 70201).
- **Box 1d:** Treasury Tipped Occupation Code(s) for the tips (new for 2026).
- **Box 5a-5l:** Gross amount of reportable payment transactions for each month, January (5a) to December (5l).
- **Box 6-8:** State, filer's state number and state income tax withheld, for up to two states.

## Questions

### Does the 1099-K include refunds?

Yes. Box 1a is gross "without regard to" refunded amounts (Instructions for Form 1099-K), so refunded sales stay in it. Box 3, the number of transactions, leaves refund transactions out.

### Does the 1099-K include fees?

Yes, in the sense that fees are not taken off: Box 1a is the gross before fees. The IRS says fees are not taxable income and can be deducted from the gross.

### Does the 1099-K include sales tax?

The IRS does not name sales tax; it depends on the platform. eBay says sales tax is excluded; Square says taxes collected on card transactions are included; Shopify's page does not say.

### What are Boxes 5a to 5l?

The gross for each month, January (5a) to December (5l). They should add up to Box 1a.
