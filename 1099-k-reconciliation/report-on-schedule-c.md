# How to report a 1099-K on Schedule C

Report the business part of Box 1a as gross receipts on Schedule C line 1, refunds on line 2 and fees on line 10: a $32,500.00 form with $1,900.00 of sales tax paid over gives $30,600.00 on line 1 and $1,460.00 on line 2.

- Line 1: $30,600.00
- Line 2: $1,460.00
- Fees, line 10: $4,062.50
- Box 1a: $32,500.00

The IRS page on Form 1099-K says gig workers, freelancers, hobby sellers and other self-employed people report Form 1099-K payments on Schedule C. The Schedule C instructions say line 1 is gross receipts (check any Forms 1099 for amounts that belong there), line 2 is returns and allowances, and line 10 is commissions and fees. Sales tax imposed on the buyer that you collected and paid over is not in gross receipts (line 23).

## Steps

1. **Start from Box 1a.** Add Box 1a from each Form 1099-K. It is gross: refunds, fees and other amounts are still in it.
2. **Take out what is not business income.** Personal payments, duplicates, sales tax imposed on the buyer that you paid over, and personal items sold.
3. **Line 1.** Enter gross receipts: what is left, plus cash, check and other sales not on a 1099-K.
4. **Line 2.** Enter refunds, chargebacks and allowances as a positive number.
5. **Line 10.** Include the platform and processing fees in commissions and fees.
6. **Keep the worksheet.** Keep the walkdown from Box 1a to line 1 with your records.

## Questions

### Do I enter the 1099-K gross on Schedule C line 1?

Line 1 is your gross receipts. The IRS says Box 1a is not adjusted for refunds, fees and the like and that those can be deducted from the gross; refunds go on line 2 and fees on line 10. Amounts that are not business income (personal payments, sales tax paid over) are not gross receipts.

### Where do partnerships and corporations report it?

The IRS page says a partnership uses Schedule E (Form 1040), Supplemental Income and Loss. If your name and TIN are on the form but the business files Form 1065, ask the issuer for a corrected form. A corporation uses Form 1120 or Form 1120-S. If your name and TIN are on the form but the business files Form 1120 or 1120-S, the IRS says to have the Form 1099-K corrected.

### Do I report sales that were not on a 1099-K?

Yes. All income is reported, including cash, checks and amounts under the threshold.
