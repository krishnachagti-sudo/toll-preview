# eBay 1099-K reconciliation

Your eBay 1099-K is gross: sales tax and canceled transactions are already left out, but credits, discounts, fees and refunds are not, so a $23,400.00 Box 1a with the example's refunds and fees comes down to $23,400.00 of gross receipts and $19,654.25 kept.

- Box 1a: $23,400.00
- Gross receipts: $23,400.00
- Kept: $19,654.25
- Time zone: Not stated

eBay's help page says the form covers this: Payments received for sales on eBay; if PayPal or another payment provider processed some of your eBay payments, that provider sends its own form. The gross amount of all reportable payments in the calendar year, not adjusted for credits, discounts, fees, refunds or other adjustable amounts. Sales tax and canceled transactions are excluded. The IRS defines Box 1a the same way for every platform: the gross, without regard to credits, cash equivalents, discounts, fees, refunds, shipping or any other amounts ([what Box 1a includes](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-k-reconciliation/what-box-1a-includes/)).

## Questions

### Does the eBay 1099-K include refunds and fees?

Yes: The gross amount of all reportable payments in the calendar year, not adjusted for credits, discounts, fees, refunds or other adjustable amounts. Sales tax and canceled transactions are excluded. The refunds go on Schedule C line 2 and the fees on line 10.

### What does the eBay 1099-K cover?

Payments received for sales on eBay; if PayPal or another payment provider processed some of your eBay payments, that provider sends its own form.

### Where do I download my eBay 1099-K?

Seller Hub: Payments \> Taxes \> 1099-K details \> Generate (the 1099-K detailed report). My eBay: Payments \> Payment reports and taxes \> Form 1099-K \> Request 1099-K detailed report.

### Why does my eBay 1099-K not match my sales report?

Based on when gross funds are processed by eBay, not necessarily when the seller receives them. The gross amount of all reportable payments in the calendar year, not adjusted for credits, discounts, fees, refunds or other adjustable amounts. Sales tax and canceled transactions are excluded.

### Does the eBay 1099-K include sales tax?
