Example: Shopify Payments, tax year 2025, Box 1a $48,000 in 612 transactions, with refunds, chargebacks, fees, labels and sales tax from the books

Your 2025 Form 1099-K Box 1a of $48,000.00 reconciles to $45,140.00 of business gross receipts after $2,860.00 of sales tax; $2,450.00 of refunds and chargebacks go on line 2 and $2,602.40 of fees and shipping are expenses, so you kept $40,087.60. It ties to your books.

CPA worksheet

The walkdown with every line and where it goes, each form's threshold check, Boxes 5a to 5l against your books or export, your personal items, the dates, and a corrected-form request when one is needed, as a document and a CSV. $3.99 per Form 1099-K; the same forms again within 90 days are free.

The rules it applies

  • Box 1a is gross: fees, refunds, shipping and discounts are not taken out.
  • The threshold: payment apps file over $20,000 and 200 transactions; card processors report any amount.
  • Where things go: refunds on Schedule C line 2, fees on line 10, personal items on Schedule 1 or Form 8949.
  • Wrong forms: ask the filer for a corrected form, and do not wait to file.

What it does not cover

State 1099-K thresholds are not covered: check your state revenue department. You decide which payments are business, personal or in error; the tool applies the IRS reporting text to your classification.

Read the full rules
  • Box 1a is gross. It is the year's total of card or payment-network transactions without any adjustment for credits, cash equivalents, discounts, fees, refunds, shipping or any other amounts (Instructions for Form 1099-K). The IRS says fees, credits, refunds, shipping, cash equivalents and discounts are not taxable income and can be deducted from the gross.
  • The threshold. A payment app or online marketplace files only when your gross payments are over $20,000 and your transactions over 200; card processors report any amount. A form can still come below it, and all income is reportable with or without a form.
  • Where things go. Self-employed: Schedule C, with refunds and allowances on line 2 and fees on line 10. Sales tax imposed on the buyer and paid over is not gross receipts. Personal items sold at a loss: the proceeds in the space at the top of Schedule 1 (or Form 8949 with code L); at a gain: Form 8949 and Schedule D.
  • Wrong forms. Ask the filer for a corrected form, not the IRS; a form you should not have received should be corrected to zero. Do not wait to file.
