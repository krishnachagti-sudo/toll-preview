# A2X and a 1099-K walkdown

A2X for Shopify starts at US$29 a month (up to 200 orders, 3 months history) and US$45 for Basic; a yearly 1099-K walkdown here is $3.99 per form.

Posts marketplace settlements to QuickBooks or Xero. Plans are sized by combined monthly orders across channels. The free trial has full access and posts three settlements to your accounting system. Mini keeps 3 months of history; Cost of Goods Sold needs Basic or above. The page says A2X plan prices increase from November 14, 2026 (excluding Mini plans).

## When they are the better choice

A2X is the better choice for ongoing bookkeeping: every payout posted to QuickBooks or Xero with fees and refunds split all year. With books kept that way, this tool is the yearly tie-out from the form to them.
