# The IRS 1099-K guide and this tool

The IRS pages are the authority: Box 1a is gross, a payment app files when payments pass $20,000 in more than 200 transactions, and fees, refunds and the like can be deducted; this tool applies those rules to your own Box 1a and totals.

Understanding your Form 1099-K and What to do with Form 1099-K explain who sends the form, the threshold, what Box 1a leaves in, where to report by taxpayer type, personal items, and what to do about a wrong form. They are free and they are the rules this tool follows, quoted with the date read.

## When they are the better choice

The IRS pages are better for the question of what the law says, and they are the source to cite. This tool is for the arithmetic: turning your form and your records into the lines of the return.

- [IRS: Understanding your Form 1099-K, read November 8, 2026](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/understanding-your-form-1099-k)
- [IRS: What to do with Form 1099-K, read November 8, 2026](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/what-to-do-with-form-1099-k)
- [IRS: Form 1099-K FAQs, General information, read November 8, 2026](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/form-1099-k-faqs-general-information)

> Computed from IRS instructions and FAQs (each cited with the date read) and the amounts you enter. Not tax or legal advice. You decide which payments are business, personal or in error; the tool applies the IRS reporting text to your classification. State 1099-K thresholds are not covered: check your state revenue department.
