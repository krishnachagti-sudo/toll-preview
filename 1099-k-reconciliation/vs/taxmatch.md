TaxMatch's Standard plan is about $9.95 a month after a 30-day free trial, for Shopify stores; this tool's worksheet is $3.99 per Form 1099-K, so 2 forms cost $7.98 a year.

TaxMatch: 1099-K Reconciler is a Shopify app by JMS Dev Lab, launched August 7, 2026, with 0 reviews when read. A Shopify app: installed in a Shopify store and billed through Shopify every 30 days. Shopify Payments connects itself; PayPal and Stripe 1099-K data is added by hand. Explains refunds, chargebacks, fees and sales tax, with a CPA worksheet as PDF and CSV. Its data-access list includes the store's orders for the last 60 days and Shopify Payments payouts and disputes. ## When they are the better choice

TaxMatch is the better choice for a Shopify-only store that wants the data pulled from the admin with no export, or that can finish inside the 30-day free trial. This tool fits sellers on several platforms, sellers outside Shopify, and anyone who wants to pay per form rather than per month.
