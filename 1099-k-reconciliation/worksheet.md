## Steps

1. **Box 1a.** Add Box 1a from every Form 1099-K you received for the year.
2. **Not income.** Take out personal payments, duplicate amounts, sales tax you collected and paid over, cash back and personal items you sold.
3. **Gross receipts.** What is left is business gross receipts from these forms (Schedule C line 1); add cash and other sales from your books.
4. **Returns.** Refunds, chargebacks and discounts go on Schedule C line 2.
5. **Expenses.** Fees go on line 10; shipping labels are an expense.
6. **Tie out.** What you kept should be close to the payouts; compare Boxes 5a to 5l with your books month by month.
