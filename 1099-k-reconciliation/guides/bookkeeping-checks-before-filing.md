## Steps

1. **Months add up.** Boxes 5a to 5l should add up to Box 1a; if not, ask the filer.
2. **Gross receipts vs sales.** Walk Box 1a down to gross receipts and compare with the books; cash and check sales make the books higher.
3. **Sales tax.** Check whether the platform put sales tax in Box 1a and take out what you paid over.
4. **Tips and shipping.** From 2026 Box 1c shows cash tips included in Box 1a; shipping charged is in the gross.
5. **Timing.** Compare each month: platforms date by UTC posting or processing, books by order date.
6. **Log it.** Keep the walkdown and the month table with the return for the preparer.
