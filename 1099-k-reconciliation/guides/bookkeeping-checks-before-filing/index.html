Bookkeeping checks before you file

Before you file, tie the form to the books: here $48,000.00 in Box 1a ties to $45,140.00 of gross receipts after $2,860.00 of sales tax, and 2 of the 12 months differ by $50.00 each way, a year-end timing shift.

Shopify Payments: $48,000.00 in 612 transactions is over $20,000 and over 200, so the platform had to file it. Your books' gross receipts of $45,140.00 tie to the walkdown exactly. 2 months differ from your books: January by -$50.00, December by $50.00. Sales near midnight and month ends often move between months because platforms date payments in UTC or by when funds are processed.

State 1099-K thresholds are not covered: check your state revenue department.

Steps

  1. Months add up. Boxes 5a to 5l should add up to Box 1a; if not, ask the filer.
  2. Gross receipts vs sales. Walk Box 1a down to gross receipts and compare with the books; cash and check sales make the books higher.
  3. Sales tax. Check whether the platform put sales tax in Box 1a and take out what you paid over.
  4. Tips and shipping. From 2026 Box 1c shows cash tips included in Box 1a; shipping charged is in the gross.
  5. Timing. Compare each month: platforms date by UTC posting or processing, books by order date.
  6. Log it. Keep the walkdown and the month table with the return for the preparer.
