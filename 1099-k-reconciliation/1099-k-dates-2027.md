# 1099-K dates for 2026 forms

Forms 1099-K for 2026 reach you by February 1, 2027, because January 31, 2027 is a Sunday; filers send them to the IRS by March 1, 2027 on paper or March 31, 2027 electronically.

- Payee copy: February 1, 2027
- IRS, paper: March 1, 2027
- IRS, e-file: March 31, 2027

The Guide to Information Returns sets January 31 for the payee copy of Form 1099-K and February 28 (March 31 if filed electronically) for the IRS; a due date on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday moves to the next business day.

## Questions

### When will I get my 2026 Form 1099-K?

By February 1, 2027. The due date is January 31, moved to the next business day because January 31, 2027 is a Sunday.

### When do platforms send 1099-Ks to the IRS?

By March 1, 2027 on paper (February 28, 2027 is a Sunday) or March 31, 2027 electronically.
