How it works

Each refunded FBA order is matched to the returns and the reimbursements by order ID. Every order still owed gets the first and last day Amazon accepts a claim, from Amazon's own help page for your marketplace: days 60 to 120 after the refund in the US, 45 to 105 in Canada and Australia.

What it does not cover

  • Seller-fulfilled refunds: only FBA refunds are reimbursed under this policy.
  • Amazon's "applicable fees": they are not listed, so a gap is shown to ask about, not as a sum owed.
  • Whether Amazon pays: Amazon decides every claim.

When can I claim one refund?

A refund made on September 20, 2026 can be claimed from November 19, 2026 to January 18, 2027 (days 60 to 120 after the refund, Amazon's United States help page).

Read how orders are joined, dated and valued

Joined by order ID

Every Refund row of an Amazon-fulfilled order is one refunded order. Units received back in the FBA Customer Returns report are returned; units with the disposition DAMAGED or CARRIER DAMAGED are Amazon's responsibility and still owed; CUSTOMER DAMAGED and DEFECTIVE are not eligible under Amazon's policy. Cash and inventory units in the Reimbursements report count as paid, and a reversed reimbursement is netted off.

Dated from Amazon's own page

Amazon's US help page says a customer return claim is filed no sooner than 60 days and no later than 120 days after the refund. Canada and Australia say 45 to 105. Each order owed gets its opening and closing day and its status on the day you choose. Nothing is drafted before the window opens: Amazon warns that premature requests can lead to account action.

The gap, not a guess

Amazon values a refund that never came back at the refund amount minus "applicable fees", without listing them. So a reimbursement below the refund is shown with its gap and the 60-day dispute deadline, and the case text asks Amazon which fees make up the difference.

The same check by hand, in Excel or Google Sheets
  1. Download the Date Range Transaction report and filter column type to Refund and fulfillment to Amazon.
  2. Download the FBA Customer Returns and Reimbursements reports for the same dates and later.
  3. Beside each refund, count its returns: =COUNTIFS(Returns!B:B, D2), where column B of the returns sheet is order-id and D2 the refund's order id.
  4. Count its reimbursements the same way on amazon-order-id, and sum amount-total with SUMIFS.
  5. For a refund with neither, the claim opens on the refund date + 60 and closes on the refund date + 120 (US).
  6. Sort by the closing date and file the ones open today, one order at a time.

A refund for two units with one returned, a CARRIER DAMAGED return, a reversed reimbursement and a reimbursement in inventory units each need their own formula; the checker handles them.

Please read

The check is free and runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded until you ask for the order list. The order list, case text and CSV are $0.25 per order found still owed, and you pay nothing for the orders that were returned or paid. At most $9.99 a month.

Read the notes, the prices and the sources
  • Files are processed in memory and not kept.
  • Not legal or accounting advice: Amazon decides every claim. Check the Eligible for claim tab of the Inventory Defect and Reimbursement portal before you file.
  • Claim windows are the ones stated on Amazon's help page for the marketplace, read on November 8, 2026. Amazon's US page says days 60 to 120 after the refund; its Canada and Australia pages say days 45 to 105.
  • Values are the refund amounts. Amazon pays the refund minus "applicable fees" and does not list those fees, so a gap is shown for you to ask about, not as a sum owed.

Prices

  • Claim window for refund dates: free.
  • Check (how many refunds are owed, and their total): free.
  • Audit (every order, its claim dates, case text and CSV): $0.25 per order found still owed; you pay only for what is delivered and the rest is refunded at once; each refunded FBA order in the file is reserved first.
  • At most $9.99 a calendar month for this tool; after that it is free until the month ends.
