# Every FBA reimbursement claim window

On Amazon's US help pages (read November 8, 2026), a refund that was never returned or reimbursed can be claimed from day 60 to day 120 after the refund (for a refund on November 9, 2026: January 8, 2027 to March 9, 2027); warehouse lost or damaged within 60 days of the report; a removal lost in transit from 15 days after its last movement to 75 days after the shipment was created; removal damage within 60 days of delivery.

## Why vendors disagree

Many guides give 45 to 105 days for customer returns. That is what Amazon's Canada and Australia help pages say, and Amazon's announcement effective October 15, 2025 was posted in the Australia forum. Amazon's US page says 60 to 120, and it also says that a customer who does not return the item within 60 days is in most cases charged and you are reimbursed. Older guides quote other windows again, such as 18 months. The table follows each marketplace's own page as read on November 8, 2026.

## Before you file

- Check the Eligible for claim, In progress and Resolved tabs of the Inventory Defect and Reimbursement portal; Amazon reimburses most of these by itself.
- Do not file before the window opens. Amazon's policy names submitting premature requests, or submitting a large number of requests in a short time: sellers who do this may receive delayed support on their cases or be subject to monitoring, investigation, and account action.
- Your account must be in normal status: "Your selling account is in normal status when you file a claim or when your claim is under review".
- Shipment to Amazon claims: You may file only one claim per shipment.

## Questions

### When can I file a claim for a refunded FBA item that never came back (US)?

Amazon's US help page says: You must submit your claim no sooner than 60 days and no later than 120 days after the customer refund or replacement.

### Is it 45 to 105 days or 60 to 120?

Both appear on Amazon's own pages. The US page, read November 8, 2026, says 60 to 120 days. The Canada and Australia pages say 45 to 105, and so does Amazon's October 15, 2025 announcement in the Australia Seller Forums. Use your marketplace's page.

### How long do I have to claim warehouse lost or damaged inventory?

Amazon's US help page says no later than 60 days after the date the item was reported as lost or damaged in your Inventory Defect and Reimbursement portal or Inventory Ledger report.
