# Why the reimbursement is below the refund

Amazon pays a refund that never came back at the refund amount minus applicable fees, without listing the fees: in the sample, a $24.99 refund was reimbursed at $8.12, $16.87 (67.5%) below the refund, and the value can be disputed until November 30, 2026.

## What the fee pages say

- The policy: we calculate the reimbursement value as the refund amount or the price of the replacement item on the original order minus applicable fees.
- The returns policy: FBA credits your account for all or part of the referral fee for selling on Amazon and, where applicable, the variable closing fee for a returned item. We don't reimburse you for applicable FBA fees.
- The fee schedule's refund administration fee is the applicable refund administration fee, which is the lesser of $5.00 or 20% of the applicable referral fee. Its example: if you refund a customer the $10.00 total sales price of an item in a category with a 15% referral fee, your refund administration fee is $0.30.
- None of these pages says which fees are "applicable" to a reimbursement, so a gap is a question to ask Amazon, not a sum owed.

## What to do

- Compare amount-total in the Reimbursements report with the refund in your transaction report, order by order.
- Ask within 60 days: within 60 days after we have issued the reimbursement.
- Ask which fees make up the difference; the case text page has the words.
