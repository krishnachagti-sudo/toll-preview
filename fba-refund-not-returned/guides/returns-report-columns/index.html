Returns report

The FBA Customer Returns report, column by column

Amazon's FBA Customer Returns report has 13 columns and 6 dispositions; DAMAGED and CARRIER DAMAGED mean Amazon takes responsibility, CUSTOMER DAMAGED and DEFECTIVE are not eligible, and a refunded order missing from the report entirely is a candidate claim once checked against the Reimbursements report (7 of the sample's 11 have no return line).

The dispositions

DispositionWhat Amazon saysFor a claim
SELLABLEThe unit was returned to stock and is available for purchase.Returned, not owed
DAMAGEDWe take responsibility for the damage. Ownership is transferred to us.Owed if not reimbursed
CUSTOMER DAMAGEDReturned to stock in unsellable condition; the customer is refunded but the unit stays in your inventory as unsellable.Not eligible
DEFECTIVEReturned to stock in unsellable condition; obviously damaged or the customer stated it is faulty.Not eligible
CARRIER DAMAGEDWe take responsibility for the damage. Ownership is transferred to Amazon.Owed if not reimbursed
EXPIREDWithin approximately 50 days of the expiration date; may be set aside as unsellable and removed for disposal.Returned, not owed

The help page writes the return date as DD-MM-YYYY; downloads may carry an ISO date and time. The checker reads both, and reads CARRIER_DAMAGED and CARRIER DAMAGED alike. Statuses: Unit returned to inventory; Reimbursed; Pending repackaging; Repackaged successfully; To be auto- removed; Immediate disposal; Immediate donation; Immediate liquidation; Policy-related disposal.

Questions

Why is a refunded order missing from the returns report?

At our discretion, we may give a refund without requiring that the customer return the item. These refunds don't appear in the FBA Customer Returns report.

What does the status Reimbursed mean?

A reimbursement has been approved for the unit. The unit has not been returned to your inventory (allow 5 days for reimbursements to post).
