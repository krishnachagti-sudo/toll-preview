# Reversed reimbursements and disputing the value

Amazon reverses a reimbursement when it was made in error or the unit is found, and the reversal shows as a negative cash quantity with the Original Reimbursement ID; in the sample, order 112-0000005-0000005 was reimbursed $42.00 on September 25, 2026 and reversed on October 20, 2026, so with no unit back it is owed again and can be claimed until November 22, 2026.

## How a reversal shows

| Case | What the report shows |
| --- | --- |
| Replacement units instead of cash | Quantity Reimbursed \[Cash\] \< 0, Quantity Reimbursed \[Inventory\] \> 0, Quantity Reimbursed \[Total\] = 0 |
| The defect was fixed (the unit was found) | Quantity Reimbursed \[Cash\] \< 0, Quantity Reimbursed \[Inventory\] empty, Quantity Reimbursed \[Total\] \< 0 |

These reversed reimbursements appear on your Reimbursements report under the "Reversed reimbursement" reason. To find the original, expand the search history by using exact dates to increase the search period to past 18 months.

## Disputing the value

The US policy gives one deadline: within 60 days after we have issued the reimbursement. In the sample, order 112-0000006-0000006 was reimbursed $8.12 for a $24.99 refund; the dispute is due by November 30, 2026.

Amazon's US help pages read on November 8, 2026 give no separate "re-evaluation" window. A 90-day re-evaluation window appears in an Amazon announcement in the Australian Seller Forums, not on the US pages, so it is not used here.

## Questions

### When does Amazon reverse a reimbursement?

Amazon's help says reversals happen when you receive replacement inventory units of the same product instead of the reimbursement, or (2) when the original defect has been fixed after you were reimbursed for it.

### How long do I have to dispute a reimbursement amount?

Amazon's US policy: within 60 days after we have issued the reimbursement.
