# A weekly 20-minute refund routine

Once a week is enough: Amazon's US window runs 61 days (day 60 to day 120 after the refund), so a weekly check sees every refund at least 8 times inside it; in the sample, the first window closes November 12, 2026, 4 days after the check.

## Why weekly

- Reimbursements can take up to five days to appear in the report after they have been approved.
- If a customer is refunded but does not return the item to a fulfillment center within 60 days, Amazon will in most cases charge the customer and reimburse you.
- Returns after the holidays: Customers can return eligible items shipped during November and December until the end of January.

## Steps

1. **Pull the reports.** Download the Date Range Transaction report, the FBA Customer Returns report and the Reimbursements report for the last 120 days.
2. **Check what closes this week.** Run the check; sort the orders owed by their closing day.
3. **File.** File the orders whose window closes in the next 7 days first, one order per claim.
4. **Track.** Note each case ID; a reimbursement can take up to five days to show in the report.
5. **Log.** Keep the CSV: next week, an order that was paid shows as reimbursed.
