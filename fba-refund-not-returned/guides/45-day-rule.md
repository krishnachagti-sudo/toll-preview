# The 45-day rule, marketplace by marketplace

45 days is the rule on Amazon's Canada, Australia and UK pages; Amazon's US page opens the claim at day 60 and closes it at day 120, so a refund on September 20, 2026 can be claimed from November 19, 2026 to January 18, 2027 in the US, and from November 4, 2026 to January 3, 2027 in Canada.

## What each page says

- US: You must submit your claim no sooner than 60 days and no later than 120 days after the customer refund or replacement.
- Canada: You can file a customer return claim no sooner than 45 days and no later than 105 days after the customer refund or replacement. The page's own table gives the start as 60 days from the customer refund date beside a 105-day end; its sentence says 45. The tool uses the sentence (45 to 105).
- Australia: You can file a customer return claim no sooner than 45 days and no later than 105 days after the customer refund or replacement.
- UK: If customer does not return your item to an Amazon fulfilment centre within 45 days of the refund (return less refund), we will issue a reimbursement and credit your account for the item. The page read gives no last day to file a customer return claim.

## Questions

### Does Amazon reimburse automatically after 45 days?

In Australia: "If a customer is refunded for your item, but the item is not returned to an Amazon fulfilment centre within 45 days of the refund, in most cases, we will reimburse you." In the UK: "If customer does not return your item to an Amazon fulfilment centre within 45 days of the refund (return less refund), we will issue a reimbursement and credit your account for the item." The US page says 60 days: "If a customer is refunded but does not return the item to a fulfillment center within 60 days, Amazon will in most cases charge the customer and reimburse you."

### Can I file before the window opens?

No. The US page says: You must submit your claim no sooner than 60 days and no later than 120 days after the customer refund or replacement. Claims outside the window are not eligible.
