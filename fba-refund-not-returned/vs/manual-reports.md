# By hand with Amazon's own reports

By hand is free: Amazon's portal lists claimable defects, and a spreadsheet join of three reports by order ID finds the rest; in the sample, 4 of the 11 refunded orders need more than a lookup (a reversal, a CARRIER DAMAGED return, a CUSTOMER DAMAGED return, or 1 of 2 units back).

## Check your own refunds

[Open the checker with the sample](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fba-refund-not-returned/?marketplace=US&asOf=2026-11-08)

## What Amazon gives you

- The Inventory Defect and Reimbursement portal: Inventory Defect and Reimbursement portal: Eligible for claim, In progress and Resolved tabs.
- The FBA Customer Returns report; but At our discretion, we may give a refund without requiring that the customer return the item. These refunds don't appear in the FBA Customer Returns report.
- The Reimbursements report: The Reimbursements report provides itemized details for all reimbursements, including those requested by you and those that are generated automatically.
- The Date Range Transaction report: Reports can be generated for up to 365 days at a time, going as far back as 2012.

## When by hand is better

- Manual is better if you have few orders and want zero third-party contact with your data.
- Amazon's portal already lists every defect you can claim and you just work through it.

## When this is enough

- You have more refunds than you can look up one at a time.
- You want the windows, the gaps and the case text in one pass.
