# Threecolts Margin Pro compared

Threecolts Margin Pro is 100% performance-based with no fee percentage published on the page, for sellers doing $1M+ a year through Amazon FBA or Walmart Fulfillment Services; doing customer-return claims yourself costs $0.25 an order found here, so 20 orders cost $5.00, against $100.00 at a 20% rate on $25 refunds.

## Check your own refunds

[Open the checker with the sample](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fba-refund-not-returned/?marketplace=US&asOf=2026-11-08)

## What Threecolts says

- Fee: 100% performance-based: only pay if we successfully recover money for you; no fee percentage published on the page.
- Who: sellers doing $1M+ a year through Amazon FBA or Walmart Fulfillment Services.
- Access: it connects in minutes with read-only access.
- Claim types: losses, damage, reimbursement revaluations, shipping errors, Walmart WFS; customer returns are not named.

The 20% in the answer is Getida's published starting rate, used only because Threecolts publishes none.

## When Threecolts is better

- A service is better if you want someone else to file and chase every case for a share of recoveries.
- You sell over $1M a year and also need Walmart WFS claims handled.
- Lost, damaged and inbound claims are most of your money.

## When this is enough

- Customer-return refunds are what you want to recover, and you can paste a case.
- You want no account connection and a known price.
