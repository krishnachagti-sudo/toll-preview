# AMZ Prep's free reimbursement tool compared

AMZ Prep's tool is free and gives estimated recovery value per SKU, exportable to CSV; here the same kind of upload gives each order (11 refunded orders in the sample, 4 claimable today worth $146.45 in refunds) with its claim window and case text, free to check and $0.25 an order found for the list.

## Check your own refunds

[Open the checker with the sample](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fba-refund-not-returned/?marketplace=US&asOf=2026-11-08)

## What AMZ Prep says

- Inputs: Inventory Ledger and Reimbursements reports required; FBA Customer Returns and FBA Inventory recommended; Removal Order Detail optional.
- Price: 100% Free Forever; No Signup Required.
- Output: estimated recovery value per SKU, exportable to CSV.
- Privacy: reports processed locally in your browser (their statement).
- Returns: it mentions identifying returns where customer kept the item.

## Side by side

|  | AMZ Prep | Here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reports | Inventory Ledger, Reimbursements (Returns recommended) | Refunds, FBA Customer Returns, Reimbursements |
| Result | Estimated recovery per SKU | Each order: returned, reimbursed, paid less, or owed |
| Claim window | Not dated per order | From Amazon's page: US days 60 to 120 |
| Case text | No | Per order, only inside its window |
| Price | Free | Check free; list $0.25 an order found, at most $9.99 a month |

## When AMZ Prep is better

- You want a quick free estimate across lost and damaged inventory, not only refunds.
- You work per SKU rather than per order.

## When this is enough

- You need the exact orders, the days each can be claimed, and the words to file.
- You want reversals and inventory reimbursements taken into account per order.
