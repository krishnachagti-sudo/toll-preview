## When sellerboard is better

- You want an always-on profit dashboard connected to your account, with reimbursements as one part of it.
- You track cost of goods already and want reimbursements judged against it.
- You want alerts without downloading reports.

## When this is enough

- You want each unpaid refund dated to Amazon's claim window for your marketplace.
- You want the case text written for you.
- You would rather not connect a third party to Seller Central: the free check runs in your browser.
