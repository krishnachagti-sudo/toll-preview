Reimbursement policy

The FBA reimbursement policy, with the numbers

Amazon values a refund that was never returned at the refund amount minus applicable fees, up to $5,000 a unit, and you may dispute the value within 60 days of the reimbursement: in the sample, a $24.99 refund reimbursed at $8.12 is $16.87 below the refund, and its dispute is due by November 30, 2026.

What the policy pays

  • A customer return claim: we calculate the reimbursement value as the refund amount or the price of the replacement item on the original order minus applicable fees.
  • Before a customer ordered (inbound, removal, warehouse): For shipment to Amazon claims, removals claims, and fulfillment center operations claims that occur before fulfillment, we will reimburse you the sourcing cost of the item.
  • The maximum reimbursement amount for a single unit of any FBA eligible item is $5,000.
  • If you disagree: within 60 days after we have issued the reimbursement.
  • Amazon's US returns page: FBA credits your account for all or part of the referral fee for selling on Amazon and, where applicable, the variable closing fee for a returned item. We don't reimburse you for applicable FBA fees.

The sample's reimbursements

Who is eligible

  • The item was registered in FBA when it was lost or damaged, and met FBA product restrictions and inventory requirements.
  • The shipment is not canceled or deleted, and the item is not pending disposal.
  • The item is not defective and was not damaged by a customer.
  • Your selling account is in normal status when you file a claim or when your claim is under review.
  • From the customer return claims page: Amazon won't reimburse you for any refund that you issue directly to a customer.

What Amazon says about filing

The policy warns against submitting premature requests, or submitting a large number of requests in a short time; sellers who repeatedly do this may receive delayed support on their cases or be subject to monitoring, investigation, and account action. The checker drafts a claim only for orders inside their window.

Questions

How much does Amazon reimburse for a refund that was never returned?

Amazon's policy says we calculate the reimbursement value as the refund amount or the price of the replacement item on the original order minus applicable fees.

What is the most Amazon reimburses for one unit?

The maximum reimbursement amount for a single unit of any FBA eligible item is $5,000.

What if I disagree with the amount?

File a claim on the Get help and resources page or with Seller Assistant within 60 days after we have issued the reimbursement.

Are items damaged by the customer reimbursed?

No. One of the policy's eligibility conditions is: "The item is not defective and was not damaged by a customer."

Can Amazon take a reimbursement back?

Yes. If a reimbursement was made in error, or if a reimbursed item is later found and returned to your inventory, we reserve the right to reverse the reimbursement credit that was applied to your account.
