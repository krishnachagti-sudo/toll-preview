Claim calendar, November 2026 to December 2027

On Amazon's US window (days 60 to 120 after the refund), refunds made from December 1 to 31, 2026 can first be claimed from January 30, 2027 to March 1, 2027 and must be claimed by March 31, 2027 to April 30, 2027; the table gives all 14 months from November 2026 to December 2027.
