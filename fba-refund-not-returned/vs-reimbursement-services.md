## What each option says it does

- Getida: performance-based pricing starting at 20%; No monthly subscription fees; a web offer: "Pay nothing for the first $400 we recover for you in FBA reimbursements". Its pricing page does not name customer-return refunds.
- Threecolts Margin Pro: 100% performance-based: only pay if we successfully recover money for you; no fee percentage published on the page; for sellers doing $1M+ a year through Amazon FBA or Walmart Fulfillment Services.
- sellerboard: the Reimbursement Gap report lists each case where the reimbursement is lower than your COGS, with the shortfall, included on all plans; plans from Standard $15/mo ($179 billed annually), 3,000 orders a month.
- AMZ Prep: 100% Free Forever; No Signup Required; estimated recovery value per SKU, exportable to CSV.

## When a service is better

- You want someone else to file and chase every case, for every claim type, and accept a share of what comes back.
- You have no time to check the reports at all.
- You sell at a volume where lost and damaged inventory claims dwarf returns.

## When this is enough

- Customer-return refunds are the job: which orders, which window, what to write.
- You want to keep 100% of what Amazon pays, for a known price.
- You do not want to give a third party access to your account: the free check runs in your browser.
