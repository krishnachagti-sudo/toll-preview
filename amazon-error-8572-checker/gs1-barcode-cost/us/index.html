GS1 US barcode cost: single GTINs or a prefix

For 12 products over 1 year, 12 single GTINs cost $360; the cheapest GS1 US option is 10-item prefix + 2 single GTINs at $310 up front + $50 a year after the first = $310. Over 5 years the same 12 products are cheapest as 12 single GTINs ($360), and 120 products over 3 years as 100-item prefix + 20 single GTINs ($1,650).

Questions

How much does a GS1 US barcode cost?

A single GS1 US GTIN is $30 with no annual renewal. A GS1 Company Prefix for 10 items is $250 plus $50 a year.

How much is a GS1 prefix for 100 or 1,000 products?

$750 and $150 a year for 100; $2,500 and $500 a year for 1,000.

Are GS1 US fees refundable?

No. GS1 US: "Payments are non-refundable."
