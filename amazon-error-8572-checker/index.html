Letter of affiliation

Built to the five things Amazon's Error 8572 help checks in a letter: letterhead, the brand and the GTIN owner as on GS1, the affiliation, the issuer's name and contact, and a signature or seal. Every GTIN in it must have a valid check digit.

GS1 US: single GTINs or a prefix?

Free. GS1 US prices read November 8, 2026: a single GTIN $30 with no renewal; prefixes for 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000 items at $250, $750, $2,500, $6,500 and $10,500, renewed yearly at $50, $150, $500, $1,300 and $2,100.

What it checks

  • Length and spreadsheet damage: 12, 13 or 14 digits, with lost leading zeros and 6.14141E+11 caught.
  • Check digit by GS1's method, and a GS1 Prefix issued for products.
  • Your company prefix: the code starts with the prefix on your certificate.
  • Brand against the GS1 licensee: identical, or exactly what differs. Duplicates in one file.

What it does not cover

It does not look up GS1 records, and other countries' GS1 ranges are not named. Amazon does not publish how it matches names, so a pass here is not Amazon's approval.

Read the full rules
  • Length and spreadsheet damage: Amazon's help lists 12 digits for a UPC, 13 for EAN and JAN, 14 for GTIN-14. Scientific notation (6.14141E+11) and an 11-digit UPC that lost its leading zero are caught, with the zero put back when that makes a valid code.
  • Check digit: GS1's method: from the right, weights 3 and 1, then up to the next multiple of ten.
  • GS1 Prefix: the first three digits of the 13-digit form (a 12-digit UPC has an implied leading zero). Prefixes GS1 keeps for restricted circulation, coupons, refund receipts, ISSN, demonstrations and unused ranges fail; 978 and 979 are ISBNs. GS1 US's ranges are named; other countries' are not looked up.
  • Your company prefix: the code must start with the prefix on your certificate (the U.P.C. Company Prefix is the GS1 one without its leading zero), and its length gives how many items it can number.
  • Brand against the GS1 licensee: identical, or what differs: capitals or spacing, punctuation, the legal form (LLC, Inc), extra words, or a different name. The brand value is also checked for extra spaces, trademark symbols and underscores.
  • Duplicates: the same GTIN on two rows of one file.

This checker does not look up GS1 records. It compares the licensee name and company prefix you give it (from your GS1 certificate or your own Verified by GS1 search) with the brand you list. Amazon decides which listings it accepts and does not publish how it matches names; a pass here is not Amazon's approval. Not legal advice.
