# An agency guide to 8572 and a checker

MyAmazonGuy's article (by Francisco Valadez, dated November 20, 2024 and last updated August 25, 2025 on the page) explains 8572 as an identifier conflict and ends by offering its agency's help; Amazon's own Error 8572 help adds the parts a seller acts on: wait 72 hours after buying new GS1 codes, the documents to attach, and the five checks a letter of affiliation must pass.

## What the article covers

- Causes: a UPC that conflicts with an ASIN, another seller using your UPC, codes not registered with GS1, typing mistakes.
- Fixes: confirm the UPC is GS1-registered, gather the GS1 certificate and product photos, search Amazon for conflicting listings, open a case.
- Prevention: buy UPCs from GS1, keep records, check listings, enter codes carefully.
- A closing offer to contact the agency for help.

## What Amazon's own Error 8572 help adds

- The error text and cause: "This error occurs when the provided product ID (ASIN, UPC, EAN, ISBN, or JAN) does not match with the brand or product. The barcode you are using doesn’t belong to the brand."
- A 72-hour wait after buying newly issued GS1 codes.
- Separate documents for brand owners, non-owners and third-party licensing.
- The letter checks: issued by the brand or parent brand on their letterhead (preferably with logo), clearly calls out the brand and the GTIN owner as on GS1, clearly mentions the affiliation, includes the issuer's name and contact information and signed by the brand representative, or carries the brand seal.
- "We do not accept that the barcode which is from a third party."

## When an agency is better
