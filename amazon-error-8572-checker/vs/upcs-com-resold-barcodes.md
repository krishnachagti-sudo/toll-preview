# UPCs.com codes and Amazon

UPCs.com sells codes from $5 each (10 for $25) with no renewal, and its own Error 8572 page says the GS1 database "still lists the original owner" of its codes, not your brand; Amazon's Error 8572 help says "We do not accept that the barcode which is from a third party.", while 10 GS1 US codes cost $250 in the first year (10-item prefix).

## What UPCs.com says

- Home page: one code is $5.00; 250 cost $175.00 ($0.70 each); bulk prices fall to $0.03 a code; no renewal fees; "we guarantee our codes work for your needs".
- Its 8572 page (published December 12, 2025, modified November 2, 2026 per its page data): 10 codes $25, 100 codes $90, 1,000 codes $350; its codes are UCC codes issued before 2002, and the GS1 database still lists the original owner.
- Its fix: a "Proof of Affiliation" letter and its Certificate of Ownership, sent to Seller Support.

## What Amazon and GS1 US say

- Amazon, Error 8572: "We do not accept that the barcode which is from a third party."
- Amazon, Valid UPCs: "We verify the authenticity of product UPCs by checking the GS1 database. UPCs that do not match the information provided by GS1 will be considered invalid."
- Amazon, Valid UPCs: obtain your UPCs directly from GS1, not from other third parties selling UPC licenses.
- GS1 US: "a GS1 Company Prefix, GS1 US GTIN or GLN is for the sole use of the member to which it is licensed."

## Check before you buy

The GS1 record for a resold code names its licensee. Compare that name with your brand: for a made-up code 614142000004 whose record shows "Example Code Seller Inc" and a brand "Harbor & Pine", the checker says: The brand "Harbor & Pine" and the GS1 licensee "Example Code Seller Inc" are different names.

## When a reseller is the better choice

- A channel that does not check the GS1 record and only needs a unique number.
- You accept the risk of an 8572 case and a letter, for a much lower price per code.
- Codes for internal use, where no marketplace looks them up.
