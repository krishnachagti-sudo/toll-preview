Letter of affiliation for Amazon

A letter of affiliation for Error 8572 is a letter from the brand (or its parent) on letterhead that names the brand and the GTIN owner exactly as on GS1, states how they are related, gives the issuer's name and contact and is signed or sealed; Amazon's help lists these five checks, accepts PDF documents, Scanned images of PDF and Word, and asks a seller who is not the brand owner for one.

Who needs which document

You areAmazon asks for
The brand ownerProof that the legal entity on the GS1 certificate is related to your brand (such as a copyright, trademark or website which displays your products or brand and references the legal entity).
Not the brand ownerA letter or legal agreement from the manufacturer or brand confirming that the GTIN you are using is valid and owned by them, or proof that you are authorized to use your own GTIN to sell unique products under their brand.
Licensed through a licensing companyTwo documents for the chain of affiliation: a letter of affiliation or licensing agreement between the brand and the licensing company, and one between you and the licensing company.

Send it with the documents every 8572 case needs:

  • A screenshot of the error with the product ID, brand name, manufacturer and timestamp (Add a Product), or the batch ID (inventory file upload)
  • Product name, manufacturer, brand name and the UPC, EAN, JAN or ISBN
  • A real-world image of the product that clearly shows the barcode and the brand permanently affixed to it
  • The certificate issued by GS1 showing your legal entity and the associated GTIN prefix, or a screenshot of the GS1 website

Questions

Who signs a letter of affiliation?

Amazon's help: it should be issued by the brand or parent brand on its letterhead, with the issuer's name and contact information, and signed by the brand representative or carry the brand seal.

What file formats does Amazon accept?

PDF documents, Scanned images of PDF, Word (self declarations are only acceptable if you are the brand owner) and Screenshots of email from the domain of the company holding rights to the protected brand.

What if I licensed the brand through a licensing company?

Two documents for the chain of affiliation: a letter of affiliation or licensing agreement between the brand and the licensing company, and one between you and the licensing company.
