# Letter of affiliation for Amazon

A letter of affiliation for Error 8572 is a letter from the brand (or its parent) on letterhead that names the brand and the GTIN owner exactly as on GS1, states how they are related, gives the issuer's name and contact and is signed or sealed; Amazon's help lists these five checks, accepts PDF documents, Scanned images of PDF and Word, and asks a seller who is not the brand owner for one.

## Amazon's checks

Amazon's Error 8572 help, read November 8, 2026.

| Component | In the template |
| --- | --- |
| Issued by the brand or parent brand on their letterhead (preferably with logo) | Print it on the letterhead of the brand "Harbor & Pine" or its parent company (Amazon's help: issued by the brand or parent brand); the first line marks where. |
| Clearly calls out the brand and the GTIN owner as on GS1 | Names "Harbor & Pine" (brand) and "Harbor & Pine LLC" (GTIN owner on GS1); make the licensee name the same as on the GS1 certificate. |
| Clearly mentions the affiliation | States the relationship: ""Harbor & Pine" is the registered trade name of Harbor & Pine LLC, which designs and sells these products." |
| Includes the issuer's name and contact information | Names Dana Reyes with dana@harborpine.example and +1 503 555 0142. |
| Signed by the brand representative, or carries the brand seal | Leaves the place for a signature or the brand seal: sign it, or stamp it. |

## The template, filled in

```
[Letterhead of the brand "Harbor & Pine" or its parent company, with logo]

November 9, 2026

To: Amazon Selling Partner Support
Re: Affiliation between the brand "Harbor & Pine" and the GS1 licensee "Harbor & Pine LLC" (Error 8572)

Harbor & Pine LLC is the company to which GS1 has licensed the GTINs listed below (GS1 Company Prefix 0614141), as shown on our GS1 certificate.
"Harbor & Pine" is the brand of these products. "Harbor & Pine" is the registered trade name of Harbor & Pine LLC, which designs and sells these products.

We confirm that the following 3 GTINs are valid, licensed to Harbor & Pine LLC and assigned to products of the brand "Harbor & Pine":
  614141000012
  614141000029
  614141000036

Signed,

[signature or brand seal]
Dana Reyes, Owner
Harbor & Pine LLC
dana@harborpine.example | +1 503 555 0142
```

## Who needs which document

| You are | Amazon asks for |
| --- | --- |
| The brand owner | Proof that the legal entity on the GS1 certificate is related to your brand (such as a copyright, trademark or website which displays your products or brand and references the legal entity). |
| Not the brand owner | A letter or legal agreement from the manufacturer or brand confirming that the GTIN you are using is valid and owned by them, or proof that you are authorized to use your own GTIN to sell unique products under their brand. |
| Licensed through a licensing company | Two documents for the chain of affiliation: a letter of affiliation or licensing agreement between the brand and the licensing company, and one between you and the licensing company. |

Send it with the documents every 8572 case needs:

- A screenshot of the error with the product ID, brand name, manufacturer and timestamp (Add a Product), or the batch ID (inventory file upload)
- Product name, manufacturer, brand name and the UPC, EAN, JAN or ISBN
- A real-world image of the product that clearly shows the barcode and the brand permanently affixed to it
- The certificate issued by GS1 showing your legal entity and the associated GTIN prefix, or a screenshot of the GS1 website

## Questions

### Who signs a letter of affiliation?

Amazon's help: it should be issued by the brand or parent brand on its letterhead, with the issuer's name and contact information, and signed by the brand representative or carry the brand seal.

### What file formats does Amazon accept?

PDF documents, Scanned images of PDF, Word (self declarations are only acceptable if you are the brand owner) and Screenshots of email from the domain of the company holding rights to the protected brand.

### What if I licensed the brand through a licensing company?

Two documents for the chain of affiliation: a letter of affiliation or licensing agreement between the brand and the licensing company, and one between you and the licensing company.
