# Amazon GTIN exemption

Amazon grants a GTIN exemption for products with no barcode (private label, handmade, generic, bundles and parts), per store and category: automatic for a brand owner or a generic product, within 48 hours when brand name approval is needed, and never for a product that already has a GS1 barcode or a brand that requires a UPC; after approval, wait 30 minutes before listing.

## Who can apply

- Private-label or handmade products
- Products you manufacture, brand, or publish without barcodes
- Parts without a product ID (for example, automotive parts, mobile accessories)
- Bundled products
- Generic, unbranded products

## Who cannot

- Has an existing GS1-approved barcode
- Has an existing GTIN/EAN/UPC/ISBN
- Is from a brand requiring mandatory UPC
- A book listed with the publisher as "generic"
- Is already available on Amazon

## The four cases and their timelines

Amazon's help, read November 8, 2026.

| Case | What happens | Timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand owner without a product ID, or a generic product | Auto-approval for the GTIN exemption | Automatic; wait 30 minutes before listing |
| Brand on Amazon, not UPC-mandatory | Brand name approval (BNA) and the exemption together | Within 48 hours |
| Brand on Amazon, seller not affiliated | Approval to create new ASINs for the brand, and the exemption | Within 48 hours |
| UPC-mandatory brand (GS1 enforced) | No exemption: get the GTIN from the brand owner or buy from GS1 | Automatic decline |

## Brand name formatting in the application

Correct, per Amazon: standard capitalization, all caps if it matches the product, hyphen, period and first letter lowercase (for example, iPhone). Incorrect: extra spaces inside the name, trademark symbols and underscores, or capitals different from the product. The checker flags the first three in your brand value.

## If your brand is refused but should qualify

Amazon lists cases for an exception: a homonym of a mandatory brand, a letter of authorization from the brand or manufacturer, GTINs not available in the country, GTINs too expensive for the product, or replacement parts without UPCs. The request goes through Get help and resources, Add a product, Product identifier exemption.

## Steps

1. **Check you qualify.** Your product is not yet listed on Amazon, has no product ID or GS1 barcode, and you have category approval if the category needs it.
2. **Start a listing without a product ID.** In Add Products, create a new listing, fill in the details and brand, check "I don't have a product ID" and click Next.
3. **Complete the application if asked.** A brand owner or generic product is approved automatically; a brand on Amazon needs brand name approval and is reviewed within 48 hours; a UPC-mandatory brand is declined.
4. **Wait 30 minutes, then list.** After approval, wait 30 minutes and enter the category and brand exactly as in the approval notice.

## Questions

### Can I get a GTIN exemption if my product has a GS1 barcode?

No. Amazon's help lists "Has an existing GS1-approved barcode" and "Has an existing GTIN/EAN/UPC/ISBN" among the products that cannot apply.

### Does one exemption cover all my products?

Per store and category. Amazon: "You must request a GTIN exemption approval separately for each store and category where you want to list without product IDs." Within one category, one exemption covers future products too.

### Can an exemption stop working?

Yes. Amazon: "Amazon may expire previous GTIN exemptions toward brands that initially didn’t have GTINs, but have since acquired GS1 barcodes."
