# GS1, a reseller, or an exemption?

For a new brand selling on Amazon, GS1 US codes are the route Amazon's help names (it checks UPCs against GS1 and does not accept barcodes from a third party): over 3 years, 1 product cost $30 (1 single GTIN), 5 products cost $150 (5 single GTINs), 10 products cost $300 (10 single GTINs), 25 products cost $750 (25 single GTINs) and 100 products cost $1,050 (100-item prefix); a GTIN exemption costs nothing but only for products without any barcode.

## The three routes

Amazon's help and GS1 US, read November 8, 2026; UPCs.com, read November 8, 2026.

|  | GS1 US | A reseller | GTIN exemption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GS1 record names | Your company | The original licensee (UPCs.com says so of its own codes) | No code |
| Amazon's help | Get UPCs directly from GS1 | "We do not accept that the barcode which is from a third party." | Allowed for products with no barcode, per store and category |
| Cost | $30 per single GTIN; prefixes from $250 + $50 a year | From $5 a code at UPCs.com | None |
| Other stores | Yes, GS1 US says across channels | Depends on each retailer | Amazon only |

## Brand Registry

Amazon's product ID help lists registering the brand in Amazon Brand Registry as an option when a product has no product ID; its Brand Name policy says enrollment needs a registered or pending trademark.
