# Fix Error 8572 in seven checks

Work through seven checks in order: the length (12, 13 or 14 digits), the GS1 check digit, the GS1 Prefix, your company prefix, the 72-hour wait for new GS1 codes, the brand against the GS1 licensee name, and only then one Seller Support case with every document Amazon's Error 8572 help lists for your situation; the checker does the first six for a whole file.

## Steps

1. **1. Length.** A UPC has 12 digits, an EAN or JAN 13, a GTIN-14 14. An 11-digit UPC usually lost its leading zero in a spreadsheet.
2. **2. Check digit.** Work the GS1 check digit; a wrong one means a typo.
3. **3. GS1 Prefix.** Codes from restricted-circulation, coupon or demonstration prefixes are not product GTINs.
4. **4. Your company prefix.** Your own codes start with the GS1 Company Prefix on your certificate.
5. **5. The 72-hour wait.** New codes from GS1: wait at least 72 hours before listing.
6. **6. Brand against the GS1 name.** Compare the brand you type with the company GS1 has; note exactly how they differ.
7. **7. The documents.** Open one case with everything the Error 8572 help lists for your situation, and a letter of affiliation if the names differ.
