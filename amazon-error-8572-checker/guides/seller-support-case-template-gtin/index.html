A Seller Support case for Error 8572

A Seller Support case for Error 8572 should arrive complete: Amazon's 8572 help asks for the error screenshot (or the batch ID for an upload), product name, manufacturer, brand and code, a real-world photo showing the barcode and brand, the GS1 certificate (or a screenshot of the GS1 website), and proof for your situation; the template below lists them in one message.

  • Made by Conyso
  • Exact, no AI
  • Official sources, dated
  • Files you upload are not kept
  • SecurityTerms

For a brand owner (Add a Product)

Subject: Error 8572 on 2 GTINs for the brand "Harbor & Pine"

I receive Error 8572 when listing these products with the Add a Product tool. The GTINs are licensed to Harbor & Pine LLC by GS1, and "Harbor & Pine" is our brand.

Attached, as the Error 8572 help page asks:
1. A screenshot of the error with the product ID, brand name, manufacturer and timestamp
2. Product name, manufacturer, brand name and the UPC, EAN, JAN or ISBN
3. A real-world image of the product that clearly shows the barcode and the brand permanently affixed to it
4. The certificate issued by GS1 showing your legal entity and the associated GTIN prefix, or a screenshot of the GS1 website
5. Proof that the legal entity on the GS1 certificate is related to your brand (such as a copyright, trademark or website which displays your products or brand and references the legal entity).

GTINs: 614141000012, 614141000029
Brand: Harbor & Pine
GS1 licensee: Harbor & Pine LLC

Question: please validate these GTINs for the brand "Harbor & Pine" so the listing can be created.

For a seller who is not the brand owner (inventory file upload)

Subject: Error 8572 on GTIN 614141000012 for the brand "Harbor & Pine"

I receive Error 8572 when listing this product by inventory file upload. The GTIN is licensed to Harbor & Pine LLC by GS1, and I list them for the brand "Harbor & Pine".

Attached, as the Error 8572 help page asks:
1. The batch ID of the upload where the error appeared (below)
2. Product name, manufacturer, brand name and the UPC, EAN, JAN or ISBN
3. A real-world image of the product that clearly shows the barcode and the brand permanently affixed to it
4. The certificate issued by GS1 showing your legal entity and the associated GTIN prefix, or a screenshot of the GS1 website
5. A letter or legal agreement from the manufacturer or brand confirming that the GTIN you are using is valid and owned by them, or proof that you are authorized to use your own GTIN to sell unique products under their brand.

GTIN: 614141000012
Brand: Harbor & Pine
GS1 licensee: Harbor & Pine LLC
Batch ID: [batch ID]

Question: please validate this GTIN for the brand "Harbor & Pine" so the listing can be created.

What Amazon does not accept

"We do not accept that the barcode which is from a third party." A letter must pass the five checks on the letter of affiliation page.

Sources

Every company name and code in the examples is made up; a code may happen to belong to a real company, and this page does not say who.

Steps

  1. Check the codes first. Fix typos and spreadsheet damage before opening a case; the case is for codes that are right.
  2. Collect the attachments. Error screenshot (or batch ID), a real-world photo showing the barcode and brand, the GS1 certificate or a GS1 website screenshot, and the proof for your situation.
  3. Write one message. Use the template: the codes, the brand, the GS1 licensee, what is attached, and one clear question.
  4. Keep the case number. Reply in the same case with anything Amazon asks for.
