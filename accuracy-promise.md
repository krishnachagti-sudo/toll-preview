# Our accuracy promise

3 cases are covered. A call that fails while running is refunded to your balance in the same moment. A call that is refused is never charged. If an answer is wrong because of an error on our side, you write to us, and we check it, fix the tool and refund the charge.

## What happens when a call fails?

These come from the [refund policy](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/refunds/) and the [terms](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/terms/). We repeat them here in plain words and add nothing to them.

- A call that fails while running is refunded to your balance in the same moment. You do not need to ask.
- A call that is refused (invalid input, a limit reached, not enough balance) is never charged.
- A charge held for a job that is not confirmed is refunded when the hold ends, as the tool's page explains.

## What happens when an answer is wrong?

If a tool gave a wrong answer because of an error on our side, write to us with the details. We will check it, fix the tool, and refund the charge.

For a downloaded file such as a worksheet or a filing file that was wrong because of our mistake, tell us and we will put it right or refund it.

## What is not covered?

- Calls that ran and gave their answer. Every tool shows its price before you use it, and can quote it without running.
- Anything beyond what the answers are. They are information to help you with a task, not tax, legal, accounting or financial advice. Check anything important before you rely on it, especially before you file, pay or sign.
- Perfect availability. The terms say we aim to keep the service running, but we do not promise it will always be available or free of errors.

The [terms](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/terms/) also set out liability, and nothing on this page changes them.

## How do I report a wrong answer?

Use the [contact page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/contact/). Give the tool, the exact inputs you used and the answer you got, and say what you expected and why. The same inputs give the same answer, so we can repeat your call.

When we fix a mistake in the data or the rules, the change is logged on the [data updates page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/updates/) with its official source.

## How are answers checked?

Each tool works out its answer from the rules and published data it names, and gives the same answer to the same input. The [sources page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/sources/) lists the official documents behind each tool and what its tests compare the answers with. The [methodology page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/methodology/) explains how the answers are worked out.
