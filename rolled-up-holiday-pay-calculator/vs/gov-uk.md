# GOV.UK holiday calculator vs this calculator

GOV.UK's holiday calculator works out statutory leave in days or hours for one worker; this calculator gives the pound amount too: 30 hours paid £391.68 is 4 hours of holiday and £47.28 of rolled-up holiday pay, for one worker or a whole payroll CSV.

- Hours of holiday: 4
- Holiday pay line: £47.28

## When GOV.UK's calculator is better

When you need the official figure for one worker's leave entitlement, for a full or part leave year, in days, hours or shifts, including regular-hours staff this calculator does not cover.

A payroll CSV costs $0.02 a worker row (at least $0.25 a file), and a re-run of the same rows within 30 days is free; one worker at a time is free on the page.

> Worked out under GOV.UK's guidance for Great Britain; Northern Ireland has its own rules and is not covered. Whether a worker is an irregular-hours or part-year worker depends on the contract. Not legal advice.

## Questions

### Does the GOV.UK calculator work out rolled-up holiday pay?

It is described as a "Holiday calculator to work out statutory holiday leave in days or hours": entitlement in days or hours, not a pound amount.
