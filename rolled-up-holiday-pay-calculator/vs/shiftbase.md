# Shiftbase and rolled-up holiday pay

Shiftbase is rota and time-tracking software (€30 a month for 6 employees on Basic, then €4 each) that exports payroll-ready hours, and its pricing page lists no payslip; this calculator turns a pay run into the 12.07% line, £151.51 for the 4 eligible rows of the example.

- Shiftbase Basic: €30 a month
- Example holiday pay: £151.51

## Shiftbase's plans

From https://www.shiftbase.com/pricing, read 8 November 2026, in euros.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | €0 a month | up to 15 employees, 1 department, 5 teams |
| Basic | €30 a month | 6 employees, then €4 a month each (€3.60 billed yearly) |
| Premium | €75 a month | 12 employees, then €5.25 a month each (€4.73 billed yearly) |

Its payroll export: "Contract versus worked hours, surcharges, and leave payouts, as Excel or CSV". Its absence management: "Requests, approvals, and balances". It names no payslip output.

## What each does

Side by side.

|  | Shiftbase | This calculator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job | Rotas, clocking in, absence, payroll export | The 12.07% holiday pay line and hours from a pay run |
| Payslip | None listed on its pricing page | A payslip\_line column to copy into payroll |
| 40 casual staff paid weekly | €30 + 34 x €4 = €166 a month (Basic) | about 173 rows a month = $3.46 |

## When Shiftbase is better

When you want rotas, time tracking and leave balances in one product: it records the hours this calculator needs.

A payroll CSV costs $0.02 a worker row (at least $0.25 a file), and a re-run of the same rows within 30 days is free; one worker at a time is free on the page.

> Worked out under GOV.UK's guidance for Great Britain; Northern Ireland has its own rules and is not covered. Whether a worker is an irregular-hours or part-year worker depends on the contract. Not legal advice.
