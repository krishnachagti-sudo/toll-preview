# Acas guidance vs this calculator

Acas explains rolled-up holiday pay in plain words and gives worked examples (£1,000 earned in June gives £120.70, £500 in July gives £60.35); this calculator does the same sum for your figures, £60.35 on £500, and for a whole payroll at once.

- June: £1,000: £120.70
- July: £500: £60.35

## When Acas is better

When you need plain official guidance, or advice on a particular worker or a dispute: call the Acas helpline on 0300 123 11 00.

A payroll CSV costs $0.02 a worker row (at least $0.25 a file), and a re-run of the same rows within 30 days is free; one worker at a time is free on the page.

> Worked out under GOV.UK's guidance for Great Britain; Northern Ireland has its own rules and is not covered. Whether a worker is an irregular-hours or part-year worker depends on the contract. Not legal advice.
