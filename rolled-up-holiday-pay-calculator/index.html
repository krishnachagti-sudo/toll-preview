The whole payroll at once

Paste or choose your pay run as CSV. Columns are found by name: worker, period_end (or set it below), pay (the total) or parts such as basic and overtime, and optionally hours, worker_type (zero-hours, casual, agency, term-time, part-year, regular), leave_year_start and entitlement_weeks or entitlement_days. You get the same file back with the rate, the holiday pay, the hours accrued, the payslip line and a status on every row.

A week's holiday pay from the last 52 paid weeks

For employers who pay holiday when it is taken. Weekly gross pay, most recent week first, one per line or separated by commas. Write 0 for a week with no pay, and add sick or statutory after a week with any sick or statutory leave: both are skipped and earlier weeks counted, up to 104 weeks back.

Holiday accrued while off sick or on statutory leave

GOV.UK's method: hours worked in the 52-week relevant period ÷ 46.4 (its recommended divisor, or your own), x 12.07%, x the weeks of leave, then to the nearest hour.

Read the notes and sources
  • Worked out under GOV.UK's guidance for Great Britain. Northern Ireland has its own rules and is not covered.
  • Whether a worker is an irregular-hours or part-year worker depends on the contract: the calculator applies your answer. Pay figures are yours. Not legal advice.
  • Sources, read 8 November 2026: GOV.UK, Holiday pay and entitlement reforms from 1 January 2024 (updated 1 April 2024), GOV.UK, Holiday entitlement (updated 5 November 2026) and SIA approved contractors and rolled-up holiday pay. The statute is the Working Time Regulations 1998, regulation 16B.

Who and when

Irregular-hours and part-year workers only, for leave years from 1 April 2024.

Read the full rules

Rolled-up holiday pay is allowed only for irregular-hours workers (paid hours in each pay period wholly or mostly variable under the contract) and part-year workers (periods of at least a week with no work required and no pay), for leave years beginning on or after 1 April 2024. Not for regular-hours workers, full or part time.

How much

12.07% of total pay in the period, as a separate payslip item.

Read the full rules

12.07% of total pay in the pay period: 5.6 weeks of leave over 46.4 working weeks. For a longer contract, weeks ÷ (52 - weeks): 6 weeks is 13.04%. Paid with the work, on top of pay at or above the minimum wage, as a separate item on each payslip.

Hours and rounding

Hours accrue at the same rate, to the nearest hour; both penny roundings are shown.

Read the full rules

Holiday accrues at the same rate on hours worked, on the last day of each pay period, rounded down under 30 minutes and up at 30 minutes or more. The pound amount is 12.07% of the whole pay to the nearest penny; GOV.UK's worked tables round pay ÷ 100 first, which can differ by a few pence, so both are shown.
