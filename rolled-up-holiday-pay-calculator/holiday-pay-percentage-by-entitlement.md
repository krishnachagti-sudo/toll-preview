# Holiday pay percentage by entitlement

The accrual rate is weeks of holiday ÷ (52 - weeks): 28 days on a 5-day week (5.6 weeks) is 12.07%, 30 days (6 weeks) is 13.04% and 33 days (6.6 weeks) is 14.54%.

- 28 days: 12.07%
- 30 days: 13.04%
- 33 days: 14.54%

## The formula

GOV.UK: "To find the relevant percentage for these workers, you would need to do the following calculation: (total holiday entitlement ÷ remaining working weeks in the year) x 100." Its example: 6 ÷ 46 = 0.1304, so 13.04%. Days become weeks by dividing by the days worked a week. Each rate is rounded to two decimal places, as GOV.UK shows them.

```text
rate = weeks ÷ (52 - weeks) x 100
```

## Questions

### What percentage is holiday pay if they get 33 days?

On a 5-day week 33 days is 6.6 weeks: 6.6 ÷ 45.4 = 14.54%.

### What percentage is 30 days of holiday?

13.04%: 30 days on a 5-day week is 6 weeks, and 6 ÷ 46 = 0.1304 (GOV.UK's own example).

### Why is 35 days not 8 weeks?

On a 5-day week 35 days is 7 weeks, so the rate is 7 ÷ 45 = 15.56%.
