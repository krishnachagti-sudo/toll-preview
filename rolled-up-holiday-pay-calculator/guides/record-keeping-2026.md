# Holiday record keeping from 6 April 2026

From 6 April 2026, employers must keep records of annual leave and holiday pay for at least 6 years from the date they were made, so a holiday pay line on a payslip dated 6 November 2026 is kept until at least 6 November 2032; the payroll CSV below records 5 rows with the rate, the amount and the hours.

- Kept for: 6 years
- Rows in the example: 5

## The duty

- "From 6 April 2026, employers must keep detailed records of annual leave and holiday pay for a minimum of 6 years from the date they were made."
- "Employers can choose how to create, maintain and keep records."
- "Employers may have to pay a fine if they cannot show that they keep holiday records."

GOV.UK gives examples: the number of annual leave days taken and how much holiday pay is paid.

## Questions

### How long must holiday pay records be kept?

At least 6 years from the date they were made, from 6 April 2026 (GOV.UK).

### Is there a set format?

No. GOV.UK: "Employers can choose how to create, maintain and keep records."
