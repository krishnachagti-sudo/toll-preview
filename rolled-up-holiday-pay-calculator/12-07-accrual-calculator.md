# 12.07% holiday accrual calculator

68 hours worked accrues 8 hours of holiday (68 x 12.07% = 8.2076, rounded to the nearest hour at 30 minutes); 30 hours accrues 4 (3.621) and 70 hours accrues 8 (8.449).

- 30 hours worked: 4 hours

## The rule

GOV.UK: "Holiday entitlement for these workers will be calculated as 12.07% of actual hours worked in a pay period." The three steps are hours ÷ 100, x 12.07, then round. On rounding: "the hours can be rounded down (to zero if it is less than 30 minutes) but will be rounded up to one hour if it is 30 minutes or more than 30 minutes."

## Questions

### How much holiday does an irregular-hours worker accrue?

12.07% of the hours worked in each pay period, for leave years beginning on or after 1 April 2024 (GOV.UK). With a longer contract, use the enhanced percentage.

### How are the accrued hours rounded?

To the nearest hour: under 30 minutes rounds down, 30 minutes or more rounds up. GOV.UK's example: 68 hours gives 8.2076, so 8 hours.

### What about a worker on 4 hours a week or less?

GOV.UK says it may be appropriate to round up to the next half hour or hour so the worker accrues holiday. The last column shows that rounding.
