# QuickBooks mileage or Endwise

Use QuickBooks mileage tracking when your books are in QuickBooks Online and you want trips recorded by its mobile app. Use Endwise to price each trip at the IRS rate for its date: 72.5 cents through June 30, 2026 and 76 cents from July 1, free on the page and $0.002 a calculation over the API.

## How do they differ?

|  | Endwise | QuickBooks Online mileage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A calculator and log builder for mileage at the IRS rate. | A feature of the QuickBooks accounting subscription. |
| Records your drives | No. You supply the trips. | Yes, with auto-tracking in the QuickBooks mobile app. |
| Rate applied | The IRS rate for each trip date, with the IRS source on each line. | "QuickBooks uses the IRS mileage rate to estimate the potential deduction for the year" (help page). |
| Export | PDF log and CSV results. | Download Trips gives a CSV file (help page). |
| Plans | Pay per use; no subscription. Log $4 per vehicle-year. | Its help page says the feature is limited for QuickBooks Online Free and Lite. |

On November 6, 2026, the QuickBooks mileage page gave the 2026 rate as 72.5 cents per business mile. That is the IRS rate for January 1 to June 30, 2026; from July 1 the IRS rate is 76 cents. If you estimate from a single rate, check which one is set.

## When is QuickBooks the better choice?

- Your accounting is already in QuickBooks Online and you want trips next to your books.
- You want drives recorded on your phone and sorted with a swipe.
- You want one subscription for invoicing, expenses and mileage.

## When is Endwise the better choice?

- You need the exact amount by trip date. The six example trips on the split guide come to $676.23 priced by date.
- You reimburse employees and need per-employee totals, the July 2026 true-up and the W-2 split.
- You need a vehicle-year log with a completeness check, once a year, with no subscription.

## Questions

### Does QuickBooks track mileage automatically?

Yes. QuickBooks Online's help page says auto-tracking runs in the QuickBooks mobile app and needs the right device permissions; you then mark each trip business or personal.

### Which rate does QuickBooks use for mileage?

Its help page says "QuickBooks uses the IRS mileage rate to estimate the potential deduction for the year." It does not say how it handles the July 1, 2026 change.

### Can I use QuickBooks trips with Endwise?

Yes. QuickBooks Online can download trips as a CSV file; open that file in the Endwise mileage calculator to price each trip at the rate for its date.
