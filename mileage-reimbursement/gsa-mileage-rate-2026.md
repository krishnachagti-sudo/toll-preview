# GSA mileage rate 2026

For federal official travel on or after July 1, 2026 the GSA rate is $0.76 a mile by car when no government car was available, $0.235 if one was, $0.74 by motorcycle and $1.935 by airplane; travel that began January 1 to June 30 uses $0.725, $0.205, $0.705 and $1.78.

- Car, from July 1: $0.76
- Car, January 1 to June 30: $0.725
- Government car available: $0.235
- Airplane, from July 1: $1.935

## 2026 rates by vehicle

Dollars per mile, by the date travel began. Source: GSA.

| Vehicle | From January 1, 2026 | From July 1, 2026 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Automobile (no government car authorized or available) | $0.725 | $0.76 |
| Automobile, when a government car was authorized and available | $0.205 | $0.235 |
| Motorcycle | $0.705 | $0.74 |
| Airplane (per statute mile) | $1.78 | $1.935 |
| Automobile on a work-related move | Not listed | $0.235 |

Airplane miles are statute miles: GSA converts nautical miles at 1 NM = 1.15077945 statute miles, so 100 nautical miles is 115.08 statute miles, $222.68 at $1.935.

These are the rates agencies pay federal employees under the Federal Travel Regulation (41 CFR 301, Subpart D). Private employers and the self-employed use the IRS rates, which match the automobile rate in 2026.

## Questions

### What is the GSA mileage rate for 2026?

For official travel on or after July 1, 2026: $0.76 per mile by car when no government vehicle is available, $0.235 if one was available, $0.74 by motorcycle and $1.935 by airplane. From January 1, 2026: $0.725, $0.205, $0.705 and $1.78.

### Which GSA rate applies to a trip that began in June 2026?

GSA says to use the rate that applied when the travel began, so travel that began in June 2026 uses $0.725 a mile by car.
