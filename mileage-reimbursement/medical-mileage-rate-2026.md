# Medical mileage rate 2026

The 2026 IRS medical mileage rate is 20.5 cents a mile through June 30 and 23.5 cents from July 1, 2026; so 1,000 medical miles, 600 before July 1, 2026 and 400 from July 1: 600 x $0.205 + 400 x $0.235 = $217.00.

- January 1 to June 30: 20.5 cents
- From July 1: 23.5 cents
- Same rate for: Military moving

Medical mileage is an input to the medical and dental expense deduction on Schedule A; this page does not apply that deduction's limits. Each mile takes the rate of the day it was driven.

## Moving

The same rate covers moving miles, but only for the people Notice 2026-10 names: members of the Armed Forces on active duty who move under a military order for a permanent change of station, and "members of the intelligence community who move after December 31, 2025, pursuant to a change of assignment which requires relocation."

## Questions

### What is the 2026 medical mileage rate?

20.5 cents per mile through June 30, 2026 and 23.5 cents from July 1, 2026.

### Is the moving mileage rate available to everyone?

No. The IRS table lists the rate for military moving only, and Notice 2026-10 extends it to members of the intelligence community who move after December 31, 2025 for a change of assignment that requires relocation.
