# Charity mileage rate 2026

The 2026 IRS charity mileage rate is 14 cents a mile all year, fixed by section 170(i) of the Internal Revenue Code, so 500 miles driven for a charity are worth $70.00. The business rate is 76 cents from July 1, 2026; the charity rate does not follow it.

## Why is the charity rate always 14 cents?

Announcement 2026-11: "The mileage rate that applies to the deduction for charitable contributions is fixed under § 170(i) of the Internal Revenue Code at 14 cents per mile."

Every period in the IRS table since 2011 shows 14 cents for charity, including the mid-year changes in 2011, 2022 and 2026, when the business rate moved and the charity rate did not.

## What counts as charity miles?

Notice 2026-10 describes the rate as the one for "use of an automobile in rendering gratuitous services to a charitable organization under § 170". Driving to medical care takes a different rate: see [medical mileage rate 2026](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/mileage-reimbursement/medical-mileage-rate-2026/).

## Questions

### What is the 2026 charity mileage rate?

14 cents a mile for the whole of 2026, both before and after July 1.

### Why did the charity rate not go up in July 2026?

Announcement 2026-11: "The mileage rate that applies to the deduction for charitable contributions is fixed under § 170(i) of the Internal Revenue Code at 14 cents per mile."

$70.00 (500 x $0.14).
