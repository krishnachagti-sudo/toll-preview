# TruckLogics IFTA alternative: Endwise IFTA calculator compared

Use TruckLogics when you want a state-format, file-ready report, an e-filing worksheet or a Motive import ($24.95 per report for business owners). Use Endwise for a quick estimate with no account, a per-jurisdiction breakdown at the final matrix rates, and a pay-per-return price ($5 for 1 to 5 vehicles); it does not file for you.

## How do Endwise and TruckLogics differ for IFTA?

Both prepare an IFTA quarterly return. Facts about TruckLogics come from its IFTA reporting page, as of November 2026. Anything not listed was not checked.

|  | Endwise | TruckLogics IFTA reporting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it does | Works out an IFTA quarterly return per jurisdiction and fuel at the quarter's final matrix rates, surcharges included, from totals or from trip sheets and receipts. | Calculates the tax due and penalties if any, with a state adjacency check and tax rates updated automatically for each state. |
| Getting your data in | Totals by state, or trip sheets and fuel receipts, with odometer reconciliation across trips. | Manual entry per truck, bulk upload with two Excel templates, or import from a Motive account. |
| Output | PDF and CSV return. | File-ready IFTA reports to download or print where paper filing is allowed, and an e-filing worksheet where e-filing is allowed. |
| Account needed | No for estimates and checks on the page (5 free runs a day). An account for returns over the API. | Not checked. |

| Files the return | No. You file in your base jurisdiction's portal. | Produces file-ready reports and an e-filing worksheet; not checked whether it submits the return. |
| Same answer every time | Yes. Fixed published rates, no AI. | Not checked. |

## When is TruckLogics the better choice?

- You want a report in your state's file-ready format, or an e-filing worksheet where e-filing is allowed.
- Your ELD data is in Motive and you want to import it instead of typing or uploading it.
- You want penalties computed for you as part of the report.
- You manage IFTA for several businesses and want them under one account.

## When is Endwise the better choice?

- You want a quick estimate from your state totals before the due date, with no account.
- You need to see the rate, surcharge and net tax for every jurisdiction, with the source of the rates.
- You want your trip records checked against the IFTA distance and fuel record elements before an audit.
- You want to pay for the filing quarters you use, with no subscription, and a repeat of the same return free for 90 days.
- A program or AI assistant has to compute it the same way every time.

## Questions

### Is there a free alternative to TruckLogics for IFTA?

Endwise lets you estimate an IFTA return from totals by state without an account, within a daily free limit, then charges $0.03 per estimate and $5 per quarterly return over the API or MCP. TruckLogics says it is free to explore and you pay when you generate a report (as of November 2026).

### Does Endwise file my IFTA return?

No. It prepares the return as a PDF and CSV. You file and pay in your base jurisdiction's portal.

### What does TruckLogics charge for an IFTA report?

Its IFTA page lists $24.95 per report for business owners and $19.95 per report for service providers who manage multiple businesses under one account (as of November 2026).

### Which IFTA rates does Endwise use?

The final IFTA tax rate matrix for each quarter, including surcharges, as listed on the tool page with its sources.
