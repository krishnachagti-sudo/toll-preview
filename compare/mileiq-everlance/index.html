MileIQ and Everlance alternative: Endwise compared

Use MileIQ or Everlance when you want your drives recorded automatically on your phone. Use Endwise when you already have the miles and need each trip priced at the right IRS rate, or a vehicle-year log, with no subscription (calculations on the page are free; a log is $4).

How do Endwise, MileIQ and Everlance differ?

MileIQ and Everlance are phone apps that record your drives. Endwise is a calculator and log builder: you give it trips and it returns the amount at the IRS rate for each date. They overlap on mileage reports. Facts about the apps come from their own pricing pages, as of November 2026. Anything not listed was not checked.

When are MileIQ or Everlance the better choice?

  • You want your drives recorded without typing them in. That is what both apps are built for, and Endwise does not do it.
  • You drive every day for work and want a phone app that sorts business from personal drives.
  • You also want expense tracking. Everlance lists expense tracking with receipt uploads on its Starter plan.
  • You want a monthly plan with support rather than paying per use.

When is Endwise the better choice?

  • You already have the miles, from a spreadsheet, a payroll sheet or a notebook, and need the amount at the right IRS rate for each trip date.
  • Your trips cross a rate change, such as the mid-2026 change, and you want each part priced at its own rate with the IRS source shown.
  • You need a log for a vehicle-year with a completeness check, once a year, and do not want a subscription.
  • A program or AI assistant has to calculate it the same way every time.

What does Endwise return for a real case?

4,200 business miles, 2,600 before July 1, 2026 and 1,600 from July 1: 2,600 x $0.725 + 1,600 x $0.76 = $3,101.00.

The two IRS rates and their notices appear on the result, line by line. MileIQ and Everlance do not publish this exact case on the pages we could read, so no side-by-side figure is given.

How do you switch to Endwise?

Export your trips from the app as a spreadsheet, then open the mileage reimbursement tool and paste the trips or upload the file. For the rates by year, see IRS mileage rate history.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to MileIQ or Everlance?

MileIQ and Everlance both have free plans with limits (as of November 2026: MileIQ 40 drives a month, Everlance 30 automatically detected trips a month). Endwise's page calculates reimbursement at the IRS rate for free, but it does not track drives for you.

Does Endwise track my drives automatically?

No. Endwise prices trips you give it at the IRS rate in force on each trip date and builds a log from them. If you want drives recorded automatically on your phone, use an app built for that.

What does an IRS mileage log from Endwise cost?

$4 per vehicle-year log, with a free repeat for the same vehicle and tax year for 30 days. Calculations on the web page are free.

Can I use both?

Yes. Many people record drives in an app, export them, and use Endwise to price a trip sheet at the right IRS rate for each date or to produce a log with a completeness check.
