## How they are worked out

A duplicate pair is two equal amounts no more than N days apart. For a month that closes on day E, a twin of a line dated E can be dated up to E + N, so the check runs from the 1st to N days past the month end. The default window in the tool is 3 days; 7 suits a feed known to post late. Weekends are not skipped: the window counts calendar days.
