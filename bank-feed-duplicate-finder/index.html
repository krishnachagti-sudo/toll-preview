Bookkeeping cleanup

Bank-feed duplicate finder

Find what your bank feed brought in twice, with the copy to keep and the fix in your books.

Settings, and the exports it reads

Bank feed: the lines waiting for review, as a bank CSV (Date, Description, Amount), or Xero's Uncoded statement lines export. Books: in QuickBooks Online, Transaction Detail by Account for the bank account, exported to Excel and saved as CSV; in Xero, Account Transactions for the bank account, saved as CSV.

Example: a made-up bakery's October register and the bank lines waiting in For review

Found 6 likely duplicates in 7 bank-feed lines and 16 books entries from October 1, 2026 to October 31, 2026, each the same amount within 3 days: 1 is in the feed twice ($1,198.05); 2 are feed lines already entered in the books ($1,358.50); 3 are in the books twice ($786.58). How it works

  • Pairs of the same amount: to the cent, no more than 3 days apart (you can set 0 to 31), scored on date, payee and reference.
  • Which copy stays: the reconciled one first, then the vendors' own rules.
  • The action: in QuickBooks Online's or Xero's own words, with an exclude-ready CSV.

What it does not cover

It never excludes, deletes or changes anything in your books, and two equal charges on one day can both be real: check each pair against the statement.

Read how it works in full

Pairs of the same amount

Every two lines with the same amount, to the cent, dated no more than 3 days apart (you can set 0 to 31) are a candidate: two in the bank feed, two in the books, or a feed line and a books entry. Each is scored: 2 points for the same date (1 for a day apart), 2 when the payee words match (1 for a partial match), 2 for the same reference. 4 or more is high confidence, 2 or 3 medium, 0 or 1 low. An amount that appears three or more times on one side, like rent, is never above medium.

Which copy stays

Pairs are settled best score first, and a line is removed at most once. The copy kept follows the vendors' own rules: the reconciled copy, as Intuit says; the entry already in the books over a feed line waiting for review; in Xero, the bank-feed line over an imported one; otherwise the earlier line. A run of dates where nearly every line came in twice is named as one pattern.

The action, in the platform's words

QuickBooks Online: exclude the extra copy in For review; match a feed line you had already entered instead of adding it; for a duplicate already in the register, undo it in Categorized and exclude it, or delete or void a hand-entered one. Xero: delete the extra statement line, or reconcile a statement line to the transaction already entered. The exclude-ready CSV lists them in date order.

Please read
  • Not accounting or tax advice. The tool lists likely duplicates and the action for each; it never excludes, deletes or changes anything in your books.
  • Check every pair against the bank statement before you act on it: two equal charges on the same day can both be real.
  • Never remove a reconciled copy, and ask your accountant before changing anything in a closed or filed period.
  • Files are processed in memory and not kept.
