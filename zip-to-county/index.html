The sources

What the answer is built from, file by file.

A ZIP's counties come from the Census 2020 relationship file, a postcode's areas from the ONS Postcode Directory. Every answer names the file and its date.

What it does not cover

Northern Ireland postcodes, PO box ZIPs with no area, and the county of one street address.

Read the full list

Northern Ireland (BT) postcodes are not covered: commercial use needs a separate license. Channel Islands and Isle of Man postcodes are outside UK local government and are named, not looked up.

A ZIP used only for PO boxes or by one organization has no area, so it has no ZCTA and is reported as not found, free.

Shares are by land area, not by addresses or population: for counting people, an address-based source such as HUD's USPS crosswalk is closer, and for one address the street address decides the county. Connecticut ZIPs are in its 8 counties of the 2020 file, not the planning regions federal data has used since 2022.

US ZIP codes

Each county a ZIP's area has land in, with its share of that land, largest first.

Census 2020 ZCTA to county file

Read how it works

A ZIP code is a USPS delivery route, not an area. The Census Bureau draws an area for each one, a ZIP Code Tabulation Area (ZCTA), and its 2020 relationship file lists the land of every ZCTA in every county. That file is the answer here: each county with its share of the ZCTA's land, largest first. 30.1% of ZCTAs have land in more than one county.

The county's metro or micro area comes from OMB's July 2023 delineation, and the region from the Census Bureau's regions and divisions.

UK postcodes

Every Great Britain postcode, live or terminated, with its council, county, region and nation.

ONS Postcode Directory

Read how it works

Every Great Britain postcode, live or terminated, from the ONS Postcode Directory, with its local authority district. In the two-tier parts of England that district sits under a county council; elsewhere the council is single-tier and is its own "county or unitary authority". England's postcodes also get their region. Terminated postcodes keep the areas of their last location and are marked.

Prices

One code or one county is free on this page. Lists are charged per code found; codes not found cost nothing.

Read the full prices
  • One ZIP code or postcode, or one county's ZIP list, on this page: free.
  • ZIP codes and postcodes (lists, REST and MCP): $0.0005 per code found for the first 100,000 each calendar month, then $0.0002 per code found; you pay only for what is delivered and the rest is refunded at once; codes not found are not charged.
  • A county's ZIP list over REST or MCP: $0.001 per county.
