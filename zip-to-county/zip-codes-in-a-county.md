> A county list holds ZIP code areas (ZCTAs). PO-box and single-organization ZIPs have no area, so they are not listed.

> Answers come from the Census 2020 ZCTA5-county relationship file, OMB's July 2023 metro area delineation and the ONS Postcode Directory (August 2026). A ZCTA is the Census Bureau's area for a ZIP code; USPS ZIP codes are delivery routes and change. County shares are by land area, not by addresses or population.

## Questions

### How many ZIP codes are in Travis County, Texas?

Travis County, TX (FIPS 48453) has land in 63 ZCTAs: 37 wholly inside it and 26 shared with other counties (Census 2020 ZCTA file).

### Should I include ZIPs with a tiny share?

Usually not for mailing or targeting: 3 of Travis County's shared ZIPs have under 1% of their land in it. Filter by share to the threshold your job needs.

### Where do I find a county's FIPS code?
