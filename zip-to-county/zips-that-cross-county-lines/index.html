Answers come from the Census 2020 ZCTA5-county relationship file, OMB's July 2023 metro area delineation and the ONS Postcode Directory (August 2026). A ZCTA is the Census Bureau's area for a ZIP code; USPS ZIP codes are delivery routes and change. County shares are by land area, not by addresses or population.

Questions

Can a ZIP code be in more than one county?

Yes: 10,180 of the 33,791 ZCTAs in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file (30.1%) have land in more than one county.

Which ZIP codes touch the most counties?

00650 (PR), 00725 (PR), 39573 (MS), 68823 (NE): 6 counties each.

Can a ZIP code cross a state line?

Yes: 137 ZCTAs have land in two states, such as 02861 (RI and MA), 03579 (ME and NH), 19973 (DE and MD).

Which county does a split ZIP code belong to?

It has land in each of them. This table gives each county's share of the land; the county with the largest share is marked. For addresses, use the street address to place each one.
