ZIP code to CBSA crosswalk

26,524 of the 33,791 ZCTAs have land in a metro or micro area (CBSA) under OMB's July 2023 delineation; New York-Newark-Jersey City, NY-NJ (35620) has the most, 904. A ZCTA's CBSA comes from its counties, so a ZCTA across a CBSA line gets each CBSA's share of its land.

Questions

What is a CBSA?

A core based statistical area: a metropolitan or micropolitan statistical area defined by OMB from whole counties. The July 2023 delineation used here has 388 metro areas among the CBSAs with ZCTA land.

How many ZIP codes are in no metro or micro area?

9,075 ZCTAs have most of their land outside any CBSA.

Why do Connecticut ZIP codes have no CBSA here?

OMB's 2023 delineation builds Connecticut's CBSAs from its 9 planning regions, but the Census 2020 ZCTA file places ZCTAs in the 8 old counties, so the 288 ZCTAs wholly in Connecticut are left without one rather than guessed.
