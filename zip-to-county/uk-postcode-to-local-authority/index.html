UK postcode to local authority and county

The ONS Postcode Directory (August 2026) places 1,749,110 live Great Britain postcodes in 350 local authorities, 296 in England, 32 in Scotland and 22 in Wales; 164 of them are districts under one of 21 county councils. North Yorkshire has the most live postcodes, 23,966.

Questions

What county is my postcode in?

Look it up above. In the two-tier parts of England a postcode is in a district and a county council, such as Maidstone in Kent; everywhere else the local authority is single-tier and is also its own "county or unitary authority". Ceremonial and historic counties are not in the ONS directory.

How often is the UK postcode directory updated?

ONS releases its postcode products quarterly, in February, May, August and November.

Is the ONS Postcode Directory free?

Yes, under the Open Government Licence, but commercial use of Northern Ireland (BT) postcodes needs a separate Land and Property Services license, so they are not covered here.

What happens to a terminated postcode?

ONS keeps it, with the areas of its last location: the August 2026 directory has 903,514 terminated Great Britain postcodes beside 1,749,110 live ones.
