Questions

How many ZIP codes are in New Jersey?

598 ZCTAs have land in New Jersey in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many New Jersey ZIP codes cross a county line?

63 of 598 (10.5%), such as 07009, 07043, 07060.

Which New Jersey county has the most ZIP codes?

Bergen County, NJ (34003), with land in 66 ZCTAs.
