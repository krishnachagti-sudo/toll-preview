Questions

How many ZIP codes are in West Virginia?

741 ZCTAs have land in West Virginia in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many West Virginia ZIP codes cross a county line?

180 of 741 (24.3%), such as 20135, 24604, 24622.

Which West Virginia county has the most ZIP codes?

Kanawha County, WV (54039), with land in 48 ZCTAs.
