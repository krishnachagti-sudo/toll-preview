Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Alaska?

245 ZCTAs have land in Alaska in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Alaska ZIP codes cross a county line?

12 of 245 (4.9%), such as 99503, 99588, 99613.

Which Alaska county has the most ZIP codes?

Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area, AK (02290), with land in 33 ZCTAs.
