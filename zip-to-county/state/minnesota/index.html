Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Minnesota?

887 ZCTAs have land in Minnesota in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Minnesota ZIP codes cross a county line?

364 of 887 (41.0%), such as 51360, 55006, 55007.

Which Minnesota county has the most ZIP codes?

Hennepin County, MN (27053), with land in 73 ZCTAs.
