Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Alabama?

658 ZCTAs have land in Alabama in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Alabama ZIP codes cross a county line?

254 of 658 (38.6%), such as 30165, 35006, 35010.

Which Alabama county has the most ZIP codes?

Jefferson County, AL (01073), with land in 64 ZCTAs.
