Questions

How many ZIP codes are in North Carolina?

853 ZCTAs have land in North Carolina in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many North Carolina ZIP codes cross a county line?

329 of 853 (38.6%), such as 27009, 27012, 27013.

Which North Carolina county has the most ZIP codes?

Wake County, NC (37183), with land in 41 ZCTAs.
