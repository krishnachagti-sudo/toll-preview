Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Oklahoma?

666 ZCTAs have land in Oklahoma in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Oklahoma ZIP codes cross a county line?

277 of 666 (41.6%), such as 71937, 71953, 73002.

Which Oklahoma county has the most ZIP codes?

Oklahoma County, OK (40109), with land in 55 ZCTAs.
