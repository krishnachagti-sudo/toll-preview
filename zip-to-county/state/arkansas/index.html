Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Arkansas?

620 ZCTAs have land in Arkansas in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Arkansas ZIP codes cross a county line?

242 of 620 (39.0%), such as 38769, 65729, 65733.

Which Arkansas county has the most ZIP codes?

Pulaski County, AR (05119), with land in 36 ZCTAs.
