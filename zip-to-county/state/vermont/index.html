Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Vermont?

265 ZCTAs have land in Vermont in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Vermont ZIP codes cross a county line?

39 of 265 (14.7%), such as 05060, 05065, 05068.

Which Vermont county has the most ZIP codes?

Windsor County, VT (50027), with land in 39 ZCTAs.
