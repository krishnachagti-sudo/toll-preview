# Utah ZIP codes by county

Utah has land in 303 ZIP code areas (ZCTAs) across 29 counties; 30 of them cross a county line and 8 reach into another state (Census 2020 ZCTA file). The CSV below is the Utah ZIP to county crosswalk.

- ZCTAs: 303
- Counties: 29

### How many ZIP codes are in Utah?

303 ZCTAs have land in Utah in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

30 of 303 (9.9%), such as 81324, 82930, 83342.
