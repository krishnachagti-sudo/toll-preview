Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Maine?

427 ZCTAs have land in Maine in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Maine ZIP codes cross a county line?

17 of 427 (4.0%), such as 03579, 04040, 04224.

Which Maine county has the most ZIP codes?

Penobscot County, ME (23019), with land in 47 ZCTAs.
