Illinois ZIP codes that cross a county line

Answers come from the Census 2020 ZCTA5-county relationship file, OMB's July 2023 metro area delineation and the ONS Postcode Directory (August 2026). A ZCTA is the Census Bureau's area for a ZIP code; USPS ZIP codes are delivery routes and change. County shares are by land area, not by addresses or population.

Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Illinois?

1,397 ZCTAs have land in Illinois in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Illinois ZIP codes cross a county line?

486 of 1,397 (34.8%), such as 60007, 60010, 60013.

Which Illinois county has the most ZIP codes?

Cook County, IL (17031), with land in 178 ZCTAs.
