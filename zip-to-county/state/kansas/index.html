Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Kansas?

710 ZCTAs have land in Kansas in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Kansas ZIP codes cross a county line?

338 of 710 (47.6%), such as 66002, 66006, 66010.

Which Kansas county has the most ZIP codes?

Sedgwick County, KS (20173), with land in 52 ZCTAs.
