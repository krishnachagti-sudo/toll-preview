Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Pennsylvania?

1,833 ZCTAs have land in Pennsylvania in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Pennsylvania ZIP codes cross a county line?

361 of 1,833 (19.7%), such as 15003, 15005, 15012.

Which Pennsylvania county has the most ZIP codes?

Allegheny County, PA (42003), with land in 119 ZCTAs.
