Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Tennessee?

641 ZCTAs have land in Tennessee in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Tennessee ZIP codes cross a county line?

318 of 641 (49.6%), such as 30741, 37010, 37012.

Which Tennessee county has the most ZIP codes?

Shelby County, TN (47157), with land in 41 ZCTAs.
