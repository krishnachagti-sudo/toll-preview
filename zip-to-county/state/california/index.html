Questions

How many ZIP codes are in California?

1,808 ZCTAs have land in California in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many California ZIP codes cross a county line?

174 of 1,808 (9.6%), such as 89010, 89019, 89060.

Which California county has the most ZIP codes?

Los Angeles County, CA (06037), with land in 304 ZCTAs.
