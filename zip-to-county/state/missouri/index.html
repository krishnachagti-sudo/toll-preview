Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Missouri?

1,040 ZCTAs have land in Missouri in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Missouri ZIP codes cross a county line?

467 of 1,040 (44.9%), such as 51640, 52542, 52573.

Which Missouri county has the most ZIP codes?

Jackson County, MO (29095), with land in 58 ZCTAs.
