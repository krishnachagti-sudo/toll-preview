Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Louisiana?

540 ZCTAs have land in Louisiana in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Louisiana ZIP codes cross a parish line?

148 of 540 (27.4%), such as 70030, 70040, 70068.

Which Louisiana county has the most ZIP codes?

Rapides Parish, LA (22079), with land in 29 ZCTAs.
