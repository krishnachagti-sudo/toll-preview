Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Nevada?

182 ZCTAs have land in Nevada in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Nevada ZIP codes cross a county line?

27 of 182 (14.8%), such as 84034, 89010, 89019.

Which Nevada county has the most ZIP codes?

Clark County, NV (32003), with land in 76 ZCTAs.
