Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Maryland?

478 ZCTAs have land in Maryland in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Maryland ZIP codes cross a county line?

82 of 478 (17.2%), such as 19973, 20601, 20607.

Which Maryland county has the most ZIP codes?

Baltimore County, MD (24005), with land in 57 ZCTAs.
