Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Nebraska?

592 ZCTAs have land in Nebraska in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Nebraska ZIP codes cross a county line?

293 of 592 (49.5%), such as 51557, 57735, 66541.

Which Nebraska county has the most ZIP codes?

Lancaster County, NE (31109), with land in 44 ZCTAs.
