# Rhode Island ZIP codes by county

Rhode Island has land in 81 ZIP code areas (ZCTAs) across 5 counties; 3 of them cross a county line and 1 reach into another state (Census 2020 ZCTA file). The CSV below is the Rhode Island ZIP to county crosswalk.

- ZCTAs: 81
- Counties: 5
- Crossing a county line: 3
- Most ZCTAs: Providence County: 43
