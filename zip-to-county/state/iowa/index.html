Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Iowa?

972 ZCTAs have land in Iowa in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Iowa ZIP codes cross a county line?

472 of 972 (48.6%), such as 50002, 50006, 50014.

Which Iowa county has the most ZIP codes?

Polk County, IA (19153), with land in 42 ZCTAs.
