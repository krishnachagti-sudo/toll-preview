# Maine ZIP codes by county

Maine has land in 427 ZIP code areas (ZCTAs) across 16 counties; 17 of them cross a county line and 1 reach into another state (Census 2020 ZCTA file). The CSV below is the Maine ZIP to county crosswalk.

- ZCTAs: 427
- Counties: 16
