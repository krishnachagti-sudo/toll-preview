Questions

How many ZIP codes are in South Dakota?

401 ZCTAs have land in South Dakota in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many South Dakota ZIP codes cross a county line?

229 of 401 (57.1%), such as 51001, 51023, 56136.

Which South Dakota county has the most ZIP codes?

Minnehaha County, SD (46099), with land in 23 ZCTAs.
