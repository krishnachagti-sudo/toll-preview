Questions

How many ZIP codes are in South Carolina?

424 ZCTAs have land in South Carolina in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many South Carolina ZIP codes cross a county line?

165 of 424 (38.9%), such as 29001, 29006, 29009.

Which South Carolina county has the most ZIP codes?

Spartanburg County, SC (45083), with land in 34 ZCTAs.
