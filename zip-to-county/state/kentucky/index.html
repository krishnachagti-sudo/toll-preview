Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Kentucky?

782 ZCTAs have land in Kentucky in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Kentucky ZIP codes cross a county line?

295 of 782 (37.7%), such as 38079, 38549, 40003.

Which Kentucky county has the most ZIP codes?

Jefferson County, KY (21111), with land in 43 ZCTAs.
