## Questions

### How many ZIP codes are in Virginia?

907 ZCTAs have land in Virginia in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

### How many Virginia ZIP codes cross a county line?

320 of 907 (35.3%), such as 20106, 20110, 20111.

### Which Virginia county has the most ZIP codes?

Fairfax County, VA (51059), with land in 49 ZCTAs.
