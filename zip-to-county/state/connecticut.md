# Connecticut ZIP codes by county

Connecticut has land in 288 ZIP code areas (ZCTAs) across 8 counties; 11 of them cross a county line (Census 2020 ZCTA file). The CSV below is the Connecticut ZIP to county crosswalk.

- ZCTAs: 288
- Counties: 8
- Crossing a county line: 11
- Most ZCTAs: Hartford County: 61

> Answers come from the Census 2020 ZCTA5-county relationship file, OMB's July 2023 metro area delineation and the ONS Postcode Directory (August 2026). A ZCTA is the Census Bureau's area for a ZIP code; USPS ZIP codes are delivery routes and change. County shares are by land area, not by addresses or population.

## Questions

### How many ZIP codes are in Connecticut?

288 ZCTAs have land in Connecticut in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

### How many Connecticut ZIP codes cross a county line?

11 of 288 (3.8%), such as 06065, 06226, 06242.

### Which Connecticut county has the most ZIP codes?

Hartford County, CT (09003), with land in 61 ZCTAs.
