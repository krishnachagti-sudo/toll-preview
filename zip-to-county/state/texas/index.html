Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Texas?

1,992 ZCTAs have land in Texas in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Texas ZIP codes cross a county line?

691 of 1,992 (34.7%), such as 73949, 75007, 75009.

Which Texas county has the most ZIP codes?

Harris County, TX (48201), with land in 143 ZCTAs.
