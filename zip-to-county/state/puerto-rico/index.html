Questions

How many ZIP codes are in Puerto Rico?

132 ZCTAs have land in Puerto Rico in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many Puerto Rico ZIP codes cross a municipio line?

74 of 132 (56.1%), such as 00601, 00602, 00603.

Which Puerto Rico county has the most ZIP codes?

San Juan Municipio, PR (72127), with land in 21 ZCTAs.
