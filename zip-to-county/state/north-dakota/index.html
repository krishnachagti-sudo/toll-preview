North Dakota ZIP codes that cross a county line

Answers come from the Census 2020 ZCTA5-county relationship file, OMB's July 2023 metro area delineation and the ONS Postcode Directory (August 2026). A ZCTA is the Census Bureau's area for a ZIP code; USPS ZIP codes are delivery routes and change. County shares are by land area, not by addresses or population.

Questions

How many ZIP codes are in North Dakota?

400 ZCTAs have land in North Dakota in the Census 2020 ZCTA-county file. PO-box-only ZIP codes have no ZCTA and are not counted.

How many North Dakota ZIP codes cross a county line?

197 of 400 (49.3%), such as 56744, 57255, 57430.

Which North Dakota county has the most ZIP codes?

Cass County, ND (38017), with land in 30 ZCTAs.
