How many ZIP codes are in Mississippi?

How many Mississippi ZIP codes cross a county line?

233 of 428 (54.4%), such as 38326, 38603, 38606.

Which Mississippi county has the most ZIP codes?

Hinds County, MS (28049), with land in 24 ZCTAs.
