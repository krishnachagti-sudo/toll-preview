# Can back of house share in the tip pool?

Under the FLSA, cooks and dishwashers may share a tip pool only if the employer takes no tip credit and pays everyone in the pool at least the full minimum wage in cash; the same $1,500.00 pool with a cook and a dishwasher is allowed federally with no tip credit, not allowed with one, and not allowed in New York either way.

“The employer may require an employee for whom the employer takes a tip credit to contribute tips to a tip pool only if it is limited to employees who customarily and regularly receive tips”

“An employer that pays its tipped employees the full minimum wage and does not take a tip credit may impose a tip pooling arrangement that includes dishwashers, cooks, or other employees in the establishment who are not employed in an occupation in which employees customarily and regularly receive tips. An employer may not receive tips from such a tip pool and may not allow supervisors and managers to receive tips from the tip pool.”

“an employer that implements a nontraditional tip pool may require tipped employees, such as servers, to share tips with non-tipped employees, such as dishwashers and cooks, but only if all workers receive a direct cash wage of at least the federal minimum wage.”

Federal rules, no tip credit.

| Setup | Result | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |

| New York, no tip credit | not allowed | 12 NYCRR 146-2.16(b) |

- **Not allowed:** Gus and Ivy: with a tip credit, the pool may include only employees who customarily and regularly receive tips, so these roles are not allowed. Pay everyone in the pool at least the full minimum wage in cash to include them. (29 CFR 531.54(c)(1); 29 CFR 531.54(d); DOL Fact Sheet #15)

## States that go further

- [Massachusetts](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/tip-pool-calculator/laws/massachusetts/): only wait staff, service employees and service bartenders may share, so cooks and dishwashers may not; the card processing fee may not come out of card tips.
- [New York](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/tip-pool-calculator/laws/new-york/): only food service workers who give personal service to patrons may share, so cooks and dishwashers may not; only the card company's pro-rated fee may come off a card tip.
- [Washington](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/tip-pool-calculator/laws/washington/): pools may include kitchen staff and hourly leads but not salaried-exempt managers or owners.

## Questions

### Can back-of-house staff share in a tip pool?

Federally, only when the employer takes no tip credit and every pool member gets at least the full minimum wage in cash (29 CFR 531.54(d)). Some states keep them out regardless, such as Massachusetts and New York.
