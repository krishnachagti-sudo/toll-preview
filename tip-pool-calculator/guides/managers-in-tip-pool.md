# Can managers be in a tip pool?

Under the FLSA, employers, managers and supervisors may not receive tips from a pool, whether or not the employer takes a tip credit; a manager who worked 6 hours of a $1,000.00 pool would take $206.90, which belongs to the other three (Ana's share goes from $275.86 to $347.83).

“An employer may not allow managers and supervisors to keep any portion of an employee's tips, regardless of whether the employer takes a tip credit. A manager or supervisor may keep tips that he or she receives directly from customers based on the service that he or she directly and solely provides.”

## Who counts as a manager or supervisor

“Managers and supervisors include any employee (1) whose primary duty is managing the enterprise or a customarily recognized department or subdivision of the enterprise; (2) who customarily and regularly directs the work of at least two or more other full-time employees or their equivalent; and (3) who has the authority to hire or fire other employees, or whose suggestions and recommendations as to the hiring or firing are given particular weight.”

“Business owners who own at least a bona fide 20 percent equity interest in the enterprise in which they are employed and who are actively engaged in its management are also managers and supervisors who may not keep employees' tips.”

Job titles do not decide it; duties do. A shift lead who does not meet all three parts of the test is not a manager for this rule, though some states are stricter: Massachusetts keeps out anyone with managerial responsibility on the day.

## Their own tables

“a restaurant manager who serves their own tables may keep their own tips from customers they served but would not be able to receive other employees' tips by participating in a tip pool.”

## What it costs

“An employer who violates the FLSA by requiring tipped employees to share their tips with a manager or supervisor may be required to return the tips to the employee and pay the full minimum wage.”

## Questions

### Can managers or supervisors take from a tip pool?

No. Under the FLSA, employers may not receive tips from a tip pool or allow managers and supervisors to receive them, whether or not the employer takes a tip credit (29 CFR 531.54).

### Can the owner be in the tip pool?

No. An employer may not receive tips from a pool, and DOL Fact Sheet #15 treats an owner of at least 20 percent who actively manages the business as a manager.

### Can a manager keep tips from tables they served?

Yes: “a restaurant manager who serves their own tables may keep their own tips from customers they served but would not be able to receive other employees' tips by participating in a tip pool.” (DOL Fact Sheet #15).
