## What the law says about tip-outs

A required tip-out is a tip pool in law: the amounts passed on count as tips of the people who keep them (29 CFR 531.54(a)). So the same rules apply: no employer, manager or supervisor may receive any of it, and with a tip credit only staff who customarily and regularly receive tips may (531.54(c)). 

“Section 3(m)(2)(A) does not impose a maximum contribution percentage on mandatory tip pools.”

The check found nothing to fix on these facts.

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.
