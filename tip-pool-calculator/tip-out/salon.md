## What the law says about tip-outs

A required tip-out is a tip pool in law: the amounts passed on count as tips of the people who keep them (29 CFR 531.54(a)). So the same rules apply: no employer, manager or supervisor may receive any of it, and with a tip credit only staff who customarily and regularly receive tips may (531.54(c)). The DOL names "barbers, or beauty operators" among customarily tipped occupations (29 CFR 531.57); a salon assistant or shampoo assistant is not named either way, so whether they customarily and regularly receive more than $30 a month in tips is a fact to check before they share under a tip credit.

“Section 3(m)(2)(A) does not impose a maximum contribution percentage on mandatory tip pools.”

The check found nothing to fix on these facts.
