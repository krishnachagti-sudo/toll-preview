# Tip pooling in Maine

In Maine the minimum cash wage for tipped staff is $7.55 and the tip credit is 50% of the applicable minimum wage ($7.55) against a $15.10 minimum wage (DOL table revised November 5, 2026); with the tip credit the pool must be only among service employees; without it, a wider group may share; the card processing fee may not come out of card tips.

- Minimum wage: $15.10
- Cash wage: $7.55
- Tip credit: $7.55

## Maine's own rules

- “The tip pooling arrangement is only among service employees when the employer uses the tip credit under subsection 2” (26 M.R.S. 664(2-A)(A))
- “The tip pooling arrangement is among a group of employees when the employer pays all employees in the group the minimum hourly wage and does not use the tip credit under subsection 2. An employer may not receive tips from such a tip pool and may not allow supervisors and managers to receive tips from the tip pool.” (26 M.R.S. 664(2-A)(B))
- “The employer may not deduct any amount from employee tips charged to a credit card, including, but not limited to, service fees assessed to the employer in connection with the credit card transaction.” (26 M.R.S. 664(2))
- “A tip that is charged to a credit card must be paid by the employer to the employee by the next regular payday and may not be held while the employer is awaiting reimbursement from a credit card company.” (26 M.R.S. 664(2))

## The federal floor

“When state law differs from the federal FLSA, an employer must comply with the standard most protective to employees.”

Federal rules apply everywhere: no employer, manager or supervisor may take from a pool (29 CFR 531.54(b), (c)(3), (d)), pooled tips must be paid by the regular payday (531.54(b)(2)), and with a tip credit only customarily tipped staff may share (531.54(c)(1)).

## Questions

### Is tip pooling legal in Maine?

In Maine the minimum cash wage for tipped staff is $7.55 and the tip credit is 50% of the applicable minimum wage ($7.55) against a $15.10 minimum wage (DOL table revised November 5, 2026); with the tip credit the pool must be only among service employees; without it, a wider group may share; the card processing fee may not come out of card tips.

### Can a manager be in a tip pool in Maine?

No. Under the FLSA an employer may not allow managers or supervisors to receive tips from a pool, whether or not it takes a tip credit (29 CFR 531.54), and that applies in every state.
