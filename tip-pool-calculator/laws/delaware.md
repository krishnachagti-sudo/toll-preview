# Tip pooling in Delaware

In Delaware the minimum cash wage for tipped staff is $2.23 and the tip credit is $12.77 against a $15.00 minimum wage (DOL table revised November 5, 2026); an employer may require pooling only among direct service employees who served the same customer, up to 15% of the main server's tips.

- Minimum wage: $15.00
- Cash wage: $2.23
- Tip credit: $12.77

## Delaware's own rules

- “Employees may establish a system for the sharing or pooling of gratuities among direct service employees, provided that the employer shall not in any fashion require or coerce employees to agree upon such a system. Where more than 1 direct service employee provides personal service to the same customer from whom gratuities are received, the employer may require that such employees establish a tip pooling or sharing system not to exceed 15% of the primary direct service employee’s gratuities.” (19 Del. C. 902(d)(2))
- “The employer shall not, under any circumstances, receive any portion of the gratuities received by the employees.” (19 Del. C. 902(d)(2))

## The federal floor

“When state law differs from the federal FLSA, an employer must comply with the standard most protective to employees.”

Federal rules apply everywhere: no employer, manager or supervisor may take from a pool (29 CFR 531.54(b), (c)(3), (d)), pooled tips must be paid by the regular payday (531.54(b)(2)), and with a tip credit only customarily tipped staff may share (531.54(c)(1)).

## Questions

### Is tip pooling legal in Delaware?

In Delaware the minimum cash wage for tipped staff is $2.23 and the tip credit is $12.77 against a $15.00 minimum wage (DOL table revised November 5, 2026); an employer may require pooling only among direct service employees who served the same customer, up to 15% of the main server's tips.

### Can a manager be in a tip pool in Delaware?

No. Under the FLSA an employer may not allow managers or supervisors to receive tips from a pool, whether or not it takes a tip credit (29 CFR 531.54), and that applies in every state.
