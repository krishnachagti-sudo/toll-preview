# Tip pooling in the District of Columbia

In the District of Columbia the minimum cash wage for tipped staff is $10.30 and the tip credit is $8.10 against an $18.40 minimum wage (DOL table revised November 5, 2026); the tipped-wage notice must state the tip-sharing policy and the percentage card fees take off card tips; otherwise the federal pool rules apply.

- Minimum wage: $18.40
- Cash wage: $10.30
- Tip credit: $8.10

## District of Columbia's own rules

- “If tips are shared, the employer's tip-sharing policy; and” (D.C. Code 32-1003(g)(1)(C))
- “The percentage by which tips paid via credit card will be reduced by credit card fees;” (D.C. Code 32-1003(g)(1)(D))
- “as of July 1, 2026, the tipped minimum wage shall be 56% of the minimum wage provided in subsection (a) of this section, with tips on top” (D.C. Code 32-1003(f)(6A))
- “as of July 1, 2028, the tipped minimum wage shall be 60% of the minimum wage provided in subsection (a) of this section, with tips on top” (D.C. Code 32-1003(f)(7))

## The federal floor

“When state law differs from the federal FLSA, an employer must comply with the standard most protective to employees.”

Federal rules apply everywhere: no employer, manager or supervisor may take from a pool (29 CFR 531.54(b), (c)(3), (d)), pooled tips must be paid by the regular payday (531.54(b)(2)), and with a tip credit only customarily tipped staff may share (531.54(c)(1)).

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.

## Questions

### Is tip pooling legal in the District of Columbia?

In the District of Columbia the minimum cash wage for tipped staff is $10.30 and the tip credit is $8.10 against an $18.40 minimum wage (DOL table revised November 5, 2026); the tipped-wage notice must state the tip-sharing policy and the percentage card fees take off card tips; otherwise the federal pool rules apply.

### Can a manager be in a tip pool in the District of Columbia?

No. Under the FLSA an employer may not allow managers or supervisors to receive tips from a pool, whether or not it takes a tip credit (29 CFR 531.54), and that applies in every state.
