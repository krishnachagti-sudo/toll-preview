# Tip pooling in Maryland

In Maryland the minimum cash wage for tipped staff is $3.63 and the tip credit is $11.37 against a $15.00 minimum wage (DOL table revised November 5, 2026); the statute says it does not prohibit the pooling of tips; otherwise the federal pool rules apply.

- Minimum wage: $15.00
- Cash wage: $3.63
- Tip credit: $11.37

## Maryland's own rules

- “Notwithstanding paragraph (1)(iii) of this subsection, this section does not prohibit the pooling of tips.” (LE 3-419(a)(2))
- “The tip credit amount that the employer may include under subsection (b) of this section may not exceed the minimum wage established under § 3–413 of this subtitle for the employee less $3.63.” (LE 3-419(c))

## The federal floor

“When state law differs from the federal FLSA, an employer must comply with the standard most protective to employees.”

Federal rules apply everywhere: no employer, manager or supervisor may take from a pool (29 CFR 531.54(b), (c)(3), (d)), pooled tips must be paid by the regular payday (531.54(b)(2)), and with a tip credit only customarily tipped staff may share (531.54(c)(1)).

## Questions

### Is tip pooling legal in Maryland?

In Maryland the minimum cash wage for tipped staff is $3.63 and the tip credit is $11.37 against a $15.00 minimum wage (DOL table revised November 5, 2026); the statute says it does not prohibit the pooling of tips; otherwise the federal pool rules apply.

### Can a manager be in a tip pool in Maryland?

No. Under the FLSA an employer may not allow managers or supervisors to receive tips from a pool, whether or not it takes a tip credit (29 CFR 531.54), and that applies in every state.
