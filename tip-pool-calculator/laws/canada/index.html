Tip laws

Tip pooling in Canada: Ontario

In Ontario, an employer may require staff to pool tips and redistribute them among some or all employees, managers may share if the policy allows, the employer itself only as an owner who regularly does the same work, and only a credit card processing fee may come off card tips; C$900.00 split by hours here pays about C$25.47 an hour. Other provinces are not covered.

Pools are allowed

“An employer may withhold or make deductions from an employee’s tips or other gratuities if the amount collected will be redistributed among some, or all, of the employees at the workplace.”

“Under the ESA, employers do not need the employees’ agreement to make deductions from their tips or other gratuities if the amount will be redistributed as part of a tip pool. Participating in a tip pool could be a condition of employment.”

“tip pooling amounts cannot come from any source other than tips”

Managers and owners

“Managers are allowed to keep the tips and gratuities they receive themselves, and generally may participate in tip pooling arrangements if their employers’ policy permits them to do so.”

“An employer cannot share in a tip pool unless the following apply:” they are a sole proprietor, partner, director or shareholder in the business; and they regularly perform to a substantial degree the same work as either: some or all the employees who share in the redistribution (the guide continues with employees of other employers in the same industry).

“Effective June 21, 2024, where an employer has a policy with respect to the employer, or a director or shareholder of the employer, sharing in a tip pool, the employer is required to post a copy of its tips sharing policy in at least one clearly visible place in the workplace where it is likely to be seen by employees.”

Card fees

The guide excludes from tips a portion of a credit card processing fee: the greater of the tip times the card company's percentage and the tip times 1.5%.

“Employers are not permitted to deduct or withhold from an employee's tips or other gratuities any amounts representing debit card processing fees or any other type of processing fee.”

A worked pool

Your C$900.00 pool after C$8.40 of card fees over 35 hours pays about C$25.47 an hour: Ava C$203.79, Liam C$152.85, Noor C$152.85, Theo C$178.32 and Zoe C$203.79 (to the cent, adding up to C$891.60). No rule is broken on these facts.

  • Note: Zoe: managers generally may share in a pool if your policy allows it. (Ontario ESA guide: tips or other gratuities)
  • Note: Only a credit card processing fee may come off, at the card company's percentage or 1.5%, whichever is greater; never a debit card or other processing fee. (Ontario ESA guide: tips or other gratuities)

Questions

Is tip pooling legal in Canada?

Each province sets its own rules. In Ontario, yes: an employer may deduct tips for redistribution in a tip pool, and taking part can be a condition of employment (Ontario ESA guide). Other provinces are not covered here.

Can managers share the tip pool in Ontario?

“Managers are allowed to keep the tips and gratuities they receive themselves, and generally may participate in tip pooling arrangements if their employers’ policy permits them to do so.” (Ontario ESA guide).
