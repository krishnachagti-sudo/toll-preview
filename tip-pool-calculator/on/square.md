# How to split tips on Square

Square pools card tips per transaction among staff clocked in, by hours over a workday or workweek, or by job percentages, on its Plus and Premium plans; a $420.00 day split by hours here pays $21.00 an hour (Ana $126.00), with the check of who may be in the pool that Square leaves to a manual setting.

## Square's modes

- Per transaction: “Transaction tips are split equally among all tip-eligible team members clocked in at the time of the transaction.”
- By hours: “Transaction tips from sales are split among tip-eligible team members based on their hours worked. You can specify whether to split tips based on a 24-hour workday or across a 7-day workweek.”
- By percentages: “Tips are split by a user-defined percentage per transaction, at the end of each workday, or at the end of each workweek among tip-eligible jobs. Tip-eligible team members with the same job will receive tips based on hours worked, and tip percentage amounts assigned to each job must add up to 100%.”

Available to: “Square Shifts Plus, Square for Retail Premium, Square for Restaurants Premium, and Square Appointments Premium subscribers”.

## Managers are a setting

“For example, if you don’t want a manager to receive tips, you’ll need to toggle off Tip eligible in their team member profile from your Square Dashboard.”

Federal law makes this a rule, not a preference: no manager or supervisor may receive tips from a pool (29 CFR 531.54).
