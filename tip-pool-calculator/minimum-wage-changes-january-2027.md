# Tipped wage changes, January 1, 2027

On January 1, 2027 Washington's minimum wage rises to $17.73 with still no tip credit, Virginia's to $13.75, and South Dakota's to $12.30 with a $6.15 tipped minimum wage, each as published by the state; changes in other states are not covered here.

Today's figure from the DOL table; the 2027 figure from the state's own site.

| State | Now | From Jan 1, 2027 | Tipped staff | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Washington | $17.13 | $17.73 | No tip credit: the cash wage is the full minimum wage | Washington Department of Labor & Industries: Minimum Wage |
| Virginia | $12.77 | $13.75 | Cash wage stays at least $2.13; the tip credit can grow to the difference | Code of Virginia 40.1-28.10 (Virginia Law, Legislative Information System) |
| South Dakota | $11.85 | $12.30 | Tipped minimum wage $6.15 (from $5.93) | South Dakota Department of Labor and Regulation: Minimum Wage |

- “The 2027 minimum wage in the state of Washington is $17.73 per hour.”
- “From January 1, 2027, until January 1, 2028, every employer shall pay to each of its employees wages at a rate not less than the greater of (i) $13.75 per hour or (ii) the federal minimum wage.”
- “Effective Jan. 1, 2027, the minimum wage for tipped employees in South Dakota will be $6.15/hour.”

Check payroll before the first pay period of 2027: a tip credit is the minimum wage minus the cash wage, so a higher minimum raises either the cash wage or the tips the employee must get (29 CFR 531.59).

The DOL table also notes, for Michigan: “The Michigan minimum wage is scheduled to increase on January 1, 2027 (to $15.00 per hour); it is then scheduled to be adjusted annually based on a set formula..” Michigan's own site was not read.

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.
