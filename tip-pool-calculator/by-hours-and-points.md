# Hours x points: the weighted tip pool

Split by hours x points: $1,240.00 / 342 point-hours = about $3.63 a point-hour; a server with 10 points for 7.5 hours (75 point-hours) gets $271.93, a busser with 5 points for 4 hours gets $72.51, and the shares add up to exactly $1,240.00.

> \* got one of the 4 leftover cents. Each share is the pool times that person's part, rounded down to the cent; the cents left over go one each to the largest remainders (ties to the person listed first), so the shares add up exactly.

## The legal side

The method is yours to choose; who is in the pool is not. Employers, managers and supervisors may never receive tips from a pool, and with a tip credit the pool may include only staff who customarily and regularly receive tips (29 CFR 531.54(c)). [Who can be in the pool](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/tip-pool-calculator/guides/who-can-be-in-the-pool/) sets out each role.

- **Note:** Pay out every collected tip by the regular payday for the workweek (or for the pay period it ends in). (29 CFR 531.54(b)(2))

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.

## Steps

1. **Points times hours.** For each person, multiply their role's points by the hours they worked.
2. **Value one point-hour.** Divide the pool by the total point-hours: about $3.63 a point-hour.
3. **Pay each person.** Multiply each person's point-hours by that value, round down to the cent, and hand out the leftover cents to the largest remainders.

## Questions

### Why weight points by hours?

Plain points pay a two-hour shift the same as an eight-hour one. Multiplying by hours keeps the role weighting and pays for time worked.
