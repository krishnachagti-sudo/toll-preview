# Printable tip-out sheet

A tip sheet lists each person's role, hours and share to the cent with a line to sign; this one splits $1,240.00 over 48 hours at about $25.83 an hour, adds up to exactly $1,240.00, and is the sheet the $0.25 pay-run record prints with the payroll CSV.

```text
Tip pool: Main Street, period ending November 15, 2026
Pool $1,240.00: $1,240.00 split by hours worked.
Rate: about $25.83 an hour.

Ana    Server                     7.5     10      $193.75   signed ____________
Ben    Server                       6     10      $155.00   signed ____________
Carla  Server                    5.25     10      $135.62   signed ____________
Cleo   Bartender                    8      8      $206.67   signed ____________
Dev    Busser                     6.5      5      $167.92   signed ____________
Ezra   Busser                       4      5      $103.33   signed ____________
Fay    Host                         5      3      $129.17   signed ____________
Gus    Food runner               5.75      4      $148.54   signed ____________
Total                                           $1,240.00

Check: no rule broken on these facts.
Each share is the pool times that person's part, rounded down to the cent; the cents left over go one each to the largest remainders (ties to the person listed first), so the shares add up exactly.

```

## Keep it

An employer that takes no tip credit but runs a mandatory pool must keep records of each employee who receives tips and the amount (DOL Fact Sheet #15). New York requires six years of pool records (12 NYCRR 146-2.17); the UK three years (code para 43).

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.
