# UK tip deadlines, November 2026 to December 2027

In the UK, tips customers pay in a month must reach staff by the last day of the next month: November 2026 tips by 31 December 2026, December 2026 tips by 31 January 2027, and January 2027 tips by 28 February 2027.

“employers must ensure that all tips are distributed to staff, at the latest, by the end of the month following the month in which the tips are paid by customers (section 27G). For example, if a customer leaves a tip on 23 June, it must be distributed by 31 July at the latest.”
