## The legal side

The method is yours to choose; who is in the pool is not. Employers, managers and supervisors may never receive tips from a pool, and with a tip credit the pool may include only staff who customarily and regularly receive tips (29 CFR 531.54(c)). [Who can be in the pool](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/tip-pool-calculator/guides/who-can-be-in-the-pool/) sets out each role.

- **Note:** Pay out every collected tip by the regular payday for the workweek (or for the pay period it ends in). (29 CFR 531.54(b)(2))

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.

## Steps

1. **Set the percentages.** Give each role a share of the pool; together they must make 100%. No law sets these percentages.
2. **Split by role.** Multiply the pool by each role's percentage, to the cent.
3. **Split inside each role.** Divide each role's amount among the people who worked it, by hours (or equally), and hand out leftover cents to the largest remainders.
4. **Roles nobody worked.** If nobody worked a role, this calculator shares its percentage among the roles that did, in proportion. Toast instead gives it back to the contributor; decide which you use and write it in your policy.

## Questions

### What percentage should a server tip out?

No federal rule sets one: "The FLSA does not impose a limit on the percentage or amount of the contribution of each employee in valid mandatory tip pools." (DOL Fact Sheet #15). The percentage is your house policy, written down and told to staff. With a tip credit you must tell staff the required contribution (29 CFR 531.54(c)(2)).

### Is tip-out a percentage of sales or of tips?

Either, by house policy. A tip-out from sales can be more than the tips a server got on a slow night; see the server tip-out page.
