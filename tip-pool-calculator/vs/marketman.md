# MarketMan's tip pooling calculator compared

MarketMan's free tip pooling calculator is a download that splits tips by hours worked for front and back of house and tells you to make sure you know your state's labor laws (read November 8, 2026); this calculator splits by hours, points, hours x points or percentages to the cent and checks the laws for you, including whether back of house may share.

## What MarketMan's tip pooling calculator does (read November 8, 2026)

“Our Tip Pooling Calculator simplifies this process by distributing tips based on the hours worked by each employee.”

“Ensure you're familiar with the labor laws and minimum wage requirements in your state to stay compliant.”

“Download our Tip Pooling Calculator now to simplify your tip distribution process and ensure accurate allocation for your entire team!”

## What this calculator adds

- A legality check on every run: managers, supervisors and owners, back of house with or without a tip credit, card fees, against 29 CFR 531.54, Fact Sheet #15 and the rules of each state whose own site we read, with the rule quoted.
- Cent-exact shares that add up to the pool, with the leftover-cent rule stated.
- Free on its page; $0.002 per call over the API or MCP; $0.25 for a pay-run record (payroll CSV with the IRS occupation code, tip sheet to sign); never more than $4.99 a month.

A pool that splits by hours across front and back of house is allowed federally only when no tip credit is taken (29 CFR 531.54(d)); with a tip credit the cooks must come out.

## When MarketMan's tip pooling calculator is the better choice

For a quick hours-only split in a spreadsheet you keep offline, when everyone in the pool is clearly allowed to be there.

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.
