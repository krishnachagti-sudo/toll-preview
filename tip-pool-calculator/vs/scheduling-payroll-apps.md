# Homebase, 7shifts and Connecteam compared

Homebase charges $25 a month per location for Tip Manager, which pulls tips from your POS into timesheets; 7shifts includes tip pooling and payouts in its plans (its pricing page showed no prices to us on November 8, 2026); Connecteam has a free calculator by role, hours and percentage; this calculator costs nothing to split and $0.25 per pay-run record, at most $4.99 a month, and checks who may be in the pool.

## What scheduling and payroll apps does (read November 8, 2026)

**Homebase.** “$25/month per location”: “Homebase automatically pulls tips from your POS, calculates the tip pool, and adds it to timesheets.”

**7shifts.** “Create custom tip pools that fit your team's needs”; “You can send your pooled and non-pooled tips directly to employee debit accounts in seconds.” The 7shifts pricing page (https://www.7shifts.com/pricing/) showed no prices to our reader on 2026-11-08.

**Connecteam.** “Our free tip pooling calculator lets you distribute tips among your team based on roles, hours worked, and percentages with just a few clicks.”

None of the three pages read describes checking pool members against the FLSA or a state's rules.

## What this calculator adds

- A legality check on every run: managers, supervisors and owners, back of house with or without a tip credit, card fees, against 29 CFR 531.54, Fact Sheet #15 and the rules of each state whose own site we read, with the rule quoted.
- Cent-exact shares that add up to the pool, with the leftover-cent rule stated.
- Free on its page; $0.002 per call over the API or MCP; $0.25 for a pay-run record (payroll CSV with the IRS occupation code, tip sheet to sign); never more than $4.99 a month.

A month for one location, from the prices read.

| Pay runs a month | Homebase Tip Manager | This calculator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | $25.00 | $1.00 |
| 9 | $25.00 | $2.25 |
| 30 | $25.00 | $4.99 (the monthly cap) |

## When scheduling and payroll apps is the better choice

When pools recur every shift and you want them tied to schedules, time clocks, payouts and payroll in one app, and you already pay for that app.

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.
