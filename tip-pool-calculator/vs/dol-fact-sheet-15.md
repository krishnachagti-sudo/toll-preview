# DOL Fact Sheet #15 and this calculator

DOL Fact Sheet #15 is the authority on the federal rules: a $2.13 cash wage and $5.12 tip credit, pools limited to customarily tipped staff with a tip credit, no managers or supervisors ever, and card fees capped at the processor's percentage; the calculator applies each of those lines to your staff list and quotes the line it used.

## What DOL Fact Sheet #15 does (read November 8, 2026)

| Fact Sheet #15 says | The calculator |
| --- | --- |
| “An employer that takes a tip credit can require tipped employees to contribute tips only to a tip pool which is limited to employees in occupations in which they customarily and regularly receive tips, such as waiters, bellhops, counter personnel (who serve customers), bussers, and service bartenders.” | Flags non-tipped roles as not allowed when you tick the tip credit box. |
| “an employer that implements a nontraditional tip pool may require tipped employees, such as servers, to share tips with non-tipped employees, such as dishwashers and cooks, but only if all workers receive a direct cash wage of at least the federal minimum wage.” | Allows them with no tip credit, with a note that everyone must get the full minimum wage in cash. |
| “Business owners who own at least a bona fide 20 percent equity interest in the enterprise in which they are employed and who are actively engaged in its management are also managers and supervisors who may not keep employees' tips.” | Flags an owner line as not allowed. |
| “the employer cannot reduce the amount of tips paid to the employee by any amount greater than the transactional fee charged by the credit card company, regardless of whether or not it takes a tip credit.” | Takes off only the percentage you enter, on the card tips only. |
| “When state law differs from the federal FLSA, an employer must comply with the standard most protective to employees.” | Adds the state's own rules where its site was read, and says when it was not. |

## What this calculator adds

- A legality check on every run: managers, supervisors and owners, back of house with or without a tip credit, card fees, against 29 CFR 531.54, Fact Sheet #15 and the rules of each state whose own site we read, with the rule quoted.
- Cent-exact shares that add up to the pool, with the leftover-cent rule stated.
- Free on its page; $0.002 per call over the API or MCP; $0.25 for a pay-run record (payroll CSV with the IRS occupation code, tip sheet to sign); never more than $4.99 a month.

## When DOL Fact Sheet #15 is the better choice

Always, when you need the authority itself for a dispute or an audit: read the DOL page. The calculator is a working aid, not legal advice.

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.
