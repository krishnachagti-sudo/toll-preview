# Toast Tips Manager compared

Toast Tips Manager splits a pool inside Toast by points (optionally times hours) or by job percentages, and returns an unworked job's percentage to the contributor; its help article (read November 8, 2026) does not describe checking whether a job may legally be in the pool, which is what this calculator adds, along with a free split for anyone not on Toast.

## What Toast Tips Manager does (read November 8, 2026)

“When more than one recipient job is in a tip pool, Toast Tips Manager splits the contribution among those jobs using either points or percentages.”

“With points, that share is redistributed among the other recipients who are clocked in. With percentages, the share returns to the contributor who originally earned the tip.”

“You cannot assign more than 10 points to an individual job, but you can use decimals.”

The Toast tip pooling calculator page (https://pos.toasttab.com/resources/tip-pooling-calculator) answered 403 to our request on November 8, 2026, so nothing about it is stated here. Prices for Tips Manager were not found on the pages read.

## What this calculator adds

- A legality check on every run: managers, supervisors and owners, back of house with or without a tip credit, card fees, against 29 CFR 531.54, Fact Sheet #15 and the rules of each state whose own site we read, with the rule quoted.
- Cent-exact shares that add up to the pool, with the leftover-cent rule stated.
- Free on its page; $0.002 per call over the API or MCP; $0.25 for a pay-run record (payroll CSV with the IRS occupation code, tip sheet to sign); never more than $4.99 a month.

Toast's own worked example comes out the same here, to the cent: see [how to split tips on Toast](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/tip-pool-calculator/on/toast/).

## When Toast Tips Manager is the better choice

When your tips are already in Toast: its distribution feeds Toast payroll and tip adjustments with no re-entry. Use a separate check only to decide who may be in the pool.

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.
