# Square tip pooling compared

Square's tip pooling, on its Plus and Premium plans, splits card tips per transaction among staff clocked in, by hours over a workday or week, or by job percentages, and leaves managers out only if you turn off their Tip eligible toggle (read November 8, 2026); this calculator checks that rule, and the others, on every run.

## What Square tip pooling does (read November 8, 2026)

“Square Shifts Plus, Square for Retail Premium, Square for Restaurants Premium, and Square Appointments Premium subscribers”

“Transaction tips are split equally among all tip-eligible team members clocked in at the time of the transaction.”

“Transaction tips from sales are split among tip-eligible team members based on their hours worked. You can specify whether to split tips based on a 24-hour workday or across a 7-day workweek.”

“Tips are split by a user-defined percentage per transaction, at the end of each workday, or at the end of each workweek among tip-eligible jobs. Tip-eligible team members with the same job will receive tips based on hours worked, and tip percentage amounts assigned to each job must add up to 100%.”

“For example, if you don’t want a manager to receive tips, you’ll need to toggle off Tip eligible in their team member profile from your Square Dashboard.”

## What this calculator adds

- A legality check on every run: managers, supervisors and owners, back of house with or without a tip credit, card fees, against 29 CFR 531.54, Fact Sheet #15 and the rules of each state whose own site we read, with the rule quoted.
- Cent-exact shares that add up to the pool, with the leftover-cent rule stated.
- Free on its page; $0.002 per call over the API or MCP; $0.25 for a pay-run record (payroll CSV with the IRS occupation code, tip sheet to sign); never more than $4.99 a month.

## When Square tip pooling is the better choice

When your tips are collected in Square and you want the split posted to staff and payroll automatically, on a plan you already pay for.

> Not legal or tax advice. Shares are arithmetic on the hours, points and roles shown; the check applies the quoted rules to those facts. Whether someone is a manager or supervisor, or customarily tipped, depends on their duties. State rules are shown only for states whose own site was read; city rules are not covered.
