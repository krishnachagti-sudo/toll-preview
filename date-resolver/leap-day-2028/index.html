Leap day 2028: February 29 and the dates around it

February 29, 2028 is a Tuesday, 424 days after January 1, 2027. One year after it resolves to Wednesday, February 28, 2029, because 2029 has no February 29, and 365 days after February 28, 2028 is Tuesday, February 27, 2029, a day short of the same date a year on.

What is one year after February 29?

The date resolver keeps the day of the month and, when the target month is shorter, uses its last day: Wednesday, February 28, 2029. That is also how it treats January 31 plus one month. Another convention uses March 1 for an anniversary of February 29, so for a birthday, a lease or a legal deadline, use what the document or the law says and write the date down.

Questions

Is 2028 a leap year?

Yes. February 29, 2028 is a Tuesday, and 2028 has 366 days. A year divisible by 4 is a leap year, except years divisible by 100 that are not divisible by 400, so 2100 is not one.

What is one year after February 29, 2028?

Wednesday, February 28, 2029. The day of the month is kept when the month has it, otherwise the month's last day is used. Another convention uses March 1; when it matters, the document decides.

When is the next leap day after 2028?

Sunday, February 29, 2032.

Is 365 days the same as one year?

Not across a leap day: 365 days after February 28, 2028 is Tuesday, February 27, 2029, and 366 days after it is Wednesday, February 28, 2029.
