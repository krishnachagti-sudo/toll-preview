# What date is 120 days from today?

120 days from today, Monday, November 9, 2026 (today's date in New York, US Eastern time), is Tuesday, March 9, 2027. Counted from tomorrow it is Wednesday, March 10, 2027.

- Date: March 9, 2027
- Weekday: Tuesday
- ISO week: 2027-W10

## How it is counted

Today is day 0 and tomorrow is day 1, so the start day is not counted, as in a spreadsheet's TODAY()+N. Every calendar day counts: weekends and holidays included. For working days only, use business days: "[10 business days from today](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/date-resolver/?text=10+business+days+from+today&ref=2026-11-07)" in the [date resolver](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/date-resolver/) skips weekends and US federal holidays and names each one.

120 days is 17 weeks and 1 day, so the answer always falls on the weekday after the start's.

> "Today" is the date in New York, US Eastern time; this page turns over at midnight there. Elsewhere, put your own date in the tool.

## Questions

### Does 120 days from today count today?

No. Today is day 0, so the count starts with tomorrow as day 1, the way a spreadsheet adds TODAY()+120.

### How many weeks is 120 days?

120 days is 17 weeks and 1 day, so the date is on the weekday after the start's.

### What is 120 days from tomorrow?

One day later than 120 days from today: the same as 121 days from today.
