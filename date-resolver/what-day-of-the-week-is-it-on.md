# What day of the week is it on?

December 25, 2027 is a Saturday, and January 1, 2028 is a Saturday. A date falls one weekday later each year, or two after a February 29: Christmas is on a Friday in 2026, Saturday in 2027, Monday in 2028, Tuesday in 2029.

- December 25, 2027: Saturday
- February 29, 2028: Tuesday

## How the weekday moves from year to year

| Year | December 25 |
| --- | --- |
| 2026 | Friday |
| 2027 | Saturday |
| 2028 | Monday |
| 2029 | Tuesday |

365 days is 52 weeks and 1 day, so the weekday moves on by one. The leap day of February 29, 2028 adds a second day, which is why Christmas jumps from Saturday in 2027 to Monday in 2028.

## Before a date goes into an email

- Write the weekday with the date ("Friday, December 24, 2027"): a mismatch shows the mistake to the reader.
- Check the pair in a calendar or the tool, never from memory.
- With a phrase such as "next Friday", say which date you mean.

## Questions

### What day of the week is December 25, 2027?

A Saturday. As a federal holiday it is observed on Friday, December 24, 2027.

### Why does a date move one weekday each year?

A common year has 365 days, 52 weeks and 1 day, so a date falls one weekday later the next year; when February 29 comes in between, two weekdays later.

### Can a chatbot tell me the weekday of a date?

It can be wrong: a language model predicts text and does not consult a calendar. Check the weekday with a calendar or a tool before you send a date.
