# What date is 90 days from today?

- Date: February 6, 2027
- Weekday: Saturday
- ISO week: 2027-W05

## How it is counted

Today is day 0 and tomorrow is day 1, so the start day is not counted, as in a spreadsheet's TODAY()+N. Every calendar day counts: weekends and holidays included. For working days only, use business days: "[10 business days from today](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/date-resolver/?text=10+business+days+from+today&ref=2026-11-07)" in the [date resolver](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/date-resolver/) skips weekends and US federal holidays and names each one.

90 days is 12 weeks and 6 days, so the answer always falls on the weekday before the start's.

> "Today" is the date in New York, US Eastern time; this page turns over at midnight there. Elsewhere, put your own date in the tool.

## Questions

### Does 90 days from today count today?

No. Today is day 0, so the count starts with tomorrow as day 1, the way a spreadsheet adds TODAY()+90.

### How many weeks is 90 days?

90 days is 12 weeks and 6 days, so the date is on the weekday before the start's.

### What is 90 days from tomorrow?

One day later than 90 days from today: the same as 91 days from today.
