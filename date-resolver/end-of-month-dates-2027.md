# The end of every month in 2027

In 2027, 6 of the 12 months end on a day that is not a US business day; the last business day of December 2027 is Thursday, December 30, 2027, because Friday, December 31 is the observed New Year's Day holiday.

- Months ending off a business day: 6
- Last business day of 2027: December 30, 2027

## Every month of 2027

US business days skip weekends and federal holidays as OPM observes them.

## What "end of next month" means

The date resolver reads "end of next month" as the last calendar day of the month after the reference date: said on January 15, 2027, that is Sunday, February 28, 2027. Add "business" ("last business day of next month") for the last working day instead.

## Questions

### What is the last business day of December 2027?

Thursday, December 30, 2027. December 31, 2027 is a Friday, but it is the federal holiday observed for New Year's Day 2028, which falls on a Saturday.

### Which months of 2027 end on a weekend?

January, February, July, October.

### How do I get the last day of a month in a spreadsheet?

EOMONTH(date, 0) in Excel and Google Sheets returns the last day of that date's month; EOMONTH(date, 1) the last day of the next month.
