# 30, 60 or 90 days notice vs 1, 2 or 3 months

Thirty days and one month are different notice periods: notice given on January 31, 2027 ends Tuesday, March 2, 2027 counted as 30 days but Sunday, February 28, 2027 counted as one month; 90 days ends Saturday, May 1, 2027 and 3 months ends Friday, April 30, 2027.

- 30 days after Jan 31, 2027: March 2, 2027
- 1 month after Jan 31, 2027: February 28, 2027
- 60 days / 2 months: Thu, Apr 1, 2027 / Wed, Mar 31, 2027

## Days and months, five start dates

Calendar days count every day, weekends and holidays included. Months keep the day of the month, or use the month's last day when it is shorter.

## Why the two differ

Months are 28 to 31 days long, so "one month" is shorter than 30 days when February is in the way and longer when a 31-day month is. A month from the 31st lands on the last day of a shorter month. Over three months the gap can be a day or two either way.

## Before you send a notice

- Read how the contract states the period: days, business days or months.
- Find day zero: the day notice is given, sent or received, as the contract says.
- Count, then check whether a weekend or holiday end date moves.
- Write the end date, with its weekday, in the notice itself.

> This page shows how dates count. It is not legal advice: your contract, a statute or a court rule decides a real deadline.

## Questions

### Is 30 days notice the same as one month?

No. Given on January 31, 2027, 30 days ends on Tuesday, March 2, 2027 and one month ends on Sunday, February 28, 2027, 2 days apart.

### Does the day notice is given count?

In the date resolver, no: day 1 is the day after. Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 6(a)(1) also excludes the day of the event for court deadlines, but your contract or the law that applies decides.

### What if the last day is a weekend?

A calendar-day count can end on a Saturday or Sunday. Whether it moves to the next business day depends on the contract or rule. The tool can roll it forward (roll: following) when yours says so.

### How do I count a notice period in business days?

Add business days, skipping weekends and the holidays your contract names; the business days tool has a guide to that.
