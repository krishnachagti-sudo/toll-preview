How it is counted

Today is day 0 and tomorrow is day 1, so the start day is not counted, as in a spreadsheet's TODAY()+N. Every calendar day counts: weekends and holidays included. For working days only, use business days: "10 business days from today" in the date resolver skips weekends and US federal holidays and names each one.

60 days is 8 weeks and 4 days, so the answer always falls on 4 weekdays after the start's (counting round the week).

"Today" is the date in New York, US Eastern time; this page turns over at midnight there. Elsewhere, put your own date in the tool.

Questions

Does 60 days from today count today?

No. Today is day 0, so the count starts with tomorrow as day 1, the way a spreadsheet adds TODAY()+60.

How many weeks is 60 days?

60 days is 8 weeks and 4 days, so the date is on 4 weekdays after the start's (counting round the week).

What is 60 days from tomorrow?

One day later than 60 days from today: the same as 61 days from today.
