- Date: January 31, 2027

## How it is counted

Today is day 0 and tomorrow is day 1, so the start day is not counted, as in a spreadsheet's TODAY()+N. A week is 7 calendar days. For working days only, use business days: "[10 business days from today](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/date-resolver/?text=10+business+days+from+today&ref=2026-11-07)" in the [date resolver](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/date-resolver/) skips weekends and US federal holidays and names each one.

84 days is 12 weeks, so the answer always falls on the same weekday as the start.

> "Today" is the date in New York, US Eastern time; this page turns over at midnight there. Elsewhere, put your own date in the tool.

## Questions

### Does 12 weeks from today count today?

No. Today is day 0, so the count starts with tomorrow as day 1, the way a spreadsheet adds TODAY()+84.

### How many weeks is 84 days?

84 days is 12 weeks, so the date is on the same weekday as the start.

### What is 12 weeks from tomorrow?

One day later than 12 weeks from today: the same as 85 days from today.
