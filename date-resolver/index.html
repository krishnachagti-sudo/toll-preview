Dates, per phrase

What date is that, exactly?

Type a phrase such as "90 days from today", "10 business days after next Friday", "end of next month" or "first Monday of March 2027". You get one exact date with its weekday, the steps taken, every holiday skipped and the assumptions used. When a phrase can mean two dates, such as "next Friday" said on a Monday, you get both.

Days, business days, weeks, months, quarters and years; ago, from, after, before, in; weekdays; start or end of a week, month, quarter or year; dates like 2027-03-03 or March 3, 2027.

"Today" in a phrase means this date. It starts as today on your device's clock.

More options

The answer always shows the other reading too when the two differ.

Company closures or state holidays, skipped like public holidays. Dates like 2026-12-24.

How phrases are counted

  • The start day is not counted. 1 day after Friday is Saturday; 1 business day after Friday is Monday, as Excel's WORKDAY counts.
  • Business days skip Saturdays, Sundays and the holidays you pick. US holidays are the 11 federal holidays of 5 U.S.C. 6103 on the days OPM observes them: a Saturday holiday is the Friday before, a Sunday holiday the Monday after.
  • Months, quarters and years keep the day of the month, or the month's last day when it is shorter: January 31 plus 1 month is February 28 (29 in a leap year).
  • "Friday" and "this Friday" are the coming Friday, or the reference date if it is a Friday. "Next Friday" is the nearest Friday after the reference date unless you choose otherwise; the Friday of next week is shown as the other reading when it is a different day.
  • A date with no year, like "March 3", is the next one on or after the reference date. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.
  • Times of day are not read: this works in whole calendar dates. A phrase it cannot read is refused, never guessed, and a refused phrase is never charged.

Price

  • Resolved phrase: $0.0005 per phrase for the first 10,000 each calendar month, then $0.0002 per phrase.

Without an account you get 5 free runs a day. Accounts get a small free allowance each month. A list of up to 50 phrases in one call costs the same per phrase.

Dates are computed from the reference date and holiday calendar shown. Contracts, courts and carriers can count differently (court days, a start day counted, state or bank holidays), so check the rule your deadline comes from. This is not legal advice. Sources: OPM federal holidays (read November 7, 2026), 5 U.S.C. 6103 and the Business days holiday calendars.
