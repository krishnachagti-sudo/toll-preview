# Intermittent FMLA leave calculator

Intermittent FMLA leave is taken hour for hour against 12 times the employee's normal workweek: on a 30-hour week the entitlement is 360 hours, and a reduced schedule of 2 hours less each weekday from September 7 to October 30, 2026 uses 80 hours, leaving 280 on November 10, 2026.

- Entitlement on 30 hours: 360
- Used: 80

## The rules

- Only the amount of leave actually taken counts; the actual workweek is the basis (825.205(b)(1)).
- A part-time or variable schedule is counted pro rata: 10 hours off a 30-hour week is one-third of a week.
- Overtime the employee would have been required to work but cannot may count (825.205(c)).
- A holiday inside a full week of FMLA leave still counts; a holiday during shorter leave does not, unless the employee was scheduled to work it (825.200(h)).

> Worked out from the facts shown under 29 CFR Part 825. State family and medical leave laws are not covered; an employee who is not eligible under the FMLA may still have leave under state law. Not legal advice.

## Questions

### How is intermittent FMLA leave counted?

Only the leave actually taken is counted, against the employee's actual workweek: 8 hours off a 40-hour week is one-fifth of a week (29 CFR 825.205(b)(1)).

### What smallest increment can an employer charge?

No more than the shortest period it uses for other leave, and never more than one hour (29 CFR 825.205(a)(1)).

### What if the schedule varies week to week?

Use the weekly average of the hours scheduled over the 12 months before the leave began, including hours of any leave taken (29 CFR 825.205(b)(3)).
