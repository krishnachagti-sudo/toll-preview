# The five-business-day FMLA notices

The eligibility and rights notice is due within five business days of the leave request, and the designation notice within five business days of having enough information: a request on Monday, November 23, 2026 must be answered by Tue, Dec 1, 2026 (Thanksgiving Day skipped), and one on Monday, December 21 by Tue, Dec 29, 2026 (Christmas Day skipped).

- Request Nov 23: Tue, Dec 1, 2026
- Request Dec 21: Tue, Dec 29, 2026

## The two notices

- Eligibility notice, with the rights and responsibilities notice: within five business days of the request, or of learning the leave may be for an FMLA reason (825.300(b), (c)). Optional form WH-381.
- Designation notice: within five business days of having enough information to decide whether the leave qualifies (825.300(d)). Optional form WH-382. If paid leave will run with it, say so then.

## Questions

### How long does an employer have to respond to an FMLA request?

Five business days to give the eligibility notice (with the rights and responsibilities notice), absent extenuating circumstances, and five business days to designate the leave once it has enough information (29 CFR 825.300(b)-(d)).

### Can an employer wait to designate leave as FMLA?

The designation notice is due within five business days of having enough information to decide, for example after receiving a certification (29 CFR 825.300(d)(1)). Failing to give notices can be interference with FMLA rights (825.300(e)).
