# 12 weeks of FMLA in hours

12 weeks of FMLA leave is 12 times the employee's normal workweek: 480 hours on a 40-hour week, 432 on 36 hours and 384 on 32 hours; 26 weeks of military caregiver leave on a 40-hour week is 1,040 hours.

- 40 hours a week: 480
- 36 hours a week: 432
- 32 hours a week: 384

An employee does not accrue FMLA leave at an hourly rate: the hours depend on the hours the employee would have worked but for the leave (825.205(b)(1)). A permanent schedule change made before the notice, for reasons other than FMLA, sets the new weekly hours (825.205(b)(2)).

> Worked out from the facts shown under 29 CFR Part 825. State family and medical leave laws are not covered; an employee who is not eligible under the FMLA may still have leave under state law. Not legal advice.

## Questions

### How many hours is 12 weeks of FMLA?

12 times the employee's normal workweek: 480 hours for 40 hours a week, 432 for 36 hours, 384 for 32 hours.

### How many hours of FMLA does a part-time employee get?

12 times their normal weekly hours, so 240 hours on 20 hours a week. Leave is counted pro rata (29 CFR 825.205(b)(1)).

### What if the schedule varies?

Use the weekly average of hours scheduled over the 12 months before the leave began, including any leave taken (29 CFR 825.205(b)(3)).
