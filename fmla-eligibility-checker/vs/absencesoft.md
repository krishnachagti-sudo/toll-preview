# AbsenceSoft alternative for small HR teams

AbsenceSoft sells leave and accommodation management software for HR teams and administrators, priced by custom proposal after a demo and tracking more than 200 federal and state laws (its own site, read November 8, 2026); this checker covers the federal FMLA only, at no charge on the page, $0.01 per API check and $1.00 for an employee's leave record with 12 months of updates.

## When AbsenceSoft is better

When you manage a large number of leaves at once, need state family and medical leave laws and accommodations tracked with federal leave, or want case management, workflows and employee self-service in one place.

> Worked out from the facts shown under 29 CFR Part 825. State family and medical leave laws are not covered; an employee who is not eligible under the FMLA may still have leave under state law. Not legal advice.
