DOL elaws FMLA Advisor vs this checker

The DOL elaws FMLA Advisor is the Wage and Hour Division's free question-and-answer guide to coverage, eligibility and entitlements, labelled general guidance; this checker runs the same tests on an employee's own dates, hours and sites, for example finding a teleworker assigned from headquarters eligible with 300 employees within 75 miles and the notice due November 13, 2026.

When the Advisor is better

When you want official guidance in plain questions and answers, straight from the agency that enforces the law, or are reading about leave in general rather than deciding one employee's case. The Advisor says it was updated for the 2013 final rule.

Worked out from the facts shown under 29 CFR Part 825. State family and medical leave laws are not covered; an employee who is not eligible under the FMLA may still have leave under state law. Not legal advice.
