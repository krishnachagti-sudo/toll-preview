# The FMLA 50 employees within 75 miles rule

Count every employee on the payroll whose worksite is within 75 surface miles of the employee's worksite, measured when the employee gives notice: at this plant that is 49 (the plant, the warehouse 9 miles away and the sales office 74 miles away), one short of 50, because the second plant is 76 miles away and the Toronto staff work outside the United States.

- Within 75 miles: 49
- Needed: 50

Total within 75 miles: 49. The employee is not eligible: You are employed at a worksite where the employer has 49 employees within 75 miles of Plant, fewer than 50.

## What a worksite is

- A single location, or a group of contiguous locations such as a campus or industrial park (825.111(a)).
- Buildings in reasonable proximity, used for the same purpose and sharing staff and equipment are one worksite (825.111(a)(1)).
- Employees with no fixed worksite, such as drivers and salespeople, belong to the site they are assigned to as a home base, report to or get work from (825.111(a)(2)).
- [A home is never a worksite: see remote employees.](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/remote-employees/)

## Who is counted

Every employee maintained on the payroll, full-time or part-time, including those on paid or unpaid leave whom the employer expects back (825.105(c), 825.111(c)). Employees outside the United States and its territories are not counted (825.105(b)).

> Worked out from the facts shown under 29 CFR Part 825. State family and medical leave laws are not covered; an employee who is not eligible under the FMLA may still have leave under state law. Not legal advice.

## Questions

### How are the 75 miles measured?

In surface miles over public streets, roads, highways and waterways, by the shortest route from the facility where the employee needing leave works (29 CFR 825.111(b)). Without surface transportation, the most used mode, such as airline miles.

### When is the 50-employee count made?

When the employee gives notice of the need for leave. A later drop below 50 does not end eligibility for that notice (29 CFR 825.110(e)).

### Are part-time employees counted?

Yes. Employees maintained on the payroll are counted, part-time or full-time, and so are employees on leave the employer expects back (29 CFR 825.105(c), 825.111(c)).
