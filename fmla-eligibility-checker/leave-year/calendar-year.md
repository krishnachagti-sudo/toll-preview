# FMLA calendar year vs rolling 12 months

Under the calendar-year method an employee on leave 40 hours a week from October 5, 2026 to March 26, 2027 has 960 hours protected (24 weeks: 12 in 2026 and 12 more from January 1, 2027), while the rolling method protects 480 hours (12 weeks).

- Calendar year: 960
- Rolling: 480

## Questions

### Can an employee take 24 weeks of FMLA in a row?

Under the calendar-year or a fixed leave year, yes: 12 weeks at the end of one year and 12 at the start of the next (29 CFR 825.200(c)). Under the rolling method, no.

### Which FMLA method applies if the employer never chose one?

The one that gives the employee the most beneficial outcome (29 CFR 825.200(e)).

### How does an employer change its FMLA method?

With at least 60 days' notice to all employees, and employees keep the full benefit of 12 weeks under whichever method is better for them during the change (29 CFR 825.200(d)(1)).
