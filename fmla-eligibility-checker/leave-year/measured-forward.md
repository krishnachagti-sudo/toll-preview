# The FMLA measured-forward method

Under the measured-forward method the 12-month period starts on the first day of FMLA leave, January 12, 2026, and ends January 11, 2027: on November 10, 2026 this employee has used 400 hours and has 80 left, and leave starting January 18, 2027 opens a new period with the full 480 hours.

- Used: 400
- Left: 80
- New period: Mon, Jan 18, 2027

## Questions

### When does the next measured-forward period start?

The first time FMLA leave is taken after the previous 12-month period ends (29 CFR 825.200(c)), not on the anniversary itself.

### Which method does military caregiver leave use?

Always a single 12-month period measured forward from the first day of that leave, whatever method the employer uses for other FMLA leave, with up to 26 workweeks (29 CFR 825.127(e)(1), 825.200(f)).
