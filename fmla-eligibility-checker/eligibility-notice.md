# The FMLA eligibility notice

The eligibility notice is due within five business days of the request: for a request on November 9, 2026 that is November 17, 2026, skipping Veterans Day; for this rehire the reason to state is: You have been employed by the employer for 8 months as of November 16, 2026, less than 12 months.

## The deadline

Five business days run from the day after the request. Part 825 does not define business days; the checker counts Monday to Friday less US federal holidays, and you can add the days your office is closed.

Eligibility is decided at the first leave for each reason in the 12-month period. If the employee's eligibility changes for a later reason, a new notice is due within five business days (825.300(b)(3)).

> Worked out from the facts shown under 29 CFR Part 825. State family and medical leave laws are not covered; an employee who is not eligible under the FMLA may still have leave under state law. Not legal advice.

## Questions

### How long does an employer have to give the FMLA eligibility notice?

Five business days from the employee's request, or from learning the leave may be for an FMLA reason, absent extenuating circumstances (29 CFR 825.300(b)(1)).

### What must the notice say if the employee is not eligible?

At least one reason why, such as the months employed, the hours of service in the 12 months, or whether 50 employees work within 75 miles (29 CFR 825.300(b)(2)).

### Which DOL form is the eligibility notice?

Optional form WH-381, the Notice of Eligibility and Rights and Responsibilities. The notice may also be given orally or in another written form.
