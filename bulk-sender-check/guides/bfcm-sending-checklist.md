# Black Friday sending checklist

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026 and Cyber Monday Monday, November 30, 2026 (in 2027: November 26, 2027 and November 29, 2027); check a real message from each stream by Friday, October 30, 2026, fix authentication and one-click unsubscribe by November 6, 2026, and raise volume gradually from November 13, 2026, because Gmail and Yahoo judge spam rate (below 0.3%) as well as the headers.

- Black Friday 2026: November 27, 2026
- Cyber Monday 2026: November 30, 2026
- Black Friday 2027: November 26, 2027

## 2026 countdown

Thanksgiving November 26, 2026

| Date | When | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friday, October 30, 2026 | 28 days before | Send a real message from every stream (newsletter, promotions, receipts) to Gmail, Yahoo and Outlook.com addresses and run each copy through the check. |
| Friday, November 6, 2026 | 21 days before | Fix what failed: DMARC record and alignment, DKIM key of 1024 bits or more, one-click unsubscribe signed by DKIM, PTR. |
| Friday, November 13, 2026 | 14 days before | Start raising volume gradually; Google says to increase volume slowly and avoid sudden spikes. Watch Postmaster Tools spam rate (keep it below 0.1%, never 0.3%). |
| Friday, November 20, 2026 | 7 days before | Remove bounced and long-inactive addresses; confirm unsubscribes are processed within 48 hours (Google) and 2 days (Yahoo). |
| Thursday, November 26, 2026 | 1 day before | Check the final campaign message itself. |

Google: since November 2025 Gmail is ramping up enforcement on non-compliant traffic, with temporary and permanent rejections. A holiday volume spike from a domain that fails a rule is the worst time to find out.

- [Google: Email sender guidelines, read November 8, 2026](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126?hl=en)
- [Google: Email sender guidelines FAQ, read November 8, 2026](https://support.google.com/a/answer/14229414?hl=en)
- [Yahoo Sender Hub: Sender Requirements & Recommendations, read November 8, 2026](https://senders.yahooinc.com/best-practices/)
- [Outlook.com Postmaster: Policies, Practices, and Guidelines, read November 8, 2026](https://substrate.office.com/ip-domain-management-snds/postmaster/Policies)

> Rules as published by Google, Yahoo and Microsoft and read on November 8, 2026. Meeting every rule does not guarantee the inbox; spam rate, volume and unsubscribe handling cannot be judged from one message.
