How to get the raw message, in each mailbox.

The rules

What is checked, and what cannot be.

The receiver's own verdict, then our own checks of the signature, the alignment and the unsubscribe headers.

What it does not cover

Spam rate, daily volume and unsubscribe speed, and Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace mailboxes. Passing is not a promise of the inbox.

Read the full list

Not from one message: spam rate (Google Postmaster Tools, Yahoo's Complaint Feedback Loop), daily volume and how fast you process unsubscribes.

Providers also use reputation and content, so passing every rule does not guarantee the inbox. Microsoft's rules cover Outlook.com consumer mailboxes (hotmail.com, live.com, outlook.com), not Microsoft 365 business mailboxes; Google's cover personal Gmail accounts, not Google Workspace.

DNS answers come from public resolvers at the time shown and may differ from when the message was sent. Private and internal names are never queried.

From the receiver

The topmost Authentication-Results header and its Received line.

Read the full rules

The topmost Authentication-Results header is the receiving server's own SPF, DKIM and DMARC verdict, and its Received line shows whether the hop used TLS.

Our own checks

DKIM verified with the key in DNS, DMARC alignment worked out, one-click unsubscribe checked to RFC 8058.

Read the full rules

Every DKIM signature is verified (RFC 6376) with the key in DNS, and the key length measured; DMARC alignment is worked out from the From: domain using the Public Suffix List (RFC 7489); one-click unsubscribe is checked to RFC 8058: exactly "List-Unsubscribe=One-Click", one HTTPS URI, and both headers signed by a DKIM signature that passes.

Rules as read
