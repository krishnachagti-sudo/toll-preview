# Illinois security deposit deadline

In Illinois a landlord must furnish the itemized statement of damage with paid receipts by Sunday, August 30, 2026, 30 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under 710/1(a).

- Days: 30 calendar days
- Clock starts: the later of the day the tenant vacated and the day the right of possession ended
- Sample due date: August 30, 2026

## What the statute says

710/1(a): "a lessor of residential real property, containing 5 or more units, who has received a security deposit from a lessee to secure the payment of rent or to compensate for damage to the leased premises may not withhold any part of that deposit as reimbursement for property damage unless the lessor has, within 30 days of the date that the lessee vacated the leased premises or within 30 days of the date the lessee's right of possession ends, whichever is later, furnished to the lessee, by personal delivery, by postmarked mail directed to his or her last known address, or by electronic mail to a verified electronic mail address provided by the lessee, an itemized statement of the damage allegedly caused to the leased premises and the estimated or actual cost for repairing or replacing each item on that statement, attaching the paid receipts, or copies thereof, for the repair or replacement."

## The sample move-out

- August 30, 2026: Furnish the itemized statement of damage with paid receipts (710/1(a))
- September 14, 2026: If no statement with receipts went out, return the full deposit (45 days after the tenant vacated) (710/1(a))

- City ordinances (for example Chicago's) can add rules; they were not checked.
- The statute does not say that mailing on the last day is enough, so plan for the tenant to have it by the due date.
- The due date falls on a Sunday. The statute does not move it to the next business day, and neither does this tool.

## Delivery

By personal delivery, by postmarked mail to the tenant's last known address, or by e-mail to a verified e-mail address the tenant provided.

## If the deadline is missed

On a court finding that the statement was refused or given in bad faith and the deposit not returned on time, twice the deposit due, plus court costs and attorney's fees (710/1(c)).

> "Upon a finding by a circuit court that a lessor has refused to supply the itemized statement required by this Section, or has supplied such statement in bad faith, and has failed or refused to return the amount of the security deposit due within the time limits provided, the lessor shall be liable for an amount equal to twice the amount of the security deposit due, together with court costs and reasonable attorney's fees."

## Questions

### How long does a landlord have to return a security deposit in Illinois?

30 days after the later of the day the tenant vacated and the day the right of possession ended (710/1(a)).

### What happens if a Illinois landlord misses the deadline?

On a court finding that the statement was refused or given in bad faith and the deposit not returned on time, twice the deposit due, plus court costs and attorney's fees (710/1(c)).

### How must the Illinois deposit statement be sent?

By personal delivery, by postmarked mail to the tenant's last known address, or by e-mail to a verified e-mail address the tenant provided.
