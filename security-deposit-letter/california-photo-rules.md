# California security deposit photo rules

California landlords must photograph the unit after the tenant leaves, for move-outs from April 1, 2025, and at move-in, for tenancies beginning on or after July 1, 2025, and send the photographs with any deduction for repairs or cleaning. A $260 mirror repair with no photographs fails 2 requirements in the tool's check; deductions of $125 or less for repairs and cleaning together are exempt from the documents rule.

## What the statute says

- **Move-out, from April 1, 2025**: "Beginning April 1, 2025, the landlord shall take photographs of the unit within a reasonable time after the possession of the unit is returned to the landlord, but prior to any repairs or cleanings for which the landlord will make a deduction from or claim against the security deposit pursuant to this section, and shall also take photographs of the unit within a reasonable time after such repairs or cleanings are completed."
- **Move-in, tenancies from July 1, 2025**: "For tenancies that begin on or after July 1, 2025, the landlord shall take photographs of the unit immediately before, or at the inception of, the tenancy."
- **With the statement**: "If a deduction is made for repairs or cleanings allowed by this section, the landlord shall provide photographs taken pursuant to subdivision (g), along with a written explanation of the cost of the allowable repairs or cleanings, as described in subparagraphs (A) to (C), inclusive."

## A worked check

Tenancy began August 1, 2025; moved out September 30, 2026; a $260 repair with an invoice, and no photographs taken. The tool flags:

- Deductions for repairs or cleaning need the photographs taken after the unit was returned (and after the work), sent with the statement. (§ 1950.5(g)(2), (h)(2)(D))
- This tenancy began on or after July 1, 2025, so the move-in photographs are required too. (§ 1950.5(g)(1))

The same move-out with a $40 repair instead: nothing missing, because the deductions for repairs and cleaning are $125 or less.

## Questions

### When did California start requiring move-out photos for deposits?

From April 1, 2025: "Beginning April 1, 2025, the landlord shall take photographs of the unit within a reasonable time after the possession of the unit is returned to the landlord, but prior to any repairs or cleanings for which the landlord will make a deduction from or claim against the security deposit pursuant to this section, and shall also take photographs of the unit within a reasonable time after such repairs or cleanings are completed."

### Do California landlords need move-in photos?

For tenancies that begin on or after July 1, 2025: "For tenancies that begin on or after July 1, 2025, the landlord shall take photographs of the unit immediately before, or at the inception of, the tenancy."

### Are photos needed for a small deduction?

No. The documents rule, photographs included, does not apply when the deductions for repairs and cleaning together do not exceed $125 (Civil Code § 1950.5(h)).
