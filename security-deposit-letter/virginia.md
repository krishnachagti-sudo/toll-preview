# Virginia security deposit deadline

In Virginia a landlord must give the written notice itemizing the deposit and deductions, with any amount due by Monday, September 14, 2026, 45 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under § 55.1-1226(A).

- Days: 45 calendar days
- Clock starts: the later of the tenancy ending and the day the tenant vacated
- Sample due date: September 14, 2026

## What the statute says

§ 55.1-1226(A): "The security deposit and any deductions, damages, and charges shall be itemized by the landlord in a written notice given to the tenant, together with any amount due to the tenant, within 45 days after the termination date of the tenancy or the date the tenant vacates the dwelling unit, whichever occurs last."

## The sample move-out

- September 14, 2026: Give the written notice itemizing the deposit and deductions, with any amount due (§ 55.1-1226(A))

- If damages exceed the deposit and need a third-party contractor, a written notice saying so within the 45 days gives 15 more days for the itemization (§ 55.1-1226(E)).
- The statute does not say that mailing on the last day is enough, so plan for the tenant to have it by the due date.
- Whether the Virginia Residential Landlord and Tenant Act covers your rental (§ 55.1-1201) was not checked.

## Delivery

A written notice itemizing the deposit and every deduction, with any amount due. With several tenants, one check payable to all of them, sent to a forwarding address one of them gave.

## If the deadline is missed

For a willful failure, the court orders the deposit returned with actual damages and reasonable attorney fees (§ 55.1-1226(E)).

> "If the landlord willfully fails to comply with this section, the court shall order the return of the security deposit to the tenant, together with actual damages and reasonable attorney fees"
