# Georgia security deposit deadline

In Georgia a landlord must return the deposit, with a written statement of the exact reasons for anything kept by Sunday, August 30, 2026, 30 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under § 44-7-34(a).

- Days: 30 calendar days
- Clock starts: the day the landlord obtained possession
- Sample due date: August 30, 2026

## What the statute says

§ 44-7-34(a): "Within 30 days after obtaining possession of the premises as provided in subsection (b) of Code Section 44-7-33, a landlord shall return to the tenant the full security deposit which was deposited with the landlord by the tenant."

## The sample move-out

- August 30, 2026: Return the deposit, with a written statement of the exact reasons for anything kept (§ 44-7-34(a))

- Mailing it first-class to the last known address complies (§ 44-7-34(a)).
- The due date falls on a Sunday. The statute does not move it to the next business day, and neither does this tool.
- Other sections of the article (44-7-30 to 44-7-37, including the move-out damage list of 44-7-33) were not read; check whether any of them changes your duties.

## Delivery

Mailing the statement and payment to the tenant's last known address by first-class mail complies with the section.

## If the deadline is missed

Three times the sum improperly withheld plus attorney's fees (only the sum itself for a proven bona fide error); missing the deadline forfeits the right to withhold for damages (§ 44-7-35(b), (c)).

> "Any landlord who fails to return any part of a security deposit which is required to be returned to a tenant pursuant to this article shall be liable to the tenant in the amount of three times the sum improperly withheld plus reasonable attorney's fees; provided, however, that the landlord shall be liable only for the sum erroneously withheld if the landlord shows by the preponderance of the evidence that the withholding was not intentional and resulted from a bona fide error which occurred in spite of the existence of procedures reasonably designed to avoid such errors."

> "The failure of a landlord to provide the lists and written statements within the time periods specified in Code Section 44-7-34 shall work a forfeiture of all the landlord's rights to withhold any portion of the security deposit or to bring an action against the tenant for damages to the premises."

## Questions

### How long does a landlord have to return a security deposit in Georgia?

### What happens if a Georgia landlord misses the deadline?

Three times the sum improperly withheld plus attorney's fees (only the sum itself for a proven bona fide error); missing the deadline forfeits the right to withhold for damages (§ 44-7-35(b), (c)).
