# No forwarding address: what happens to the deposit deadline

The clock still runs in most states, but the address changes what the landlord owes. In Texas the landlord need not refund or itemize until the tenant gives a written forwarding address; in Ohio and Pennsylvania a tenant who gives none loses some remedies; in California mail goes to the vacated unit; and in North Carolina the landlord holds the balance for at least 6 months. Of the 13 states covered, 6 address it in the text read here.

## What the tool says for Texas

Texas: refund the deposit with a written description and itemized list of deductions by Sunday, August 30, 2026, 30 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under § 92.103(a). § 92.107: the landlord need not refund or itemize until the tenant gives a written forwarding address; the tenant does not lose the deposit by not giving one.

## Questions

### Does the deposit deadline run if the tenant gives no forwarding address?

In most of the states covered, the statute starts the clock at move-out or the end of the tenancy whatever the address. Texas is the exception that matters: "The landlord is not obligated to return a tenant's security deposit or give the tenant a written description of damages and charges until the tenant gives the landlord a written statement of the tenant's forwarding address for the purpose of refunding the security deposit."

### Where does a California landlord mail the statement with no address?

To the vacated unit: "Any mailings to the tenant pursuant to this subdivision shall be sent to the address provided by the tenant. If the tenant does not provide an address, mailings pursuant to this subdivision shall be sent to the unit that has been vacated."

### What should a landlord do with no address?

Keep a record of the attempts made, send the statement to the address the statute names (the vacated unit or the last known address), keep proof of mailing, and hold any balance.
