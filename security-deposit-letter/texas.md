# Texas security deposit deadline

In Texas a landlord must refund the deposit with a written description and itemized list of deductions by Sunday, August 30, 2026, 30 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under § 92.103(a).

- Days: 30 calendar days
- Clock starts: the day the tenant surrendered the premises
- Sample due date: August 30, 2026

## What the statute says

§ 92.103(a): "the landlord shall refund a security deposit to the tenant on or before the 30th day after the date the tenant surrenders the premises."

## The sample move-out

- August 30, 2026: Refund the deposit with a written description and itemized list of deductions (§ 92.103(a))

- § 92.107: the landlord need not refund or itemize until the tenant gives a written forwarding address; the tenant does not lose the deposit by not giving one.
- Mail counts as on time when postmarked on or before the due date (§ 92.1041).
- The due date falls on a Sunday. The statute does not move it to the next business day, and neither does this tool.

## Delivery

A refund or accounting placed in the US mail and postmarked on or before the due date is presumed on time. The landlord need not refund or itemize until the tenant gives a written forwarding address.

## If the deadline is missed

For bad-faith retention, $100 plus three times the amount wrongfully withheld, plus the tenant's attorney's fees; bad faith is presumed after the 30th day; a bad-faith failure to itemize forfeits the right to withhold (§ 92.109).

> "A landlord who in bad faith retains a security deposit in violation of this subchapter is liable for an amount equal to the sum of $100, three times the portion of the deposit wrongfully withheld, and the tenant's reasonable attorney's fees in a suit to recover the deposit."

> "A landlord who in bad faith does not provide a written description and itemized list of damages and charges in violation of this subchapter: (1) forfeits the right to withhold any portion of the security deposit or to bring suit against the tenant for damages to the premises; and"

> "A landlord who fails either to return a security deposit or to provide a written description and itemization of deductions on or before the 30th day after the date the tenant surrenders possession is presumed to have acted in bad faith."
