# Pennsylvania security deposit deadline

In Pennsylvania a landlord must provide the written list of damages with payment of the balance by Sunday, August 30, 2026, 30 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under sec. 512(a).

- Days: 30 calendar days
- Clock starts: the earlier of the lease ending and the surrender and acceptance of the premises
- Sample due date: August 30, 2026

## What the statute says

sec. 512(a): "Every landlord shall within thirty days of termination of a lease or upon surrender and acceptance of the leasehold premises, whichever first occurs, provide a tenant with a written list of any damages to the leasehold premises for which the landlord claims the tenant is liable."

## The sample move-out

- August 30, 2026: Provide the written list of damages with payment of the balance (sec. 512(a))

- A tenant who gives no new address in writing at the end of the lease relieves the landlord of liability under section 512 (sec. 512(e)).
- The statute does not say that mailing on the last day is enough, so plan for the tenant to have it by the due date.
- The due date falls on a Sunday. The statute does not move it to the next business day, and neither does this tool.

## Delivery

The statute sets no delivery method; the list must be delivered with payment of the balance within the 30 days. A tenant who gives no new address in writing relieves the landlord of liability under the section.

## If the deadline is missed

A late list forfeits the right to withhold for damages; failing to pay the balance in 30 days makes the landlord liable for double the amount by which the deposit exceeds the damages a court finds (at most twice the deposit when it finds none) (sec. 512(b), (c)).

> "Any landlord who fails to provide a written list within thirty days as required in subsection (a), above, shall forfeit all rights to withhold any portion of sums held in escrow, including any unpaid interest thereon, or to bring suit against the tenant for damages to the leasehold premises."

> "If the landlord fails to pay the tenant the difference between the sum deposited, including any unpaid interest thereon, and the actual damages to the leasehold premises caused by the tenant within thirty days after termination of the lease or surrender and acceptance of the leasehold premises, the landlord shall be liable in assumpsit to double the amount by which the sum deposited in escrow, including any unpaid interest thereon, exceeds the actual damages to the leasehold premises caused by the tenant as determined by any court of record"
