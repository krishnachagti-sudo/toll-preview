## Please read

- Not legal advice. We are not a law firm, and this tool does not review your case.
- Rules are as of the statute reading date shown on each result; the rule version is printed on every letter and proof record.
- Check that the state statute applies to your rental (unit counts, exemptions, local adoption) and whether a city ordinance adds rules; city rules were not checked.
- The due date depends on the dates you enter (move-out, end of tenancy, the tenant's demand or forwarding address). Wrong dates give a wrong due date.
- You decide what is damage beyond normal wear and tear and whether a cost is reasonable; the tool only checks that the statute's required documents and words are there.
- Penalty figures are the statute's maximums on the amounts you entered. Bad faith, willfulness and actual awards are for a court.

## Questions

### Which state has the shortest security deposit deadline?

Of the states covered here, New York: 14 days after the tenant vacates (Gen. Oblig. Law § 7-108(1-a)(e)). Arizona's is 14 business days, which runs longer on the calendar.

### Why are some states missing?

A state is listed only after its statute text has been read and quoted; the tool refuses the others rather than guess.
