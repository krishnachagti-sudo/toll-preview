# The itemized security deposit statement

An itemized deposit statement lists the deposit held, each deduction with what it was for and what it cost, and the refund due, sent by the deadline. In a California example with a $2,400.00 deposit, $1,520.00 of deductions leaves $880.00 to refund by November 5, 2026, with the invoice, the hours and rate for the landlord's own repair, and the photographs attached.

## A filled example

California, moved out October 15, 2026, deposit $2,400.00. Worked out by the tool.

| Deduction | What backs it | Amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Carpet cleaning, two bedrooms | Copy of invoice enclosed | $180.00 |
| Repair hole in bedroom door | Own labor: 3 hours at $80 an hour | $240.00 |
| Unpaid rent, October 1 to 15 | No document needed | $1,100.00 |
| Refund due |  | $880.00 |

California: furnish the itemized statement and return the rest of the deposit by Thursday, November 5, 2026, 21 calendar days after October 15, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under § 1950.5(h)(1). That is 16 days after October 20, 2026. Of $2,400.00 held, $1,520.00 is kept and $880.00 goes back. Nothing the statute requires is missing from what you entered.

## What the tool flags

The same example without the cleaning invoice and without move-out photographs:

- Deduction 1 (Carpet cleaning, two bedrooms): repairs and cleaning above $125 in total need a copy of the bill, invoice or receipt, or for your own labor the time spent and the hourly rate. (§ 1950.5(h)(2))
- Deductions for repairs or cleaning need the photographs taken after the unit was returned (and after the work), sent with the statement. (§ 1950.5(g)(2), (h)(2)(D))

## Questions

### What must an itemized deposit statement include?

The deposit held, each deduction with a description and amount, and the balance refunded. Several statutes add documents: California and Washington want invoices or receipts, or for the landlord's own work the time spent and the hourly rate; Illinois wants the paid receipts attached.

### Do I need receipts for small deductions in California?

Not for the documents rule: Civil Code § 1950.5(h) lifts it when the deductions for repairs and cleaning together do not exceed $125.

### Can I deduct before the repair is done?

California allows a good-faith estimate when the work cannot reasonably be finished within 21 days, with the documents sent later; Illinois allows an estimate with receipts within 30 days.
