Mich. Comp. Laws §§ 554.609 to 554.613

Michigan security deposit deadline

In Michigan a landlord must mail the itemized list of damages with a check for the balance by Sunday, August 30, 2026, 30 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under § 554.609.

What the statute says

§ 554.609: "In case of damage to the rental unit or other obligation against the security deposit, the landlord shall mail to the tenant, within 30 days after the termination of occupancy, an itemized list of damages claimed for which the security deposit may be used as provided in section 7, including the estimated cost of repair of each property damaged item and the amounts and bases on which he intends to assess the tenant."

The sample move-out

  • August 30, 2026: Mail the itemized list of damages with a check for the balance (§ 554.609)
  • September 14, 2026: Sue for the damages claimed, or return the balance held (unless an exception in § 554.613(1) applies) (§ 554.613(1))
  • The tenant must give an address in writing within 4 days after occupancy ends; without it the landlord is relieved of the notice of damages (§ 554.611).
  • The statute does not say that mailing on the last day is enough, so plan for the tenant to have it by the due date.
  • The due date falls on a Sunday. The statute does not move it to the next business day, and neither does this tool.

Delivery

The landlord must mail the itemized list, with a check or money order for the balance, within the 30 days. The list must carry the statute's 7-day response statement in 12-point bold type at least 4 points larger than the body.

If the deadline is missed

Failing to comply with section 13 waives all claimed damages and makes the landlord liable for double the amount retained; a late notice of damages is an agreement that no damages are due (§§ 554.610, 554.613(2)).

"Failure of the landlord to comply fully with this section constitutes waiver of all claimed damages and makes him liable to the tenant for double the amount of the security deposit retained."

"Failure by the landlord to comply with the notice of damages requirement within the 30 days after the termination of occupancy, constitutes agreement by the landlord that no damages are due and he shall remit to the tenant immediately the full security deposit."
