# Ohio security deposit deadline

In Ohio a landlord must deliver the itemized notice of deductions with the amount due by Sunday, August 30, 2026, 30 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under § 5321.16(B).

- Days: 30 calendar days
- Clock starts: the later of the rental agreement ending and delivery of possession
- Sample due date: August 30, 2026

## What the statute says

§ 5321.16(B): "Any deduction from the security deposit shall be itemized and identified by the landlord in a written notice delivered to the tenant together with the amount due, within thirty days after termination of the rental agreement and delivery of possession."

## The sample move-out

- August 30, 2026: Deliver the itemized notice of deductions with the amount due (§ 5321.16(B))

- If the tenant gives no forwarding address in writing, the tenant cannot recover damages or attorney's fees under § 5321.16(C).
- The statute does not say that mailing on the last day is enough, so plan for the tenant to have it by the due date.
- The due date falls on a Sunday. The statute does not move it to the next business day, and neither does this tool.

## Delivery

A written notice delivered to the tenant with the amount due. A tenant who gives no forwarding address in writing loses the damages and attorney's fees under division (C).

## If the deadline is missed

The amount wrongfully withheld again as damages, plus reasonable attorney's fees (§ 5321.16(C)).

> "If the landlord fails to comply with division (B) of this section, the tenant may recover the property and money due him, together with damages in an amount equal to the amount wrongfully withheld, and reasonable attorneys fees."

## Questions

### How long does a landlord have to return a security deposit in Ohio?

30 days after the later of the rental agreement ending and delivery of possession (§ 5321.16(B)).

### What happens if a Ohio landlord misses the deadline?

The amount wrongfully withheld again as damages, plus reasonable attorney's fees (§ 5321.16(C)).

### How must the Ohio deposit statement be sent?

A written notice delivered to the tenant with the amount due. A tenant who gives no forwarding address in writing loses the damages and attorney's fees under division (C).
