# Security deposit due dates for a December 31, 2026 move-out

For a tenant who moves out on December 31, 2026, the deposit statement is due January 14, 2027 in New York, January 21, 2027 in California, January 30, 2027 in Texas and February 14, 2027 in Virginia. Arizona's 14 business days skip New Year's Day and Martin Luther King Jr. Day, so a demand made on December 31 runs to January 22, 2027.

## Why does Arizona land later?

A.R.S. § 33-1321(D) counts 14 business days, leaving out Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays, and starts only once the tenancy has ended, possession is back and the tenant has demanded the deposit. Holidays skipped in the count (A.R.S. § 1-301): New Year's Day, January 1, 2027; Martin Luther King, Jr./Civil Rights Day, January 18, 2027.

## Questions

### When must a California landlord return a deposit for a December 31, 2026 move-out?

By Thursday, January 21, 2027: 21 calendar days after the tenant vacated (Cal. Civ. Code § 1950.5(h)(1)).

### Does a due date on a weekend move to Monday?

Not unless the statute says so, and the calendar-day statutes read here do not. For this move-out the date falls on a weekend in Texas (Saturday), Florida (Saturday), Pennsylvania (Saturday), Illinois (Saturday), Ohio (Saturday), Georgia (Saturday), North Carolina (Saturday), Michigan (Saturday), Virginia (Sunday) and Washington (Saturday).

### What if the lease ended on another day than the move-out?

Several states count from the later of the two, Pennsylvania from the earlier and Florida from the end of the rental agreement. Enter both dates in the tool.
