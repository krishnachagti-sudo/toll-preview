# California security deposit deadline

In California a landlord must furnish the itemized statement and return the rest of the deposit by Friday, August 21, 2026, 21 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under § 1950.5(h)(1).

- Days: 21 calendar days
- Clock starts: the day the tenant vacated
- Sample due date: August 21, 2026

## What the statute says

§ 1950.5(h)(1): "No later than 21 calendar days after the tenant has vacated the premises, but not earlier than the time that either the landlord or the tenant provides a notice to terminate the tenancy under Section 1946 or 1946.1, Section 1161 of the Code of Civil Procedure, or not earlier than 60 calendar days prior to the expiration of a fixed-term lease, the landlord shall furnish the tenant, a copy of an itemized statement indicating the basis for, and the amount of, any security received and the disposition of the security, and shall return any remaining portion of the security to the tenant as follows:"

## The sample move-out

- August 21, 2026: Furnish the itemized statement and return the rest of the deposit (§ 1950.5(h)(1))

- The statute does not say that mailing on the last day is enough, so plan for the tenant to have it by the due date.

## Delivery

Itemized statement by personal delivery or first-class mail, postage prepaid; by email or to another address only by mutual agreement. The refund by personal delivery or a check mailed first-class, or electronically if the tenant paid electronically. With several adult tenants and no written agreement, one check payable to all adult tenants.

## If the deadline is missed

Up to twice the deposit in statutory damages, plus actual damages, for bad-faith retention; a bad-faith failure to comply forfeits any claim to the deposit (§ 1950.5(h)(7), (m)).

> "may subject the landlord or the landlord's successors in interest to statutory damages of up to twice the amount of the security, in addition to actual damages."

> "The landlord shall not be entitled to claim any amount of the security if the landlord, in bad faith, fails to comply with this subdivision."
