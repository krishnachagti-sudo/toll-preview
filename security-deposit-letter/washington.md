# Washington security deposit deadline

In Washington a landlord must give the full and specific statement, documents and any refund by Sunday, August 30, 2026, 30 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under RCW 59.18.280(1)(a).

- Days: 30 calendar days
- Clock starts: the later of the rental agreement ending and the tenant vacating, or the day the landlord learned of an abandonment
- Sample due date: August 30, 2026

## What the statute says

RCW 59.18.280(1)(a): "Within 30 days after the termination of the rental agreement and vacation of the premises or, if the tenant abandons the premises as defined in RCW 59.18.310, within 30 days after the landlord learns of the abandonment, the landlord shall give a full and specific statement of the basis for retaining any of the deposit, and any documentation required by (b) of this subsection, together with the payment of any refund due the tenant under the terms and conditions of the rental agreement."

## The sample move-out

- August 30, 2026: Give the full and specific statement, documents and any refund (RCW 59.18.280(1)(a))

- Mailing it first-class within the 30 days complies (RCW 59.18.280(1)(a)).
- The due date falls on a Sunday. The statute does not move it to the next business day, and neither does this tool.

## Delivery

Delivered personally, or deposited in the US mail to the last known address with first-class postage prepaid, within the 30 days.

## If the deadline is missed

A late or incomplete statement makes the landlord liable for the full deposit and bars defenses; up to twice the deposit for an intentional refusal; the winner recovers costs and attorney's fees (RCW 59.18.280(2)).

> "If the landlord fails to give the statement and any documentation required by subsection (1) of this section together with any refund due the tenant within the time limits specified in subsection (1) of this section he or she shall be liable to the tenant for the full amount of the deposit. The landlord is also barred in any action brought by the tenant to recover the deposit from asserting any claim or raising any defense for retaining any of the deposit unless the landlord shows that circumstances beyond the landlord's control prevented the landlord from providing the statement and any documentation within the 30 days or that the tenant abandoned the premises as defined in RCW 59.18.310. The court may in its discretion award up to two times the amount of the deposit for the intentional refusal of the landlord to give the statement, documentation, or refund due unless the landlord shows that circumstances beyond the landlord's control prevented the landlord from providing the statement and any such documentation within 30 days or that the tenant abandoned the premises as described in RCW 59.18.310. In any action brought by the tenant to recover the deposit, the prevailing party shall additionally be entitled to the cost of suit or arbitration including a reasonable attorneys' fee."

## Questions

### How long does a landlord have to return a security deposit in Washington?

30 days after the later of the rental agreement ending and the tenant vacating, or the day the landlord learned of an abandonment (RCW 59.18.280(1)(a)).

### What happens if a Washington landlord misses the deadline?

A late or incomplete statement makes the landlord liable for the full deposit and bars defenses; up to twice the deposit for an intentional refusal; the winner recovers costs and attorney's fees (RCW 59.18.280(2)).

### How must the Washington deposit statement be sent?

Delivered personally, or deposited in the US mail to the last known address with first-class postage prepaid, within the 30 days.
