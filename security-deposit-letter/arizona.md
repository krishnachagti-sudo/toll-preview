# Arizona security deposit deadline

In Arizona a landlord must provide the itemized list of deductions with the amount due by Friday, August 21, 2026, 14 business days (excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays) after August 3, 2026 (the day the tenant demanded the deposit), under A.R.S. § 33-1321(D).

- Days: 14 business days
- Clock starts: the latest of the tenancy ending, delivery of possession and the tenant's demand
- Sample due date: August 21, 2026

## What the statute says

A.R.S. § 33-1321(D): "Within fourteen days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays or other legal holidays, after termination of the tenancy and delivery of possession and demand by the tenant the landlord shall provide the tenant an itemized list of all deductions together with the amount due and payable to the tenant, if any. Unless other arrangements are made in writing by the tenant, the landlord shall mail the itemized list and any amount due, by first class mail, to the tenant's last known place of residence."

## The sample move-out

- August 21, 2026: Provide the itemized list of deductions with the amount due (A.R.S. § 33-1321(D))

- The statute does not say that mailing on the last day is enough, so plan for the tenant to have it by the due date.

## Delivery

By first-class mail to the tenant's last known place of residence, unless the tenant arranged otherwise in writing. If the tenant does not dispute within 60 days after mailing, the list becomes final.

## If the deadline is missed

Twice the amount wrongfully withheld, on top of the money due (A.R.S. § 33-1321(E)).

> "If the landlord fails to comply with subsection D of this section, the tenant may recover the property and money due the tenant together with damages in an amount equal to twice the amount wrongfully withheld."

## Questions

### How long does a landlord have to return a security deposit in Arizona?

14 business days after the latest of the tenancy ending, delivery of possession and the tenant's demand, counting only business days (A.R.S. § 33-1321(D)).

### What happens if a Arizona landlord misses the deadline?

Twice the amount wrongfully withheld, on top of the money due (A.R.S. § 33-1321(E)).

### How must the Arizona deposit statement be sent?

By first-class mail to the tenant's last known place of residence, unless the tenant arranged otherwise in writing. If the tenant does not dispute within 60 days after mailing, the list becomes final.
