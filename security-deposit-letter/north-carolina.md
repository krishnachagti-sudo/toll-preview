# North Carolina security deposit deadline

In North Carolina a landlord must mail or deliver the itemized accounting with the balance of the deposit by Sunday, August 30, 2026, 30 calendar days after July 31, 2026 (the day the tenant moved out and gave back possession), under § 42-52.

- Days: 30 calendar days
- Clock starts: the later of the tenancy ending and delivery of possession
- Sample due date: August 30, 2026

## What the statute says

§ 42-52: "In either case the landlord in writing shall itemize any damage and mail or deliver same to the tenant, together with the balance of the security deposit, no later than 30 days after termination of the tenancy and delivery of possession of the premises to the landlord."

## The sample move-out

- August 30, 2026: Mail or deliver the itemized accounting with the balance of the deposit (§ 42-52)
- September 29, 2026: Final accounting, if only an interim accounting was possible at 30 days (§ 42-52)

- The statute does not say that mailing on the last day is enough, so plan for the tenant to have it by the due date.
- The due date falls on a Sunday. The statute does not move it to the next business day, and neither does this tool.

## Delivery

Mailed or delivered to the tenant with the balance of the deposit. If the tenant's address is unknown, hold the balance for collection for at least six months.
