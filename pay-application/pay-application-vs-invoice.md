# Pay application vs invoice

An invoice bills an amount for goods or work; a pay application bills a share of the whole contract and shows how it got there: on a $248,500.00 contract, $99,250.00 completed and stored, $9,925.00 retained and $46,800.00 already certified leave $42,525.00 due.

|  | Invoice | Pay application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it bills | An amount for work or goods | A share of each schedule-of-values line, to date |
| Shows the contract | Usually not | Contract sum, change orders, balance to finish |
| Retainage | Rarely | Held on the total to date and shown each month |
| Previous payments | Not needed | Subtracted as previous certificates |
| Review | Accounts payable | The GC, owner or architect checks it against progress and may certify a different amount |
| When used | Time and materials, small jobs | Lump-sum commercial contracts billed by progress |
