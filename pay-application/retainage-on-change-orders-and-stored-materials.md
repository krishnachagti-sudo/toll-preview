# Retainage on stored materials and change orders

Stored materials are billed in their own column and held at the contract rate unless the contract sets another: on $18,000.00 of stored switchgear, 10% holds $1,800.00 and a 5% stored-materials rate holds $900.00; signed change orders are held like any other line.

## Stored materials

- AIA's instructions: materials presently stored are recalculated at the end of each period, and move into this period's work only as they are installed. Payment for them does not take them out of the stored column.
- Your subcontract says what backup stored materials need before they are paid, for example invoices, photos or proof of insurance.
- Texas public works: the limit applies "for any item in a bid schedule or schedule of values for the project, including materials and equipment delivered on site to be installed" ([Tex. Gov't Code 2252.032](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.2252.htm)).

| Contract rule | Held on stored | Total held | Due now |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10% on work and stored | $1,800.00 | $9,925.00 | $42,525.00 |
| 10% on work, 5% on stored | $900.00 | $9,025.00 | $43,425.00 |
| 10% on work, none on stored | $0.00 | $8,125.00 | $44,325.00 |

## Change orders

- A signed change order is added to the contract sum and billed like a line, so the contract rate applies unless the change order says otherwise.
- An unsigned one is listed as pending and not billed; the tool flags any amount billed on it.
- Work it out on one change order with the [change order calculator](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/retainage-on-change-orders/).
