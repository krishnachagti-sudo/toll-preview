# Siteline and a pay-per-use pay application

Siteline sells subcontractors a billing, collections and lien waiver suite (pay apps on GC forms, GC portal submission, ERP sync) at a quoted price; Endwise makes one G702/G703-style pay application at a time, with checks, for $2.50 per pay application, with corrections of the same application free for 60 days and at most $29.00 a month.

From Siteline's own pages, read November 8, 2026.

|  | Siteline | Endwise pay application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | The Siteline Suite: billing (digitize GC-specific forms, create pay apps, submit via email or GC portals, manage pending change orders), collections, lien waivers and compliance, reporting. | One pay application at a time: schedule of values, summary, checks, PDF and CSV. |
| Price | Not published: "We work with our customers to offer pricing that best suits their product usage, billing volume, and integration needs." | $2.50 per pay application, with corrections of the same application free for 60 days and at most $29.00 a month. |
| Users | Unlimited users and offices, no per-seat fees (pricing page). | One account; any number of applications. |
| Accounting | ERP sync (Sage, Viewpoint, QuickBooks, Foundation and others listed). | CSV download; no sync. |
| Lien waivers | Included (create, submit and track). | Separate tool on this site. |

## When Siteline is better

- You bill many projects every month and want pay apps, waivers, collections and ERP sync in one place.
- Your GCs use portals Siteline submits to, or GC-specific forms you want digitized.

## When Endwise is better

- You bill a few projects and want a correct pay application today without a sales call or contract.
- You want the price up front, per application.
