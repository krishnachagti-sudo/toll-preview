# Retainage caps by state

Of the 7 states whose retainage statutes were read on November 8, 2026, Minnesota caps private retainage at 5%, Florida and Kansas set no private percentage, and Texas instead makes the owner reserve 10%; on public work the limits run from 5% (Florida, Kansas, Minnesota, Virginia, Washington, Texas at $5 million or more) to 10% (Arizona until 50% complete, Texas under $5 million). The other 44 jurisdictions are not covered here.

Covered states. Most held: on a $1,000,000.00 public contract, fully billed, at the limit.

| State | Private projects | Public projects | Most held, public |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| [Arizona](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/retainage-laws/arizona/) | Not covered | 10%, then 5% of later payments after 50% complete (A.R.S. 34-221(C)) | $75,000.00 |
| [Florida](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/retainage-laws/florida/) | No percentage in the statute (Fla. Stat. 715.12(7)) | 5% (contracts over $200,000) (Fla. Stat. 255.078) | $50,000.00 |
| [Kansas](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/retainage-laws/kansas/) | No percentage in the statute (K.S.A. 16-1803) | 5% (up to 10% if needed to ensure performance) (K.S.A. 16-1904) | $50,000.00 |
| [Minnesota](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/retainage-laws/minnesota/) | 5% (Minn. Stat. 337.10, subd. 4) | 5% (Minn. Stat. 15.72, subd. 2) | $50,000.00 |
| [Texas](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/retainage-laws/texas/) | No cap; the owner reserves 10% (Tex. Prop. Code 53.101 and 28.002) | 10% under $5 million; 5% at $5 million or more (Tex. Gov't Code 2252.032) | $100,000.00 |
| [Virginia](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/retainage-laws/virginia/) | Not covered | 5% (Va. Code 2.2-4333) | $50,000.00 |
| [Washington](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/retainage-laws/washington/) | Not covered | 5% (RCW 60.28.011) | $50,000.00 |

## Not covered

Alabama, Alaska, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Utah, Vermont, West Virginia, Wisconsin and Wyoming: their statutes were not read on the state's own site, so no rule is shown. Some sites refused the request or did not answer: California, Illinois, Nevada, New York, North Carolina and Utah.
