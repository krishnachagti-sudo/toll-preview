# Schedule of values template

This schedule of values template totals 5 lines to a $240,000.00 contract sum, and the pay application bills each line by percent complete; it is the scheduled-value column of a G702/G703-style application.

## Rules for a schedule of values

- The lines add up exactly to the original contract sum; the tool refuses to make the PDF until they do.
- AIA's instructions say the breakdown may be by sections of the work or by subcontractors, and should remain consistent throughout the project.
- Change orders go on their own lines when they are signed; the original lines stay as they were.
- Split large lines so each can be measured: a line that is either 0% or 100% done is hard to bill fairly month to month.

## Questions

### What is a schedule of values?

A list of the parts of the work with a dollar value on each, adding up to the contract sum. Each pay application bills a share of each line; AIA's instructions call it the schedule of values the contractor submits at the start of the project.

### Should the schedule of values change during the job?

AIA's instructions say the breakdown should stay consistent through the project; change orders are usually added as separate lines rather than by editing the original lines.
