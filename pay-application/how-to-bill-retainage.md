# How to bill retainage on a pay application

Bill retainage by applying the contract rate to the total completed and stored to date, then subtracting previous certificates: at 10% on $99,250.00 that is $9,925.00 retained, leaving $42,525.00 due on this application.

## Try your rate and reduction

[Open in the pay application tool](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/?rate=10&reduceAt=50&reducedRate=5)

## The same application, three retainage rules

| Contract rule | Retainage held | Current payment due |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 10% on work and stored materials | $9,925.00 | $42,525.00 |
| 10% on work, 5% on stored materials | $9,025.00 | $43,425.00 |
| 10%, then 5% on later work after 50% complete (not reached yet) | $9,925.00 | $42,525.00 |

At 39.9% complete the 50% point has not been reached, so the reduction changes nothing yet.

## Mistakes that get a pay app sent back

- Retainage on this month only: the rate applies to the total to date; the previous certificates already reflect earlier retainage.
- Previous certificates as requested, not as certified: use the amounts actually approved.
- A rate above the contract or the state limit: the tool checks the states whose statutes were read.
- Stored materials carried into the previous-work column: AIA's instructions say previous work excludes stored materials, which are recalculated each period.

When the job is done, bill the release with a [retainage release request](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pay-application/retainage-release-request/).

## Steps

1. **Apply the rate to the total to date.** Retainage is the rate times the work completed and stored to date, so it grows each month with the work.
2. **Subtract it before previous certificates.** Earned less retainage, less what was certified before, is the amount due now; the retainage itself is billed later.
3. **Use the contract's rule for stored materials and reductions.** Some contracts hold a different rate on stored materials or drop the rate at a percent complete.
4. **Release it on a later application.** A partial or final release lowers the retainage held, and the difference becomes part of the amount due.
