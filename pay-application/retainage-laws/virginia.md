# Virginia retainage rules for your pay application

On a public project in Virginia, the payer may hold 5% retainage (Va. Code 2.2-4333); on a $2,400,000.00 contract that is $120,000.00 at most by completion. Private work in Virginia is not covered here: the statute for it was not read.

- Private: not covered
- Public: 5%
- Read: November 8, 2026

| Projects | Applies to | Retainage limit | Most held on the sample | Statute |

| Public | Public contracts for construction with progress payments, and subcontracts on public projects (Va. Code 2.2-4333) | 5% | $120,000.00 | Va. Code 2.2-4333 |

> Most held: on a $248,500.00 private or $2,400,000.00 public contract, fully billed, at the statute's limit.

## Public projects: Va. Code 2.2-4333

The contractor is paid at least 95% of the earned sum when payment is due, with no more than 5% retained. Subcontracts on public projects with similar progress payments are held to the same rule.

- Release: Amounts withheld may be included in the final payment.

> Private projects: no Virginia private-project retainage section was read, so private work in Virginia is not covered.

## In the statute's words

- "the contractor shall be paid at least ninety-five percent of the earned sum when payment is due, with no more than five percent being retained to ensure faithful performance of the contract" ([Va. Code 2.2-4333](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title2.2/chapter43/section2.2-4333/))
- "Any subcontract for a public project that provides for similar progress payments shall be subject to the provisions of this section." ([Va. Code 2.2-4333](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title2.2/chapter43/section2.2-4333/))
