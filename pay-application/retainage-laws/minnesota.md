# Minnesota retainage rules for your pay application

On a private project in Minnesota, the payer may hold 5% retainage (Minn. Stat. 337.10, subd. 4); on a $248,500.00 contract that is $12,425.00 at most by completion. On public work the limit is 5% (Minn. Stat. 15.72, subd. 2).

- Private: 5%
- Public: 5%
- Read: November 8, 2026

## Private projects: Minn. Stat. 337.10, subd. 4

Retainage on a building and construction contract may not exceed 5%. The owner may reduce or eliminate it if work is progressing satisfactorily; if it does, the contractor reduces subcontractors' retainage at the same rate. Withholding retainage for warranty work is prohibited.

- Release: The owner releases all retainage no later than 60 days after substantial completion (after that it may withhold up to 250% of the cost to correct or complete known work, and the greater of 1% of the contract or $500 pending final paperwork). The contractor pays remaining retainage to subcontractors within 10 days of receiving it.
- Payment timing: Prime contractors and subcontractors pay their subcontractors and suppliers within 10 days of being paid; late undisputed amounts bear 1-1/2% a month interest.

## Public projects: Minn. Stat. 15.72, subd. 2

A public contracting agency may hold no more than 5% of any progress payment as retainage, and may reduce or eliminate it if work is progressing satisfactorily.

- Release: All retainage is released no later than 60 days after substantial completion; the contractor pays remaining retainage to subcontractors within 10 days of receiving it.
- Payment timing: Progress payments are monthly as the work progresses unless the contract says otherwise.

## In the statute's words

- "Retainage on a building and construction contract may not exceed five percent." ([Minn. Stat. 337.10, subd. 4](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/337.10))
- "The owner or the owner's agent must release all retainage no later than 60 days after substantial completion subject to the terms of this subdivision." ([Minn. Stat. 337.10, subd. 4](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/337.10))
- "A contractor must pay all remaining retainage to its subcontractors no later than ten days after receiving payment of retainage" ([Minn. Stat. 337.10, subd. 4](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/337.10))
- "Withholding retainage for warranty work is prohibited." ([Minn. Stat. 337.10, subd. 4](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/337.10))
- "This subdivision does not apply to a public agency as defined in section" ([Minn. Stat. 337.10, subd. 4](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/337.10))
- "A public contracting agency may reserve as retainage from any progress payment on a public contract for a public improvement an amount not to exceed five percent of the payment." ([Minn. Stat. 15.72, subd. 2](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/15.72))
- "The public contracting agency must release all retainage no later than 60 days after substantial completion, subject to the terms of this subdivision." ([Minn. Stat. 15.72, subd. 2](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/15.72))
