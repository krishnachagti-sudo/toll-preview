# Washington retainage rules for your pay application

On a public project in Washington, the payer may hold 5% retainage (RCW 60.28.011); on a $2,400,000.00 contract that is $120,000.00 at most by completion. Private work in Washington is not covered here: the statute for it was not read.

- Private: not covered
- Public: 5%
- Read: November 8, 2026

## Public projects: RCW 60.28.011

Public bodies reserve contract retainage of no more than 5% of the moneys earned by the contractor, as a trust fund for claims and certain state taxes. The contractor may withhold no more than 5% from its subcontractors and suppliers. Contracts funded by federal transportation money rely on the bond instead.

- Release: After all work other than landscaping is complete the contractor may ask for release, and 60 days later the public body pays it (5% may stay for landscaping); 60 days after all work is complete it is released in full, subject to RCW 39.12 and chapter 60.28. Claimants must give notice of a lien on the retainage within 45 days of completion.
- Payment timing: At the contractor's option the retainage is held in a fund, an interest-bearing account or escrow.

> Private projects: no Washington private-project retainage section was read, so private work in Washington is not covered.

## In the statute's words

- "public improvement contracts must provide, and public bodies must reserve, a contract retainage not to exceed five percent of the moneys earned by the contractor" ([RCW 60.28.011](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=60.28.011))
- "Sixty days after completion of all contract work the public body must release and pay in full the amounts retained during the performance of the contract" ([RCW 60.28.011](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=60.28.011))
- "The contractor or subcontractor may withhold payment of not more than five percent from the moneys earned by any subcontractor or sub-subcontractor or supplier" ([RCW 60.28.011](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=60.28.011))
