# Arizona retainage rules for your pay application

On a public project in Arizona, the payer may hold 10% retainage until 50% complete and 5% of later payments (A.R.S. 34-221(C)); on a $2,400,000.00 contract that is $180,000.00 at most by completion. Private work in Arizona is not covered here: the statute for it was not read.

- Private: not covered
- Public: 10%, then 5% of later payments after 50% complete
- Read: November 8, 2026

> Most held: on a $248,500.00 private or $2,400,000.00 public contract, fully billed, at the statute's limit.

## Public projects: A.R.S. 34-221(C)

The owner retains 10% of each estimate. When the contract is 50% complete and progress is satisfactory, half of the amount retained is paid on the contractor's request, and no more than 5% of later progress payments may be retained (10% again if progress becomes unsatisfactory).

- Release: Paid within 60 days after completion or filing notice of completion; holding it longer needs a specific written finding. Securities may be substituted for the retention at the contractor's option.
- Payment timing: An estimate is deemed approved 7 days after submission unless the owner issues a written finding, and progress payments are due within 14 days after the estimate is certified and approved.

> Private projects: the Arizona Legislature's page for the private-project section could not be read (it returned not found), so private work in Arizona is not covered.

## In the statute's words

- "the owner shall retain ten percent of the amount of each estimate until final completion and acceptance of all material, equipment and work covered by the contract" ([A.R.S. 34-221(C)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/34/00221.htm))
- "After the contract is fifty percent completed, not more than five percent of the amount of any subsequent progress payments made under the contract may be retained if the contractor is making satisfactory progress on the project" ([A.R.S. 34-221(C)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/34/00221.htm))
- "to be paid to the contractor within sixty days after completion or filing notice of completion of the contract" ([A.R.S. 34-221(C)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/34/00221.htm))
- "The progress payments shall be paid on or before fourteen days after the estimate of the work is certified and approved." ([A.R.S. 34-221(C)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/34/00221.htm))
