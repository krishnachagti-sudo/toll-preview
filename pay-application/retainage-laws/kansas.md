# Kansas retainage rules for your pay application

On a private project in Kansas, K.S.A. 16-1803 sets no retainage percentage; the contract sets the rate, and the statute sets when it is paid: when the owner releases retainage, the contractor pays it to its subcontractors within 7 business days of receiving it (with a proper, undisputed request), or owes 18% a year interest. On public work the limit is 5% (up to 10% if needed to ensure performance) (K.S.A. 16-1904).

- Private: no retainage percentage
- Public: 5% (up to 10% if needed to ensure performance)
- Read: November 8, 2026

## Private projects: K.S.A. 16-1803

K.S.A. 16-1803 sets payment deadlines and excludes retainage from the owner's 30-day deadline; it sets no retainage percentage.

- Release: When the owner releases retainage, the contractor pays it to its subcontractors within 7 business days of receiving it (with a proper, undisputed request), or owes 18% a year interest.
- Payment timing: The owner pays amounts due, except retainage, within 30 days after a timely, properly completed, undisputed request; then 18% a year interest. The contractor pays subcontractors within 7 business days of being paid.

## Public projects: K.S.A. 16-1904

Retainage may not exceed 5% of the value of the contract or subcontract unless the owner or contractor decides a higher rate is needed to ensure performance, and never more than 10%.

- Release: All remaining retainage on undisputed payments is released within 30 days after substantial completion, as part of the regular payment cycle (work still being performed may be held until 30 days after it is done). Late retainage bears 18% a year interest.
- Payment timing: The act's rights and duties cannot be waived or varied by contract.

## In the statute's words

- "All contracts for private construction shall provide that payment of amounts due a contractor from an owner, except retainage, shall be made within 30 days after the owner receives a timely, properly completed, undisputed request for payment." ([K.S.A. 16-1803](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch16/016_018_0003.html))
- "A contractor shall pay its subcontractors any amounts due within seven business days of receipt of payment from the owner, including payment of retainage, if retainage is released by the owner" ([K.S.A. 16-1803](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch16/016_018_0003.html))
- "Retainage shall not exceed 5% of the value of the contract or subcontract unless the owner or contractor determines that a higher rate of retainage is required to ensure performance of the contract. Retainage, however, shall not exceed 10% of the value of the contract or subcontract." ([K.S.A. 16-1904](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch16/016_019_0004.html))
- "An owner, contractor or subcontractor must release all remaining retainage on any undisputed payment due on a construction project within 30 days after substantial completion of the project as part of the regular payment cycle" ([K.S.A. 16-1904](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch16/016_019_0004.html))
- "at a rate of 18% per annum" ([K.S.A. 16-1904](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch16/016_019_0004.html))
- "shall be known and may be cited as the Kansas fairness in public construction contract act" ([K.S.A. 16-1901](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch16/016_019_0001.html))
