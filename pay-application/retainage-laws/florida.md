# Florida retainage rules for your pay application

On a private project in Florida, Fla. Stat. 715.12(7) sets no retainage percentage; the contract sets the rate, and the statute sets when it is paid: the owner pays the balance, including the amounts withheld, within 14 days after substantial completion is certified (or a certificate of occupancy issues, or the owner takes possession) and the contractor substantially completes the written punchlist. Retainage held beyond that bears interest. On public work the limit is 5% (contracts over $200,000) (Fla. Stat. 255.078).

- Private: no retainage percentage
- Public: 5% (contracts over $200,000)
- Read: November 8, 2026

## Private projects: Fla. Stat. 715.12(7)

The section lets an owner and contractor agree to withhold a portion of each progress payment until substantial completion, and an obligor and obligee to withhold a portion until completion of the entire project. It sets no percentage.

- Release: The owner pays the balance, including the amounts withheld, within 14 days after substantial completion is certified (or a certificate of occupancy issues, or the owner takes possession) and the contractor substantially completes the written punchlist. Retainage held beyond that bears interest.
- Payment timing: A payment due bears interest from the 14th day after it is due; an incomplete or wrong request may be returned within 14 days with the reasons in writing.

## Public projects: Fla. Stat. 255.078

A public entity may withhold no more than 5% of each progress payment as retainage. It may withhold less, reduce the rate on a schedule in the contract, or release retainage early.

- Release: Retainage paid to the contractor for a subcontractor's or supplier's work must be passed on timely. The same payment deadlines apply to a request that is for, or includes, retainage.
- Payment timing: The same time limits apply whether or not a payment request includes retainage.

## Public projects: Fla. Stat. 218.735(8)

A local governmental entity may withhold no more than 5% of each progress payment as retainage, and may withhold less or reduce it on a schedule in the contract.

- Release: Retainage paid to the contractor for a subcontractor's or supplier's work must be passed on timely.

For Florida public work (Fla. Stat. 255.078) the limit turns on the contract's value:

- A $150,000.00 contract: Fla. Stat. 255.078(6): the section does not apply when the construction services in the contract total $200,000 or less.
- A $2,400,000.00 contract: 5%, so at most $120,000.00 held once fully billed.
- A $6,200,000.00 contract: 5%, so at most $310,000.00 held once fully billed.

## In the statute's words

- "An owner and a contractor may agree to a provision that allows the owner to withhold a portion of each progress payment until substantial completion of the entire project." ([Fla. Stat. 715.12(7)](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0715/Sections/0715.12.html))
- "The owner shall pay the contractor the balance of the contract price, including the amounts withheld from the progress payments, within 14 days after any of the following events occur." ([Fla. Stat. 715.12(7)](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0715/Sections/0715.12.html))
- "computed beginning on the 14th day after payment is due pursuant to subsection (4)" ([Fla. Stat. 715.12(7)](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0700-0799/0715/Sections/0715.12.html))
- "a public entity may withhold from each progress payment made to the contractor an amount not exceeding 5 percent of the payment as retainage" ([Fla. Stat. 255.078](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0200-0299/0255/Sections/0255.078.html))
- "This section does not apply to any construction services purchased by a public entity if the total cost of the construction services purchased as identified in the contract is $200,000 or less." ([Fla. Stat. 255.078](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0200-0299/0255/Sections/0255.078.html))
- "The same time limits for payment of a payment request apply regardless of whether the payment request is for, or includes, retainage." ([Fla. Stat. 255.078](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0200-0299/0255/Sections/0255.078.html))
- "a local governmental entity may withhold from each progress payment made to the contractor an amount not exceeding 5 percent of the payment as retainage" ([Fla. Stat. 218.735(8)](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0200-0299/0218/Sections/0218.735.html))
