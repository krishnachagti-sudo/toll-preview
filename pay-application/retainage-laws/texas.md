# Texas retainage rules for your pay application

On a private project in Texas, the statutes read set no cap on contract retainage, but the owner must reserve 10% of the contract price until 30 days after completion (Tex. Prop. Code 53.101 and 28.002); on a $248,500.00 contract that reserve is $24,850.00. On public work the limit is 10% under $5 million; 5% at $5 million or more (Tex. Gov't Code 2252.032).

- Private: owner must reserve 10%
- Public: 10% under $5 million; 5% at $5 million or more
- Read: November 8, 2026

## Private projects: Tex. Prop. Code 53.101 and 28.002

Texas does not cap private retainage in the sections read. Instead the owner must reserve 10% of the contract price (or of the value of the work done) during the work and for 30 days after it is completed; claimants who give the required notices have a lien on that reserve.

- Release: The owner's 10% reserve is kept for 30 days after the work under the original contract is completed. To claim against it, a claimant files a lien affidavit within 30 days of completion (see the lien deadline tool).
- Payment timing: The owner pays a contractor's written request within 35 days of receiving it; the contractor pays each subcontractor its share within 7 days of being paid, and the same for each tier below.

## Public projects: Tex. Gov't Code 2252.032

Under $5 million, retainage may not exceed 10% of the contract price, and 10% on any item of the schedule of values, including materials and equipment delivered on site; at $5 million or more the limit is 5%. Dam contracts are 10% at any value. A prime contractor may not hold a greater percentage from a subcontractor than is held from it.

- Release: The contract must say when the project is substantially complete and when retainage is released for completed portions. Retainage may not be held after the prime contractor's work is complete, including during the warranty period, except for a bona fide dispute with written notice.
- Payment timing: Payment deadlines for public work are in Tex. Gov't Code chapter 2251, which was not read here.

For Texas public work (Tex. Gov't Code 2252.032) the limit turns on the contract's value:

- A $150,000.00 contract: 10%, so at most $15,000.00 held once fully billed.
- A $2,400,000.00 contract: 10%, so at most $240,000.00 held once fully billed.
- A $6,200,000.00 contract: 5%, so at most $310,000.00 held once fully billed.

## In the statute's words

- "During the progress of work under an original contract for which a mechanic's lien may be claimed and for 30 days after the work under the contract is completed, the owner shall reserve: (1) 10 percent of the contract price of the work to the owner" ([Tex. Prop. Code 53.101 and 28.002](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.53.htm))
- "not later than the 35th day after the date the owner receives the request" ([Tex. Prop. Code 28.002](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.28.htm))
- "The payment required by this subsection must be made not later than the seventh day after the date the contractor receives the owner's payment." ([Tex. Prop. Code 28.002](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.28.htm))
- "if the total value of a public works contract is less than $5 million, a governmental entity may not withhold retainage in an amount that exceeds 10 percent of the contract price" ([Tex. Gov't Code 2252.032](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.2252.htm))
- "if the total value of a public works contract is $5 million or more, a governmental entity may not withhold retainage in an amount that exceeds five percent of the contract price" ([Tex. Gov't Code 2252.032](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.2252.htm))
- "the prime contractor may not withhold from a subcontractor a greater percentage of retainage than the percentage that may be withheld from the prime contractor by the governmental entity under the contract" ([Tex. Gov't Code 2252.032](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.2252.htm))
- "after completion of the work required to be performed under the contract by the prime contractor, including during the warranty period" ([Tex. Gov't Code 2252.032](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.2252.htm))
