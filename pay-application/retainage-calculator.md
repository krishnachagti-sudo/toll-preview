# Retainage calculator

At 10% retainage on $50,000.00 billed, $5,000.00 is held and $45,000.00 is earned net of retainage; at 5% it is $2,500.00 held and $47,500.00 earned.

- 10% of $50,000: $5,000.00
- 5% of $50,000: $2,500.00
- With 10% to 5% at 50%: $15,000.00

## With a reduction point

Many contracts hold 10% until the work is half done and less after. On a $240,000.00 contract the point is $120,000.00. At $180,000.00 complete:

| Contract wording | Held |
| --- | ---: |
| 10% to 50% complete, then 5% on later work | $15,000.00 |
| 5% on all work once past 50% complete | $9,000.00 |
| 10% throughout (no reduction) | $18,000.00 |

## Questions

### How do you calculate retainage?

Multiply the amount billed (work completed plus stored materials, to date) by the retainage rate: 10% of $50,000.00 is $5,000.00 held, so $45,000.00 is paid before previous payments are taken off.

### What is 5% retainage?

The payer holds 5% of each billing: $2,500.00 on $50,000.00.

### How does a reduction at 50% complete work?

It depends on the contract's words. "10% until 50% complete, then 5% on later work" holds $15,000.00 at $180,000.00 of $240,000.00; "5% on all work once past 50%" holds $9,000.00.
