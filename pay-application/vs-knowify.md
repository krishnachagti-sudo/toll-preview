# Knowify and a one-off pay application

Knowify is a subscription for trade contractors whose Core plan "Bid, execute, and invoice for fixed-price and AIA jobs" with one user (each additional user $29 a month); Endwise makes one G702/G703-style pay application at a time for $2.50 per pay application, with corrections of the same application free for 60 days and at most $29.00 a month.

From Knowify's own pages, read November 8, 2026.

|  | Knowify | Endwise pay application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Job management: estimating, project management, change orders, invoicing, AIA billing, job costing, QuickBooks integrations. | One pay application at a time, with checks. |
| Plans | Core (1 user), Advanced (10 users) and Enterprise; additional users $29/month. The base plan prices were not in the page text read. | $2.50 per pay application, with corrections of the same application free for 60 days and at most $29.00 a month. |
| AIA billing | Listed in its plans and finance features; its guide explains filling out a G702. | G702/G703-style summary and continuation sheet, PDF and CSV. |
| Trial | Free trial. | Totals, checks and a watermarked preview are free. |

## When Knowify is better

- You want estimating, job costing and billing in one system, synced with QuickBooks.
- Several people in the office work on the same jobs every day.

## When Endwise is better

- You only need the monthly pay application and already use something else for the rest.
- You do not want another subscription for one form.
