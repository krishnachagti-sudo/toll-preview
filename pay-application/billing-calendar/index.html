Pay application cut-off dates, November 2026 to December 2027

With a cut-off on the 25th, November 2026's pay application is due on November 25, 2026; across November 2026 to December 2027, 17 of the 42 cut-off dates on the 20th, the 25th or the month end fall on a weekend or a federal holiday and move to the business day before.

Holidays are the US federal holidays as observed (5 U.S.C. 6103 and OPM), from the business days tool on this site; your GC's office may close on other days.

A pay application that misses the cut-off usually waits for the next cycle, so a month's billing can slip by 30 days. Make it a few days early, check it, and send it with the lien waiver the contract asks for.

Lien waivers for each payment: lien waiver generator. Make the pay application on the tool page.
