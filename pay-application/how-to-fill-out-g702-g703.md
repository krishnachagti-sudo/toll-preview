## Steps

1. **Schedule of values (columns A to C).** List each part of the work and its scheduled value, as in the schedule of values submitted at the start. The total starts at the original contract sum.
2. **Previous applications (column D).** The work completed on earlier applications: last month's previous plus this-period columns, without the stored materials.
3. **This period (column E).** The work completed since, including stored materials that were installed this period.
4. **Materials presently stored (column F).** Materials on hand and not yet installed, recalculated each period; once installed they move into column E.
5. **Total, percent and balance (G, %, H).** G is D + E + F; the percent is G over C; H, the balance to finish, is C less G.
6. **Retainage (column I).** Used where retainage varies by line; with one rate for the whole contract it can be left empty and the retainage shown on the summary.
7. **Change orders.** Usually listed separately (their own sheet or at the end of the schedule); the contract sum adjusted by them goes on the summary.
8. **The summary sheet.** Carry the totals over: contract sum to date, completed and stored, retainage, earned less retainage, previous certificates, current payment due.
