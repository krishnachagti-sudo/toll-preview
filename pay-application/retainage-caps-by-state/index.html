State retainage law

Retainage caps by state

Of the 7 states whose retainage statutes were read on November 8, 2026, Minnesota caps private retainage at 5%, Florida and Kansas set no private percentage, and Texas instead makes the owner reserve 10%; on public work the limits run from 5% (Florida, Kansas, Minnesota, Virginia, Washington, Texas at $5 million or more) to 10% (Arizona until 50% complete, Texas under $5 million). The other 44 jurisdictions are not covered here.
