## What to collect per vendor

- Start date of the term and its length in months (or its last day).
- Amount paid, from the invoice.
- Which expense account it belongs to.
- The convention: these rows use actual days, so a renewal on the 19th posts 12 days in its first month.

## One journal per month

By schedule, November is 56 lines (two per vendor); by account it nets to 3. Use by-account for a short journal, by-schedule when the auditor wants each vendor's line.

```csv
Journal No.,Journal Date,Account Name,Debits,Credits,Description,Name
AM-20261130,11/30/2026,Marketing Software,1632.79,,"Amortization schedules, November 30, 2026 (28 entries)",
AM-20261130,11/30/2026,Prepaid Software,,5969.90,"Amortization schedules, November 30, 2026 (28 entries)",
AM-20261130,11/30/2026,Software Subscriptions,4337.11,,"Amortization schedules, November 30, 2026 (28 entries)",
```

## Renewals, price changes and upgrades

- Renewal: add a row with the new term; the old one ends on its own.
- Mid-term upgrade billed separately: add a row for the upgrade amount from its date to the end of the term.
- Cancelled with a refund: set the last day used and the refund; see [a prepaid cancelled mid-term](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/amortization-schedule-journal-entries/guides/prepaid-cancelled-mid-term/).

## Questions

### How do I expense an annual subscription over 12 months?

Record the payment to a prepaid account, then each month move the month's share to the software expense account until the term ends. With actual days, a subscription starting mid-month posts part of a month first and the rest in the 13th calendar month.

### What happens at renewal?

The renewal is a new prepaid row with its own start date and amount. The old row runs out on its own, so nothing is rebuilt; a price change is simply the new row's amount.
