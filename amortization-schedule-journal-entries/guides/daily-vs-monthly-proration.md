# Daily vs monthly proration for prepaids

For a $12,000.00 12-month policy starting November 17, 2026, equal months post $1,000.00 in November, the half-month convention $500.00, and actual days $460.27 (14 of 365 days); all three reach $12,000.00, and by December 31, 2026 they differ by up to $520.55.

## The three conventions

- monthly: equal months (the start month counts in full).
- half-month: half-month (half a month in the first and last month).
- daily: actual days in each month.

## Which is more common

Prepaid tools offer both: Dext's help names a by-months and a by-days method ("The "by months" method divides the prepayment equally across the months in the period."), and Ramp's guide says "Prorating by days is the other common approach." What matters most is applying one convention to every item and every month, so the balance can be rebuilt by anyone from the schedule.

## How auditors look at it

An auditor checks that the method is stated, applied to every item the same way, and that the balance at the period end recomputes from the schedule. The differences between conventions are timing within the term; over the term every one totals the amount paid.

## Questions

### Should prepaid amortization be daily or monthly?

Either is acceptable if applied the same way to every item: equal months is the simplest, actual days the closest to the coverage for mid-month starts. Write the choice down in your close procedures.
