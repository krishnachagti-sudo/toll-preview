## Steps

1. **Opening balance.** Agree each prepaid account's opening balance to last month's closing balance on the schedule.
2. **Additions.** Add the prepaids paid this month (bills coded to a prepaid account), each as a new schedule row.
3. **Amortization.** Take off this month's amortization from the schedule and post the journal.
4. **Closing balance.** Confirm the schedule's closing balance equals the GL balance of each prepaid account.
5. **Explain any difference.** Find the missed entry, the bill coded to the wrong account or the row missing from the schedule before closing the month.
