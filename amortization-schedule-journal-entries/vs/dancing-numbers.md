# Compared with Dancing Numbers

Dancing Numbers imports transactions you already have into QuickBooks, from $10.00 per month for 1 company file (Online) or $199 per year (Desktop); it does not compute the schedule, whereas this tool computes it and writes the file: a year of the worked loan is 12 journals and 36 lines for $0.50.

## Side by side

Dancing Numbers' side from its pricing page, read November 8, 2026

|  | Dancing Numbers | This tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job | Import, export and delete many transaction types | Compute prepaid, deferred revenue, loan and loan fee entries |
| Ledgers | Dancing Numbers for QuickBooks Online & Desktop | QuickBooks Online and Xero import files; a generic CSV |
| Schedule and interest split | Not part of an import tool | Computed |
| Price | $10.00 per month (Online, 1 company file); $199 per year (Desktop, up to 3 company files) | $0.50 per schedule, re-runs free for 12 months |

## When Dancing Numbers is better

- Bulk import of many transaction types (invoices, bills, payments) into QuickBooks.
- QuickBooks Desktop: the tool writes no Desktop import file.
- You already have the entries computed and only need them in.

## When this tool is

- The work is computing the entries: proration, rounding, the loan split.
- Xero as well as QuickBooks Online.
- A few schedules a month, without a subscription.
