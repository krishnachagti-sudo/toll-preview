# Month-end amortization calendar, 2026 to 2027

Month ends from November 2026 to December 2027 run from November 30, 2026 to December 31, 2027; with actual days a $36,500.00 policy from November 1, 2026 posts $100.00 a day, so $2,800.00 for the 28 days of February 2027 against $3,000.00 for November's 30, and the table gives each close's weekday, days and amounts.

## Dates that bring the most work

- December 31, 2026 and March 31, 2027: common year ends; run the [rollforward checklist](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/amortization-schedule-journal-entries/guides/prepaid-rollforward-checklist/) on every prepaid and deferred revenue account.
- January 2027: insurance and software renewals add new rows; see [annual SaaS for many vendors](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/amortization-schedule-journal-entries/guides/saas-prepaid-schedule/).
- January 1 and July 1: common policy renewal dates; a policy paid in December 2026 for 2027 is a new prepaid row.

For tax, a payment made in 2026 by a calendar-year business meets the 12-month rule's second limit only when the benefit ends by December 31, 2027 ("The end of the taxable year following the taxable year in which the payment is made.").
