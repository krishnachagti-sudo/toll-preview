Amortization of loan fees

Straight-line or effective interest

The tool spreads the fees evenly over the term. Lenders' fees are often required to be amortized with the effective-interest method instead, which puts more in the early months as interest falls; the two can differ. Ask your accountant which applies before relying on straight-line for financial statements.

The fees sit next to the loan's own interest: book both in the same month. The loan's schedule is on loan amortization journal entries.
