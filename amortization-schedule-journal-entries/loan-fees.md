# Amortization of loan fees

$4,500.00 of loan fees on a 60-month loan amortizes $75.00 a month straight-line: debit Interest Expense $75.00, credit Debt Issuance Costs $75.00, leaving $3,450.00 unamortized at December 31, 2027.

## Straight-line or effective interest

The tool spreads the fees evenly over the term. Lenders' fees are often required to be amortized with the effective-interest method instead, which puts more in the early months as interest falls; the two can differ. Ask your accountant which applies before relying on straight-line for financial statements.

The fees sit next to the loan's own interest: book both in the same month. The loan's schedule is on [loan amortization journal entries](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/amortization-schedule-journal-entries/loan/).
