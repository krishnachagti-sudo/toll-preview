# South Carolina PTO payout and sick leave

In South Carolina, unused vacation at separation is owed only as the written policy or agreement says (S.C. Code 41-10-10(2) (2019 copy)); under a policy that pays it out, 64 hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. We did not find a statewide paid sick leave law on the South Carolina sites we read.

## The rule

Wages include vacation, holiday and sick leave payments due under an employer policy or contract. (S.C. Code 41-10-10(2) (2019 copy)).

- "includes vacation, holiday, and sick leave payments which are due to an employee under any employer policy or employment contract." [S.C. Code 41-10-10 (2019 session version, the newest copy the legislature's site served)](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/Archives/CodeofLaws2019/t41c010.php)
