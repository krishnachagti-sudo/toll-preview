# Year-end carryover, December 31, 2026

On December 31, 2026, a full-time employee who worked 40 hours a week all year and used no sick time carries 40 hours into 2027 in Washington, where the 12 hours above the 40-hour carryover limit can be dropped, and 40 in Maryland, where earning stopped at the 40-hour yearly limit; the table works out every place we read.
