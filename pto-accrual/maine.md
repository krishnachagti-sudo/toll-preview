# Maine PTO payout and sick leave

In Maine, unused vacation at separation must be paid, as earned vacation counts as wages (26 M.R.S. 626); 64 unused hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. Maine's paid sick leave law is a separate bank: 1 hour per 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year (26 M.R.S. 637).

## The rule

Unused paid vacation accrued on and after January 1, 2023 is paid at separation, unless the employer has 10 or fewer employees or is public (a union contract can override). (26 M.R.S. 626).

- "All unused paid vacation accrued pursuant to the employer's vacation policy on and after January 1, 2023 must be paid to the employee on cessation of employment unless the employee is employed by an employer with 10 or fewer employees or by a public employer." [26 M.R.S. 626, Cessation of employment](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/26/title26sec626.html)

Unused hours times the final regular rate (salary divided by 2,080 for a full-time salaried employee).

## Sick leave

Maine's paid sick leave law is a separate bank: 1 hour per 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year (26 M.R.S. 637). [Maine sick leave calculator](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pto-accrual/sick-leave/maine/).

## When the final paycheck is due

The payout goes in the final paycheck where it is owed; the state's final pay law sets when that paycheck is due.

## Questions

### Does Maine require PTO payout at termination?

Unused paid vacation accrued on and after January 1, 2023 is paid at separation, unless the employer has 10 or fewer employees or is public (a union contract can override). (26 M.R.S. 626).

### Does Maine require paid sick leave?

Yes: 1 hour per 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year. (26 M.R.S. 637).
