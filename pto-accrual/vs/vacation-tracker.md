# A Vacation Tracker alternative for the numbers

For 25 employees paid every two weeks, running every employee's PTO and sick balances each payday over the API costs 25 x 26 x $0.01 = $6.50 a year here; Vacation Tracker lists $2 per user a month with a $50 monthly minimum, $600 a year (read November 9, 2026), for a full leave tracker rather than the state-law numbers alone.

## What Vacation Tracker's page says

- Core: $2 per user a month, billed monthly, with a $50 monthly minimum that covers up to 25 users
- Complete: $4 per user a month, with a $100 monthly minimum
- A 14-day free trial with no credit card
- A free PTO payout calculator page (before and after taxes, by state), published October 20, 2026

Read on November 9, 2026: [Vacation Tracker, Pricing](https://vacationtracker.io/pricing/).

## What this tool does

- Two banks per employee, paycheck by paycheck: your PTO policy and the state or city sick law, with the yearly cap, balance cap, waiting period and year-end carryover applied.
- Every rule quoted from the state's or city's own statute or agency page with the date read; places we could not read are listed as not covered.
- The payout owed at separation with the state's vacation rule.
- The same answers over a REST API and MCP, $0.01 a lookup or $0.01 an employee in a batch (at least $0.10), free on the web page, never more than $9.90 in a month.

## When Vacation Tracker is better

You want employees to request leave and managers to approve it in Slack or Teams, with a shared calendar; this tool keeps the numbers, not the requests.

> Worked out from each state's or city's own published text, quoted with the date we read it. Not legal advice. The limits shown are the most an employer may apply; your written policy, a union contract or a local ordinance can give more. Places whose official sites we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.
