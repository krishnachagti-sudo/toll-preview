# BambooHR and this tool

For 25 employees paid every two weeks, running every employee's PTO and sick balances each payday over the API costs 25 x 26 x $0.01 = $6.50 a year here; BambooHR lists $10 per employee a month on Core, $3,000 a year (read November 9, 2026), for a full HR system rather than the state-law numbers alone.

## What BambooHR's page says

- Core: $10 per employee a month; Pro: $17 per employee a month (prices marked with an asterisk; a quote form)
- Priced per employee per month with automatic volume discounts

Read on November 9, 2026: [BambooHR, Pricing](https://www.bamboohr.com/pricing/).

## What this tool does

- Two banks per employee, paycheck by paycheck: your PTO policy and the state or city sick law, with the yearly cap, balance cap, waiting period and year-end carryover applied.
- Every rule quoted from the state's or city's own statute or agency page with the date read; places we could not read are listed as not covered.
- The payout owed at separation with the state's vacation rule.
- The same answers over a REST API and MCP, $0.01 a lookup or $0.01 an employee in a batch (at least $0.10), free on the web page, never more than $9.90 in a month.

## When BambooHR is better

You want one HR system for records, onboarding, payroll and time off together, and the per-employee fee is worth it for all of that.

Places whose official sites we could not read are listed as not covered rather than guessed.
