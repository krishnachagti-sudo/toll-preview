# Maryland PTO payout and sick leave

In Maryland, unused vacation at separation must be paid unless a written policy says otherwise (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-505(b)); 64 unused hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. Maryland's paid sick leave law is a separate bank: at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304), and unused sick leave need not be paid out (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(j)).

## The rule

Accrued leave is paid at termination unless a written policy limits it, the employee was told of the leave benefits, and the policy denies payment. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-505(b)).

- "An employer is not required to pay accrued leave to an employee if: (1) the employer has a written policy that limits the compensation of accrued leave to employees; (2) the employer notified the employee of the employer's leave benefits" [Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-505](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gle&section=3-505&enactments=false)

## Sick leave

Maryland's paid sick leave law is a separate bank: at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304), and unused sick leave need not be paid out (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(j)). The payout goes in the final paycheck where it is owed; the state's final pay law sets when that paycheck is due.

## Questions

### Does Maryland require PTO payout at termination?

Accrued leave is paid at termination unless a written policy limits it, the employee was told of the leave benefits, and the policy denies payment. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-505(b)).

### Does Maryland require paid sick leave?

Yes: At least 1 hour per 30 hours worked. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304).
