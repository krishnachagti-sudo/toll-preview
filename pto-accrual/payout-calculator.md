# PTO payout calculator

Paying out 64 hours at a final rate of $28.00 owes $1,792.00 gross; Illinois requires it in the final pay (820 ILCS 115/5; 56 Ill. Adm. Code 300.520), while Texas owes it only under a written policy or agreement (Tex. Lab. Code 61.001(7)). A $62,400 salary is $30.00 an hour (divided by 2,080), so 40 hours is $1,200.00.

## Taxes

The figures are gross. A payout is wages and is taxed as wages; this tool does not work out withholding.

## Steps

1. **Find the final rate.** The final regular hourly rate, or the salary divided by 2,080 hours a year (by 40 a week).
2. **Multiply.** Unused hours times that rate is the gross payout.
3. **Check the state.** Some states require the payout; others follow your written policy. Sick leave under a state law is usually not paid out.

## Questions

### How do I calculate a PTO payout?

Unused hours times the final regular rate (salary divided by 2,080 for salaried staff): 64 hours x $28 = $1,792.00 gross. Whether it is owed depends on the state and your policy.
