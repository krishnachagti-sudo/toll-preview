Paid sick leave laws by state and city

As of November 9, 2026, we read a statewide paid sick leave law on the own sites of 17 jurisdictions (Alaska, Arizona, California, Colorado, Connecticut, District of Columbia, Illinois, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, New Jersey, New York, Oregon, Vermont, Washington), plus Virginia's law for home health workers; 12 of them use 1 hour per 30 hours worked, the most common rate. 5 more states with laws listed by payroll vendors (Missouri, Nebraska, Nevada, New Mexico, Rhode Island) could not be read from our network and are not shown.

Worked out from each state's or city's own published text, quoted with the date we read it. Not legal advice.
