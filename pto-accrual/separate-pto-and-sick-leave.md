# Separate PTO and sick leave banks

A combined PTO bank meets a state sick law only if it can be used for the same purposes and under the same conditions (A.R.S. 23-372(E)); in Illinois a combined bank that also serves the Paid Leave for All Workers Act becomes payable vacation, so 80 combined hours at $28.00 owe $2,240.00 at separation, where 40 vacation hours and 40 sick hours kept in separate banks owe $1,120.00.

## What the agencies say

- "Any employer with a paid leave policy, such as a paid time off policy, who makes available an amount of paid leave sufficient to meet the accrual requirements of this section that may be used for the same purposes and under the same conditions as earned paid sick time under this article is not required to provide additional paid sick time." [A.R.S. 23-372, Accrual of earned paid sick time](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00372.htm)
- "A paid time off policy or collective bargaining agreement satisfies HFWA if it gives all the same rights" [Colorado Department of Labor and Employment, INFO #6: Paid Leave under the Healthy Families and Workplaces Act (last updated July 19, 2023)](https://cdle.colorado.gov/sites/cdle/files/info_%236_hfwa_summary_%26_overview_07.19.23.pdf)
- "if your employer provides 1 week of vacation and 1 week of PLAWA and maintains separate recordkeeping for both types of leave, then your employer would not have to payout any unused accrued PLAWA time, but would have to payout any unused accrued vacation time." [Illinois Department of Labor, Paid Leave for All Workers Act FAQ](https://labor.illinois.gov/faqs/paidleavefaq.html)

## The difference at termination

Illinois, $28.00 an hour: separate banks against one combined bank

| Setup | Vacation hours | Sick hours | Owed at separation |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Separate banks | 40 | 40 | $1,120.00 |
| One combined PTO bank | 80 (combined) | 0 | $2,240.00 |

## Questions

### Can PTO and sick leave be combined?

Yes, where the combined bank gives at least what the sick law requires and can be used for the same reasons under the same conditions (Arizona and Colorado say so in these words). In Illinois a combined bank is payable at separation as vacation.

### Is PTO different from sick leave?

A state sick leave law sets a minimum for one purpose; PTO is the employer's own policy. Many states treat earned vacation as wages at separation, while their sick leave laws do not require a payout.
