# South Dakota PTO payout and sick leave

In South Dakota, unused vacation at separation is owed only as the written policy or agreement says (South Dakota DLR); under a policy that pays it out, 64 hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. South Dakota's labor department says the state has no law requiring paid leave, sick time included.

## The rule

No law requires paid leave; it is a matter of employer policy. (South Dakota DLR).

- "South Dakota has no law requiring paid leave. This is a matter of employer policy." [South Dakota Department of Labor and Regulation, What you need to know about South Dakota labor laws (rev. 01/2026)](https://dlr.sd.gov/employment_laws/publications/what_need_know_laws.pdf)

South Dakota's labor department says the state has no law requiring paid leave, sick time included.
