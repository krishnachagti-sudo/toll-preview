# West Virginia PTO payout and sick leave

In West Virginia, unused vacation at separation is owed only as the written policy or agreement says (W. Va. Code 21-5 (Fact Sheet 6)); under a policy that pays it out, 64 hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. We did not find a statewide paid sick leave law on the West Virginia sites we read.

## The rule

Fringe benefits payable under a written policy are part of final wages unless the policy sets a later date. (W. Va. Code 21-5 (Fact Sheet 6)).

- "Fringe benefits that are earned and payable upon separation of employment based on the terms and conditions of written company policy are normally considered as part of "final wages" unless the policy specifically designates them payable at a later date." [West Virginia Division of Labor, WPCA Fact Sheet 6: Payment of Final Wages (July 2016)](https://labor.wv.gov/media/85/download?inline=)
