# Oklahoma PTO payout and sick leave

In Oklahoma, unused vacation at separation is owed only as the written policy or agreement says (Oklahoma DOL); under a policy that pays it out, 64 hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. We did not find a statewide paid sick leave law on the Oklahoma sites we read.

## The rule

A written policy promising payout makes vacation wages. (Oklahoma DOL).

- "If your employer has a written policy (like in an employee handbook) that promises payout of vacation, sick leave, or bonuses" [Oklahoma Department of Labor, Protect Your Pay](https://oklahoma.gov/labor/workplace-rights/protect-your-pay.html)
