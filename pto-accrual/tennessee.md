# Tennessee PTO payout and sick leave

In Tennessee, unused vacation at separation is owed only as the written policy or agreement says (Tenn. Code Ann. 50-2-103 (TDLWD)); under a policy that pays it out, 64 hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. We did not find a statewide paid sick leave law on the Tennessee sites we read.

## The rule

Only if the employer's policy or labor agreement requires it. (Tenn. Code Ann. 50-2-103 (TDLWD)).

- "Tennessee law does not require that an employee's final wages include such compensation." [Tennessee Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Wages, fringe benefits, paychecks (Tenn. Code Ann. 50-2-103)](https://www.tn.gov/workforce/employees/labor-laws/labor-laws-redirect/wages-breaks.html)
