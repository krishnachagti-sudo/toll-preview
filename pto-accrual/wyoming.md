# Wyoming PTO payout and sick leave

In Wyoming, unused vacation at separation must be paid unless a written policy says otherwise (Wyoming DWS employer FAQ); 64 unused hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. We did not find a statewide paid sick leave law on the Wyoming sites we read.

## The rule

Payout can be denied only if a written policy clearly says so and the employee acknowledged it. (Wyoming DWS employer FAQ).

- "Vacation: You can deny payout only if your written policy clearly states it and the employee acknowledged it." [Wyoming Department of Workforce Services, Labor Standards employer FAQ](https://dws.wyo.gov/dws-division/labor-standards/employers/faq/)

## Payout amounts

Unused hours times the final regular rate (salary divided by 2,080 for a full-time salaried employee).
