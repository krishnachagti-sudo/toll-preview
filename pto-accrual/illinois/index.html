Illinois PTO payout and sick leave

In Illinois, unused vacation at separation must be paid, as earned vacation counts as wages (820 ILCS 115/5; 56 Ill. Adm. Code 300.520); 64 unused hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. Illinois's paid sick leave law is a separate bank: 1 hour per 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours in a 12-month period; or front-loaded (820 ILCS 192), and unused sick leave need not be paid out (IDOL PLAWA FAQ).

The rule

Earned vacation is final compensation, paid by the next regular payday; a policy may not forfeit earned vacation at separation. (820 ILCS 115/5; 56 Ill. Adm. Code 300.520).

  • "All final compensation, including bonus payments, vacation pay, wages and commissions must be paid on your next regularly scheduled payday." Illinois Department of Labor, Wage Payment and Collection Act FAQ (820 ILCS 115/5)
  • "the employment policy is not allowed to provide for a forfeiture of earned time upon separation." Illinois Department of Labor, Vacation FAQ

Use it or lose it

Allowed if employees get a reasonable chance to use the vacation (Section 300.520). "Can a company have a policy that vacation must be used by a certain date or it is lost? Yes." Illinois Department of Labor, Vacation FAQ

Payout amounts

Unused hours times the final regular rate (salary divided by 2,080 for a full-time salaried employee).

Sick leave

Illinois's paid sick leave law is a separate bank: 1 hour per 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours in a 12-month period; or front-loaded (820 ILCS 192), and unused sick leave need not be paid out (IDOL PLAWA FAQ). Illinois sick leave calculator.

When the final paycheck is due

The payout goes in the final paycheck where it is owed; the state's final pay law sets when that paycheck is due.
