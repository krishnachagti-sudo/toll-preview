One hour per 30 hours worked

At 1 hour per 30 hours worked, a 20-hour-a-week employee earns 0.67 hours a week and 34.67 hours over 52 weeks before any cap; the places we read use rates from 1 per 30 to 1 per 87 (D.C.'s smallest employers).

Rounding

Work from the hours worked so far in the year, not paycheck by paycheck, so fractions never get lost: 44 hours every two weeks at 1 per 40 is 1.1 hours a paycheck and exactly 28.6 hours over 26 paychecks.

Front-loading instead

  • Alaska: 56 hours at the start of the year (Alaska DOLWD FAQ)
  • Arizona: 40 hours at the start of the year (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(1))
  • Illinois: 40 hours at the start of the year (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)
  • Maryland: 40 hours at the start of the year (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(d))
  • Minnesota: 80 hours at the start of the year (Minn. Stat. 181.9446(b)(2))
  • New Jersey: 40 hours at the start of the year (NJDOL)
  • Oregon: 40 hours at the start of the year (Oregon BOLI)
  • Los Angeles, CA: 48 hours at the start of the year (LA Office of Wage Standards)
  • Philadelphia, PA: 40 hours at the start of the year (Philadelphia DOL)
