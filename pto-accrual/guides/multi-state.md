# Which sick leave law applies

Sick leave follows where the employee works, not where the employer is: Oregon employees earn sick time even when the employer is out of state, and a more generous city ordinance applies over Washington's law. A full-time Seattle employee at a 250-person employer earns 69.33 hours in 2027 under Seattle's 1-per-30 tier, against 52 hours under the state's 1 per 40.

## What the agencies say about location

- Maine: Applies to employers with more than 10 employees for more than 120 days in a calendar year; the leave can be used for any reason under the section. "An employer that employs more than 10 employees in the usual and regular course of business for more than 120 days in any calendar year shall permit each employee to earn paid leave" [26 M.R.S. 637, Earned paid leave](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/26/title26sec637-2.html)
- Minnesota: Covers employees expected to work at least 80 hours a year in Minnesota. "are anticipated to work at least 80 hours in a year for an employer in Minnesota" [Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry, Earned sick and safe time](https://www.dli.mn.gov/sick-leave)
- Oregon: Oregon employees earn sick time even when the employer is located in another state. "Oregon employees earn sick time even if their employer is located out of state." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Sick time](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/sick-time.aspx)
- Virginia: Only home health workers who average at least 20 hours a week or 90 hours a month. ""Employee" means a home health worker who works on average at least 20 hours per week or 90 hours per month." [Va. Code 40.1-33.3, Definitions (home health worker)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title40.1/chapter3/section40.1-33.3/)
- Washington: Where a local ordinance is more generous than state law, the ordinance applies. "If a local ordinance requires more generous paid sick leave benefits for employees than state law, those requirements will apply." [Washington Department of Labor & Industries, Paid Sick Leave](https://lni.wa.gov/workers-rights/leave/paid-sick-leave/)
- New York City, NY: Employees anywhere count toward employer size, but only those who work in New York City have the rights. "However, only employees who work in New York City have rights to protected time off under the Law." [NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection, Protected Time Off Law FAQs](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/paid-sick-leave-FAQs.page)
- Philadelphia, PA: Covers employees who work at least 40 hours a year in Philadelphia. "Employees who work at least 40 hours a year in Philadelphia can earn sick leave." [City of Philadelphia Department of Labor, Paid sick leave one-pager (2025)](https://www.phila.gov/media/20250731151754/dol-paid-sick-leave-one-pager-en.pdf)
- Saint Paul, MN: Covers employees who work at least 80 hours a year inside the city. "who work within the geographic boundaries of the Saint Paul for at least 80 hours in a year." [City of Saint Paul HREEO, Earned Sick and Safe Time](https://www.stpaul.gov/departments/human-rights-equal-economic-opportunity/labor-standards-enforcement-and-education/earned-sick-safe-time)

## The same hours under two laws

80 hours every two weeks through 2027

| Law | Rule | Earned in 2027 |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Washington | 1 hour per 40 hours worked; carryover may be limited to 40 hours | 52 |
| Seattle, 250 or more FTEs | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; carryover may be limited to 72 hours | 69.33 |

## Keeping records

- Record hours by work location each pay period; the hours in each place drive that place's accrual.
- Count employer size the way each law does (Seattle and New York City count employees everywhere).
- Keep one sick bank per law that applies and a separate PTO bank for your own policy.

The limits shown are the most an employer may apply; your written policy, a union contract or a local ordinance can give more.
