# PTO hours per pay period

120 hours a year is 4.62 hours a paycheck every two weeks (120 / 26), 5.00 twice a month, 2.31 weekly and 10.00 monthly; per hour worked it is 0.0577 hours (120 / 2,080).

## Rounding and the true-up

Crediting 4.62 hours on each of 26 paychecks gives 120.12 hours, not 120. The engine credits the year to date instead (120 x paychecks so far / 26, rounded), so the last paycheck of the year lands on exactly 120.

## Per hour worked instead

Hourly staff often accrue per hour worked: 120 / 2,080 = 0.0577 hours per hour. A part-timer at 20 hours a week then earns 60.01 hours a year.
