# Carryover, caps and use-it-or-lose-it

Washington lets an employer limit carryover to 40 hours (RCW 49.46.210(1)(j)): a full-time employee who earns 52 hours in 2027 and uses none carries 40 into 2028 and loses 12; a cap on accrual stops earning during the year, a cap on use limits what can be taken, and a carryover limit trims the balance at year end.

## Three different limits

- A cap on accrual stops earning for the rest of the year (Minnesota: 48 hours).
- A cap on use limits what can be taken in a year, even with more in the bank (Maryland: 64 hours).
- A carryover limit trims the balance at year end (Washington: 40 hours).

## Vacation is different

For vacation under your own policy, the state's wage rules decide: Illinois allows a use-by date but not forfeiting earned vacation at separation (820 ILCS 115/5; 56 Ill. Adm. Code 300.520).
