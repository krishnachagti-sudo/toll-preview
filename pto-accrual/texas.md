# Texas PTO payout and sick leave

In Texas, unused vacation at separation is owed only as the written policy or agreement says (Tex. Lab. Code 61.001(7)); under a policy that pays it out, 64 hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. We did not find a statewide paid sick leave law on the Texas sites we read.

## The rule

Vacation and sick leave pay are wages only when owed under a written agreement or written policy. (Tex. Lab. Code 61.001(7)).

- "vacation pay, holiday pay, sick leave pay, parental leave pay, or severance pay owed to an employee under a written agreement with the employer or under a written policy of the employer." [Texas Labor Code 61.001, Definitions (Payment of Wages)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/LA/htm/LA.61.htm)
