# Oregon PTO payout and sick leave

In Oregon, unused vacation at separation is owed only as the written policy or agreement says (Oregon BOLI); under a policy that pays it out, 64 hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. Oregon's paid sick leave law is a separate bank: at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year, or 40 hours front-loaded (ORS 653.606), and unused sick leave need not be paid out (Oregon BOLI).

## The rule

No legal requirement; an established policy or agreement on accrued vacation must be honored at termination. (Oregon BOLI).

- "There is no legal requirement to offer these benefits. Your employer is required to honor any established policy or agreement relating to the payment of benefits, such as accrued vacation or severance pay, upon termination." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Paychecks](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/paychecks.aspx)

## Questions

### Does Oregon require PTO payout at termination?

No legal requirement; an established policy or agreement on accrued vacation must be honored at termination. (Oregon BOLI).

### Does Oregon require paid sick leave?

Yes: At least 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year, or 40 hours front-loaded. (ORS 653.606).
