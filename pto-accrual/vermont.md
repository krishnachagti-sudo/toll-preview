# Vermont PTO payout and sick leave

In Vermont, unused vacation at separation is owed only as the written policy or agreement says (Vermont DOL WH-13 (2019)); under a policy that pays it out, 64 hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. Vermont's paid sick leave law is a separate bank: 1 hour per 52 hours worked, overtime included; up to 40 hours a year may be used (2019 on) (21 V.S.A. 482).

## The rule

Not required, but written agreements (a handbook counts) providing vacation make the employer liable for it. (Vermont DOL WH-13 (2019)).

- "employers who are parties to written agreements, which can be in the form of an employee handbook, memorandum, correspondence, etc., providing for vacation time, sick leave, holidays and/or severance pay are liable to their employees for these benefits." [Vermont Department of Labor, A Summary of Vermont Wage and Hour Laws (WH-13, 2019)](https://labor.vermont.gov/sites/labor/files/doc_library/WH-13-Wage-and-Hour-Laws-2019%20.pdf)

## Sick leave

Vermont's paid sick leave law is a separate bank: 1 hour per 52 hours worked, overtime included; up to 40 hours a year may be used (2019 on) (21 V.S.A. 482). [Vermont sick leave calculator](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/pto-accrual/sick-leave/vermont/).

## Questions

### Does Vermont require PTO payout at termination?

Not required, but written agreements (a handbook counts) providing vacation make the employer liable for it. (Vermont DOL WH-13 (2019)).

### Does Vermont require paid sick leave?

Yes: 1 hour per 52 hours worked, overtime included; up to 40 hours a year may be used (2019 on). (21 V.S.A. 482).
