# North Dakota PTO payout and sick leave

In North Dakota, unused vacation at separation must be paid, with narrow written exceptions (N.D. Cent. Code 34-14-09.2); 64 unused hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. We did not find a statewide paid sick leave law on the North Dakota sites we read.

## The rule

Accrued PTO is paid at separation; on a voluntary quit a private employer may withhold it only with written notice at hiring, under one year of service and under five days' notice. (N.D. Cent. Code 34-14-09.2).

- "If an employee separates from employment voluntarily, a private employer may withhold payment for accrued paid time off if: a. At the time of hiring, the employer provided the employee written notice of the limitation on payment of accrued paid time off; b. The employee has been employed by the employer for less than one year; and c. The employee gave the employer less than five days' written or verbal notice." [N.D. Cent. Code 34-14-09.2, Limitations on accrued paid time off](https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t34c14.pdf)
