# Vermont paid sick leave

Vermont: 1 hour per 52 hours worked, overtime included; up to 40 hours a year may be used (2019 on) (Vermont DOL notice). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 22 hours of sick time.

## Vermont in its own words

- "An employee will earn one hour of earned sick time for every 52 hours of actual work, including overtime. An employee will be entitled to use up to 40 hours in 2019 and subsequent years."
