# Michigan paid sick leave

Michigan: at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked; use may be limited to 72 hours a year (40 at a small business) (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 11-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

Michigan: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 11 or more employees | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; use may be limited to 72 hours a year |
| Small business, 10 or fewer employees (accrual from October 1, 2025) | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; use may be limited to 40 hours a year |

- A small business employs 10 or fewer employees. (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ)
- Accrual began February 21, 2025 (October 1, 2025 for a small business), or at hire if later. (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ)
- Front-loading is allowed as an alternative to accrual (at least 72 hours, or 40 at a small business, for full-time employees); the front-load amount is not worked out here. (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ)

## Michigan in its own words

- "An employer is considered a “small business” if it employs 10 or fewer employees." [Michigan Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity, Earned Sick Time Act: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)](https://www.michigan.gov/leo/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WAGE-HOUR/LEO-ESTA-FAQ-English.pdf) (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ)
- "Employees of a small business shall accrue a minimum of one hour of earned sick time for every 30 hours worked but, shall not be entitled to use more than 40 hours of paid earned sick time in a calendar year unless the employer selects a higher limit." [Michigan Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity, Earned Sick Time Act: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)](https://www.michigan.gov/leo/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WAGE-HOUR/LEO-ESTA-FAQ-English.pdf) (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ)
- "All other employees shall accrue a minimum of one hour of paid earned sick time for every 30 hours worked but shall not be entitled to use more than 72 hours of paid earned sick time per year, unless the employer selects a higher limit." [Michigan Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity, Earned Sick Time Act: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)](https://www.michigan.gov/leo/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WAGE-HOUR/LEO-ESTA-FAQ-English.pdf) (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ)
- "For a small business, October 1, 2025, or upon commencement of the employee’s employment, whichever is later." [Michigan Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity, Earned Sick Time Act: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)](https://www.michigan.gov/leo/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WAGE-HOUR/LEO-ESTA-FAQ-English.pdf) (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ)
- "For all other employers, accrual begins on February 21, 2025, or upon commencement of the employee's employment, whichever is later." [Michigan Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity, Earned Sick Time Act: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)](https://www.michigan.gov/leo/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WAGE-HOUR/LEO-ESTA-FAQ-English.pdf) (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ)
- "Yes. Frontloading is considered an alternative to the accrual method outlined above. If frontloading, the hours must be available for immediate use." [Michigan Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity, Earned Sick Time Act: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)](https://www.michigan.gov/leo/-/media/Project/Websites/leo/Documents/WAGE-HOUR/LEO-ESTA-FAQ-English.pdf) (Michigan LEO ESTA FAQ)
