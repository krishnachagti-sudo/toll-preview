Sick leave by state

Maine paid sick leave

Maine: 1 hour per 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year (26 M.R.S. 637(3)). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 28.60 hours of sick time.

The rule

Maine: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below
EmployerRule
More than 10 employees1 hour per 40 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year
  • Applies to employers with more than 10 employees for more than 120 days in a calendar year; the leave can be used for any reason under the section. (26 M.R.S. 637(2))
  • Use may be held until 120 days of employment. (26 M.R.S. 637(3))

Maine in its own words

  • "An employer that employs more than 10 employees in the usual and regular course of business for more than 120 days in any calendar year shall permit each employee to earn paid leave" 26 M.R.S. 637, Earned paid leave (26 M.R.S. 637(2))
  • "An employee is entitled to earn one hour of paid leave from a single employer for every 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours in one year of employment." 26 M.R.S. 637, Earned paid leave (26 M.R.S. 637(3))
  • "the employer is not required to permit use of the leave before the employee has been employed by that employer for 120 days during a one-year period." 26 M.R.S. 637, Earned paid leave (26 M.R.S. 637(3))
  • "Accrued and unused hours of earned paid leave from the previous year of employment must be available for use by an employee in the year of employment immediately following the previous year." 26 M.R.S. 637, Earned paid leave (26 M.R.S. 637(3))

Questions

How much sick leave do employees earn in Maine?

1 hour per 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year. (26 M.R.S. 637(3)).

When can a new employee in Maine start using sick leave?

Use may be held until 120 days of employment. (26 M.R.S. 637(3)).

Does sick leave carry over to the next year in Maine?

Unused hours carry into the next year and do not reduce what can be earned that year. (26 M.R.S. 637(3)).
