# Arizona paid sick leave

Arizona: 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours a year to accrue or use (15 or more employees), 24 hours (fewer than 15) (A.R.S. 23-372(A), (B)). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 15-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

Arizona: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 15 or more employees | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; or 40 hours front-loaded each year |
| Fewer than 15 employees | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 24 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 24 hours a year; or 24 hours front-loaded each year |

- Carried over, subject to the yearly use limit; or paid out at year end with the next year front-loaded. (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(4))
- The whole year may be given at the start of the year. (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(1))
- An employer may make new hires wait until the 90th calendar day. (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(2))
- No payout required at separation. (A.R.S. 23-372(F))
- Reinstated on rehire within nine months. (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(5))
- A PTO policy that gives enough leave for the same purposes and conditions satisfies the law. (A.R.S. 23-372(E))

## Arizona in its own words

- "Employees of an employer with 15 or more employees shall accrue a minimum of one hour of earned paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, but employees shall not be entitled to accrue or use more than 40 hours of earned paid sick time per year, unless the employer selects a higher limit." [A.R.S. 23-372, Accrual of earned paid sick time](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00372.htm) (A.R.S. 23-372(A), (B))
- "Employees of an employer with fewer than 15 employees shall accrue a minimum of one hour of earned paid sick time for every 30 hours worked, but employees shall not be entitled to accrue or use more than 24 hours of earned paid sick time per year, unless the employer selects a higher limit." [A.R.S. 23-372, Accrual of earned paid sick time](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00372.htm) (A.R.S. 23-372(A), (B))
- "Earned paid sick time shall be carried over to the following year, subject to the limitations on usage in subsections A and B." [A.R.S. 23-372, Accrual of earned paid sick time](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00372.htm) (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(4))
- "An employer may provide all earned paid sick time that an employee is expected to accrue in a year at the beginning of the year." [A.R.S. 23-372, Accrual of earned paid sick time](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00372.htm) (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(1))
- "an employer may require an employee hired after July 1, 2017, to wait until the ninetieth calendar day after commencing employment before using accrued earned paid sick time" [A.R.S. 23-372, Accrual of earned paid sick time](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00372.htm) (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(2))
- "Nothing in this article shall be construed as requiring financial or other reimbursement to an employee from an employer upon the employee's termination, resignation, retirement or other separation from employment for accrued earned paid sick time that has not been used." [A.R.S. 23-372, Accrual of earned paid sick time](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00372.htm) (A.R.S. 23-372(F))
- "When there is a separation from employment and the employee is rehired within nine months of separation by the same employer, previously accrued earned paid sick time that had not been used shall be reinstated." [A.R.S. 23-372, Accrual of earned paid sick time](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00372.htm) (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(5))
- "Any employer with a paid leave policy, such as a paid time off policy, who makes available an amount of paid leave sufficient to meet the accrual requirements of this section that may be used for the same purposes and under the same conditions as earned paid sick time under this article is not required to provide additional paid sick time." [A.R.S. 23-372, Accrual of earned paid sick time](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00372.htm) (A.R.S. 23-372(E))

## Questions

### How much sick leave do employees earn in Arizona?

1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours a year to accrue or use (15 or more employees), 24 hours (fewer than 15). (A.R.S. 23-372(A), (B)).

### Does unused sick leave have to be paid out when someone leaves in Arizona?

No payout required at separation. (A.R.S. 23-372(F)).

### When can a new employee in Arizona start using sick leave?

An employer may make new hires wait until the 90th calendar day. (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(2)).

### Does sick leave carry over to the next year in Arizona?

Carried over, subject to the yearly use limit; or paid out at year end with the next year front-loaded. (A.R.S. 23-372(D)(4)).
