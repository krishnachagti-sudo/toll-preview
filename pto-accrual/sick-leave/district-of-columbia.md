# District of Columbia paid sick leave

District of Columbia: 1 hour per 37 hours worked up to 7 days a year (100 or more employees); per 43 hours up to 5 days (25 to 99); per 87 hours up to 3 days (24 or fewer) (D.C. Code 32-531.02(a)). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 100-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 30.92 hours of sick time.

## The rule

District of Columbia: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 100 or more employees | 1 hour per 37 hours worked; up to 7 days a year (56 hours at 8 hours a day) |
| 25 to 99 employees | 1 hour per 43 hours worked; up to 5 days a year (40 hours at 8 hours a day) |
| 24 or fewer employees | 1 hour per 87 hours worked; up to 3 days a year (24 hours at 8 hours a day) |

- Use may start after 90 days of service. (D.C. Code 32-531.02(c)(1))
- Reinstated on rehire within one year. (D.C. Code 32-531.02(c)(3))

## District of Columbia in its own words

- "An employer with 100 or more employees shall provide for each employee not less than one hour of paid leave for every 37 hours worked, not to exceed 7 days per calendar year." [D.C. Code 32-531.02, Provision of paid leave](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-531.02) (D.C. Code 32-531.02(a))
- "An employer with at least 25, but not more than 99, employees shall provide for each employee not less than one hour of paid leave for every 43 hours worked, not to exceed 5 days per calendar year." [D.C. Code 32-531.02, Provision of paid leave](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-531.02) (D.C. Code 32-531.02(a))
- "An employer with 24 or fewer employees shall provide not less than one hour of paid leave for every 87 hours worked, not to exceed 3 days per calendar year." [D.C. Code 32-531.02, Provision of paid leave](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-531.02) (D.C. Code 32-531.02(a))
- "An employee may begin to access paid leave after 90 days of service with his or her employer." [D.C. Code 32-531.02, Provision of paid leave](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-531.02) (D.C. Code 32-531.02(c)(1))
- "When there is a separation from employment and the employee is rehired within one year of separation by the same employer, previously accrued unused paid leave shall be reinstated." [D.C. Code 32-531.02, Provision of paid leave](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-531.02) (D.C. Code 32-531.02(c)(3))

## Questions

### How much sick leave do employees earn in District of Columbia?

1 hour per 37 hours worked up to 7 days a year (100 or more employees); per 43 hours up to 5 days (25 to 99); per 87 hours up to 3 days (24 or fewer). (D.C. Code 32-531.02(a)).

### When can a new employee in District of Columbia start using sick leave?

Use may start after 90 days of service. (D.C. Code 32-531.02(c)(1)).
