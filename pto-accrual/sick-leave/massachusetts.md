# Massachusetts paid sick leave

Massachusetts: at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year to earn and use (Massachusetts Attorney General). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 11-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

Massachusetts: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 11 or more employees: paid | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year |
| Fewer than 11 employees: unpaid | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; unpaid (job-protected) at this employer size |

- Paid with 11 or more employees; earned but unpaid with fewer than 11. (Massachusetts Attorney General)

## Massachusetts in its own words

- "Most workers in Massachusetts have the right to earn and use up to 40 hours of job-protected sick time per year to take care of themselves and certain family members. Workers must earn at least one hour of earned sick leave for every 30 hours worked." [Massachusetts Attorney General's Office, Fair Labor Division: Earned Sick Time](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/earned-sick-time) (Massachusetts Attorney General)
- "Employers with 11 or more employees must provide paid sick time. Employers with fewer than 11 employees must provide earned sick time, but it does not need to be paid." [Massachusetts Attorney General's Office, Fair Labor Division: Earned Sick Time](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/earned-sick-time) (Massachusetts Attorney General)
