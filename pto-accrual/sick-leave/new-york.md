# New York paid sick leave

New York: at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked (New York State). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 100-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

New York: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 100 or more employees: up to 56 hours paid | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 56 hours earned a year |
| 5 to 99 employees: up to 40 hours paid | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year |
| Fewer than 5 employees: up to 40 hours unpaid (paid if net income is over $1 million) | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; unpaid (job-protected) at this employer size |

- Up to 56 hours paid a calendar year with 100 or more employees; up to 40 hours paid with 5 to 99; up to 40 hours unpaid with fewer than 5, paid if net income is over $1 million. (New York State)
- All private-sector employees, including part-time and overtime-exempt. (New York State)

## New York in its own words

- "Employees begin accruing leave on September 30, 2020. Leave must be accrued at rate not less than one hour for every thirty hours worked." [The State of New York, New York Paid Sick Leave](https://www.ny.gov/programs/new-york-paid-sick-leave) (New York State)
- "Employers with 100 or more employees must provide up to 56 hours of paid sick leave per calendar year." [The State of New York, New York Paid Sick Leave](https://www.ny.gov/programs/new-york-paid-sick-leave) (New York State)
- "Employers with 5-99 employees must provide up to 40 hours of paid sick leave per calendar year." [The State of New York, New York Paid Sick Leave](https://www.ny.gov/programs/new-york-paid-sick-leave) (New York State)
- "If net income is $1 million or less, employer must provide up to 40 hours of unpaid sick leave. If net income is greater than $1 million, employer must provide up to 40 hours of paid sick leave." [The State of New York, New York Paid Sick Leave](https://www.ny.gov/programs/new-york-paid-sick-leave) (New York State)
- "All private-sector employees in New York State are covered, regardless of industry, occupation, part-time status, and overtime exempt status." [The State of New York, New York Paid Sick Leave](https://www.ny.gov/programs/new-york-paid-sick-leave) (New York State)
