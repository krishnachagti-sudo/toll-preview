# Montgomery County, MD paid sick leave

Montgomery County, MD: 1 hour per 30 hours worked in the County; up to 56 paid hours a year (5 or more employees), or 32 paid and 24 unpaid (fewer than 5); use may be held to 80 hours a year (Montgomery County OHR poster). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 5-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## Montgomery County, MD in its own words

- "Earned paid sick and safe leave must accrue at a rate of at least 1 hour for every 30 hours an employee works in the County." [Montgomery County Office of Human Rights, Earned Sick and Safe Leave poster (County Code ch. 27, art. XIII)](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/humanrights/Resources/Files/EarnedSickandSavedLeave_Poster.pdf) (Montgomery County OHR poster)
- "Must provide up to 56 hours of earned paid sick and safe leave in a calendar year." [Montgomery County Office of Human Rights, Earned Sick and Safe Leave poster (County Code ch. 27, art. XIII)](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/humanrights/Resources/Files/EarnedSickandSavedLeave_Poster.pdf) (Montgomery County OHR poster)
- "Must provide up to 32 hours of earned paid sick and safe leave and 24 hours of unpaid earned sick and safe leave in a calendar year." [Montgomery County Office of Human Rights, Earned Sick and Safe Leave poster (County Code ch. 27, art. XIII)](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/humanrights/Resources/Files/EarnedSickandSavedLeave_Poster.pdf) (Montgomery County OHR poster)
- "Is not required to allow an employee to use more than 80 hours of earned sick and safe leave in a calendar year." [Montgomery County Office of Human Rights, Earned Sick and Safe Leave poster (County Code ch. 27, art. XIII)](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/humanrights/Resources/Files/EarnedSickandSavedLeave_Poster.pdf) (Montgomery County OHR poster)
