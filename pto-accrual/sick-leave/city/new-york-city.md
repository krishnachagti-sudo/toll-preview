# New York City, NY paid sick leave

New York City, NY: 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours a calendar year (1 to 99 employees) or 56 (100 or more), plus 32 hours of unpaid protected time off (NYC DCWP FAQ). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 100-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

New York City, NY: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 100 or more employees | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 56 hours earned a year |
| 99 or fewer employees | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year |

- Employees anywhere count toward employer size, but only those who work in New York City have the rights. (NYC DCWP FAQ)
- Unused paid time carries over unless the employer front-loads the full amount and pays out unused leave at year end; carried leave can be used all year. (NYC DCWP FAQ)

> Employers with fewer than 5 employees: whether the 40 hours are paid depends on net income (see the FAQ); the engine treats them as the 99-or-fewer tier.

## New York City, NY in its own words

- "accrue paid protected time off at the rate of 1 hour for every 30 hours worked, up to a maximum of: 40 hours per Calendar Year if their employer employs 1-99 employees; or 56 hours per Calendar Year if their employer employs 100 or more employees." [NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection, Protected Time Off Law FAQs](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/paid-sick-leave-FAQs.page) (NYC DCWP FAQ)
- "32 immediately available hours of unpaid protected time off." [NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection, Protected Time Off Law FAQs](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/paid-sick-leave-FAQs.page) (NYC DCWP FAQ)
- "However, only employees who work in New York City have rights to protected time off under the Law." [NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection, Protected Time Off Law FAQs](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/paid-sick-leave-FAQs.page) (NYC DCWP FAQ)
- "the employer pays employees for any unused leave at the end of the Calendar Year in which the leave is accrued or front-loaded; AND the employer front-loads 40 or 56 hours (as applicable)." [NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection, Protected Time Off Law FAQs](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/paid-sick-leave-FAQs.page) (NYC DCWP FAQ)
- "Employees must be allowed to use carried over leave during the entire Calendar Year." [NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection, Protected Time Off Law FAQs](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/paid-sick-leave-FAQs.page) (NYC DCWP FAQ)
