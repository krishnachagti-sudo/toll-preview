# Philadelphia, PA paid sick leave

Philadelphia, PA: 1 hour per 40 hours worked, at most 40 hours a calendar year; use after 90 days; paid with 10 or more employees, unpaid with 9 or fewer (Philadelphia DOL). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 10-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 28.60 hours of sick time.

## The rule

Philadelphia, PA: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 10 or more employees: paid | 1 hour per 40 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; or 40 hours front-loaded each year |
| 9 or fewer employees: unpaid | 1 hour per 40 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; or 40 hours front-loaded each year; unpaid (job-protected) at this employer size |

- Covers employees who work at least 40 hours a year in Philadelphia. (Philadelphia DOL)
- Unused time carries over, but accrual and use may be held to 40 hours a year; no carryover needed if 40 hours are available at the start of the year. (Phila. Sick Leave Regulations 4.8)

## Philadelphia, PA in its own words

- "Employees can earn 1 hour of sick leave for every 40 hours they work. A maximum of 40 sick leave hours may be earned in a calendar year. Sick leave may be used after an employee has worked a minimum of 90 days." [City of Philadelphia Department of Labor, Paid sick leave one-pager (2025)](https://www.phila.gov/media/20250731151754/dol-paid-sick-leave-one-pager-en.pdf) (Philadelphia DOL)
- "Employers with 10 or more employees must provide paid sick leave. Employers with 9 or fewer employees must provide unpaid sick leave." [City of Philadelphia Department of Labor, Paid sick leave one-pager (2025)](https://www.phila.gov/media/20250731151754/dol-paid-sick-leave-one-pager-en.pdf) (Philadelphia DOL)
- "Employees who work at least 40 hours a year in Philadelphia can earn sick leave." [City of Philadelphia Department of Labor, Paid sick leave one-pager (2025)](https://www.phila.gov/media/20250731151754/dol-paid-sick-leave-one-pager-en.pdf) (Philadelphia DOL)
- "Accrued unused sick time shall be carried over from one Calendar Year to the next. An Employee may not accrue, or use, more than 40 hours of sick time in a Calendar Year, unless the Employer sets a higher limit pursuant to its own policies." [City of Philadelphia Office of Worker Protections, Sick Leave Regulations](https://www.phila.gov/departments/department-of-records/regulations/sick-leave-regulations.pdf) (Phila. Sick Leave Regulations 4.8)
