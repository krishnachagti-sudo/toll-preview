# Saint Paul, MN paid sick leave

Saint Paul, MN: 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours a year; carryover up to 80 hours; use after 80 hours worked (Saint Paul HREEO). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.
