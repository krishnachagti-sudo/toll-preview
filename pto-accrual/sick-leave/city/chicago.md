# Chicago, IL paid sick leave

Chicago, IL: One hour of paid leave and one hour of paid sick leave per 35 hours worked, from the first day; covers anyone working 80 hours in 120 days (Chicago BACP). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 32.69 hours of sick time.

## Chicago, IL in its own words

- "For every 35 hours worked, employees accrue one hour of paid leave and one hour of paid sick leave." [City of Chicago BACP, Paid Leave and Paid Sick Leave](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/bacp/supp_info/paidsickleaveinfo.html) (Chicago BACP)
- "Any employee who works at least 80 hours for an employer in Chicago within any 120-day period is covered by the ordinance" [City of Chicago BACP, Paid Leave and Paid Sick Leave](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/bacp/supp_info/paidsickleaveinfo.html) (Chicago BACP)
