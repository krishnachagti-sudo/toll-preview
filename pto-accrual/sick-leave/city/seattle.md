# Seattle, WA paid sick leave

Seattle, WA: Tier 1 (up to 49 FTEs) and Tier 2 (50 to 249): 1 hour per 40 hours, carryover 40 and 56 hours; Tier 3 (250 or more): 1 hour per 30, carryover 72 (108 with PTO) (Seattle OLS). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 250-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.
