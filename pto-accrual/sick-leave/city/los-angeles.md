# Los Angeles, CA paid sick leave

Los Angeles, CA: 1 hour per 30 hours worked, or at least 48 hours front-loaded (LA Office of Wage Standards). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

Los Angeles, CA: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| All employers | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; use may be limited to 48 hours a year; balance may be capped at 72 hours; or 48 hours front-loaded each year |

- Unused time carries over and may be capped at no less than 72 hours; use may be limited to 48 hours a year. (LA Office of Wage Standards)
- Use may start on the 90th day of employment. (LA Office of Wage Standards)
- No compensation required at separation. (LA Office of Wage Standards)
- Reinstated on rehire within one year. (LA Office of Wage Standards)

## Los Angeles, CA in its own words

- "At least 48 hours provided either at the beginning of each year of employment, calendar year, or 12-month period; OR - Accrual One (1) hour of paid sick leave for every thirty (30) hours worked." [City of Los Angeles Office of Wage Standards, paid sick leave](https://wagesla.lacity.gov/) (LA Office of Wage Standards)
- "shall carry over to the following year of employment and may be capped at a minimum of 72 hours." [City of Los Angeles Office of Wage Standards, paid sick leave](https://wagesla.lacity.gov/) (LA Office of Wage Standards)
- "The use of paid sick leave may be limited to 48 hours leave annually." [City of Los Angeles Office of Wage Standards, paid sick leave](https://wagesla.lacity.gov/) (LA Office of Wage Standards)
- "An Employee may use paid sick leave beginning on the 90th day of" [City of Los Angeles Office of Wage Standards, paid sick leave](https://wagesla.lacity.gov/) (LA Office of Wage Standards)
- "An Employer is not required to provide compensation to an Employee for accrued or unused sick days at separation from employment." [City of Los Angeles Office of Wage Standards, paid sick leave](https://wagesla.lacity.gov/) (LA Office of Wage Standards)
- "If an Employee is re-hired within one (1) year of separation from employment, previously accrued and unused paid sick leave shall be reinstated." [City of Los Angeles Office of Wage Standards, paid sick leave](https://wagesla.lacity.gov/) (LA Office of Wage Standards)
