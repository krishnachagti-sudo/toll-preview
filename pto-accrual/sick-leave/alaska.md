# Alaska paid sick leave

Alaska: 1 hour per 30 hours worked; accrue and use up to 40 hours a year (fewer than 15 FTEs) or 56 hours (15 or more) (Alaska DOLWD FAQ). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 15-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

Alaska: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 15 or more employees (FTEs) | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 56 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 56 hours a year; or 56 hours front-loaded each year |
| Fewer than 15 employees (FTEs) | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; or 40 hours front-loaded each year |

- Unused leave carries over and the balance may exceed the yearly limit; not when the full year is front-loaded. (Alaska DOLWD FAQ)
- No payout required at the end of employment. (Alaska DOLWD FAQ)

## Alaska in its own words

- "Sick leave is accrued at the minimum rate of one hour of sick leave for every 30 hours of work" [Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Minimum Wage and Paid Sick Leave FAQ (AS 23.10.066-23.10.069)](https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/sick-leave-faq.html) (Alaska DOLWD FAQ)
- "Employers with fewer than 15 employees (FTE's) must allow workers to accrue and use up to 40 hours of paid sick leave per year. Employers with 15 or more employees (FTE's) must allow up to 56 hours per year." [Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Minimum Wage and Paid Sick Leave FAQ (AS 23.10.066-23.10.069)](https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/sick-leave-faq.html) (Alaska DOLWD FAQ)
- "Unused sick leave carries over into the next year, and employees continue to accrue leave up to the annual cap." [Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Minimum Wage and Paid Sick Leave FAQ (AS 23.10.066-23.10.069)](https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/sick-leave-faq.html) (Alaska DOLWD FAQ)
- "if an employer front-loads the full annual amount of sick leave at the start of the year, they are not required to carry over unused leave into the next year." [Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Minimum Wage and Paid Sick Leave FAQ (AS 23.10.066-23.10.069)](https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/sick-leave-faq.html) (Alaska DOLWD FAQ)
- "Employers are not required to pay out accrued sick leave at the end of employment." [Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Minimum Wage and Paid Sick Leave FAQ (AS 23.10.066-23.10.069)](https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/sick-leave-faq.html) (Alaska DOLWD FAQ)
