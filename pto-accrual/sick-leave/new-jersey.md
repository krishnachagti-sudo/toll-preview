# New Jersey paid sick leave

New Jersey: 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours a benefit year; or 40 hours up front (NJDOL). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

New Jersey: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| All employers | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; carryover may be limited to 40 hours; or 40 hours front-loaded each year |

- Up to 40 unused hours carry over; use may be limited to 40 a year; or the employer may pay unused leave at year end. (NJDOL)
- Returned on rehire within six months. (NJDOL)

## New Jersey in its own words

- "You accrue 1 hour of earned sick leave for every 30 hours worked, up to a maximum of 40 hours of leave per benefit year." [New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Earned Sick Leave](https://www.nj.gov/labor/myworkrights/leave-benefits/sick-leave/law.shtml) (NJDOL)
- "Alternatively, your employer can provide you with 40 hours of earned sick leave up front." [New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Earned Sick Leave](https://www.nj.gov/labor/myworkrights/leave-benefits/sick-leave/law.shtml) (NJDOL)
- "You may carry over up to 40 hours of unused earned sick leave into the next benefit year. However, your employer is required only to let you use up to 40 hours of leave per benefit year." [New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Earned Sick Leave](https://www.nj.gov/labor/myworkrights/leave-benefits/sick-leave/law.shtml) (NJDOL)
- "If you terminate, lay off or furlough an employee, then rehire or reinstate her/him in New Jersey within six months, the prior accrued/advanced sick leave must be returned to the employee." [New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Earned Sick Leave](https://www.nj.gov/labor/myworkrights/leave-benefits/sick-leave/law.shtml) (NJDOL)
