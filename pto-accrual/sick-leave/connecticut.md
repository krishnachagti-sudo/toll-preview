# Connecticut paid sick leave

Connecticut: 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year (Connecticut DOL). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

Connecticut: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 11 or more employees in Connecticut (from January 1, 2026; 1 or more from January 1, 2027) | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; carryover may be limited to 40 hours; or 40 hours front-loaded each year |

- Employers with 11 or more employees in Connecticut from January 1, 2026; 1 or more from January 1, 2027. (Connecticut DOL)
- Up to 40 unused hours carry over; use is limited to 40 hours a year; front-loading may replace carryover. (Connecticut DOL)
- Use may wait until the employee has worked 120 calendar days. (Connecticut DOL)
- Unused leave is not kept after a break in service. (Connecticut DOL)
- No payout required at separation unless a policy, contract or bargaining agreement provides it. (Connecticut DOL)

## Questions

### How much sick leave do employees earn in Connecticut?

1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year. (Connecticut DOL).

### Does unused sick leave have to be paid out when someone leaves in Connecticut?

No payout required at separation unless a policy, contract or bargaining agreement provides it. (Connecticut DOL).

### When can a new employee in Connecticut start using sick leave?

Use may wait until the employee has worked 120 calendar days. (Connecticut DOL).

### Does sick leave carry over to the next year in Connecticut?

Up to 40 unused hours carry over; use is limited to 40 hours a year; front-loading may replace carryover. (Connecticut DOL).
