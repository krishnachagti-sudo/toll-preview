# Oregon paid sick leave

Oregon: at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year, or 40 hours front-loaded (Oregon BOLI). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 10-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

Oregon: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 10 or more employees in Oregon (6 or more with a Portland location): paid | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; balance may be capped at 80 hours; carryover may be limited to 40 hours; or 40 hours front-loaded each year |
| Fewer than 10 employees (fewer than 6 with a Portland location): unpaid, protected | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; balance may be capped at 80 hours; carryover may be limited to 40 hours; or 40 hours front-loaded each year; unpaid (job-protected) at this employer size |

- Paid when the employer has 10 or more Oregon employees (6 or more with a Portland location). (Oregon BOLI)
- Carryover may be limited to 40 hours and the balance capped at 80. (Oregon BOLI)
- Oregon employees earn sick time even when the employer is located in another state. (Oregon BOLI)
- An employer that front-loads need not carry unused time forward. (Oregon BOLI)
- Use may start after 90 days of employment. (Oregon BOLI)
- No payout at termination or resignation. (Oregon BOLI)

## Oregon in its own words

- "Employees earn at least 1 hour of protected sick time for every 30 hours of work, up to 40 hours per year. (Employers can choose to front-load at least 40 hours of sick time at the beginning of the year.)" [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Sick time](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/sick-time.aspx) (Oregon BOLI)
- "Protected sick leave is also paid time when an employer has 10 or more Oregon employees (six or more if they have a" [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Sick time](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/sick-time.aspx) (Oregon BOLI)
- "employer policies may also limit carry-over of unused sick time from one year to the next to up to 40 hours a year and cap total accrual balances to 80 hours." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Sick time](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/sick-time.aspx) (Oregon BOLI)
- "Oregon employees earn sick time even if their employer is located out of state." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Sick time](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/sick-time.aspx) (Oregon BOLI)
- "Employers that front-load sick time are not required to carry forward unused time from one year to the next." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Sick time](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/sick-time.aspx) (Oregon BOLI)
- "Employees may begin taking sick time after 90 days of employment." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Sick time](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/sick-time.aspx) (Oregon BOLI)
- "Your employer is not required to pay you for unused accrued sick time upon termination or resignation." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Sick time](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/sick-time.aspx) (Oregon BOLI)

## Questions

### How much sick leave do employees earn in Oregon?

At least 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours a year, or 40 hours front-loaded. (Oregon BOLI).

### Does unused sick leave have to be paid out when someone leaves in Oregon?

No payout at termination or resignation. (Oregon BOLI).

### When can a new employee in Oregon start using sick leave?

Use may start after 90 days of employment. (Oregon BOLI).

### Does sick leave carry over to the next year in Oregon?

Carryover may be limited to 40 hours and the balance capped at 80. (Oregon BOLI).
