Sick leave by state

Minnesota paid sick leave

Minnesota: 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours a year unless the employer agrees to more (Minn. Stat. 181.9446(a)). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

The rule

  • Unused time carries over; the balance need not exceed 80 hours. (Minn. Stat. 181.9446(b)(1))
  • Instead of carryover: 48 hours at the start of the year if unused time is paid out at year end, or 80 hours if it is not. (Minn. Stat. 181.9446(b)(2))
  • No payout required at separation. (Minn. Stat. 181.9448 subd. 2)
  • Reinstated on rehire within 180 days. (Minn. Stat. 181.9448 subd. 2)
  • Covers employees expected to work at least 80 hours a year in Minnesota. (Minnesota DLI)

Minnesota in its own words

  • "An employee accrues a minimum of one hour of earned sick and safe time for every 30 hours worked up to a maximum of 48 hours of earned sick and safe time in a year." Minn. Stat. 181.9446, Accrual of earned sick and safe time (2026) (Minn. Stat. 181.9446(a))
  • "The total amount of accrued but unused earned sick and safe time for an employee must not exceed 80 hours at any time, unless an employer agrees to a higher amount." Minn. Stat. 181.9446, Accrual of earned sick and safe time (2026) (Minn. Stat. 181.9446(b)(1))
  • "(ii) 80 hours, if an employer does not pay an employee for accrued but unused sick and safe time at the end of a year." Minn. Stat. 181.9446, Accrual of earned sick and safe time (2026) (Minn. Stat. 181.9446(b)(2))
  • "Sections 181.9445 to 181.9448 do not require financial or other reimbursement to an employee from an employer upon the employee's termination, resignation, retirement, or other separation from employment for accrued earned sick and safe time that has not been used." Minn. Stat. 181.9448, Earned sick and safe time: separation, rehire (2026) (Minn. Stat. 181.9448 subd. 2)

Questions

How much sick leave do employees earn in Minnesota?

1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours a year unless the employer agrees to more. (Minn. Stat. 181.9446(a)).

Does unused sick leave have to be paid out when someone leaves in Minnesota?

No payout required at separation. (Minn. Stat. 181.9448 subd. 2).

Does sick leave carry over to the next year in Minnesota?

Unused time carries over; the balance need not exceed 80 hours. (Minn. Stat. 181.9446(b)(1)).
