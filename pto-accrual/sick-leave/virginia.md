# Virginia paid sick leave

Virginia: 1 hour per 30 hours worked, carried over, up to 40 hours a year to accrue or use (Va. Code 40.1-33.4(A)). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## Virginia in its own words

- ""Employee" means a home health worker who works on average at least 20 hours per week or 90 hours per month." [Va. Code 40.1-33.3, Definitions (home health worker)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title40.1/chapter3/section40.1-33.3/) (Va. Code 40.1-33.3)
- "All employees shall accrue a minimum of one hour of paid sick leave for every 30 hours worked. Paid sick leave shall be carried over to the year following the year in which it was accrued. An employee shall not accrue or use more than 40 hours of paid sick leave in a year, unless the employer selects a higher limit." [Va. Code 40.1-33.4, Accrual of paid sick leave (home health workers)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title40.1/chapter3/section40.1-33.4/) (Va. Code 40.1-33.4(A))
