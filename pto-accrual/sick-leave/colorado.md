# Colorado paid sick leave

Colorado: 1 hour per 30 hours worked, up to 48 hours a benefit year, for every employer regardless of size (CDLE INFO #6). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## Colorado in its own words
