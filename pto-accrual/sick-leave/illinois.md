# Illinois paid sick leave

Illinois: 1 hour per 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours in a 12-month period; or front-loaded (IDOL PLAWA FAQ). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 28.60 hours of sick time.

## The rule

Illinois: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| All employers (Chicago and Cook County have their own ordinances) | 1 hour per 40 hours worked; up to 40 hours earned a year; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; or 40 hours front-loaded each year |

- Use may start after 90 days. (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)
- Unused accrued leave carries over (not front-loaded leave); use may be limited to 40 hours a year. (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)
- Not paid out at the end of employment unless it is part of a vacation or general PTO bank, which the Wage Payment and Collection Act then makes payable. (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)
- Separate banks keep the PLAWA hours out of the final pay; a combined vacation bank is payable. (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)

## Illinois in its own words

- "Under the Act, employees can earn at least 1 hour of paid time off for every 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours in a 12-month period." [Illinois Department of Labor, Paid Leave for All Workers Act FAQ](https://labor.illinois.gov/faqs/paidleavefaq.html) (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)
- "Employees are entitled to begin using the accrued paid leave after 90 days." [Illinois Department of Labor, Paid Leave for All Workers Act FAQ](https://labor.illinois.gov/faqs/paidleavefaq.html) (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)
- "Under PLAWA, employees are allowed to carry over unused, accrued leave from one year to the next under this law. However, employees who received frontloaded leave would not be entitled to carry over unused paid leave into the next 12-month period." [Illinois Department of Labor, Paid Leave for All Workers Act FAQ](https://labor.illinois.gov/faqs/paidleavefaq.html) (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)
- "Under PLAWA, an employer does not have to let an employee use more than 40 hours of paid leave off in a year." [Illinois Department of Labor, Paid Leave for All Workers Act FAQ](https://labor.illinois.gov/faqs/paidleavefaq.html) (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)
- "Under PLAWA, unused paid leave time does not have to be paid out at the end of employment UNLESS it is part of a vacation bank or general PTO bank." [Illinois Department of Labor, Paid Leave for All Workers Act FAQ](https://labor.illinois.gov/faqs/paidleavefaq.html) (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)
- "if your employer provides 1 week of vacation and 1 week of PLAWA and maintains separate recordkeeping for both types of leave, then your employer would not have to payout any unused accrued PLAWA time, but would have to payout any unused accrued vacation time." [Illinois Department of Labor, Paid Leave for All Workers Act FAQ](https://labor.illinois.gov/faqs/paidleavefaq.html) (IDOL PLAWA FAQ)

## Questions

### How much sick leave do employees earn in Illinois?

1 hour per 40 hours worked, up to 40 hours in a 12-month period; or front-loaded. (IDOL PLAWA FAQ).

### Does unused sick leave have to be paid out when someone leaves in Illinois?

Not paid out at the end of employment unless it is part of a vacation or general PTO bank, which the Wage Payment and Collection Act then makes payable. (IDOL PLAWA FAQ).

### When can a new employee in Illinois start using sick leave?

Use may start after 90 days. (IDOL PLAWA FAQ).

### Does sick leave carry over to the next year in Illinois?

Unused accrued leave carries over (not front-loaded leave); use may be limited to 40 hours a year. (IDOL PLAWA FAQ).
