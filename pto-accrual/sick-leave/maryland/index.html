Sick leave by state

Maryland paid sick leave

Maryland: at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(b)). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week at a 15-employee employer, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

The rule

  • Paid with 15 or more employees; at least unpaid with 14 or fewer. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(a)(1))
  • An employer may limit earning to 40 hours a year, use to 64 hours a year and the balance to 64 hours, and bar use in the first 106 calendar days. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(c))
  • No accrual in a 2-week period under 24 hours, a 1-week period when it and the one before total under 24 hours, or a twice-monthly period under 26 hours. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(c)(5))
  • Carryover may be limited to 40 hours. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(g)(2))
  • The full year may be awarded at the start of the year. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(d))
  • No payout required at termination. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(j))
  • Reinstated on rehire within 37 weeks unless paid out. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(h))

Maryland in its own words

  • "shall accrue at a rate of at least 1 hour for every 30 hours an employee works." Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-1304, Earned sick and safe leave (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(b))
  • "An employer that employs 14 or fewer employees shall at least provide an employee with unpaid earned sick and safe leave." Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-1304, Earned sick and safe leave (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(a)(1))
  • "An employer may not be required to allow an employee to: (1) earn more than 40 hours of earned sick and safe leave in a year; (2) use more than 64 hours of earned sick and safe leave in a year; (3) accrue a total of more than 64 hours at any time; (4) use earned sick and safe leave during the first 106 calendar days the employee works for the employer;" Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-1304, Earned sick and safe leave (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(c))
  • "(i) 2-week pay period in which the employee worked fewer than 24 hours total; (ii) 1-week pay period if the employee worked fewer than a combined total of 24 hours in the current and the immediately preceding pay period; or (iii) pay period in which: 1. the employee is paid twice a month regardless of the number of weeks in a pay period; and 2. the employee worked fewer than 26 hours in the pay period." Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-1304, Earned sick and safe leave (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(c)(5))
  • "An employer may not be required to allow an employee to carry over more than 40 hours of earned sick and safe leave under paragraph (1) of this subsection." Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-1304, Earned sick and safe leave (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(g)(2))
  • "At the beginning of each year, an employer may award to an employee the full amount of earned sick and safe leave that an employee would earn over the course of the year rather than awarding the leave as the leave accrues during the year." Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-1304, Earned sick and safe leave (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(d))
  • "An employer may not be required to pay out on the termination of employment unused earned sick and safe leave accrued by an employee." Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-1304, Earned sick and safe leave (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(j))
  • "If an employee is rehired by the employer within 37 weeks after leaving the employment of the employer, the employer shall reinstate any unused earned sick and safe leave" Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-1304, Earned sick and safe leave (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(h))

Questions

How much sick leave do employees earn in Maryland?

At least 1 hour per 30 hours worked. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(b)).

Does unused sick leave have to be paid out when someone leaves in Maryland?

No payout required at termination. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(j)).

Does sick leave carry over to the next year in Maryland?

Carryover may be limited to 40 hours. (Md. Lab. & Empl. 3-1304(g)(2)).
