# California paid sick leave

California: at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked, from the start of employment (Cal. Lab. Code 246(b)(1)). A part-time employee working 22 hours a week, paid every two weeks through 2027, earns 38.13 hours of sick time.

## The rule

California: sick leave rule by employer size, as quoted below

| Employer | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| All employers | 1 hour per 30 hours worked; use may be limited to 40 hours a year; balance may be capped at 80 hours; or 40 hours front-loaded each year |

- An employee who works in California for the same employer for 30 or more days within a year from the start of employment. (Cal. Lab. Code 246(a)(1))
- Use may start on the 90th day of employment (the employer may refuse use before it). (Cal. Lab. Code 246(c))
- Unused time carries over; use may be limited to 40 hours or 5 days a year; front-loading 40 hours or 5 days at the start of each year replaces accrual and carryover. (Cal. Lab. Code 246(d))
- The balance may be capped at 80 hours or 10 days. (Cal. Lab. Code 246(j))
- No payout required at separation. (Cal. Lab. Code 246(g)(1))
- Reinstated on rehire within one year of separation. (Cal. Lab. Code 246(g)(2))

## California in its own words

- "An employee who, on or after July 1, 2015, works in California for the same employer for 30 or more days within a year from the commencement of employment is entitled to paid sick days as specified in this section." [Cal. Labor Code 246, paid sick days (California Legislative Information)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=246.) (Cal. Lab. Code 246(a)(1))
- "An employee shall accrue paid sick days at the rate of not less than one hour per every 30 hours worked, beginning at the commencement of employment or the operative date of this article, whichever is later, subject to the use and accrual limitations set forth in this section." [Cal. Labor Code 246, paid sick days (California Legislative Information)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=246.) (Cal. Lab. Code 246(b)(1))
- "An employee shall be entitled to use accrued paid sick days beginning on the 90th day of employment, after which day the employee may use paid sick days as they are accrued." [Cal. Labor Code 246, paid sick days (California Legislative Information)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=246.) (Cal. Lab. Code 246(c))
- "Accrued paid sick days shall carry over to the following year of employment. However, an employer may limit an employee’s use of accrued paid sick days to 40 hours or five days in each year of employment, calendar year, or 12-month period. This section shall be satisfied and no accrual or carryover is required if the full amount of leave is received at the beginning of each year of employment, calendar year, or 12-month period. The term “full amount of leave” means five days or 40 hours." [Cal. Labor Code 246, paid sick days (California Legislative Information)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=246.) (Cal. Lab. Code 246(d))
- "An employer has no obligation under this section to allow an employee’s total accrual of paid sick leave to exceed 80 hours or 10 days, provided that an employee’s rights to accrue and use paid sick leave are not limited other than as allowed under this section." [Cal. Labor Code 246, paid sick days (California Legislative Information)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=246.) (Cal. Lab. Code 246(j))
- "an employer is not required to provide compensation to an employee for accrued, unused paid sick days upon termination, resignation, retirement, or other separation from employment." [Cal. Labor Code 246, paid sick days (California Legislative Information)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=246.) (Cal. Lab. Code 246(g)(1))
- "If an employee separates from an employer and is rehired by the employer within one year from the date of separation, previously accrued and unused paid sick days shall be reinstated." [Cal. Labor Code 246, paid sick days (California Legislative Information)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=246.) (Cal. Lab. Code 246(g)(2))

## Questions

### How much sick leave do employees earn in California?

At least 1 hour per 30 hours worked, from the start of employment. (Cal. Lab. Code 246(b)(1)).

### Does unused sick leave have to be paid out when someone leaves in California?

No payout required at separation. (Cal. Lab. Code 246(g)(1)).

### When can a new employee in California start using sick leave?

Use may start on the 90th day of employment (the employer may refuse use before it). (Cal. Lab. Code 246(c)).

### Does sick leave carry over to the next year in California?

Unused time carries over; use may be limited to 40 hours or 5 days a year; front-loading 40 hours or 5 days at the start of each year replaces accrual and carryover. (Cal. Lab. Code 246(d)).
