# Iowa PTO payout and sick leave

In Iowa, unused vacation at separation is owed only as the written policy or agreement says (Iowa Code 91A.4); under a policy that pays it out, 64 hours at $28.00 is $1,792.00. We did not find a statewide paid sick leave law on the Iowa sites we read.

## The rule

Owed only where an agreement or policy provides it; pro rata if the policy accrues pro rata. (Iowa Code 91A.4).

- "If vacations are due an employee under an agreement with the employer or a policy of the employer establishing pro rata vacation accrued, the increment shall be in proportion to the fraction of the year which the employee was actually employed." [Iowa Code 91A.4 (2026), Employment suspension or termination](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/91A.4.pdf)
