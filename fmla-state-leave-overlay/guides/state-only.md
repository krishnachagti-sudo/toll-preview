## What to do

- Check the FMLA tests first in the eligibility checker; a "no" there does not end the question.
- Check the state law for the employee's work state: headcount (in the state or worldwide), time with the employer and hours differ by state.
- Paid programs without job protection (California PFL and SDI, Connecticut Paid Leave, DC PFL) still pay; give the state's notices and keep the record.

[The three FMLA tests](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/guides/three-tests/).

> Not legal advice. Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it, and from 29 CFR Part 825. States whose official sites we could not read are named as not covered, never filled in from someone else's chart. Whether a condition qualifies, the earnings test and benefit amounts are decided by the employer and the state program; local ordinances and contracts can give more.
