# FMLA and Maryland family and medical leave

In Maryland, FAMLI and the FMLA count against each other when the leave qualifies under both (29 CFR 825.701(a)). For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 12 weeks (0 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 6 weeks.

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).

## Questions

### Does FAMLI run at the same time as the FMLA in Maryland?

Starts January 1, 2028. Under 29 CFR 825.701(a) leave that qualifies under both counts against both.

### Does Parental Leave Act run at the same time as the FMLA in Maryland?

Applies only to employers the federal FMLA does not cover.

### How many weeks does FAMLI pay in Maryland?

12 weeks (year).
