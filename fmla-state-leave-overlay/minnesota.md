# FMLA and Minnesota family and medical leave

In Minnesota, the employer may require Minnesota Paid Leave to run concurrently with the FMLA. For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 20 weeks (20 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 20 weeks.

## Minnesota in its own words

**Read the law's own words (6 quotations)**

- "The total number of weeks that an applicant may take benefits in a single benefit year for a serious health condition is the lesser of 12 weeks, or 12 weeks minus the number of weeks within the same benefit year that the applicant received benefits for bonding, safety leave, family care, and qualifying exigency plus eight weeks." [Minn. Stat. 268B.04, Financial eligibility; benefits](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/268B.04)
- "Ninety calendar days from the date of hire, an employee has a right and is entitled to reinstatement as provided under this subdivision" [Minn. Stat. 268B.09, Employment protections](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/268B.09)
- "An employer may require leave taken under this chapter to run concurrently with leave taken for the same purpose under section 181.941 or the Family and Medical Leave Act" [Minn. Stat. 268B.27, Relationship to other leave](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/268B.27)
- "The length of the leave shall be determined by the employee, but must not exceed 12 weeks, unless agreed to by the employer." [Minn. Stat. 181.941, Pregnancy and parenting leave](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/181.941)
- "a female employee for prenatal care, or incapacity due to pregnancy, childbirth, or related health conditions." [Minn. Stat. 181.941, Pregnancy and parenting leave](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/181.941)
- ""Employer" means a person or entity that employs one or more employees" [Minn. Stat. 181.940, Definitions](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/181.940)

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). ## Questions

### Does Minnesota Paid Leave run at the same time as the FMLA in Minnesota?

An employer may require Minnesota Paid Leave to run concurrently with FMLA leave for the same purpose.

### Does Pregnancy and parenting leave run at the same time as the FMLA in Minnesota?

An employer may require Minnesota Paid Leave to run with this leave (268B.27); with FMLA, 29 CFR 825.701(a) counts leave that qualifies under both against both.

### How many weeks does Minnesota Paid Leave pay in Minnesota?

medical: 12 weeks; family: 12 weeks, at most 20 weeks combined (benefit year).
