# FMLA and New York family and medical leave

In New York, PFL runs with the FMLA once the employer designates the leave as both. For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 22 weeks (12 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 12 weeks.

## Worked totals

Each row is the engine's answer for an employee hired June 3, 2024 with 1,980 hours in the last 12 months, leave from January 11, 2027, everyone in the state and within 75 miles, and the earnings test met. A birth counts 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, then bonding.

New York: protected and paid weeks by case

| Case | FMLA | Protected weeks | Paid weeks | Laws |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Birth, 100 employees | yes | 22 | 12 | PFL |
| Birth, 20 employees | no | 12 | 12 | PFL |
| Own health, 100 employees | yes | 12 | 0 | - |
| Family care, 100 employees | yes | 12 | 12 | PFL |
| Bonding (other parent), 100 employees | yes | 12 | 12 | PFL |
| Bonding, 20 employees | no | 12 | 12 | PFL |

The birth at 100 employees: 10 weeks of pregnancy disability protected by FMLA, then 12 weeks of bonding protected by FMLA and PFL. [Why the weeks add up this way](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/stacking/).

- A birth parent's pregnancy disability is counted under each state law's own-health (medical) leave unless the state names pregnancy separately (California PDL, Oregon OFLA, Iowa, Montana, Minnesota).
- New York disability benefits (DBL) pay for a birth parent's own disability, but their weeks were not on the pages we read; the totals count only Paid Family Leave and the 26-week combined cap.

## New York in its own words

- "If you are eligible and have a qualifying event, you can take up to 12 weeks of job-protected, paid time off." [New York State Paid Family Leave, Benefits (read through WebFetch: curl got 403)](https://paidfamilyleave.ny.gov/benefits)
- "Employees who work a regular schedule of 20 or more hours per week are eligible after 26 consecutive weeks of employment." [New York State Paid Family Leave, Eligibility (read through WebFetch: curl got 403)](https://paidfamilyleave.ny.gov/eligibility)
- "Employees who work a regular schedule of less than 20 hours per week are eligible after working 175 days" [New York State Paid Family Leave, Eligibility (read through WebFetch: curl got 403)](https://paidfamilyleave.ny.gov/eligibility)
- "If an employee has an event that qualifies for leave under both FMLA and Paid Family Leave, and the employer is covered under both laws, the employer can require them to run concurrently." [New York State Paid Family Leave, Paid Family Leave and Other Benefits (read through WebFetch: curl got 403)](https://paidfamilyleave.ny.gov/paid-family-leave-and-other-benefits)
- "In order for the two types of leaves to run together, the employer must notify the employee that the leave qualifies for both FMLA and Paid Family Leave, and that it will be designated as such." [New York State Paid Family Leave, Paid Family Leave and Other Benefits (read through WebFetch: curl got 403)](https://paidfamilyleave.ny.gov/paid-family-leave-and-other-benefits)
- "In all cases, employees cannot take more than 26 weeks of combined short-term disability and Paid Family Leave benefits in a 52-week period." [New York State Paid Family Leave, Paid Family Leave and Other Benefits (read through WebFetch: curl got 403)](https://paidfamilyleave.ny.gov/paid-family-leave-and-other-benefits)
- "$1,228.53 Maximum Weekly Benefit" [New York State Paid Family Leave, home page (read through WebFetch: curl got 403)](https://paidfamilyleave.ny.gov/)

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).

## Questions

### Does PFL run at the same time as the FMLA in New York?

The employer can require PFL and FMLA to run together only after notifying the employee that the leave qualifies for both and is designated as both.

### How many weeks does PFL pay in New York?

12 weeks (52 weeks).
