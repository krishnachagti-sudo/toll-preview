# Paid maternity leave by state

For a birth parent with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability at a 100-person employer, the states whose laws we read protect between 12 and 26 weeks (the District of Columbia the most) and pay up to 22 (New Jersey); the federal FMLA alone is 12 weeks unpaid.

Each row is the engine's answer for a birth parent hired June 3, 2024 with 1,980 hours in the last 12 months, leave from January 11, 2027, 10 weeks of pregnancy disability (4 before and 6 after a vaginal birth), no pregnancy complications, and the earnings test met.

Other states: the FMLA alone (12 weeks, unpaid, at a covered employer) unless we have read a state law; Hawaii, Massachusetts, Rhode Island and Virginia could not be read. [Work out your own case](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/)

> Not legal advice. Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it, and from 29 CFR Part 825. States whose official sites we could not read are named as not covered, never filled in from someone else's chart. Whether a condition qualifies, the earnings test and benefit amounts are decided by the employer and the state program; local ordinances and contracts can give more.
