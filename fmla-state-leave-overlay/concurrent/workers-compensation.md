# FMLA and workers' compensation

It can: a workers' compensation absence that is also a serious health condition may run concurrently with FMLA leave once the employer gives notice and designates it, so 6 weeks off on comp leave 6 weeks of the 12; because comp pays, no one can require PTO to be substituted during those weeks.

- "Because the workers' compensation absence is not unpaid, the provision for substitution of the employee's accrued paid leave is not applicable, and neither the employee nor the employer may require the substitution of paid leave." [29 CFR 825.207, Substitution of paid leave (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-825.207)
- "The workers' compensation absence and FMLA leave may run concurrently (subject to proper notice and designation by the employer)." [29 CFR 825.702, Interaction with anti-discrimination laws (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-825.702)

## Questions

### Does workers' comp run concurrently with FMLA?

It may, subject to proper notice and designation by the employer (29 CFR 825.702(d)(2)).
