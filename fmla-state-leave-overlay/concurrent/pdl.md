# FMLA and pregnancy disability leave (PDL)

Yes: PDL runs at the same time as the FMLA, and CFRA bonding leave runs after PDL, so a birth parent disabled for the full 4 months (17 1/3 weeks) at a 100-person employer has 29 1/3 weeks of protected leave: 17 1/3 of PDL with the FMLA and then 12 of CFRA bonding.

- "I have a pregnancy-related disability and my employer has 5+ employees." [California Civil Rights Department, Leave for Pregnancy Disability and Child Bonding: Quick Reference Guide](https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/employment/pdl-bonding-guide/)
- "Up to 4 months, based on hours worked per week and duration of disability. PDL will run at the same time as FMLA." [California Civil Rights Department, Leave for Pregnancy Disability and Child Bonding: Quick Reference Guide](https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/employment/pdl-bonding-guide/)

The birth-parent totals for every state are in [paid maternity leave by state](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/paid-maternity-leave-by-state/), and the California timeline in [California PDL, CFRA and FMLA](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/california/).
