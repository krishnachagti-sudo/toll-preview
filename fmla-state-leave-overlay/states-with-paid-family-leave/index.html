States with paid family and medical leave

We read 12 state paid family and medical leave programs on their own states' sites on November 8, 2026: 10 pay benefits today (CA, CO, CT, DE, DC, ME, NJ, NY, OR, WA), Maryland's start January 1, 2028, and Minnesota's start date was not on a page we could read. Hawaii, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Virginia refused our reads and are not covered.

Not covered

  • Hawaii runs temporary disability insurance; labor.hawaii.gov and the legislature's site returned 403, so its rules are not covered.
  • Massachusetts runs a paid family and medical leave program; mass.gov returned 403 to every read (and malegislature.gov did not answer), so its rules are not covered.
  • Rhode Island runs temporary disability and caregiver insurance; dlt.ri.gov returned 403 and the legislature's site did not answer, so its rules are not covered.
  • Virginia enacted a paid family and medical leave law in 2026; the Virginia Employment Commission's site returned 403 and the law is not yet in the Code of Virginia online, so its rules are not covered.

Not legal advice. Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it, and from 29 CFR Part 825. States whose official sites we could not read are named as not covered, never filled in from someone else's chart. Whether a condition qualifies, the earnings test and benefit amounts are decided by the employer and the state program; local ordinances and contracts can give more.
