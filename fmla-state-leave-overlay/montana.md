# FMLA and Montana family and medical leave

In Montana, Maternity leave and the FMLA count against each other when the leave qualifies under both (29 CFR 825.701(a)). For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 12 weeks (0 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 0 weeks.

## Worked totals

Each row is the engine's answer for an employee hired June 3, 2024 with 1,980 hours in the last 12 months, leave from January 11, 2027, everyone in the state and within 75 miles, and the earnings test met. A birth counts 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, then bonding.

Montana: protected and paid weeks by case

| Case | FMLA | Protected weeks | Paid weeks | Laws |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Birth, 100 employees | yes | 12 | 0 | Maternity leave |
| Birth, 20 employees | no | 0 | 0 | Maternity leave |
| Own health, 100 employees | yes | 12 | 0 | - |
| Family care, 100 employees | yes | 12 | 0 | - |
| Bonding (other parent), 100 employees | yes | 12 | 0 | - |
| Bonding, 20 employees | no | 0 | 0 | - |

The birth at 100 employees: 10 weeks of pregnancy disability protected by FMLA, then 2 weeks of bonding protected by FMLA. [Why the weeks add up this way](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/stacking/).

- A birth parent's pregnancy disability is counted under each state law's own-health (medical) leave unless the state names pregnancy separately (California PDL, Oregon OFLA, Iowa, Montana, Minnesota).
- Montana gives a reasonable leave of absence for the pregnancy without a number of weeks, so it is listed but not counted in the totals.

## Montana in its own words

- "refuse to grant to the employee a reasonable leave of absence for the pregnancy;" [Mont. Code Ann. 49-2-310, Maternity leave](https://archive.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0490/chapter_0020/part_0030/section_0100/0490-0020-0030-0100.html)
- "the employee must be reinstated to the employee's original job or to an equivalent position" [Mont. Code Ann. 49-2-311, Reinstatement following pregnancy-related leave](https://archive.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0490/chapter_0020/part_0030/section_0110/0490-0020-0030-0110.html)

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).
