# FMLA and Oregon family and medical leave

In Oregon, Paid Leave Oregon and the FMLA count against each other when the leave qualifies under both (29 CFR 825.701(a)). For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 22 weeks (12 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 12 weeks.

The birth at 100 employees: 10 weeks of pregnancy disability protected by FMLA and OFLA, then 12 weeks of bonding protected by FMLA and Paid Leave Oregon. [Why the weeks add up this way](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/stacking/).

- A birth parent's pregnancy disability is counted under each state law's own-health (medical) leave unless the state names pregnancy separately (California PDL, Oregon OFLA, Iowa, Montana, Minnesota).
- Extra weeks some states give for pregnancy incapacity or complications are not counted; tick the box if the provider certifies them.
- Oregon: OFLA and Paid Leave Oregon cannot be taken at the same time for one reason. The total assumes OFLA for the pregnancy disability and Paid Leave Oregon for bonding, which protects the most weeks; Paid Leave Oregon would then pay only the bonding weeks.

## Oregon in its own words

**Read the law's own words (8 quotations)**

- "Employees can take up to 12 weeks paid leave in a 52-week period (starting the Sunday before the date their leave begins) for family, medical, or safe leave. If pregnant, in some situations, an employee may be able to take up to 2 more weeks for a total of 14 weeks." [Paid Leave Oregon, home page](https://paidleave.oregon.gov/)
- "Employees who currently work in Oregon and who made at least $1,000 in Oregon in their base year before they apply for Paid Leave may be eligible for benefits." [Paid Leave Oregon, home page](https://paidleave.oregon.gov/)
- "Your job is protected by law while you're on paid leave if you have worked at least 90 consecutive days for your employer." [Paid Leave Oregon, Overview for employees](https://paidleave.oregon.gov/employees/overview.html)
- "To be eligible, your employer must have at least 25 employees. In addition, you must have worked an average of 25 hours per week for 180 days." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Oregon Family Leave Act](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/oregon-family-leave.aspx)
- "Qualifying reasons for OFLA are pregnancy disability leave, bereavement leave, and sick child leave." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Oregon Family Leave Act](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/oregon-family-leave.aspx)
- "additional weeks for pregnancy disability for the employee's own pregnancy. Employees need not exhaust one bank of leave in order to draw from the other." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Oregon Family Leave Act](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/oregon-family-leave.aspx)
- "Other leave laws can run at the same time. For example, both FMLA and OFLA would permit an employee to take pregnancy disability leave. Time taken for that event would be drawn down from both banks simultaneously." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Oregon Family Leave Act](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/oregon-family-leave.aspx)
- "If both Paid Leave Oregon and OFLA cover the reason for leave, say for pregnancy disability or caring for a child with a serious health condition, the employee may decide to apply for either leave. They cannot be taken at the same time." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Oregon Family Leave Act](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/oregon-family-leave.aspx)

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).

## Questions

### Does Paid Leave Oregon run at the same time as the FMLA in Oregon?

Oregon's pages do not say; under 29 CFR 825.701(a) leave that qualifies under both counts against both. Paid Leave Oregon and OFLA cannot be taken at the same time.

### Does OFLA run at the same time as the FMLA in Oregon?

FMLA and OFLA pregnancy disability leave are drawn down together. OFLA and Paid Leave Oregon cannot be taken at the same time; the employee picks one for a reason both cover.

### How many weeks does Paid Leave Oregon pay in Oregon?

12 weeks (52 weeks).
