# FMLA and District of Columbia family and medical leave

In the District of Columbia, DC PFL pays benefits, and the pages we read give it no job protection of its own; the FMLA and the DC FMLA protect the job. For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 26 weeks (at least 12 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 26 weeks.

The birth at 100 employees: 10 weeks of pregnancy disability protected by FMLA and DC FMLA, then 16 weeks of bonding protected by FMLA and DC FMLA. [Why the weeks add up this way](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/stacking/).

- A birth parent's pregnancy disability is counted under each state law's own-health (medical) leave unless the state names pregnancy separately (California PDL, Oregon OFLA, Iowa, Montana, Minnesota).
- The page we read gives each type's weeks but not a combined maximum, so the tool never adds medical and parental weeks together for DC; it shows the larger as a minimum.
- The page also lists 2 weeks of prenatal leave (for prenatal care, not counted in the totals here).

## District of Columbia in its own words

- "An employee shall be entitled to a total of 16 workweeks of family leave during any 24-month period for:" [D.C. Code 32-502, Family leave requirement](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-502)
- "except that the medical leave shall not exceed 16 workweeks during any 24-month period." [D.C. Code 32-503, Medical leave requirement](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-503)
- "Been employed by the same employer for at least 12 consecutive or non-consecutive months" [D.C. Code 32-501, Definitions (D.C. Family and Medical Leave Act)](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-501)
- "Worked at least 1,000 hours for the employer during the 12-month period" [D.C. Code 32-501, Definitions (D.C. Family and Medical Leave Act)](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-501)
- "to any employer who employs 20 or more persons in the District." [D.C. Code 32-516, Applicability](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-516)
- "The District will continue to provide benefits for up to six (6) weeks for family leave, ten (10) weeks for medical leave, twelve (12) weeks for parental leave, and two (2) weeks for prenatal leave." [DC Paid Family Leave, Employer Information](https://dcpaidfamilyleave.dc.gov/employer-information/)
- "DC Paid Family Leave is only available to private-sector employees whose employer pays the PFL tax." [DC Paid Family Leave, Employer Information](https://dcpaidfamilyleave.dc.gov/employer-information/)

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).

## Questions

### Does DC FMLA run at the same time as the FMLA in the District of Columbia?

Under 29 CFR 825.701(a) leave that qualifies under both counts against both. DC family leave and medical leave are separate 16-week banks.

### Does DC PFL run at the same time as the FMLA in the District of Columbia?

DC PFL pays benefits; the pages we read do not give it job protection. DC FMLA and federal FMLA protect the job.

### How many weeks does DC PFL pay in the District of Columbia?

family: 6 weeks; medical: 10 weeks; parental: 12 weeks (year).
