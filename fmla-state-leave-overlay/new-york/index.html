FMLA and New York family and medical leave

In New York, PFL runs with the FMLA once the employer designates the leave as both. For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 22 weeks (12 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 12 weeks.

Worked totals

Each row is the engine's answer for an employee hired June 3, 2024 with 1,980 hours in the last 12 months, leave from January 11, 2027, everyone in the state and within 75 miles, and the earnings test met. A birth counts 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, then bonding.

The birth at 100 employees: 10 weeks of pregnancy disability protected by FMLA, then 12 weeks of bonding protected by FMLA and PFL. Why the weeks add up this way.

  • A birth parent's pregnancy disability is counted under each state law's own-health (medical) leave unless the state names pregnancy separately (California PDL, Oregon OFLA, Iowa, Montana, Minnesota).
  • New York disability benefits (DBL) pay for a birth parent's own disability, but their weeks were not on the pages we read; the totals count only Paid Family Leave and the 26-week combined cap.

Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the FMLA eligibility checker. Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them (DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A).

Not legal advice. Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it, and from 29 CFR Part 825. States whose official sites we could not read are named as not covered, never filled in from someone else's chart. Whether a condition qualifies, the earnings test and benefit amounts are decided by the employer and the state program; local ordinances and contracts can give more.

Questions

Does PFL run at the same time as the FMLA in New York?

The employer can require PFL and FMLA to run together only after notifying the employee that the leave qualifies for both and is designated as both.

How many weeks does PFL pay in New York?

12 weeks (52 weeks).
