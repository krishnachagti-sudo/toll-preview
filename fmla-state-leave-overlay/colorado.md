# FMLA and Colorado family and medical leave

In Colorado, FAMLI runs at the same time as the FMLA when the leave qualifies under both. For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 12 weeks (12 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 12 weeks.

The birth at 100 employees: 10 weeks of pregnancy disability protected by FMLA and FAMLI, then 2 weeks of bonding protected by FMLA and FAMLI. [Why the weeks add up this way](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/stacking/).

- A birth parent's pregnancy disability is counted under each state law's own-health (medical) leave unless the state names pregnancy separately (California PDL, Oregon OFLA, Iowa, Montana, Minnesota).
- Extra weeks some states give for pregnancy incapacity or complications are not counted; tick the box if the provider certifies them.

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).

## Questions

### Does FAMLI run at the same time as the FMLA in Colorado?

FAMLI is designed to run concurrently with FMLA: FAMLI leave for a reason FMLA also covers counts as FMLA leave. Employers cannot require employees to use PTO before or while receiving FAMLI.

### How many weeks does FAMLI pay in Colorado?

12 weeks (year).
