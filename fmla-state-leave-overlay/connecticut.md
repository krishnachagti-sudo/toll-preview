# FMLA and Connecticut family and medical leave

In Connecticut, CT Paid Leave pays benefits but does not itself protect the job. For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 12 weeks (12 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 12 weeks.

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). ## Questions

### Does CT FMLA run at the same time as the FMLA in Connecticut?

Federal FMLA, CT FMLA and CT Paid Leave are generally limited together to 12 weeks in 12 months (plus 2 for pregnancy incapacity). An employer may require accrued PTO during CT FMLA but must let the employee keep at least two weeks.

### How many weeks does CT Paid Leave pay in Connecticut?

12 weeks (12 months (rolling)).
