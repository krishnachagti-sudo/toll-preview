# New America's explainer and this overlay

New America's explainer (dated May 19, 2026) describes every state program and its history; this tool works out one employee's case from each state's own rules, such as a California birth at a 100-person employer: 22 protected, 18 paid.

## What New America offers

- A policy explainer dated May 19, 2026, free to read.
- It counts 14 states and the District of Columbia with programs in place or coming, and says that as of May 1, 2026, 13 programs were paying benefits.
- It gives each program's history and start dates (for example, Minnesota's benefits began January 1, 2026, and Colorado's January 1, 2024).

Read on November 8, 2026: [Explainer: Paid and Unpaid Leave Policies in the United States](https://www.newamerica.org/better-life-lab/briefs/explainer-paid-and-unpaid-leave-policies-in-the-united-states/).

## What this tool does

- For one employee: state, reason, hire date, hours and headcount in; each law that applies, how it runs with the FMLA, and the weeks protected and paid out.
- Every state rule quoted from the state's own site with the date read; states we could not read are named, not guessed.
- Free on its page; $0.01 a lookup over the API or MCP; $1.00 for a case plan with a designation note and CSV.

## When New America is better

You want the history and policy context of paid leave in the US, or national data and research, rather than an answer for one employee.

> Not legal advice. Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it, and from 29 CFR Part 825. States whose official sites we could not read are named as not covered, never filled in from someone else's chart. Whether a condition qualifies, the earnings test and benefit amounts are decided by the employer and the state program; local ordinances and contracts can give more.
