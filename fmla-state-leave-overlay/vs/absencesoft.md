# An AbsenceSoft alternative for one question

AbsenceSoft sells leave case management by custom quote after a demo (no published price); this tool answers one question, which state laws stack on the FMLA and for how many weeks, for $0.01 a lookup over the API or free on its page, such as 22 protected, 18 paid for a California birth at a 100-person employer.

## What AbsenceSoft offers

- Leave and accommodation management software, run in-house or through a TPA.
- Says its compliance engine tracks over 200 federal and state leave and accommodation laws, with audit trails.
- Pricing is a custom proposal after a demo; no price is published.

Read on November 8, 2026: [absencesoft.com home page and pricing answer](https://absencesoft.com/).

## What this tool does

- For one employee: state, reason, hire date, hours and headcount in; each law that applies, how it runs with the FMLA, and the weeks protected and paid out.
- Every state rule quoted from the state's own site with the date read; states we could not read are named, not guessed.
- Free on its page; $0.01 a lookup over the API or MCP; $1.00 for a case plan with a designation note and CSV.

## When AbsenceSoft is better

You run many leave cases a year and need case management: intake, certifications, approvals, employee self-service, accommodations, audit trails and a team working in one system.
