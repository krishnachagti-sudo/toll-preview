# FMLA and California family and medical leave

In California, PFL pays benefits but does not itself protect the job. For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 22 weeks (18 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 22 weeks.

## California laws next to the FMLA

California: family and medical leave laws, read November 8, 2026

| Law | Who is protected | Job-protected weeks | Paid weeks | With the FMLA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pregnancy Disability Leave (PDL) | 5 or more employees; no length-of-service or hours test | up to 4 months (17 1/3 weeks on a full-time schedule, our conversion of 4 x 52 / 12): 17 1/3 weeks | - | PDL runs at the same time as FMLA. |
| California Family Rights Act (CFRA) | 5 or more employees; 1 year with the employer and 1,250 hours in the past year | 12 weeks | - | CFRA runs at the same time as FMLA; CFRA bonding leave runs after PDL. |
| State Disability Insurance (SDI) | no job protection on the pages we read | - | 52 weeks | SDI pays; it does not protect the job. FMLA, CFRA or PDL protect it. |
| Paid Family Leave (PFL) | no job protection on the pages we read | - | 8 weeks | PFL provides benefit payments but not job protection. |

## Worked totals

Each row is the engine's answer for an employee hired June 3, 2024 with 1,980 hours in the last 12 months, leave from January 11, 2027, everyone in the state and within 75 miles, and the earnings test met. A birth counts 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, then bonding.

California: protected and paid weeks by case

| Case | FMLA | Protected weeks | Paid weeks | Laws |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Birth, 100 employees | yes | 22 | 18 | PDL, CFRA, SDI, PFL |
| Birth, 20 employees | no | 22 | 18 | PDL, CFRA, SDI, PFL |
| Own health, 100 employees | yes | 12 | 52 | CFRA, SDI |
| Family care, 100 employees | yes | 12 | 8 | CFRA, PFL |
| Bonding (other parent), 100 employees | yes | 12 | 8 | CFRA, PFL |
| Bonding, 20 employees | no | 12 | 8 | CFRA, PFL |

The birth at 100 employees: 10 weeks of pregnancy disability protected by FMLA and PDL, then 12 weeks of bonding protected by FMLA and CFRA. [Why the weeks add up this way](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/stacking/).

- A birth parent's pregnancy disability is counted under each state law's own-health (medical) leave unless the state names pregnancy separately (California PDL, Oregon OFLA, Iowa, Montana, Minnesota).

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).

## Questions

### Does PDL run at the same time as the FMLA in California?

PDL runs at the same time as FMLA.

### Does CFRA run at the same time as the FMLA in California?

CFRA runs at the same time as FMLA; CFRA bonding leave runs after PDL.

### Does SDI run at the same time as the FMLA in California?

SDI pays; it does not protect the job. FMLA, CFRA or PDL protect it.

### How many weeks does PFL pay in California?

8 weeks (12 months).
