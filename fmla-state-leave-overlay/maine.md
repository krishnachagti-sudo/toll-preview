# FMLA and Maine family and medical leave

In Maine, Maine PFML runs at the same time as the FMLA when the leave qualifies under both. For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 12 weeks (12 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 12 weeks.

Each row is the engine's answer for an employee hired June 3, 2024 with 1,980 hours in the last 12 months, leave from January 11, 2027, everyone in the state and within 75 miles, and the earnings test met. A birth counts 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, then bonding.

The birth at 100 employees: 10 weeks of pregnancy disability protected by FMLA and Maine PFML and Maine FMLA, then 2 weeks of bonding protected by FMLA and Maine PFML. [Why the weeks add up this way](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/stacking/).

- A birth parent's pregnancy disability is counted under each state law's own-health (medical) leave unless the state names pregnancy separately (California PDL, Oregon OFLA, Iowa, Montana, Minnesota).
- Employees at the work location is taken as the count within 75 miles; enter it if it differs.

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).

## Questions

### Does Maine PFML run at the same time as the FMLA in Maine?

Maine PFML, federal FMLA and Maine FMLA run at the same time when they all apply; FMLA leave in the 12 months before counts against the 12 PFML weeks.

### Does Maine FMLA run at the same time as the FMLA in Maine?

Runs at the same time as federal FMLA and Maine PFML when they all apply.

### How many weeks does Maine PFML pay in Maine?

12 weeks (benefit year).
