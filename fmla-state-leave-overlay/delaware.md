# FMLA and Delaware family and medical leave

In Delaware, Delaware Paid Leave and the FMLA count against each other when the leave qualifies under both (29 CFR 825.701(a)). For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 12 weeks (12 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 0 weeks.

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).

## Questions

### Does Delaware Paid Leave run at the same time as the FMLA in Delaware?

The Delaware page we read does not say how the program runs with FMLA or whether it protects the job; under 29 CFR 825.701(a) and the DOL opinion letter, FMLA-qualifying leave is designated FMLA.

### How many weeks does Delaware Paid Leave pay in Delaware?

parental (per year): 12 weeks; family care (per 24 months): 6 weeks; own health (per 24 months): 6 weeks; military (per 24 months): 6 weeks, at most 12 weeks combined (year).
