# FMLA and Washington family and medical leave

In Washington, Paid Leave runs at the same time as the FMLA when the leave qualifies under both. For a birth parent at a 100-person employer, hired in June 2024 and out from January 11, 2027 with 10 weeks of pregnancy disability, the laws together protect 16 weeks (16 paid); at a 20-person employer, where the FMLA does not apply, 0 weeks.

## Federal side

FMLA eligibility (50 employees within 75 miles, 12 months, 1,250 hours) is worked out by the [FMLA eligibility checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-eligibility-checker/). Leave that qualifies under the FMLA and a state law counts against both (29 CFR 825.701(a)); state-paid weeks that the FMLA also covers must still be designated as FMLA leave, and no one can force PTO to run during them ([DOL Opinion Letter FMLA2025-01-A](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fmla-state-leave-overlay/guides/dol-opinion-letter-fmla2025-01-a/)).

## Questions

### Does Paid Leave run at the same time as the FMLA in Washington?

Paid Leave and FMLA usually run concurrently; from January 1, 2026 an employer may count FMLA leave against Paid Leave job protection (16 typical workweeks in 52, 18 with pregnancy incapacity) if it gives the state's written notice within 5 business days and monthly.

### How many weeks does Paid Leave pay in Washington?

medical: 12 weeks; family: 12 weeks, at most 16 weeks combined (claim year).
