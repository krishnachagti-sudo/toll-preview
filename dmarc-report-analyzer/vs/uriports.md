# URIports and this analyzer

URIports' Pebble plan is USD 6 a month billed annually (USD 7 monthly) for 100,000 reports a month and 5 domains, and its Sand plan for personal and hobby projects is USD 15 a year; here reports cost $0.01 each up to 1,000 a month, so URIports is cheaper for anyone above about 700 reports a month, and this analyzer for occasional reading with no setup (read November 8, 2026).

## Try a report here

[Open this sample in the analyzer](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/dmarc-report-analyzer/?sample=google)

## What URIports offers

- Sand (personal and hobby): USD 15 a year, 3 domains, 10,000 reports a month.
- Pebble: USD 6 a month annually, USD 7 monthly; 100,000 reports a month, 5 domains, 30 days retention; hosted MTA-STS optional at USD 6 a month.
- Stone: USD 30 a month annually (USD 33 monthly), 500,000 reports, 25 domains, a reporting API; larger plans up to USD 480 a month.
- DMARC, CSP, TLS-RPT, DNS and certificate monitoring in one platform; one-month free trial.

## What this analyzer does

- Reads one report, or up to 50 at once, the moment you have it: .xml, .gz, .zip or the .eml, RFC 9990 or RFC 7489. You do not change your rua= address.
- Names the service behind each IP from published provider data, says why it failed and how it is usually fixed, with the evidence.
- One report on the page is free; over the API, MCP or several files at once it is $0.01 a report file for the first 1,000 a month, then $0.002; no subscription.
- Keeps nothing: there is no history or dashboard, by design.

## When URIports is better

- You want continuous monitoring of many reports: at USD 7 a month it is far cheaper than per-report pricing above about 700 reports a month.
- You also want TLS-RPT, CSP reports, hosted MTA-STS or certificate monitoring.
