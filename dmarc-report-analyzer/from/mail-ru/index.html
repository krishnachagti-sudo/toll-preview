DMARC reports from Mail.ru

Reports from Mail.ru arrive from dmarc_support@corp.mail.ru as gzip (.xml.gz); each one lists the mail Mail.ru's systems received claiming your domain over one UTC day, grouped by sending IP, and is sent because your domain's DMARC record has a rua= address. In Mail.ru's format, a sample for example.com reads: 670 messages from 7 sources; 90 messages (13.4%) failed DMARC from 4 sources. The largest is Amazon SES (54.240.8.17), 57 failing messages: DKIM passed for d=amazonses.com and SPF passed for us-east-1.amazonses.com, but neither is example.com, so neither aligns.

What a Mail.ru report looks like

Its quirks

  • The subject separates its parts with semicolons and names the submitter as "Mail.Ru", not a domain name.
  • extra_contact_info is http://help.mail.ru/mail-help.

What Mail.ru says about its reports

  • Aggregate (RUA) reports are sent daily by a mail server that received mail from your domain in the past day, and contain SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
  • Reports arrive only if mail with your domain in the From field reached the receiving server in the past day.
  • A report is a GZIP archive (.gz) holding an XML file; Mail.ru's own sample names org_name Mail.Ru and the address dmarc_support@corp.mail.ru.

Questions

Is the DMARC report email from Mail.ru legitimate?

A real one comes from dmarc_support@corp.mail.ru, names Mail.Ru as the submitter in the subject and carries mail.ru!<your domain>!<begin>!<end>.xml.gz. It is sent only because your DMARC record asks for reports (rua=). Do not open other attachment types it might carry.

How do I stop the Mail.ru DMARC reports?

Remove the rua= tag (or the address in it) from your domain's DMARC TXT record at _dmarc.<your domain>; RFC 9989 says receivers must not send aggregate reports when there is no rua tag. Keep it if you are still checking your senders or moving to quarantine or reject.
