SPF pass but DMARC fail

SPF can pass and DMARC still fail because DMARC needs SPF or DKIM to pass for a domain aligned with your From: domain: in the sample, 54.240.8.17 (Amazon SES) passed SPF for us-east-1.amazonses.com and DKIM for d=amazonses.com, but neither is example.com, so the receiver reported dkim=fail and spf=fail under policy_evaluated.

Analyzer's reasonDKIM passed for d=amazonses.com and SPF passed for us-east-1.amazonses.com, but neither is example.com, so neither aligns.
FixSet up the sender's custom DKIM so it signs with d=example.com (or a subdomain), or a custom MAIL FROM (return-path) under example.com.

The causes

PatternCauseFix
SPF auth pass, SPF aligned failThe service uses its own bounce (MAIL FROM) domainSet a custom MAIL FROM or return-path under your domain, or rely on DKIM
DKIM auth pass, DKIM aligned failThe service signs with its own domainTurn on custom DKIM so it signs with d=<your domain>
Both fail, DKIM for your domain failsThe message was changed in transit (forwarding, a list, a gateway)Usually nothing; see forwarding and mailing lists
Passes on SPF onlyNo DKIM for your domainAdd DKIM: SPF breaks on every forward

Relaxed and strict alignment

With adkim=r and aspf=r (the defaults), a subdomain aligns with its organizational domain: mail.example.com aligns with example.com. With s (strict) the domains must match exactly. In the sample, HubSpot passes because it signs with d=example.com, even though SPF passed only for its own bounce domain.
