## What are the 2026 Section 179 limits?

You can expense up to $2,560,000 of equipment under Section 179 for 2026. The limit shrinks dollar for dollar once the equipment you place in service passes $4,090,000, so it is gone at $6,650,000. Work out a full schedule with [the depreciation tool](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/macrs-depreciation/).

Section 179 figures. Source: Rev. Proc. 2025-32 sec. 4.24, and IRS Publication 946 (2025), What's New for 2026.

| Figure | 2025 | 2026 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Maximum deduction | $2,500,000 | $2,560,000 |
| Phase-out starts when equipment placed in service costs more than | $4,000,000 | $4,090,000 |
| Deduction fully phased out at | $6,500,000 | $6,650,000 |
| Cap for a sport utility vehicle | $31,300 | $32,000 |
| Special (bonus) depreciation, property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025 | 100% | 100% |

## What are the 2026 self-employment tax rate and wage base?

Self-employment tax is 15.3% for 2026: 12.4% for Social Security on the first $184,500 of net earnings and 2.9% for Medicare on all of it. It applies to 92.35% of your net profit. ## Questions

### What is the IRS business mileage rate for 2026?

72.5 cents a mile to June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. The medical and moving rate is 20.5 cents and then 23.5 cents, and the charity rate is 14 cents.

### What is the 1099-NEC reporting threshold for 2026?

$2,000 in nonemployee compensation paid to a payee during 2026 (1099-NEC box 1a), per the Rev. 12/2026 IRS instructions.

### What is the Section 179 limit for 2026?

$2,560,000, reduced dollar for dollar once the cost of Section 179 property placed in service in the year passes $4,090,000. The cap for a sport utility vehicle is $32,000.

### What is the 2026 standard deduction?

$16,100 for a single filer, $32,200 for married filing jointly and $24,150 for head of household (Rev. Proc. 2025-32).

### What is the Social Security wage base for 2026?

$184,500. Self-employment tax is 12.4% Social Security up to that amount and 2.9% Medicare on all of it, on 92.35% of net earnings.
