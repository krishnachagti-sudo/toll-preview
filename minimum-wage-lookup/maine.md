# Maine Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027 (Plus 1 Local Rate)

The Maine minimum wage is $15.10 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $15.70. 1 city or county in Maine set its own higher rate we have read; the tipped cash wage is $7.55 with a tip credit of up to $7.55 (DOL).

## Questions

### What is the minimum wage in Maine in 2026?

$15.10 an hour, per DOL's table updated November 5, 2026; some cities and counties set higher rates.

### What will the Maine minimum wage be in 2027?

$15.70 an hour from January 1, 2027 (Maine Department of Labor: Maine's Minimum Wage to Increase to $15.70 Per Hour in 2027).

### Does Maine allow a tip credit?

The tipped cash wage is $7.55 with a tip credit of up to $7.55 (DOL).
