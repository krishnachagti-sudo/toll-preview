# New York Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The New York minimum wage is $16.00 an hour (New York City $17.00; Long Island and Westchester $17.00; Remainder of New York State $16.00) (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is no change. No city or county in New York sets its own rate in the inventory we used; tipped cash wages vary by region and type of employer.

New York: no change on January 1, 2027 (New York's rate stays the same on January 1, 2027 (an inflation "off-ramp" was met).).
