# Massachusetts Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Massachusetts minimum wage is $15.00 an hour since January 1, 2023; on January 1, 2027 there is no change. No city or county in Massachusetts sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $6.75 with a tip credit of up to $8.25 (DOL).
