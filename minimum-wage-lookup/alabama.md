# Alabama Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

Alabama has no minimum wage law of its own, so the federal $7.25 an hour applies to employers covered by the Fair Labor Standards Act (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); the tipped cash wage is $2.13 with a tip credit of up to $5.12 (DOL).
