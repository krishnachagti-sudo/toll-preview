# Symmetry Minimum Wage Finder Alternative: Pay per Lookup

Symmetry's Minimum Wage Finder geocodes work addresses against its own library of geospatial files and is sold through a demo and sales process (no price on its page when we read it on November 8, 2026); this lookup answers one place free on the web and $0.002 a place over the API, with the official source and read date on every rate.

## What Symmetry's page says

- Lookups by state, city, work or home address, job type, job function and effective date.
- Addresses are geocoded and checked against "35,000 geospatial files".
- Batch lookups from CSV for audits; embed or integrate into payroll products.
- No public price: the page offers a demo and a pricing link.

## Where this lookup differs

- A price on the page: one place free on the web, $0.002 a place over REST and MCP, $0.001 past 10,000 a month.
- Every rate links to the city, county or state page it was read from, with the date.
- Places whose pages we could not read are named as not covered instead of filled in.
- No job-function or industry rates: hotel, health care and fast food minimums are named as notes only.

## When Symmetry is the better choice

When you run a payroll product and need job-function and industry rates, a support contract and geospatial coverage of every jurisdiction, Symmetry is built for that.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.
