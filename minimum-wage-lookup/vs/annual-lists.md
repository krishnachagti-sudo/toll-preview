# Annual Minimum Wage Lists vs a Live Lookup by Address

An annual list such as Experian's 2027 article is one reading of many sources on one day; a lookup answers a specific address on a specific date, names the government that sets the rate and the page it was read from, and lists what it could not read.

## What the annual list gives

- Statewide 2027 increases with notes on tipped and training wages and salary thresholds.
- A selection of local changes and posting reminders.
- One article, read once a year.

## What it leaves to you

- Which side of a city line an address is on (unincorporated county areas have their own rates in Los Angeles, San Mateo, King and Boulder counties).
- Rates for a past or future payroll date, and mid-year (July 1) changes.
- Which employer-size tier applies.

## When the annual list is the better choice

When you want a printable overview of the year, with posting-requirement reminders, to read once.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.
