# New Hampshire Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The New Hampshire minimum wage is $7.25 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is no change scheduled. No city or county in New Hampshire sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $3.27 with a tip credit of up to $3.98 (DOL).
