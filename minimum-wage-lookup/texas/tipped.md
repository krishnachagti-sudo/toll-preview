# Texas Tipped Minimum Wage 2026: Cash Wage and Tip Credit

In Texas a tipped employee must get at least $2.13 an hour in cash; the maximum tip credit is $5.12 and tips must bring pay to $7.25 (DOL's tipped table updated November 5, 2026).

## A worked week

A server works 30 hours and reports $90 in tips. Cash wages at $2.13: $63.90. Wages plus tips: $153.90, against $217.50 at the full $7.25, so the employer adds $63.60.

## Questions

### What is the tipped minimum wage in Texas?

$2.13 an hour in cash wages, with a tip credit of up to $5.12 toward the $7.25 minimum (DOL).
