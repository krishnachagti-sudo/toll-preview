# Maryland Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027 (Plus 2 Local Rates)

The Maryland minimum wage is $15.00 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is no change scheduled. 2 cities and counties in Maryland set their own higher rates we have read; the tipped cash wage is $3.63 with a tip credit of up to $11.37 (DOL).
