# Florida Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Florida minimum wage is $15.00 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is no change scheduled. No city or county in Florida sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $11.98 with a tip credit of up to $3.02 (DOL).
