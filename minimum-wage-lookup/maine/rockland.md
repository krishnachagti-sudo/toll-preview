# Rockland Minimum Wage 2026: $16.00

The Rockland minimum wage is $16.00 an hour from January 1, 2026 (tipped employees: $8.00 cash wage); it applies to work performed inside Rockland city limits, and the Maine rate is $15.10. Next: changes on January 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

## Where it applies

Inside Rockland city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Maine rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
