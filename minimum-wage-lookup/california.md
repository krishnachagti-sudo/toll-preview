# California Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027 (Plus 38 Local Rates)

The California minimum wage is $16.90 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $17.40. 38 cities and counties in California set their own higher rates we have read; California allows no tip credit: tipped employees get the full minimum wage before tips.

### What will the California minimum wage be in 2027?

### Does California allow a tip credit?
