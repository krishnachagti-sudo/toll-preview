# Virginia Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Virginia minimum wage is $12.77 an hour since January 1, 2026; on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $13.75. No city or county in Virginia sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $2.13 with a tip credit of up to $10.64 (DOL).

## January 1, 2027

Virginia: $13.75 from January 1, 2027 (up from $12.77).

> We answer for dates up to December 31, 2028; Virginia's rate after that was not on the pages we read on November 8, 2026.

## Questions

### What is the minimum wage in Virginia in 2026?

$12.77 an hour, per DOL's table updated November 5, 2026.

### What will the Virginia minimum wage be in 2027?

$13.75 an hour from January 1, 2027 (Virginia Department of Labor and Industry: Payment of Wage).
