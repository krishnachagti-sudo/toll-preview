Questions

What is the federal tipped minimum wage?

$2.13 an hour in cash wages, with a maximum tip credit of $5.12 toward the $7.25 minimum (DOL).

Which states do not allow a tip credit?

Alaska, California, Minnesota, Montana (businesses over $110,000 in sales), Nevada, Oregon and Washington require the full state minimum before tips (DOL).
