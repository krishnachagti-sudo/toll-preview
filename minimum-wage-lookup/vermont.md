# Vermont Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Vermont minimum wage is $14.42 an hour since January 1, 2026; on January 1, 2027 there is a change we could not read from the state's own page. No city or county in Vermont sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $7.21 with a tip credit of up to $7.21 (DOL).
