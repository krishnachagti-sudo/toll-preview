# Minimum Wage Changes on July 1, 2027

2 July 1, 2027 changes were published on official pages by November 8, 2026 (Saint Paul: Micro businesses (5 or fewer employees) $15.00; Everett: Covered employers (15 to 499 employees, or over $2 million annual gross revenue in Everett) $21.52); 17 more states, cities and counties adjust that day and publish their rates in the spring.

## Published

- [Saint Paul, Minnesota](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/minimum-wage-lookup/minnesota/saint-paul/): Micro businesses (5 or fewer employees) $15.00
