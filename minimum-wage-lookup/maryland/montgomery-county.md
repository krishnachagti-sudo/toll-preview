# Montgomery County Minimum Wage 2026: $18.00

The Montgomery County minimum wage is $18.00 an hour from July 1, 2026 (tipped employees: $4.00 cash wage); it applies to work performed in Montgomery County, and the Maryland rate is $15.00. Next: changes on July 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

## Montgomery County minimum wage by date

Montgomery County: rates from its own page

| From | Rate (largest employers) | Other employers | Tipped cash wage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| July 1, 2025 | $17.65 | Mid-sized employers (11 to 50 employees): $16.00; Small employers (10 or fewer employees): $15.50 | $4.00 |
| July 1, 2026 | $18.00 | Mid-sized employers (11 to 50 employees): $16.50; Small employers (10 or fewer employees): $15.95 | $4.00 |

Tipped employees: the employer pays at least $4.00 an hour and tips must bring pay to the county rate.

We answer for dates up to June 30, 2027: Montgomery County adjusts its rate every July 1, and the rate after that was not published when we read its page on November 8, 2026.

## Where it applies

In Montgomery County. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Maryland rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
