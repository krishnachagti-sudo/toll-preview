# Howard County Minimum Wage 2026: $16.00, $16.45 in 2027

The Howard County minimum wage is $16.00 an hour from January 1, 2026; it applies to work performed in Howard County, and the Maryland rate is $15.00. Next: $16.45 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

In Howard County. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Maryland rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
