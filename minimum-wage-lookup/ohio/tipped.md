# Ohio Tipped Minimum Wage 2026: Cash Wage and Tip Credit

In Ohio a tipped employee must get at least $5.50 an hour in cash; the maximum tip credit is $5.50 and tips must bring pay to $11.00 (DOL's tipped table updated November 5, 2026). From January 1, 2027 the cash wage is $5.70 on a $11.40 minimum.

Ohio tipped employees (DOL, updated November 5, 2026)

| --- | --- |

| Minimum cash wage | $5.50 |

DOL: Employers with annual gross receipts of $405,000 or more.

## A worked week

A server works 30 hours and reports $90 in tips. Cash wages at $5.50: $165.00. Wages plus tips: $255.00, against $330.00 at the full $11.00, so the employer adds $75.00.

### What is the tipped minimum wage in Ohio?
