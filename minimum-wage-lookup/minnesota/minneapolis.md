# Minneapolis Minimum Wage 2026: $16.37, $17.02 in 2027

The Minneapolis minimum wage is $16.37 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Minneapolis city limits, and the Minnesota rate is $11.41. Next: $17.02 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

Inside Minneapolis city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Minnesota rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ.
