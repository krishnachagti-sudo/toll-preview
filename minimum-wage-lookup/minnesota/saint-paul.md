# Saint Paul Minimum Wage 2026: $16.37

The Saint Paul minimum wage is $16.37 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Saint Paul city limits, and the Minnesota rate is $11.41. Next: changes on January 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

> The city's table shows the 2027 rates for businesses over 5 employees only as "City Rate" (an inflation formula), without the figure: those dates are not covered until the city posts it.

Large and macro businesses have 101 or more employees.

We answer for dates up to December 31, 2027: Saint Paul adjusts its rate every January 1 (micro businesses July 1), and the rate after that was not published when we read its page on November 8, 2026.

## Where it applies

Inside Saint Paul city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Minnesota rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.
