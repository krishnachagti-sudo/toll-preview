# New Jersey Tipped Minimum Wage 2026: Cash Wage and Tip Credit

In New Jersey a tipped employee must get at least $6.05 an hour in cash; the maximum tip credit is $9.87 and tips must bring pay to $15.92 (DOL's tipped table updated November 5, 2026).

DOL: The tip credit needs advance notice to the employee.

## A worked week

A server works 30 hours and reports $90 in tips. Cash wages at $6.05: $181.50. Wages plus tips: $271.50, against $477.60 at the full $15.92, so the employer adds $206.10.
