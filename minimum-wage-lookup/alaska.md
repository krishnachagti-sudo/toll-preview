# Alaska Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Alaska minimum wage is $14.00 an hour since July 1, 2026; on January 1, 2027 there is no change scheduled. No city or county in Alaska sets its own rate in the inventory we used; Alaska allows no tip credit: tipped employees get the full minimum wage before tips.
