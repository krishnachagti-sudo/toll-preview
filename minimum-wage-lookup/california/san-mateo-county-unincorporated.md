# San Mateo County (unincorporated) Minimum Wage 2026: $17.95, $18.58 in 2027

The San Mateo County (unincorporated) minimum wage is $17.95 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed in the unincorporated areas of San Mateo County (outside any city), and the California rate is $16.90. Next: $18.58 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

In the unincorporated areas of San Mateo County (outside any city). A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
