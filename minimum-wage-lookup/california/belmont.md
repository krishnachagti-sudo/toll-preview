# Belmont Minimum Wage 2026: $18.95, $19.60 in 2027

The Belmont minimum wage is $18.95 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Belmont city limits, and the California rate is $16.90. Next: $19.60 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

Inside Belmont city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
