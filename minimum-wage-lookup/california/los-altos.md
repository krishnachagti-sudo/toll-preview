# Los Altos Minimum Wage 2027: $19.25

The Los Altos minimum wage is $19.25 an hour from January 1, 2027; it applies to work performed inside Los Altos city limits. The city's page gives only the 2027 rate, so dates before January 1, 2027 are not covered.

## Where it applies

Inside Los Altos city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
