# San Francisco Minimum Wage 2026: $19.61

The San Francisco minimum wage is $19.61 an hour from July 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside San Francisco city limits, and the California rate is $16.90. Next: changes on July 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

## Where it applies

Inside San Francisco city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.
