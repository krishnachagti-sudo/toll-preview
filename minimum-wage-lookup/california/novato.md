# Novato Minimum Wage 2026: $17.73, $18.25 in 2027

The Novato minimum wage is $17.73 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Novato city limits, and the California rate is $16.90. Next: $18.25 on January 1, 2027.

## Novato minimum wage by date

Novato: rates from its own page

| From | Rate (largest employers) | Other employers | Tipped cash wage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January 1, 2026 | $17.73 | Large businesses (26 to 99 employees): $17.46; Small businesses (1 to 25 employees): the state rate | no tip credit |
| January 1, 2027 | $18.25 | Large businesses (26 to 99 employees): $17.97; Small businesses (1 to 25 employees): the state rate | no tip credit |

The headline rate is for very large businesses with 100 or more employees.

We answer for dates up to December 31, 2027: Novato adjusts its rate every January 1, and the rate after that was not published when we read its page on November 8, 2026.

## Where it applies

Inside Novato city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.
