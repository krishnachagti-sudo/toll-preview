# Redwood City Minimum Wage 2026: $18.65, $19.30 in 2027

The Redwood City minimum wage is $18.65 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Redwood City city limits, and the California rate is $16.90. Next: $19.30 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

Inside Redwood City city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
