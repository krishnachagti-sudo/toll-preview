# Richmond Minimum Wage 2026: $19.18, $19.61 in 2027

The Richmond minimum wage is $19.18 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Richmond city limits, and the California rate is $16.90. Next: $19.61 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

Inside Richmond city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ.
