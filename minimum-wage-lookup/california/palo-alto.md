# Palo Alto Minimum Wage 2026: $18.70, $19.25 in 2027

The Palo Alto minimum wage is $18.70 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Palo Alto city limits, and the California rate is $16.90. Next: $19.25 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

Inside Palo Alto city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed.
