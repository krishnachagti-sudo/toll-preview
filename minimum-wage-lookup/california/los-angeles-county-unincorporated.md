# Los Angeles County (unincorporated) Minimum Wage 2026: $18.47

The Los Angeles County (unincorporated) minimum wage is $18.47 an hour from July 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed in the unincorporated areas of Los Angeles County (outside any city), and the California rate is $16.90. Next: changes on July 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

## Where it applies

In the unincorporated areas of Los Angeles County (outside any city). A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
