# Burlingame Minimum Wage 2026: $17.86, $18.47 in 2027

The Burlingame minimum wage is $17.86 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Burlingame city limits, and the California rate is $16.90. Next: $18.47 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

Inside Burlingame city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
