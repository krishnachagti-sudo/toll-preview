# Fremont Minimum Wage 2026: $18.05

The Fremont minimum wage is $18.05 an hour from July 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Fremont city limits, and the California rate is $16.90. Next: changes on July 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

## Where it applies

Inside Fremont city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ.
