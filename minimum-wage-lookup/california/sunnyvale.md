# Sunnyvale Minimum Wage 2026: $19.50, $20.05 in 2027

The Sunnyvale minimum wage is $19.50 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Sunnyvale city limits, and the California rate is $16.90. Next: $20.05 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

Inside Sunnyvale city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the California rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ.
