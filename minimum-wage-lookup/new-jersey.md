# New Jersey Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The New Jersey minimum wage is $15.92 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $16.48. No city or county in New Jersey sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $6.05 with a tip credit of up to $9.87 (DOL).
