Minimum Wage by State 2027: Every Announced Rate

10 states have announced January 1, 2027 rates on their own labor pages we read; the highest is Washington at $17.73 from January 1, 2027. 11 states' pages could not be read (Arizona, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Hawaii, Missouri, Nebraska, Nevada, New Mexico, Rhode Island, Vermont), so their 2027 rates are marked not covered rather than guessed.

  • Delaware: The Delaware Department of Labor page we read gives no rate.

  • New Mexico: The New Mexico Department of Workforce Solutions page refused this network (HTTP 403) on 2026-11-08.
  • Rhode Island: The Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training page refused this network (HTTP 403) on 2026-11-08.
  • Vermont: null

Cities and counties on January 1, 2027

Local changes on the same day are in the change calendar and on each city's page.

Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.
