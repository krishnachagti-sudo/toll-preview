# Everett Minimum Wage 2026: $20.77, $21.52 in 2027

The Everett minimum wage is $20.77 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Everett city limits, and the Washington rate is $17.13. Next: $21.52 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

Inside Everett city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Washington rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
