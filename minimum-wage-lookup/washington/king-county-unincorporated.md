# King County (unincorporated) Minimum Wage 2026: $20.82, $21.57 in 2027

The King County (unincorporated) minimum wage is $20.82 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed in the unincorporated areas of King County (outside any city), and the Washington rate is $17.13. Next: $21.57 on January 1, 2027.

## Where it applies

In the unincorporated areas of King County (outside any city). A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Washington rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
