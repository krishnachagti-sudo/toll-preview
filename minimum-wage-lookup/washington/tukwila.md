# Tukwila Minimum Wage 2026: $21.65, $22.45 in 2027

The Tukwila minimum wage is $21.65 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Tukwila city limits, and the Washington rate is $17.13. Next: $22.45 on January 1, 2027.

We answer for dates up to December 31, 2027: Tukwila adjusts its rate every January 1, and the rate after that was not published when we read its page on November 8, 2026.

## Where it applies

Inside Tukwila city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Washington rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.
