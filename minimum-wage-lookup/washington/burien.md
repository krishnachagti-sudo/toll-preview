# Burien Minimum Wage 2026: $21.78, $22.49 in 2027

The Burien minimum wage is $21.78 an hour from January 1, 2026 (no tip credit: tipped employees get the full rate); it applies to work performed inside Burien city limits, and the Washington rate is $17.13. Next: $22.49 on January 1, 2027.

## Burien minimum wage by date

Burien: rates from its own page

| From | Rate (largest employers) | Other employers | Tipped cash wage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January 1, 2026 | $21.78 | Medium employers (16 to 500 employees): $20.78; Small employers (15 or fewer employees): $19.28 | no tip credit |
| January 1, 2027 | $22.49 | Medium employers (16 to 500 employees): equal to the large employer rate: $22.49; Small employers (15 or fewer employees): $20.49 | no tip credit |

Large employers have 501 or more employees.

We answer for dates up to December 31, 2027: Burien adjusts its rate every January 1, and the rate after that was not published when we read its page on November 8, 2026.

## Where it applies

Inside Burien city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Washington rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed.
