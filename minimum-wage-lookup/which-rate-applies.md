# Federal, State or City Minimum Wage: Which One Applies?

The employee gets the highest applicable rate, and local ordinances follow where the work is performed: on November 8, 2026 that is $18.42 in the City of Los Angeles against California's $16.90 and the federal $7.25; enter the address to see which one wins.

Unincorporated means outside any city: Los Angeles County's ordinance covers its unincorporated areas only, while the City of Los Angeles sets its own rate inside city limits. A ZIP code can cover both, so a street address decides.

## Steps

1. **Start with the federal floor.** Employers covered by the Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least $7.25 an hour.
2. **Add the state rate.** If the state sets a higher rate for your employer (some states exempt small employers), it applies.
3. **Add the county and city rates.** A county or city ordinance applies to work performed inside its boundary; unincorporated county ordinances apply only outside cities.
4. **Pay the highest.** The employee gets the highest applicable rate for each hour, by where the work is performed.

## Questions

### If my city's minimum wage is higher than the state's, which do I get?

The higher one: employers must pay the highest applicable rate where the work is performed.

### Can you earn below minimum wage?

Only where a law allows it: a tip credit for tipped employees, or youth, training and learner rates some states and cities set; employers outside a state law's coverage may still owe the federal $7.25.
