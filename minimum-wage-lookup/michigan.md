# Michigan Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Michigan minimum wage is $13.73 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $15.00. No city or county in Michigan sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $5.49 with a tip credit of up to $8.24 (DOL).
