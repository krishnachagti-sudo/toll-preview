# Oregon Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Oregon minimum wage is $15.55 an hour (Portland metro (inside the urban growth boundary) $16.80; Standard $15.55; Non-urban counties $14.55) since July 1, 2026; on January 1, 2027 there is no change (it adjusts on July 1). No city or county in Oregon sets its own rate in the inventory we used; Oregon allows no tip credit: tipped employees get the full minimum wage before tips.

DOL's table on Oregon: Standard state rate; $16.80 in the Portland metro area and $14.55 in non-urban counties.

Clackamas, Multnomah and Washington counties are split: inside the Portland urban growth boundary the metro rate applies, outside it the standard rate.

## January 1, 2027

Oregon: no change on January 1, 2027 (Oregon adjusts on July 1; the July 1, 2027 rates were not published when we read the page.).
