# South Dakota Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The South Dakota minimum wage is $11.85 an hour since January 1, 2026; on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $12.30. No city or county in South Dakota sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $5.93 with a tip credit of up to $5.92 (DOL).

## Questions

### What is the minimum wage in South Dakota in 2026?

$11.85 an hour, per DOL's table updated November 5, 2026.

### What will the South Dakota minimum wage be in 2027?

$12.30 an hour from January 1, 2027 (South Dakota Department of Labor and Regulation: Minimum Wage).

### Does South Dakota allow a tip credit?

The tipped cash wage is $5.93 with a tip credit of up to $5.92 (DOL).
