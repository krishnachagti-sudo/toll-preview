# Ohio Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Ohio minimum wage is $11.00 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $11.40. No city or county in Ohio sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $5.50 with a tip credit of up to $5.50 (DOL).
