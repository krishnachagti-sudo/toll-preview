# Denver Minimum Wage 2026: $19.29

The Denver minimum wage is $19.29 an hour from January 1, 2026 (tipped employees: $16.27 cash wage); it applies to work performed inside Denver city limits, and the Colorado rate is $15.16. Next: changes on January 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

## Where it applies

Inside Denver city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Colorado rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ.
