# Edgewater Minimum Wage 2026: $18.17

The Edgewater minimum wage is $18.17 an hour from January 1, 2026 (tipped employees: $15.15 cash wage); it applies to work performed inside Edgewater city limits, and the Colorado rate is $15.16. Next: changes on January 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

## Where it applies

Inside Edgewater city limits. A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Colorado rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
