# Colorado Tipped Minimum Wage 2026: Cash Wage and Tip Credit

In Colorado a tipped employee must get at least $12.14 an hour in cash; the maximum tip credit is $3.02 and tips must bring pay to $15.16 (DOL's tipped table updated November 5, 2026).

## A worked week

A server works 30 hours and reports $90 in tips. Cash wages at $12.14: $364.20. Wages plus tips: $454.20, against $454.80 at the full $15.16, so the employer adds $0.60.
