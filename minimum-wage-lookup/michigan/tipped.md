# Michigan Tipped Minimum Wage 2026: Cash Wage and Tip Credit

In Michigan a tipped employee must get at least $5.49 an hour in cash; the maximum tip credit is $8.24 and tips must bring pay to $13.73 (DOL's tipped table updated November 5, 2026).

DOL: The cash wage for tipped employees rises each year until it reaches 50% of the state minimum wage in 2031.

## A worked week

A server works 30 hours and reports $90 in tips. Cash wages at $5.49: $164.70. Wages plus tips: $254.70, against $411.90 at the full $13.73, so the employer adds $157.20.

## Questions

### What is the tipped minimum wage in Michigan?

$5.49 an hour in cash wages, with a tip credit of up to $8.24 toward the $13.73 minimum (DOL).
