# Santa Fe County (unincorporated) Minimum Wage 2026: $15.40

The Santa Fe County (unincorporated) minimum wage is $15.40 an hour from March 1, 2026 (tipped employees: $4.62 cash wage); it applies to work performed in the unincorporated areas of Santa Fe County (outside any city), and the New Mexico rate is $12.00. Next: changes on January 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

## Where it applies

In the unincorporated areas of Santa Fe County (outside any city). A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the New Mexico rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.
