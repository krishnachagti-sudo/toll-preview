# Nevada Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Nevada minimum wage is $12.00 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is a change we could not read from the state's own page. No city or county in Nevada sets its own rate in the inventory we used; Nevada allows no tip credit: tipped employees get the full minimum wage before tips.
