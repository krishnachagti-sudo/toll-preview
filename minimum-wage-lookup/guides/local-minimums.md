## Unincorporated county rates

Los Angeles, San Mateo, King, Boulder and Santa Fe counties set rates for their unincorporated areas only: a city inside the county follows its own rate or the state's.

## Questions

### Does city or state minimum wage apply?

Where both exist, the higher rate applies to work performed in that city.
