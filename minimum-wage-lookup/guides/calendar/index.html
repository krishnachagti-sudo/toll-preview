Minimum Wage Change Calendar: January 1 and July 1, 2027

43 changes we hold take effect on January 1, 2027 and 2 on July 1, 2027; places that adjust on July 1 (Los Angeles, San Francisco, Chicago, Oregon, DC and others) publish their new rates in the spring, and are marked not yet published until then.

  • Made by Conyso
  • Exact, no AI
  • Official sources, dated
  • Files you upload are not kept
  • SecurityTerms

Use the API's changes operation to get the same list for your own window and states, and recheck before each January 1 and July 1.

Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.
