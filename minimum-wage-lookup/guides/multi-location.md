# Multi-Location Minimum Wage Audit: A Checklist

Put every work site's street address in one list with the payroll date and run it once: five stores in Seattle, Spokane, Brooklyn, Chicago and Austin get $22.14, $17.73, $17.00, $17.05, $7.25 on January 1, 2027.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.

## Steps

1. **Collect work addresses.** One street address per work site, where the work is performed (not the head office).
2. **Run one list.** Send them as one lookup with the payroll date and your employee count; $0.002 a place over the API.
3. **Compare with payroll.** Flag every pay rate below the place's minimum wage, and tipped cash wages below its tipped cash wage.
4. **Flag scheduled changes.** Each answer has the next change date; list them by date.
5. **Update posters.** Cities and states publish new notices with each change.
6. **Recheck each November and May.** Before the January 1 and July 1 changes.
