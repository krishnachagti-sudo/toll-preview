# District of Columbia Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The District of Columbia minimum wage is $18.40 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is no change (it adjusts on July 1). No city or county in District of Columbia sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $10.30 with a tip credit of up to $8.10 (DOL).
