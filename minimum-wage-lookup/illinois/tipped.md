# Illinois Tipped Minimum Wage 2026: Cash Wage and Tip Credit

In Illinois a tipped employee must get at least $9.00 an hour in cash; the maximum tip credit is $6.00 and tips must bring pay to $15.00 (DOL's tipped table updated November 5, 2026).

## The numbers

Illinois tipped employees (DOL, updated November 5, 2026)

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Full minimum wage | $15.00 |
| Maximum tip credit | $6.00 |
| Minimum cash wage | $9.00 |
| Who counts as tipped | Not specified |

DOL: Tip credit of 40% of the applicable minimum wage.

## A worked week

A server works 30 hours and reports $90 in tips. Cash wages at $9.00: $270.00. Wages plus tips: $360.00, against $450.00 at the full $15.00, so the employer adds $90.00.

> The hours and tips are made-up figures to show the arithmetic.
