# Cook County Minimum Wage 2026: $15.40

The Cook County minimum wage is $15.40 an hour from July 1, 2026 (tipped employees: $9.25 cash wage); it applies to work performed in Cook County (except in municipalities that opted out), and the Illinois rate is $15.00. Next: changes on July 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page.

Applies in unincorporated Cook County and in municipalities that have not opted out; a municipality with its own minimum wage (Chicago) follows its own. The county ordinance covers employees 18 and over.

We answer for dates up to June 30, 2027: Cook County adjusts its rate every July 1, and the rate after that was not published when we read its page on November 8, 2026.

## Where it applies

In Cook County (except in municipalities that opted out). A ZIP code can cross the boundary, so check the street address. Where the Illinois rate is higher on a date, the higher rate applies.

> Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.
