# Minnesota Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027 (Plus 2 Local Rates)

The Minnesota minimum wage is $11.41 an hour since January 1, 2026; on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $11.87. 2 cities and counties in Minnesota set their own higher rates we have read; Minnesota allows no tip credit: tipped employees get the full minimum wage before tips.

## Questions

### What is the minimum wage in Minnesota in 2026?

$11.41 an hour, per DOL's table updated November 5, 2026; some cities and counties set higher rates.

### What will the Minnesota minimum wage be in 2027?

$11.87 an hour from January 1, 2027 (Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry: Minimum wage in Minnesota).

### Does Minnesota allow a tip credit?

Minnesota allows no tip credit: tipped employees get the full minimum wage before tips.
