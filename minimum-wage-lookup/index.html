Example: Los Angeles, California, on November 8, 2026

$18.42an hour, Los Angeles, November 8, 2026

In Los Angeles, California on November 8, 2026 the minimum wage is $18.42 an hour, set by Los Angeles (since July 1, 2026). Tipped employees get the full rate: no tip credit. The rate changes on July 1, 2027, but the new rate is not covered yet. The state rate under it is $16.90 and the federal $7.25; in unincorporated Los Angeles County it is $18.47.

  1. USFederal (FLSA)$7.25Tipped cash $2.13. U.S. Department of Labor, read Nov 8, 2026
  2. CACalifornia$16.90No tip credit. U.S. Department of Labor table of Nov 5, 2026
  3. LALos Angeles, since July 1, 2026$18.42The highest rate applies. City of Los Angeles Office of Wage Standards, read Nov 8, 2026

Next change: July 1, 2027; the new rate was not published when we read the page. Los Angeles also sets a separate, higher Citywide Hotel Worker Minimum Wage; it is not the general rate shown here.

The rules

How the rate is worked out: the highest one wins.

Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed.

What it does not cover

Industry, hotel, health care, youth and training rates, places we could not read, and legal advice.

Read the full notes
  • Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ.
  • Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed.
  • Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.

The stack

Federal, then state, then any county and city ordinance; the highest rate in force on the date applies.

Read the full rule

Federal $7.25 (employers covered by the Fair Labor Standards Act), the state's rate for your employer size and region, then any county and city ordinance that covers the place. The highest rate in force on the date applies. Unincorporated county ordinances (Los Angeles, San Mateo, King, Boulder, Santa Fe) apply only outside cities.

The sources

The Department of Labor's state tables and each place's own page, read November 8, 2026.

Read the full rule

State rates: the U.S. Department of Labor's state and tipped tables (updated November 5, 2026) and each state's labor department page. City and county rates: each place's own page, read November 8, 2026. Some state and city sites refused our requests; they are listed as not covered on every answer that depends on them.

Prices

  • One place, or the change list, on this page: free.
  • Places (lists, REST and MCP): $0.002 per place answered for the first 10,000 each calendar month, then $0.001 per place answered; you pay only for what is delivered and the rest is refunded at once; places not found or not covered are not charged.
  • The list of changes in a date window over REST or MCP: $0.005 per list.
