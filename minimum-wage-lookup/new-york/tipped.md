# New York Tipped Minimum Wage 2026: Cash Wage and Tip Credit

In New York tipped minimum wages vary by region and type of employer (DOL); from January 1, 2027 the state labor department lists $14.15 cash for tipped service employees and $11.35 for tipped food service workers in New York City, Long Island and Westchester, and $13.30 and $10.70 in the rest of the state.
