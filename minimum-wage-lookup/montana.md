# Montana Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Montana minimum wage is $10.85 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $11.20. No city or county in Montana sets its own rate in the inventory we used; Montana allows no tip credit: tipped employees get the full minimum wage before tips.
