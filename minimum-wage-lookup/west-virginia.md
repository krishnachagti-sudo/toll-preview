# West Virginia Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The West Virginia minimum wage is $8.75 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is no change scheduled. No city or county in West Virginia sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $2.62 with a tip credit of up to $6.13 (DOL).
