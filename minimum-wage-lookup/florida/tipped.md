# Florida Tipped Minimum Wage 2026: Cash Wage and Tip Credit

In Florida a tipped employee must get at least $11.98 an hour in cash; the maximum tip credit is $3.02 and tips must bring pay to $15.00 (DOL's tipped table updated November 5, 2026).

## A worked week

A server works 30 hours and reports $90 in tips. Cash wages at $11.98: $359.40. Wages plus tips: $449.40, against $450.00 at the full $15.00, so the employer adds $0.60.
