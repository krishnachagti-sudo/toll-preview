# Washington Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027 (Plus 7 Local Rates)

The Washington minimum wage is $17.13 an hour since January 1, 2026; on January 1, 2027 there is a rise to $17.73. 7 cities and counties in Washington set their own higher rates we have read; Washington allows no tip credit: tipped employees get the full minimum wage before tips.

## Questions

### What is the minimum wage in Washington in 2026?

$17.13 an hour, per DOL's table updated November 5, 2026; some cities and counties set higher rates.

### What will the Washington minimum wage be in 2027?

$17.73 an hour from January 1, 2027 (Washington State Department of Labor and Industries: Minimum wage).

### Does Washington allow a tip credit?

Washington allows no tip credit: tipped employees get the full minimum wage before tips.
