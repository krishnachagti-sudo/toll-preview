Minimum Wage by State 2026 (DOL Table, Updated November 5)

The federal minimum wage is $7.25; in 2026 30 states and DC pay more, from $8.75 to $18.40: the highest state rate is Washington at $17.13 and DC is $18.40 (DOL table updated November 5, 2026).

New York and Oregon set regional rates: the table shows the standard (lowest-tier New York) rate; the state pages give each region. Where DOL lists several rates for employer size or receipts, the table shows the standard rate and the state page the others.

Not legal advice. The rate shown is the highest general minimum wage we hold for the place and date; industry, hotel, health care, youth, training and other special rates can differ. Every rate names the official page it was read from and the date we read it. Places and states whose pages we could not read are listed as not covered, never guessed. Employers covered by the federal Fair Labor Standards Act pay at least the federal $7.25; state laws differ on which employers they cover.

Questions

What is the federal minimum wage in 2026?

$7.25 an hour for employers covered by the Fair Labor Standards Act (DOL).

Which states have no minimum wage law?

Alabama, Louisiana, Mississippi, South Carolina and Tennessee; the federal $7.25 applies to covered work there (DOL).
