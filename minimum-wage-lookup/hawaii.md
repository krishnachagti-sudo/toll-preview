# Hawaii Minimum Wage 2026 and 2027

The Hawaii minimum wage is $16.00 an hour (DOL's table updated November 5, 2026); on January 1, 2027 there is a change we could not read from the state's own page. No city or county in Hawaii sets its own rate in the inventory we used; the tipped cash wage is $14.75 with a tip credit of up to $1.25 (DOL).
