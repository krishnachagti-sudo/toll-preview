# Microsoft 550 5.7.515

550 5.7.515 is a policy rejection: Microsoft's published text "does not meet the required authentication level" means: Outlook.com rejected mail from a domain sending over 5,000 messages a day that does not meet its SPF, DKIM and DMARC requirements; pass SPF and DKIM and publish DMARC (at least p=none) aligned with the From domain.

- Verdict: policy
