# Gmail 421 4.7.32

421 4.7.32 is a policy rejection: Gmail's published text "isn't aligned with either the authenticated SPF or DKIM" means: the From domain is not aligned with the domain that passed SPF or DKIM, so Gmail rate limits it; align the From domain with your SPF or DKIM domain.

- Verdict: policy
