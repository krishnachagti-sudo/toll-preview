# Gmail 550 5.7.40

550 5.7.40 is a policy rejection: Gmail's published text "sending domain doesn't have a DMARC record" means: your sending domain has no DMARC record or no DMARC policy; publish a DMARC record with a policy.

- Verdict: policy
