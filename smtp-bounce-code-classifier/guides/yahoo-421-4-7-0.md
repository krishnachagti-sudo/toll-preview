# Yahoo 421 4.7.0: temporarily deferred

Yahoo's 421 4.7.0 with a TS code is a block on the sender, temporary: Yahoo's Sender Hub ties TS deferrals to user complaints, spam-like content, poor IP reputation or unusual traffic, so retry later at a lower rate and keep the address; only "doesn't have a yahoo.com account" is hard.

## What Yahoo says

- A 421 or 451 is a temporary problem; you may retry later.
- A 553 or 554 is permanent; Yahoo says not to retry and that list managers should have a policy for removing addresses that generate 5xx bounces.
- TS errors: temporarily deferred. If it persists over an extended period, Yahoo asks for a Sender Support Request with the error and diagnostic codes.
- Complaint errors: if you have received one for more than 48 hours, Yahoo asks you to review your outgoing messages and practices.

## What to change

- Send less, more slowly, to Yahoo while the deferrals last.
- Find where complaints come from: list sources, frequency, unsubscribe that works.
- Authenticate: Yahoo asks all senders for SPF or DKIM, and bulk senders for SPF, DKIM and a DMARC policy.

> Yahoo's 5xx guidance says to remove addresses that bounce with 5xx; this classifier is stricter about which 5xx mean a bad address and keeps addresses for 5xx policy and content rejections.

## Questions

### Why is Yahoo deferring my mail?

Yahoo's Sender Hub lists, for TS errors: complaints from Yahoo users, objectionable or spam-like content, a poor reputation for your IP or its subnet, and unusual traffic from your servers.

### Should I remove Yahoo addresses that get 421 4.7.0?

No. It is classed block and temporary: the problem is the sender, and Yahoo says your server may retry later.
