# How a commission split works

Start with your side of the commission, take off any referral fee, apply your brokerage split until the cap, then any team share and per-deal fees: on a $400,000 sale at 3% with a 20% referral, a 70/30 split, a 25% team share and a $300 fee, $12,000.00 becomes $4,740.00.

## The deal, step by step

On a $400,000 sale at 3%, your side is $12,000.00; after the $2,400.00 referral fee, the $2,880.00 brokerage split, the team's $1,680.00 and $300.00 in fees, you net $4,740.00. $12,120.00 is left to your $20,000 cap. (Example plan: 70/30 split, $20,000 cap from March 15, 25% team share after the brokerage split, $300.00 transaction fee per deal; $5,000 already paid toward the cap.)

| Step | How | Amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Your side of the commission | $400,000.00 x 3% | $12,000.00 |
| Referral fee paid out | 20% of your side | -$2,400.00 |
| Brokerage split | 30% of $9,600.00 | -$2,880.00 |
| Team's share (after the brokerage split) | 25% of $6,720.00 | -$1,680.00 |
| Transaction fee | every deal | -$300.00 |
| You net |  | $4,740.00 |

## Steps

1. **Your side.** Start from the commission on your side of the deal: the price times the rate in your agreement, or the amount the agreement gives.
2. **Referral fee.** Take off any referral fee you owe another agent; plans usually work the split out after it.
3. **Royalty or franchise fee.** If your brokerage charges one, take it off the top, or out of your share, as your plan says, until its own cap.
4. **Brokerage split.** Apply your split to what is left until the brokerage has received your cap for the cap year; the deal that crosses the cap pays part at each rate.
5. **Team share.** If you are on a team, the team takes its share, before or after the brokerage split as your team agreement says.
6. **Per-deal fees.** Take off transaction and other per-deal fees, which may continue after the cap.
