A commission-only year: checklist

For 2026 the last estimated tax payment is due January 15, 2027 (Form 1040-ES); set aside from what reaches you, which in the example year was $42,022.50 from $64,550.00 of commission.

2026 estimated tax dates

You don't have to make the payment due January 15, 2027, if you file your 2026 tax return by February 1, 2027, and pay the entire balance due with your return.

Sources

  • IRS, Form 1040-ES (2026), Payment Due Dates (read November 8, 2026)irs.gov

Steps

  1. Know your net per deal. Work out what reaches you after the referral fee, split, team share and fees; the tracker does it deal by deal.
  2. Set aside from the net. Put your set-aside on what reached you, not on the gross commission.
  3. Pay estimated tax on the dates. Form 1040-ES (2026) gives April 15, 2026, June 15, 2026, September 15, 2026, January 15, 2027.
  4. Keep the records. Commission statements, fees charged to you, mileage and business costs.
  5. Check the 1099 in January. Compare the brokerage's 1099-NEC with your tracker's totals for the calendar year.
