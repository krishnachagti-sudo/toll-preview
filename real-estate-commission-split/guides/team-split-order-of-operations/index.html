Team splits: who is paid first

On a $9,000 commission with a 50% team share and an 80/20 split you net $3,600.00 either way; across twelve such deals with a $16,000 cap you net $43,200.00 if the team takes its share first and $46,000.00 if the brokerage does, because team first the brokerage only takes from your half and your cap fills more slowly.

Put these in the team agreement

  • Which is taken first, the team's share or the brokerage split.
  • Whose cap the brokerage split counts toward, yours or the team lead's.
  • Whether the team has a cap, and when it resets.
  • Who pays the per-deal fees.

Arithmetic on the plan shown, rounded to the cent at each step. Your brokerage's commission statement and your agreements decide what you are paid. The splits, caps, fees and rates on this page are worked examples, not typical or recommended figures. Not tax or legal advice.

One deal, a comparison or a goal is free on the calculator's page and $0.002 a call over the API or MCP; the year tracker (every deal in date order, cap progress, the 1099 total and a CSV) is $3.99 per agent cap year, free to re-run for 12 months.
