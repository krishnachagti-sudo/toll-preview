# Why your 1099 can be higher than you took home

A 1099-NEC reports what the brokerage counts as paid to you, which can be your share before the fees it charged you: in the example year the agent took home $42,022.50 in 2026 but their share before the brokerage's transaction fees was $43,522.50; the form for 2026 is due by February 1, 2027.

## What to check

- Which total your brokerage reports: the commission statements show whether fees were deducted from your share before payment.
- Fees the brokerage charged you are business expenses you track yourself; ask your tax adviser how to claim them.
- The threshold: Form 1099-NEC is for nonemployee compensation of $2,000 or more in 2026 ("Enter nonemployee compensation (NEC) of $2,000 or more. Include fees, commissions, prizes and awards for services performed as a nonemployee").
- The date: "Section 6071(c) requires you to file Form 1099-NEC on or before January 31", and "If any date shown falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday in the District of Columbia or where the return is to be filed, the due date is the next business day." January 31, 2027 is a Sunday, so February 1, 2027.
