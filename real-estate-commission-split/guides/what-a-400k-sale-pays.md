# What a $400,000 sale pays you

A $400,000 sale at 3% puts $12,000.00 on your side; on a 70/30 plan with a $20,000 cap and a $300 transaction fee you net $8,100.00 before the cap and $11,700.00 once capped, or $6,000.00 if you also owe a 25% referral fee.

## Before the cap

On a $400,000 sale at 3%, your side is $12,000.00; after the $3,600.00 brokerage split and $300.00 in fees, you net $8,100.00. $16,400.00 is left to your $20,000 cap.

| Step | How | Amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Your side of the commission | $400,000.00 x 3% | $12,000.00 |
| Brokerage split | 30% of $12,000.00 | -$3,600.00 |
| Transaction fee | every deal | -$300.00 |
| You net |  | $8,100.00 |

## After the cap

On a $400,000 sale at 3%, your side is $12,000.00; after the $0.00 brokerage split and $300.00 in fees, you net $11,700.00. You were already capped.

| Step | How | Amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Your side of the commission | $400,000.00 x 3% | $12,000.00 |
| Brokerage split | capped: 0% after the cap | $0.00 |
| Transaction fee | every deal | -$300.00 |
| You net |  | $11,700.00 |

## With a 25% referral fee

On a $400,000 sale at 3%, your side is $12,000.00; after the $3,000.00 referral fee, the $2,700.00 brokerage split and $300.00 in fees, you net $6,000.00. $17,300.00 is left to your $20,000 cap.

| Step | How | Amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Your side of the commission | $400,000.00 x 3% | $12,000.00 |
| Referral fee paid out | 25% of your side | -$3,000.00 |
| Brokerage split | 30% of $9,000.00 | -$2,700.00 |
| Transaction fee | every deal | -$300.00 |
| You net |  | $6,000.00 |

## What the 1099 shows

Before the cap, the brokerage pays you $8,100.00; your share before the $300 fee it charges you is $8,400.00. Which of the two your brokerage reports is on your statement; see [why your 1099 can be higher](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/real-estate-commission-split/guides/why-your-1099-is-higher/).

## Three questions for your broker

- Is the referral fee taken before the split, and does it count toward my cap?
- Which per-deal fees continue after I cap?
- Does my cap reset on my anniversary or on January 1?
