## What to weigh

- A cap you never reach is worth nothing: below it you pay the full split on every deal.
- A monthly fee is paid in months with no closings too.
- Ask for the plan in writing: split, cap, reset date, per-deal fees, monthly fees.

## Buyer-side pay since August 17, 2024

- The practice changes from NAR's settlement went into effect on August 17, 2024.
- Agents who work with buyers through an MLS must enter into a written agreement with the buyer before touring a home.
- That agreement must disclose the amount or rate of compensation the agent will receive, or how it will be determined, and it must be objective, not open-ended or a range.
- Offers of compensation to buyer brokers are no longer allowed on MLS platforms; sellers can still offer compensation off an MLS.
- NAR's consumer guide says compensation between a buyer and their agent is negotiable and not set by law.
