# GCI calculator

To net $100,000.00 on a 70/30 split, $20,000 cap per calendar year, $300.00 transaction fee per deal, you need 14 deals of $9,000.00: $126,000.00 in GCI, netting $101,800.00 (you cap along the way). Deals counted at $9,000 of commission each.

## GCI and deals for a net goal

Deals of $9,000 of commission on your side, in one cap year; the plans are examples. GCI is the smallest whole number of such deals that nets the goal.

| Net goal | 60/40, no cap | 70/30, $20,000 cap, $300 fee | 85/15, $12,000 cap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $50,000 | $90,000.00 (10 deals) | $81,000.00 (9 deals) | $63,000.00 (7 deals) |
| $75,000 | $126,000.00 (14 deals) | $99,000.00 (11 deals) | $90,000.00 (10 deals) |
| $100,000 | $171,000.00 (19 deals) | $126,000.00 (14 deals) | $117,000.00 (13 deals) |
| $150,000 | $252,000.00 (28 deals) | $180,000.00 (20 deals) | $162,000.00 (18 deals) |
| $200,000 | $342,000.00 (38 deals) | $234,000.00 (26 deals) | $216,000.00 (24 deals) |

The goal is what reaches you after the brokerage split, the cap and the fees, before your own business costs and taxes.

> Arithmetic on the plan shown, rounded to the cent at each step. Your brokerage's commission statement and your agreements decide what you are paid. The splits, caps, fees and rates on this page are worked examples, not typical or recommended figures. Not tax or legal advice.
