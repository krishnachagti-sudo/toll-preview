# Referral fee calculator

A 25% referral on a $500,000 sale at 3% is $3,750.00 (3% x price x 25%), paid from the receiving agent's side before the brokerage split; on a 70/30 split the receiving agent then nets $7,875.00.

## The deal, step by step

On a $500,000 sale at 3%, your side is $15,000.00; after the $3,750.00 referral fee and the $3,375.00 brokerage split, you net $7,875.00.

| Step | How | Amount |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Your side of the commission | $500,000.00 x 3% | $15,000.00 |
| Referral fee paid out | 25% of your side | -$3,750.00 |
| Brokerage split | 30% of $11,250.00 | -$3,375.00 |
| You net |  | $7,875.00 |

Whether the referral fee is taken before your brokerage split, and whether it counts toward your cap, is in your plan. Several plans take it off the top; one franchise office's policy manual, for example, says its splits are calculated after the franchise fee and referral fees. The rules on who may be paid are on [the referral fee rules page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/real-estate-commission-split/referral-fee/rules/).
