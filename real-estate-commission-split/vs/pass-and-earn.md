# Pass and Earn compared

Pass and Earn's free calculator "focuses on per-transaction splits", and after the cap you set the split to 100% by hand (its page, read November 8, 2026); the deal that crosses the cap needs both rates at once: with $18,000 paid toward a $20,000 cap at 70/30, a $10,000 commission nets $7,700.00 after a $300 fee.

## What Pass and Earn offers (read November 8, 2026)

- A free per-transaction split calculator with a field for recurring fees (desk, technology, E&O and franchise fees).
- Its page says: "This calculator focuses on per-transaction splits, but you can set the split to 100% for deals closed after reaching your cap."
- It defines a cap as "a maximum dollar amount your broker collects per year".

## What this calculator adds

- Your own plan, in the order it takes its cuts: referral fee, royalty (with its cap), team share before or after the brokerage split (with a team cap), the split up to the cap, per-deal fees before, after or always.
- A year of deals in date order across anniversary caps, with the crossing deal split at both rates, deals left to cap, and the 1099 totals by calendar year, with a CSV.
- Plans compared at your deal count, and the deals needed for a net goal.
- One deal, a comparison or a goal is free on the calculator's page and $0.002 a call over the API or MCP; the year tracker (every deal in date order, cap progress, the 1099 total and a CSV) is $3.99 per agent cap year, free to re-run for 12 months.

## When Pass and Earn is the better choice

When you are still choosing a brokerage and want its reviews and brokerage pages alongside a quick per-deal split.
