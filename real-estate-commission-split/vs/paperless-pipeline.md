# Paperless Pipeline compared

Paperless Pipeline is brokerage software: plans start at $69 a month and its Commission Module (splits, caps, fees, agent net statements and CDAs inside each transaction) adds $58 to $185 a month (pricing page, read November 8, 2026); an agent checking their own split, cap and 1099 total here pays $3.99 per cap year for the tracker, and single deals are free on the page.

## What Paperless Pipeline offers (read November 8, 2026)

- Brokerage plans start at $69/month for up to 5 new transactions a month, priced by new transactions, not by agents.
- The Commission Module ("Splits, caps, fees, agent net statements and auto-generated CDAs, inside each transaction") is $58/month on the Up to 5 and Up to 10 plans, $64 on Up to 25 and Up to 40, $119 on Up to 80, $125 on Up to 150 and Up to 250, and $185 on Up to 350 and 450+.
- Up to 5 plan with the Commission Module: $127/month.
- Its blog has a free team split calculator, and says the brokerage cut is always applied to the gross, not the agent's share, in the team model it describes.

## What this calculator adds

- Your own plan, in the order it takes its cuts: referral fee, royalty (with its cap), team share before or after the brokerage split (with a team cap), the split up to the cap, per-deal fees before, after or always.
- A year of deals in date order across anniversary caps, with the crossing deal split at both rates, deals left to cap, and the 1099 totals by calendar year, with a CSV.
- Plans compared at your deal count, and the deals needed for a net goal.
- One deal, a comparison or a goal is free on the calculator's page and $0.002 a call over the API or MCP; the year tracker (every deal in date order, cap progress, the 1099 total and a CSV) is $3.99 per agent cap year, free to re-run for 12 months.

## When Paperless Pipeline is the better choice

When your brokerage runs its deals in Paperless Pipeline: its Commission Module produces the statements and CDAs you are actually paid by. This calculator is for checking and planning those numbers yourself.

> Arithmetic on the plan shown, rounded to the cent at each step. Your brokerage's commission statement and your agreements decide what you are paid. The splits, caps, fees and rates on this page are worked examples, not typical or recommended figures. Not tax or legal advice.
