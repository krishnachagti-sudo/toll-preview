# Real Brokerage commission split and cap

Under Real Brokerage's plan as published on joinreal.com (read November 8, 2026), 85/15 with a $12,000 cap, $100,000 of commission in a cap year nets you $88,000.00 before Real's fixed transaction fee after capping, whose amount that page does not state; enter yours to include it.

## What Real's page says

- "Real's standard plan guarantees an 85/15 commission split, allowing you to keep 100% of your earnings after capping"
- "After capping, agents keep 100% of their commission and pay a fixed transaction fee."
- "DIFFERENT CAPS BASED ON YOUR BUSINESS MODEL $12K USD / $15K CAD $6K USD / $7.5K CAD $4K USD / $5K CAD Subject to certain requirements."

Not on that page: the amount of the fixed transaction fee after capping; which business model gets which cap; the anniversary date (yours is in your agreement). The calculator takes them from your agreement.
