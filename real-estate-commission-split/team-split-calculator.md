# Team split calculator

On a $15,000 commission with a 50% team share and an 80/20 brokerage split you net $6,000.00 whichever is taken first; the order matters once there is a cap: over eight such deals with a $16,000 cap you net $48,000.00 if the team takes its share first and $52,000.00 if the brokerage does.

## One $15,000 deal, both orders

With percentages only, the order gives the same net: 50% then 20% of what is left is the same as 20% then 50% of what is left. Who receives what differs: team first, the brokerage takes its 20% of your half; brokerage first, it takes 20% of the whole commission and the team takes half of the rest.

Team first: Your side is $15,000.00; after the team's $7,500.00 and the $1,500.00 brokerage split, you net $6,000.00.

## Where the order changes your net

- A cap: team first, the brokerage's share is taken from your part only, so your cap fills more slowly. Eight $15,000 deals with a $16,000 cap: $12,000.00 paid toward the cap (net $48,000.00) team first, $16,000.00 (net $52,000.00) brokerage first.
- A flat per-deal fee comes off your share after both cuts, so on its own it changes nothing between the orders: with a $400 fee you net $5,600.00 team first and $5,600.00 brokerage first.
- A team cap: once the team has taken its cap in the year, it takes nothing more until the reset.
