## When you reach a cap

A cap is the most you pay the brokerage in a cap year. At 30% the brokerage's share reaches a cap after the cap divided by 30% of commission; after that your split rises (often to 100%, but your plan says).

The caps are examples, not typical figures. Deals counted at $9,000 each.

## Questions

### What is a 70/30 commission split?

You keep 70% and the brokerage 30% of the commission on your side of the deal; on $9,000 that is $6,300.00 and $2,700.00.

### With a 70/30 split and a $20,000 cap, when do I cap?

When the brokerage's 30% has added up to $20,000 in your cap year: after $66,666.67 of commission on your side, about 8 deals of $9,000.
