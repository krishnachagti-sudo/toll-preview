What the tracker does with the next deals

  • The deal that crosses the cap is split at both rates: the brokerage takes 30% of the part that fills the cap and the post-cap rate on the rest (how a commission cap works).
  • On your anniversary the cap paid goes back to zero; a deal on the anniversary itself starts the new cap year. A February 29 anniversary falls on February 28 in common years.
  • Pending deals can be added; they count as if they close on their date and stay out of the 1099 totals.
  • Whether a fee or a royalty counts toward the cap is a setting, because plans differ.

Arithmetic on the plan shown, rounded to the cent at each step. Your brokerage's commission statement and your agreements decide what you are paid. The splits, caps, fees and rates on this page are worked examples, not typical or recommended figures. Not tax or legal advice.

One deal, a comparison or a goal is free on the calculator's page and $0.002 a call over the API or MCP; the year tracker (every deal in date order, cap progress, the 1099 total and a CSV) is $3.99 per agent cap year, free to re-run for 12 months.

Questions

What does "capped" mean for a real estate agent?

You have paid the brokerage the most your plan asks for in a cap year (the cap), so its split stops (or drops to the post-cap rate) until your anniversary resets it. Per-deal fees may still apply after the cap; your plan says.
