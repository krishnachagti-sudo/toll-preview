Agent money dates, November 2026 to December 2027

Between November 2026 and December 2027 the dated deadlines are January 15, 2027 (the 4th estimated tax payment for 2026), February 1, 2027 (Form 1099-NEC for 2026 payments, since January 31 is a Sunday), and the third anniversary of the NAR practice changes on August 17, 2027; your own cap resets on your anniversary.
