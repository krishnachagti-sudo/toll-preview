When you reach a cap

A cap is the most you pay the brokerage in a cap year. At 20% the brokerage's share reaches a cap after the cap divided by 20% of commission; after that your split rises (often to 100%, but your plan says).

Arithmetic on the plan shown, rounded to the cent at each step. Your brokerage's commission statement and your agreements decide what you are paid. The splits, caps, fees and rates on this page are worked examples, not typical or recommended figures. Not tax or legal advice.

One deal, a comparison or a goal is free on the calculator's page and $0.002 a call over the API or MCP; the year tracker (every deal in date order, cap progress, the 1099 total and a CSV) is $3.99 per agent cap year, free to re-run for 12 months.

Questions

What is an 80/20 commission split?

You keep 80% and the brokerage 20% of the commission on your side of the deal; on $9,000 that is $7,200.00 and $1,800.00.

With a 80/20 split and a $20,000 cap, when do I cap?

When the brokerage's 20% has added up to $20,000 in your cap year: after $100,000.00 of commission on your side, about 12 deals of $9,000.
