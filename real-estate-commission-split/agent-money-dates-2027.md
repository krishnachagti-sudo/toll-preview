# Agent money dates, November 2026 to December 2027

Between November 2026 and December 2027 the dated deadlines are January 15, 2027 (the 4th estimated tax payment for 2026), February 1, 2027 (Form 1099-NEC for 2026 payments, since January 31 is a Sunday), and the third anniversary of the NAR practice changes on August 17, 2027; your own cap resets on your anniversary.

Read November 8, 2026. The 2027 estimated tax dates are not listed until the IRS publishes the 2027 Form 1040-ES.

| Date | Day | What | Source | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| January 15, 2027 | Friday | 4th estimated tax payment for 2026 | IRS Form 1040-ES (2026) | Not needed if you file your 2026 return by February 1, 2027 and pay the balance. |
| February 1, 2027 | Monday | Form 1099-NEC for 2026 payments due to you (and to the IRS) | IRS Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC | January 31, 2027 is a Sunday, so the next business day. |
| August 17, 2027 | Tuesday | Three years since the NAR settlement practice changes took effect (August 17, 2024) | nar.realtor | Written buyer agreements with objective compensation; no offers of compensation on MLSs. |

Your cap year starts again on your anniversary: put it in the cap tracker and it splits your deals into cap years for you.

> Arithmetic on the plan shown, rounded to the cent at each step. Your brokerage's commission statement and your agreements decide what you are paid. The splits, caps, fees and rates on this page are worked examples, not typical or recommended figures. Not tax or legal advice.
