Stripe report

The Stripe payout reconciliation report, explained

Stripe's itemized payout reconciliation report (payout_reconciliation.itemized.7) has 11 default columns: gross is the transaction, fee is Stripe's fee, net is what reached the payout, and automatic_payout_id ties each row to its payout. In the sample below, 2 payouts come to $396.26.

The default columns

ColumnWhat it holds
automatic_payout_effective_atThe date Stripe expects the payout to arrive; also when it leaves your Stripe balance.
automatic_payout_idThe automatic payout this row belongs to. Rows with the same id add up to that payout.
available_onWhen the row's net became available in your Stripe balance.
balance_transaction_idThe row's own id.
createdWhen the balance transaction was created.
currencyThe currency of gross, fee and net.
descriptionFree text, often the order or the fee's name.
feeFees for this row, in dollars (major units). Any withheld_tax is already inside it.
grossThe row's gross amount: a charge's full price, a refund as a negative.
netGross less fee: what the row added to or took from the payout.
reporting_categoryWhat the row is: charge, refund, dispute, fee and so on. The split places rows by it.

Two worth knowing: Summing the two balance transactions yields the captured portion of the charge. So a partial_capture_reversal reduces the sale rather than counting as a refund. And tax is "Taxes collected by Stripe to be remitted to the appropriate local governments. Typically, this is a tax on Stripe fees."

Before you download

  • The payout reconciliation report is only available for users with automatic payouts enabled, or a platform on manual payouts whose Connected Accounts have automatic payouts enabled.
  • You control the timing and amount of instant payouts, so Stripe can't identify which transactions are included in each payout.
  • The report groups a payout by the estimated arrival date shown on the Payouts page, not the date your bank posts the deposit.
  • Expressed in major units of the currency (e.g. dollars for USD, yen for JPY).

Fees

Stripe's US prices: 2.9% + 30¢ per successful transaction for domestic cards, + 1.5% for international cards, + 1% if currency conversion is required; $15.00 for each dispute you receive; Instant Payouts 1.5% of Instant Payouts volume. The payment processing, Connect and currency conversion fees from the original transaction are not returned.

Questions

Which Stripe report lists every transaction in a payout?

The Payout reconciliation report. Download the Itemized CSV to review every payment, refund, dispute, fee and other balance transaction included in the payout.

Can I reconcile Stripe instant payouts with this report?

No. You control the timing and amount of instant payouts, so Stripe can't identify which transactions are included in each payout. You reconcile those against your transaction history yourself.

What date does Stripe use to group a payout in the report?

The report groups a payout by the estimated arrival date shown on the Payouts page, not the date your bank posts the deposit.

What is the withheld_tax column?

withheld_tax: Sales tax, VAT or GST withheld. This amount is already included within the fee column.
