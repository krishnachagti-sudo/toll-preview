The PayPal Activity Download, explained

PayPal's Activity Download gives each row a Gross (fee included), a Fee and a Net, and its Balance Impact says Debit, Credit or Memo. In the sample month below, 6 balance rows net to $133.05 before $150.00 of withdrawals, and the Balance column runs from $74.73 to $57.78.

The columns the split reads

ColumnWhat it holds
DateFor US accounts, it uses this format: MM/DD/YYYY.
TypeType: The transaction event code T-code description.
StatusCompleted, Pending, Reversed and others.
CurrencyThe ISO currency code of the row.
GrossGross: The localized gross amount of the transaction, including fees.
FeeThe fee on the row; most files write it as a negative amount.
NetNet: The localized net amount of the transaction (typically the gross minus any fees)
Transaction IDPayPal's id for the row.
Reference Txn IDThe parent transaction: a refund points to its sale.
BalanceBalance: The running available balance in the merchant's account in the transaction currency. The balance is the previous balance plus the net amount of the current activity.
Sales TaxSales Tax: The localized sales tax amount paid as part of the transaction, as reported by PayPal or the merchant
Balance ImpactBalance Impact: The impact of the transaction on the account balance: Debit, Credit, or Memo.

Type (T-code)Booked as

Reserve hold (T2103)Reserves and holds
General currency conversion (T0200)Currency conversion
General withdrawal, bank account (T0400)Withdrawal to the bank

A sample month

The Memo row (an authorization) is left out because it does not move the balance. The fee check: PayPal Checkout is PayPal Checkout: 3.49% + fixed fee, with Fixed fee for commercial transactions, US dollar: 0.49 USD; on these 3 sales it gives $7.95, against $7.95 charged.

What does Balance Impact mean in the PayPal download?

Is the PayPal fee included in Gross?

Gross: The localized gross amount of the transaction, including fees.
