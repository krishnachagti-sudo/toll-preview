An A2X alternative, priced per payout

A2X for Shopify starts at US$29 a month (Mini, up to 200 orders); here a month of 22 payouts costs $2.20, a single payout $0.50, and no month costs more than $9.00. A2X posts to QuickBooks Online or Xero for you every day; this splitter turns the file you upload into the journal you import.

When A2X is the better choice

  • You want postings made for you every day, with no upload.
  • You need cost of goods sold or the Shopify gift card report A2X includes.
  • Your accountant already works in A2X.
  • You sell on marketplaces this splitter does not read (Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Walmart).

When this is enough

  • You close the books once a month and want the payouts split and proved in one go.
  • You use more than one processor (Stripe, Shopify Payments, PayPal, Square) and want one place for all.
  • You do not want to connect your store or ledger to another app.
