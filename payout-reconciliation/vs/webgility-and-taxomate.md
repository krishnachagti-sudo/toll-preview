# Webgility and Taxomate vs a one-off split

Webgility starts at $69 a month billed annually ($79 monthly) for 300 orders, and Taxomate at $16 a month billed yearly ($19 monthly) for 200 orders on one channel; here 22 payouts in a month cost $2.20. Both sync orders continuously; Webgility also syncs inventory.

## What Webgility and Taxomate does

- Webgility: Ecommerce Automation: $69 a month billed annually, $79 billed monthly, 300 orders a month, 2 sales channels; B2B & Multi-channel: $129 billed annually, $149 monthly, 800 orders a month; Complete ecommerce reconciliation: $299 billed annually, $349 monthly, 1,500 orders a month. Additional orders beyond your plan's limit can be purchased at $10/mo per 500 orders.
- Taxomate: Starter, up to 200 orders: $16 per month billed yearly, or $19 monthly; 1 sales channel; Growth, up to 200 orders: $24 per month billed yearly, or $29 monthly; all 6 sales channels. Books: QuickBooks Online, Xero or Wave. 14-day free trial, card required.

## What the splitter does

- Reads the processor's own export (Stripe, Shopify Payments, PayPal, Square, or any CSV with its columns named) and splits every payout into sales, refunds, chargebacks, fees and adjustments.
- Proves the rows against the deposit and shows any gap as its own line.
- Builds a balanced journal through a clearing account, as a QuickBooks Online import file or Xero manual journal lines.
- The split is free in the browser; the journal is $0.10 a payout, at least $0.50 a file and at most $9 a month. Nothing is connected and nothing is kept.

## When Webgility and Taxomate is the better choice

- You need orders, refunds and inventory kept in sync across channels (Webgility).
- You sell on Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart or TikTok Shop and want each sale posted (Taxomate).
- You want posting to happen with no upload.

## When the splitter is enough

- You only need each payout split and booked, once a month.
