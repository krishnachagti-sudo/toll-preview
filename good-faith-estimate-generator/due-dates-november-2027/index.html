Good Faith Estimate due dates, November 2027

For a self-pay service scheduled on Monday, November 1, 2027, a Good Faith Estimate is needed for any service on or after November 4, 2027 and is due by 11:59 PM on November 2, 2027, or on November 4, 2027 for a service on or after November 16, 2027. In November 2027, Veterans Day (November 11, 2027) and Thanksgiving Day (November 25, 2027) are skipped.

Questions

If a self-pay service is scheduled on Monday, November 1, 2027 for 5 business days later, when is the estimate due?

By 11:59 PM on Tuesday, November 2, 2027: 1 business day after scheduling (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vi)(A)).
