## Every required element

- (c)(1)(i): Patient name and date of birth given.
- (c)(1)(ii): Primary service described, 26 dates from December 1, 2026 to May 25, 2027.
- (c)(1)(iii): 2 items, grouped by 1 provider.
- (c)(1)(iv): No diagnosis code: fine where none is required for the estimate, such as a first evaluation (CMS GFE FAQs Part 2 Q1).
- (c)(1)(v): Name, NPI (check digit valid), TIN and location for the provider.
- (c)(1)(vi): No separately scheduled items listed.
- (c)(1)(viii): Added in the CMS model notice's words (there may be additional items or services, scheduled or requested separately, not reflected in the estimate).
- (c)(1)(ix): Added in the CMS model notice's words (it is only an estimate, and actual items, services or charges may differ).

## Questions

### Do audiologists and speech-language pathologists give Good Faith Estimates?

Yes, when they are licensed providers seeing uninsured or self-pay patients: the rule covers a physician or other provider acting within the scope of a state license or certification (149.610(a)(2)(viii)).

### When is the estimate due for sessions scheduled 10 or more business days ahead?

Within 3 business days of scheduling (149.610(b)(1)(vi)(B)). The speech sessions here start December 1, 2026, 10 business days after scheduling, so the estimate is due November 19, 2026.
