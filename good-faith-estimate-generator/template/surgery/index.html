The filled example

Made-up practice and patient. The estimate is dated Tuesday, December 1, 2026, the day the service was scheduled; Scheduled 28 business days ahead (10 or more): due by 11:59 PM on Friday, December 4, 2026, 3 business days after scheduling.

Total: $9,100.00. Dispute line: a bill from any one of them at least $400 above its own estimate ($3,600.00 for Sample Orthopedics PC (made up); $5,200.00 for Sample Surgery Center LLC (made up); $1,500.00 for Sample Anesthesia Group PC (made up)) lets the patient start dispute resolution within 120 calendar days of the bill (CMS model notice).

The codes and rates are examples, not advice on what to bill: use your own codes, your own words for the description and your own self-pay rates, discounts included (149.610(a)(2)(v)).

Separately scheduled care

Physical therapy after surgery is listed under the required disclaimer (149.610(c)(1)(vi)): its estimate comes from the therapist when it is scheduled. CMS's consumer guide suggests a patient ask both the surgeon and the hospital for estimates. Due date: Scheduled 28 business days ahead (10 or more): due by 11:59 PM on Friday, December 4, 2026, 3 business days after scheduling.

Every required element

  • (c)(1)(i): Patient name and date of birth given.
  • (c)(1)(ii): Primary service described, scheduled for January 12, 2027.
  • (c)(1)(iii): 3 items, grouped by 3 providers or facilities.
  • (c)(1)(iv): Service codes and expected charges on every item.
  • (c)(1)(v): Name, NPI (check digit valid), TIN and location for all 3.
  • (c)(1)(vi): 1 separately scheduled item, with the required disclaimer printed directly above the list.
  • (c)(1)(viii): Added in the CMS model notice's words (there may be additional items or services, scheduled or requested separately, not reflected in the estimate).
  • (c)(1)(ix): Added in the CMS model notice's words (it is only an estimate, and actual items, services or charges may differ).
  • (c)(1)(x): Added in the CMS model notice's words (the right to start patient-provider dispute resolution, where to find how, and that starting it will not adversely affect the quality of care).
  • (c)(1)(xi): Added in the CMS model notice's words (the estimate is not a contract and does not require the patient to get the items or services from these providers).

Questions

Must a surgeon's self-pay estimate include the hospital and the anesthesiologist?

The rule says it should, through co-provider estimates (149.610(b)(1)(v), (c)(1)(iii)), but HHS is extending enforcement discretion, pending future rulemaking, where a GFE does not include co-provider or co-facility charges (CMS GFE FAQs Part 3, December 2, 2022).

Is the $400 dispute line for the whole surgery?

No, per provider or facility: a bill at least $400 more than that provider's or facility's own estimate (CMS sample notice of the right to a GFE).
