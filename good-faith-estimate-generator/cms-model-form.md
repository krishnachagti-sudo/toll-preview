# The CMS model Good Faith Estimate, filled online

The CMS model Good Faith Estimate has 6 sections, from the patient block to the disclaimer page, plus an abbreviated no-cost version; CMS estimates 1.3 hours to complete one by hand. This generator fills the same sections in the same order: the weekly therapy example comes to $8,000.00.

- Sections: 6

- OMB number: 0938-1433

## The six sections

1. Patient (name, date of birth, contact and contact preference)
2. Patient diagnosis and the primary service or item, with its date(s) or a box if not scheduled
3. Date of the estimate and a summary of expected charges by provider, with the total
4. One estimate per provider or facility: name, type, address, contact, NPI, TIN and the itemized services with address, diagnosis code, service code, quantity and expected cost
5. Items expected to be scheduled separately, under a disclaimer
6. The disclaimer page: estimate only, not a contract, the right to dispute ($400, 120 days, $25 fee) and where to get help

## What the generator adds to the model

- The due date, in business days, from the scheduling or request date.
- Recurring sessions turned into dates, counted and totaled, refused past 12 months.
- Totals per provider and overall, worked out from quantity and rate.
- Two sentences the regulation requires that the standard model's disclaimer page does not have: the additional-items disclaimer (149.610(c)(1)(viii)) and that starting a dispute will not adversely affect the quality of care (149.610(c)(1)(x)). Both are taken from the abbreviated model form.
- An element-by-element check, including the NPI check digit.

## The abbreviated no-cost form

For a provider that does not expect to bill the patient and has no co-providers: patient, provider, NPI, TIN, address, the date and a shorter disclaimer (CMS GFE FAQs Part 4 Q2). Tick "will not bill" in the generator to make one.

## When the blank CMS form is the better choice

If you write one or two estimates a year and are happy to fill a PDF by hand, the CMS form is free and is the reference itself. The generator saves time when there are recurring sessions, several providers or a deadline to work out.

## Questions

### Do I have to use the CMS Good Faith Estimate template?

No. CMS says use of the model notice is not required; HHS considers its use good-faith compliance, and an estimate that meets all of 45 CFR 149.610 is needed for the patient to start dispute resolution.

### Is the CMS form out of date?

The copy read (captured October 20, 2026) shows OMB control number 0938-1433 with an expiration date of 11/2025. Its content still follows the regulation; check cms.gov for a renewed copy.
