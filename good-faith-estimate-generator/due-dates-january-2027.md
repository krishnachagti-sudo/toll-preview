# Good Faith Estimate due dates, January 2027

For a self-pay service scheduled on Monday, January 4, 2027, a Good Faith Estimate is needed for any service on or after January 7, 2027 and is due by 11:59 PM on January 5, 2027, or on January 7, 2027 for a service on or after January 19, 2027. In January 2027, New Year's Day (January 1, 2027) and Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. (January 18, 2027) are skipped.

- Due, scheduled January 4, 2027: January 5, 2027

Each day of January 2027. "Needed from": a service on or after this date (3 business days ahead) needs an estimate on scheduling.

## Questions

### If a self-pay service is scheduled on Monday, January 4, 2027 for 5 business days later, when is the estimate due?

By 11:59 PM on Tuesday, January 5, 2027: 1 business day after scheduling (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vi)(A)).
