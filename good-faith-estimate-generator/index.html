What the regulation asks for

The estimate is due within 1 business day when the service is scheduled 3 to 9 business days ahead, within 3 when it is 10 or more ahead or the patient asked, and it must carry ten elements, from the patient and the service to each provider's charges and four disclaimers (45 CFR 149.610).

What it does not cover

  • Insured patients' estimates and Advanced EOBs (enforcement deferred pending rulemaking).
  • State rules other than Colorado's.
  • Whether your codes, descriptions and rates are the right ones: only their format is checked.
Read the timing, the elements and the 12-month rule

When

Scheduled 3 to 9 business days ahead: within 1 business day. Scheduled 10 or more ahead: within 3. Asked for: within 3. Fewer than 3 business days ahead: none is required. Business days are Monday to Friday without federal holidays, and the estimate is due by 11:59 PM (CMS GFE FAQs Part 5).

What

Ten elements in 45 CFR 149.610(c)(1), paragraph (vii) being reserved: the patient, the primary service and date, the items grouped by provider with codes and charges, each provider's NPI, TIN and location, the separately scheduled items, and four disclaimers.

How long

One estimate may cover recurring sessions for up to 12 months; a change of scope (charges, frequency, providers) needs a new one at least 1 business day before the service. Copies of estimates from the last 6 years must be given on request.
