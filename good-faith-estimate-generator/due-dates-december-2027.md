# Good Faith Estimate due dates, December 2027

For a self-pay service scheduled on Wednesday, December 1, 2027, a Good Faith Estimate is needed for any service on or after December 6, 2027 and is due by 11:59 PM on December 2, 2027, or on December 6, 2027 for a service on or after December 15, 2027. In December 2027, Christmas Day (observed) (December 24, 2027) and New Year's Day (observed) (December 31, 2027) are skipped.

- Due, scheduled December 1, 2027: December 2, 2027

Each day of December 2027. "Needed from": a service on or after this date (3 business days ahead) needs an estimate on scheduling.

## Questions

### If a self-pay service is scheduled on Wednesday, December 1, 2027 for 5 business days later, when is the estimate due?

By 11:59 PM on Thursday, December 2, 2027: 1 business day after scheduling (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vi)(A)).
