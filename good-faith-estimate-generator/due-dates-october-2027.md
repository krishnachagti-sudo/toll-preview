# Good Faith Estimate due dates, October 2027

For a self-pay service scheduled on Friday, October 1, 2027, a Good Faith Estimate is needed for any service on or after October 6, 2027 and is due by 11:59 PM on October 4, 2027, or on October 6, 2027 for a service on or after October 18, 2027. In October 2027, Columbus Day (October 11, 2027) is skipped.

- Due, scheduled October 1, 2027: October 4, 2027

Each day of October 2027. "Needed from": a service on or after this date (3 business days ahead) needs an estimate on scheduling.

## Questions

### If a self-pay service is scheduled on Friday, October 1, 2027 for 5 business days later, when is the estimate due?

By 11:59 PM on Monday, October 4, 2027: 1 business day after scheduling (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vi)(A)).
