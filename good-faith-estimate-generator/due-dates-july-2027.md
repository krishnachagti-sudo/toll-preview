# Good Faith Estimate due dates, July 2027

For a self-pay service scheduled on Thursday, July 1, 2027, a Good Faith Estimate is needed for any service on or after July 7, 2027 and is due by 11:59 PM on July 2, 2027, or on July 7, 2027 for a service on or after July 16, 2027. In July 2027, Independence Day (observed) (July 5, 2027) is skipped.

- Due, scheduled July 1, 2027: July 2, 2027

Each day of July 2027. "Needed from": a service on or after this date (3 business days ahead) needs an estimate on scheduling.

## Questions

### If a self-pay service is scheduled on Thursday, July 1, 2027 for 5 business days later, when is the estimate due?

By 11:59 PM on Friday, July 2, 2027: 1 business day after scheduling (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vi)(A)).
