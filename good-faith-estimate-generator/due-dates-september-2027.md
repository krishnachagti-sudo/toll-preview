# Good Faith Estimate due dates, September 2027

For a self-pay service scheduled on Wednesday, September 1, 2027, a Good Faith Estimate is needed for any service on or after September 7, 2027 and is due by 11:59 PM on September 2, 2027, or on September 7, 2027 for a service on or after September 16, 2027. In September 2027, Labor Day (September 6, 2027) is skipped.

- Due, scheduled September 1, 2027: September 2, 2027

Each day of September 2027. "Needed from": a service on or after this date (3 business days ahead) needs an estimate on scheduling.

## Questions

### If a self-pay service is scheduled on Wednesday, September 1, 2027 for 5 business days later, when is the estimate due?

By 11:59 PM on Thursday, September 2, 2027: 1 business day after scheduling (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vi)(A)).
