# Convening provider and co-providers

The convening provider must ask each co-provider for its estimate no later than 1 business day after scheduling, and each must answer within 1 business day of the request (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(v), (b)(2)(i)). Scheduled Tuesday, December 1, 2026, the requests go out by Wednesday, December 2, 2026; the three-provider surgery example totals $9,100.00.

## Enforcement discretion

"HHS is extending enforcement discretion, pending future rulemaking, for situations where GFEs for uninsured (or self-pay) individuals do not include expected charges from co-providers or co-facilities." (CMS GFE FAQs Part 3, December 2, 2022.) CMS's consumer guide tells patients that an estimate may list one provider's charges and suggests asking both the surgeon and the hospital.

## Questions

### If I refer to an anesthesiologist or a lab, whose estimate is it?

The convening provider (who schedules the primary service) gives the estimate and asks co-providers for theirs; but HHS is extending enforcement discretion, pending future rulemaking, where an estimate does not include co-provider or co-facility charges (CMS GFE FAQs Part 3). If the patient schedules with the co-provider directly, that provider becomes a convening provider for its service (149.610(b)(2)(iv)).

### How fast must a co-provider answer?

No later than 1 business day after it receives the convening provider's request (149.610(b)(2)(i)).
