# When a Good Faith Estimate is due

A self-pay Good Faith Estimate is due 1 business day after scheduling when the service is 3 to 9 business days away, 3 business days when it is 10 or more away, and 3 business days after a request. Scheduled Wednesday, November 25, 2026 for December 1, it is due by 11:59 PM on Friday, November 27, 2026: Thanksgiving is skipped.

- Due, Nov 25 scheduling: Friday, November 27, 2026

## How the days are counted

CMS's example: an item scheduled on Monday, January 3 at 3:00 PM for Thursday, January 6 is 3 business days ahead, so the estimate is due by 11:59 PM on Tuesday, January 4. The scheduling day itself is not counted; the service day is.

## Questions

### What counts as a business day for a Good Faith Estimate?

HHS considers "business days" for GFE purposes to include Monday through Friday, not including federal holidays. The GFE must be provided by 11:59PM on the business day that it is due (CMS GFE FAQs Part 5 Q7).

### Do I need a Good Faith Estimate for a same-day or next-day appointment?

No: the requirement is not triggered on scheduling when the service is fewer than 3 business days away (CMS GFE FAQs Part 2 Q5). One given anyway is voluntary and not eligible for dispute resolution (Part 5 Q5).

### Can my state count its own holidays?

States that are primary enforcers of GFE requirements may exclude both state and federal holidays from the definition of "business days" for GFE purposes.
