Retention and copies

A Good Faith Estimate is part of the patient's medical record, and copies of any issued in the last 6 years must be given on request (45 CFR 149.610(f)(1)). An estimate dated November 16, 2026 is within that window until November 16, 2032.

Questions

How long must I keep Good Faith Estimates?

An estimate is part of the patient's medical record and must be kept the same way; copies of any estimate furnished within the last 6 years must be given to the patient on request (149.610(f)(1)). Your state's medical record rules may ask for longer.

Does this generator keep a copy?

No. It makes the PDF in memory and keeps nothing about the patient, so file the PDF in your own record system.
