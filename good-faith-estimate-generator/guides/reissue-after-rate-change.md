# Reissuing a Good Faith Estimate after a fee change

A fee change needs a new Good Faith Estimate no later than 1 business day before the next session (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vii)). For a first January session on Monday, January 4, 2027, that is Thursday, December 31, 2026: January 1 is a federal holiday.

## Questions

### I raised my rates. Do existing clients need new Good Faith Estimates?

Yes, for clients who scheduled under the old estimate: an anticipated change in expected charges is a change of scope, and a new estimate must be issued no later than 1 business day before the items or services are furnished (149.610(b)(1)(vii)).

### What if I bill the new rate on the old estimate?

If the bill for a provider ends up at least $400 more than that provider's estimate, the patient can start dispute resolution within 120 calendar days of the bill (CMS model notice). A $10 raise on weekly sessions reaches $400 after 40 sessions.
