# Estimating recurring sessions

A recurring Good Faith Estimate must state the timeframe, frequency and total number of sessions, for at most 12 months. Weekly therapy at $150 from November 19, 2026 is 52 sessions through November 11, 2027, $7,800.00 in all.

## Steps

1. **Pick the period.** Up to 12 months from the estimate date.
2. **Pick the frequency.** Weekly, every 2 weeks, or whatever you expect, and say so.
3. **Count.** Count the sessions in the period and multiply by your self-pay rate.
4. **State the scope.** Write the timeframe, frequency and total number on the estimate (149.610(b)(1)(x)(A)).

## Questions

### Do I need a new Good Faith Estimate for every session?

No. CMS says a provider may issue a single estimate for recurring items or services, such as periodic counseling or physical therapy, rather than one for each instance (GFE FAQs Part 2 Q3).

### What if the client stops early?

The estimate is only an estimate and is not a contract; it does not require the patient to get the services (149.610(c)(1)(ix), (xi)). If the frequency or number of sessions changes, a new estimate is due no later than 1 business day before the next service (149.610(b)(1)(vii)).
