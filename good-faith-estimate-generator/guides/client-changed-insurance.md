# When a client's insurance changes

A client who becomes uninsured or self-pay gets a Good Faith Estimate on the usual timing; if you learn of it fewer than 3 business days before an appointment, no estimate is required for it (CMS GFE FAQs Part 2 Q6). For a Monday, January 4, 2027 appointment the cut-off is Tuesday, December 29, 2026.

- Cut-off for Jan 4, 2027: Tuesday, December 29, 2026

Appointment, and the last day learning of the change still requires an estimate (3 business days before)

| Appointment | Estimate required if known by |
| --- | --- |
| Monday, January 4, 2027 | Tuesday, December 29, 2026 |
| Tuesday, January 5, 2027 | Wednesday, December 30, 2026 |
| Wednesday, January 6, 2027 | Thursday, December 31, 2026 |
| Thursday, January 7, 2027 | Monday, January 4, 2027 |
| Friday, January 8, 2027 | Tuesday, January 5, 2027 |
| Monday, January 11, 2027 | Wednesday, January 6, 2027 |
| Tuesday, January 12, 2027 | Thursday, January 7, 2027 |

From then on, each new scheduling follows the normal timing: 1 business day for a service 3 to 9 business days away, 3 for 10 or more.

## Questions

### My client lost their insurance on January 1. Do I need a Good Faith Estimate?

Yes for sessions scheduled once you know they are uninsured or self-pay, on the usual timing. If you learn it fewer than 3 business days before an appointment, nothing in 45 CFR 149.610 requires an estimate for it or a rescheduled appointment (CMS GFE FAQs Part 2 Q6).

### Should I ask clients to tell me about coverage changes?

CMS encourages providers to tell patients, when scheduling, to contact them if their insurance status changes before the appointment, so a new estimate can be given if needed.
