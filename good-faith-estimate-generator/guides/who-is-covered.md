# Who gets a Good Faith Estimate

Two kinds of patient get a Good Faith Estimate: the uninsured, and those with a group plan, insurance or FEHB plan who do not want a claim sent for the service (45 CFR 149.610(a)(2)(xiii)). CMS adds 4 kinds of plan that still count as uninsured: health care sharing ministries, short-term limited-duration insurance, self-funded student health plans and farm bureau plans.

The patient's situation and what the rule says

| Situation | For the estimate | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No coverage at all | Uninsured: gets the estimate | 149.610(a)(2)(xiii)(A) |
| Employer plan or individual insurance, not using it for this service | Self-pay: gets the estimate | 149.610(a)(2)(xiii)(B) |
| FEHB plan, not using it for this service | Self-pay: gets the estimate | 149.610(a)(2)(xiii)(B) |
| Health care sharing ministry only | Uninsured for GFE purposes | CMS GFE FAQs Part 5 Q1 |
| Short-term, limited-duration insurance only | Uninsured for GFE purposes | CMS GFE FAQs Part 5 Q1 |
| Self-funded student health plan only | Uninsured for GFE purposes | CMS GFE FAQs Part 5 Q1 |
| Farm bureau plan only | Uninsured for GFE purposes | CMS GFE FAQs Part 5 Q1 |
| Federal health care program (for example Medicare or Medicaid) | Not uninsured; the self-pay branch (B) does not list federal programs | 149.610(a)(2)(xiii) |

Ask every patient, when scheduling or when they ask about cost, whether they have coverage and whether they want a claim submitted (149.610(b)(1)(i)-(ii)). Medicare, Medicaid and other federal programs have their own billing rules, which this page does not cover.

## Questions

### Do I need a Good Faith Estimate for an out-of-network client who pays cash?

Yes if they have a group plan, insurance or FEHB plan but do not seek to have a claim submitted for the service: they are self-pay (149.610(a)(2)(xiii)(B)). If they want a claim sent (even out of network), they are not self-pay under this rule.

### Do I have to check a client's coverage?

You must ask whether they are enrolled and whether they want a claim submitted, and take reasonable steps to determine whether they are enrolled; contacting the plan is encouraged but not required (CMS GFE FAQs Part 5 Q2).
