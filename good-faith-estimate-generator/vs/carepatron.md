# Carepatron's GFE template, compared

Carepatron's Good Faith Estimate template is a free fillable PDF ($0) where you type each date and charge; it works nothing out. Here the due date and check are free and the PDF is $1.00, with 12 months of sessions counted: the weekly $150 example is $8,000.00.

- Carepatron template: $0

## What Carepatron offers

- A free fillable, printable PDF, or the same template opened in the Carepatron app to customize.
- Fields: patient information and contact preference, primary and secondary diagnoses with codes, the scheduled date or timeline, each item with its expected charge, and a signature block.
- No calculation: dates and charges are entered by hand.
- Practice software: a Free plan (100 appointments, among other limits), Plus at $31 and Advanced at $39 a month, shown at half price for 6 months, billed per user.

> From carepatron.com's template page (dated August 2, 2025) and pricing page, read November 8, 2026.

## When Carepatron's template is the better choice

A practice that writes two estimates a year and has time to type them gets a free form. A practice already on Carepatron keeps the estimate with the client's record.

## When a generated estimate saves time

- Recurring sessions: counted, dated and totaled, stopped at 12 months.
- The due date in business days.
- Several providers with totals each, and the $400 dispute line per provider.
- Each required element checked, and the NPI check digit.
