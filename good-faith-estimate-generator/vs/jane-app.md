# Jane's GFE template, compared

Jane builds a Good Faith Estimate from a chart template, with code search and a PDF print; its plans are CAD 59, 79 and 99 a month (pricing page, November 8, 2026). Its guide does not cover recurring sessions or several providers, which a standalone estimate here works out for $1.00.

- Jane plans from: CAD 59

## What Jane's guide describes

- A "Jane Team- Good Faith Estimate" chart template, or your own, used as a new chart entry; CPT and diagnosis code search in note fields.
- Client and practitioner details filled in for the PDF export; the PDF is uploaded to the client's files and can be made visible in the client's account.
- Jane cannot flag on the intake form that a client will not use insurance; the guide suggests a task to check forms 3 to 4 days before appointments.
- Plans: Balance CAD 59 (20 appointments a month), Practice CAD 79 and Thrive CAD 99 a month.

> From jane.app's guide "Creating and managing Good Faith Estimates in Jane" and its pricing page, read November 8, 2026. The guide does not mention recurring sessions or multiple providers.

## When practice software is the better choice

If your scheduling and billing system makes the estimate at booking, from the chart, an integrated module beats a standalone tool: less typing, and the estimate stays with the record.

## When a standalone estimate fits

- A 12-month course of sessions to count and total.
- Several providers or a facility on one estimate.
- The due date worked out in business days.
- No practice software, or one without a GFE form.

## Questions

### How do I make a Good Faith Estimate in Jane?

Jane's guide: use the "Jane Team- Good Faith Estimate" template from the Chart Template Library (or your own), start a new chart entry with it, add CPT and diagnosis codes, sign it, then print it as a PDF and upload it to the client's files, visible to the client.
