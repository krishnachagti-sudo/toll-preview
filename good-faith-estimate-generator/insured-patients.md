# Good Faith Estimates for insured patients

Since January 1, 2022, the Good Faith Estimate rule in force covers uninsured or self-pay patients (45 CFR 149.610). For insured patients, CMS's progress report of April 23, 2024 says enforcement of the insured GFE and the Advanced Explanation of Benefits is deferred pending future rulemaking; an insured patient who chooses not to use their coverage counts as self-pay.

## What CMS says

"The Departments and OPM deferred enforcement of GFE requirements for insured individuals and AEOB requirements, pending future rulemaking." (CMS, Progress Toward AEOB Rulemaking and Implementation, April 23, 2024). CMS's GFE FAQs Part 2 (Q6) say HHS will not enforce the requirement to send GFEs to plans and issuers until rulemaking to implement it is adopted and applicable.

## Who is self-pay

Uninsured: no group health plan, individual or group insurance, federal health care program or FEHB plan. Self-pay: has a group plan, insurance or FEHB plan but does not seek to have a claim submitted for the item or service (149.610(a)(2)(xiii)).

## Questions

### Do insured patients get a Good Faith Estimate?

Not yet under an enforced rule. CMS's progress report of April 23, 2024 says the Departments and OPM deferred enforcement of GFE requirements for insured individuals and AEOB requirements, pending future rulemaking. Check cms.gov for anything newer.

### What if an insured patient pays cash?

A patient with coverage who does not seek to have a claim submitted to it is self-pay and gets the estimate (149.610(a)(2)(xiii)(B)).
