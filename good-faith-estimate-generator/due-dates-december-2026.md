# Good Faith Estimate due dates, December 2026

For a self-pay service scheduled on Tuesday, December 1, 2026, a Good Faith Estimate is needed for any service on or after December 4, 2026 and is due by 11:59 PM on December 2, 2026, or on December 4, 2026 for a service on or after December 15, 2026. In December 2026, Christmas Day (December 25, 2026) is skipped.

- Due, scheduled December 1, 2026: December 2, 2026

Each day of December 2026. "Needed from": a service on or after this date (3 business days ahead) needs an estimate on scheduling.

## Questions

### If a self-pay service is scheduled on Tuesday, December 1, 2026 for 5 business days later, when is the estimate due?

By 11:59 PM on Wednesday, December 2, 2026: 1 business day after scheduling (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vi)(A)).
