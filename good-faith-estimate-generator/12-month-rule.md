# The 12-month rule for recurring sessions

One Good Faith Estimate may cover recurring sessions for at most 12 months (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(x)(B)). An estimate dated November 16, 2026 can cover weekly sessions through November 15, 2027: 52 of them starting November 19, 2026. Sessions after that need a new estimate.

- Longest scope: 12 months
- Last date for a GFE of Nov 16, 2026: November 15, 2027

## What the new estimate must say

"The scope of a good faith estimate for recurring primary items or services must not exceed 12 months." When a new one is given, the provider communicates what changed (timeframes, frequency and total number) so the patient can compare (149.610(b)(1)(x)(B)).

## Questions

### How often do I need to redo Good Faith Estimates for ongoing clients?

At least every 12 months: the scope of an estimate for recurring services must not exceed 12 months, and a new one is due for recurrences beyond that (149.610(b)(1)(x)(B)). A change in charges, frequency, duration or providers needs a new one sooner, no later than 1 business day before the service (149.610(b)(1)(vii)).

### From what date do the 12 months run?

The regulation caps the scope at 12 months; the CMS model notice says the estimated costs are valid for 12 months from the date of the Good Faith Estimate. The generator keeps every session within 12 months of the estimate date, the stricter reading.
