# Good Faith Estimate due dates, November 2026

For a self-pay service scheduled on Monday, November 2, 2026, a Good Faith Estimate is needed for any service on or after November 5, 2026 and is due by 11:59 PM on November 3, 2026, or on November 5, 2026 for a service on or after November 17, 2026. In November 2026, Veterans Day (November 11, 2026) and Thanksgiving Day (November 26, 2026) are skipped.

- Due, scheduled November 2, 2026: November 3, 2026

Each day of November 2026. "Needed from": a service on or after this date (3 business days ahead) needs an estimate on scheduling.

## Questions

### If a self-pay service is scheduled on Monday, November 2, 2026 for 5 business days later, when is the estimate due?

By 11:59 PM on Tuesday, November 3, 2026: 1 business day after scheduling (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vi)(A)).
