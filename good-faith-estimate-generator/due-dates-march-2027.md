# Good Faith Estimate due dates, March 2027

For a self-pay service scheduled on Monday, March 1, 2027, a Good Faith Estimate is needed for any service on or after March 4, 2027 and is due by 11:59 PM on March 2, 2027, or on March 4, 2027 for a service on or after March 15, 2027.

- Due, scheduled March 1, 2027: March 2, 2027

Each day of March 2027. "Needed from": a service on or after this date (3 business days ahead) needs an estimate on scheduling.

## Questions

### If a self-pay service is scheduled on Monday, March 1, 2027 for 5 business days later, when is the estimate due?

By 11:59 PM on Tuesday, March 2, 2027: 1 business day after scheduling (45 CFR 149.610(b)(1)(vi)(A)).
