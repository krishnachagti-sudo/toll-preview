# Ontario rent increase guidelines, 1991 to 2027

Ontario's rent increase guideline has ranged from 0% (2021 freeze) to 6.0% (1992); it is 1.9% for 2027 and 2.1% for 2026, capped at 2.5% since 2013.

- 2027: 1.9%
- 2026: 2.1%
- Highest: 6.0% (1992)
- Cap: 2.5%

## How it is set

The Minister determines it from the Ontario Consumer Price Index, averaged over the 12 months ending at the end of May of the previous year, rounded to one decimal point, and capped at 2.5% (RTA s.120(2), in force since June 19, 2012, which is why 2012's 3.1% is above the cap). It must be published in The Ontario Gazette by August 31 of the year before (s.120(3)).

- [Ontario.ca: Residential rent increases (updated June 23, 2026, read November 8, 2026)](https://www.ontario.ca/page/residential-rent-increases)
- [Residential Tenancies Act, 2006, s.120 and s.136.1 (e-Laws, current from September 21, 2026, read November 8, 2026)](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/06r17)

> Computed from the Residential Tenancies Act, 2006, the Legislation Act, 2006 and ontario.ca, each cited with the date read. Not legal advice. Above-guideline increases need an order of the Landlord and Tenant Board or a signed N10 agreement. Courier, fax and email service depend on the Landlord and Tenant Board's Rules and are not computed here.

## Questions

### What is the Ontario rent increase guideline for 2027?

1.9% (ontario.ca, updated June 23, 2026).

### How is the guideline set?

From the Ontario Consumer Price Index averaged over the 12 months ending in May of the year before, rounded to one decimal and not more than 2.5% (RTA s.120(2)). The Minister publishes it by August 31 of the year before (s.120(3)).

### What was the guideline in 2021?

0%: a rent freeze set in law (RTA s.120(3.1) and s.136.1). Otherwise it would have been 1.5% (ontario.ca).

### What was the highest Ontario guideline?

6.0%, in 1992.
