# The LTB's Form N1 vs this calculator

The LTB's Form N1 (version 01/04/2022) is the notice itself and must be used (RTA s.116(3)); this calculator works out the figures for it: for $2,000 on March 1, 2027, $2,038.00, served by December 1, 2026.

- N1 version: 01/04/2022
- Serve by (March 1, 2027): December 1, 2026

Checked November 8, 2026

| Point | Them | This calculator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | The approved notice (PDF), with instructions | The numbers and dates to put on it |
| Instructions | Dated November 30, 2015 | Rules read from the Act as current from September 21, 2026 |
| Computes the new rent | No: the instructions show the formula | Yes, rounded down to the cent |
| Serve-by date | States 90 days | Works out the date, with 5 days for mail |
| Price | Free | Free on the page |

## When they are the better choice

Always: the LTB form is the legal document. Serve the official N1; use the calculator only to fill it in and to time it.
