# When to get help instead

A calculator answers the arithmetic: the amount, the 12 months and the 90 days. For a dispute you have 1 year from the first charge to apply to the Landlord and Tenant Board (RTA s.136), and free advice is available, for example Queen's off-campus living advisors for its students.

- Time to dispute: 1 year

## When they are the better choice

Whenever the facts are disputed or a hearing is coming. The Landlord and Tenant Board answers questions at 416-645-8080 or 1-888-332-3234 (ontario.ca). Queen's University's Off-Campus Living Advisors review leases and N1 questions with its students; other campuses and community legal clinics offer similar help for those eligible.
