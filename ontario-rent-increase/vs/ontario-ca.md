## When they are the better choice

Ontario.ca is the source of truth for the guideline. If a number here ever differs from it, ontario.ca wins: this calculator copies its table by hand and shows the date it was read. It also lists the Landlord and Tenant Board's contact details: ltb@ontario.ca, 416-645-8080, 1-888-332-3234.
