# TenantPay vs this calculator

TenantPay's article (October 5, 2026) gives 1.9% for 2027 with four amounts, $2,000 rising by $38; this calculator gives the same $38.00 and the dates: for June 1, 2027, serve by March 3, 2027.

- Increase on $2,000: $38.00
- Serve by (June 1, 2027): March 3, 2027

Checked November 8, 2026

| Point | Them | This calculator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Format | An article with examples for $1,000, $1,500, $2,000, $2,500 | A calculator for any rent |
| Rules | 12 months, 90 days, the 2018 exemption in words | The same, worked out on your dates |
| Mail rule | Not mentioned | Fifth day after mailing (s.191(3)) |
| Company | A rent-payment and rewards service for renters (fees from $4.99 on its home page) | Pay per use, no subscription |

## When they are the better choice

If you pay or collect rent through TenantPay, its own records and reminders sit where your payments are. For the rules themselves, both rest on the same Act.
