# liv.rent vs this calculator

liv.rent's guide (updated May 22, 2026) gives 2026's 2.1% and the rules in words; the 2027 guideline is 1.9% (ontario.ca, June 23, 2026), which this calculator applies to increases from January 1, 2027.

- 2027 guideline: 1.9%
- liv.rent updated: May 22, 2026

Checked November 8, 2026

| Point | Them | This calculator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Latest guideline shown | 2.1% for 2026 | 1.9% for 2027, and every year since 1991 |
| Rules | 12 months, 90 days, Form N1, exemptions, above-guideline | The same, with each section cited |
| Calculator | No | Yes: rent, dates, exemption, deposit interest |
| Price | Free article | Free on the page |

## When they are the better choice

For a broader introduction to renting in Ontario, and for listing a unit, liv.rent covers more ground than a single calculator.

Courier, fax and email service depend on the Landlord and Tenant Board's Rules and are not computed here.
