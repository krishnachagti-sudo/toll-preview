# N1 notice timing, counted

Give the N1 at least 90 days before the increase, not counting the day it is given (Legislation Act s.89(3)); mail counts from the fifth day after mailing (RTA s.191(3)), so a January 1, 2027 increase needed it by October 3, 2026 by hand or mailed by September 28, 2026, and March 1, 2027 needs December 1, 2026 or November 26, 2026.

- January 1, 2027, by hand: October 3, 2026
- Notice: 90 days
- Mail: +5 days

Worked dates

| Increase on | Given by (hand or mail box) | Mailed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January 1, 2027 | Saturday, October 3, 2026 | Monday, September 28, 2026 |
| March 1, 2027 | Tuesday, December 1, 2026 | Thursday, November 26, 2026 |
| June 1, 2027 | Wednesday, March 3, 2027 | Friday, February 26, 2027 |
| September 1, 2027 | Thursday, June 3, 2027 | Saturday, May 29, 2027 |

Why not 91? The Legislation Act settles it: a number of days between two events excludes the first and includes the second, even when the law says "at least" (s.89(3)). A notice given October 3, 2026 is 90 days before January 1, 2027.

A deadline on a Sunday or holiday: the Legislation Act extends time limits that expire on a holiday (s.89(1)), but whether that helps a notice that must come "at least" 90 days before is not settled by the text. Serve earlier.

## Steps

1. **Start from the increase date.** Take the day the new rent starts. It must also be at least 12 months after the last increase or the start of the tenancy (RTA s.119).
2. **Count back 90 days.** The day the notice is given is not counted and the increase day is (Legislation Act s.89(3)), so the notice is given on the increase date minus 90 days at the latest.
3. **Add 5 days for mail.** A mailed notice is deemed given on the fifth day after mailing (RTA s.191(3)): mail it 95 days before.
4. **Keep proof.** Write down who delivered it, how, the date and time; keep a copy of the signed notice.

## Questions

### What happens if the N1 was served late?

The increase is void and the landlord must give a new notice before taking it (RTA s.116(4)). If the tenant nonetheless pays the increase for 12 consecutive months and does not apply to the Board within a year of it first being charged, it is deemed not void (s.135.1).

### Can the N1 be posted on the door?

The Act lists handing it to the tenant or an adult in the unit, the mail box, and mail, plus means in the Board's Rules (s.191(1)). A notice given another way counts only if it is proven it reached the tenant in time (s.191(2)).
