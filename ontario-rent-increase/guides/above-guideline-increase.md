# Above-guideline increases

An above-guideline increase needs a Board order applied for at least 90 days before the first increase (RTA s.126(3)); for capital work and security costs the extra is at most 3% a year above the guideline (s.126(11)), so a $2,000 rent in 2027 could go from $2,038.00 at the guideline to $2,098.00 with a 3% order.

- 2027 guideline on $2,000: $2,038.00
- With a 3% order: $2,098.00
- Apply before: 90 days

Three ways above the guideline

| Point | N10 agreement (s.121) | Board order (s.126) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who decides | Landlord and tenant sign | The Landlord and Tenant Board |
| Grounds | A capital expenditure or a new service | Municipal taxes, eligible capital work, security services |
| Limit | Guideline + 3% of the previous lawful rent | For capital and security: 3% a year above the guideline, spread over up to three 12-month periods (s.126(11)) |
| Timing | In force 6 days after signing at the earliest; tenant may cancel within 5 days | Apply 90 days before the first increase; the N1 is still needed (s.116(2)) |
| $2,000 in 2027 | At most $2,098.00 | $2,098.00 with a 3% order |

A 7% justified capital increase would be taken as 3%, 3% and 1% over three years, each on top of that year's guideline (s.126(11)(b)). The Board dismisses or delays the increase for a unit affected by serious unrepaired breaches or unfinished elevator orders (s.126(12) to (13)).

## Questions

### What can justify an above-guideline increase in Ontario?

An extraordinary increase in municipal taxes and charges, eligible capital expenditures, or operating costs for security services provided by people not employed by the landlord (RTA s.126(1)).

### What is an eligible capital expenditure?

Work needed to protect or restore the building's physical integrity, to meet maintenance standards, to keep plumbing, heating, mechanical, electrical, ventilation or air conditioning running, or that provides disability access, saves energy or water, or improves security (s.126(7)); not a replacement of something that did not need it (s.126(8)), and not for a tenant who moved in after the work was done (s.126(9)).

### Do I pay the higher rent before the order?

No more than the lesser of the new rent in the notice and the guideline amount (s.126(5)); you may choose to pay the full amount and get back any excess (s.126(6)).
