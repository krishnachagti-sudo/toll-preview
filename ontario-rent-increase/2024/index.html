Ontario rent increase 2024: 2.5%

The Ontario rent increase guideline for 2024 is 2.5%: a $2,000 rent can rise to $2,050.00 (up $50.00) for increases taking effect in 2024.

The guideline applies by the calendar year the increase takes effect (RTA s.120(2)), not the year the notice is given. An increase on December 31, 2023 uses 2.5%; one on January 1, 2024 uses 2.5%. The guideline was also 2.5% in 2013, 2023, 2025.

At least 90 days' written notice (RTA s.116(1)), counted the Legislation Act way: the day the notice is given is not counted and the increase day is (s.89(3)). Mail is deemed given on the fifth day after mailing (s.191(3)), so it leaves 95 days before.

A date that falls on a Sunday or holiday is shown as it is. Serve earlier rather than rely on the Legislation Act's holiday extension (s.89(1)).

Who it covers

Most private rentals: houses, apartments, basement apartments, condos, care homes (rent portion), mobile homes and land lease communities (ontario.ca). Not units first occupied for residential purposes after November 15, 2018 (exemption check), not a new tenant's starting rent (between tenants), not community housing, long-term care or commercial property.

Questions

What is the Ontario rent increase guideline for 2024?

2.5%. It is the most most landlords can raise a sitting tenant's rent for an increase taking effect in 2024 without an order of the Landlord and Tenant Board.

How much can a $2,000 rent go up in 2024?

$50.00, to $2,050.00 a month, if the unit is covered by the guideline, 12 months have passed since the last increase and the tenant got 90 days' written notice.

When must the N1 be served for a January 1, 2024 increase?

By October 3, 2023 if handed over or left in the mail box, or mailed by September 28, 2023: mail counts as given on the fifth day after mailing (RTA s.191(3)).

What interest is owed on last month's rent in 2024?

2.5% of the deposit, for interest that falls due in 2024: the rate is the guideline in effect when the payment becomes due (RTA s.106(6)).
