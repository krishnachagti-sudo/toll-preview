# N1, N2 or N10: which form

Use N1 for a guideline increase (at most $2,038.00 from $2,000 in 2027), N10 when the tenant signs an agreement to more (at most the guideline plus 3%, so $2,098.00), and N2 for a unit partially exempt from the limits.

- N1 from $2,000 (2027): $2,038.00
- N10 at most: $2,098.00
- N10 cancel window: 5 days
- N1 notice: 90 days

The routes to a higher rent, for $2,000 in 2027

| Route | When | Limit | Notice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| N1 Notice of Rent Increase | A sitting tenant, covered unit | Guideline (1.9%): $2,038.00 | 90 days in writing (s.116) |
| N10 Agreement to Increase the Rent Above the Guideline | Tenant agrees, for a capital expenditure or a new service (s.121(1)) | Guideline + 3%: $2,098.00 (s.121(3)) | None (s.121(6)); in force 6 days after signing at the earliest |
| Parking or a prescribed service added (s.123) | Tenant agrees to the added item | As prescribed | None; does not reset the 12 months (s.119(2)) |
| Above-guideline application to the Board (s.126) | Municipal taxes, eligible capital work, security services | 3% a year above the guideline for capital and security (s.126(11)) | Application 90 days before (s.126(3)), plus the N1 |
| N2 Notice of Rent Increase (Unit Partially Exempt) | A unit exempt from the limits | No guideline | 90 days (s.116 still applies) |

## Questions

### When is Form N10 used?

When landlord and tenant agree to an increase above the guideline in exchange for a capital expenditure or a new service (RTA s.121(1)). It may not exceed the guideline plus 3% of the previous lawful rent (s.121(3)); the tenant can cancel within 5 days of signing (s.121(4)); it takes effect no earlier than 6 days after signing (s.121(5)); no 90-day notice is needed (s.121(6)).

### When is Form N2 used?

The LTB lists N2 as the Notice of Rent Increase for a unit partially exempt; its instructions name non-profit and social housing and care home units exempt from the rules limiting increases.

### Which form for a unit first occupied after November 15, 2018?

Neither the Act nor ontario.ca names one. The LTB's N1 instructions (2015, before that exemption) say to use N2 when the unit is exempt from the rules limiting the amount. Check with the Landlord and Tenant Board.
