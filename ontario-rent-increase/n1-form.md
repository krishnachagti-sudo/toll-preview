## Serving it

- Deliver it by mail (RTA s.191(1)(f), 191(3)) on or before November 26, 2026.
- Mail counts as given on the fifth day after mailing (s.191(3)): mailed November 26, 2026, it is given December 1, 2026, 90 days before March 1, 2027.
- Write down who delivered it, how, the date and time, and keep a copy of the signed notice.
- Serving earlier than the last day is always fine.

[Download the official Form N1 from the Landlord and Tenant Board](https://tribunalsontario.ca/ltb/forms/). Do not post it on the door: the Act lists handing it over, the mail box and mail (s.191(1)).

## Questions

### Where do I get Form N1?

From the Landlord and Tenant Board's forms page (tribunalsontario.ca/ltb/forms), as a PDF. The version read on November 8, 2026 is dated 01/04/2022. The Act requires the form approved by the Board (s.116(3)).

### What must the N1 say?

The landlord's intention to increase the rent and the amount of the new rent (RTA s.116(3)), with the date it takes effect.

### Does the tenant have to sign the N1?

No. The N1 is a notice from the landlord; the form has a landlord signature box only. An N10 agreement is the form both sign.
