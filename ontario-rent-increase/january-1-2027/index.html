A January 1, 2027 increase

For an Ontario rent increase on January 1, 2027, the N1 had to be given by October 3, 2026 by hand or mailed by September 28, 2026; the 2027 guideline of 1.9% applies, so $2,000 becomes $2,038.00.

Questions

If my rent increase starts January 1, 2027, when must the N1 be served?

By October 3, 2026 if handed over (90 days); by September 28, 2026 if sent by regular mail, which counts as given 5 days after mailing.
