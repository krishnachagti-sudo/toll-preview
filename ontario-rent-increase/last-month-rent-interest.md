# Interest on last month's rent

Ontario landlords pay interest on a last month's rent deposit every year at the guideline in effect when it falls due (RTA s.106(6)): 1.9% for 2027, so $38.00 on a $2,000 deposit, and the landlord may keep it to top the deposit up to the new rent (s.106(7)).

- 2027 rate: 1.9%
- On $2,000: $38.00
- 2026 rate: 2.1%

## Worked example

On a $1,800.00 deposit paid June 1, 2023, 3 yearly interest payments came due through November 8, 2026, $130.84 in all: $130.83 kept to top the deposit up to the rent and $0.01 owed to the tenant; the deposit is now $1,930.83.

An $1,800 deposit paid June 1, 2023, rent raised by the guideline each June

| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| June 1, 2024 | 2.5% | $45.00 | $1,845.00 | $45.00 | $0.00 | $1,845.00 |
| June 1, 2025 | 2.5% | $46.13 | $1,891.12 | $46.12 | $0.01 | $1,891.12 |

> Interest falls due on each anniversary of the day the deposit was paid; the Act says "annually" without naming a day. Amounts are rounded to the cent.

## Questions

### Do Ontario landlords pay interest on last month's rent?

Yes, annually, at a rate equal to the rent increase guideline in effect when the payment becomes due (RTA s.106(6)).

### What is the interest rate on last month's rent in Ontario for 2027?

1.9%, the 2027 guideline, for interest that falls due in 2027.

### Can the landlord keep the interest?

Only to top the deposit up to the current rent: the landlord may deduct from the interest the amount by which the most the deposit may be (one month's rent) exceeds the deposit held, and that amount becomes part of the deposit (s.106(7)). The rest is the tenant's; unpaid, the tenant may deduct it from rent (s.106(9)).
