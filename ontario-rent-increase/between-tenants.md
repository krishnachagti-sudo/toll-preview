# Rent between tenants

When a unit turns over, the landlord and the new tenant agree the starting rent and the guideline does not apply (ontario.ca; RTA s.113); a tenancy starting December 1, 2026 at $2,400 can first rise on December 1, 2027, by at most 1.9% to $2,445.60.

- First increase: December 1, 2027
- At most: $2,445.60

## Questions

### Can a landlord raise the rent by any amount between tenants in Ontario?

The guideline does not apply on turnover: the landlord and the new tenant agree the rent (ontario.ca). The lawful rent for a new tenant is the rent first charged (RTA s.113).

### When can the new tenant's rent first go up?

12 months after the tenancy began (s.119(1)(b)), with 90 days' written notice, by at most the guideline for the year the increase takes effect.
