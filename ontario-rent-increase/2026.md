# Ontario rent increase 2026: 2.1%

The Ontario rent increase guideline for 2026 is 2.1%: a $2,000 rent can rise to $2,042.00 (up $42.00) for increases taking effect in 2026.

- 2026 guideline: 2.1%
- $2,000 becomes: $2,042.00
- 2025 guideline: 2.5%
- Notice: 90 days

The guideline applies by the calendar year the increase takes effect (RTA s.120(2)), not the year the notice is given. An increase on December 31, 2025 uses 2.5%; one on January 1, 2026 uses 2.1%.

## Who it covers

Most private rentals: houses, apartments, basement apartments, condos, care homes (rent portion), mobile homes and land lease communities (ontario.ca). Not units first occupied for residential purposes after November 15, 2018 ([exemption check](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/ontario-rent-increase/exemption-check/)), not a new tenant's starting rent ([between tenants](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/ontario-rent-increase/between-tenants/)), not community housing, long-term care or commercial property.

## Questions

### What is the Ontario rent increase guideline for 2026?

2.1%. It is the most most landlords can raise a sitting tenant's rent for an increase taking effect in 2026 without an order of the Landlord and Tenant Board.

### How much can a $2,000 rent go up in 2026?

$42.00, to $2,042.00 a month, if the unit is covered by the guideline, 12 months have passed since the last increase and the tenant got 90 days' written notice.

### When must the N1 be served for a January 1, 2026 increase?

By October 3, 2025 if handed over or left in the mail box, or mailed by September 28, 2025: mail counts as given on the fifth day after mailing (RTA s.191(3)).

### What interest is owed on last month's rent in 2026?

2.1% of the deposit, for interest that falls due in 2026: the rate is the guideline in effect when the payment becomes due (RTA s.106(6)).
