QR TIGER or a static QR code?

QR TIGER suits campaigns that need dynamic codes and scan data on a paid plan; its free plan allows 3 dynamic codes with 500 scans each. A static code suits print that must keep working with no plan at all.

When QR TIGER is the better choice

When you need dynamic codes you can redirect, scan analytics and a dashboard, and you will keep a paid plan for as long as the codes are in print.

When a static code is the better choice

When the code is printed to last and you do not need scan counts. Bulk codes from a spreadsheet covers batches.

Questions

Do QR TIGER codes expire?

QR TIGER says its static codes do not expire, and that if a plan expires, codes and data are retained for up to one year (page updated September 11, 2026; read 6 November 2026).

How many dynamic codes does QR TIGER give free?

Its page says three dynamic QR codes with a 500-scan limit each.
