# Landed Cost Receiving vs this allocator

Landed Cost Receiving costs about $28.89 a month and splits by value, quantity or weight, writing a weighted average to Cost per item; at $1.50 a shipment of up to 50 lines this allocator never costs more than $14.90 in a month, adds CBM and mixed bases, and keeps late invoices off units already sold.

## Landed Cost Receiving, from its own page

- Price: about $28.89 a month (one Standard plan; the listing showed us a local-currency price converted at its own rate of 0.0106 US dollars); 7-day free trial (read November 8, 2026).
- Allocate freight, duty and fees.
- Value, quantity or weight basis.
- Writes cost per item to Shopify as a weighted average.
- Cost history, undo and CSV export.
- Received transfer shipments appear automatically.
- Launched June 16, 2026; no reviews yet.

## This allocator

- Price: $1.50 per shipment of up to 50 lines, free for 3 lines or fewer, re-runs for the same shipment free for a year, at most $14.90 a month.
- Bases: value, weight, CBM, units, per line, as given per SKU, or a mix, chosen per invoice.
- Every invoice split to the cent and added back exactly; Shopify's two-decimal drift shown.
- Late invoices: units on hand re-costed, sold units' share as a COGS variance.
- Your own Shopify export returned with only Cost per item filled; a CSV per SKU and a spreadsheet with formulas.
- No app install and no write access to your store: you import the file yourself.

## When Landed Cost Receiving is the better choice

When you receive stock continuously inside Shopify and want each receipt costed and written back automatically, with a weighted average against what is already on hand.
