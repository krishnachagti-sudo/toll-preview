How to write the lines and invoices

PO lines: one each, SKU, units, unit cost, kg per unit, CBM per unit, units on hand. Weight, CBM and units on hand are optional: weight and CBM are needed only for a charge split by them, units on hand only for a late invoice. You can also paste a CSV with a header row (SKU, Qty, Unit cost, Weight kg, CBM, Unit CBM, Unit cm, On hand).

Invoices: one each, name, kind, amount, basis; add late for an invoice that came after receipt. Kinds: freight, duty, brokerage, insurance, fees, discount, other. Bases: value, weight, volume (CBM), quantity (units), equal (per line), a mix such as volume 70 weight 30, or amounts per SKU such as MUG-12=216.45 TEE-M=793.67. A discount is a negative amount. End a line with only MUG-12 TEE-M to split it over some SKUs.

How each invoice is split

Each invoice is split over the lines by the basis you choose (value, weight, CBM, units, per line, as given, or a mix) and rounded to the cent so the lines add back to the invoice exactly. Unit costs keep four decimals, with the two-decimal figure for Shopify's Cost per item beside them.

A basis per invoice, to the cent, late invoices

A basis per invoice

Each line's share is the invoice times its part of the basis: its value, kilograms, cubic meters, units, one equal part per line, or the amounts you give. Freight priced by space suits CBM, heavy freight suits weight, and duty follows value because customs assesses most duty on value. A mix splits the invoice into parts first.

To the cent, adding back

Shares are rounded down to the cent, then the leftover cents go one each to the lines with the largest remainders, so every invoice's lines add back to it exactly. Unit costs keep four decimals; Shopify keeps two, so the table shows how far the two-decimal figure is from the line total.

Late invoices

An invoice marked late is split over the lines like any other, then each line's share is cut between its units on hand and the units already sold. Units on hand take their part in their new cost; the sold part is a cost of goods sold variance, so sold units are never re-costed.

Sources, read November 8, 2026

IRS Publication 538 (01/2022), Shopify Help: using CSV files for products and Shopify Help: transitioning from Stocky. IRS Publication 538 says the cost of merchandise purchased during the year is "the invoice price minus appropriate discounts plus transportation or other charges incurred in acquiring the goods".

Prices
  • A shipment of 3 lines or fewer: free.
  • Landed cost for a shipment (per-SKU table, CSV, spreadsheet with formulas, Shopify file): $1.50 per 50 PO lines; a repeat for the same shipment is free for 365 days, and a larger one pays only the difference.
  • At most $14.90 a calendar month for this tool; after that it is free until the month ends.

A 200-line container is 4 x $1.50 = $6.00. This tool never costs more than $14.90 in a month.

What it does not cover

  • Arithmetic on the lines and invoices you enter: every freight, duty and fee amount is yours, not ours.
  • It does not work out duty. For US duty use the broker's entry summary (CBP Form 7501).
  • Not tax, accounting or customs advice. How you book a late invoice is for you and your accountant.

The files

The per-SKU CSV with every charge, the late-invoice split and the totals; the spreadsheet with live formulas; and, if you paste it, your own Shopify product export with only Cost per item changed. Export the products from Shopify admin first: Shopify overwrites a blank cell with a blank, so the file keeps every other column exactly as exported, and only the products in this shipment.
