# Stockroom vs this allocator

Stockroom is free and rated 4.9 from 59 reviews on its Shopify listing, with purchase orders, receiving and landed costs at receiving; this allocator costs $1.50 a shipment of up to 50 lines and does only the costing, with a basis per invoice, cent-exact splits and late invoices kept off sold units.

## Stockroom, from its own page

- Price: Free (Shopify App Store, read November 8, 2026); launched July 3, 2026.
- Unlimited purchase orders and suppliers, receiving in full or in part, stock counts, transfers and labels.
- Track landed costs at receiving and report on margins.
- Migrate from Stocky.
- Sync with QuickBooks Online or Desktop through MyWorks.
- The listing does not say which bases landed costs are split by or how a late invoice is handled.

## This allocator

- Price: $1.50 per shipment of up to 50 lines, free for 3 lines or fewer, re-runs for the same shipment free for a year, at most $14.90 a month.
- Bases: value, weight, CBM, units, per line, as given per SKU, or a mix, chosen per invoice.
- Every invoice split to the cent and added back exactly; Shopify's two-decimal drift shown.
- Late invoices: units on hand re-costed, sold units' share as a COGS variance.
- Your own Shopify export returned with only Cost per item filled; a CSV per SKU and a spreadsheet with formulas.
- No app install and no write access to your store: you import the file yourself.

## When Stockroom is the better choice

When you want a full Stocky replacement inside Shopify (purchase orders, receiving, counts, transfers and QuickBooks sync) at no cost, and are happy with how it splits landed costs.
