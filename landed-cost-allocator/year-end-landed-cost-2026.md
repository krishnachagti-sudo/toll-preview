## Steps

1. **List the shipments received this year.** With every invoice booked against each.
2. **Chase the missing invoices.** Freight, drayage, storage, duty and broker bills that have not arrived; ask for them before the close.
3. **Post the late ones.** Mark each late and give the units on hand at year end, so on-hand units take their part and sold units' part is a variance.
4. **Value the stock on hand.** Units on hand x their landed unit cost, per SKU.
5. **Hand over the files.** The per-SKU CSV per shipment is the trail your accountant needs.
