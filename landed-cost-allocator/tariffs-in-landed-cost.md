# Tariffs in landed cost

Enter each duty line from the broker's entry summary as its own charge, split by value (or per SKU as the entry lists it): on this shipment an additional duty of $1,146.00 moves a mug from $3.8374 to $4.1524 landed, and its margin at $9.00 from 57.4% to 53.9%.

## Where the duty figures come from

The duty you paid is on the entry summary (CBP Form 7501) your broker files, line by line, including any additional duties. Enter the total by value, or use "as given per SKU" with each line's amount so nothing is averaged. When a refund or a new rate comes later, enter it as a late charge (negative for a refund) so it lands on the units still on hand.
