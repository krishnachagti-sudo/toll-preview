Refunds

Refunds and cancellation

You pay for what you use, from money you chose to add. This page says what comes back to you, and how.

Last updated [To be filled in: the date these pages were last updated]

Refunded automatically

  • A call that fails while running is refunded to your balance in the same moment. You do not need to ask.
  • A call that is refused (invalid input, a limit reached, not enough balance) is never charged.
  • A charge held for a job that is not confirmed is refunded when the hold ends, as the tool's page explains.

Unused money you added

You can ask for a refund of unused money you added within 30 days of adding it. Write to [To be filled in: the support email] from your account's email address and say which top-up. We refund the unused part to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days of approving it, depending on your bank.

After 30 days, unused money stays in your balance and never expires, so you can keep using it.

Passes

You can ask for a refund of a pass within 30 days of buying it. We refund its price less the list price of the use it covered up to then, and the pass ends. A pass never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

A pass bought with a nonprofit code is refunded the same way, from the price you paid.

Worksheet bundles

You can ask for a refund of a per diem worksheet bundle within 30 days of buying it. We refund its price less the single-worksheet list price of each worksheet it has paid for, and the unused worksheets are removed. After 30 days, unused worksheets stay in your account and never expire.

What is not refunded

  • The monthly free allowance and a pass's monthly allowance. They have no cash value.
  • Bonus credit added with a credit pack or a pass. It is a gift on top of what you paid, and it is removed if the payment it came with is refunded.
  • Calls that ran and gave their answer. Every tool shows its price before you use it, and can quote it without running.
  • Downloads that were delivered, such as a worksheet or a filing file, except where the file was wrong because of our mistake. Then tell us and we will put it right or refund it.

If we got something wrong

If a tool gave a wrong answer because of an error on our side, write to us with the details. We will check it, fix the tool, and refund the charge.

Cancelling

There is nothing to cancel: Toll has no subscription, passes never renew, and it never takes money on its own. To close your account, write to us. Unused money you added within the last 30 days is refunded as above.

Delivery

Toll sells digital services only. Nothing is shipped. A top-up is added to your balance as soon as the payment is confirmed, and each tool delivers its answer, and any file, on screen or through the API straight away.

Disputes with your bank

Please write to us before you dispute a payment with your bank. We can usually sort it out faster. While a payment is disputed, the money from that top-up cannot be spent.
