Upwork payments to Philippines: what reaches the bank in PHP

From a $1,000 Upwork payment at a 10% service fee ($900.00 after the fee), a freelancer in the Philippines receives about 55,013.47 PHP through PayPal at its published 3.0% conversion fee and 54,446.32 PHP to 56,034.33 PHP through Payoneer at its published 1.2% to 4%, at 63.01657 PHP per US dollar (ECB reference rate, November 6, 2026), before Upwork's own withdrawal fee.

What is not in these numbers

  • Upwork's own fee to withdraw by each method. It is on Upwork's help pages, which could not be read here; the calculator takes the figure your Get Paid page shows.
  • PayPal's fee to receive the payment, if your account shows one (PayPal's merchant fee page). The bank withdrawal row for Philippines: "PH: No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved) where PHP withdrawal amount is greater than 7000.00 PHP; 50.00 PHP if PHP withdrawal amount is less than 7000.00 PHP".
  • Payoneer's fee to receive from a marketplace, which its page says "Varies by marketplace", and its $29.95 annual account fee under $6,000 a year.
  • Your bank's own charges, and the gap between the provider's base rate and the reference rate.

An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it. Questions

How much of a $1,000 Upwork payment reaches a bank in Philippines?

From a $1,000 Upwork payment at a 10% service fee ($900.00 after the fee), a freelancer in the Philippines receives about 55,013.47 PHP through PayPal at its published 3.0% conversion fee and 54,446.32 PHP to 56,034.33 PHP through Payoneer at its published 1.2% to 4%, at 63.01657 PHP per US dollar (ECB reference rate, November 6, 2026), before Upwork's own withdrawal fee.

What does PayPal charge to convert dollars in Philippines?

PayPal's Philippines fee page (last updated October 28, 2026) lists: "Converting balance, transfers, and payments received into another currency, ASIA PACIFIC (Philippines): 3.0%". Its bank withdrawal row: "PH: No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved) where PHP withdrawal amount is greater than 7000.00 PHP; 50.00 PHP if PHP withdrawal amount is less than 7000.00 PHP".

What does Payoneer charge to withdraw dollars to a local bank?

Payoneer's pricing page (last updated January 1, 2026) gives "1.2% – 4%" to withdraw to a bank account in a non-local currency with conversion, and says the fees that apply to you are on the Fees link in your account.
