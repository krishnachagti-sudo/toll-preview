# What Connects cost per proposal and per job won

At $0.15 a Connect (Upwork's own Connects page), a proposal that takes 8 Connects costs $1.20; at a 1-in-10 win rate each job won costs $12.00 in Connects. On 40 proposals with 4 wins of $500, Connects are 2.4% of what was billed, and 12.4% with a 10% service fee.

## What Upwork says about Connects

“Connects cost $0.15 (USD) each and are sold in bundles or a custom amount (10 minimum).”

“The amount of Connects needed to apply to a job is based on the project size, scope, market demand, and other factors.”

“Connects expire one year from the date of issue.”

40 proposals at 8 Connects each use 320 Connects, $48.00 at $0.15 a Connect ($1.20 a proposal); with 4 jobs won that is $12.00 per win, 2.4% of the $500.00 average contract, and 12.4% with the 10% service fee.

> An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it.
