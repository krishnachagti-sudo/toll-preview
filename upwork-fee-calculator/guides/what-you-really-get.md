# What Upwork really pays you

$800 billed on a 10% Upwork contract leaves $720.00 after the service fee; then comes Upwork's withdrawal fee for your method (shown on your Get Paid page), and the conversion: through PayPal at its published 3.0% that $720.00 reaches an Indian bank as about 65,595.49 INR, against 67,624.22 INR at the reference rate with no margin. At 0% it would be 72,883.88 INR, at 15% 61,951.30 INR.

## Steps

1. **The service fee.** Upwork takes the fee shown on the contract, 0% to 15%, from what the client paid: $800 at 10% leaves $720.00.
2. **The withdrawal fee.** Upwork charges a fee per withdrawal that depends on the method and your tax address. Its help pages could not be read here, so type the figure your Get Paid page shows.
3. **The conversion.** The provider converts at its own rate: PayPal adds 3.0% above its base rate in India, so $720.00 becomes about 65,595.49 INR rather than 67,624.22 INR at the reference rate.

## Questions

### How much do I get from $800 on Upwork at a 10% fee?

$720.00 after the service fee (Upwork's own example: a 10% contract billing $500 pays out $450), then less your withdrawal fee and the conversion: about 65,595.49 INR through PayPal to an Indian bank at 93.922526 INR per US dollar (ECB reference rate, November 6, 2026).

### Is the Upwork fee 10 percent?

Not always. Upwork's own calculator page says "Fees can vary between 0% and 15%"; your contract shows its fee.
