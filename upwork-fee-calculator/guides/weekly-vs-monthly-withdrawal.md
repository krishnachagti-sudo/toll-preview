# Weekly or monthly withdrawals?

A fixed fee is paid once per withdrawal, so withdrawing monthly instead of weekly pays it 12 times a year instead of 52: at Freelancer.com's $1.00 Express Withdrawal that saves $40.00 a year, at Wise's 6.11 USD to receive a wire $244.40, and at any fee you type, 40 times that fee (a $3.00 fee: $120.00).

## Your own fee

At $3.00 per withdrawal, withdrawing weekly costs $156.00 a year and monthly $36.00, so monthly saves $120.00 (0.65% vs 0.15% of $24,000.00 a year); the fee is under 1% of any withdrawal above $300.00.

## The trade-off

- Cash flow: a monthly withdrawal leaves up to a month's earnings waiting.
- Exchange rates move: one large conversion takes one day's rate; four small ones average four.
- Percentage fees (PayPal's 3.0%, Payoneer's 1.2% to 4%) cost the same however you batch; only fixed fees shrink. A fixed fee is under 1% of any withdrawal above 100 times the fee ($300.00 for $3.00).
- Upwork says payments can transfer "as frequently as every week".

> An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it. ## Questions

### Should I withdraw weekly or monthly?

Fewer, larger withdrawals pay a fixed fee fewer times: At Freelancer.com Express Withdrawal ($1.00), withdrawing weekly costs $52.00 a year and monthly $12.00, so monthly saves $40.00 (0.43% vs 0.1% of $12,000.00 a year); the fee is under 1% of any withdrawal above $100.00. You hold the balance longer in exchange.
