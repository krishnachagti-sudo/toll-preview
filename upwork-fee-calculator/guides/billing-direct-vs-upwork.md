# Staying on Upwork vs billing a client direct

Billing a client $50,000 a year through a 10% Upwork contract costs $5,000 in service fees; the same amount invoiced direct and collected by US ACH debit through Payoneer is published at 1%, $500, so the fee gap is $4,500 a year, before what you give up on Upwork and before Upwork's own terms on moving a client off the platform, which you should read first.

## The fee cost of staying, a year

Upwork's service fee at three contract levels against Payoneer's published 1% for a payer using US ACH bank debit (Payoneer pricing, last updated January 1, 2026). Card payments through Payoneer are "Up to 3.99% + 0.49 USD".

| Billed a year | Upwork at 5% | Upwork at 10% | Upwork at 15% | Payoneer ACH 1% |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $10,000.00 | $500.00 | $1,000.00 | $1,500.00 | $100.00 |
| $25,000.00 | $1,250.00 | $2,500.00 | $3,750.00 | $250.00 |
| $50,000.00 | $2,500.00 | $5,000.00 | $7,500.00 | $500.00 |
| $100,000.00 | $5,000.00 | $10,000.00 | $15,000.00 | $1,000.00 |

## What you give up

- Discovery: the client found you, or you found the client, through the marketplace.
- Whatever payment protection and dispute process your contract type has on Upwork; read Upwork's terms for yours.
- Your Job Success Score and reviews from this work, which future clients on Upwork see.

## Read the terms first

Upwork's Terms of Service and user agreement set rules on taking a relationship that started on Upwork off the platform. They could not be read from here, so nothing is quoted: read them in your account at [upwork.com/legal](https://www.upwork.com/legal) before you move a client.

## The break-even

Going direct saves the service fee less what the direct route costs: at a 10% fee and 1% collection, 9% of everything billed. At 3.99% + $0.49 by card, it is about 6% less the fixed $0.49 a payment. A client who would not have found you elsewhere is worth more than that gap.

> An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it.
