# Upwork payments to India: what reaches the bank in INR

From a $1,000 Upwork payment at a 10% service fee ($900.00 after the fee), a freelancer in India receives about 82,424.53 INR through Skydo after its $19 flat fee and GST, 81,994.37 INR through PayPal at its published 3.0% conversion fee and 81,149.06 INR to 83,515.91 INR through Payoneer at its published 1.2% to 4%, at 93.922526 INR per US dollar (ECB reference rate, November 6, 2026), before Upwork's own withdrawal fee.

## What is not in these numbers

- Upwork's own fee to withdraw by each method. It is on Upwork's help pages, which could not be read here; the calculator takes the figure your Get Paid page shows.
- PayPal's fee to receive the payment, if your account shows one (PayPal's merchant fee page). The bank withdrawal row for India: "Bank account, Standard: No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved)".
- Payoneer's fee to receive from a marketplace, which its page says "Varies by marketplace", and its $29.95 annual account fee under $6,000 a year.
- Your bank's own charges, and the gap between the provider's base rate and the reference rate.

> An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it. ## Questions

### How much of a $1,000 Upwork payment reaches a bank in India?

From a $1,000 Upwork payment at a 10% service fee ($900.00 after the fee), a freelancer in India receives about 82,424.53 INR through Skydo after its $19 flat fee and GST, 81,994.37 INR through PayPal at its published 3.0% conversion fee and 81,149.06 INR to 83,515.91 INR through Payoneer at its published 1.2% to 4%, at 93.922526 INR per US dollar (ECB reference rate, November 6, 2026), before Upwork's own withdrawal fee.

### What does PayPal charge to convert dollars in India?

PayPal's India fee page (last updated May 31, 2021) lists: "Converting balance and payments received into another currency: 3.0% above the base exchange rate (to form the transaction exchange rate)". Its bank withdrawal row: "Bank account, Standard: No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved)".

### What does Payoneer charge to withdraw dollars to a local bank?

Payoneer's pricing page (last updated January 1, 2026) gives "1.2% – 4%" to withdraw to a bank account in a non-local currency with conversion, and says the fees that apply to you are on the Fees link in your account.
