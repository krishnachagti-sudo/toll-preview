PayPal conversion

PayPal's conversion fee on dollars you receive, by country

PayPal's own fee pages add 3.0% above its base exchange rate when it converts dollars received into rupees, pesos, naira or pounds in India, the Philippines, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Nigeria and Egypt: $1,000 received in the Philippines becomes about 61,126.07 PHP at 63.01657 PHP per US dollar (ECB reference rate, November 6, 2026), not 63,016.57 PHP.

The rows on PayPal's pages

  • United States: "Currency Conversion Spread, All other transactions: 3.00%, or such other amount as may be disclosed to you during the transaction.". Bank: "Bank account, Standard: No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved)".
  • India: "Converting balance and payments received into another currency: 3.0% above the base exchange rate (to form the transaction exchange rate)". Bank: "Bank account, Standard: No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved)".
  • Philippines: "Converting balance, transfers, and payments received into another currency, ASIA PACIFIC (Philippines): 3.0%". Bank: "PH: No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved) where PHP withdrawal amount is greater than 7000.00 PHP; 50.00 PHP if PHP withdrawal amount is less than 7000.00 PHP".
  • Nepal: "Converting balance, transfers, and payments received into another currency, ASIA PACIFIC (Nepal): 3.0%". Bank: "All other markets (where available): No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved)".
  • Sri Lanka: "Converting balance, transfers, and payments received into another currency, ASIA PACIFIC (Sri Lanka): 3.0%". Bank: "All other markets (where available): No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved)".
  • Nigeria: "Converting balance, transfers, and payments received into another currency, MIDDLE EAST & AFRICA (Nigeria): 3.0%". Bank: "All other markets (where available): No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved)".
  • Egypt: "Converting balance, transfers, and payments received into another currency, MIDDLE EAST & AFRICA (Egypt): 3.0% (read on PayPal's English multi-market page, which lists Egypt (EG); the EG page is in Arabic)". Bank: "EG: 125.00 EGP (per EGP withdrawal)".

Pakistan is not in the market list of PayPal's consumer fee pages read (paypal.com/pk redirects to the India page). Bangladesh is in PayPal's market list but in none of the regions of its conversion table; neither gets a PayPal figure here.

An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it. Not tax or financial advice.

Questions

What is PayPal's currency conversion fee for freelancers in India?

PayPal's India fee page (last updated May 31, 2021): "Converting balance and payments received into another currency: 3.0% above the base exchange rate (to form the transaction exchange rate)".

And in the Philippines?

PayPal's page for the Philippines and other markets (last updated October 28, 2026) lists 3.0% for Asia Pacific, which names the Philippines, under "Converting balance, transfers, and payments received into another currency".
