# Upwork payments to Egypt: what reaches the bank in EGP

From a $1,000 Upwork payment at a 10% service fee ($900.00 after the fee), a freelancer in Egypt receives about 45,174.97 EGP through PayPal at its published 3.0% conversion fee and 44,832.96 EGP to 46,140.59 EGP through Payoneer at its published 1.2% to 4%, at 51.89 EGP per US dollar (US Treasury reporting rate as of September 30, 2026), before Upwork's own withdrawal fee.

## What is not in these numbers

- Upwork's own fee to withdraw by each method. It is on Upwork's help pages, which could not be read here; the calculator takes the figure your Get Paid page shows.
- PayPal's fee to receive the payment, if your account shows one (PayPal's merchant fee page). The bank withdrawal row for Egypt: "EG: 125.00 EGP (per EGP withdrawal)".
- Payoneer's fee to receive from a marketplace, which its page says "Varies by marketplace", and its $29.95 annual account fee under $6,000 a year.
- Your bank's own charges, and the gap between the provider's base rate and the reference rate.

> An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it. ### What does PayPal charge to convert dollars in Egypt?
