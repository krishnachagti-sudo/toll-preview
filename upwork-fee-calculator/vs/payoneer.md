# Payoneer for Upwork earnings

Payoneer's pricing page (last updated January 1, 2026) gives 1.2% to 4% to withdraw dollars to a bank in another currency and says the marketplace fee "varies by marketplace"; on $1,000 billed on Upwork at 10% that is about 81,149.06 INR to 83,515.91 INR in India, against 81,994.37 INR through PayPal at its 3.0%.

## What Payoneer says (read November 8, 2026)

“To a bank account in the recipient's non-local currency (with currency conversion): 1.2% – 4%”

“From marketplaces and integrated platforms: Varies by marketplace”

“To a bank account in the same country as yours, in local currency (in supported countries), e.g. USA to USA, USD: 1.50 USD”

“Annual account fee: 29.95 USD. Only applies if account receives less than 6,000.00 USD or equivalent in any 12 consecutive months.”

“The pricing and fees applicable to you are provided for review during account registration and are also available any time from the Fees link in your Payoneer account.”

## $1,000 to India, both ends of the range

$1,000.00 billed at a 10% Upwork fee leaves $900.00; withdrawn through Payoneer to a INR bank account at Payoneer's published 1.2% to 4%, about 81,149.06 INR to 83,515.91 INR arrives at 93.922526 INR per dollar (86.39% to 88.91% of the gross at that rate).

## What this calculator adds

- One chain from what the client paid to what your bank receives: the service fee, the withdrawal fee, the provider's fee and its conversion margin, at a dated ECB or US Treasury reference rate.
- Every fee quoted from the company's own page with the date read; a fee we could not read there (Upwork's own withdrawal fees) is never assumed, you type it.
- The rate to bill to keep a target, Connects per job won, and fixed fees a year by how often you withdraw.
- Free on the calculator's page; $0.002 a calculation over the API or MCP; a statement of many payments with its CSV is $0.001 a payment ($0.25 at least), never more than $2.49 a month.

## When Payoneer is the better choice

When you are paid on several platforms and want one balance to collect them in, or when your account's Fees page puts you at the low end of the range.

> An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it.
