# Wise for Upwork earnings

Wise's pricing pages say receiving US dollars by a domestic (non-wire) payment is free, a USD wire costs 6.11 USD, and converting costs "from 0.23%" depending on the currency (read November 8, 2026); at that lowest fee, $1,000 billed on Upwork at 10% would reach the Philippines as about 56,584.47 PHP, but Wise's own calculator is the source for your currency's fee.

## What Wise says (read November 8, 2026)

“Receiving domestic payments (non-Swift / non-wire) Receive AUD, CAD, EUR, GBP, HUF, NZD, PHP, SGD, USD: Free”

“Receiving USD wire and Swift payments, fixed fee per payment: 6.11 USD”

“Converting money: Fee varies by currency, From 0.23%”

Whether Upwork accepts your Wise account details for a given withdrawal method is on Upwork's help pages, which could not be read here.

## At Wise's lowest published conversion fee

$1,000.00 billed at a 10% Upwork fee leaves $900.00; converted through Wise at a 0.23% conversion fee, about 56,584.47 PHP reaches the bank at 63.01657 PHP per dollar (89.79% of the gross at that rate).

## What this calculator adds

- One chain from what the client paid to what your bank receives: the service fee, the withdrawal fee, the provider's fee and its conversion margin, at a dated ECB or US Treasury reference rate.
- Every fee quoted from the company's own page with the date read; a fee we could not read there (Upwork's own withdrawal fees) is never assumed, you type it.
- The rate to bill to keep a target, Connects per job won, and fixed fees a year by how often you withdraw.
- Free on the calculator's page; $0.002 a calculation over the API or MCP; a statement of many payments with its CSV is $0.001 a payment ($0.25 at least), never more than $2.49 a month.

## When Wise is the better choice

Wise is the source for its own live rate and fee: check its calculator for your currency before you choose a route, and use its figure in this calculator's margin field.

> An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it.
