# Payoneer fees on Upwork earnings, to eight currencies

Payoneer's pricing page gives 1.2% to 4% to withdraw dollars to a bank account in another currency, so $1,000 billed on Upwork at a 10% fee ($900.00 after it) reaches a bank in Pakistan as about 239,241.60 PKR to 246,219.48 PKR at 276.9 PKR per US dollar (US Treasury reporting rate as of September 30, 2026); your account's Fees page shows where in that range you are.

## Payoneer's own words

“To a bank account in the recipient's non-local currency (with currency conversion): 1.2% – 4%”

“From marketplaces and integrated platforms: Varies by marketplace”

“Annual account fee: 29.95 USD. Only applies if account receives less than 6,000.00 USD or equivalent in any 12 consecutive months.”

“The pricing and fees applicable to you are provided for review during account registration and are also available any time from the Fees link in your Payoneer account.”

> An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it.
