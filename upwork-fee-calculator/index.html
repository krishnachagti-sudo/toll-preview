What you get

The service fee on your contract, the withdrawal fee, your payout provider's fee and its conversion margin, in one chain from what the client paid to what your bank receives in your currency. Every fee is quoted from the company's own page with the date it was read. A fee we could not read there, such as Upwork's own withdrawal fees, is never guessed: you type the figure your account shows.

Free on this page. Over the API or MCP a calculation costs $0.002, and a statement of many payments with its CSV costs $0.001 a payment ($0.25 at least).

Upwork

0% to 15% on all earnings, included in what clients see. Connects cost $0.15 each.

Upwork Fee Calculator

Read the full rules

"Fees can vary between 0% and 15%", charged "on all earnings, including bonuses", and "included in the amounts clients see". Its own example: a 10% contract billing $500 pays out $450. Connects cost $0.15 each, 10 at least (Upwork Connects Calculator).

Upwork's withdrawal fees and client fees are on help pages that could not be read here, so the calculator asks for your withdrawal fee rather than assume one.

PayPal, Payoneer, Wise, Skydo

PayPal adds 3.0% to convert, Payoneer 1.2% to 4%, Wise from 0.23%, Skydo $19 or $29 a payment.

Payoneer pricing Wise receiving fees

Read the full rules

PayPal adds 3.0% above its base rate when it converts payments received in India, the Philippines, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Nigeria and Egypt, and 3.00% for US accounts. Payoneer publishes 1.2% to 4% to withdraw with conversion. Wise receives US dollars free (6.11 USD for a wire) and converts from 0.23%. Skydo charges $19 up to $2,000 and $29 up to $10,000.

Sources: PayPal consumer fees for the US, India and the Philippines and other markets, Skydo, and the ECB and US Treasury rates.

Freelancer.com

10% or $5 on fixed price, 10% on hourly. Withdrawals cost $1.00, or $25.00 by wire.

Freelancer.com fees and charges

Read the full rules

10% or $5, whichever is greater, on a fixed-price project; 10% on hourly. Employers pay 3% (at least $3 on fixed price). Withdrawals cost $1.00 (Express, PayPal, Skrill, Payoneer card) or $25.00 by international wire, with a $50 minimum after fees.

Price

Free here, per call over the API.

The free $0.25 each month on a signed-in account covers a statement of up to 250 payments. This tool never costs more than $2.49 in a month.
