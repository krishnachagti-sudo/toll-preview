# What to bill on Upwork to keep your target rate

To keep $50.00 an hour after a 10% Upwork service fee, bill $55.56 an hour. The formula is the rate you want to keep divided by one minus the service fee, rounded up to the cent; at 15% the same $50 needs $58.82, and with PayPal's 3% conversion on top of a 10% fee, $57.28.

## Steps

1. **Take your contract's fee.** Upwork shows the service fee, from 0% to 15%, on the contract.
2. **Divide.** Bill = what you want to keep / (1 - fee). At 10%: $50 / 0.9 = $55.56, rounded up to the cent.
3. **Cover conversion too.** If PayPal converts at 3% on top, divide again by (1 - 0.03): $50 needs $57.28 an hour.
