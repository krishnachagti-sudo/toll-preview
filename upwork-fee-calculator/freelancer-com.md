# Freelancer.com fees, worked out

On a $300 fixed-price project Freelancer.com takes $30.00 (10%, or $5.00 if that is more), leaving $270.00; withdrawing by Express Withdrawal costs $1.00, so $269.00 comes out ($245.00 by international wire). The employer pays a further 3%, at least $3.00, on top.

## Questions

### How much does Freelancer.com charge freelancers?

"The fee for fixed price projects is 10% or $5.00 USD, whichever is greater, and 10% for hourly projects." (Freelancer.com Fees and Charges, read November 8, 2026).

### What does it cost to withdraw from Freelancer.com?

$1.00 by Express Withdrawal, PayPal, Skrill or Payoneer Debit Card, and $25.00 by international wire. "We impose a minimum withdrawal, after fees, of USD $50."
