# Upwork payments to Nigeria: what reaches the bank in NGN

From a $1,000 Upwork payment at a 10% service fee ($900.00 after the fee), a freelancer in Nigeria receives about 1,158,278.94 NGN through PayPal at its published 3.0% conversion fee and 1,146,337.92 NGN to 1,179,772.78 NGN through Payoneer at its published 1.2% to 4%, at 1326.78 NGN per US dollar (US Treasury reporting rate as of September 30, 2026), before Upwork's own withdrawal fee.

## What is not in these numbers

- Upwork's own fee to withdraw by each method. It is on Upwork's help pages, which could not be read here; the calculator takes the figure your Get Paid page shows.
- PayPal's fee to receive the payment, if your account shows one (PayPal's merchant fee page). The bank withdrawal row for Nigeria: "All other markets (where available): No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved)".
- Payoneer's fee to receive from a marketplace, which its page says "Varies by marketplace", and its $29.95 annual account fee under $6,000 a year.
- Your bank's own charges, and the gap between the provider's base rate and the reference rate.

> An estimate from the fees each company publishes, read on its own page on the date shown; your contract and your account's fee page win. Upwork's own withdrawal fees could not be read on Upwork's pages, so they are left out rather than guessed. Providers convert at their own base rate; the dated reference rate stands in for it. ## Questions

### How much of a $1,000 Upwork payment reaches a bank in Nigeria?

### What does PayPal charge to convert dollars in Nigeria?

PayPal's Nigeria fee page (last updated October 28, 2026) lists: "Converting balance, transfers, and payments received into another currency, MIDDLE EAST & AFRICA (Nigeria): 3.0%". Its bank withdrawal row: "All other markets (where available): No Fee (when no currency conversion is involved)".

### What does Payoneer charge to withdraw dollars to a local bank?

Payoneer's pricing page (last updated January 1, 2026) gives "1.2% – 4%" to withdraw to a bank account in a non-local currency with conversion, and says the fees that apply to you are on the Fees link in your account.
