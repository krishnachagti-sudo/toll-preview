# An A2X alternative for Amazon settlements

A2X for Amazon starts at US$29 a month (Mini, up to 200 orders); here one settlement costs $1.00, a typical month of about 2.17 settlements $2.17, and no month more than $19.00. A2X posts every settlement to your books for you; this reconciler checks and splits the report you upload.

## A2X's Amazon prices

| Plan | Price and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Mini | US$29 a month, up to 200 orders, 1 Amazon Merchant ID, 3 months history |
| Starter | US$59 a month, up to 1,000 orders, 12 months history |
| Standard | US$79 a month, up to 5,000 orders, 24 months history |
| Standard 10k | US$159 a month, up to 10,000 orders |

A2X posts to QuickBooks Online, Xero or NetSuite. Its trial: "Try A2X free with all features".

Here: $1.00 a settlement, at most $19 a month, and the check (proof, bank tie, month split) is free. A2X Mini counts orders and covers one Merchant ID with 3 months of history.

## When A2X is the better choice

- You want every settlement posted for you, with no download or upload.
- You need cost of goods sold or inventory locations, which A2X lists in its plans.
- You sell on several channels and want one subscription for all of them.
- Your accountant already works in A2X.

## When this is enough

- A catch-up or clean-up of past settlements, without a plan's history limit.
- A second check of a settlement a connector posted, proved row by row.
- A small seller who closes the books once a month.
