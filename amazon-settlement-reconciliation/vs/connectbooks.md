# ConnectBooks compared, for Amazon settlements

ConnectBooks starts at $24 a month (Lite, 1 store, up to 200 orders), about $11.08 a settlement at Amazon's 14-day cycle; here a settlement is $1.00 and a month at most $19.00.

## ConnectBooks' plans

| Plan | Price and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Lite | $24 a month, 1 store, up to 200 orders a month |
| Gold | from $149 a month, 1 store, up to 10,000 orders |
| Platinum | from $349 a month |
| Extra stores | Each store after the first adds $25 a month |

Every plan is free for 30 days. What it syncs: settlements synced to QuickBooks or Xero as a daily summary.

## When ConnectBooks is better

- You want settlements synced every day without uploading anything.
- You want SKU profit and inventory, which its higher plans include.
- You want a bookkeeper to set it up with you (its Gold plan).

## When this is enough

- An independent proof of a settlement you already have, row by row against total-amount.
- One settlement or a catch-up, without a monthly plan.
- A month-end split you can hand your accountant as journals and CSVs.
