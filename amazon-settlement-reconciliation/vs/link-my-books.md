# Link My Books compared, for Amazon settlements

Link My Books prices by rolling average orders and sales channels in a calculator (its plan prices were not read here); its trial is 14 days with 90 days of settlements. Here a year of 26 settlements costs $26.00, and any one month at most $19.00.

## What Link My Books says about its plans

- 14-Day Free Trial, no card required
- History: it imports the last 90 days of settlements during the free trial; up to 24 months on paid plans.
- Plan size: rolling average monthly order count; upgraded automatically 7 days after an email if over the plan.
- Channels: 1 Amazon Merchant ID counts as one sales channel, with no limit on marketplaces.
- Prices: plan prices are shown by an interactive calculator and were not read.

## When Link My Books is better

- You want each settlement posted to Xero or QuickBooks for you as it closes.
- You sell in several Amazon marketplaces and want them in one connection.
- You want VAT handled in the posting rules, set up with their team.

## When this is enough

- Pay per settlement with no subscription and no automatic plan upgrades.
- Settlements older than a plan's history window.
- A row-by-row proof of one settlement and its bank deposit.
