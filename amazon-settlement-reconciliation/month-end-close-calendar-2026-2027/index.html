Amazon month-end close calendar, November 2026 to December 2027

From November 2026 to December 2027 there are 14 month ends; on the sample's 14-day cycle (from October 24, 2026), 13 of them fall inside a settlement that has to be split, and a deposit Amazon sends on November 30, 2026 can take until December 7, 2026 to reach the bank (up to five business days, Amazon says).

Business days here are Monday to Friday, without bank holidays. It can take up to five business days for your money to appear in your bank account after we initiate payment. Your own cycle depends on your account; the reconciler splits whatever your settlement's posted dates are.

Each month end

  • Pull the settlement that straddles it and split it by posted date.
  • Tie the deposit that lands in the first business days of the new month to the settlement it belongs to.
  • Statements only reflects transactions that were released and included in payout during a settlement period. Deferred transactions are not included.
