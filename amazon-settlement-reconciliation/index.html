How it works

  • Proved to the cent: the rows must add up to total-amount, and the total to what reached the bank. Any gap is shown, never absorbed.
  • Split by posted date: each month gets its own journal, dated its last day.
  • Every value shown: each amount type goes to a line by a named rule, and you can move it.

What it does not cover

A settlement lists only released transactions, so earnings by shipment date need Amazon's Date Range Transaction report. It is not accounting or tax advice.

Read how it works in full

Proved to the cent

Amazon puts every amount in one column, amount, and the settlement's total in total-amount. The rows must add up to the total; any gap is shown, never absorbed. The total is then tied to the bank lines you give, and a gap gets Amazon's own reasons: a second payer, up to five business days in transit, a negative balance or a currency conversion.

Split by posted date

Each row goes to the month of its posted date (or the period before your cut-off). Each month gets its own journal, dated the month's last day, and the clearing account holds exactly the settlement until the deposit is matched. The settlement lists released transactions, by release date.

Every value shown, nothing guessed

Amazon does not publish a list of amount-type and amount-description values. Endwise's word rules place each one (principal to sales, commission to selling fees, reserve to a balance-sheet account), the list shows every value with its line and rule, and you can move any of them. A value no rule knows goes to Other, by name.

Please read
  • Files are processed in memory and not kept.
  • This is arithmetic on Amazon's own settlement file. It is not accounting or tax advice: confirm how fees, sales tax and the reserve are booked with your accountant.
  • Amazon publishes the settlement report's columns but not a list of its amount-type and amount-description values. Each value in your file is shown with the line it was booked to, by Endwise's word rules, and you can move it.
  • A settlement lists only released transactions, by the date they were released (Amazon's help). For earnings by shipment date, Amazon points accrual users to the Date Range Transaction report.
Prices
  • Check (the proof, the bank tie and the split per month): free.
  • Reconcile (the lines per month, the journals and the import files): $1.00 per settlement; a repeat for the same file is free for 7 days, and a larger one pays only the difference.
  • At most $19.00 a calendar month for this tool; after that it is free until the month ends.
