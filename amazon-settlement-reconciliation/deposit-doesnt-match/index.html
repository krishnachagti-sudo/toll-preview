Why the Amazon deposit does not match the settlement

In the sample, the settlement's total-amount is $188.14 but the first bank line is $150.00, $38.14 short; Amazon's help says one payment can come from two payers, and the second deposit of $38.14 closes the gap.

Eight causes and where to look

CauseWhat Amazon saysWhere to look
Two payersYour bank statement might show that Amazon's payments come from more than one source: Amazon Payments, Inc. and Amazon Services LLC.Two bank lines on or near the same day that add up to total-amount.
Still in transitIt can take up to five business days for your money to appear in your bank account after we initiate payment.deposit-date in the report's first row, against the bank date.
Reserve held or releasedAmazon's account level reserve is the amount of money that is reserved to ensure that you have enough funds to fulfill any financial obligations, such as refunds, claims, or chargebacks not covered by Delivery date-based reserve policies.Reserve rows in the report (amount-description names a reserve).
Deferred transactionsStatements only reflects transactions that were released and included in payout during a settlement period. Deferred transactions are not included.Seller Central, Payments: the deferred transactions in the Transaction view.
A negative balance carriedA negative balance is when the amount of fees you owe and refunds you have issued exceeds your sales during a settlement period. If your negative balance is not resolved by your next settlement, you will need to make a payment.A negative total-amount, or the next settlement starting below zero.
Currency conversionA settlement in one currency paid into a bank account in another arrives converted.The report's currency column against the bank account's currency.
A bank account changeWhenever you add or update your bank account information, a mandatory three-day security hold takes effect.Seller Central, Settings, Deposit methods.
Express payoutAmazon offers Express payout to receive transfers within twenty-four hours, so money can leave before the settlement's deposit-date.Seller Central, Payments: the payouts list.

The rows still add up

A gap with the bank is different from a gap inside the report. Here the 36 rows add up to $188.14, the total-amount, so the report is whole and the difference is on the bank side.

Questions

Why is my Amazon deposit less than the settlement?

One common reason: Your bank statement might show that Amazon's payments come from more than one source: Amazon Payments, Inc. and Amazon Services LLC. In the sample, the first deposit is $150.00 and the second, $38.14, makes up the $188.14 total.

How long can an Amazon deposit take?

It can take up to five business days for your money to appear in your bank account after we initiate payment.
