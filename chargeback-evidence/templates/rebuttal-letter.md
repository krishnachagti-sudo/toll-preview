# Chargeback rebuttal letter template

A chargeback rebuttal letter states the payment, the reason code and its claim, the facts that answer it, and the exhibits in order, in a neutral tone. The sample below answers a $89.00 Visa 13.1 (not received) dispute in 166 words and 5 paragraphs; the tool writes yours from your facts.

- Words in the sample: 166

## The sample letter

```
Sample Goods Co. (made up)
support@example.com
November 8, 2026
Re: Stripe dispute du_SAMPLE0001
Amount: $89.00, charged October 2, 2026 as "SAMPLEGOODS"

We contest this dispute. Jordan Sample paid $89.00 on October 2, 2026 for order #1042 (one canvas weekender bag).

The dispute is filed under Visa reason code 13.1 (Merchandise/Services Not Received): the customer says they did not receive what they paid for.

The customer did not contact us before disputing the charge. We shipped the order on October 3, 2026 by USPS, tracking number 9400100000000000000000. The carrier recorded delivery on October 6, 2026 to 12 Example Lane, Austin, TX 78701, US. The delivery address matches the billing address the card issuer verified.

Enclosed, in order: Exhibit A: Proof of delivery. USPS delivery record to the order address, with the delivery photo. Exhibit B: Receipt or order confirmation. Order confirmation emailed to the customer on October 2, 2026.

We ask the card issuer to reverse this dispute on the records enclosed.

Sample Goods Co. (made up)
```

> Made-up seller and customer. Stripe's advice: factual, professional and concise; no long explanation.

## The fact paragraph for other dispute types

| Dispute | What the tool writes from your facts |
| --- | --- |
| Not received (physical) | We shipped the order on October 3, 2026 by UPS. The carrier recorded delivery on October 6, 2026 to the address on the order. |
| Not received (digital) | The customer logged in to or downloaded the purchase on October 2, 2026, after the payment. |
| Subscription cancelled | Our cancellation policy was shown to the customer on the sign-up page before payment. The customer asked to cancel on October 5, 2026, effective October 31, 2026 under the terms shown at sign-up. This charge, on October 2, 2026, was made before the cancellation took effect. |
| Fraud or unrecognized | The same customer made 3 earlier payments with us that were not disputed. The payment was authenticated with 3D Secure. |
| Credit not processed | We refunded $89.00 on October 10, 2026. |
| Duplicate | The other payment (ch\_SAMPLE2) was for a separate purchase; receipts for both are enclosed. |

## Where the letter goes

- Stripe: paste it in the Additional information text field (uncategorized\_text); text fields share a 150,000-character limit.
- Shopify: upload it as its own PDF.
- PayPal: upload it as a PDF with evidence type OTHER.

## Steps

1. **The payment.** Who paid, how much, when, for which order.
2. **The claim.** The network, the reason code and what it claims.
3. **The facts.** Only what your records show, with dates: shipped, delivered, used, cancelled, refunded.
4. **The exhibits.** Each enclosed file by letter, in order, with one line on what it shows.
5. **The ask.** One sentence asking the issuer to reverse the dispute.
