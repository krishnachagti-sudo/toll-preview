# Is it worth fighting a small chargeback?

A $35 Stripe dispute is worth countering above a 30% chance of winning: the $15 received fee is lost either way, and countering adds $15 that comes back only if you win. Counting 30 minutes of your time at $40 an hour and a $2.90 packet, the bar rises (table below). Shopify charges nothing extra to respond.

- $35 on Stripe: fight above: 30%

## Break-even by amount (Stripe, US)

| Amount | Fees only | Extra cost with time and packet | With time and packet |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $20.00 | 42.9% | $37.90 | 108.3% |
| $35.00 | 30% | $37.90 | 75.8% |
| $50.00 | 23.1% | $37.90 | 58.3% |
| $75.00 | 16.7% | $37.90 | 42.1% |
| $100.00 | 13% | $37.90 | 33% |
| $150.00 | 9.1% | $37.90 | 23% |
| $200.00 | 7% | $37.90 | 17.6% |
| $300.00 | 4.8% | $37.90 | 12% |

The rule: `break-even = extra cost of fighting / (amount + fees returned on a win) = $15.00 / $50.00 = 30%`.

For a sense of the odds, Stripe's own ranking runs from 5% (one dot) to 60% (five dots) for disputes countered with relevant evidence.

> Visa and Mastercard compliance cases on Stripe add a $500 network amount, returned if you win.
