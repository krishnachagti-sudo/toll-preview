# Response deadlines: Stripe, Shopify and PayPal

For a dispute opened Monday, November 9, 2026: Stripe and Shopify usually allow 7 to 21 days (due between November 16 and November 30, 2026; the exact date is on the dispute), PayPal gives 10 days for a card chargeback (November 19) and 20 days to settle a PayPal dispute (November 29). A missed date loses the dispute.

- PayPal chargeback window: 10 days

| Platform | Window | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stripe | 7 to 21 days | November 16, 2026 to November 30, 2026 |
| Shopify Payments | 7 to 21 days | November 16, 2026 to November 30, 2026 |
| PayPal chargeback | 10 days | November 19, 2026 |
| PayPal dispute | 20 days | November 29, 2026 |

- Stripe: "usually 7 to 21 days, depending on the card network"; evidence\_details.due\_by on the dispute is the date.
- Shopify: the due date is on the chargeback; Shopify submits what is saved on that day.
- PayPal: seller\_response\_due\_date on the case; a case not answered by then closes in the buyer's favor.
