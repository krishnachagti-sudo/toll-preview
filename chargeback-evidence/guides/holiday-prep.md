# Before the holiday disputes arrive

A Visa card-absent fraud dispute (10.4) can arrive up to 120 days after the payment is processed, so a Black Friday order of November 27, 2026 can be disputed until about March 27, 2027. Save 8 kinds of record from each order now, including the device ID or IP and login that Visa CE 3.0 compares.

- Kinds of record: 8

## Keep from every order

| Record | What to keep |
| --- | --- |
| Proof of delivery | The carrier record showing delivery status and the full delivery address (it must match the order), plus the signature or delivery photo where the carrier has one. |
| Signature or ID at pickup or delivery | A signed delivery or pickup record, or the ID the customer showed, linking the cardholder to the goods. |
| Receipt or order confirmation | The receipt or confirmation sent to the customer: date, amount, items and the address it went to. |
| Messages with the customer | Only the relevant emails, chats or dated call notes, combined into one file. If the customer never contacted you before disputing, say so in the letter. |
| Refund or return policy, and where it was shown | Only the relevant section of the policy, plus a screenshot of where the customer saw it before paying (for example the checkout page). |
| Cancellation policy, and where it was shown | The cancellation terms as shown to the customer and a screenshot of where they agreed to them before paying. |
| Access or download log | Server or account records of logins, downloads or use after the payment, with dates, times and IP addresses. |
| Earlier undisputed payments by the same customer | Earlier payments on the same card that were not disputed, with the matching device ID or IP address plus login, delivery address or email. |

## And on your store

- A statement descriptor customers recognize (Stripe lists this first to prevent fraud and unrecognized disputes).
- Receipts sent at payment, so customers remember what they paid for.
- The refund and cancellation policy shown before payment, with a screenshot of where.
