How to win a chargeback

Match the evidence to the reason code, respond once before the due date (usually 7 to 21 days on Stripe and Shopify, 10 on a PayPal chargeback), keep the files within the limits (4.5 MB on Stripe) and skip disputes you cannot evidence. Even Stripe's best-ranked disputes win about 60% of the time.

What Visa looks for

  • Evidence that clearly links the transaction to the cardholder.
  • Evidence of the cardholder's participation in, or benefit from, the transaction.
  • Confirmation it was processed, fulfilled and billed as agreed.
  • In English, or with an English translation; more documents do not mean a better outcome.

Please read

  • Not legal advice. The card issuer (or PayPal, for a PayPal claim) decides every dispute; no packet guarantees a win.
  • Rules, limits and fees are as read on the dates shown. Card networks change their rules in April and October, and platforms change fees with notice.
  • Your platform's due date is the deadline. The tool counts from the date you enter; it does not read your account.
  • Files you upload are processed in memory to build the packet and are not kept. Only send evidence you are allowed to share with the card issuer.
  • Mastercard, American Express and Discover codes are shown as Stripe publishes them; those networks' own rule books were not read.

Steps

  1. Read the reason code. The code (Visa 13.1, Mastercard 4853) decides what evidence counts. Evidence for another claim is ignored.
  2. Check the due date. Stripe and Shopify usually allow 7 to 21 days; PayPal 10 days for a card chargeback. A missed date loses the dispute.
  3. Decide if it is worth it. On Stripe countering costs $15 more, returned if you win; on a $40 dispute it pays above a 27.3% chance.
  4. Gather the evidence, strongest first. Proof of delivery for not-received, the listing for not-as-described, the cancellation terms and dates for subscriptions.
  5. Fit the limits. One file per evidence type; 4.5 MB combined on Stripe (a 19-page limit for Mastercard); 2 MB per file and 4 MB total on Shopify.
  6. Write a short letter. The payment, the claim, the facts with dates, the exhibits in order.
  7. Submit once. Stripe and Shopify take one submission; check it before you send.
