# Visa 13.1: Merchandise/Services Not Received

Visa 13.1 (Merchandise/Services Not Received): the cardholder says goods or services they paid for were not received by the expected date (or were not available for pickup). Stripe files it under "Product not received". The strongest evidence for a physical product is proof of delivery, shipping details and signature or ID at pickup or delivery.

- Stripe category: Product not received
- Issuer time limit: 120 days

## What it means

The cardholder says goods or services they paid for were not received by the expected date (or were not available for pickup).

## What Visa says to show

- Proof of delivery with the full delivery address matching the order, and the courier's delivery status
- A recipient signature or a delivery photo where you have one
- Delivery confirmation sent to the email or phone the customer gave at purchase
- For services: records, logs or a signed acknowledgement that the cardholder received the service
- For future services: proof the service is still available and you are ready to provide it

## Watch for

- Visa says you stay responsible for delivery even when a courier or customs causes the failure.
- The cardholder does not have to accept another date or a replacement service.
- Visa's own guide heads this section "Invalid Data" by mistake; the text is about non-receipt.

| 7 | Delivery not due yet, or held by customs | The agreed delivery date shown at checkout (still in the future), or the customs hold record. | uncategorized\_file | PROOF\_OF\_EXPECTED\_DELIVERY\_DATE |

## Time limit

The issuer may raise it up to 120 calendar days from the Transaction Processing Date or from the last date the cardholder expected to receive the goods or services, with waiting periods in some cases. (Visa Core Rules, Table 11-93 (24 October 2026)) Your own deadline to respond is shorter: Stripe and Shopify usually allow 7 to 21 days, PayPal 10 days for a card chargeback.
