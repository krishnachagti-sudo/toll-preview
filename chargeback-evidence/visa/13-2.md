# Visa 13.2: Cancelled Recurring Transaction

Visa 13.2 (Cancelled Recurring Transaction): the cardholder says you kept billing a subscription, membership or other recurring charge after they cancelled or withdrew permission. Stripe files it under "Subscription canceled". The strongest evidence for a physical product is cancellation policy, and where it was shown, why this charge stands after the claimed cancellation and renewal notices and use after the claimed cancellation.

- Stripe category: Subscription canceled
- Issuer time limit: 120 days

## What it means

The cardholder says you kept billing a subscription, membership or other recurring charge after they cancelled or withdrew permission.

## What Visa says to show

- The date the cancellation request arrived and the effective cancellation date you told the customer
- That this charge falls before the effective date, or covers service already used
- Records of use until a clearly communicated end date
- If no cancellation was received, proof none was on record when the charge was made

## Watch for

- Charges after a valid cancellation are generally invalid; respond only when timing or use supports the charge.
- Visa's example: cancelled on April 22 with use allowed to April 30 makes April 30 the cancellation date.

## Time limit

The issuer may raise it up to 120 calendar days from the Transaction Processing Date. (Visa Core Rules, Table 11-100 (24 October 2026)) Your own deadline to respond is shorter: Stripe and Shopify usually allow 7 to 21 days, PayPal 10 days for a card chargeback.
