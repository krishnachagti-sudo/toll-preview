# Visa 13.7: Cancelled Merchandise/Services

Visa 13.7 (Cancelled Merchandise/Services): the cardholder says they cancelled goods or services but were still charged, or did not get the refund due after cancelling. Stripe files it under "Credit not processed". The strongest evidence for a physical product is proof a refund or credit was already issued, refund or return policy, and where it was shown and why no refund (or no further refund) is due. The issuer must wait 15 calendar days after the return or cancellation, then may raise it up to 120 calendar days from the Transaction Processing Date or from the date the goods or services were received or expected (never more than 540 days after processing).

- Stripe category: Credit not processed
- Issuer time limit: 120 days

## What it means

The cardholder says they cancelled goods or services but were still charged, or did not get the refund due after cancelling.

## What Visa says to show

- That the cancellation did not meet the conditions or timing you disclosed at purchase
- That the charge or fee is allowed by the disclosed cancellation policy
- That a refund was issued under the disclosed terms (date, amount and method)
- For guaranteed reservations: the cancellation terms and that the no-show fee was charged correctly

## Watch for

- Cancellation terms count only if disclosed before purchase.
- The response is about policy, timing and how it was applied, not about the quality of the goods.

## Time limit

The issuer must wait 15 calendar days after the return or cancellation, then may raise it up to 120 calendar days from the Transaction Processing Date or from the date the goods or services were received or expected (never more than 540 days after processing). (Visa Core Rules, Table 11-132 (24 October 2026)) Your own deadline to respond is shorter: Stripe and Shopify usually allow 7 to 21 days, PayPal 10 days for a card chargeback.
