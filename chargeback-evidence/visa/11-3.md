# Visa 11.3: No Authorization / Late Presentment

Visa 11.3 (No Authorization / Late Presentment): the payment was processed without a valid authorization, or was settled outside the time allowed or for more than was authorized. Stripe files it under "General". The strongest evidence for a physical product is receipt or order confirmation, messages with the customer and proof of delivery. The issuer may raise it up to 75 calendar days from the Transaction Processing Date.

- Stripe category: General
- Issuer time limit: 75 days

## What it means

The payment was processed without a valid authorization, or was settled outside the time allowed or for more than was authorized.

## What Visa says to show

- The authorization approval code and date, showing it was obtained for this transaction
- A record that the transaction was completed and submitted within the allowed time
- For estimated and incremental authorizations: each request with the right indicators, one transaction ID, and the dates, amounts and approval codes

## Watch for

- This is judged on authorization steps, not on whether the customer got the goods. Delivery proof does not answer it.
- Visa notes the dispute amount may be limited to the part that was not properly authorized.

## Time limit

The issuer may raise it up to 75 calendar days from the Transaction Processing Date. (Visa Core Rules, Table 11-50 (24 October 2026)) Your own deadline to respond is shorter: Stripe and Shopify usually allow 7 to 21 days, PayPal 10 days for a card chargeback.
