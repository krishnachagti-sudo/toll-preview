# Visa 13.3: Not as Described or Defective Merchandise/Services

Visa 13.3 (Not as Described or Defective Merchandise/Services): the cardholder says what they received was materially different from the description, damaged, defective, or did not perform as reasonably expected. Stripe files it under "Product unacceptable". The strongest evidence for a physical product is how the product was described, messages with the customer and refund or return policy, and where it was shown. The issuer may raise it up to 120 calendar days from the Transaction Processing Date or from the date the cardholder received the goods or services (never more than 540 days after processing), or 60 days from the first cardholder notice where earlier negotiation with you is shown.

- Stripe category: Product unacceptable
- Issuer time limit: 120 days

## What it means

The cardholder says what they received was materially different from the description, damaged, defective, or did not perform as reasonably expected.

## What Visa says to show

- The exact product or service description shown at checkout (screenshots or archived pages)
- Images or specifications that match what was delivered
- Inspection or quality records from before shipment (supporting only)
- Records that a repair, replacement or refund was offered under your stated policy

## Watch for

- Saying "the item is as described" is not enough without documents.
- Internal notes do not replace what the customer was shown.

## Time limit

The issuer may raise it up to 120 calendar days from the Transaction Processing Date or from the date the cardholder received the goods or services (never more than 540 days after processing), or 60 days from the first cardholder notice where earlier negotiation with you is shown. (Visa Core Rules, Table 11-107 (24 October 2026)) Your own deadline to respond is shorter: Stripe and Shopify usually allow 7 to 21 days, PayPal 10 days for a card chargeback.
