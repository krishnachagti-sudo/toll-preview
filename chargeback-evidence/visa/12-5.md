# Visa 12.5: Incorrect Amount

Visa 12.5 (Incorrect Amount): the cardholder says the amount charged is not the amount they agreed to pay (a keying error, a misplaced decimal, a wrong tip or add-on). Stripe files it under "General". The strongest evidence for a physical product is receipt or order confirmation, messages with the customer and proof of delivery.

- Stripe category: General
- Issuer time limit: 120 days

## What it means

The cardholder says the amount charged is not the amount they agreed to pay (a keying error, a misplaced decimal, a wrong tip or add-on).

## What Visa says to show

- A receipt or system record with the correct amount and the cardholder's confirmation or acceptance
- The order confirmation, invoice or checkout screen showing the agreed total
- For amounts that may change (tips), how and when the final amount was set and shown

## Watch for

- It does not cover differences caused only by exchange rates or issuer fees.
- If the amount really was wrong and is not yet corrected, a refund of the difference may be the right answer.

## Time limit

The issuer may raise it up to 120 calendar days from the Transaction Processing Date. (Visa Core Rules, Table 11-75 (24 October 2026)) Your own deadline to respond is shorter: Stripe and Shopify usually allow 7 to 21 days, PayPal 10 days for a card chargeback.
