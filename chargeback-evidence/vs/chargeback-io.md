# chargeback.io alternative

chargeback.io's pricing page lists three alert plans, Ethoca at $29 and RDR and CDRN at $15 per alert, which stop a dispute before it is filed (read November 8, 2026). This tool does the other half: once a dispute is filed, the evidence and packet, at $2.90 per dispute.

- Ethoca alert: $29

## Their plans

| Plan | Price | Network |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Ethoca | $29 per alert | Mastercard, worldwide |
| RDR | $15 per alert | Visa, worldwide |
| CDRN | $15 per alert | Visa, United States |

## When chargeback.io is the better choice

- Your dispute ratio is near a card network monitoring threshold: an alert refunds before it counts as a dispute.
- Small orders where a refund costs less than any response.

## When a packet is the better choice

- The dispute is already filed: alerts cannot help then.
- You have the evidence and the amount is worth recovering.
