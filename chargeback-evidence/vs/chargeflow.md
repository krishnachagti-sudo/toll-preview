# Chargeflow alternative

Chargeflow's Automation plan takes 25% of each recovered chargeback and nothing on a loss (its pricing page, read November 8, 2026); a packet here is $2.90 per dispute, win or lose. On a $100 win that is $25.00 against $2.90; per win, Chargeflow costs less only on recoveries under $11.60.

- Chargeflow on a $100 win: $25.00

## What each charges

Chargeflow Automation (25% of recovered chargebacks) against $2.90 per packet

| Recovered | Chargeflow fee | This tool |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $50.00 | $12.50 | $2.90 |
| $100.00 | $25.00 | $2.90 |
| $200.00 | $50.00 | $2.90 |
| $400.00 | $100.00 | $2.90 |

This tool charges whether or not you win; Chargeflow charges only on a win. Weighed by your chance of winning, Chargeflow costs less in expectation when the amount times the chance is under $11.60 (a $40 dispute at a 25% chance: $2.50 expected).

## What Chargeflow offers

- Automated recovery with AI-written evidence, CE 3.0 support and integrations with Shopify, Stripe, WooCommerce and PayPal (its pricing page).
- Prevention at $0.10 per scanned transaction after 1,000 free, and alerts at $29 per deflected chargeback.
- A free Insights dashboard. On the Shopify App Store it shows 4.5 stars from 437 reviews.

## When Chargeflow is the better choice

- Many disputes a month and no time to handle them: it submits for you.
- You want prevention alerts (Verifi, Ethoca) in the same place.
- You would rather pay only when you win.

## When building it yourself is better

- Disputes above about $12 that you expect to win: you keep the 25%.
- You want to see and approve every word and file before the one submission.
- No store access or AI-written text: the letter here is built only from facts you give.
