# Hand-written rebuttal letters

A hand-written response most often fails on four things Stripe and Visa warn about: evidence for a different claim than the code, links or videos the bank will not open, long documents nobody reads, and files over the limit (4.5 MB on Stripe, 2 MB per file on Shopify). One submission means no second try.

- Stripe size limit: 4.5 MB

## The common failures

- Evidence about the refund policy for a not-received claim: Stripe says to keep it relevant to the reason.
- Tracking links: banks do not review external content; use screenshots.
- The whole terms of service: upload only the relevant section, marked.
- Statements like "the item is as described" without documents: Visa says they are not enough.
- Color highlighting: Stripe asks for bold text, callouts or arrows instead.

## When writing it yourself is right

- Unusual facts that no template covers: write them in your own words (the tool prints your summary as written).
