# Shopify's dispute flow vs a packet builder

Shopify's form is free, fills in order data and sends what is saved on the due date (usually 7 to 21 days), adding Shopify-generated insights unless you opt out; your files must be PDF, JPEG or PNG, each under 2 MB and 4 MB together, one per evidence type.

- Each file: 2 MB

## When Shopify alone is enough

- A simple one-off dispute where the order data says it all (a delivered order with tracking).
- You are happy with the automatic submission on the due date.

## When the packet helps

- Screenshots that are too large: PNGs are scaled to fit and every file is checked against the 2 MB and 4 MB limits before you upload.
- You want the evidence in the order the reason code calls for, with a short factual letter.
- You want to write your own case rather than send AI-generated insights.
