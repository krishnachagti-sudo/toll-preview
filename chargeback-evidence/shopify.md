# Shopify Payments chargeback evidence: limits, deadlines and fees

Shopify Payments gives you usually 7 to 21 days; each evidence file must be under 2 MB and all of them under 4 MB, PDF, JPEG or PNG, one file per evidence type. The US chargeback fee is $15 and comes back if you win; the decision can take up to 75 days.

- Each file: 2 MB
- All files: 4 MB
- Chargeback fee (US): $15

## Upload limits

| Rule | Shopify Payments |
| --- | --- |
| File types | PDF, JPEG, PNG |
| Combined size | 4 MB |
| Each file | under 2 MB |
| Pages in each PDF | under 50 |
| One file per evidence type | Yes: combine items of one type into one file |
| Links, audio, video | Not reviewed: use screenshots |
| Submissions | Shopify takes one submission: it sends your evidence on the due date, or when you click Submit now, and after that you cannot edit it. |

Window: Usually 7 to 21 days; the due date is on the chargeback in your Shopify admin. Decision: The card company can take up to 75 days after you submit.

## Chargeback reasons and the evidence first in line

Shopify names eight chargeback reasons; the evidence order is from the Stripe category guidelines, physical products

| Reason | Lead with |
| --- | --- |
| Fraudulent | Proof of delivery, shipping details and signature or ID at pickup or delivery |
| Unrecognized | Proof of delivery, shipping details and signature or ID at pickup or delivery |
| Duplicate | Records showing the two charges are separate, proof of delivery and messages with the customer |
| Subscription canceled | Cancellation policy, and where it was shown, why this charge stands after the claimed cancellation and renewal notices and use after the claimed cancellation |
| Product not received | Proof of delivery, shipping details and signature or ID at pickup or delivery |
| Product unacceptable | How the product was described, messages with the customer and refund or return policy, and where it was shown |
| Credit not processed | Proof a refund or credit was already issued, refund or return policy, and where it was shown and why no refund (or no further refund) is due |
| General | Receipt or order confirmation, messages with the customer and proof of delivery |

> Shopify sends whatever evidence is saved on the due date, even if you add nothing yourself; Submit now sends it early and locks it. It also adds Shopify-generated insights unless you untick that box.

## Please read

- Not legal advice. The card issuer (or PayPal, for a PayPal claim) decides every dispute; no packet guarantees a win.
- Rules, limits and fees are as read on the dates shown. Card networks change their rules in April and October, and platforms change fees with notice.
- Your platform's due date is the deadline. The tool counts from the date you enter; it does not read your account.
- Files you upload are processed in memory to build the packet and are not kept. Only send evidence you are allowed to share with the card issuer.
- Mastercard, American Express and Discover codes are shown as Stripe publishes them; those networks' own rule books were not read.

## Questions

### How long do I have to respond to a Shopify Payments dispute?

Usually 7 to 21 days; the exact due date is on the dispute.

### Is it worth fighting a $40 Shopify Payments dispute?

On a $40.00 Shopify Payments dispute, fighting adds no fee (the $15.00 fee comes back if you win), so submit evidence whenever you have it.
