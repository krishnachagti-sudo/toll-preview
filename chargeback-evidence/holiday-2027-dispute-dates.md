# Holiday 2027: dispute dates for sale-day orders

A Black Friday payment on November 26, 2027 can be disputed as Visa card-absent fraud (10.4) until about March 25, 2028, 120 days later, and as Visa 11.3 until February 9, 2028. The same payment, if never disputed, can back a CE 3.0 response to disputes submitted from March 26, 2028 to November 25, 2028.

- 10.4 last day for Black Friday: March 25, 2028

## Sale days and the limits

Counted from the payment date; Visa counts from the Transaction Processing Date, often a day or two later

| Payment | Visa 11.3 | Visa 10.4, 12.5, 13.2 | CE 3.0 window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| November 25, 2027 (Thanksgiving) | February 8, 2028 | March 24, 2028 | March 25, 2028 to November 24, 2028 |
| November 26, 2027 (Black Friday) | February 9, 2028 | March 25, 2028 | March 26, 2028 to November 25, 2028 |
| November 27, 2027 | February 10, 2028 | March 26, 2028 | March 27, 2028 to November 26, 2028 |
| November 28, 2027 | February 11, 2028 | March 27, 2028 | March 28, 2028 to November 27, 2028 |
| November 29, 2027 (Cyber Monday) | February 12, 2028 | March 28, 2028 | March 29, 2028 to November 28, 2028 |
| December 2, 2027 | February 15, 2028 | March 31, 2028 | April 1, 2028 to December 1, 2028 |
| December 6, 2027 | February 19, 2028 | April 4, 2028 | April 5, 2028 to December 5, 2028 |
| December 9, 2027 | February 22, 2028 | April 7, 2028 | April 8, 2028 to December 8, 2028 |
| December 13, 2027 | February 26, 2028 | April 11, 2028 | April 12, 2028 to December 12, 2028 |
| December 16, 2027 | February 29, 2028 | April 14, 2028 | April 15, 2028 to December 15, 2028 |
| December 20, 2027 | March 4, 2028 | April 18, 2028 | April 19, 2028 to December 19, 2028 |
| December 23, 2027 | March 7, 2028 | April 21, 2028 | April 22, 2028 to December 22, 2028 |

Visa 13.1, 13.3, 13.5 and 13.7 can count from the date goods or services were received or expected instead, up to 540 days after processing, so late deliveries stretch these dates.
