Visa 10.4 fraud

Does Compelling Evidence 3.0 apply? Check two earlier payments.

Two earlier payments on the same card that match the disputed one can answer a Visa 10.4 fraud claim.

Read the full rule

Visa's rule (in force for disputes processed from October 24, 2026): two earlier payments on the same card, not reported as fraud, processed more than 120 and no more than 365 days before the dispute, each matching the disputed payment on the device ID or fingerprint, or the IP address, plus one more of: login ID, full delivery address, device or IP.

Please read

What the packet does, and who decides.

The card issuer (or PayPal, for a PayPal claim) decides every dispute; the packet puts your evidence in the order and slots it asks for.

What it does not cover

Legal advice, a guaranteed win, your account's own data, or the Mastercard, Amex and Discover rule books.

Read the full list
  • Not legal advice. The card issuer (or PayPal, for a PayPal claim) decides every dispute; no packet guarantees a win.
  • Rules, limits and fees are as read on the dates shown. Card networks change their rules in April and October, and platforms change fees with notice.
  • Your platform's due date is the deadline. The tool counts from the date you enter; it does not read your account.
  • Files you upload are processed in memory to build the packet and are not kept. Only send evidence you are allowed to share with the card issuer.
  • Mastercard, American Express and Discover codes are shown as Stripe publishes them; those networks' own rule books were not read.
