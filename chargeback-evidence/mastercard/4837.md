# Mastercard 4837: No Cardholder Authorization

Mastercard 4837 (No Cardholder Authorization): the cardholder says they did not authorize the payment (often a card-not-present payment on a lost, stolen or copied card). Stripe files it under "Fraudulent". The strongest evidence for a physical product is proof of delivery, shipping details and signature or ID at pickup or delivery.

- Stripe category: Fraudulent

## What it means

The cardholder says they did not authorize the payment (often a card-not-present payment on a lost, stolen or copied card).

## Evidence Stripe lists for this code

- For online payments: 3D Secure authentication, AVS and CVC match results
- IP address and device data tying the payment to a device or place the cardholder used before
- Messages where the cardholder acknowledges the purchase
- For in-person payments: chip (EMV) data or a signed receipt
