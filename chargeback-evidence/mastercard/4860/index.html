Mastercard 4860: Credit Not Processed

Mastercard 4860 (Credit Not Processed): the cardholder says a refund or credit they were owed was never processed (a return, a cancellation, or a refund you agreed to). Stripe files it under Credit not processed. Stripe files it under "Credit not processed". The strongest evidence for a physical product is proof a refund or credit was already issued, refund or return policy, and where it was shown and why no refund (or no further refund) is due.

What it means

The cardholder says a refund or credit they were owed was never processed (a return, a cancellation, or a refund you agreed to). Stripe files it under Credit not processed.

Sub-reasons Stripe lists under 4860: Credit Not Processed; Timeshares; Credit Posted as a Purchase.

Evidence Stripe lists for this code

  • The refund record showing the credit was processed
  • The refund, return or cancellation policy the cardholder agreed to
  • Proof the cardholder did not meet the return or cancellation conditions
  • Messages where you told the cardholder about refund timing or non-refundable terms

Please read
