# Holiday 2026: dispute dates for sale-day orders

A Black Friday payment on November 27, 2026 can be disputed as Visa card-absent fraud (10.4) until about March 27, 2027, 120 days later, and as Visa 11.3 until February 10, 2027. The same payment, if never disputed, can back a CE 3.0 response to disputes submitted from March 28, 2027 to November 27, 2027.

- 10.4 last day for Black Friday: March 27, 2027

## Sale days and the limits

Counted from the payment date; Visa counts from the Transaction Processing Date, often a day or two later

| Payment | Visa 11.3 | Visa 10.4, 12.5, 13.2 | CE 3.0 window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| November 26, 2026 (Thanksgiving) | February 9, 2027 | March 26, 2027 | March 27, 2027 to November 26, 2027 |
| November 27, 2026 (Black Friday) | February 10, 2027 | March 27, 2027 | March 28, 2027 to November 27, 2027 |
| November 28, 2026 | February 11, 2027 | March 28, 2027 | March 29, 2027 to November 28, 2027 |
| November 29, 2026 | February 12, 2027 | March 29, 2027 | March 30, 2027 to November 29, 2027 |
| November 30, 2026 (Cyber Monday) | February 13, 2027 | March 30, 2027 | March 31, 2027 to November 30, 2027 |
| December 3, 2026 | February 16, 2027 | April 2, 2027 | April 3, 2027 to December 3, 2027 |
| December 7, 2026 | February 20, 2027 | April 6, 2027 | April 7, 2027 to December 7, 2027 |
| December 10, 2026 | February 23, 2027 | April 9, 2027 | April 10, 2027 to December 10, 2027 |
| December 14, 2026 | February 27, 2027 | April 13, 2027 | April 14, 2027 to December 14, 2027 |
| December 17, 2026 | March 2, 2027 | April 16, 2027 | April 17, 2027 to December 17, 2027 |
| December 21, 2026 | March 6, 2027 | April 20, 2027 | April 21, 2027 to December 21, 2027 |
| December 24, 2026 | March 9, 2027 | April 23, 2027 | April 24, 2027 to December 24, 2027 |

Visa 13.1, 13.3, 13.5 and 13.7 can count from the date goods or services were received or expected instead, up to 540 days after processing, so late deliveries stretch these dates.
