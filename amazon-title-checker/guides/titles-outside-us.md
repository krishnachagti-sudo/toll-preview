## What to do

- Check characters and repeats everywhere: Amazon posted those rules for each of these marketplaces.
- Check the 75 too: it costs nothing to be under it, and Amazon's post gives the reason as display on mobile.
- Look in your own Seller Central for the title notice before treating the 75 as enforced in your marketplace.
- Titles in other languages are counted the same way: an accented letter is one character.
