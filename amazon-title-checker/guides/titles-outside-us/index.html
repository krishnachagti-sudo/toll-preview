Amazon title rules outside the US

Amazon posted its January 2025 title rules (the banned characters ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ and no word more than twice) as separate News_Amazon posts on the US, Canada, UK and Europe Seller Forums on January 3, 2025; the July 2026 75-character post names no marketplace, and we found it only as one thread, so confirm the 75 in your own Seller Central before you rely on it outside the US.

What to do

  • Check characters and repeats everywhere: Amazon posted those rules for each of these marketplaces.
  • Check the 75 too: it costs nothing to be under it, and Amazon's post gives the reason as display on mobile.
  • Look in your own Seller Central for the title notice before treating the 75 as enforced in your marketplace.
  • Titles in other languages are counted the same way: an accented letter is one character.

Sources

  • Amazon Seller Forums, News_Amazon: "New product title requirements effective January 21, 2025" (read November 8, 2026)sellercentral.amazon.com
  • Amazon Seller Forums (Canada): the same January 2025 post (read November 8, 2026)sellercentral.amazon.ca
  • Amazon Seller Forums (UK): the same January 2025 post (read November 8, 2026)sellercentral.amazon.co.uk
  • Amazon Seller Forums (Europe): the same January 2025 post (read November 8, 2026)sellercentral-europe.amazon.com
  • Amazon Seller Forums, News_Amazon: "Updates to improve your product titles begin on July 27" (updated June 10, 2026; read November 8, 2026)sellercentral.amazon.com

This checks titles against the rules Amazon published in its Seller Forums announcements on the dates shown: 75 characters including spaces outside media (July 27, 2026), the characters ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ outside the brand name and no word more than twice (January 21, 2025). Amazon's help page and category style guides can add rules this check does not cover. The split is a mechanical cut at the last whole word that fits, not a rewrite. Not affiliated with Amazon.
