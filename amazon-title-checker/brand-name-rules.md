# Brand names and the Amazon title rules

Amazon's rules name the brand once: the characters ! $ ? \_ { } ^ ¬ ¦ are allowed when they are part of the brand name; otherwise the brand is title text like any other, counted in the 75, so in "Chef Knife 8 Inch High Carbon Stainless Steel, Ergonomic Handle, Gift Box, by Harbor & Pine" the brand ends at character 91 and a cut to 75 loses it.

## The rules that mention the brand

"The special characters !, $, ?, \_, {, }, ^, ¬, and ¦ are not allowed, unless they are part of the brand name."

The 75-character rule counts the whole title "including spaces"; it makes no brand exception. Moving the brand first, "Harbor & Pine Chef Knife 8 Inch High Carbon Stainless Steel, Ergonomic Handle" is 77 characters with the brand at character 1.

## Brand words and the repeat rule

Amazon's January 2025 post makes no brand exception to "the same word more than twice", so the checker counts brand words like any other: "Old Navy Fleece Jacket, Navy Blue, Navy Zip" is flagged for "navy" 3 times. Amazon's help page on titles asks for a sign-in; if it treats brand words differently, we could not read it.
