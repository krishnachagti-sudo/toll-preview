# Checker vs Helium 10 Listing Builder

Helium 10's Listing Builder generates titles, bullets and descriptions with AI inside plans from $129 a month ($99 a month billed yearly, Platinum); the checker does one narrow job, checking existing titles against Amazon's published rules, for $0.002 per title for the first 5,000 titles in a month and $0.001 after, at most $4.99 a month; one title on the page is free, so 500 titles cost $1.00.
