Let Amazon's AI rewrite, or fix titles yourself

Amazon's own route is free: after July 27, 2026 it updates titles still over 75 characters to its AI recommendation gradually, and brand owners have 14 days to review in Review Listings Changes, one listing at a time; the checker finds every title over the limit in a file first, so you can write them yourself, for $0.002 per title for the first 5,000 titles in a month and $0.001 after, at most $4.99 a month; one title on the page is free.

When the other choice is better

Amazon's queue is the only place a change goes live under your brand, and approving a good AI suggestion there costs nothing. Use the checker to find which titles to look at first; make the change in Seller Central.

Sources

  • Amazon Seller Forums, Dougal_Amazon: "Engage With Amazon: Get your Product Title Update Questions Answered!" (read November 8, 2026)

This checks titles against the rules Amazon published in its Seller Forums announcements on the dates shown: 75 characters including spaces outside media (July 27, 2026), the characters ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ outside the brand name and no word more than twice (January 21, 2025). Amazon's help page and category style guides can add rules this check does not cover. The split is a mechanical cut at the last whole word that fits, not a rewrite. Not affiliated with Amazon.
