# Checker vs WISEPIM

WISEPIM's free checker tests one pasted title in the browser for length, the banned characters and repeats, and its own page says the rule pack in its PIM (free up to 100 products) "checks length, not the banned characters"; the checker runs every title in a file against all three rules for $0.002 per title for the first 5,000 titles in a month and $0.001 after, at most $4.99 a month; one title on the page is free.

## Side by side

Facts about the other choice from its own page, read November 8, 2026

|  | Amazon title checker | The other choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0.002 per title for the first 5,000 titles in a month and $0.001 after, at most $4.99 a month; one title on the page is free | Checker free; PIM free up to 100 products, paid tiers by product count |
| Many titles at once | Yes, up to 10,000 a run | Checker: one title; rule pack: length only |
| Banned characters with brand exception | Per row, from a brand column | One title, brand typed in |
| Promotional phrases, functional-use characters | Not checked (on Amazon's signed-in help page, not read) | Listed in its guide |
| AI title writing | No | Yes, in the PIM |

## When the other choice is better

If you want one tool to hold product data for many channels and write titles with AI, or want the promotional phrase rule checked as its guide describes it, WISEPIM is the better fit; for a single title its checker is free.
