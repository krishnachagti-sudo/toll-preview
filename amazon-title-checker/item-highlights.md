# Amazon Item Highlights

Item Highlights is the field Amazon announced in June 2026 beside the 75-character title: 125 more characters "for sharing materials or recommended use cases", searchable and shown with the title in search results and on product pages; Amazon's own example highlight is 88 characters.

## Split a long title

[Count it](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/amazon-title-checker/?title=Harbor+%26+Pine+Insulated+Water+Bottle+32+oz%2C+Leakproof+Lid%2C+Keeps+Drinks+Cold+24+Hours&brand=)

## In Amazon's words

"Item Highlights provides an additional 125 characters for sharing materials or recommended use cases that help customers compare options. This content is searchable and visible with titles in search results and on product detail pages."

## Amazon's example

From Amazon's Engage post, counted by the engine

| Field | Text | Characters | Limit |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Title | Premium Stainless Steel Insulated Water Bottle – BPA-Free | 57 | 75 |
| Item Highlights | Keeps drinks cold 24 hours, hot 12 hours. Ideal for gym, office, and outdoor adventures. | 88 | 125 |

## What spills over from a cut

"Harbor & Pine Insulated Water Bottle 32 oz, Leakproof Lid, Keeps Drinks Cold 24 Hours" is 85 characters. Cut at the last whole word that fits 75 it keeps "Harbor & Pine Insulated Water Bottle 32 oz, Leakproof Lid, Keeps Drinks" (71); the rest, "Cold 24 Hours", is 13 characters, a start for Item Highlights, not a finished one.

## Steps

1. **Open the listing.** Go to Manage All Inventory and find the listing.
2. **Edit it.** Select Edit from the drop-down menu.
3. **See the suggestions.** Click View enhancements on the left of the page to see titles and Item Highlights that follow Amazon's requirements.
4. **Write your own.** Keep the key product information in the title (75) and put materials or use cases in Item Highlights (125); count both before you save.

## Questions

### How many characters is Item Highlights?

125. Amazon: "These tools will automatically adhere to the new limits (75 characters for titles, 125 characters for Item Highlights)."

### Is Item Highlights searchable?

Amazon's post says the content "is searchable and visible with titles in search results and on product detail pages".
