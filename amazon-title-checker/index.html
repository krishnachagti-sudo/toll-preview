The rules it checks, in Amazon's words

  • Length: 75 characters or less, spaces included, from July 27, 2026 (media excepted).
  • Characters: none of ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless they are part of the brand name.
  • Repeats: no word more than twice, apart from articles, prepositions and conjunctions.
  • Over 75: cut at the last whole word, with the rest measured against Item Highlights' 125.

What it does not cover

Rules only in Amazon's sign-in help page and category style guides are not checked. The split is a mechanical cut, not a rewrite. Not affiliated with Amazon.

Read the full rules
  • Length, from July 27, 2026: "Starting July 27, 2026, titles in all categories except for media will need to be 75 characters or less including spaces." Counted in characters as you see them, spaces and punctuation included; an accented letter is one.
  • Item Highlights: "Item Highlights provides an additional 125 characters for sharing materials or recommended use cases that help customers compare options." A title over 75 is cut at the last whole word that fits; the rest is measured against 125.
  • Characters, from January 21, 2025: "The special characters !, $, ?, _, {, }, ^, ¬, and ¦ are not allowed, unless they are part of the brand name."
  • Repeats, from January 21, 2025: "Titles may not contain the same word more than twice. Prepositions, articles, and conjunctions are exceptions." Words are compared without regard to case; a hyphenated word is one word; numbers are not counted as words. The exempt words are the English articles, prepositions and conjunctions listed in the rule set (our list; Amazon names only the word classes). Plurals are not merged: Amazon's posts say "the same word".
  • Brand past 75 (not a rule, a warning): with a brand given, a title whose brand ends after character 75 is flagged, because a cut to 75 loses it.
  • What happens to a title over 75: "After July 27, any titles still over 75 characters will be updated to the AI recommendation gradually." Brand owners "will have 14 days before implementation to review, modify, and approve" them in Review Listings Changes.

This checks titles against the rules Amazon published in its Seller Forums announcements on the dates shown. Amazon's help page "Product title requirements and guidelines" asks for a Seller Central sign-in, so rules that only it states (and category style guides) are not checked. The split is a mechanical cut at the last whole word that fits, not a rewrite. Not affiliated with Amazon.
