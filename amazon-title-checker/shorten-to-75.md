## Steps

1. **Count it.** Count every character, spaces included; the checker shows how many are over.
2. **Put the brand first.** A brand after character 75 is the first thing a cut loses.
3. **Drop repeats.** A word used three times breaks the January 2025 rule anyway; keep it at most twice.
4. **Keep the product.** Amazon's post: keep "your key product information in the title" and move "additional details to Item Highlights".
5. **Move the rest.** Materials and use cases go in Item Highlights (125 characters).
6. **Check again.** Re-run the title: no character over 75, no banned character, no word three times.
