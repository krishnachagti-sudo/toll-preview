# When Amazon's AI rewrote your title

After July 27, 2026, Amazon updates titles still over 75 characters to its AI recommendation "gradually"; brand owners have 14 days before implementation to review, modify and approve in Review Listings Changes, and any seller can see AI-applied changes under View Change History. A title of 99 characters, like Amazon's own "before" example, is 24 over and in line for one.

## In Amazon's words

"After July 27, any titles still over 75 characters will be updated to the AI recommendation gradually. Your listings stay active throughout this process, and you can make changes to your titles and Item Highlights at any time."

"When changes are made to your listings, brand owners will have 14 days before implementation to review, modify, and approve AI-generated recommendations for titles and Item Highlights in Review Listings Changes."

"All sellers: View any AI-applied changes in Manage All Inventory → click View Change History."

## The same window in 2025

The January 2025 rules worked the same way: "Brand owners will have 14 days to act upon on the suggestions before we update the titles to comply with our requirements."

## Steps

1. **See what changed.** In Manage All Inventory, open View Change History for the listing (all sellers).
2. **Review before it goes live.** Brand owners: open Review Listings Changes; there are 14 days before implementation to review, modify and approve.
3. **Write your own.** Edit the title to 75 characters or fewer yourself, with the details in Item Highlights, so no title of yours is over the limit.
4. **Check the rest of the listings.** Run every title through the checker; each one still over 75 is in line for the AI recommendation.

## Questions

### How long do brand owners have to review?

14 days. Amazon: "When changes are made to your listings, brand owners will have 14 days before implementation to review, modify, and approve AI-generated recommendations for titles and Item Highlights in Review Listings Changes."

### Can I change the title back?

Amazon's post says "you can make changes to your titles and Item Highlights at any time"; a title you write must itself fit the 75.
