# Amazon title character limit: 75 characters

Since July 27, 2026, Amazon titles in all categories except media must be 75 characters or less including spaces, down from the 200 Amazon allowed for most categories from January 21, 2025; Amazon's own "before" example title is 99 characters, 24 over.

## The rule, in Amazon's words

"Starting July 27, 2026, titles in all categories except for media will need to be 75 characters or less including spaces." "This ensures that your title will fully display on mobile and is consistent with the title length used by other online stores."

"Item Highlights provides an additional 125 characters for sharing materials or recommended use cases that help customers compare options. This content is searchable and visible with titles in search results and on product detail pages."

## How the limit changed

Amazon's title length rules, from its Seller Forums announcements

| From | Limit | Announced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January 21, 2025 | 200 characters including spaces, "for most product categories" | News\_Amazon, January 3, 2025 |
| July 27, 2026 | 75 characters including spaces, all categories except media; Item Highlights adds 125 | News\_Amazon, updated June 10, 2026 |

## What counts as a character

- Every letter, number, space and punctuation mark, the brand name included: the 75 is the whole title.
- An accented letter (é) or a letter from another script counts as one.
- Spaces at the start or end, and double spaces, count too; the checker points them out.
- Emoji and combining marks can be counted differently by different systems; the checker flags titles that hold them.

## After July 27

"After July 27, any titles still over 75 characters will be updated to the AI recommendation gradually. Your listings stay active throughout this process, and you can make changes to your titles and Item Highlights at any time."

"When changes are made to your listings, brand owners will have 14 days before implementation to review, modify, and approve AI-generated recommendations for titles and Item Highlights in Review Listings Changes."

## Questions

### Do spaces count toward the 75 characters?

Yes. Amazon's announcement says titles "will need to be 75 characters or less including spaces".

### Which categories are exempt?

Media. The announcement says "all categories except for media"; it names no other exception.

### What was the limit before July 27, 2026?

200 characters including spaces "for most product categories", under Amazon's rules effective January 21, 2025.

### What happens to a title still over 75?

Amazon's post: "After July 27, any titles still over 75 characters will be updated to the AI recommendation gradually. Your listings stay active throughout this process, and you can make changes to your titles and Item Highlights at any time."
