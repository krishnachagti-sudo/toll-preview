Statement Press or Endwise for giving statements

Statement Press builds giving statements in your browser from a pasted spreadsheet, free for up to 5 statements per file and $29 for a season pass (its pricing page, read November 8, 2026). Endwise's statement maker charges $0.10 a donor statement, never more than $19.00 a month, and also works over an API and MCP.

What Statement Press does

  • Statements are built inside your browser; its page says nothing is uploaded and the donor list stays on your device.
  • Free: up to 5 statements per file, with a small "Made with Statement Press" line.
  • Season pass, $29 one payment: unlimited statements, your logo, a PDF per donor zipped, no Statement Press line; it covers statements made through October 31. No subscription and no account.
  • Columns in any order, "Last, First" names, negative refunds, and rows from other years set aside.

From Statement Press's own page, read November 8, 2026. Plans and features change; check their page.

What Endwise does

  • Works from a gift sheet you already have (CSV, or cells pasted from Excel or Google Sheets); no account with the gifts in it.
  • Groups rows by donor or household, merges "Last, First" with "First Last", sets aside rows from other years and subtracts refunds.
  • Prints the IRS Publication 1771 wording: the goods-or-services sentence, or the intangible religious benefits sentence for a church, with good faith estimates and the $75 disclosure where a gift bought something.
  • Free preview of every total and one statement; $0.10 a donor statement, never more than $19.00 in a month. Also over a REST API and MCP.

When Statement Press is the better choice

  • You want the gift list never to leave your computer.
  • You want your logo on the letterhead.
  • You have 5 or fewer donors and do not mind the small credit line.

When the statement maker is the better choice

  • You want to make statements from code, a spreadsheet automation or an AI assistant (REST API or MCP).
  • You need gift tickets with good faith estimates and the $75 disclosure on each statement.
  • A small list: $0.10 a donor statement, with a free monthly allowance on a signed-in account, may cost less than a season pass.
