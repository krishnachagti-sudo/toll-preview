  • From: Grace Community Church, 100 Main Street, Springfield, OH 45501
  • To: The Smith Family

Sources

Questions

What does a church say about goods or services?

If it gave only intangible religious benefits, a sentence saying so is enough: "The only goods or services provided in exchange for these contributions were intangible religious benefits."

Should weekly offerings be listed one by one?

Pub 1771 does not require it for gifts under $250, but a line per gift with the date matches the donor's own records; this sample lists all 54.
