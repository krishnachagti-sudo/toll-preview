Year-end giving letter template

Sample: a year-end giving letter thanking Elena and David Garcia for 4 gifts totalling $1,175.00 in 2026, every one of them $250 or more, so the letter also serves as the IRS written acknowledgment for each.

  • Made by Conyso
  • Exact, no AI
  • Official sources, dated
  • Files you upload are not kept
  • SecurityTerms

The letter

Dear Elena and David Garcia,

Thank you for standing with our neighbors all year. Your gifts kept the after-school program open five days a week. This letter lists the contributions Hope Valley Community Center received from you in 2026; please keep it with your tax records.

Every gift in 2026, as the statement lists it.
DateFundMethodAmount
3/1/2026After-school programBank transfer$250.00
6/1/2026After-school programBank transfer$275.00
9/1/2026After-school programBank transfer$250.00
12/1/2026After-school programBank transfer$400.00

Total contributions of money: $1,175.00.

No goods or services were provided in exchange for these contributions.

This statement is our written acknowledgment of each single contribution of $250 or more listed above.

With gratitude, Marcus Hill, Board Treasurer, Hope Valley Community Center

An appeal letter asking for a year-end gift is a different document; this one thanks and acknowledges.
