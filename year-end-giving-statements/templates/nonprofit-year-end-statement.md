## Questions

### How is a gift of stock shown?

Described, without a value: Pub 1771 asks for "a description (but not the fair market value) of any contribution of property".

### How is a gala ticket shown?

As the payment, the goods or services with a good faith estimate of their value, and the deductible part: $150.00 less $60.00 is $90.00.

### Does the 2026 non-itemizer deduction change the statement?

No new wording is required. Eligible taxpayers who do not itemize may be able to deduct up to $1,000 of qualifying cash contributions made to eligible charitable organizations ($2,000 for married taxpayers filing jointly). (IRS Publication 6196). The tool can add a line telling donors so.
