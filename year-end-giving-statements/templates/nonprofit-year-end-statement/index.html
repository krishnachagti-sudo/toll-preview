The filled template

A donor who gives monthly by card, bought a gala ticket, wrote a year-end check and gave shares. Made up for this page.

  • From: Riverside Food Bank, 22 Market Street, Dayton, OH 45402, EIN 12-3456789
  • To: Priya Natarajan
  • Period: 2026; statement date January 20, 2027

Total contributions of money: $950.00; less goods or services $60.00; deductible part $890.00.

Riverside Food Bank provided goods or services in exchange for the contribution marked *, described with our good faith estimate of their fair market value on that line. No goods or services were provided in exchange for the other contributions.

The amount of a contribution that is deductible for federal income tax purposes is limited to the excess of the money (and the value of any property) you gave over the value of the goods or services we provided.

This statement is our written acknowledgment of each single contribution of $250 or more listed above.

Gifts of property: 8/21/2026: 20 shares of Example Corp common stock (described, not valued).

Questions

How is a gift of stock shown?

Described, without a value: Pub 1771 asks for "a description (but not the fair market value) of any contribution of property".

How is a gala ticket shown?

As the payment, the goods or services with a good faith estimate of their value, and the deductible part: $150.00 less $60.00 is $90.00.

Does the 2026 non-itemizer deduction change the statement?

No new wording is required. Eligible taxpayers who do not itemize may be able to deduct up to $1,000 of qualifying cash contributions made to eligible charitable organizations ($2,000 for married taxpayers filing jointly). (IRS Publication 6196). The tool can add a line telling donors so.
