## Do quarterly statements replace the year-end one?

The IRS rule is about single gifts of $250 or more, and "one contemporaneous written acknowledgment, such as an annual summary, may be used to substantiate several single contributions valued at $250 or more." Nothing in Publication 1771 requires a statement every quarter. A quarterly statement that lists a large gift can serve as its acknowledgment if the donor gets it in time, but a year-end statement with the full year is what most donors file with.

## When to send them

- After each quarter closes and the deposits are reconciled, for example in April, July and October.
- A January to June statement in July, if you send only one mid-year.
- The full-year statement in January: see [the dates for 2026 gifts](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/year-end-giving-statements/2026-giving-statements-dates/).

Each quarterly run is a different period, so it is priced as new statements ($0.10 a donor, never more than $19.00 in a month); a repeat of the same period within 180 days is free.

## Questions

### Does the IRS require quarterly giving statements?

No. Publication 1771 requires a written acknowledgment for single gifts of $250 or more, and allows one annual summary to cover several. Quarterly statements are a choice.

### If we send quarterly statements, do we still need a year-end one?

Not by the IRS rules read here, as long as each gift of $250 or more is acknowledged before the donor files. Most donors still find one year-end total easier to file.

### Which ranges can the tool make?

Any from and to date. The page offers January to March, April to June, July to September, October to December and January to June.
