Church and nonprofit giving statements

Year-end giving statements

Open the sheet of this year's gifts. You get one statement per donor or household, with every gift, the total and the sentence IRS Publication 1771 asks for. Churches get the intangible religious benefits wording. A gala ticket gets its good faith estimate and the deductible part. A gift of stock or goods is described without a value.

The totals and one sample statement are free. All the statements cost $0.10 a donor, and never more than $19 in a month. Making a donor's statement again within 180 days is free.

See what you get first: the sample statements load with the page.

1. Your gifts

A heading line, then one gift a line. Copy the cells straight from Excel or Google Sheets. Download a blank template.

What the file needs

Three columns: the donor (headed Donor, Name, Customer, Family or Member), the date (Date, Gift date, Donation date) and the amount (Amount, Gift amount, Gross, Total). Dates may be written 2026-01-04, 1/4/2026 or Jan 4, 2026. Amounts may have a $ sign or commas, and a refund may be negative or in brackets.

Optional columns: Donor ID or Envelope number (gifts are grouped by it when present), Fund, Method, Type, Description, Benefit value and Benefit description (for a ticket or auction item), Address, City, State, ZIP and Email.

A gift of property: put "In-kind" or "Stock" in Type, or leave the amount empty, and describe it in Description. Its value is never printed. Gifts dated outside the period are set aside and counted.

Message and options

One household a line: the name to print, a colon, then the names or donor IDs in the file. "Smith, John" and "John Smith" are already treated as the same donor.

Check one, then make them all

The preview is free and shows the first donor's statement exactly as it will print, marked PREVIEW.

Your gifts are sent to make the statements, used for that answer and not kept. A large file goes in several parts, split by donor; the price is the same.

What the statements say, and why

The $250 rule

A donor cannot deduct a single gift of $250 or more without a written acknowledgment from you, and one annual statement may cover several such gifts (Pub 1771). Gifts under $250 are not added together for this rule, but donors still need a written record of every money gift, so most organizations send everyone a statement.

Goods or services

Each statement says no goods or services were provided, or, for a church, that only intangible religious benefits were. Where a row has a benefit value, the gift is marked, the description and your good faith estimate are printed, and the deductible part is shown. A payment over $75 that was partly for goods or services also needs a disclosure when it is made; the statement repeats it and the preview flags it.

When to send

There is no fixed IRS mailing date. The donor must have the statement by the earlier of filing their return or its due date, including extensions. Pub 1771 says charities typically send by January 31; for 2026 gifts that is Sunday, January 31, 2027. Email to the donor is fine.

  • Computed from your file and the rules in IRS Publication 1771 (Rev. 11-2023), read November 7, 2026. Your organization decides the good faith estimate of any goods or services and whether an exception applies (token items, membership benefits, intangible religious benefits); the statements print what you enter.
  • Gifts of property are described, never valued. Vehicles, boats and airplanes claimed at over $500 follow Form 1098-C rules, which this tool does not produce. The donor's Social Security number is not needed and is not printed.
  • Not tax or legal advice. Sources: IRS Publication 1771 (Rev. 11-2023), IRS: written acknowledgments and IRS: quid pro quo contributions (both updated June 28, 2026), IRS Publication 6196 (10-2026); all read November 7, 2026.

Prices

  • The IRS rules, with sources: free.
  • Preview (every donor's totals and one sample statement): free.
  • Statements (PDF per donor, print file, summary and email CSVs): $0.10 per donor statement; a repeat for the same donor statement is free for 180 days.
  • At most $19.00 a calendar month for this tool; after that it is free until the month ends.

A church with 100 donors pays $10.00. The free $0.25 each month on a signed-in account covers two statements.
