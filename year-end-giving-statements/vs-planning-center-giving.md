# Planning Center Giving statements, or a statement maker

Planning Center Giving is a church giving platform: its page (read November 8, 2026) says you can send donor statements digitally or by mail to joint and individual donors, and donors can download statements in Church Center. If your gifts are collected there, use it; if they are in a sheet or an envelope log, a statement maker for a sheet is the shorter path.

## What Planning Center Giving does

- Giving collects gifts by app or web (Church Center), where donors can see their donation history and download statements.
- Statements: sent to donors digitally or by mail, to joint and individual donors alike.
- Giving syncs with People, which Planning Center describes as its free church management database.
- Reporting by donors, funds, campus, pledge campaigns and payouts.

> From Planning Center Giving's own page, read November 8, 2026. Plans and features change; check their page.

## What Endwise does

- Works from a gift sheet you already have (CSV, or cells pasted from Excel or Google Sheets); no account with the gifts in it.
- Groups rows by donor or household, merges "Last, First" with "First Last", sets aside rows from other years and subtracts refunds.
- Prints the IRS Publication 1771 wording: the goods-or-services sentence, or the intangible religious benefits sentence for a church, with good faith estimates and the $75 disclosure where a gift bought something.
- Free preview of every total and one statement; $0.10 a donor statement, never more than $19.00 in a month. Also over a REST API and MCP.

## When Planning Center Giving is the better choice

- Your church already takes gifts through Planning Center Giving: the statements come from the same records with no export.
- You want donors to download their own statements whenever they like.

## When the statement maker is the better choice

- Gifts are recorded in a spreadsheet, a cash and envelope log, or exported from several systems.
- You need statements for one season without moving the church onto a giving platform.
- You want per-gift lines with good faith estimates and the $75 disclosure computed from your sheet.

## Questions

### Does Planning Center Giving make year-end statements?

Its Giving page lists statements, sent digitally or by mail, to joint and individual donors.

### Can I combine Planning Center gifts with cash envelopes in one statement?

With the statement maker, yes: export the gifts as a CSV, add the envelope rows to the same sheet, and the gifts group by donor.
