# Reissuing a lost giving statement, or correcting one

Make the statement again for the same donor and the same period, after fixing the gift rows: with this statement maker, a repeat of the same organization, donor and period within 180 days is free. Send it as a replacement and keep a copy with the corrected sheet.

## A lost statement

Nothing in Publication 1771 limits copies, and email is allowed: an organization can provide the acknowledgment electronically, such as via an email addressed to the donor. Send the same statement again; if months have passed, a new statement date is fine because it reflects the same gifts.

## A mistake in a gift or a total

- Fix the row in your gift sheet first, so your books and the statement agree.
- Make the statement for the whole period again, not just the missing gift: the donor should have one document with every gift and the total.
- Say in the covering note that it replaces the earlier statement.
- Keep the corrected sheet and the new PDF together as your record of what was sent.

## When is a repeat free?

The statement maker charges $0.10 a donor statement. It remembers each organization, period and donor it made a statement for, and the same three again within 180 days costs nothing. A changed donor name, organization name or period is a new statement.

What makes a repeat within 180 days free.

| You change | Repeat is free? |
| --- | --- |
| A gift amount, a date or a fund | Yes |
| Add a missing gift | Yes |
| The donor's name spelling (with no donor ID column) | No: a new donor |
| The period, for example the year to a date range | No: a new period |

Spelling only: "Smith, John" and "John Smith" are already the same donor. See [how names are grouped](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/year-end-giving-statements/offerings-spreadsheet-to-statements/).

## Steps

1. **Find the gifts.** Take the donor's rows for the same period from your gift sheet, after fixing any wrong amount or date.
2. **Same organization, same period.** Use the same organization name and the same year or date range as the first statement.
3. **Same donor name or ID.** Keep the donor's name or donor ID as it was; a corrected spelling is a new donor to the tool.
4. **Date and send.** Give the statement today's date, and say in your covering email that it replaces the earlier one if anything changed.

## Questions

### Can a church reprint a giving statement?

Yes. There is no IRS form for the acknowledgment, and nothing limits how many copies you send. Publication 1771: "There are no IRS forms for the contemporaneous written acknowledgment."

### Is making the statement again free?

With this statement maker, the same organization, donor and period again within 180 days is not charged a second time.

### A gift was left off. What do I send?

A corrected statement for the whole period, so the donor has one document with every gift and the right total.
