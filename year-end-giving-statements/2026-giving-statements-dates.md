# Giving statements for 2026 gifts: the dates

Gifts count for 2026 if they are made by Thursday, December 31, 2026; a mailed check counts on the day it is mailed. Statements usually go out by January 31, which in 2027 is a Sunday, so many organizations send them by Friday, January 29, 2027. The legal limit is that the donor has the statement before filing their return or by its due date.

## The calendar

Weekdays and business days computed with the US federal holiday calendar.

| Date | What happens | The rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thursday, December 31, 2026 | Last day a gift can be made for 2026 | IRS Publication 526: a gift counts in the year it is made, by unconditional delivery |
| Friday, January 29, 2027 | Last business day before January 31 | Practice: many organizations send on the last business day before January 31 |
| Sunday, January 31, 2027 | January 31: when charities typically have statements out | IRS Publication 1771: "Charities typically send written acknowledgments to donors no later than January 31 of the year following the donation." |
| Thursday, April 15, 2027 | Usual due date of a 2026 individual return | The statement must reach the donor before they file, and no later than the due date with extensions |

## Which late-December gifts belong on the 2026 statement?

A gift belongs to the year the donor made it, which is not always the day the money reached your account. IRS Publication 526 says: "You can deduct your contributions only in the year you actually make them in cash or other property". "Usually, you make a contribution at the time of its unconditional delivery."

IRS Publication 526 (2025), "When To Deduct", quoted.

| How the donor gave | When it counts (Pub 526) |
| --- | --- |
| Check by mail | A check you mail to a charity is considered delivered on the date you mail it. |
| Credit card | Contributions charged on your bank credit card are deductible in the year you make the charge. |
| Text message | Contributions made by text message are deductible in the year you send the text message if the contribution is charged to your telephone or wireless account. |
| Pay-by-phone account | Contributions made through a pay-by-phone account are considered delivered on the date the financial institution pays the amount. |
| Stock certificate | A properly endorsed stock certificate is considered delivered on the date of mailing or other delivery to the charity or to the charity's agent. |
| Promissory note | If you issue and deliver a promissory note to a charity as a contribution, it isn't a contribution until you make the note payments. |

So an envelope postmarked December 31 that you open on January 4 is a 2026 gift; enter it with its 2026 date. A check left in the offering plate on Sunday, January 3 is a 2027 gift. Keep the envelopes with postmarks near the cutoff in case a donor asks.

> Publication 526 is written for donors. When a gift date is unclear, the donor's own records decide their deduction; the statement shows the date you record.

## Is January 31, 2027 a deadline?

No. It is when "charities typically" send statements (Pub 1771). The rule is about the donor: a donor must receive the acknowledgment on or before the earlier of: the date on which the donor files the donor's individual federal income tax return for the year of the contribution; or the due date (including extensions) of such return. Because January 31, 2027 is a Sunday, a statement mailed on Friday, January 29, 2027 is the practical last day, and email arrives the same day: an organization can provide the acknowledgment electronically, such as via an email addressed to the donor.

For the full rule and January 31 in other years, see [when giving statements are due](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/year-end-giving-statements/deadline/).

## What is the outer limit?

The IRS lists April 15 as the filing date for calendar-year filers and moves a due date that falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday to the next business day. For 2026 returns that is Thursday, April 15, 2027. A donor who files in February needs the statement before they file, which is why January matters more than April.

An organization that does not acknowledge a contribution incurs no penalty; but, without a contemporaneous written acknowledgment, the donor cannot claim the federal income tax deduction.

## Questions

### Is a check dated December 31 but received in January a 2026 gift?

It depends on when it was mailed, not on the date written on it or when you received it. IRS Publication 526: "A check you mail to a charity is considered delivered on the date you mail it." A check mailed on December 31, 2026 belongs on the donor's 2026 statement.

### Is a card gift made on December 31 a 2026 gift?

Yes, for the donor: "Contributions charged on your bank credit card are deductible in the year you make the charge." (Publication 526).

### Do 2026 giving statements have to be sent by January 31, 2027?

January 31 is the usual practice, not an IRS deadline. Publication 1771: "Charities typically send written acknowledgments to donors no later than January 31 of the year following the donation." In 2027 that is a Sunday, so the last business day before it is Friday, January 29, 2027.

### What is the latest a donor can receive the statement?

A donor must receive the acknowledgment on or before the earlier of: the date on which the donor files the donor's individual federal income tax return for the year of the contribution; or the due date (including extensions) of such return. For most people filing a 2026 return, the due date is Thursday, April 15, 2027, and a donor who files earlier needs it before then.
