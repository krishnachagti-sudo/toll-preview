# Amazon FBA product size tiers from January 15, 2027

From January 15, 2027 Amazon replaces small and large bulky with four bulky tiers (small, medium, large and extra-large bulky) and the four extra-large tiers with six oversize tiers (small, medium, large and long oversize, special oversize light and heavy); standard sizes keep their limits, a 40 x 1 x 25 in dry erase board stays large bulky, and a 56 x 12 x 10 in floor lamp moves from large bulky to extra-large bulky.

- Change date: January 15, 2027
- Bulky tiers: 2 become 4
- Oversize tiers: 4 become 6
- Dimensional divisor: 139

## The 2027 tiers

Amazon's 2027 product size tier definitions.

| Tier | Shipping weight up to | Longest | Median | Shortest | Length + girth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small standard | 16 oz | 15 in | 12 in | 0.75 in | N/A |
| Large standard | 20 lb | 18 in | 14 in | 8 in | N/A |
| Small bulky | 50 lb | 28 in | 24 in | 20 in | 130 in |
| Medium bulky | 50 lb | 37 in | 28 in | 20 in | 130 in |
| Large bulky | 50 lb | 52 in | 28 in | 28 in | 130 in |
| Extra-large bulky | 50 lb | 59 in | 33 in | 33 in | 130 in |
| Small oversize | 50 lb | 72 in | Any | Any | 130 in |
| Medium oversize | 70 lb | 78 in | Any | Any | 130 in |
| Large oversize | 150 lb | 96 in | Any | Any | 130 in |
| Long oversize | 150 lb | 108 in | Any | Any | 165 in |
| Special oversize light | 150 lb | Any | Any | Any | N/A |
| Special oversize heavy | Over 150 lb | Any | Any | Any | N/A |

Long oversize allows a longest side up to 108 in and length plus girth up to 165 in; anything up to 150 lb beyond that is special oversize light. Two new handling fees: flat product (oversize, shortest side under 4 in and median 25 in or more) and overhang (oversize, median side over 43 in); the overmax handling fee is removed.

Dimensional weight is length x width x height / 139; for bulky and oversize items the width and height count as at least 2 inches. Girth is 2 x (median + shortest side). A set is measured packaged together.

## Questions

### When do Amazon's 2027 size tiers start?

January 15, 2027; the 2026 tiers apply through January 14, 2027.

### How does Amazon calculate dimensional weight?

Length x width x height divided by 139; for bulky and oversize items, width and height count as at least 2 inches.
