# FBA prep for apparel, fabric and textiles

Apparel, fabric, plush and textiles go in a sealed transparent poly bag (or shrink wrap) at least 1.5 mil thick, folded small, with the barcode scannable and a suffocation warning: a folded T-shirt in a 10 x 13 in bag needs the warning at 10 pt or larger, and a hoodie in a 14 x 20 in bag at 14 pt.

## What Amazon requires for apparel and textiles

- Fold items to the smallest dimensions and bag them, with any cardboard backing, in a sealed bag or shrink wrap of at least 1.5 mil.
- The bag must not protrude more than 3 inches past the product.
- Remove normal-size hangers; mini-hangers for baby clothes may stay. Accessories, bras, hats, shorts, socks, hosiery, sweaters, swimwear and underwear cannot ship on any hanger.
- Exceptions that may stay on a hanger: blazers or suit jackets, suits, and clothing listed over $299.99 that is formal or evening wear or outerwear, or would be damaged stored flat (pleats, beading, sequins; real leather, fur or wool).
- Leather and materials bags can damage: use a box instead.
- Footwear: in the original manufacturer box or a poly bag with a suffocation warning, no material exposed; measure it in its box.
- A product already in a sealed, six-sided package with no opening larger than 1 x 1 inch does not need a bag.

Amazon's prep guidance category **Apparel, fabric, plush, and textiles**: made of cloth or fabric that could be damaged by dirt, dust, moisture, or liquid (for example shirts, bags, belts, plush toys such as teddy bears).

- Place product in a transparent poly bag to protect from damage or dust.
- Products can also be shrink-wrapped.
- Bag must have a suffocation warning.
- Bag must be secured if not self-adhesive.
- Barcode must be scannable.

Amazon's prep guidance category **Apparel on hanger**: made of cloth or fabric and intended to be put on a hanger (for example scarves, shirts, jackets).

## The worked example, step by step

1. **Bag it in a sealed transparent poly bag (or shrink wrap), at least 1.5 mil, folded to its smallest size, with a suffocation warning.** Softlines must be fully enclosed in packaging that protects against dust and humidity.
2. **Put the barcode on the outside of the bag or wrap (or use a clear bag it scans through), flat, not on a corner or curve, 0.25 in from the edge.** The barcode must be scannable after prep without opening or unwrapping.

## Questions

### Do clothes need to be poly bagged for FBA?

Yes, unless they are already in a sealed, six-sided package: Amazon's prep guidance puts apparel, fabric, plush and textiles in a transparent poly bag with a suffocation warning.

### Can I send clothes on hangers to FBA?

Normal-size hangers must be removed. Blazers, suits and some clothing over $299.99 are the exceptions.
