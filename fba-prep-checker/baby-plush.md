# FBA prep for baby products and plush

Products made for children under 4 (three years or younger) must have no exposed surface larger than 1 inch by 1 inch, so a toy, bib or teether that is not in a sealed six-sided package goes in a sealed transparent poly bag with a suffocation warning; a 10 x 7 x 5 in plush bear needs a bag of about 12 in x 15 in, so the warning at 10 pt or larger.

## What Amazon requires for baby products and plush

Amazon groups baby products with softlines (clothing, plush, bedding): they need protection from dust and humidity. Products that are not in a sealed, six-sided package, or that have an opening larger than 1 x 1 inch, must be shrink-wrapped or placed in a sealed poly bag. Packaging with a window or cutout larger than one square inch counts.

Amazon's prep guidance category **Baby**: made for a child aged three years or younger, packaging with cutouts greater than one square inch (for example teething rings, bibs, exposed toys).

- Place product in a transparent poly bag.
- Bag must have a suffocation warning.
- Bag must be secured if not self-adhesive.
- Barcode must be scannable.

Plush toys that are not for babies fall under apparel, fabric, plush and textiles: the same transparent bag and suffocation warning.

## The worked example, step by step

1. **Bag it in a sealed transparent poly bag (or shrink wrap), at least 1.5 mil, folded to its smallest size, with a suffocation warning.** Baby products for children younger than 4 must have no exposed surface greater than 1 inch by 1 inch.
2. **Put the barcode on the outside of the bag or wrap (or use a clear bag it scans through), flat, not on a corner or curve, 0.25 in from the edge.** The barcode must be scannable after prep without opening or unwrapping.

## Questions

### What counts as a baby product for FBA prep?

A product made for a child aged three years or younger (Amazon: younger than 4), such as teething rings, bibs and exposed toys.

### Does a boxed baby toy need a bag?

Not if the box is sealed and six-sided with no opening larger than 1 x 1 inch.
