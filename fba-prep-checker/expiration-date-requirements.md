# Amazon FBA expiration date requirements

Amazon's FBA expiration dates must be on every unit and on the outer box in 36-point or larger type, outside any bag or bubble wrap; MM-YYYY is the format both of Amazon's pages accept, and at check-in the product needs time to be used in full plus 90 days (a 240-serving supplement expiring 2028-08 must arrive by 2027-09-06) or the minimum in Amazon's table, from 90 days for snacks to 900 days for cleaning and beauty products.

- Format on both pages: MM-YYYY
- Outer box: 36 pt or larger
- Shelf life at check-in: use + 90 days
- Baby formula: 180 days

## The date format

Amazon's two pages disagree on the order of day and month. Product packaging requirements: "MM-DD-YYYY or MM-YYYY". Expiration dates on FBA products: DD-MM-YYYY (preferred numeric), MM-YYYY, or DD-MON-YYYY such as 15-MAR-2026. **MM-YYYY is on both lists**, so it is the safe choice; Amazon reads it as the first day of the month, and you enter that day in Send to Amazon (08-2028 is entered as 01-08-2028). If the printed date is in another format, cover it with a sticker in the correct one. Medical devices follow the FDA's YYYY-MM-DD.

## Labels and boxes

- Every unit and the outer box show the date; case packs, multipacks and display boxes on the outer packaging and each item inside.
- On the outer box in 36-point or larger font.
- If the unit is bagged or bubble wrapped, the date label goes on the outside of the prep.
- One expiry date per ASIN per box: units with different dates go in different boxes.
- All ingestible products, including animal products, need an expiry or manufacturing date; baby food and formula an expiry date.
- Perishable items are not allowed.

## Questions

### What expiration date format does Amazon FBA accept?

Product packaging requirements accept MM-DD-YYYY or MM-YYYY; Expiration dates on FBA products lists DD-MM-YYYY (preferred), MM-YYYY and DD-MON-YYYY. MM-YYYY appears on both, and Amazon reads it as the first day of the month.

### How big must the expiration date be on the box?

36-point or larger on the outer box, and also on each unit. Lot numbers alone are not enough.

### How much shelf life does Amazon require?

Enough to use the product in full plus 90 days at check-in (240 daily servings: 330 days), or the minimum in Amazon's table; baby formula 180 days.
