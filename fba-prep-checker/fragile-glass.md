# FBA prep for glass and fragile items

Glass, ceramic and other fragile items must be bubble wrapped (or boxed) so nothing is exposed: at least two layers, each unit and each piece wrapped on its own, no empty space, and the item must survive five drops from 3 feet; a glass jar candle needs bubble wrap and the drop test, and a box if it fails the drop test.

## What Amazon requires for fragile items

- Wrap all units individually: in a set of four wine glasses, each glass is wrapped.
- Use two layers of bubble wrap at minimum, on all sides, leaving nothing exposed.
- No empty space in the package, and no more than three inches of wrap past the item.
- The barcode must scan without unwrapping: if the wrap covers it, put the barcode on the outside.
- If a fragile liquid leaks with bubble wrap, add a cap seal.

The drop test: drop the item five times from 3 feet onto concrete, on the top, the bottom, the largest side, a bottom corner and the longest bottom edge. Damage to the container is acceptable if nothing leaks and the product is intact. Amazon does not run the test for you. If the item fails after bubble wrap, it must be boxed in a six-sided box that does not give way, with the contents secured; over 10 lb, a double-wall corrugated box.

Books: a hardcover over $60 or a softcover over $75 needs bubble wrap rather than a bag. Premium beauty products, high retail value items and collectibles are bubble wrapped too. Sharp items are not: bubble wrap is not accepted for them.

Amazon's prep guidance category **Fragile, glass**: glass or otherwise fragile, or a liquid in a glass container (for example glass, china, picture frames, clocks, mirrors, bottles of olive oil).

- Place product in bubble wrap or a bubble bag to prevent damage. Products can also be placed in a protective overbox.
- Product bag or box must be secured or taped.
- Product can pass a drop test on a hard surface without breaking.
- Barcode must be scannable.

## The worked example, step by step

1. **Bubble wrap each unit individually: at least two layers, nothing exposed, no empty space, no more than 3 in of wrap past the item; wrap pieces within the unit separately.** It is fragile: Amazon requires bubble wrap or a box so the item is not exposed in any way.
2. **Drop test: drop it five times from 3 feet onto concrete (top, bottom, largest side, bottom corner, longest bottom edge); it must survive. If it breaks, box it.** The item must pass a three-foot drop test after bubble wrap; Amazon does not perform it.
3. **Put the barcode on the outside of the bag or wrap (or use a clear bag it scans through), flat, not on a corner or curve, 0.25 in from the edge.** The barcode must be scannable after prep without opening or unwrapping.

## Questions

### How many layers of bubble wrap does Amazon require?

At least two layers, with the item not exposed anywhere.

### What if my fragile item fails the drop test?

Box it: a six-sided box that does not give way when pressed, contents secured, and test it again.

### Can I bubble wrap knives?

No. Bubble wrap is not considered effective protection for sharp items; they need cut- and puncture-resistant packaging.
