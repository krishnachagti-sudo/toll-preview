# FBA prep for small items and jewelry

Any product whose longest side is under 2 1/8 inches (the length of a credit card) must be bagged or boxed with the barcode visible outside, and jewelry must be in a bag or box at any size: a 2 in USB drive counts as small, and stud earrings on a 1.75 in card need a bag (no suffocation warning: the smallest bag opens under 5 in).

## What Amazon requires for small items and jewelry

- Items with packaging smaller than 2-1/8 in must be bagged or boxed; the barcode must be visible on the outside.
- Box sleeves must fit securely so they do not slide off.
- Jewelry, regardless of size, must be packaged against dirt, dust and humidity, in a bag (pouch) or box with the barcode outside.
- Mesh bags must close securely with a scannable barcode, or Amazon adds a new bag and barcode.
- Boxed jewelry made of easily cleaned materials needs no extra bag; boxed jewelry with fabric or dust-prone parts needs an extra bag or box.

Amazon's bagging decision tree uses a shorter cut-off (a longest side under 2 inches) than its small-products page and prep guidance (under 2 1/8 inches). The checker uses 2 1/8 inches, the stricter of the two.

Amazon's prep guidance category **Small**: longest side less than 2 1/8 inches (width of a credit card) (for example jewelry, keychains, flash drives).

- Place product in a transparent poly bag.
- Bag must have a suffocation warning.
- Bag must be secured if not self-adhesive.
- Barcode must be scannable.

## The worked example, step by step

1. **Bag or box it, with the barcode visible on the outside; a box sleeve must fit tightly.** Items with packaging smaller than 2-1/8" (credit card length) must be bagged or boxed.
2. **Put jewelry in a bag (pouch) or box with the barcode visible outside; boxed jewelry with fabric or dust-prone parts needs an extra bag or box.** Jewelry, regardless of size, must be packaged to protect against dirt, dust, and humidity.
3. **Put the barcode on the outside of the bag or wrap (or use a clear bag it scans through), flat, not on a corner or curve, 0.25 in from the edge.** The barcode must be scannable after prep without opening or unwrapping.

## Questions

### What size counts as a small item for FBA prep?

A longest side under 2 1/8 inches (2.125 in), the width of a credit card.

### Does jewelry need a bag for FBA?

It must be in a bag (pouch) or box with the barcode outside, whatever its size.
