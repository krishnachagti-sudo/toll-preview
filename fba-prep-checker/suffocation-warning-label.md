# Suffocation warning labels to print

Print Amazon's suffocation warning, "WARNING: To avoid danger of suffocation, keep this plastic film away from babies and children. Do not use this bag in cribs, bed, carriages, or play pens. This bag is not a toy.", at the size your bag needs (10 pt for a bag under 29 inches length plus width, 14 pt for 30 to 39, 18 pt for 40 to 59, 24 pt for 60 or more) from the free warning sheet: at 10 pt, 24 fit on a Letter sheet, about 4 x 0.8 in each, ready to cut.

- At 10 pt, per Letter sheet: 24
- Price: Free
- Wording: Amazon's, exactly

## Which print size

Amazon's minimum print size by the bag's length plus width.

| Bag length plus width | Minimum print size |
| --- | ---: |
| 60 inches or more | 24 point |
| 40 to 59 inches | 18 point |
| 30 to 39 inches | 14 point |
| less than 29 inches | 10 point |

Stick it in a prominent place on the bag, where it reads clearly against the bag and any other print. A bag with the warning already printed on it needs no sticker. Bags with an opening under 5 inches need no warning.

## How the sheet prints

- Letter or A4 full-sheet label paper: boxes are tiled with dashed cut lines, two across at 10 pt and one across from 14 pt.
- Thermal labels: one warning per label; if your label is too small for the print size, the checker says the smallest size that fits.
- Print at 100% (Actual size).

Product barcodes (FNSKU) are a separate PDF, $0.001 a label with a $0.25 minimum.

## Questions

### Is there a free Amazon suffocation warning label PDF?

Yes: the checker's warning sheet is free, in Amazon's wording, at the print size your bag needs.

### What size should the warning sticker be?

Large enough for the text at the minimum print size for the bag (10 pt for a bag under 29 in length plus width). At 10 pt two fit across a Letter sheet, about 4 x 0.8 in each.
