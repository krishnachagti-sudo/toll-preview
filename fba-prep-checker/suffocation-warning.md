# Amazon suffocation warning requirements

Amazon requires a suffocation warning on every poly bag with an opening of 5 inches or larger when flat, printed on the bag or as a sticker, in its exact wording and at a minimum print size set by the bag's length plus width: 10 pt under 29 inches, 14 pt for 30 to 39, 18 pt for 40 to 59 and 24 pt for 60 or more, so a 10 x 13 in bag needs 10 pt and a 20 x 30 in bag 18 pt.

- A 10 x 13 in bag: 10 pt
- Opening that needs it: 5 in or more (flat)
- Largest print size: 24 pt (60 in or more)
- Bag thickness: 1.5 mil or more

## The wording

**WARNING: To avoid danger of suffocation, keep this plastic film away from babies and children. Do not use this bag in cribs, bed, carriages, or play pens. This bag is not a toy.**

It must be in a prominent place, legible, and contrasted to the bag and anything else printed on it, by typography, layout or color. Amazon says certain states and cities require the warning and that it applies to items sold throughout North America.

## When a bag needs it

1. Is the item bagged? If not, no warning.
2. Is the warning already printed on the bag? Then the requirement is met.
3. Is the bag opening 5 inches or more when lying flat? Then apply a warning sticker.

Adult products in opaque bags, apparel, baby products, liquids, perforated packaging, powders, small items and footwear in poly bags all carry the warning when the opening is 5 inches or more. Failure to apply it may lead to the items being re-bagged.

## Questions

### When does a poly bag need a suffocation warning for Amazon?

When its opening is 5 inches or larger, measured flat (Amazon's Bagging requirements say "five-inch opening or larger"), unless the warning is already printed on the bag.

### Does exactly 5 inches need a warning?

Yes. The requirement reads "a five-inch opening or larger", so 5 inches counts. (The page's decision tree writes "\>5", the checker uses the stricter wording.)

### What must the warning say?

WARNING: To avoid danger of suffocation, keep this plastic film away from babies and children. Do not use this bag in cribs, bed, carriages, or play pens. This bag is not a toy.

### What font size must the suffocation warning be?

By the bag's length plus width: 60 in or more 24 pt; 40 to 59 in 18 pt; 30 to 39 in 14 pt; less than 29 in 10 pt.
