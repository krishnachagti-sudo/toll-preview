"Sold as set" labels for FBA

Units sold together as one (bundles, multipacks, toy sets, a case of drinks) must be packaged so they cannot be separated and labeled "Sold as set", "Ready to ship" or "This is a set. Do not separate.", with the individual barcodes covered or turned inward and a unique ASIN for the set if the items already have one; 30 labels print on a Letter sheet of 1 x 2 5/8 in labels, free.

The rules for sets

  • Add a label to the unit that clearly states that the products are to be received and sold as a single unit. For example, "Sold as set," "Ready to ship," or "This is a set. Do not separate."
  • Provide a unique ASIN for the set if the individual units in the set already have an ASIN.
  • The barcodes on the individual units must not face outward or the barcodes must be covered.
  • The set's barcode must be scannable.

A unit in several categories gets every category's prep: shampoo and conditioner sold as a set need the liquid prep and a set label. A set that is not otherwise bagged, shrink-wrapped, bubble wrapped or contained may need a box. Weight and size for fees are those of the set packaged together.

Worked example

Ceramic mug set of 2 needs bubble wrap, a "Sold as set" label and an FNSKU label on the outside.

Read the rest: Worked example
  • Bubble wrap each unit individually: at least two layers, nothing exposed, no empty space, no more than 3 in of wrap past the item; wrap pieces within the unit separately.
  • Drop test: drop it five times from 3 feet onto concrete (top, bottom, largest side, bottom corner, longest bottom edge); it must survive. If it breaks, box it.
  • Package the set together so it cannot be separated (bag, shrink wrap or box), and label it "Sold as set", "Ready to ship" or "This is a set. Do not separate."
  • Turn the individual units' barcodes inward or cover them; only the set's barcode may scan.
  • Put the barcode on the outside of the bag or wrap (or use a clear bag it scans through), flat, not on a corner or curve, 0.25 in from the edge.

Questions

What should a sold as set label say?

Amazon's examples: "Sold as set", "Ready to ship" or "This is a set. Do not separate."

Do I need to cover the barcodes on items in a bundle?

Yes: the barcodes on the individual units must not face outward or must be covered; only the set's barcode may scan.
