FBA prep guides

FBA prep checklist for your first shipment

Before you send your first FBA box: put a scannable barcode on every unit (an FNSKU if you resell), prep each product for every category it is in, add a suffocation warning to bags with a 5-inch or larger opening, label sets and expiration dates, cover every other barcode, then pack in six-sided boxes no bigger than 36 x 25 x 25 in and no heavier than 50 lb, with 2 in of cushioning and no packing peanuts.

Do not send theft-prevention devices, pre-priced labels, price tags or marketing materials. Units that need Amazon to assemble pieces are not accepted, and perishables are not allowed. A product shipped inadequately prepped may be refused, returned or repackaged at your expense.

Steps

  1. Barcode every unit. Each unit needs an exterior scannable barcode with human-readable numbers. Since March 31, 2026 resellers must use Amazon barcodes (FNSKU); brand owners may use the manufacturer barcode. Not on a corner or curve, 0.25 in from the edge.
  2. Find the prep category. In Send to Amazon, Step 1, check each product's prep category; tick every category that applies and prep for all of them.
  3. Prep the units. Bag (1.5 mil, transparent, sealed, at most 3 in past the product), bubble wrap (two layers), box, cap seal or sharp packaging as the category says.
  4. Add warnings and set labels. Bags with a 5-inch opening or larger: suffocation warning at the print size for the bag. Sets: "Sold as set" and the item barcodes covered.
  5. Date expiring products. Expiration date on each unit and 36 pt on the box, outside any prep; one expiry date per ASIN per box.
  6. Cover old barcodes. Cover or black out every other scannable barcode on the unit and on reused boxes.
  7. Pack the boxes. Rigid six-sided boxes, at most 36 x 25 x 25 in and 50 lb (40 lb for jewelry or watches), 2 in of cushioning (bubble wrap, kraft paper, air pillows or foam sheeting; no packing peanuts, foam strips, crinkle wrap or shredded paper). Each box gets its own FBA Box ID label.

Questions

What is the maximum box size and weight for FBA?

36 x 25 x 25 inches and 50 lb for a box of several items (a single oversize item may exceed them; over 50 lb needs Team Lift labels).
