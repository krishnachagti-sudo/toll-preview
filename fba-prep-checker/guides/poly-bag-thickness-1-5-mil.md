# Poly bag thickness for FBA: what 1.5 mil means

Amazon requires FBA poly bags (and shrink wrap for softlines) to be at least 1.5 mil thick, a mil being a thousandth of an inch, so the minimum is 38.1 microns or 0.0381 mm; a bag sold as 1 mil (25.4 microns) is too thin, and 2 mil (50.8 microns) or more passes.

- Minimum: 1.5 mil
- In microns: 38.1
- In mm: 0.0381

Bag suppliers sell by mil in the US and by micron (or gauge) elsewhere; check the unit on the listing. Amazon's rule is a minimum: a thicker bag is allowed as long as it is transparent (or carries the barcode outside), sealed, and no more than 3 in past the product.

> Not affiliated with Amazon. Rules are from Amazon's US Seller Central help pages, read November 8, 2026; Amazon may change them, and product-specific guidance in Send to Amazon wins. Amazon says determining prep is the seller's responsibility.

## Questions

### What thickness poly bag does Amazon require?

At least 1.5 mil (thousandth of an inch), per Amazon's Bagging and Softlines pages.

### How many microns is 1.5 mil?

38.1 microns (1 mil = 25.4 microns).
