# Does my product need a poly bag for FBA?

Your product needs a poly bag for FBA if it is fabric or collects dust, has loose parts or is sold as a set, is smaller than 2 1/8 in, is a powder, pellet or granular product that spills, is a hazmat liquid or a spray bottle without a lock, contains scents, or is a book with a cloth, leather or glossy cover or a loose item; a boxed toaster does not, and an $85 hardcover gets bubble wrap instead.

## Amazon's bagging decision tree

1. Fabric clothing, apparel, or prone to collecting dust: bag.
2. Multiple items or loose parts: bag.
3. A book: go to the book questions.
4. Longest side under 2 in (2 1/8 in on the small-products page): bag.
5. Powder, pellet or granular product that spills when shaken hard: bag.
6. Liquid in a spray bottle: see the cap seal rules (bag unless sealed and locking).
7. Hazmat liquid: bag.
8. Otherwise no bag. Books: hardcover over $60 or softcover over $75, bubble wrap; cloth, leather, glossy or shiny cover, or a loose item such as a CD or toy, bag.

Every bag: at least 1.5 mil, transparent (opaque for adult products), sealed, no more than 3 in past the product, the barcode scannable, and a suffocation warning when the opening is 5 in or more.

## Questions

### Do all FBA products need to be poly bagged?

No. A product whose own packaging protects it (a sealed box, for example) and that is not in a bagging category needs no bag; it still needs a scannable barcode.
