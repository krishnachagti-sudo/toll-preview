FBA prep guides

What to do when Amazon flags your shipment for prep

When Amazon flags a shipment for prep, the problem shows in your Shipping Queue and Inbound Performance Dashboard as one of four kinds (barcode missing, unit mislabeled, barcode cannot be scanned, or unit or shipment preparation errors); since January 1, 2026 Amazon no longer preps units for you, so fix the units that have not shipped, change the prep for the SKU before the next shipment, and expect fees or refusal for units that arrive wrong.

Amazon's list of label problems (Use Amazon barcodes for FBA).
ProblemWhat it means
Barcode missingThe item arrived without a proper barcode.
Unit mislabeledThe barcode does not match the physical product.
Barcode cannot be scannedSmudged, on a corner or curve, or on a reflective surface.
Unit/Shipment preparation errorsLabeling, packaging and prep, or shipping requirement errors.

A product that needs an Amazon barcode and arrives without one is marked defective and stickered by Amazon; products that miss bagging, bubble wrap or opaque requirements may get a preparation defect fee. Non-compliant inventory may be refused, returned or repackaged at your expense, and non-compliant sharp items may be disposed of without reimbursement.

Not affiliated with Amazon. Rules are from Amazon's US Seller Central help pages, read November 8, 2026; Amazon may change them, and product-specific guidance in Send to Amazon wins. Amazon says determining prep is the seller's responsibility.

Steps

  1. Read the problem. In the Shipping Queue or Inbound Performance Dashboard, find which of the four problems it is and which SKU.
  2. Check the SKU's prep. Run the product through the checker (or Amazon's prep guidance) for every category it is in.
  3. Fix units not yet sent. Relabel, bag, wrap or box the units you still hold; cover any other barcode.
  4. Change the SKU going forward. Update the prep category in Send to Amazon if it was wrong; Amazon may also add prep requirements after damage.
  5. Keep records. Keep what you shipped and how it was prepped; shipments created after January 1, 2026 without proper prep and labeling are not eligible for reimbursement if damaged or untraceable.

Questions

Will Amazon fix my prep errors?

Not as a service any more: prep and item labeling ended January 1, 2026 in the US. Amazon may still sticker a unit missing a barcode and report it as a defect.
