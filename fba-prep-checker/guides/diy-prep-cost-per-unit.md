# DIY FBA prep cost per unit vs a prep center

Amazon used to bag and label a standard-size apparel unit for $1.25 ($0.70 bag plus $0.55 label), so 1,000 units cost $1,250.00; a prep center such as AMZ Prep starts at $0.40 an item ($400.00 for 1,000); doing it yourself costs your bags, labels and time per unit, and you break even with a prep center when your materials plus the value of your time per unit fall under $0.40.

- Amazon bag + label (ended): $1.25
- Prep center from: $0.40 an item
- Endwise FNSKU labels: $0.001 a label

## Your own cost per unit

Per unit: the bag (if needed), the warning sticker (if the opening is 5 in or more), the FNSKU label, bubble wrap or a box for fragile items, tape, and your minutes per unit times what your hour is worth. Material prices are your suppliers'; this page does not quote them.

One product checked on the tool page is free, as are the warning, set and expiration label sheets; a list costs $0.01 a SKU (the first 500 a month, then $0.004); FNSKU label PDFs cost $0.001 a label, at least $0.25 a PDF; never more than $4.99 a month.

> Not affiliated with Amazon. Rules are from Amazon's US Seller Central help pages, read November 8, 2026; Amazon may change them, and product-specific guidance in Send to Amazon wins. Amazon says determining prep is the seller's responsibility.
