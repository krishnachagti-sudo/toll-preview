# AccelerList's prep guide vs the prep checker

AccelerList's help article "Amazon Prep Categories and Helpful Instructions" (dated February 5, 2025) explains Amazon's prep instruction codes such as ITEM\_POLYBAGGING and ITEM\_SUFFOSTK and reproduces the prep guidance table; the checker instead works the current Seller Central rules out for one product, with the bag size, warning print size and labels.

## Try the checker on one product

[Check it in the prep checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fba-prep-checker/?name=Cotton+T-shirt%2C+folded&types=apparel&l=10&w=8&h=1&unit=in&wt=6&wtu=oz&seller=reseller&bagw=10&bagl=13&mil=1.5)

## What they offer

- A list of Amazon prep instruction codes with a one-line meaning each (ITEM\_BUBBLEWRAP, ITEM\_CAP\_SEALING, ITEM\_LABELING, ITEM\_SETCREAT and more).
- A copy of Amazon's prep guidance table by category.
- Its ITEM\_SUFFOSTK line gives the threshold as a 12 cm opening; Amazon's US pages say 5 inches (12.7 cm).
- Part of AccelerList's help center for its listing and shipping software (its pricing page did not load for us).

## What the prep checker does

- The current US rules, read November 8, 2026, with each step linked.
- Per-product verdicts, not a table to interpret.
- Pay per use, no subscription; one product free on the page.

## When AccelerList is the better choice

If you already list and ship through AccelerList, its prep codes match what its software shows you, and its workflow covers listing, which the checker does not.

One product checked on the tool page is free, as are the warning, set and expiration label sheets; a list costs $0.01 a SKU (the first 500 a month, then $0.004); FNSKU label PDFs cost $0.001 a label, at least $0.25 a PDF; never more than $4.99 a month.

> Not affiliated with Amazon. Rules are from Amazon's US Seller Central help pages, read November 8, 2026; Amazon may change them, and product-specific guidance in Send to Amazon wins. Amazon says determining prep is the seller's responsibility.
