# Doing FBA prep yourself vs a prep center

A prep center does the physical work: AMZ Prep publishes a starting rate of $0.40 per item for FBA prep (receiving, FNSKU labeling, poly bagging, carton labeling and shipment creation) on standard-size, non-hazmat items, so 1,000 units start at $400; doing it yourself costs bags, labels and time, and the checker tells you exactly what each SKU needs for $0.01 a SKU in a list (one product free).

## What they offer

- AMZ Prep: FBA and Walmart WFS prep from $0.40 per item; case forwarding to FBA from $1.50 per box; DTC fulfillment from $1.00 per order.
- Big, bulky and oversized products, hazmat and online arbitrage inventory are quoted, not at the starting rate.
- No setup or integration fee; onboarding takes 6 days (its pricing page).
- Amazon also lists vetted FBA Prep Providers in Seller Central.

## What the prep checker does

- What each SKU needs, before you buy supplies or brief a prep center.
- The labels to print in-house.
- A checklist CSV per shipment.

## When a prep center is the better choice

At volume, without space or staff, or when inventory ships from overseas, a prep center is usually better: it receives, preps and forwards, and you never touch the units. The checker is for doing small batches yourself or checking what you are paying for.

One product checked on the tool page is free, as are the warning, set and expiration label sheets; a list costs $0.01 a SKU (the first 500 a month, then $0.004); FNSKU label PDFs cost $0.001 a label, at least $0.25 a PDF; never more than $4.99 a month.
