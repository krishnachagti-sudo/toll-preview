# A prep checklist spreadsheet vs the prep checker

A spreadsheet checklist (for example in Google Sheets, which opens Excel files, works offline and shares with permissions) costs nothing and suits a small, stable range of products, but it holds whatever rules were typed into it; the checker applies Amazon's rules as read on November 8, 2026, to each product, and its sources are checked daily for changes.

## Try the checker on one product

[Check it in the prep checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fba-prep-checker/?name=Cotton+T-shirt%2C+folded&types=apparel&l=10&w=8&h=1&unit=in&wt=6&wtu=oz&seller=reseller&bagw=10&bagl=13&mil=1.5)

## What they offer

- Free, flexible, shareable with your team; Google Sheets opens and edits Excel files and works offline.
- Rules are whatever you typed: no link to Amazon's pages, no check that they are current.

## What the prep checker does

- Per-product verdicts from Amazon's current pages.
- Upload a CSV of SKUs, download a checklist CSV for the packer.
- Warning and FNSKU label PDFs.

## When a spreadsheet is the better choice

For a handful of SKUs that never change category, a sheet you trust is simpler, and you can paste the checker's checklist CSV into it.

One product checked on the tool page is free, as are the warning, set and expiration label sheets; a list costs $0.01 a SKU (the first 500 a month, then $0.004); FNSKU label PDFs cost $0.001 a label, at least $0.25 a PDF; never more than $4.99 a month.

> Not affiliated with Amazon. Rules are from Amazon's US Seller Central help pages, read November 8, 2026; Amazon may change them, and product-specific guidance in Send to Amazon wins. Amazon says determining prep is the seller's responsibility.
