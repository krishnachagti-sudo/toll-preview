# ComplianceGate's suffocation warning guide vs the prep checker

ComplianceGate's "Bag Suffocation Warning: US State & Amazon Seller Requirements" (posted February 7, 2023) explains the state laws behind the warning (California, New York, Massachusetts, Virginia and Rhode Island, plus New York City and Chicago, by its research); the checker applies Amazon's own rule (5-inch opening, exact wording, 10 to 24 pt by bag size) to your bag and prints the sticker.

## What they offer

- State-by-state comparison of the warning laws: bag dimensions, wording, placement, print methods.
- A section on Amazon's requirement.
- A compliance consultancy and a requirements platform (free consultation call offered; prices not shown on the article).

## What the prep checker does

- Amazon's US rule, applied to the bag you plan to use.
- The print size from Amazon's table, and the gaps in that table handled.
- A free printable warning sheet in Amazon's wording.

## When ComplianceGate is the better choice

For the legal background, products sold outside Amazon, or a compliance review of a product line, ComplianceGate covers what Amazon's page does not. The checker sticks to Amazon's pages.

One product checked on the tool page is free, as are the warning, set and expiration label sheets; a list costs $0.01 a SKU (the first 500 a month, then $0.004); FNSKU label PDFs cost $0.001 a label, at least $0.25 a PDF; never more than $4.99 a month.

> Not affiliated with Amazon. Rules are from Amazon's US Seller Central help pages, read November 8, 2026; Amazon may change them, and product-specific guidance in Send to Amazon wins. Amazon says determining prep is the seller's responsibility.
