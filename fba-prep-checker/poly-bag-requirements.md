# Amazon FBA poly bag requirements

Amazon's FBA poly bags must be at least 1.5 mil thick, fully enclose the item and be sealed with tape or an adhesive strip, be transparent (or carry the barcode outside), protrude no more than 3 inches past the product, and carry a suffocation warning when the opening is 5 inches or more: a folded 10 x 8 x 1 in T-shirt fits a bag of about 9 in x 11 in, so a 10 x 13 in bag is within the limit and needs the warning at 10 pt.

- Thickness, at least: 1.5 mil
- Past the product: 3 in at most
- Warning from: 5 in opening
- Sealed: Tape, adhesive strip or heat seal

## The rules

- The bag fully encloses the item and is sealed with tape or an adhesive strip; heat sealing is an acceptable alternative.
- At least 1.5 mil (thousandth of an inch) thick.
- Transparent, so the barcode scans through it, or with the barcode on the outside of the bag.
- Completely sealed.
- The poly bag or shrink wrap must not protrude more than 3 inches past the dimensions of the product.
- Bags with a 5-inch opening or larger (measured flat) need a suffocation warning.
- Adult products use opaque bagging or shrink wrap.
- A liquid that leaks in a bag also gets a cap seal.

## Bag sizes for common products

The smallest flat bag that holds a product is about its width plus its height by its length plus its height. That is our geometry, not an Amazon figure; Amazon's rule is the 3-inch limit past the product, so the largest bag column adds 3 inches to each side.

## When to bag

Amazon requires bagging for items that can be damaged by dust or leakage, have loose parts or are sold in a set, are small, contain scents, or contain adult content. Its list of product types that can require bagging: pellets, powders, and granular products, plush items, apparel, fabric, and textiles, small products, baby products, books, hazmat liquids, heavily scented items, trigger spray bottles and collectibles.

## Questions

### How thick must FBA poly bags be?

At least 1.5 mil (thousandths of an inch), per Amazon's Bagging requirements.

### How big should the poly bag be?

Big enough to hold the product without forcing it, and not protruding more than 3 inches past the product's dimensions.

### Must FBA poly bags be clear?

Transparent so the barcode scans through it, or with the barcode on the outside; adult products must use black or opaque bags.
