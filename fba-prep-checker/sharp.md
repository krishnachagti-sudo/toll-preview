# FBA prep for sharp items

Sharp items (knives, blades, scissors, needles, razors and the like) must arrive already packaged so every edge and point is covered by cut- and puncture-resistant packaging that cannot come loose, such as hardened plastic, a blister or clamshell pack, a sheath, Styrofoam or a fully enclosed thick corrugated box; bubble wrap or a non-corrugated box is not accepted, Amazon does not prep sharp items, and a product over 10 lb that is sharp goes in a double-wall corrugated box.

## What Amazon requires for sharp items

- Packaging must completely cover the sharp edges and points.
- It must be cut- and puncture-resistant, so nothing can pierce it during handling, storage or shipping.
- The item must stay contained at every step, from receiving to delivery to the customer.
- Acceptable: hardened plastic, blister packs, clamshell packs, a hardened plastic sheath, Styrofoam, or thick corrugated cardboard (fully enclosed boxes only).
- Not acceptable: bubble wrap or a non-corrugated box.

Amazon's examples of sharp units include knives, blades, shears, scissors, cutters, saw blades, barbed wire, hardware nails, pruners, axes, pinboard pins, needles, razors, trimmers, steel wire, graters, peelers, slicers, scalpels and swords. Non-compliant sharp units may be refused or disposed of, without reimbursement and at your cost.

Amazon's prep guidance category **Sharp**: sharp units are objects with an edge or point that are able to cut or pierce packaging material or human skin (for example knives, blades, shears, scissors, cutters, saw blades, barbed wire, hardware nails, axe/mattock, steel wire, graters, peelers, slicers, scalpels, swords, tools).

- No additional prep required. Instead, follow the guidelines on Packaging sharp units.
- Barcode must be scannable.

## The worked example, step by step

1. **Package it so every sharp edge and point is covered by cut- and puncture-resistant packaging that stays closed (hardened plastic, blister or clamshell pack, sheath, Styrofoam or thick, fully enclosed corrugated box).** Bubble wrap or a non-corrugated box is not acceptable for sharp items; Amazon does not prep them, and non-compliant units may be disposed of without reimbursement.

## Questions

### Will Amazon prep sharp items?

No. All sharp items must arrive at Amazon already packaged.

### Is bubble wrap enough for a knife?

No. Amazon says bubble wrap or a non-corrugated box is not acceptable because points and edges can pierce it.
