# FBA prep for liquids over 4.2 oz

A non-hazmat liquid of 4.2 oz (125 ml) or less needs no extra prep; above that, Amazon's cap seal rules decide by container: a 16 oz pump bottle with no secondary seal like this one needs a cap seal, a spray bottle without a lock needs a bag, a glass bottle needs bubble wrap, and every hazmat liquid must be bagged.

## What Amazon requires for liquids

Amazon treats liquids, pastes, gels and creams alike. What decides the prep is the volume, whether it is hazmat, whether the container is fragile, the lid, and whether it has a secondary seal (induction seal, safety ring, clips, plastic wrap band, child-resistant cap, heat shrink wrap or a sealed box).

A cap seal is sealing tape: horizontally around a twist or screw cap, vertically over a flip or clip top, and vertically over a pump, stuck to the bottle on both sides. If a liquid still leaks in a bag or shrink wrap, Amazon adds a cap seal too.

Amazon's prep guidance category **Liquids (non-glass)**: liquid or viscous without a double seal (for example soap, spray bottles, lotion, honey).

- Place product in a transparent poly bag to protect from leakage.
- Bag must have a suffocation warning.
- Bag must be secured if not self-adhesive.
- Barcode must be scannable.

## Questions

### Do liquids under 4.2 oz need prep for FBA?

No additional prep is required for a non-hazmat liquid of 4.2 oz (125 mL) or less, unless it is repeatedly damaged in transit or handling (Amazon, Liquid products).

### Does a pump bottle need a cap seal?

Yes, unless it has both a secondary seal and a locking mechanism; Amazon says pumps without a locking mechanism must have a cap seal even with a secondary seal.

### How do I prep a spray bottle for FBA?

Bag it, unless it has a secondary seal and a locking trigger.

### How do I prep a liquid in a glass bottle?

Bubble wrap it; add a cap seal only if the bubble wrap does not prevent leaks. Hazmat liquids in glass over 4.2 oz must also be boxed.
