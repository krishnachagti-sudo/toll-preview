The rules it applies

Since January 1, 2026 Amazon no longer preps or labels FBA inventory in the US store, so every unit has to arrive ready. The checker applies Amazon's US prep pages: bags and the suffocation warning, liquids, barcodes, expiration dates and the size tier.

What it does not cover

  • Stores other than Amazon's US store.
  • Hazmat status and the drop test: those are your answers.
  • Product-specific guidance in Send to Amazon, which wins over the general pages.
Read the warning sizes, bags, liquids, barcodes, notes and prices

Suffocation warning

Bags with a 5-inch opening or larger, measured flat, need the warning printed on the bag or as a sticker, in Amazon's exact wording. The print size depends on the bag's length plus width:

Length plus widthMinimum print size

Amazon's table does not cover 29 to 30, 39 to 40 or 59 to 60 inches; there the checker uses the larger size and says so.

Bags

At least 1.5 mil thick, transparent (opaque for adult products), fully sealed, the barcode scannable through it or on the outside, and no more than 3 inches past the product.

Liquids

Non-hazmat liquids of 4.2 oz (125 ml) or less need no extra prep. Above that, the container and lid decide: a cap seal, a bag (spray bottles without a lock, every hazmat liquid) or bubble wrap (glass).

Barcodes

Since March 31, 2026 resellers must put an Amazon barcode (FNSKU) on every unit even if it has a UPC; brand owners can use the manufacturer barcode. Labels from 1 x 2 to 2 x 3 inches, black on white, removable adhesive, printed at 300 dpi or more on a thermal or laser printer (not inkjet).

  • Rules from Amazon's US Seller Central help pages, read November 8, 2026: the sources load with the page.
  • Not affiliated with Amazon. Amazon says determining prep is your responsibility, and its product-specific guidance in Send to Amazon wins. Hazmat status and the drop test are your answers.
  • Label sheet positions: Amazon gives each sheet's label size and count, not the margins, so labels are centered on the page. Print the free alignment test on plain paper first and nudge if needed.
  • Product lists and label text are used to make the answer and the PDF and are not kept. The single check runs in your browser.
