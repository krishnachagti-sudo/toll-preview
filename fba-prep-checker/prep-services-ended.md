# Amazon FBA prep services ended: what you do now

Starting January 1, 2026, Amazon no longer offers prep and item labeling services for FBA shipments in the US store, including inventory from AWD, AGL, Amazon SEND and Supply Chain Portal that goes through FBA: every unit must arrive bagged, wrapped or boxed as its category requires and labeled, and shipments created after that date that arrive unprepped are not eligible for reimbursement if damaged or untraceable.

- Ended: January 1, 2026
- Old labeling fee: $0.55 a unit
- Old bagging fee: $0.70 (standard)
- Old bubble wrap fee: $1.04 (standard)

## What changed

Amazon's notice: the change applies to all inventory sent to its US FBA service, including inventory from Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD), Amazon Global Logistics (AGL), Amazon SEND, and Supply Chain Portal, when that inventory goes through FBA. Shipments created before January 1 still got prep and labeling even if they arrived later. Amazon's options: do it yourself, use your own provider or one of its vetted FBA Prep Providers, or use Ships in Product Packaging (SIPP) for eligible products.

## What to do for each SKU

1. Find its prep category (Send to Amazon, Step 1), and every category it falls in.
2. Bag, bubble wrap, box or cap seal it as the category says.
3. Bags of 5 in opening or more: a suffocation warning at the print size for the bag.
4. Barcode: since March 31, 2026 resellers put an FNSKU on every unit; brand owners may use the manufacturer barcode.
5. Sets: a "Sold as set" label and the item barcodes covered.
6. Expiration-dated products: the date outside the prep, 36 pt on the box.

One product checked on the tool page is free, as are the warning, set and expiration label sheets; a list costs $0.01 a SKU (the first 500 a month, then $0.004); FNSKU label PDFs cost $0.001 a label, at least $0.25 a PDF; never more than $4.99 a month.

## Questions

### Does Amazon still prep FBA inventory?

No. Starting January 1, 2026, Amazon no longer offers prep and item labeling services for FBA shipments in the US store.

### What happens to unprepped units now?

Shipments created after January 1, 2026 that arrive without proper prep and item labeling are not eligible for reimbursement if damaged or untraceable.

### What are my options?

Prep and label yourself, or use a service provider (your own or one from Amazon's vetted FBA Prep Providers). Ships in Product Packaging (SIPP) can lower prep needs for eligible products.
