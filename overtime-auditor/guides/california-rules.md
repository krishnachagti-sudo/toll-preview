# California overtime in one page

California pays time and a half after 8 hours in a day, after 40 in a week and for the first 8 hours on the seventh day of work in the workweek, and double time after 12 hours in a day and after 8 on that seventh day; a 12-hour seventh day is 8.00 hours at 1.5x and 4.00 at 2x.

- 12-hour 7th day: 8.00 hours at 1.5x and 4.00 at 2x
- 12-hour day: 4.00 OT, 0.00 DT
- 4 x 10 on an AWS: 0.00 OT

## The rules

Labor Code 510(a) and 511(b), read November 8, 2026.

| Hours | Rate | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Over 8 in a workday, up to 12 | 1.5x | 510(a) |
| Over 12 in a workday | 2x | 510(a) |
| Over 40 in the workweek (hours not already daily overtime) | 1.5x | 510(a) |
| First 8 on the seventh day of work in the workweek | 1.5x | 510(a) |
| Over 8 on that seventh day | 2x | 510(a) |
| Alternative workweek: over the scheduled hours, up to 12 | 1.5x | 511(b) |
| Alternative workweek: over 12, or over 8 on a day beyond the scheduled workdays | 2x | 511(b) |

## Workday and workweek

Labor Code 500(a): "“Workday” and “day” mean any consecutive 24-hour period commencing at the same time each calendar day." 500(b): "“Workweek” and “week” mean any seven consecutive days, starting with the same calendar day each week. “Workweek” is a fixed and regularly recurring period of 168 hours, seven consecutive 24-hour periods."

## Questions

### How is overtime calculated in California?

Time and a half after 8 hours in a day and after 40 in a week, and for the first 8 hours on the seventh day of work in the workweek; double time after 12 hours in a day and after 8 hours on that seventh day (Labor Code 510(a)).

### An employee works a 12-hour shift on the seventh day: what is owed?

8.00 hours at 1.5x and 4.00 hours at 2x.

### Is the same hour ever paid twice?

"Nothing in this section requires an employer to combine more than one rate of overtime compensation in order to calculate the amount to be paid to an employee for any hour of overtime work" (Labor Code 510(a)). The auditor takes daily hours out before testing the week against 40.
