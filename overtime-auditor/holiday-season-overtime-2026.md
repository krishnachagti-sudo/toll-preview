# Holiday-season overtime, 2026

Thanksgiving (November 26, 2026) and Christmas (December 25, 2026) are not overtime days by themselves, but a seven-day holiday week is: worked 8 hours a day and 10 on the seventh at $20, Christmas week owes $1,360.00 in California, $1,340.00 in Kentucky and $1,340.00 under the federal rule alone.

- Thanksgiving: November 26, 2026
- Christmas: December 25, 2026
- California week: $1,360.00
- Federal only: $1,340.00

## Questions

### Is Thanksgiving or Christmas double time in California?

Not by date. Labor Code 510 sets premiums by hours in the workday and the workweek and names no holiday premium; holiday pay comes from a policy or contract. A seven-day week does bring the seventh-day rule in.

### Does working seven days in the holiday week change overtime?

In California and Kentucky, yes. Christmas week worked 8 hours a day and 10 on the seventh day: California 16.00 overtime and 2.00 double-time hours, Kentucky 18.00 overtime, federal 18.00.
