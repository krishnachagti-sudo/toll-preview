California double time

California pays double time for hours over 12 in a workday and over 8 on the seventh day of work in the workweek; a 13-hour day gives 1.00 double-time hour, and a 12-hour seventh day gives 8.00 hours at 1.5x and 4.00 at 2x.

The rule

Labor Code 510(a): "Any work in excess of 12 hours in one day shall be compensated at the rate of no less than twice the regular rate of pay for an employee. In addition, any work in excess of eight hours on any seventh day of a workweek shall be compensated at the rate of no less than twice the regular rate of pay of an employee."

Worked out by the auditor from the hours shown and the rules quoted with the date read. It assumes a non-exempt employee covered by the rule shown; a wage order, contract or policy may require more. Not legal, tax or payroll advice.

Questions

When is double time required in California?

For hours over 12 in one workday, and for hours over 8 on the seventh day of work in a workweek (Labor Code 510(a)).

Is the seventh day all double time?

No. The first 8 hours on the seventh day are 1.5x; only the hours past 8 are 2x. A 12-hour seventh day is 8.00 hours at 1.5x and 4.00 at 2x.

Does a holiday mean double time in California?

Labor Code 510 sets premiums by hours worked in the workday and the workweek; it names no holiday premium. Holiday pay, if any, comes from a policy or contract.
