Qualified overtime for W-2 code TT

Qualified overtime for a California employee who works three 13-hour days at $22 is $0.00: only the FLSA weekly premium counts, and the $198.00 of California daily and double-time premium on a 39-hour week is excluded; report qualified overtime in W-2 box 12, code TT.

Code TT assumes an employee who is FLSA overtime-eligible and whose employer is covered by 29 U.S.C. 207(a). A workweek that spans New Year is counted in the year it ends; use the year it was paid if that differs. Not tax advice.

Steps

  1. Find the FLSA overtime hours. Hours over 40 in each workweek. California daily, double-time and seventh-day hours do not add FLSA hours.
  2. Find the FLSA regular rate. All straight-time pay divided by all hours worked that week.
  3. Multiply. FLSA overtime hours x one-half x the regular rate, per workweek (FS-2026-13, Q12).
  4. Add up the year. Report the year's total in box 12 with code TT, in full even above the employee's deduction limit (FS-2026-13, Q10).

Questions

Do California daily overtime hours count for no tax on overtime?

Only the part the FLSA requires. FS-2026-13 Q16: "only the amount minimally necessary to satisfy 29 USC § 207 is qualified overtime compensation for purposes of the tax deduction." Three 13-hour days are 39 hours, under 40, so $0.00 of the $198.00 premium is qualified.

How is qualified overtime worked out?

FS-2026-13 Q12: "Number of FLSA hours worked in excess of 40 hours in a workweek x One-half x Employee's FLSA regular rate of pay = Qualified overtime compensation for workweek"

What about double time?

FS-2026-13 Q16 gives the example: 50 hours at $20 paid at double time; only the $10 an hour FLSA premium on 10 hours, $100, is qualified. The auditor gives $100.00 for the same week.

How much is the deduction?

Up to $12,500 a year ($25,000 joint), 2025 through 2028, phasing out above $150,000 of modified adjusted gross income ($300,000 joint) (IRS).

What goes in W-2 box 12 code TT?

FS-2026-13 Q10: "an employer must report the amount of qualified overtime compensation paid to an employee beginning in 2026, on Form W-2 in box 12, using code TT." The full amount is reported, even when it is more than the employee can deduct.
