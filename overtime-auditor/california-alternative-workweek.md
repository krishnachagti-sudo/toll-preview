# California alternative workweek

Under a valid 4/10 alternative workweek, overtime starts after the 10 scheduled hours a day, so four 10-hour days have 0.00 overtime hours; hours over 12 are still double time, and without a valid election the same week has 8.00 overtime hours.

- 4/10 week, overtime: 0.00
- Same week, no election: 8.00
- 11 and 13-hour days: 3.00 OT, 1.00 DT

## The rule

Labor Code 511(a): "the employees of an employer may adopt a regularly scheduled alternative workweek that authorizes work by the affected employees for no longer than 10 hours per day within a 40-hour workweek without the payment to the affected employees of an overtime rate of compensation pursuant to this section. A proposal to adopt an alternative workweek schedule shall be deemed adopted only if it receives approval in a secret ballot election by at least two-thirds of affected employees in a readily identifiable work unit."

Labor Code 511(b): "An affected employee working longer than eight hours but not more than 12 hours in a day pursuant to an alternative workweek schedule adopted pursuant to this section shall be paid an overtime rate of compensation of no less than one and one-half times the regular rate of pay of the employee for any work in excess of the regularly scheduled hours established by the alternative workweek agreement and for any work in excess of 40 hours per week. An overtime rate of compensation of no less than double the regular rate of pay of the employee shall be paid for any work in excess of 12 hours per day and for any work in excess of eight hours on those days worked beyond the regularly scheduled workdays established by the alternative workweek agreement."

Labor Code 511(e): "The results of any election conducted pursuant to this section shall be reported by an employer to the Division of Labor Standards Enforcement within 30 days after the results are final." Labor Code 510(a) says its own daily and seventh-day rules do not apply to employees on an alternative workweek adopted under section 511.

> In a timesheet file, give the columns "aws day hours" (8.5 to 10) and "aws days" (the scheduled workdays a week) on the employee's rows. The auditor does not check that the election was held or reported.

## Questions

### What is an alternative workweek schedule in California?

A regularly scheduled workweek with days over 8 hours, adopted by the employees: under Labor Code 511(a) up to 10 hours a day within a 40-hour week, approved in a secret ballot by at least two-thirds of the affected employees in a work unit.

### When does overtime start on a 4/10 schedule?

After the 10 scheduled hours in a day and after 40 in the week, at 1.5x; after 12 hours a day at 2x (Labor Code 511(b)). An 11-hour day and a 13-hour day in a 4/10 week give 3.00 overtime and 1.00 double-time hours.

### What if the employee works a fifth day?

A day beyond the scheduled workdays is past the scheduled hours from its first hour: 1.5x for the first 8 hours and 2x after 8 (Labor Code 511(b)). A 9-hour fifth day here is 8.00 hours at 1.5x and 1.00 at 2x.

### Does a 3/12 schedule qualify?

Labor Code 511(a) authorizes days of no longer than 10 hours. Twelve-hour schedules depend on health-care wage orders, which this tool could not read, so the auditor does not apply them.
