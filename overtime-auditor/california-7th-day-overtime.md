# The seventh-day overtime rule

Yes: if an employee works all seven days of one workweek, the first 8 hours on the seventh day are 1.5x and hours past 8 are 2x (the 58.00-hour week below: 16.00 OT, 2.00 DT); seven days in a row that straddle two workweeks do not trigger it (here, 2.00 overtime hours instead of 16.00).

- 7 days in one workweek: 16.00 OT, 2.00 DT
- 7 in a row, two workweeks: 2.00 OT, 0.00 DT
- Six 8-hour days: 8.00 OT

KRS 337.050(1): "Any employer who permits any employee to work seven (7) days in any one (1) workweek shall pay him at the rate of time and a half for the time worked on the seventh day." Subsection (2)(a): "Subsection (1) shall not apply in any case in which the employee is not permitted to work more than forty (40) hours during the workweek. In that case the employer may credit against the overtime payable under this section any overtime paid by him to the employees for the same hours under the requirements of any other law or contract."

## The rule

Labor Code 510(a): "... the first eight hours worked on the seventh day of work in any one workweek shall be compensated at the rate of no less than one and one-half times the regular rate of pay ... any work in excess of eight hours on any seventh day of a workweek shall be compensated at the rate of no less than twice the regular rate of pay of an employee."

## Questions

### Is the 7th day double time in California?

Only after 8 hours. The first 8 hours on the seventh day of work in the workweek are 1.5x and hours beyond 8 are 2x: a 10-hour seventh day is 8.00 hours at 1.5x and 2.00 at 2x.

### Is the 6th day overtime in California?

Not by itself. Labor Code 510(a) names only the seventh day of work in a workweek; a sixth day is overtime only for hours over 8 that day or over 40 that week. Six 8-hour days give 8.00 overtime hours, all from the 40-hour rule.

### Do seven days in a row across two workweeks count?

No. The rule is the seventh day of work in one workweek, so seven days in a row from a Wednesday, with a Sunday workweek, never put seven days in one week: 2.00 overtime hours, against 16.00 when the same hours fall in one workweek.

### Does Kentucky have a seventh-day rule?

Yes. KRS 337.050(1) pays time and a half for the time worked on the seventh day of work in a workweek, unless the employee is not permitted to work more than 40 hours that week. In a 51-hour Kentucky week the seventh day's 6 hours are inside the 11.00 overtime hours.
