# Image to size or ImResizer?

Both are free and work in your browser. Pick ImResizer for HEIC photos or to increase a file's size; pick Image to size for a limit you type and published test results, such as a 12-megapixel portrait at 19.9 KB for a 20 KB limit.

## Side by side

|  | ImResizer (its own page) | Image to size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free, no login, no watermark, no daily limit | Free, no login |
| Where the photo goes | Stays in your browser | Stays in your browser |
| Photos it takes | JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC | JPEG, PNG and WebP |
| Output | JPG or PNG mentioned | JPEG |
| Limits | Preset pages from 15 KB to 500 KB, and a KB or MB field | Presets from 20 KB to 1 MB, or any number of KB |
| Make a file bigger | A separate increase-size page | No |
| Ads | Shown on the page | None |
| Published test results | Not on the page read | Yes: four test photos at six limits, run on 2026-10-31 |

## When ImResizer is the better choice

When your photo is HEIC straight from an iPhone, when you need PNG out, or when a form's minimum means you need to make a file larger.

## When Image to size is the better choice

When you want no ads, an exact limit you type, and results you can check before you use it: [what every size limit does to a photo](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/image-to-size/reduce-image-size-in-kb/).

## Questions

### Does ImResizer upload my photo?

Its page says everything runs inside your browser and the photo is never sent to a server (read 6 November 2026). Image to size also runs in your browser.

### Can either tool make a photo bigger in KB?

ImResizer links to a separate increase-size page. Image to size only makes files smaller.
