Your result appears here: the new size, how close it is to your limit, and a download button.

How it fits the photo

It tries your photo at its full size and checks quality levels until it finds the sharpest one that fits. Only if the lowest quality is still too big does it make the picture smaller, in small steps, and try again.

What changes in the file

The result is always a JPEG. A PNG with see-through areas gets a white background. Photos taken sideways on a phone are turned the right way up. When the photo is shrunk, camera details such as location are not copied into the new file. A JPEG that is already under your limit is handed back as it is, so it keeps whatever camera details it had.

What it does not cover

  • It will not shrink a picture past a tenth of its size. If your limit is too small for that, it says so and you can try a larger limit.
  • It does not crop the photo or change its shape to a form's required dimensions.
  • The result is a JPEG; it does not make PNG or WebP files.
