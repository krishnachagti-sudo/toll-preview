# JEE (Main) 2027 photo and signature size

NTA's JEE (Main) 2027 bulletin asks for a JPG/JPEG photograph between 10 kB and 200 kB and a JPG/JPEG signature between 10 kB and 100 kB, plus a live photograph taken while you fill the form. Session 1 forms close on 22 November 2026, up to 11:50 P.M.

## Every upload, as NTA lists it

| Upload | Format | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live photograph | Taken with your camera or webcam during the form | Not a file you upload |
| Scanned passport size photograph | JPG/JPEG | 10 kB to 200 kB |
| Signature | JPG/JPEG | 10 kB to 100 kB |
| Class 10 certificate or marks sheet | PDF | 50 kB to 300 kB |
| Disability/UDID certificate, if it applies | PDF | 50 kB to 300 kB |

The photograph must be recent, in color, with 80% face (including ears, without a mask) against a white background. For the live photograph, NTA asks for a white background, proper lighting and the same 80% face visibility; if your device has no webcam, the form shows a QR code to continue on a phone.

## What happens to a phone photo at these limits?

In our test, a 12-megapixel head-and-shoulders photo (3,024 × 4,032 px) came out at 198.2 KB for the 200 kB limit, 1,857 × 2,476 px at JPEG quality 35%. For a 100 kB limit, a scanned page came out at 98.9 KB, 817 × 1,058 px. Both are well above the 10 kB minimum.

> Results from the tool run in Chromium 149.0.7827.55 on 2026-10-31. Other browsers have their own JPEG encoders, so a result can differ by a few percent; it still fits the limit. Image to size makes JPEGs only; it does not make the PDFs the certificates need.

## Questions

### What is the photo size for JEE Main 2027?

NTA's Information Bulletin says the scanned photograph must be between 10 kB and 200 kB, in JPG/JPEG format, in color, with 80% face (including ears, without a mask) against a white background.

### What is the signature size for JEE Main 2027?

JPG/JPEG, between 10 kB and 100 kB, clearly legible.

### Is there a pixel size for the JEE Main 2027 photo?

The bulletin's upload section gives a file size range and format, not pixel dimensions.

### What is the last date for JEE Main 2027 session 1?

NTA's notice gives 22 November 2026 (up to 11:50 P.M.) for submitting the form and paying the fee, with late submission and a late fee from 23 November 2026 to 26 November 2026.
