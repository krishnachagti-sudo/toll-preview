# Certificates from Google Sheets

To make certificates from a Google Sheet, use File \> Download to save it as .csv or .xlsx and open that file here: 18 rows make 18 certificates of completion in one PDF ($0.99 clean, the proof free), with no add-on and no access to your Drive.

## Or keep it inside Google

Add-ons on the Google Workspace Marketplace merge rows into a Google Slides or Docs template inside your Drive. Autocrat lists 84M+ users and 4.4 stars from 6,308 ratings; AutoCerts makes "individual or consolidated PDFs" from Slides or Docs templates. Both ask for access to your Google files when installed (read November 8, 2026).

An add-on is the better choice when the design must be a Slides file the whole staff edits, or when certificates should be made automatically from Google Form answers. Downloading the sheet suits a one-off print run, a district that blocks add-ons, or a list you would rather not share with an app.

[Autocrat alternative for certificates](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/bulk-certificate-generator/alternative/autocrat/)

> Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.

## Steps

1. **Download the sheet.** In Google Sheets: File \> Download, then choose a file type. Comma-separated values (.csv) or Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) both open here.
2. **Open it in the certificate maker.** Open the downloaded file or paste the rows. The first worksheet of an .xlsx is read.
3. **Pick the design and check the preview.** Every certificate is drawn on the page; flagged names are listed.
4. **Download.** The proof is free; the clean PDF prints the whole class in one job.
