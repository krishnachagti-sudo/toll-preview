## When Autocrat is the better choice

Autocrat is the better choice when the certificate must be your own Slides or Docs design, when documents should be made automatically from form submissions, when they should be emailed, or when your district already approves it. It is free.

## When this is the better choice

This is the better choice when add-ons are turned off on your district account, when you do not want an app holding Drive permissions, or when you want each name fitted to its line and one PDF to print.

> Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.
