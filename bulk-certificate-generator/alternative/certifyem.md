## When Certify'em is the better choice

Certify'em is the better choice when the certificate depends on passing a quiz, when each person should get it by email, or when you want a record of who passed in a spreadsheet it keeps for you.

## When this is the better choice

This is the better choice for a printed stack for a class or event from a list you already have, when there is no quiz, or when you would rather not connect an add-on to your Google account.

> Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.
