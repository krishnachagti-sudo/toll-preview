# Mail merge certificates in Google Slides

Google Slides merges certificates through Workspace Marketplace add-ons: Autocrat (84M+ users, 4.4 stars from 6,308 ratings) and AutoCerts both fill a Slides template from a Google Sheet. Without one, download the sheet as CSV and 20 rows make 20 certificates here in one PDF ($0.99 clean).

## When an add-on is the better choice

When the design is a Google Slides file your team edits together, when certificates should go out by email, or when they should be made each time a Google Form is submitted.
