# Mail merge certificates in PowerPoint

PowerPoint has no mail merge of its own: Microsoft's help page for it says there are two known add-ins that merge Excel data into a presentation. Without an add-in, a list of 12 names becomes 12 certificates in one PDF here ($0.99 clean, the proof free).

## Your three options

| Way | What it takes | Best when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A PowerPoint add-in | Installing one of the add-ins Microsoft's page links to | The design must stay a PowerPoint slide |
| [Word mail merge](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/bulk-certificate-generator/mail-merge-word/) | Rebuilding the design in Word, then Mailings \> Start Mail Merge \> Letters | Your school works in Word already |
| This certificate maker | Opening the list and picking a design | You want one PDF of the whole class, each name fitted |

A slide design can be kept by saving the slide as a picture and using it in Word as the background, then merging the names on top; the merge then has Word's one-size-for-every-name limit.
