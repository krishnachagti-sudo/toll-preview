# Publisher certificate merges, after Publisher

Microsoft discontinued Publisher after October 13, 2026: it is no longer included in Microsoft 365, so a certificate mail merge built in Publisher has to move, to Word's mail merge or to a batch generator that takes the same Excel list.

## What Microsoft announced

Microsoft's announcement, quoted on its Q&A site, says Publisher reaches end of life in October 2026 and will no longer be included in Microsoft 365, and that existing on-premises suites will no longer be supported. A reply there notes that Publisher in the Office 2019 and 2021 Professional editions keeps running, without security updates.

## Steps

1. **Save your old designs.** While Publisher still opens, save each .pub certificate as a PDF so the design is kept.
2. **Pick where the merge goes.** Rebuild the design in Word and use Mailings \> Start Mail Merge \> Letters, or use a batch certificate generator with your list.
3. **Run the same list.** The Excel or CSV list you merged in Publisher works in both.
