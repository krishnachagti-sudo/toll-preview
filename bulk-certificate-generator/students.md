## Congratulations certificates

For a congratulations certificate, use any design and change the title to "Congratulations!" and the line under the name to what it is for, for example "on graduating from fifth grade".

## Print the whole class at once

- US Letter or A4, landscape or portrait.
- Each name fitted to its line; long names are listed before you print.
- One PDF, one page per student, in list order or sorted by homeroom, award or last name.
- Every line at least half an inch inside the paper edge.

> Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.
