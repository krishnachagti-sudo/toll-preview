# A certificate for every student, from a spreadsheet

Put one name a row under a heading, open the sheet, pick the paper and wording, check the flagged names and download one PDF: these 5 names make 5 certificates, the longest (Maximiliana Featherstonehaugh-Worthington) at 33.25 pt and the rest at 50 pt, for $0.99.

> Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.

## Steps

1. **Prepare the list.** One name a row, a heading row, no merged cells. Add Award, Hours and Homeroom columns if you need them.
2. **Choose the paper.** US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) in the US, A4 elsewhere; landscape for most certificates.
3. **Set the wording.** Title, the line above the name, the line under it, the date and up to two signers.
4. **Preview three certificates.** The shortest name, the longest and one with an accent: the flagged list shows which were made smaller.
5. **Download.** The free proof first, then the clean PDF: one file, one page per student.
6. **Print a test page, then the stack.** Print page 1 at 100% (Actual size) on plain paper; then the whole PDF on card stock.
