# Fix ALL CAPS and lower-case names before printing

Names exported in ALL CAPS or all lower case are put in title case before printing (JOHN SMITH prints as John Smith, mary o'neil as Mary O'Neil, SEAN MCDONALD as Sean McDonald); names typed in mixed case, such as McKenzie Brown or deShawn Williams, are left exactly as typed. The table shows 12 names and how each prints.

## The rules

- Only names typed all in capitals or all in lower case are changed.
- Each word, and each part after a hyphen or apostrophe, starts with a capital: Anne-Marie, O'Neil.
- Mc is followed by a capital: McDonald.
- Particles after the first word stay lower case: van, von, de, da, del, della, di, du, la, le, der, den, ter, ten, bin, al, el, y.
- A name written "Last, First" is turned round to "First Last".

Some names break any rule (MacDonald, DeShawn typed in capitals): check the preview, and type those few in mixed case in your sheet; mixed case is never changed.

> Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.
