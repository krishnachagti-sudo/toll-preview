# Printing certificates on US Letter

A US Letter certificate in landscape is 11 x 8.5 inches; every line here stays 0.5 inch inside the edge, leaving 10 x 7.5 inches, so a printer that cannot reach the outer quarter inch still prints the whole border.

## Open on US Letter landscape

[Open the certificate maker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/bulk-certificate-generator/?design=achievement&paper=letter&orientation=landscape)

Each paper and orientation here, with the size the longest sample name prints at.

| Paper | Size (in) | Safe margin (in) | Inside the margin (in) | Longest sample name |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| US Letter landscape | 11 x 8.5 | 0.5 | 10 x 7.5 | 33.25 pt |
| US Letter portrait | 8.5 x 11 | 0.5 | 7.5 x 10 | 24 pt |
| A4 landscape | 11.69 x 8.27 | 0.5 | 10.69 x 7.27 | 35.75 pt |
| A4 portrait | 8.27 x 11.69 | 0.5 | 7.27 x 10.69 | 23.25 pt |

## Why it prints cut off or off-center

- Fit to page (or Shrink oversized pages) scales the page down a few percent and moves it; print at 100% (Actual size). The PDF asks the print dialog for no scaling.
- Most desktop printers cannot print the outer edge of the sheet; check your printer's manual for its margin. Designs that run to the edge (full bleed) need a printer that supports it.
- Landscape: let the print dialog choose orientation from the PDF (Auto portrait/landscape); forcing portrait rotates or shrinks the page.
- Card stock: set the paper type to heavy or card in the print dialog, and feed it from the tray the printer's manual recommends.

## Test first

Print page 1 on plain paper, hold it to the light against a sheet of card stock and check the border sits evenly. Then print the stack.
