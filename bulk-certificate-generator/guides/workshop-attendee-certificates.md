# Workshop and training attendee certificates

A sign-in list with Name and Hours columns, and {detail} where the hours go in the line under the name, gives each attendee a completion certificate with their own hours ("has completed 3 CE hours of ..."): 6 attendees, 6 certificate pages in one PDF, $0.99.

This makes the certificates; it does not decide whether hours count for any licensing board. Check the board's rules for what a certificate must show.

> Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.

## Steps

1. **Clean the sign-in export.** Keep one row per attendee; names in ALL CAPS are fixed automatically, and repeated rows are flagged (or left out with "Leave out repeated rows").
2. **Add the hours.** An Hours (or CE hours, Credits) column with the text as it should print, such as "3 CE hours".
3. **Write the line once.** Use the completion design and put {detail} where the hours go: has completed {detail} of "Safe Lifting at Work".
4. **Download.** One PDF; attach it to your own email or print it.
5. **Keep a record.** Keep your sheet as the record of who received what; this tool keeps nothing.
