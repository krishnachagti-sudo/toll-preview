# 400 end-of-year award certificates by Friday

400 end-of-year awards are one sheet (Name, Award, Homeroom), sorted by homeroom, printed as one 400-page PDF for $4.00: about 25 certificates for each of 16 homerooms, in 8 print runs of 50.

## The arithmetic

|  | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Certificates | 400 |
| Pages in the PDF | 400 |
| Homerooms | 16 |
| Print runs of 50 | 8 |
| Clean PDF | $4.00 |
| A reprint with 20 late names, within 30 days | $0.20 (the 20 extra names) |

$0.01 a certificate, at least $0.99 a batch and never more than $9.99 in a month, whatever the size of the school.

> Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.

## Steps

1. **One sheet.** Columns: Name, Award, Homeroom (and Teacher if you like). A student with two awards gets two rows.
2. **Sort by homeroom.** Choose "By homeroom or class, then last name" so the printed stack comes out room by room.
3. **Wording per award.** The Award column prints under each name; put {award} in the line under the name if the award belongs in the sentence.
4. **Print in batches of 50.** Print pages 1 to 50, check the first and last of the run against the list, then the next 50.
5. **Hand-out day.** Keep a few blank certificates and a pen for late names, or reprint the batch with the new names added; the extra names are all you pay for within 30 days.
