Certificate wording examples

A certificate needs five parts: a title, a line such as "This certificate is presented to", the name, what it is for, and the date and signature. Here are 40 wordings for the title and the line under the name, in six groups; click one to open it in the certificate maker.

  • Made by Conyso
  • Exact, no AI
  • Official sources, dated
  • Files you upload are not kept
  • SecurityTerms

The parts of a certificate

  • Title: Certificate of Achievement, of Completion, of Appreciation, of Participation, or the award name.
  • Presented line: "This certificate is presented to", "This certifies that", "Presented with thanks to".
  • The name.
  • What it is for: one sentence, starting with "for", "in recognition of" or "has completed".
  • Date and signatures: who gives it, and when.
