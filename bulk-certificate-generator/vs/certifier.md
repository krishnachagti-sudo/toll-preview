## When Certifier is better

Certifier is better for ongoing digital credentials: verification pages, email delivery, expiry and renewals, recipient dashboards and badges that people share. If your program issues credentials all year, that is the job it is built for.

## When this is better

This is better for a single print run, a class or a workshop, where nothing should be stored or emailed and a paper certificate is the point.
