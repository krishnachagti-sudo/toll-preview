## When Word is better

Word is better when the template is already built in Word with your crest and fonts, staff know the Mailings tab, and the list is short enough to check by eye. It is free and the list never leaves the computer.

## When this is better

This is better when merge fields misalign or every page shows the same name, when names are long or accented, when the list is too long to check row by row, or when you want awards and hours from the list on each certificate without building the fields.
