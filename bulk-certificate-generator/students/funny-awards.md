## How to give one title per student

- Add a column headed Award next to the names in your sheet.
- Type or paste one title per student; a title can repeat.
- Open the list here with the funny award design; each title prints under the name.
- Sort by award to hand them out in ceremony order, or keep your own order.

Read the titles aloud before printing: a title is only funny if the student would laugh too.

> Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.
