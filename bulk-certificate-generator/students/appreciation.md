## Questions

### What should a certificate of appreciation for a student say?

This design says "Certificate of Appreciation", then "Presented with sincere thanks to", the student's name, and "in appreciation of your kindness, effort and dedication", with the date and signature lines under it. Every line can be changed.

### How much is a certificate of appreciation batch for a class of 24?

$0.99 for the clean PDF ($0.01 a certificate, at least $0.99 a batch); the watermarked proof is free.
