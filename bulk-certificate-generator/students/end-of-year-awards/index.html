Each row's Award column prints under the name, so one batch can hold every award in the class. A student with two awards takes two rows. Sort by award or by homeroom so the printed stack comes out in hand-out order.

Class lists are used to make the PDF and are not kept. The preview and the free proof are made in your browser; the clean PDF is made on the server, in memory, and the list is discarded with the answer.

Questions

What should a end-of-year award for a student say?

This design says "End of Year Award", then "This award is presented to", the student's name, the award from your list, and "in recognition of an outstanding school year", with the date and signature lines under it. Every line can be changed.

How much is a end-of-year award batch for a class of 24?

$0.99 for the clean PDF ($0.01 a certificate, at least $0.99 a batch); the watermarked proof is free.
