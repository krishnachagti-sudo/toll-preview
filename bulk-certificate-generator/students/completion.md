# Certificate of completion for students

This certificate of completion template fills 24 student names, the date June 11, 2027 and your signature line in one PDF of 24 certificate pages; the longest name, Benjamin Featherstone-Whitaker, prints at 45.75 pt so it fits its line, and the clean PDF costs $0.99.

## The wording

| Line | Says |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Certificate of Completion |
| Above the name | This certifies that |
| Name | From your list, sized to fit |
| Under the name | has successfully completed {detail} (or "has successfully completed the course" when the row has no course) |
| Bottom | Date, and up to two signature lines with names and roles |

## Questions

### What should a certificate of completion for a student say?

This design says "Certificate of Completion", then "This certifies that", the student's name, and "has successfully completed {detail}", with the date and signature lines under it. Every line can be changed.

### How much is a certificate of completion batch for a class of 24?

$0.99 for the clean PDF ($0.01 a certificate, at least $0.99 a batch); the watermarked proof is free.
