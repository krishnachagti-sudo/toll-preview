This certificate of achievement template fills 24 student names, the date June 11, 2027 and your signature line in one PDF of 24 certificate pages; the longest name, Benjamin Featherstone-Whitaker, prints at 45.75 pt so it fits its line, and the clean PDF costs $0.99.

## Questions

### What should a certificate of achievement for a student say?

This design says "Certificate of Achievement", then "This certificate is proudly presented to", the student's name, and "for outstanding achievement and hard work", with the date and signature lines under it. Every line can be changed.

### How much is a certificate of achievement batch for a class of 24?

$0.99 for the clean PDF ($0.01 a certificate, at least $0.99 a batch); the watermarked proof is free.
