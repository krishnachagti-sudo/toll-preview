Placement fee

How to avoid Amazon inbound placement fees

You avoid Amazon's inbound placement fee entirely only with Amazon-optimized splits (at least five identical cartons per item) or by sending through AWD, whose pricing covers placement; on 1,000 large standard units at 2 lb that saves $340.00 to $600.00 before the extra freight.

1. Amazon-optimized splits

No fee, five or more locations, and "To qualify, shipments must include at least five identical cartons or pallets per item. Each carton or pallet must contain the same quantity per item and the same item mix." The freight to those locations is yours, so compare it with the fee first (minimal vs Amazon-optimized). If the option is greyed out, the eligibility checker names the product and carton that block it.

2. AWD

"AWD pricing covers FBA inbound placement, so there is no separate charge for this service." AWD has its own fees: AWD costs $0.29 a unit for a carton of 24 at 1.5 cu ft held 3 months ($0.20 of it processing and transport to FBA), against a minimal-split placement fee of $0.34 to $0.60 a unit; AWD is cheaper across that range. Freight into AWD is not included. Placement fee vs AWD works it out by month.

3. Partial splits for Bulky items

Two or three locations "for a reduced fee": for small/medium bulky at 5 lb or less, $0.55 to $1.10 a unit against $1.10 to $1.60 on minimal splits. Not offered for standard-size products.

4. The new-seller credit

New sellers who create and send their first shipment to a fulfillment center within 90 days of listing their offer will qualify for $400 in credits that apply to the FBA inbound placement service fee. At the top of the large standard 1.5 to 3 lb range ($0.60 a unit) $400.00 covers 666 units. The credits expire after one year if unused. Enter what is left in the calculator's credits field.

5. Separate shipments

Amazon: "Create separate shipments for standard-size items, non-standard-size items, and each of the following categories: apparel, jewelry, shoes, watches, and dangerous goods". "If you create a shipping plan with a combination of standard-size items, non-standard-size items (for example, Large/Extra Large Bulky), or special handling categories, we may direct your inventory to more than one inbound location, even if you select the minimal shipment splits option for inbound placement."

6. Weight bands

The fee steps up with shipping weight. Large standard at 12+ oz to 1.5 lb is $0.24 to $0.50 a unit; at 1.5+ lb to 3 lb, $0.34 to $0.60: $0.10 to $0.10 more a unit for crossing 1.5 lb. For large standard and bulky, the shipping weight is the larger of unit and dimensional weight, so a smaller box can move a product down a band. Amazon's 2027 product size tiers, in the FBA prep checker

What does not avoid it

  • Sending to a location other than the plan's. "The inbound placement service fee will be charged on received units based on the plan option selected when the shipment was created." A misrouted shipment will incur a defect charge to manage both the placement and operational costs: inbound defect fee vs placement fee.
  • Deleting or abandoning a shipment: the same defect charge applies.

Questions

How do I avoid the inbound placement fee completely?

Choose Amazon-optimized splits, which have no fee but need "at least five identical cartons or pallets per item", or send through AWD: "AWD pricing covers FBA inbound placement, so there is no separate charge for this service."

Do new sellers get placement fee credits?

Yes. Amazon: "New sellers who create and send their first shipment to a fulfillment center within 90 days of listing their offer will qualify for $400 in credits that apply to the FBA inbound placement service fee". The credits expire after one year if unused.
