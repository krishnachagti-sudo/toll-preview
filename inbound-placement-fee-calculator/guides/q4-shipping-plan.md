# Inbound placement dates, November 2026 to December 2027

A shipment Amazon receives on November 30, 2026 is charged its placement fee about January 14, 2027, 45 days later; the dates that change placement costs before December 2027 are January 15, 2027 (2027 size tiers, and central inbound folded into East for standard-size, with the rate card unchanged), February 1, 2027 (new AWD fees) and March 1, 2027 (AWD aged storage).

## Planning around them

- A plan created before January 15, 2027 uses the 2026 size tiers; the calculator picks the table from the plan date.
- Amazon's defect rules: domestic shipments must arrive within 45 days of shipment creation, and in a multiple destination plan the other shipments within 30 days of the first arrival.
- Amazon's Q4 inbound cutoffs and capacity limits were not on the pages read for this site, so none are given here: check Send to Amazon and Capacity Manager in Seller Central.
