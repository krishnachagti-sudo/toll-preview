FBA inbound fees

Inbound defect fee vs placement fee

They are different fees: the placement fee is the price of the option you choose (for large standard at 12 oz or less, $0.20 to $0.40 a unit on minimal splits), while the inbound defect fee is charged when a shipment breaks the plan, such as a misrouted, deleted or abandoned shipment ($0.38 a unit for the same row).

What triggers the defect fee

  • Misrouted: Your shipment was sent to a different fulfillment center than the one listed in your shipping plan.
  • Your shipment was deleted or canceled from the shipping plan after you've approved it.
  • You didn't complete all shipments or sent incomplete shipments where item quantities differed significantly from your shipping plan.
  • Your domestic shipments didn't arrive within 45 days of shipment creation.
  • Your international shipments didn't arrive within 75 days of shipment creation.
  • In a multiple destination shipping plan, your additional shipments didn't arrive within 30 days of the first shipment arrival.

Amazon's placement page adds: shipments sent elsewhere or not arriving in time will incur a defect charge to manage both the placement and operational costs.

How to avoid it

  • Send each shipment to the location in the plan.
  • To change a multi-destination plan after approving it, delete all its shipments before any of it ships (Amazon).
  • Ship within the time limits above, with the quantities in the plan.
  • Dispute: you can request an investigation within 90 days of the shipment problem notification.

Questions

Are the inbound defect fee and the placement fee the same?

No. The placement fee pays for Amazon spreading your inventory when you choose fewer locations. The defect fee is for shipments that break the plan: "Misrouted: Your shipment was sent to a different fulfillment center than the one listed in your shipping plan.", deleted, abandoned or incomplete shipments, and late arrivals.

Can I dispute an inbound defect fee?

Amazon: you can request an investigation within 90 days of the shipment problem notification.
