# Amazon SPD vs LTL for an FBA shipment

Amazon caps a small-parcel (SPD) shipment at 200 boxes with a partnered carrier and 500 with your own, and requires pallets for partnered LTL; in one seller's estimate, $130.36 of LTL to one location plus a $108.80 fee beat $274.06 of parcels to several, by $34.90.

## Amazon's rules for each mode

- SPD: "Small-parcel deliveries (SPDs) consist of units packed in individual boxes, and each box is individually labeled for delivery." 200 boxes per shipment with a partnered carrier, 500 with your own.
- LTL and FTL: "Pallets are required for shipments sent with an Amazon Partnered carrier." You can only request up to three appointments for a single shipment.
- Boxes, either way: Boxes must not exceed these maximum size and weight requirements: Dimensions: 36.00"(L) x 25.00"(W) x 25.00"(H), and weight: No more than 50.00 lb total.
- Partnered carrier estimates: "Your actual shipping charges may be higher than the amount we estimate when you create a shipment for the Partnered Carrier program if the carrier determines that the dimensional data you provided is incorrect."

## How the mode decides the option

Minimal splits send everything to one location, which suits one LTL shipment on pallets. Amazon-optimized splits send to five or more, which often means parcels or several smaller LTL loads. So the fee saved is set against the cost of the second mode. In the seller's estimate the two totals were $239.16 and $274.06.

## Questions

### How many boxes can an SPD shipment to Amazon have?

Amazon: 200 boxes per shipment with a partnered carrier, 500 with a non-partnered carrier.

### Do I need pallets for LTL into Amazon?

Pallets are required for shipments sent with an Amazon Partnered carrier. Amazon says pallets are preferred for non-partnered carriers as well.

### How big can a box be?

Boxes must not exceed these maximum size and weight requirements: Dimensions: 36.00"(L) x 25.00"(W) x 25.00"(H), and weight: No more than 50.00 lb total.
