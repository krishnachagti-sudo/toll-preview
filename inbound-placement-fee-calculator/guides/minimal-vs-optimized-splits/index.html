Placement options

Minimal vs Amazon-optimized splits: which is cheaper?

Amazon-optimized splits remove the fee but add freight to five or more locations: for 1,000 large standard units at 2 lb, minimal splits cost $340.00 to $600.00 in placement fees (Amazon's range) plus $420.00 freight = $760.00 to $1,020.00; Amazon-optimized splits cost $690.00 in freight, no placement fee; cheapest: Amazon-optimized splits, by $70.00 to $330.00 wherever the fee falls in Amazon's range.

The worked shipment

Minimal splits are cheaper while their placement fee stays under $270.00 ($0.27 a unit across 1,000 units); above it, Amazon-optimized splits are cheaper.

When minimal splits win

In a seller's Send to Amazon estimate posted in Amazon's Seller Forums, minimal splits came to $108.80 of placement fee plus $130.36 of LTL freight = $239.16, against $274.06 of parcel freight for Amazon-optimized splits, so minimal splits were cheaper by $34.90. The seller added that sending LTL to the West gave the lowest freight but higher placement fees, and was still cheapest overall for them.

Minimal splits tend to win when one truckload to one location is much cheaper than parcels to five, and the fee for your weight band is low. SPD vs LTL covers the freight side.

The extra freight splitting can add

For large standard at 1.5+ lb to 3 lb, the fee Amazon-optimized splits save is $0.34 to $0.60 a unit. Splitting is cheaper as long as its extra freight stays under that saving:

Before you choose

  • When creating shipments, you'll only see the inbound placement options your shipments qualify for.
  • Amazon-optimized: "To qualify, shipments must include at least five identical cartons or pallets per item. Each carton or pallet must contain the same quantity per item and the same item mix."
  • Your carrier's rates will determine whether your actual shipping costs are higher or lower than our estimates. Send to Amazon's shipping estimate uses Partner Carrier rates.
  • You'll be charged the FBA inbound placement service fee 45 days after your shipment is received, based on the quantities received.

Steps

  1. Price the fee. Units times the per-unit fee for the size tier and shipping weight: the figure Send to Amazon shows, or Amazon's published range.
  2. Price the freight. Get your carrier's quote for one location (minimal) and for the five or more locations Amazon-optimized splits send to.
  3. Add and compare. Minimal: fee plus freight. Amazon-optimized: freight only. The lower total wins.
  4. Check eligibility. Amazon-optimized splits need at least five identical cartons or pallets per item.

Questions

Can Amazon-optimized splits cost more than minimal splits?

Yes. A seller in Amazon's Seller Forums posted Send to Amazon's estimate: minimal splits came to $108.80 of placement fee plus $130.36 of LTL freight = $239.16, against $274.06 of parcel freight for Amazon-optimized splits, so minimal splits were cheaper by $34.90.

At what fee do Amazon-optimized splits become cheaper?

When the fee is more than the extra freight. In the worked shipment here, minimal splits are cheaper while their placement fee stays under $270.00 ($0.27 a unit across 1,000 units); above it, Amazon-optimized splits are cheaper.
