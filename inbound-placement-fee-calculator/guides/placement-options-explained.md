# The three inbound placement options

Amazon offers three inbound placement options: minimal splits (generally one location, a fee per unit), partial splits (two or three locations, a reduced fee, Bulky items only) and Amazon-optimized splits (five or more locations, no fee, at least five identical cartons per item); for 50 large bulky units at 14.39 lb, minimal splits cost $170.00 to $205.00 in placement fees (Amazon's range) plus $200.00 freight = $370.00 to $405.00; partial splits cost $40.50 to $115.00 in placement fees (Amazon's range) plus $260.00 freight = $300.50 to $375.00; Amazon-optimized splits cost $400.00 in freight, no placement fee; cheapest: partial splits, by $25.00 to $69.50 at both ends of Amazon's fee range.

## The options side by side

Per Amazon's US placement fee page

| Option | Locations | Fee | Who can choose it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minimal shipment splits | Send to single location | Per unit, e.g. $0.14 to $0.32 small standard at 8 oz or less | Any plan; no carton or pallet packing requirements (Amazon's FAQ) |
| Partial shipment splits | Send to two or three locations | Reduced, e.g. $0.55 to $1.10 against $1.10 to $1.60 for small/medium bulky at 5 lb or less | Bulky items only |
| Amazon-optimized shipment splits | Send to five or more locations | No fee | At least five identical cartons or pallets per item |

## When each wins

- Amazon-optimized: when the extra freight to five or more locations is less than the fee it saves. [Minimal vs Amazon-optimized](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/inbound-placement-fee-calculator/guides/minimal-vs-optimized-splits/) works an example: for 1,000 large standard units at 2 lb, minimal splits cost $340.00 to $600.00 in placement fees (Amazon's range) plus $420.00 freight = $760.00 to $1,020.00; Amazon-optimized splits cost $690.00 in freight, no placement fee; cheapest: Amazon-optimized splits, by $70.00 to $330.00 wherever the fee falls in Amazon's range.
- Minimal: when one LTL load to one location is much cheaper than parcels to five, or the plan cannot have five identical cartons per item.
- Partial: Bulky items where five locations cost too much in freight but the full minimal fee is high; in the board example above it is the cheapest.

## What decides which options you see

- The availability of these inbound placement options is based on several factors, including the product types and quantities in your shipping plan, your existing inventory levels across our network, location of customer demand, and capacity availability in our fulfillment centers.
- When creating shipments, you'll only see the inbound placement options your shipments qualify for.
- If you create a shipping plan with a combination of standard-size items, non-standard-size items (for example, Large/Extra Large Bulky), or special handling categories, we may direct your inventory to more than one inbound location, even if you select the minimal shipment splits option for inbound placement.

## Questions

### What are minimal shipment splits?

Amazon: "Send your inventory to the minimal number of inbound locations, generally to a single location, for a fee, and we'll decide on your behalf how to place the inventory across our network."

### What are partial shipment splits?

Amazon: "Send your Bulky-sized inventory to multiple inbound locations (but fewer than with the Amazon-optimized option) for a reduced fee. This option is not available for shipping plans for standard-sized products."

### What are Amazon-optimized shipment splits?

Amazon: "Send your inventory to multiple inbound locations yourself for no fee. To qualify, shipments must include at least five identical cartons or pallets per item. Each carton or pallet must contain the same quantity per item and the same item mix."
