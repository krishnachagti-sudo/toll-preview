How Amazon charges it

Amazon charges a placement fee per unit when you send a shipment to one location (minimal splits) or two or three (partial splits, Bulky only), and none when you send it to five or more yourself (Amazon-optimized splits). The fee is per unit by size tier and shipping weight, within the ranges Amazon publishes.

The three options, the fee, what this adds

The three options

Minimal splits: "generally to a single location, for a fee". Partial splits: two or three locations "for a reduced fee", for Bulky items only. Amazon-optimized splits: five or more locations "for no fee", if the shipment has "at least five identical cartons or pallets per item".

The fee

Per unit, by size tier and shipping weight, within the ranges Amazon publishes; it varies by inbound location, and West can cost more. It is charged 45 days after Amazon receives the shipment, on the units received. It does not apply to extra-large (oversize) items. Amazon says the rate card does not change for 2027.

What this adds

Send to Amazon shows a fee estimate per option and a shipping estimate at Partner Carrier rates. This puts your own freight quotes next to the fee, gives the fee at which the cheapest option flips, and checks your carton plan for Amazon-optimized splits before you build it.
