Amazon inbound placement fee: the full rate table

Amazon's inbound placement fee runs from $0.14 to $0.32 a unit for small standard at 8 oz or less to $5.50 to $6.50 for large/extra-large bulky at 42+ lb to 50 lb on minimal splits, partial splits (Bulky only) cost from $0.55 to $3.50 a unit, and Amazon-optimized splits carry no fee; Amazon's 2027 update says the rate card does not change.

Amazon names the bulky rows Small/Medium Bulky and Large/Extra Large Bulky, the tiers it uses from January 15, 2027. Under the 2026 tiers, small bulky pays the first group of rows and large bulky the second.

How Amazon applies it

  • "The fee may be adjusted periodically within the ranges listed below", and it varies by inbound location: there may be higher fees for shipments sent to locations in the West compared to other parts of the country.
  • Weight: "Unit weight for Small standard. We'll use the larger of dimensional weight or unit weight for Large standard, Small/Medium Bulky and Large/Extra Large bulky." Amazon's 2027 product size tiers, in the FBA prep checker.
  • The FBA inbound placement service fee doesn't apply to Extra-Large products.
  • You'll be charged the FBA inbound placement service fee 45 days after your shipment is received, based on the quantities received.
  • Amazon's worked example: Minimal shipment split option with one inbound location in the West region. 100 units at $0.36 a unit is $36.00.
  • New sellers: New sellers who create and send their first shipment to a fulfillment center within 90 days of listing their offer will qualify for $400 in credits that apply to the FBA inbound placement service fee. The credits expire after one year if unused.

Dates

  • Read on Amazon's US Seller Central page on November 8, 2026.
  • Amazon's 2026 fee announcement in Seller Forums says: "Unless otherwise noted, all changes will be effective January 15, 2026." The details page it links (G201411300) now shows the 2027 changes, so which 2026 change touched the placement rate card cannot be read on Amazon's pages any more.
  • From January 15, 2027: There are no changes to the inbound placement service fee rate card. Standard-size shipments have two inbound regions, East and West.

Questions

How much is the inbound placement fee per unit in 2026?

On minimal splits, Amazon's table gives $0.14 to $0.32 a unit for small standard at 8 oz or less up to $5.50 to $6.50 for large/extra-large bulky at 42+ lb to 50 lb. Amazon-optimized splits have no fee. Amazon says the fee "may be adjusted periodically within the ranges"; Send to Amazon shows the figure for your plan.

Do extra-large items pay the inbound placement fee?

No. Amazon's page says: "The FBA inbound placement service fee doesn't apply to Extra-Large products." Its dimensional weight page calls oversize items "also known as 'Extra Large'".

Can I choose partial shipment splits for standard-size items?

No. Amazon: "This option is not available for shipping plans for standard-sized products."

Does the placement fee change in 2027?

Amazon's 2027 fee update says: "There are no changes to the inbound placement service fee rate card." What changes on January 15, 2027 is the regions: Amazon is consolidating central inbound locations into the East region for shipments of standard-size products, reducing the number of inbound regions from three to two (East and West).

Is the fee per unit or per shipment?

Per unit. Amazon's own example: 100 large standard units at 12 oz or less, one location in the West, at $0.36 a unit, is $36.00, charged 45 days after the shipment is received.
