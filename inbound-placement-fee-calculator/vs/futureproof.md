# Futureproof's placement fee calculator compared

Futureproof's free calculator multiplies an editable per-unit fee by units per shipment and shipments a year and shows Amazon-optimized splits at $0, leaving the freight to you; this calculator adds each option's freight: for 1,000 large standard units at 2 lb, minimal splits cost $340.00 to $600.00 in placement fees (Amazon's range) plus $420.00 freight = $760.00 to $1,020.00; Amazon-optimized splits cost $690.00 in freight, no placement fee; cheapest: Amazon-optimized splits, by $70.00 to $330.00 wherever the fee falls in Amazon's range.

## What Futureproof's calculator does

- Inputs: units per shipment, shipments per year, size tier (small standard, large standard, large bulky), split option (minimal or Amazon-optimized), and an editable fee per unit.
- Outputs: placement fee per shipment and per year, and the split options compared.
- Its note: "weigh the extra freight cost before choosing."
- Futureproof itself is a finance platform that "starts at $1,000 per month", with a 14-day trial (its pricing page).

## When Futureproof's calculator is better

- You already use Futureproof's books and want placement fees booked to each shipment's landed cost.
- You want a yearly figure from a per-shipment fee.

## When this calculator is better

- It adds your own freight for each option to the fee and names the cheapest: for 1,000 large standard units at 2 lb, minimal splits cost $340.00 to $600.00 in placement fees (Amazon's range) plus $420.00 freight = $760.00 to $1,020.00; Amazon-optimized splits cost $690.00 in freight, no placement fee; cheapest: Amazon-optimized splits, by $70.00 to $330.00 wherever the fee falls in Amazon's range.
- It gives the fee at which the answer flips: Minimal splits are cheaper while their placement fee stays under $270.00 ($0.27 a unit across 1,000 units); above it, Amazon-optimized splits are cheaper.
- It checks a carton plan for Amazon-optimized splits and names the blocking product and carton.
- Every row of Amazon's table, read November 8, 2026, with partial splits for Bulky items and no fee for extra-large.
- Partial splits and every Bulky row, which its tier list leaves out.

Price here: free on this page; $0.01 a comparison over the API or MCP, $0.10 for the send-in worksheet with its CSV, never more than $4.90 a month.
