# Placement fee vs AWD: which costs less?

Amazon says AWD pricing covers the inbound placement fee. AWD costs $0.29 a unit for a carton of 24 at 1.5 cu ft held 3 months ($0.20 of it processing and transport to FBA), against a minimal-split placement fee of $0.34 to $0.60 a unit; AWD is cheaper across that range. Freight into AWD is not included. From February 1, 2027 the same carton costs $0.32 a unit.

## When the placement fee is better

- Light, fast-selling products whose fee is at the bottom of Amazon's range.
- Stock that would sit in AWD for months: storage grows each month while the fee does not.
- Shipments that qualify for Amazon-optimized splits anyway, with no fee at all.

## Questions

### Does AWD avoid the inbound placement fee?

Yes. Amazon: "Amazon Warehousing and Distribution pricing covers FBA inbound placement services; therefore, no separate FBA inbound placement service fee is charged."

### What does AWD cost?

Until January 31, 2027: $1.40 a box in, $1.40 a box out, $1.40 a cubic foot of transport and $0.48 to $0.57 a cubic foot a month of storage. From February 1, 2027: $1.55, $1.55 and $1.58, plus a 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge on transport.
