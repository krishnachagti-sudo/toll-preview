# AMZ Prep's placement fee calculator compared

AMZ Prep's calculator asks for carton length, width and height and cartons shipped a month and returns your savings with AMZ Prep; it does not price Amazon's placement options for a shipment, which is what this calculator does: for 1,000 large standard units at 2 lb, minimal splits cost $340.00 to $600.00 in placement fees (Amazon's range) plus $420.00 freight = $760.00 to $1,020.00; Amazon-optimized splits cost $690.00 in freight, no placement fee; cheapest: Amazon-optimized splits, by $70.00 to $330.00 wherever the fee falls in Amazon's range.

## What AMZ Prep's calculator does

- Inputs on the page: Length (in), Width (in), Height (in), Cubic Feet, Cartons Shipped /mo, and a "Calculate my savings" button.
- Its page says the tool "estimates placement and freight costs only" and not prep service costs.
- It invites shippers of more than one full truckload a week to ask for a custom quote.
- Its fee notes say extra-large items are "not subject to inbound placement fees", which matches Amazon's page.

## When AMZ Prep's calculator is better

- You are choosing a prep center or 3PL and want AMZ Prep's own savings estimate and quote.
- You ship by the truckload and want someone to run the freight for you.

## When this calculator is better

- It adds your own freight for each option to the fee and names the cheapest: for 1,000 large standard units at 2 lb, minimal splits cost $340.00 to $600.00 in placement fees (Amazon's range) plus $420.00 freight = $760.00 to $1,020.00; Amazon-optimized splits cost $690.00 in freight, no placement fee; cheapest: Amazon-optimized splits, by $70.00 to $330.00 wherever the fee falls in Amazon's range.
- It gives the fee at which the answer flips: Minimal splits are cheaper while their placement fee stays under $270.00 ($0.27 a unit across 1,000 units); above it, Amazon-optimized splits are cheaper.
- It checks a carton plan for Amazon-optimized splits and names the blocking product and carton.
- Every row of Amazon's table, read November 8, 2026, with partial splits for Bulky items and no fee for extra-large.

Price here: free on this page; $0.01 a comparison over the API or MCP, $0.10 for the send-in worksheet with its CSV, never more than $4.90 a month.
