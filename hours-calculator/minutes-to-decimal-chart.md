# Minutes to decimal hours chart

To turn minutes into decimal hours, divide by 60: 15 minutes is 0.25 hours, 30 is 0.50 and 45 is 0.75, so 7 hours 53 minutes is 7.88 hours.

- 15 minutes: 0.25
- 30 minutes: 0.50
- 45 minutes: 0.75
- 7 hours 53 minutes: 7.88

## The formula and the common mistake

Decimal hours = hours + minutes / 60. In a spreadsheet, with a duration such as 7:53 in cell A2, `=A2*24` gives 7.8833; `=ROUND(A2*24,2)` gives 7.88.

Minutes are not hundredths: 7 hours 30 minutes is 7.50, not 7.30, and 7.30 hours on a pay stub is 7 hours 18 minutes. Add the minutes first and convert once at the end; adding rounded decimals day by day can drift by a cent or two of an hour.

## Questions

### How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 53 minutes is 53 / 60 = 0.8833, or 0.88 to two places.

### What is 7 hours 53 minutes in decimal?

7.88 hours (7 + 53/60 = 7.8833).

### Is 7.30 the same as 7 hours 30 minutes?

No. 7 hours 30 minutes is 7.50 hours. 7.30 decimal hours is 7:18, that is 7 hours 18 minutes.
