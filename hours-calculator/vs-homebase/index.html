Homebase vs the time card calculator

Homebase is scheduling and a staff time clock: its pricing page, read November 8, 2026, lists a free Basic plan for 1 location and up to 10 employees and paid plans from $30 per location a month; the calculator does not schedule or clock anyone in, it totals hours you already have, such as 37.50 hours for a 9 to 5 week with a 30-minute lunch.

Different jobs

Homebase runs the shift: schedules, a time clock and, on paid plans, more team tools, priced per location. The hours calculator starts after the shift: it totals punches from any source, counts overtime per workweek, checks rounding and prints a timesheet.

Homebase's plans, as listed

When Homebase is better

  • You schedule hourly staff every week and want them to clock in on a device.
  • You want the time clock, schedule and team messages in one app.
  • You have one location and up to 10 employees, which the free plan covers.

When the calculator is enough

  • The punches already exist (paper cards, a clock export) and you need totals and overtime.
  • You want to check what rounding did to a week, punch by punch.
  • You need hours from code or an AI agent over the API or MCP.

Totals, conversions and the rounding check are free on the calculator page; a signed-ready timesheet PDF and CSV costs $0.15 per employee and pay period; an API or MCP call costs $0.002.

Questions

Is Homebase free?

Its pricing page (read November 8, 2026) lists Basic at "$0 / location / month for 1 location, up to 10 employees", with basic scheduling and basic time tracking.

What do Homebase's paid plans cost?

The page lists Essentials at $30, Plus at $70 and All-in-One at $120 per location per month, with an annual or monthly toggle; check the page for the current price on each billing cycle.

When is Homebase the better choice?

When you need to build schedules and have staff clock in on a tablet, phone or point-of-sale device every shift.
