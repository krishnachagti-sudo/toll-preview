Turn logged hours into an invoice amount

Add up the minutes, divide by 60 and multiply by your rate: 18:51 of work in a week is 18.85 hours, which at $85.00 an hour is $1,602.25; rounding each session to the nearest tenth of an hour would bill $1,606.50 instead.

The week's log

Exact, tenths or quarter hours?

Rounding to the nearest increment can go either way on each session; rounding every session up only ever adds. Whichever you use, state it in the contract. Employers rounding employees' punches face a federal neutrality test, see time clock rounding rules.

Questions

How do I convert minutes to hours for an invoice?

Divide by 60: 2 hours 37 minutes is 2 + 37/60 = 2.62 hours (to two places). Bill hours times rate; round the money to the cent once, at the end.

Should I bill in tenths of an hour?

That is a term of your contract, not a rule. On this week, tenths bill $1,606.50 and quarter hours bill $1,615.00, against $1,602.25 for the exact minutes. Say in the contract which you use.

Do overtime rules apply to freelancers?

FLSA overtime is owed to employees. An independent contractor bills what the contract says; this example stays under 40 hours either way.
