Overtime in a holiday week

Only hours actually worked count toward the 40 that start overtime: an employee who works 40.00 hours in the week of December 25, 2026 (a Friday) and is paid 8 hours for the holiday has 0.00 hours of overtime, not 8.00; at $20.00 an hour the week pays $960.00.

Christmas week: the holiday off, paid

Workweek Sunday, December 20, 2026 to Saturday, December 26, 2026. Monday to Thursday 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM with an unpaid hour for lunch, Christmas Day off with 8 hours of holiday pay, and a Saturday 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM shift. Rate $20.00.

If the holiday hours were wrongly entered as worked time, the sheet would show 8.00 overtime hours and $1,040.00. Enter only the punches; add holiday pay as its own line.

New Year week: working the holiday

Workweek Sunday, December 27, 2026 to Saturday, January 2, 2027. The same schedule, but this time the employee works New Year's Day, Fri, Jan 1, too.

Hours worked: 48.00, of which 8.00 are overtime because the week passes 40, not because of the holiday. Pay at $20.00: $800.00 regular and $240.00 overtime, $1,040.00 in all. A holiday premium, if your employer pays one, is a policy on top.

What the rules say

  • Overtime: hours worked in excess of 40 in a workweek at not less than time and one-half (DOL Fact Sheet #23).
  • Holidays: "The Act does not require overtime pay for work on Saturdays, Sundays, holidays, or regular days of rest, as such." (Fact Sheet #23).
  • Paid time off: payments for "vacation, holiday, illness" and similar absences "are not made as compensation for his hours of employment", and "no part of such payments may be credited toward overtime compensation due under the Act" (29 CFR 778.218(a)).
  • Each week alone: "The Act takes a single workweek as its standard and does not permit averaging of hours over 2 or more weeks." (29 CFR 778.104).

Worked out from the times shown, under the federal rules quoted. A state law, contract or policy may require more. Not legal or payroll advice.

Questions

Do paid holiday hours count toward overtime?

Not under the federal rule. 29 CFR 778.218(a): such payments "are not made as compensation for his hours of employment" and "no part of such payments may be credited toward overtime compensation due under the Act." A state, union contract or policy may count them anyway.

Is working on Christmas paid at time and a half?

Not by federal law as such. DOL Fact Sheet #23: "The Act does not require overtime pay for work on Saturdays, Sundays, holidays, or regular days of rest, as such." Hours worked on the holiday count toward the 40 like any others.

What day is Christmas 2026?

Friday, December 25, 2026. New Year's Day 2027 is Friday, January 1, 2027.

Which year does the New Year week belong to?

For overtime, neither: the workweek is a fixed 168 hours (29 CFR 778.105), so a week from December 27, 2026 to January 2, 2027 is counted as one week even though it spans two years.
