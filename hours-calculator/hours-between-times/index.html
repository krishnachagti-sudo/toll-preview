Hours between two times

Subtract the start from the end and take off the unpaid break: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM with a 30-minute lunch is 7:30 hours (7.50), and 10:00 PM to 6:30 AM with a 30-minute break is 8.00 hours across midnight.

7.50
8 to 4:30, 30-minute lunch
8.00
10 PM to 6:30 AM, 30-minute break
8.00

Questions

How many hours is 9 to 5 with a 30-minute lunch?

7.50 hours (8 hours minus 0.5).

How many hours is 8:45 to 5:15 with a 30-minute lunch?

8.00 hours.

How do I count a shift that ends after midnight?

Add 24 hours to the end time: 10:00 PM to 6:30 AM is 8:30 before the break.
