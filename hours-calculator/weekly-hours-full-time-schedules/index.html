How many hours a week is your schedule?

Nine to five, five days a week, is 40.00 hours if lunch is paid and 37.50 hours with an unpaid 30-minute lunch; four 10-hour days (7 AM to 5:30 PM with 30 minutes off) make 40.00, and three 12-hour shifts with a 30-minute break make 34.50.

What counts as full time?

The FLSA has no definition. DOL: "The Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) does not define full-time employment or part-time employment. This is a matter generally to be determined by the employer." What the FLSA does fix is overtime after 40 hours in a workweek. Other laws and benefit plans may use their own hour counts; check the one that applies to you.

Questions

Is 37.5 hours full time?

Federal law does not say. DOL: "The Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) does not define full-time employment or part-time employment. This is a matter generally to be determined by the employer."

How many hours is 9 to 5 with an hour lunch?

35.00 hours a week: 7 hours a day for 5 days.

Do four 12-hour shifts earn overtime?

Under the federal 40-hour rule, four 7 AM to 7 PM shifts with a 30-minute break are 46.00 hours, 6.00 of them overtime. A state with daily overtime may count more.
