Military time chart for time cards

On the 24-hour clock, hours from 13 to 23 are PM: subtract 12, so 1430 is 2:30 PM and 1730 is 5:30 PM; as decimal hours from midnight 1430 is 14.50.

A shift from two punches

Subtract the in punch from the out punch in hours and minutes, then convert the minutes: 0700 to 1530 is 8 hours 30 minutes, 8.50 hours; less a 30-minute lunch, 8.00. When the out punch is smaller than the in punch, the shift crossed midnight: 2200 to 0630 is 8:30.

Worked out from the times shown, under the federal rules quoted. A state law, contract or policy may require more. Not legal or payroll advice.

Questions

What is 1430 in regular time?

2:30 PM. Subtract 12 from hours 13 to 23 for the PM hour.

How many hours is 0700 to 1530?

7:00 AM to 3:30 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes (8.50 hours).

How do I write midnight on a time card?

0000 starts the day; an out punch at the end of the day is sometimes written 2400. Both are 12:00 AM.
