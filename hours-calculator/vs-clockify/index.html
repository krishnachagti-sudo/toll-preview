Clockify vs the time card calculator

Clockify is a time tracker: as listed on November 8, 2026, its Free plan is for up to 5 users and paid plans run from $3.99 to $11.99 per seat a month billed annually, with overtime from the Standard plan; the calculator instead totals hours you already have, such as a 45-hour week that pays $950.00 at $20.00 with overtime, without an account or a seat.

Different jobs

Clockify records time as people work: a timer, a timesheet, apps and reports, priced per seat. The hours calculator does one job on times you already have, from a paper card, a punch report or a message: totals, overtime per workweek, a rounding check and a timesheet PDF, with no account needed on the web page.

The page lists the Free plan for up to 5 users with timesheets and reports; kiosk comes with Basic; timesheet approval, time off and overtime with Standard. Its FAQ mentions inviting up to 4 people free, so check the current limit.

When Clockify is better

  • A team tracks time every day and wants projects, clients and reports in one place.
  • Managers approve timesheets in an app.
  • You need a history of months of time entries, not one pay period.

When the calculator is enough

  • You have the punches and need the total and overtime now: for example a 45-hour week at $20.00 is $800.00 regular plus $150.00 overtime.
  • You need one printable timesheet for one person and pay period.
  • You want hours from code or an AI agent over the API or MCP, paid per call.

Totals, conversions and the rounding check are free on the calculator page; a signed-ready timesheet PDF and CSV costs $0.15 per employee and pay period; an API or MCP call costs $0.002.

Questions

Is Clockify free?

Clockify's pricing page (read November 8, 2026) lists a Free plan "For up to 5 users" with unlimited tracking and timesheets; kiosk, timesheet approval, time off and overtime are on paid plans.

Which Clockify plan has overtime?

Standard, listed at $5.49 per seat a month billed annually or $6.99 monthly, on the page read.

When is Clockify the better choice?

When a team needs to track time live, by project, with reports and approvals over months.
