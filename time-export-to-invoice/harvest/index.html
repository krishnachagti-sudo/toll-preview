Harvest

Harvest export to invoice lines

Harvest's pricing page lists invoicing on its Free, Teams and Enterprise plans (Free is 1 seat, 2 projects), and its API says rounded_hours is the value used in summary reports and invoices. To bill time from a Harvest export with your own lines and rates, open it here: 5 decimal-hour entries (7.6300 h) become 2 task lines, each line rounded to the nearest 6 minutes, totalling $630.50.

The fields Harvest names

Harvest's API v2 names these time entry fields; hours is decimal time tracked and rounded_hours is the value used in summary reports and invoices, rounded by the Time Rounding setting in Preferences. Harvest's help center returned 403 when read, so no help-center wording is used.

FieldWhat the lines use it for
spent_dateThe service date
clientGroup by client; a rate per client; keep one client
projectGroup by project; a rate per project
taskGroup by project and task; a rate per task
userGroup by person; a rate per person
notesThe description
hoursThe time billed (decimal hours)
rounded_hoursNot used: your rounding is applied to hours instead
billableNon-billable entries are left out (or kept, if you choose)
billable_rateThe rate, when your rate card does not set one
started_timeStart time on entry lines
ended_timeEnd time on entry lines

Harvest's own rounding example, checked

The API docs show 2.11 hours as 2.25 rounded hours and 1.35 as 1.5. Rounding up to 15 minutes gives exactly that: 2.11 to 2.25 and 1.35 to 1.50.

The sample as invoice lines

Harvest's own invoices are the simpler choice for time tracked in Harvest. The tool is for a layout or rate card Harvest's invoice does not give you, or time from more than one tracker; Everhour and other exports map the same way.

Lines are worked out from the entries shown, with the grouping, rounding and rates named. Rounding for freelance work is set by your contract; check an invoice before you send it. Not affiliated with Toggl, Clockify, Harvest or Everhour. Not tax or legal advice.

Questions

Does Harvest have invoicing on the free plan?

Yes. Harvest's pricing page lists invoicing on Free, which is 1 seat, 2 projects; Teams is $9 a seat a month billed annually (read November 8, 2026).

What rounding options does Harvest offer?

A Harvest blog post of January 5, 2010 lists rounding to nearest, or rounding up to 6, 15 or 30 minutes, on export and invoicing. Today the API docs say rounded_hours follows the Time Rounding setting in Preferences.
