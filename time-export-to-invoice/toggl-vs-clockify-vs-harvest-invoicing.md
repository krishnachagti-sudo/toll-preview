# Toggl vs Clockify vs Harvest invoicing

Toggl Track makes invoices from reports, Free plan included, with the Toggl logo on Free-plan PDFs; Clockify invoices only on Standard ($5.49 a seat a month billed annually) and up; Harvest lists invoicing on Free, Teams and Enterprise, with Free limited to 1 seat and 2 projects. Read on their own pages on November 8, 2026.

- Clockify Standard: $5.49
- Toggl Starter: $9
- Harvest Teams: $9

## Side by side

From each product's own help and pricing pages, read November 8, 2026.

|  | Toggl Track | Clockify | Harvest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invoicing on the free plan | Yes, with the Toggl logo on the PDF | No (Standard and up) | Yes (1 seat, 2 projects) |
| Cheapest paid plan, billed annually | Starter $9 a user a month | Basic $3.99; invoicing from Standard $5.49 | Teams $9 a seat a month |
| Invoice lines | Summary report: one per project; Detailed: one per entry | Single item, Detailed, or Grouped by project, user or date | From tracked time and expenses (grouping not on the pages read) |
| Rounding | Up, nearest or down; 1, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15 or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 4 hours; paid | Up, nearest or down; Standard and up | API: rounded\_hours follows the Time Rounding setting |
| Rounding level | Each entry, or the subtotal of a group | Each entry, then added up | Not stated on the pages read |
| CSV export | Paid plans | Basic and up (Free: PDF) | Not stated on the pages read |
| Layout of the invoice | Fixed | Editable line items | Not stated on the pages read |

## When each one is the better choice

- Toggl Track: you track in Toggl and a fixed layout with one line per project or per entry is enough.
- Clockify: you are on Standard already and want entries marked as invoiced when you import them.
- Harvest: you want time, invoices and online payments (PayPal, Stripe) in one place, and QuickBooks or Xero sync.
- This tool: time from any of them, lines grouped and rounded your way with a rate per task, at $0.40 an invoice with no seat plan.

## Questions

### Is Clockify really free?

Clockify's Free plan is free for up to 5 users, but invoicing, time rounding and CSV export are not on it: invoicing and rounding start at Standard ($5.49 a seat a month billed annually), CSV export at Basic.

### Which time tracker has free invoicing?

Toggl Track (from a report, with its logo on the PDF) and Harvest (Free plan of 1 seat and 2 projects).
