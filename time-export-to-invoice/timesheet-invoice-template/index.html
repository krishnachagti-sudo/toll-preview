What each column means

  • Date: the day the work was done (the service date).
  • Work: the entries' descriptions for that day, in plain words.
  • Hours: the day's billed time to two decimals of an hour (0.25 is 15 minutes, not 25).
  • Rate: per hour; a day with two kinds of work at different rates has a line for each.
  • Amount: hours times rate, to the cent.

Lines are worked out from the entries shown, with the grouping, rounding and rates named. Rounding for freelance work is set by your contract; check an invoice before you send it. Not affiliated with Toggl, Clockify, Harvest or Everhour. Not tax or legal advice.
