# Retainers and blocks of hours

A prepaid block of 20.00 hours against the sample month's 26.75 billed hours is used up day by day: 20.00 hours come off the block, 6.75 hours run over it and bill at $130 an hour ($877.50), and 0.00 hours are left to carry over.

- Over the block: 6.75
- To bill: $877.50

## Retainer, block and cap

- A block: hours paid in advance, used as work is done.
- Overage: hours past the block, billed at an agreed rate (often higher).
- A cap (not to exceed): a ceiling on the invoice, whatever the hours.
- Roll-over: unused hours that carry into the next period, if the contract says so.

## What to send the client

- The block, the hours used this period and what is left.
- Any overage, with its rate and amount.
- The dated lines, so the client can see where the block went.
