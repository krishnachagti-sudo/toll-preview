# A client-friendly time invoice

The same 13 entries can be 13 lines (one per entry), 13 dated lines (one per day) or 3 lines (one per task with the work listed), all totalling $2,760.00; most clients read the task summary best, with dates per line when they ask for them.

- Total: $2,760.00
- Task lines: 3

## What every time invoice needs

- Who it is from and who it is to, an invoice number and date, and when it is due.
- The service period (the first and last day of the work).
- Each line: what was done, the hours, the rate and the amount.
- The total, any tax, and how to pay.
- The rounding used, in one sentence, if you round.

## Summary or detail

One line per task, with the entries' descriptions under each.

| Line | Dates | Hours | Rate | Amount |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Website redesign: Design | Nov 2 to Nov 19, 2026; 4 entries; Homepage wireframes; Product page mockups; Icon set; Email templates | 8.50 | $90.00/h | $765.00 |
| Website redesign: Meetings | Nov 2 to Nov 25, 2026; 4 entries; Kickoff call; Weekly check-in; Review with client; Handover call | 3.25 | $60.00/h | $195.00 |
| Website redesign: Development | Nov 3 to Nov 24, 2026; 5 entries; Theme setup; Checkout fixes; Launch deploy; Performance pass; Bug fixes | 15.00 | $120.00/h | $1,800.00 |

Per day gives 13 dated lines; per entry gives 13. Send the entries file the tool makes as an attachment when a client wants every entry, rather than putting them all on the invoice.

## Describe the work, not the timer

- Write descriptions a client recognises ("Product page mockups", not "PPM v2 fix").
- Leave out timer notes, internal tags and non-billable admin time.
- Leave out start and stop times unless the client asks for them.

## Tax

Tax goes on its own line after the subtotal. At 8.25% the sample's $2,760.00 adds $227.70, $2,987.70 in all. Whether your services are taxed, and at what rate, depends on where you and your client are: check with your tax authority.
