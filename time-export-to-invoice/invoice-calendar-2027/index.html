Invoicing on the last business day

  • Export the month's detailed report the morning of the last business day, or the first business day after, so late entries are in.
  • Set the invoice dates to the first and last day of the month, so entries from the edges are not missed or doubled.
  • Keep the same grouping, rounding and rates each month: the tool page keeps them in the link.

For any other date range or country, the business days calculator counts working days.
