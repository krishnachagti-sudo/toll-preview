# Harvest vs this tool for time invoices

Harvest invoices the time and expenses tracked in it, takes payment through PayPal and Stripe and copies invoices to QuickBooks Online or Xero; its pricing page lists invoicing on Free (1 seat, 2 projects) and Teams at $9 a seat a month billed annually ($11 monthly), read November 8, 2026. This tool is $0.40 an invoice for time from any tracker.

- Harvest Teams: $9
- This tool: $0.40

## When Harvest is better

- You want time, invoices, payments and reports in one place.
- Clients pay online (PayPal, Stripe), and invoices and payments sync to QuickBooks Online or Xero.
- Your time is already in Harvest and its invoice layout suits your clients.

## When exporting the lines is better

- Your time lives in another tracker, or in more than one.
- You want a line layout, rate card, retainer balance or cap that your tracker's invoice does not give.
- You invoice a few times a month and do not want a seat plan for it.

For one person; a seat plan costs the same however many invoices you make. This tool's price is per invoice, with a monthly cap.

| Plan | 1 invoice a month | 4 a month | 20 a month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Harvest Teams, billed monthly ($11) | $11.00 | $2.75 | $0.55 |
| Harvest Teams, billed annually ($9) | $9.00 | $2.25 | $0.45 |
| This tool, $0.40 an invoice (cap $2.99 a month), total | $0.40 | $1.60 | $2.99 |

> Harvest Free (1 seat, 2 projects, invoicing included) costs nothing if one person and two projects are enough.
