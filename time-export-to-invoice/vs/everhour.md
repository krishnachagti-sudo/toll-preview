# Everhour vs this tool for time invoices

Everhour lists billing, budgeting and invoicing on its Team plan, $8.50 a seat a month billed yearly with a minimum of 5 seats, so at least $42.50 a month (read November 8, 2026); its Free plan has exports but no invoicing. This tool turns an Everhour report export into invoice lines for $0.40 an invoice, capped at $2.99 a month.

- Everhour Team: $8.50
- This tool: $0.40

## When Everhour is better

- Your team tracks time inside project tools Everhour connects to, and you want budgets and invoices there too.
- You need its reports with configurable columns for more than invoicing.

## When exporting the lines is better

- You are one person, or fewer than five, and invoicing alone does not justify five seats.
- You export from Everhour Free and want grouped, rounded lines with a rate per task.

For one person; a seat plan costs the same however many invoices you make. This tool's price is per invoice, with a monthly cap.

| Plan | 1 invoice a month | 4 a month | 20 a month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Everhour Team, 5 seats billed yearly ($42.50 a month) | $42.50 | $10.63 | $2.13 |
| This tool, $0.40 an invoice (cap $2.99 a month), total | $0.40 | $1.60 | $2.99 |

Everhour's report columns are chosen by you, so map Date and Time if they are headed differently: [Harvest and Everhour columns](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/time-export-to-invoice/guides/harvest-everhour-exports/).
