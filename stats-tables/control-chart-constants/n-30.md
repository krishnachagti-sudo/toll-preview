# Control chart constants for n = 30

For subgroup size 30: A3 = 0.5525, B3 = 0.6044, B4 = 1.3956, c4 = 0.9914 and d2 = 4.0855; for an X-bar and R chart, A2 = 0.1341, D3 = 0.4914 and D4 = 1.5086.

## Which chart to use at this size

At n = 30 the X-bar and s chart is the usual choice: NIST's e-Handbook says the range works well up to about 10 and that subgroup standard deviations are preferable for larger samples. With s-bar the limits are X-double-bar +/- 0.5525 s-bar, and the s chart runs from 0.6044 s-bar to 1.3956 s-bar.

If you do use ranges at this size, sigma is R-bar / 4.0855, the X-bar limits are X-double-bar +/- 0.1341 R-bar, and the R chart runs from 0.4914 R-bar to 1.5086 R-bar.

## How these are computed

- d2 and d3 are the mean and standard deviation of the range of n independent standard normal values. They are integrals with no closed form past n = 3, so they are computed by numerical integration of the range's distribution, P(W \<= w) = n times the integral of phi(x) \[Phi(x + w) - Phi(x)\]^(n - 1) dx, and checked against the exact values for n = 2 (2 / sqrt(pi)) and n = 3 (3 / sqrt(pi)) to better than 1e-10.
- c4 is exact from the gamma function, as NIST gives it: c4 = sqrt(2 / (n - 1)) Gamma(n / 2) / Gamma((n - 1) / 2).
- Every other constant is a formula of d2, d3 and c4 (the formula column), with negative lower-limit factors set to 0.

> Many printed tables stop at n = 25. Minitab's help lists d3 only to n = 25 and gives an approximation past n = 50; these values are computed from the definition at every n.
