## Formulas

- X-bar and R: `X-double-bar +/- A2 R-bar`, R chart `D3 R-bar to D4 R-bar`, sigma `R-bar / d2`
- X-bar and s: `X-double-bar +/- A3 s-bar`, s chart `B3 s-bar to B4 s-bar`, sigma `s-bar / c4`
- Individuals: `X-bar +/- E2 MR-bar` with E2 = 2.6587 for moving ranges of 2, and the moving range chart up to `D4 MR-bar` with D4 = 3.2665.

## How these are computed

- d2 and d3 are the mean and standard deviation of the range of n independent standard normal values. They are integrals with no closed form past n = 3, so they are computed by numerical integration of the range's distribution, P(W \<= w) = n times the integral of phi(x) \[Phi(x + w) - Phi(x)\]^(n - 1) dx, and checked against the exact values for n = 2 (2 / sqrt(pi)) and n = 3 (3 / sqrt(pi)) to better than 1e-10.
- c4 is exact from the gamma function, as NIST gives it: c4 = sqrt(2 / (n - 1)) Gamma(n / 2) / Gamma((n - 1) / 2).
- Every other constant is a formula of d2, d3 and c4 (the formula column), with negative lower-limit factors set to 0.
