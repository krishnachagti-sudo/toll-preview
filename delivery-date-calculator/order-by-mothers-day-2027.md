# Last day to order for Mother's Day 2027

Mother's Day 2027 is Sunday, May 9, 2027. For delivery by Saturday, May 8, 2027, a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff and 1 processing day must take the order by Friday, April 30, 2027, 2:00 pm EDT for USPS Ground Advantage, Tuesday, May 4, 2027, 2:00 pm EDT for Priority Mail, and Tuesday, May 4, 2027, 2:00 pm EDT for Priority Mail Express.

## How the dates are worked out

- USPS Ground Advantage takes up to 5 days (USPS's stated delivery time) on the carrier's calendar (USPS delivers Monday to Saturday, not on postal holidays), so the last handover that arrives by Saturday, May 8, 2027 is Monday, May 3, 2027.
- With 1 processing day on the store's calendar (the store ships Monday to Friday, not on federal holidays), the order must be received by Friday, April 30, 2027.
- So the last order is by Friday, April 30, 2027, 2:00 pm EDT, the cutoff that day.

The delivery times are what USPS states for each service on its [Mail and shipping services](https://www.usps.com/ship/mail-shipping-services.htm) page; this tool counts them on the USPS delivery calendar, Monday to Saturday without postal holidays, and every day for Priority Mail Express.

> USPS states delivery times, not guarantees, except where it sells a guarantee. Ship earlier for high-value or time-sensitive gifts, and tell customers the ships-by date as well as the arrives-by date (the FTC Mail Order Rule expects a reasonable basis for any shipping time you state).

## Questions

### What day is Mother's Day 2027?

Sunday, May 9, 2027. Mother's Day is the second Sunday in May (36 U.S.C. 117), so for most USPS services the last delivery day before it is Saturday, May 8.

### Can a parcel arrive on Mother's Day itself?

Only by Priority Mail Express, which USPS delivers every day of the year with limited exceptions. For arrival by Sunday, May 9, 2027 with 1 processing day, the last order is Wednesday, May 5, 2027, 2:00 pm EDT. Other services do not deliver on Sundays, so they must arrive by Saturday, May 8.

### Are these USPS guarantees?

No. They use the upper end of USPS's stated delivery time for each service (for example 2 to 5 days for Ground Advantage), counted on the days USPS delivers. Leave a margin for busy weeks.
