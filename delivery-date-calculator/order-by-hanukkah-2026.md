# Last day to order for Hanukkah 2026

For delivery by Friday, December 4, 2026, the first day of Hanukkah, a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff and 1 processing day must take the last USPS Ground Advantage order by Wednesday, November 25, 2:00 pm EST, because Thanksgiving falls in the way, and the last Priority Mail Express order by Monday, November 30, 2:00 pm EST.

- Ground Advantage, 1 day: Wednesday, November 25
- Priority Mail Express, 1 day: Monday, November 30
- Arrive by: Friday, December 4

## Questions

### When does Hanukkah start in 2026?

At sundown on Friday, December 4, 2026; USPS lists Hanukkah as December 4 to December 12 and notes that it is earlier this year.

### Does USPS publish Hanukkah shipping deadlines?

No. USPS publishes send-by dates only for delivery before December 25, so these dates count back from December 4 with the slowest of USPS's stated delivery times.
