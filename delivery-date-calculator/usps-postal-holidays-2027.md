# USPS postal holidays 2027

USPS has 11 postal holidays in 2027, with no regular mail delivery and Post Offices closed. 2 fall differently from federal offices: Juneteenth and Christmas Day fall on a Saturday, so USPS closes on the Saturday and delivers normally on Friday, June 18 and Friday, December 24, the Fridays federal offices take off.

## Why do USPS and federal offices close on different days?

- Post Offices are closed and there is no regular delivery on each federal holiday.
- A holiday on a Saturday closes that Saturday only: USPS says Post Offices are open with normal deliveries on the Friday before (Friday, June 18 and Friday, December 24, 2027). The federal observed Friday is not a postal closure.
- A holiday on a Sunday closes the Monday after (Independence Day 2027 is listed on Monday, July 5).

So a delivery estimate needs two calendars: your shipping calendar, which may follow the federal days off, and the carrier's delivery calendar, which follows these postal holidays. The calculator keeps them apart and names each day it skips.

> USPS delivers Monday to Saturday; Priority Mail Express delivers every day with limited exceptions. The 11 closures USPS lists for 2027 on its newsroom page match the rule above.

## Questions

### Is there mail on Friday, June 18, 2027?

Yes. Juneteenth falls on Saturday, June 19, 2027. USPS says Post Offices are open with normal deliveries on the Friday before and closed on the Saturday.

### Is there mail on Friday, December 24, 2027?

Yes. Christmas Day 2027 is a Saturday, and USPS lists Friday, December 24 as a normal delivery day, with the Saturday closed.

### Which day is the Independence Day postal holiday in 2027?

Monday, July 5, 2027. July 4 falls on a Sunday, so the holiday moves to the Monday, for USPS and federal offices alike.
