# Delivery dates for orders in Thanksgiving week 2026

Thanksgiving, Thursday, November 26, 2026, is a federal and postal holiday: no USPS delivery and, for a store that follows federal holidays, no shipping. For a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff and 1 processing day, a USPS Ground Advantage order placed at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 25 ships Monday, November 30 and arrives Wednesday, December 2 to Saturday, December 5.

## Which days are skipped?

- Thursday, November 26, 2026: Thanksgiving Day, a federal holiday (the store does not ship) and a postal holiday (USPS does not deliver).
- Saturdays and Sundays: the store does not ship.
- Sundays: USPS does not deliver, except Priority Mail Express.

For Christmas, use the USPS send-by dates on [holiday shipping deadlines 2026](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/delivery-date-calculator/holiday-shipping-deadlines-2026/).

## Questions

### Does USPS deliver on Thanksgiving 2026?

No regular delivery. Thanksgiving Day is a postal holiday: Post Offices are closed and there is no regular delivery. USPS says Priority Mail Express delivers 365 days a year, with limited exceptions.

### Does USPS deliver on Black Friday?

Yes. Friday, November 27, 2026 is not a federal or postal holiday, so it is a normal USPS delivery day.

### When does a Black Friday order ship?

For a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff and 1 processing day, an order at 10:00 am on Black Friday ships Monday, November 30 and by Ground Advantage arrives Wednesday, December 2 to Saturday, December 5.
