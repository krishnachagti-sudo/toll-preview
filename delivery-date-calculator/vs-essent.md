# Essent Estimated Delivery Date or Endwise?

Use Essent if you want a delivery-date widget on Shopify product, cart and checkout pages that runs on every view; its listing shows a free plan up to 1,000 monthly views. Use Endwise if you need the date itself, with the steps and skipped holidays named, on the web or over an API: an order at 3:12 pm on Friday, December 11, 2026 after a 2 pm cutoff arrives Thursday, December 17 to Monday, December 21 by Ground Advantage.

## What does each one do?

|  | Essent Estimated Delivery Date | Endwise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A Shopify app | A calculator on the web, a REST API and an MCP tool |
| Where the date shows | Product page, cart and checkout placements, per its listing | On its own page, in your code, or a pasted Christmas banner |
| Inputs | Processing time, cut-off hours, holidays, delivery timer, per its listing | Cutoff, time zone, shipping days, closures, processing days, carrier and service |
| Holiday calendars | Holidays you set in the app | US federal holidays for shipping and USPS postal holidays for delivery, built in, plus your closures |
| Pricing model | Monthly plans by widget views, with a free plan up to 1,000 views | Per use from a prepaid balance |
| Shows its working | Not stated on the listing | Every answer lists the steps and each skipped day by name |

## When is Essent the better choice?

- You run Shopify and want a live estimate on every product page with no code.
- You want countdown timers, many pre-built widget designs, translations and geolocation targeting, which its listing includes.
- You want an app with many reviews: its listing showed 1,058 reviews averaging 5.0 on the day we read it.

## When is Endwise the better choice?

- You need the date in your own system: an order system, a support reply, an agent or a spreadsheet, over the API or MCP.
- You want the carrier's delivery calendar kept apart from your shipping calendar, as USPS closes on different days from federal offices.
- You need a one-off check or a seasonal deadline, such as the [last day to order for Christmas](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/delivery-date-calculator/order-by-christmas-2026/), without a monthly plan.

## Questions

### Is Essent free?

Its Shopify App Store listing shows a free plan of up to 1,000 monthly views and paid plans by monthly views (5,000, 15,000 and unlimited), as read on Friday, November 6, 2026. Prices are shown in your store's currency on the listing.

### Does Endwise put a widget on my product page?

Not a live widget. It works out dates on its page, over an API or MCP, and makes a Christmas order-by banner you paste into a Custom Liquid section.
