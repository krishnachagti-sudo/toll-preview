# Last day to order for Valentine's Day 2027

Valentine's Day 2027 is Sunday, February 14, 2027. For delivery by Saturday, February 13, 2027, a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff and 1 processing day must take the order by Friday, February 5, 2027, 2:00 pm EST for USPS Ground Advantage, Tuesday, February 9, 2027, 2:00 pm EST for Priority Mail, and Tuesday, February 9, 2027, 2:00 pm EST for Priority Mail Express.

## How the dates are worked out

- USPS Ground Advantage takes up to 5 days (USPS's stated delivery time) on the carrier's calendar (USPS delivers Monday to Saturday, not on postal holidays), so the last handover that arrives by Saturday, February 13, 2027 is Monday, February 8, 2027.
- With 1 processing day on the store's calendar (the store ships Monday to Friday, not on federal holidays), the order must be received by Friday, February 5, 2027.
- So the last order is by Friday, February 5, 2027, 2:00 pm EST, the cutoff that day.

The delivery times are what USPS states for each service on its [Mail and shipping services](https://www.usps.com/ship/mail-shipping-services.htm) page; this tool counts them on the USPS delivery calendar, Monday to Saturday without postal holidays, and every day for Priority Mail Express.

> USPS states delivery times, not guarantees, except where it sells a guarantee. Ship earlier for high-value or time-sensitive gifts, and tell customers the ships-by date as well as the arrives-by date (the FTC Mail Order Rule expects a reasonable basis for any shipping time you state).

## Questions

### What day is Valentine's Day 2027?

Sunday, February 14, 2027. It falls on a Sunday, so for most USPS services the last delivery day before it is Saturday, February 13.

### Can a parcel arrive on Valentine's Day itself?

Only by Priority Mail Express, which USPS delivers every day of the year with limited exceptions. For arrival by Sunday, February 14, 2027 with 1 processing day, the last order is Wednesday, February 10, 2027, 2:00 pm EST. Other services do not deliver on Sundays, so they must arrive by Saturday, February 13.

### Are these USPS guarantees?

No. They use the upper end of USPS's stated delivery time for each service (for example 2 to 5 days for Ground Advantage), counted on the days USPS delivers. Leave a margin for busy weeks.

### Does Presidents Day 2027 affect Valentine's deliveries?

No. Washington's Birthday (Presidents Day) is Monday, February 15, 2027, the day after Valentine's Day, so it falls after every deadline on this page.
