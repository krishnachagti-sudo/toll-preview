# Holiday shipping deadlines 2026

USPS recommends sending by Thursday, December 17, 2026 for Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, Friday, December 18 for Priority Mail and Saturday, December 19 for Priority Mail Express. For a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff and 1 processing day, the last Ground Advantage order is Wednesday, December 16, 2:00 pm EST and the last Priority Mail Express order is Thursday, December 17, 2:00 pm EST.

- Ground Advantage send-by: Thursday, December 17
- Priority Mail send-by: Friday, December 18
- Priority Mail Express send-by: Saturday, December 19
- Last Ground Advantage order: Wednesday, December 16

Each last order date is the day the order must be in by 2:00 pm EST. Priority Mail Express can be sent on Saturday, December 19, but a store that does not ship on Saturdays hands it over on Friday, so its last order comes a day earlier. [Alaska, Hawaii, international and military dates](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/delivery-date-calculator/holiday-shipping-deadlines-2026/usps/) are on the USPS page.

> USPS's dates are recommendations for delivery before December 25, not guarantees; USPS says actual delivery varies with origin, destination and acceptance time. Last order dates assume the parcel is handed to USPS on the day it is ready.

## Holidays in the way

- Thursday, November 26, 2026: Thanksgiving Day. No USPS delivery, and closed for stores that follow federal holidays.
- Friday, December 25, 2026: Christmas Day. A postal holiday; Priority Mail Express still delivers.
- Friday, January 1, 2027: New Year's Day.

## UPS and FedEx

## Questions

### What are the USPS 2026 holiday shipping deadlines for Christmas?

USPS recommends sending by Thursday, December 17, 2026 for Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, Friday, December 18 for Priority Mail and Saturday, December 19 for Priority Mail Express, for delivery in the contiguous US before December 25, 2026.

### What is the last day to order for Christmas 2026 delivery?

It depends on your processing time and cutoff. For a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff and 1 processing day it is Wednesday, December 16, 2:00 pm EST for Ground Advantage and Thursday, December 17, 2:00 pm EST for Priority Mail Express.

### Does USPS deliver on Christmas Eve 2026?

Yes. Thursday, December 24, 2026 is not a federal or postal holiday, so USPS delivers. Christmas Day, Friday, December 25, is a postal holiday, when only Priority Mail Express delivers.
