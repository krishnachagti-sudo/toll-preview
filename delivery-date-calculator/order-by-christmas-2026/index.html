Last day to order for Christmas 2026

To arrive by Christmas Eve, Thursday, December 24, 2026, by USPS Ground Advantage, a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff must take the last order by Thursday, December 17, 2:00 pm EST with no processing time, and by Thursday, December 10, 2:00 pm EST with 5 processing days.

Processing days are whole shipping days after the order is received: 0 means it is handed to USPS the same day. Orders after the cutoff, or on a Saturday or Sunday, count from the next shipping day.

USPS's dates are recommendations for delivery before December 25, not guarantees; USPS says actual delivery varies with origin, destination and acceptance time. Last order dates assume the parcel is handed to USPS on the day it is ready.

Questions

What is the last day to order online for Christmas 2026?

For USPS Ground Advantage and a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff: Wednesday, December 16, 2:00 pm EST with 1 processing day, Tuesday, December 15, 2:00 pm EST with 2. Faster services allow a day or two more: Priority Mail Express until Thursday, December 17, 2:00 pm EST with 1 processing day.
