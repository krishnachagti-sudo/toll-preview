# Priority Mail Express: Christmas 2026 deadline

USPS recommends sending Priority Mail Express by Saturday, December 19, 2026 for delivery before Christmas; a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff and 1 processing day must take the last order by Thursday, December 17, 2:00 pm EST.

- USPS send-by: Saturday, December 19
- Last order, 1 processing day: Thursday, December 17
- Cutoff: 2:00 pm EST
- USPS delivery time: 1 to 3 days
