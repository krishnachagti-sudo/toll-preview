# Priority Mail: Christmas 2026 deadline

USPS recommends sending Priority Mail by Friday, December 18, 2026 for delivery before Christmas; a store that ships Monday to Friday with a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff and 1 processing day must take the last order by Thursday, December 17, 2:00 pm EST.

- USPS send-by: Friday, December 18
- Last order, 1 processing day: Thursday, December 17
- Cutoff: 2:00 pm EST
- USPS delivery time: 2 to 3 days

## Questions

### What is the last day to ship Priority Mail for Christmas 2026?

USPS recommends Friday, December 18, 2026 for delivery in the contiguous US before December 25.

### How long does Priority Mail take?

USPS states 2 to 3 days. USPS delivers it Monday to Saturday, not on postal holidays.
