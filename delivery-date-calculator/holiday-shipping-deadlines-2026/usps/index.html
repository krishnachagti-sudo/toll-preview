USPS, Christmas 2026

USPS holiday shipping deadlines 2026

USPS recommends sending by Thursday, December 17, 2026 for Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail, Friday, December 18 for Priority Mail and Saturday, December 19 for Priority Mail Express; with 1 processing day and a 2:00 pm Eastern cutoff, your last Ground Advantage order is Wednesday, December 16, 2:00 pm EST. International mail goes by Wednesday, December 9 (Priority Mail Express International Wednesday, December 16), and Ground Advantage to APO/FPO/DPO addresses by Wednesday, November 4.

USPS's dates are recommendations for delivery before December 25, not guarantees; USPS says actual delivery varies with origin, destination and acceptance time. Last order dates assume the parcel is handed to USPS on the day it is ready.

UPS and FedEx

These pages restate only dates read from the carrier's own page. UPS and FedEx publish their 2026 dates on ups.com and fedex.com. Put the date for your service into the calculator as the carrier's send-by date (choose "Another carrier") and it works out your last order date the same way.

Questions

What is the USPS international holiday shipping deadline for 2026?

For every region USPS lists, Wednesday, December 9 for First-Class Package International and Priority Mail International, and Wednesday, December 16 for Priority Mail Express International.

When should I mail to APO, FPO and DPO addresses for Christmas 2026?

By Wednesday, November 4 for USPS Ground Advantage, Wednesday, December 9 for First-Class Mail and Priority Mail, and Wednesday, December 16 for Priority Mail Express Military where it is offered (not to ZIP 093).
