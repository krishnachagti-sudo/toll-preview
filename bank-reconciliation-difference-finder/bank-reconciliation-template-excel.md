## How the layout works

- Two sides that must meet. AccountingCoach: "Adjusted balance per BANK must equal Adjusted balance per BOOKS."
- Deposits in transit and outstanding checks go on the bank side: real items the bank has not processed yet. AccountingCoach: "the company's bank reconciliation will show the deposits in transit as an addition to the balance per bank"
- Bank items the books lack (fees, interest) and corrections go on the books side, as entries you still have to make. AccountingCoach: "Record in the company's general ledger the adjustments to the balance per BOOKS."
- The last line is the difference. Anything but 0.00 means a line is missing from both lists, or an opening balance is wrong.

To get a PDF, open the CSV in Excel and print it to PDF, or print this page. A blank template tells you nothing until each line is filled; the tool fills every one from your statement and ledger.
