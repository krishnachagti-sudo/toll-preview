## The date rule

An item is outstanding when it is in the books on or before the statement date and has no bank line. A books line dated after the statement date is left out of the list. The aging counts days from the item's date to the statement date.

## Questions

### What is a deposit in transit?

A deposit in the books that the bank has not processed by the statement date. AccountingCoach: "the company's bank reconciliation will show the deposits in transit as an addition to the balance per bank".

### What is an outstanding check?

A check written and recorded in the books that has not cleared the bank by the statement date. It is subtracted from the balance per bank.
