## The routine

- Pick the age that counts as stale for your business (the tool flags 90 days by default; this page uses 180).
- Contact each payee: was it received, lost, or not cashed on purpose?
- Reissue, or void it in the books, as your policy and your accountant decide. Voiding returns the amount to the balance per books; the item then drops off the outstanding list.
- Unclaimed money may have to go to the state rather than back to you. Rules and dormancy periods differ by state: check your state's unclaimed property rules. This page is not legal advice.
- Keep the contact notes and the decision with the reconciliation.
