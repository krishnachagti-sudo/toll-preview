## December 31 traps

- Deposits made on December 31 that the bank posts in January: deposits in transit, not errors (here $386.40).
- Checks written in the last week: outstanding until they clear.
- Card and bank fees charged on the last day: enter them in December.
- Transfers between your own accounts dated December 31 on one side and January 2 on the other.

## Steps

1. **The bank statement.** For the period ending December 31, with its ending balance.
2. **The ledger report at the same date.** The cash account's lines and balance at December 31 (QuickBooks Online: Transaction Detail by Account; Xero: Account Transactions).
3. **The reconciliation statement.** Adjusted bank balance and adjusted books balance, equal.
4. **The outstanding items list.** Every outstanding check and deposit in transit, with dates and ages; stale ones explained.
5. **Sign-off.** Prepared by and date, reviewed by and date.
