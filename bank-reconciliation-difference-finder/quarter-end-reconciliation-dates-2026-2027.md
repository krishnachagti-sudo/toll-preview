# Quarter-end reconciliation dates, 2026 to 2027

For the quarter ending December 31, 2026 (a Thursday), an outstanding check written before October 2, 2026 is over 90 days old and one before July 4, 2026 over 180; the table gives the same cut-offs for all 5 quarter ends to December 31, 2027.
