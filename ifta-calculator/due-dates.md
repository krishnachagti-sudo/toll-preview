# IFTA quarterly due dates

An IFTA return is due on the last day of the month after the quarter: January 31, April 30, July 31 and October 31, moved to the next business day when that is a weekend, so the 3rd quarter 2026 return is due November 2, 2026.

- 3rd quarter 2026 due: November 2, 2026
- Late penalty: $50 or 10%
- Interest, 2026: 0.75% a month

## Late cost

Oregon DOT Form 9740: "Multiply the tax due in Column I by 0.0075 for each month or partial month the report is late. Do not calculate interest on credit amounts in Column I." and "If this return is filed late, enter 10% of the total amount of Column I, or $50, whichever is greater." Idaho: "For purposes of calculating interest, a report is one month late, even if you submitted it only a day late."

## Questions

### What if I had no miles this quarter?

File anyway. Virginia DMV says a return is due whether or not you had operations in the period.

### What does filing late cost?

Interest on each line with tax due for every month or part of a month late (0.75% a month in 2025 and 2026), plus a penalty of $50 or 10% of the net tax, whichever is greater.
