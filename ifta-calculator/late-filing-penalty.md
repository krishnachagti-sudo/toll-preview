# Late IFTA return: penalty and interest

A late IFTA return costs a penalty of $50 or 10% of the net tax, whichever is greater, plus interest of 0.75% a month in 2026 on each jurisdiction with tax due, with any part of a month counted as a full month: $1,200 of tax filed a few days late is $120.00 + $9.00 = $129.00.

- $1,200 due, a few days late: $129.00
- Minimum penalty: $50
- Interest, 2026: 0.75% a month

## How much is the IFTA late penalty?

The greater of $50 or 10% of the net tax due on the return (R1220.100). Below $500 of net tax, the $50 minimum is the larger one.

## How does IFTA interest add up?

By jurisdiction and by month. R1230.300: "A full month's interest shall accrue for any portion of a month on which tax owed remains unpaid." The Idaho State Tax Commission puts it this way: "For purposes of calculating interest, a report is one month late, even if you submitted it only a day late." For a fleet based in the US the yearly rate is 2 points above the IRS underpayment rate, reset every January 1; IFTA, Inc. tells the jurisdictions the new rate by December 1 (R1230.100). Canadian-based fleets use a rate tied to the 1-year Treasury Bill (R1230.200).

## When does a late return lead to suspension or revocation?

R1270: if a tax delinquency is not satisfied, or no written appeal is filed within 30 days of the notice of delinquency, the base jurisdiction sends a notice of immediate revocation. A license is not renewed for the next year until all returns are filed and all tax, penalty and interest paid (R345.100). Virginia DMV: "Failure to pay delinquent tax, penalty, and/or interest may result in the suspension of your IFTA License."

## Questions

### What is the penalty for filing IFTA late?

IFTA Articles of Agreement R1220.100: the base jurisdiction may assess "a penalty of $50.00 or 10 percent of delinquent taxes, whichever is greater, for failing to file a tax return, filing a late tax return, underpaying taxes due." Some base jurisdictions add penalties of their own (R1220.300).

### How is IFTA interest worked out?

Separately for each jurisdiction with tax due, from the due date, for each month or part of a month until paid (R1230.300). The rate was 0.75% a month in 2025 and 2026 for US-based fleets, the IRS underpayment rate plus 2 points divided by 12.

### Is a credit return penalized if it is late?

It can be. In the worked example the return is a credit overall, but the lines with tax due still earn interest and the $50 minimum applies: $51.28 in all.

### Can the penalty be waived?

R1260.100: the base jurisdiction may waive penalties for reasonable cause. Interest can be waived only where the late filing came from misinformation given by the base jurisdiction, and only as its law allows.

### Can I lose my IFTA license for not paying?

Yes. R1270: if a delinquency is not satisfied and no written appeal is filed within 30 days of the notice, a notice of immediate revocation is sent. Virginia DMV says unpaid tax, penalty or interest may lead to suspension, with a $50 fee to reinstate.
