# Renew your IFTA license for 2027

File your 2027 IFTA renewal with your base jurisdiction before December 31, 2026. January and February 2027 are a 2-month grace period to put the new decals on, not to apply: IFTA, Inc. says renewals for 2027 must be filed before the end of 2026.

- Apply by: December 31, 2026
- Display grace: January and February 2027
- Decals per truck: 2

## When is the 2027 IFTA renewal due?

Before the end of 2026. The IFTA, Inc. memorandum of October 1, 2026: "IFTA License Renewals for 2027 MUST BE FILED WITH YOUR BASE JURISDICTION BEFORE THE END OF 2026." Each base jurisdiction sets its own application window and fee; check its site.

## What does the January and February grace period cover?

Only the display of the new credentials. R655.200 gives carriers renewing their license and decals January and February to display them. To run in IFTA jurisdictions in those months you need one of:

- a valid 2027 IFTA license and two 2027 decals;
- a valid 2026 IFTA license and two 2026 decals, if a 2027 renewal application has been filed; or
- a valid trip permit from the jurisdiction you are running in.

## What blocks a renewal?

R345.100: a renewal is issued only if the license is not revoked, suspended or cancelled, all tax returns have been filed and all fuel use taxes, penalties and interest have been paid. A missing quarter, even one with no miles, holds it up: see [zero IFTA returns](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/ifta-calculator/zero-return/) and [late IFTA penalties](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/ifta-calculator/late-filing-penalty/).

## Decals and the license in the truck

The base jurisdiction issues one license and two decals per vehicle (R605). The decals go on the outside of both sides of the cab (R625), and a copy of the license goes in each qualified vehicle (R610). Adding a truck mid-year? Ask your base jurisdiction for decals for it; a temporary decal permit is valid 30 days (R263).

## Renewing early still means a 4th quarter return

R630: carriers renewing may run on the new decals and license two months before the effective date, but they still file the 4th quarter report, with all operations for that quarter. That return is due [February 1, 2027](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/ifta-calculator/q4-2026-return/).

## Steps

1. **Clear what you owe.** File every return and pay all tax, penalty and interest: R345.100 renews only a license that is not revoked, suspended or cancelled, with all returns filed and paid.
2. **Apply before December 31, 2026.** File the 2027 renewal with your base jurisdiction. Some jurisdictions renew automatically and tell you so (R345.200).
3. **Put the decals on.** Two decals per vehicle, on the outside of both sides of the cab (R605, R625), and a copy of the license in each truck (R610).
4. **File the 4th quarter 2026 return.** Due February 1, 2027, with all operations for the quarter, even if you already run on the 2027 credentials (R630).

## Questions

### When do IFTA decals expire?

The license runs for the calendar year and ends December 31 (R610), and decals are issued annually. 2026 credentials expire December 31, 2026.

### Is there a grace period for 2027 IFTA decals?

Yes, for display only. R655.200 gives January and February to display the renewal license and decals; during that time you may run on the valid 2026 license and decals if a 2027 renewal application has been filed, or on a trip permit.

### Can I renew if I owe IFTA tax?

No. R345.100 issues a renewal only if all tax returns have been filed and all fuel use taxes, penalties and interest have been paid, and the license is not revoked, suspended or cancelled.

### Can I put the 2027 decals on early?

Yes. R630 lets carriers operate with the new decals and license up to two months before the effective date, but they still file the 4th quarter report for the year before.
