Guide

IFTA records an auditor asks for

IFTA records are trip sheets (individual vehicle distance records) with dates, origin and destination, route, odometer readings, distance by jurisdiction and the unit number, plus fuel receipts with the date, seller name and address, gallons, fuel type, price and unit, kept 4 years from the due or filing date, whichever is later.

Keep for
4 years
Trip fields
7
Receipt fields
6 + company name

Each trip

  • Beginning and ending dates of the trip
  • Origin and destination of the trip
  • Route of travel
  • Beginning and ending odometer or hubometer readings for each trip
  • Trip distance by jurisdiction
  • Vehicle unit or identification number

Each fuel receipt

  • Date of each fuel purchase
  • Name and address of the person who sold or gave you the fuel
  • Number of gallons received
  • Type of fuel
  • Price per gallon or total price of the fuel
  • Vehicle unit or identification number that the fuel was placed in
  • your company name must appear on all receipts or invoices for tax-paid fuel purchases

The calculator's trip sheet check names every trip and receipt missing one of these fields, and joins each truck's odometer readings from trip to trip so unlogged miles show up before an auditor finds them.

The sample fleet on the calculator page: 2 vehicles, 4 trips and 5 receipts: 0 errors, 1 warning and 1 missing record field. The prepared return lists each one.

Please read

  • Computed from rates published by IFTA member jurisdictions in the IFTA tax rate matrix, as copied by Oregon DOT and New York State Tax (final rates, sources listed); not affiliated with IFTA, Inc. Your base jurisdiction's return governs.
  • This is a calculation, not tax or legal advice; keep your original trip records and receipts for 4 years from the due or filing date, whichever is later.
  • Record checks compare your records with the IFTA distance and fuel record elements; passing them does not guarantee an audit outcome.
  • Rounding follows Oregon DOT Form 9740 (whole miles and gallons, MPG to two decimals); penalty and interest use the general IFTA rules. Your base jurisdiction may compute or round differently.

Sources

  • Idaho State Tax Commission, IFTA licenses: records (IVDR) (read November 2, 2026)
  • Oregon DOT, IFTA Tax Return Form 735-9740 and its filing instructions (03-26) (read November 2, 2026)

Questions

How long do I keep IFTA records?

The Idaho State Tax Commission: four years from the due date of the return or the actual date the return is filed, whichever is later.

What happens if records are missing?

Oregon DOT Form 9740: "At time of audit, failure to maintain adequate records may result in disallowance of tax-paid credits claimed on the IFTA tax returns, assessing fleet miles per gallon to 4.0 mpg, or both."
