# Your base jurisdiction's portal and an IFTA calculator

You file and pay an IFTA return only with your base jurisdiction, in its portal or on its form; a calculator prepares the numbers. Under R960 the return and full payment are due together, February 1, 2027 for the 4th quarter 2026. This calculator works the return out ($0.03 an estimate, $5 a return for 1 to 5 vehicles) and does not file it.

- Return, 1 to 5 vehicles: $5
- Estimate: $0.03

## What does the base jurisdiction portal do?

It is where the return is filed and the tax paid. R940 lets a base jurisdiction accept an electronic return, and R960 makes the return and full payment due on the last day of the month after the quarter. The portal distributes the tax to every jurisdiction you drove in; filing and paying there discharges your duty to all of them (R910).

## What does a calculator add?

- An estimate before you file, from miles and gallons by state, at the final matrix rates with surcharges.
- Trip sheet and receipt checks against the IFTA record elements, and an odometer chain per truck, before an auditor does it.
- A late-cost figure if you are past the due date.
- A PDF and CSV of the return to keep with your records, over the web, the REST API or MCP, with 5 free runs a day on the web without an account.

## When is the portal alone enough?

When you already have clean totals by jurisdiction, for example from an ELD that summarizes miles by state and a fuel card that summarizes gallons, and you trust them. Type them in and pay.

## Please read

- Computed from rates published by IFTA member jurisdictions in the IFTA tax rate matrix, as copied by Oregon DOT and New York State Tax (final rates, sources listed); not affiliated with IFTA, Inc. Your base jurisdiction's return governs.
- This is a calculation, not tax or legal advice; keep your original trip records and receipts for 4 years from the due or filing date, whichever is later.

## Questions

### Can I file IFTA through a calculator?

Not through this one. The return is filed with your base jurisdiction, on its form or electronically where it allows it (R940). The calculator gives you the figures, a PDF and a CSV to copy in.

### Do I need a calculator if my portal works out the tax?

Not to file. A calculator is for checking the figures before you type them in: trip sheets against odometer readings, receipts against the record elements, and the total due.

### Which rates does the calculator use?

The final IFTA tax rate matrix for each quarter, copied from Oregon DOT and checked against New York State Tax's IFTA-105 table, with the sources on each quarter's rate page.
