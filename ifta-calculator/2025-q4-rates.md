# IFTA tax rates, 4th quarter 2025

For the 4th quarter 2025 (October 1, 2025 to December 31, 2025), final IFTA diesel rates run from $0.1900 a gallon in Oklahoma to $0.9710 in California, with diesel surcharges in Kentucky $0.1050 and Virginia $0.1430; the return is due February 2, 2026.

- Highest diesel, California: $0.9710
- Lowest diesel, Oklahoma: $0.1900
- Due: February 2, 2026
- Exchange rate: US $0.7248 / CA $1

## Please read

- Computed from rates published by IFTA member jurisdictions in the IFTA tax rate matrix, as copied by Oregon DOT and New York State Tax (final rates, sources listed); not affiliated with IFTA, Inc. Your base jurisdiction's return governs.
- This is a calculation, not tax or legal advice; keep your original trip records and receipts for 4 years from the due or filing date, whichever is later.
- Record checks compare your records with the IFTA distance and fuel record elements; passing them does not guarantee an audit outcome.
- Rounding follows Oregon DOT Form 9740 (whole miles and gallons, MPG to two decimals); penalty and interest use the general IFTA rules. Your base jurisdiction may compute or round differently.

## Questions

### What is the IFTA diesel surcharge in the 4th quarter 2025?

Kentucky $0.1050 and Virginia $0.1430 a gallon, on taxable gallons with no credit for fuel bought there. Indiana's surcharge row covers only propane.

### When is the 4th quarter 2025 IFTA return due?

February 2, 2026: January 31, 2026 is a weekend, so it moves to the next business day.

### Are Canadian rates in US dollars?

Yes. The matrix prints every rate in US dollars a gallon, converted at US $0.7248 per Canadian dollar for this quarter.
