Zero IFTA returns

Yes: with an active IFTA license you file every quarter, even with 0 miles. IFTA Articles of Agreement R930.100 says returns are required "even if no operations were conducted or no taxable fuel was used", and a zero return filed late can still draw the $50.00 minimum penalty.

Why file a return with no miles?

The license, not the driving, creates the duty to file. IFTA Articles of Agreement R930.100 requires a quarterly return "even if no operations were conducted or no taxable fuel was used during the tax reporting period." Virginia DMV: "you must file a tax return regardless of whether you had any operations during the filing period."

What does a late zero return cost?

The calculator's late-cost step, for a 3rd quarter 2026 return with no tax filed November 20, 2026: Filed November 20, 2026, 1 month late counting each month or part month after October 31, 2026: $0.00 interest on the lines with tax due and a $50.00 penalty (the greater of $50 or 10% of the $0.00 net tax), $50.00 in all.

The penalty floor is $50 under R1220.100; your base jurisdiction may set its own rules on top. More on late IFTA penalties. A missed return also blocks renewal: R345.100 renews a license only when all tax returns have been filed.

Annual filing for low-mileage carriers

R930.200 lets a licensee with less than 5,000 miles or 8,000 kilometers in member jurisdictions other than the base jurisdiction over 12 consecutive months ask to report annually. The annual return is due January 31 after the year (R960.100). The base jurisdiction has to approve it.

Cancelling the license instead

If the truck is off the road for good, ask your base jurisdiction to cancel the license. R410.100 allows it once all motor fuel use tax obligations for the license period are met, and says the license, all copies and all decals are then destroyed. Without IFTA you need a trip permit to run interstate. Renewing for 2027 instead? See renew your IFTA license for 2027.

Steps

  1. Check the license is active. If your base jurisdiction issued an IFTA license for the year and it is not cancelled, a return is due for every quarter.
  2. File with zeros. Enter 0 miles and 0 gallons for the quarter in your base jurisdiction's portal or on its form. Many portals have a no-operations option.
  3. File by the due date. The same due date as any return: the last day of the month after the quarter, moved off weekends and legal holidays.
  4. Decide about the license. If the truck will stay parked, ask your base jurisdiction about annual filing or cancelling the license.

Questions

Do I file IFTA if I did not drive?

Yes, as long as you hold an IFTA license. R930.100: "Tax returns are required even if no operations were conducted or no taxable fuel was used during the tax reporting period." Virginia DMV says the same.

Is there a penalty for a late zero return?

R1220.100 lets the base jurisdiction assess $50 or 10% of delinquent taxes, whichever is greater, for filing a late return. With no tax, that is the $50.00 minimum, and there is no interest because no tax is due.

Can I file once a year instead?

Possibly. R930.200: a licensee whose operations total less than 5,000 miles (8,000 kilometers) in all member jurisdictions other than the base jurisdiction in 12 consecutive months may ask its base jurisdiction for approval to report annually.

How do I stop filing?

Ask your base jurisdiction to cancel the license. R410.100: it may cancel once all fuel tax obligations for the license period are met; the license, its copies and all decals are then destroyed.
