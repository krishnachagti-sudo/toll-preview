The 4th quarter 2026 IFTA return

The 4th quarter 2026 IFTA return (October 1 to December 31, 2026) is due Monday, February 1, 2027: January 31, 2027 is a Sunday, and IFTA Articles of Agreement R960 moves a due date that falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday to the next business day. The return and full payment are both due that day.

Why is the Q4 2026 IFTA return due February 1, not January 31?

January 31, 2027 is a Sunday. IFTA Articles of Agreement R960.100: "If the last day of the month falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, the next business day shall be considered the due date." Virginia DMV prints the same rule for its carriers. The 3rd quarter return worked the same way: October 31, 2026 was a Saturday, so it was due November 2, 2026.

What is different about the 4th quarter of 2026?

Several jurisdictions cut or suspended fuel tax during the quarter. IFTA returns are still required in every case. From the notices posted by IFTA, Inc.:

Read the rest: What is different about the 4th quarter of 2026?
  • Ohio: from 12:01 a.m. October 4, 2026 to 11:59 p.m. January 2, 2027 the motor fuel tax is $0.0001 a gallon (down from 47 cents for diesel and 38.5 cents for gasoline). Ohio says IFTA carriers will see a split rate for the 4th quarter 2026 and the 1st quarter 2027.
  • Georgia: motor fuel excise tax suspended September 29 to November 5, 2026. Its filing guidance says to include the suspension-period miles in total miles but leave them out of taxable miles, and to count the tax-free fuel in total gallons but not in tax-paid gallons.
  • North Carolina: motor fuel excise tax set to zero October 10 to November 30, 2026; returns are still required, with gallons and miles reported.
  • Alberta: fuel tax rates nil from October 1 to December 31, 2026, recorded in the IFTA tax rate matrix; report Alberta travel and fuel as usual.

Details and the dyed diesel relief orders are on IFTA fuel tax holidays in 2026.

Can I work out the Q4 2026 return now?

On November 6, 2026 the calculator's rates run through the 3rd quarter 2026; the 4th quarter 2026 matrix on iftach.org says it is not final until December 4, 2026, and a quarter is added here only once its final matrix is cross-checked.

Meanwhile the method is the same every quarter (how an IFTA return is worked out), and you can check your trip sheets now: the calculator's record check runs on any quarter on file.

What does filing late cost?

The base jurisdiction may assess "a penalty of $50.00 or 10 percent of delinquent taxes, whichever is greater" (R1220.100), and interest on each jurisdiction's tax "for each month or fraction thereof" (R1230.300). For a US-based fleet the yearly rate is the IRS underpayment rate plus 2 points, reset each January 1, so a return due in February 2027 uses the 2027 rate. Worked examples: late IFTA penalty and interest.

And the 2027 license?

IFTA, Inc. says 2027 renewals must be filed with your base jurisdiction before the end of 2026. See renew your IFTA license for 2027.

Steps

  1. Total every mile. Add every mile each qualified vehicle drove from October 1 to December 31, 2026, by jurisdiction, loaded, empty and bobtail, including miles outside IFTA (R266, "Total Distance").
  2. Total every gallon. Add every gallon put in the tanks in the quarter, tax-paid or not, and the tax-paid gallons by jurisdiction from your receipts.
  3. Split the holiday jurisdictions. Ohio has a split rate for the quarter (its holiday started October 4, 2026), and Georgia asks for its suspension-period miles to be left out of taxable miles. Keep those miles apart by date.
  4. Work out the return. Fleet MPG, taxable gallons by jurisdiction, tax-paid credit, rate, surcharges. The calculator does this from totals or from trip sheets and receipts once the quarter's final rates are on file.
  5. File and pay by February 1, 2027. File in your base jurisdiction's portal or on its form, and pay the net tax the same day: R960 makes the return and full payment due together.

Questions

When is the 4th quarter 2026 IFTA return due?

Monday, February 1, 2027. The rule date is January 31, 2027, a Sunday, so it moves to the next business day. Georgia's Department of Revenue lists the same date (2/01/2027).

Is the payment due on February 1 too?

Yes. IFTA Articles of Agreement R960.100: "The tax return and full payment of taxes shall be due on the last day of the month following the close of the reporting period."

Do I file a 4th quarter return if I renewed for 2027 early?

Yes. R630 lets you run on the 2027 credentials up to two months early, but you still file the 4th quarter 2026 report with all operations for that quarter.

What if I file on February 2, 2027?

The base jurisdiction may charge $50 or 10% of the net tax, whichever is greater (R1220), plus a full month of interest on each line with tax due (R1230).
