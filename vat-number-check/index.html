The rules

What an answer means, and what to keep.

Valid, not valid, or could not check: only the first two are answers from the member state, and only they are charged.

What it does not cover

GB numbers (use GOV.UK for now), official certificates, and tax or legal advice.

Please read
  • A check says what VIES or HMRC answered at that moment. VIES treats a valid number as only one of the elements of evidence for not charging VAT on a supply to another member state, and its answer does not in itself give a right to exempt it. Not tax or legal advice.
  • The answers come from national tax administrations. This tool passes them on unchanged and cannot correct them; if a number shows as not valid, the number's owner must take it up with their own tax administration.
  • "Could not check" means the service did not answer. It says nothing about whether the number is valid, and it is not charged.
  • GB numbers are checked with HMRC, not VIES, since January 1, 2021. Northern Ireland numbers (XI) are checked with VIES.
  • The receipt's hash and signature show that it has not changed since this tool issued it. It is not an official certificate; the consultation number, where there is one, is the official reference.
  • Checks run only on your instruction, for your own customers or suppliers. This tool does not keep the answers: save the receipt files yourself.

Valid is one piece of evidence

VIES confirms the issuing state lists the number at the moment you ask. Keep the receipt with the invoice.

VIES

Read the full rule

VIES confirms that the state that issued the number lists it, at the moment you ask. VIES itself says this is only one of the elements of evidence for not charging VAT on a supply to another member state, and does not on its own give a right to exempt it. Keep the receipt with the invoice.

Not valid, and could not check

"Not valid" is the state's answer and is charged. "Could not check" says nothing about the number and is free.

Read the full rule

"Not valid" is an answer from the member state, and it is charged. VIES says it means not valid for cross-border trade within the EU, so a number registered only for domestic VAT can show this way; the customer should take it up with their own tax administration. "Could not check" means VIES or the state did not answer. It says nothing about the number and costs nothing.

The consultation number

VIES returns one only when your own EU or XI VAT number is sent with the check.

Read the full rule

VIES returns one only when your own EU or XI VAT number is sent with the check. It is the official reference for that check. Without it, the receipt still holds VIES's own timestamp and its reply word for word. GB numbers are checked with HMRC, not VIES; that check is not switched on here yet, so use GOV.UK for now.

Prices and sources

A valid or not-valid answer is charged; no answer, a format check and verifying a receipt are free.

Read the prices and sources
  • Check: $0.0025 per valid or invalid answer; you pay only for what is delivered and the rest is refunded at once; no answer, a refused requester number or a blocked number costs nothing.
  • Wrong shape: free; a number with the wrong shape, or one this tool cannot check, is answered without calling VIES or HMRC.
  • Receipt: free; verify a receipt or print its PDF again.
  • Formats and status: free; format checks, the VIES format table, VIES availability and the public signing key.
