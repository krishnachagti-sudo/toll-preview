# Arizona lien waiver forms

Arizona sets out 4 statutory lien waiver forms in A.R.S. 33-1008(D): conditional and unconditional, each for a progress or a final payment. A waiver is unenforceable unless it follows one of them substantially.

- Statutory forms: 4
- Notary: Not on the form
- Source read: November 2, 2026

## The forms

Arizona statutory lien waiver forms

| Form | Statute | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conditional waiver and release on progress payment | A.R.S. 33-1008(D)(1) | With a pay application, or in exchange for a check for a progress payment, before the money has cleared. |
| Unconditional waiver and release on progress payment | A.R.S. 33-1008(D)(2) | After a progress payment has cleared. |
| Conditional waiver and release on final payment | A.R.S. 33-1008(D)(3) | For the last payment, before the money has cleared. |
| Unconditional waiver and release on final payment | A.R.S. 33-1008(D)(4) | After the last payment has cleared. |

## What the statute says

- **A.R.S. 33-1008(D)**: "The waiver and release given by any claimant is unenforceable unless it follows substantially the following forms in the following circumstances"
- **A.R.S. 33-1008(A)**: "Evidence of payment may be by the claimant's endorsement on a single or joint payee check that has been paid by the bank on which it was drawn or by written acknowledgment of payment given by the claimant."
- **A.R.S. 33-1008(D)(2) and (4)**: "(Each unconditional waiver shall contain the following language, in type at least as large as the largest type otherwise on the document:)"
