# Conditional or unconditional lien waiver

Sign the conditional waiver while you are waiting for the money, with the pay application or in exchange for the check, and the unconditional waiver for the same payment once it has cleared. An unconditional waiver states that you have been paid and is enforceable when signed, even if you have not been. In Texas, Property Code 53.283 says no one may require one before you receive payment in good and sufficient funds.

## Before the money clears: conditional

Arizona's, California's and Texas's conditional forms take effect only when the check has been paid by the bank it is drawn on. Until then the waiver gives nothing up, which is why it is the one to hand over with a pay application.

## After it clears: unconditional

Texas Property Code 53.283: "A person may not require a claimant or potential claimant to execute an unconditional waiver and release for a progress payment or final payment amount unless the claimant or potential claimant received payment in that amount in good and sufficient funds."

Arizona and Texas print a notice on their unconditional forms saying the document is enforceable against you if you sign it, even if you have not been paid. California's unconditional forms carry the same notice in capital letters.

## Florida and Wyoming

Florida's two statutory forms and Wyoming's one form have no conditional version: each says the payment has been received. Florida Statutes 713.20(7) lets a lienor who signs for a check condition the waiver on payment of the check; the generator does not add that wording to the statutory form, so it holds these forms until the payment has cleared.

## Questions

### Can a contractor make me sign an unconditional waiver before I am paid?

In Texas, no: "A person may not require a claimant or potential claimant to execute an unconditional waiver and release for a progress payment or final payment amount unless the claimant or potential claimant received payment in that amount in good and sufficient funds." (Property Code 53.283). Elsewhere, an unconditional waiver is effective when signed, so signing one before the money clears gives up the rights even if the payment never arrives.

### When does a conditional waiver take effect?

On payment. The Arizona, California and Texas forms say so in their text, and Arizona adds what counts as evidence of payment: "Evidence of payment may be by the claimant's endorsement on a single or joint payee check that has been paid by the bank on which it was drawn or by written acknowledgment of payment given by the claimant."
