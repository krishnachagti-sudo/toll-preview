Please read

Conditional until the money clears

A conditional waiver takes effect only when the payment is made.

Read the full rule

A conditional waiver takes effect only when the payment is made, so it is the one to hand over with a pay application or in exchange for a check. Once the bank has paid the check, the unconditional waiver for the same payment matches. Texas Property Code 53.283 says no one may require an unconditional waiver before the claimant receives payment in good and sufficient funds.

The statute's words, not ours

Only the blanks are filled in; the wording is checked against a dated copy.

Read the full rule

Arizona, California, Florida, Texas and Wyoming set out the wording of their waivers in statute, and a waiver that does not follow it can fail. Each form here is copied from the official source and checked word for word against a dated copy of that source before every release; only the blanks are filled in.

Texas: the contract date picks the version

A prime contract signed before 2022 still needs a notarized waiver.

Read the full rule

H.B. 2237 (2021) removed the notary requirement for Texas waivers from January 1, 2022, but only for original contracts entered into on or after that date. A job whose owner signed the prime contract earlier still needs a signed and notarized waiver, so the page asks for that date and adds the notary's certificate.
