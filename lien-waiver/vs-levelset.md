# Levelset and Endwise for a lien waiver

Levelset lists free lien waiver templates for all 50 states and offers a waiver workflow product. Endwise fills in the statutory form for 5 states (Arizona, California, Florida, Texas and Wyoming) word for word from the official text read on November 2, 2026, and refuses other states rather than guess.

## When is Levelset the better choice?

- You need a template for a state with no statutory form, which the generator does not make.
- You collect and track waivers across many projects and tiers and want a workflow product.

## When is Endwise the better choice?

- Your project is in Arizona, California, Florida, Texas or Wyoming and you want the statute's exact wording with the source and date.
- You want the tool to stop you from signing an unconditional waiver before the money clears.
- You want to make a waiver from code or an AI assistant: Endwise answers over REST and MCP.

## Questions

### Are Levelset lien waiver forms free?

Its free forms page says: "To download a free lien waiver form, simply find your state, the specific lien waiver form you need, and click download."

### Which is better for a one-off waiver?

In Arizona, California, Florida, Texas or Wyoming, a form filled in with the statute's own words and dated is what the generator makes. In other states the generator makes no form, and a template like Levelset's is an option.
