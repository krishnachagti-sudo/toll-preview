Statutory lien waiver forms by state

Twelve states regulate lien waiver forms by AIA's list: Arizona, California, Florida, Georgia, Massachusetts, Michigan, Mississippi, Missouri, Nevada, Texas, Utah and Wyoming. This generator makes the forms for 5 of them (Arizona, California, Florida, Texas and Wyoming), whose official statute text was read and checked on November 2, 2026; for the others it says which waiver type to sign but makes no form.

Every state

The list of regulated states is AIA's (a secondary source): "Several states regulate lien waiver and Release forms and require that a specific form be used on projects in that state." It is used only to say which states those are; every form made here comes from the state's own official source.

Please read

  • Not legal advice and not a law firm. This fills in the statutory waiver form for the state, payment and dates you enter, in the statute's own words, and names the official source it was copied from.
  • An unconditional waiver takes effect when signed, whether or not you have been paid. Sign it only after the payment has cleared.
  • Each form is current as of the date its source was read, shown with the source. Your contract may require a specific form.
  • Wyoming's form, and a Texas waiver under an original contract signed before January 1, 2022, must be notarized. The document has the notary block; this tool does not notarize.
  • For disputes, large amounts, or public and federal projects, consult a construction attorney licensed in the project's state.
  • Forms are made for Arizona, California, Florida, Texas and Wyoming. Other states are refused rather than guessed at.
