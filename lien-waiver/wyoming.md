# Wyoming lien waiver forms

Wyoming sets out 1 lien waiver form in Wyoming Statutes 29-10-101(b): a waiver for payment received to date that reserves retainage and amounts not yet paid, with a notary's acknowledgment.

- Statutory forms: 1
- Notary: Yes, block on the form
- Source read: November 2, 2026
