# AIA waiver forms and state statutory waivers

AIA sells generic lien waiver forms, G901 to G904, at $59.99 each for one-time use, and its own guide says several states require a specific form. In the 5 statutory states read here, a waiver must follow the statute's form (Arizona, California, Texas) or no one may require a different one (Florida 713.20(6)).

## When is AIA Contract Documents the better choice?

- Your contract calls for AIA documents and the project is in a state without a statutory form.
- You need a contractor's affidavit such as G706A alongside the waivers.

## When is Endwise the better choice?

- The project is in Arizona, California, Florida, Texas or Wyoming, where the statute prescribes the wording.
- You want the form with its source and the date it was read.

## Questions

### What do AIA lien waiver forms cost?

AIA's store lists G901, Lien Waiver Form - Conditional Waiver on Progress Payment, at $59.99 for a one-time use license; G902, G903 and G904 cover the unconditional progress, conditional final and unconditional final waivers.

### Can I use an AIA waiver in a state with a statutory form?

AIA's own page on the types of lien waivers says: "Several states regulate lien waiver and Release forms and require that a specific form be used on projects in that state." Texas Property Code 53.284(a), for example, says a waiver is unenforceable unless it substantially complies with the statutory forms.
