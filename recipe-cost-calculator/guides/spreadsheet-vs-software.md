## When a spreadsheet is enough

- You keep one price list tab and every recipe line looks its price up (no prices typed into recipes).
- You have the time to keep the units straight: cups against pounds needs a weight per cup.
- A few dozen recipes and one location.

## When software earns its fee

- You want invoices read for you so prices update without typing (MarginEdge, meez Premium).
- You count inventory and order from the same system (MarketMan, meez Pro).
- Several locations sharing recipes and prices.

## Where Endwise fits

Between the two: paste the price list and recipes you already have, get every cost, the dishes over target and a workbook with live formulas, and pay per re-cost. It does not read invoices or count stock. [Download the free template](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/recipe-cost-calculator/templates/excel/) if a spreadsheet is all you need.
