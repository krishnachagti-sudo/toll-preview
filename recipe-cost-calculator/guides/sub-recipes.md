# Costing sub-recipes

A quart of lemon butter sauce costs $9.61 ($0.60 for each of 16 portions, $0.30 a fluid ounce); butter's rise from $3.80 to $4.60 a pound moves it from $8.61 and carries into Lemon chicken plate and Pan-seared salmon.

- Batch cost: $9.61
- Per fluid ounce: $0.30
- Per portion: $0.60

## Using it

- By the portion: "Lemon butter sauce, 1, portion". The dish gets one sixteenth of the batch.
- By volume or weight: "Lemon butter sauce, 2, fl oz". The batch must say what it makes (1 qt = 32 fl oz exactly), and the amount is converted.
- Sub-recipes can use other sub-recipes, as deep as you like; a loop (a sauce that uses itself) is refused.

> Costs are only as current as the prices entered and only as true as the yields. A target food cost % is your choice; ranges such as 28% to 35% are rules of thumb, not a standard.

## Steps

1. **Cost the batch.** Cost the sauce like any recipe.
2. **Say what it makes.** Its yield in portions, and in volume or weight (1 qt).
3. **Use it.** In each dish, use it by the portion or by the fluid ounce, cup or gram.
4. **Let it cascade.** When an ingredient of the sauce changes price, every dish that uses the sauce re-costs.
