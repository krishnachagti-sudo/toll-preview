Measure your own yields with a trim test: weigh the product as it arrives, trim and cook it as you serve it, weigh again. Yields vary by supplier, cut and cook, so a book figure is a starting point at best.

## Questions

### How do I calculate yield percentage?

Yield % = edible portion weight / as-purchased weight x 100: 7.68 lb usable from 12 lb is 64%.

### What is edible portion cost?

The as-purchased cost divided by the yield: a turkey at $0.99 a pound with a 36% yield costs $2.75 per edible pound.
