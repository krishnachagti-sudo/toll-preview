# Recipe costing example

Spaghetti Alfredo costs $3.18 a plate: the Alfredo sauce batch costs $12.15 for 12 portions ($1.01 each), plus pasta, parmesan and $1.50 of garlic bread, 26.5% of its $12.00 price.

- Plate cost: $3.18
- Sauce per portion: $1.01
- Food cost: 26.5%

## Step 1: the sauce, a sub-recipe

Alfredo sauce: makes 1.5 qt, 12 portions

| Ingredient | Amount | In pack units | Cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Butter, unsalted | 0.5 lb | 0.5 lb | $2.30 |
| Heavy cream | 1 qt | 1 qt | $5.89 |
| Parmesan, grated | 2 cup | 0.4409 lb | $3.75 |
| Garlic | 1 oz | 0.0625 lb | $0.22 |

The batch costs $12.15; / 12 portions = $1.01 a portion. Parmesan is bought by the pound and measured by the cup: USDA weighs a cup of grated parmesan at 100 g, so 2 cups are 200 g, 0.4409 lb.

## Step 2: the plate

| Line | Amount | Cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Spaghetti | 5 oz | $0.41 |
| Alfredo sauce (sub-recipe) | 1 portion | $1.01 |
| Parmesan, grated | 0.5 oz | $0.27 |
| Garlic bread (fixed cost per portion) | 1 | $1.50 |
| Plate cost |  | $3.18 |

On a $12.00 menu price that is 26.5%. At a 30% target the price would be $10.62.

When cream goes up, the sauce re-costs and so does every dish that uses it: see [menu price calculator](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/recipe-cost-calculator/menu-price-calculator/) and the full [recipe cost calculator](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/recipe-cost-calculator/).

> Costs are only as current as the prices entered and only as true as the yields. A target food cost % is your choice; ranges such as 28% to 35% are rules of thumb, not a standard.
