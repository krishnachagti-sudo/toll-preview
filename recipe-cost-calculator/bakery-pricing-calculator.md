# Bakery pricing calculator

A batch of 24 shortbread cookies has $5.06 of ingredients; with an hour at $18, 15% overhead and $0.12 of packaging each it costs $1.23 a cookie, and a 30% margin prices it at $2.00.

- Ingredients per batch: $5.06
- Cost per cookie: $1.23
- Price per cookie: $2.00
