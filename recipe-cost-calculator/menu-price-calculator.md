# Menu price calculator

Menu price = plate cost / target food cost: a $6.00 plate at a 30% target sells for $20.00 ($20.95 rounded up to .95), $21.43 at 28% and $17.15 at 35%.

- At 30%: $20.00
- Rounded to .95: $20.95
- At 28%: $21.43
- At 35%: $17.15

## Questions

### How do I price a menu item for a 30% food cost?

Divide the plate cost by 0.30: a $6.00 plate sells for $20.00.

### Should I round the price up or down?

Up. Rounding down puts the food cost above your target; this calculator rounds up to the cent, quarter, half dollar, dollar, .95 or .99.
