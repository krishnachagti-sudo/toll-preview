Cups to grams

A cup of all-purpose flour weighs 125.0 g, granulated sugar 200.0 g and butter 227.0 g by USDA's household weights; a cup is 8 US fluid ounces exactly (NIST), 16 tablespoons or 48 teaspoons.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of all-purpose flour?

125.0 g, by USDA's household weight for a cup of all-purpose flour. Scooped flour can weigh more; weigh yours for precision.

How big is a US cup?

NIST Handbook 44 defines the measuring cup as 8 US fluid ounces exactly, about 237 ml (236.5882365 ml).
