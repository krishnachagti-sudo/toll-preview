# A meez alternative for recipe costing

meez's lowest published plan is $19 a month (Starter, billed yearly; $24 billed monthly; read November 8, 2026); Endwise re-costs 15 recipes monthly for $0.15, 40 weekly for $1.60 and never more than $2.99 a month, but does not hold your recipe methods or read invoices.

## meez's published prices

- Starter: $24 a month, or $19 a month billed yearly, with basic recipe costing, 2 devices
- Pro: $119 a month, or $89 billed yearly, adds inventory
- Premium: $199 a month, or $179 billed yearly, adds a costing feed and invoice processing
- A 14-day free trial; no free plan

## When meez is the better choice

- You want a full recipe system for the kitchen: methods, photos, scaling and training, not only costs.
- You want invoice processing feeding live costs (Premium).
- Your team works from shared recipes on several devices.

## When Endwise is the better choice

- You already have your prices (an invoice, a supplier list) and want every dish re-costed now, without a subscription.
- One location, or a caterer, baker or food truck, re-costing a few times a month.
- You want the result as a spreadsheet you keep, or from code.

> meez facts are from its own pricing page as read on November 8, 2026; check it for current prices.

> Costs are only as current as the prices entered and only as true as the yields. A target food cost % is your choice; ranges such as 28% to 35% are rules of thumb, not a standard.
