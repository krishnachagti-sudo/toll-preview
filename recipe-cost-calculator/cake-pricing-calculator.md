## Questions

### How much should I charge for a cake?

Add ingredients, your labor at an hourly rate, overhead and the box, then divide by 1 minus your profit margin. This cake costs $83.74; at a 30% margin it is $7.48 a serving.

### Should I pay myself for my time?

Yes. Labor is a cost: leave it out and the price only covers the ingredients. The hourly rate is yours to set; this page uses $20 as an example, not a typical rate.
