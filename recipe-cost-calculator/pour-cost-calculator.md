## Questions

### How many 1.5 oz pours are in a 750 ml bottle?

16.9: 750 ml is 25.3605 US fluid ounces (1 fl oz = 29.5735295625 ml exactly), before any spill or loss.

### What is the pour cost formula?

Pour cost % = cost of the pour / drink price x 100, where the cost of the pour is the bottle price / pours per bottle.
