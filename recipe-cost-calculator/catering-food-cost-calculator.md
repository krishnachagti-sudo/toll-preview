## Questions

### How do I calculate food cost for catering?

Multiply each item's cost per portion by the portions each guest eats, add them up for the cost per guest, then multiply by the head count. Here that is $3.94 a guest, $472.20 for 120.

### How do I price catering per person?

Divide the food cost per guest by your target food cost, then add costs you pass through at cost, such as disposables: $3.94 / 0.32 + $0.45 = $12.75.
