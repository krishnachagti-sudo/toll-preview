MarginEdge or this recipe cost calculator?

MarginEdge's lowest published plan is $350 a month (per location; read November 8, 2026); Endwise re-costs 15 recipes monthly for $0.15, 40 weekly for $1.60 and never more than $2.99 a month, but does not read invoices or sync your books.

When MarginEdge is the better choice

  • You want every invoice captured and prices updated without typing.
  • You want the restaurant's bookkeeping and daily food cost in one place.

When Endwise is the better choice

  • You already have your prices (an invoice, a supplier list) and want every dish re-costed now, without a subscription.
  • One location, or a caterer, baker or food truck, re-costing a few times a month.
  • You want the result as a spreadsheet you keep, or from code.

MarginEdge facts are from its own pricing page as read on November 8, 2026; check it for current prices.

Costs are only as current as the prices entered and only as true as the yields. A target food cost % is your choice; ranges such as 28% to 35% are rules of thumb, not a standard.
