# Recipe cost calculator for Google Sheets

Import the free workbook into Google Sheets with File, Import: its formulas use only INDEX, MATCH, SUMIFS and IF, so a price changed on the Prices tab re-costs all 24 lines and 6 recipes.

## Get the workbook

[Open the download](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/recipe-cost-calculator/?template=1)

## What carries over

- Every formula, including the price lookups and the per-recipe sums.
- The cached numbers, so the sheet reads right before Sheets recalculates.
- Number formats: dollars, four-place costs and percentages.

The formulas are listed on the [Excel template page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/recipe-cost-calculator/templates/excel/).

> Costs are only as current as the prices entered and only as true as the yields. A target food cost % is your choice; ranges such as 28% to 35% are rules of thumb, not a standard.

## Steps

1. **Download.** Download the free sample workbook from the calculator, or your own for $0.25.
2. **Import.** In Google Sheets choose File, Import, Upload, pick the file and choose "Insert new sheets" or "Replace spreadsheet".
3. **Edit prices.** Change a price on the Prices tab: the Lines and Menu tabs recalculate.
