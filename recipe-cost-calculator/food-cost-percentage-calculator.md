# Food cost percentage calculator

Food cost % = plate cost / menu price x 100: a $4.20 plate on a $14.00 menu price is 30.0%, leaving $9.80 a plate before labor and overhead.

- Food cost: 30.0%
- Left per plate: $9.80
- Price at 30%: $14.00

## Questions

### What is the food cost percentage formula?

Food cost % = ingredient cost of the dish / menu price x 100.

### What is a good food cost percentage?

It is your choice and depends on the concept and your other costs. Figures from 28% to 35% are often quoted as a rule of thumb, not a standard.
