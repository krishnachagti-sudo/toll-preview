# 1099 threshold for 2026 payments

For payments made in 2026, file Form 1099-NEC for nonemployee compensation of $2,000 or more to a payee; royalties stay at $10 and attorney gross proceeds at $600.

- 1099-NEC line: $2,000
- Royalties: $10
- Attorney gross proceeds: $600
- 1099-NEC due: February 1, 2027

## Due dates for 2026 payments

| What | Due |
| --- | --- |
| Form 1099-NEC, to the IRS and the payee | February 1, 2027 |
| Form 1099-MISC, to the payee | February 1, 2027 |
| Form 1099-MISC reporting only box 8 or 10, to the payee | February 16, 2027 |
| Form 1099-MISC to the IRS, on paper | March 1, 2027 |
| Form 1099-MISC to the IRS, electronically | March 31, 2027 |

A due date on a weekend or legal holiday moves to the next business day. You can e-file free through the IRS's [IRIS Taxpayer Portal](https://www.irs.gov/filing/e-file-information-returns-with-iris), up to 100 returns at a time; the tracker makes the upload file.

## From 2027

The $2,000 line is indexed for inflation from calendar year 2027 ([26 USC 6041(h)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6041)): the increase follows the chained consumer price index and is rounded to the nearest $100. The $10, $600 and $5,000 lines are not indexed. Until the IRS publishes the 2027 figure, check 2027 payments in the tracker.

## Questions

### What is the 1099-NEC threshold for 2026?

$2,000 or more of nonemployee compensation paid to a payee in 2026, up from $600, per the IRS instructions (Rev. 12/2026).

### Is the 1099 threshold still $600?

Only for payments made in 2025, and for two 1099-MISC boxes in 2026: gross proceeds paid to an attorney (box 10) and cash paid for fish bought for resale (box 11) stay at $600. Royalties stay at $10.

### When is Form 1099-NEC due for 2026 payments?

February 1, 2027, to the IRS and to the payee, because January 31, 2027 is a Sunday.

### Do I have to e-file my 1099s?

Yes if you file 10 or more information returns in total for the year, counting every type, W-2s included.

### What is the penalty for a late 1099 due in 2027?

$60 per return up to 30 days late, $130 through August 1, $340 after August 1 or not filed, and $690 for intentional disregard. The payee statement is a separate failure with its own penalty.

### Will the $2,000 line change in 2027?

The IRS instructions say the $2,000 may be adjusted for inflation beginning in calendar year 2027. The IRS had not published a 2027 figure when this page was checked.
