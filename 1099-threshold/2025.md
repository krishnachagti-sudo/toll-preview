# 1099 threshold for 2025 payments

For payments made in 2025, file Form 1099-NEC for nonemployee compensation of $600 or more to a payee; royalties are $10 and direct sales $5,000. The forms were due February 2, 2026; a 2025 return still not filed now carries a $340 penalty, and the payee statement another.

- 1099-NEC line: $600
- Royalties: $10
- 1099-NEC was due: February 2, 2026

## Questions

### What is the 1099 threshold for 2025?

$600 or more for nonemployee compensation, rents, other income and medical payments made in 2025; $10 for royalties (IRS instructions, Rev. 4-2025).

### Does the $2,000 line apply to forms filed in 2026?

No. The line follows the year of payment: payments made in 2025 use $600, even though their forms were filed in early 2026. The $2,000 line starts with payments made in 2026.

### What is the penalty for a 2025 Form 1099 filed late?

For returns due in 2026: $60 per return up to 30 days late, $130 through August 1, $340 after August 1 or not filed, and $680 for intentional disregard, with a separate penalty for the payee statement.
