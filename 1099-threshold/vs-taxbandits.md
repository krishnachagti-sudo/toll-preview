# TaxBandits and Endwise compared

Use TaxBandits to e-file and mail 1099s: its pricing page starts at $2.75 a form. Use Endwise first to decide who needs one: the 4 payees in [the sample payment log](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/contractor-payment-log/) come out at 2 forms owed for 2026, from the IRS line.

> TaxBandits facts are from its own site, read on November 6, 2026 (as of November 2026). Plans and prices change, so check its pages before you decide.

## When is TaxBandits the better choice?

- You need to e-file with the IRS and states, mail copies or correct returns.
- You want W-9 requests and TIN matching handled for you.
- You file for many clients and want one place for the filings.

## When is Endwise the better choice?

- You want to know who needs a form before you pay to file each one.
- You want the reason for each payee from a named IRS document, the same answer every time.
- You want to call the check from code or an AI assistant: Endwise answers over REST and MCP at the same price.

## Can I use both?

Yes. Scan your payments in the [1099 threshold tracker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/) to get the list of payees who need a form, then file those with the service you prefer.

## Questions

### How much does TaxBandits charge per 1099?

Its pricing page lists federal 1099 e-filing at $2.75 a form for the first 10 forms, falling with volume ($1.75 for 11 to 100), with state filing through the combined federal/state program free.

### Does Endwise file 1099s?

No. It decides who needs a form and writes the CSV for the IRS IRIS Taxpayer Portal. You upload it yourself or use a filing service such as TaxBandits.
