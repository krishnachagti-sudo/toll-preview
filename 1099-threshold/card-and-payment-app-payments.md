# Card and payment app payments and the 1099

Payments you make by credit card, debit card or through a payment app do not go on Form 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC: the payment processor reports them on Form 1099-K. Only the rest counts toward the $2,000 line, so a contractor paid $5,000 by card and $1,000 by check in 2026 needs no 1099-NEC from you, because $1,000 is under the line.

## Why are card payments left off the 1099-NEC?

The IRS instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC (Rev. 12/2026) say payments made with a credit card or payment card, and certain other payments, must be reported on Form 1099-K by the payment settlement entity under section 6050W, and are not subject to reporting on Form 1099-MISC or 1099-NEC. A third party settlement organization files a 1099-K for a payee when payments pass $20,000 in more than 200 transactions.

## What counts toward the line?

| Paid by | Counts toward your 1099-NEC? |
| --- | --- |
| Check | Yes |
| ACH or bank transfer | Yes |
| Wire | Yes |
| Cash, cashier's check or money order | Yes |
| Other | Yes |
| Credit, debit or gift card | No: reported on Form 1099-K |
| Payment app or online marketplace (paid through it) | No: reported on Form 1099-K |

## What records should I keep?

- Each payment's date, amount and how it was paid, so the card and app payments can be separated at year end.
- The payee's Form W-9, even if you paid only by card: a later check payment may bring them over the line.
- A column for the method in your payment log; see [the contractor payment log](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/contractor-payment-log/).

## Questions

### Do I send a 1099-NEC to a contractor I paid by credit card?

No. The IRS instructions (Rev. 12/2026) say payments made with a credit card or payment card are reported on Form 1099-K by the payment settlement entity and are not subject to reporting on Form 1099-MISC or 1099-NEC.

### What about PayPal, Venmo or another payment app?

When you pay through the app as a payment for goods or services, the same rule applies: the app reports on Form 1099-K, and it files one only when payments to the payee exceed $20,000 in more than 200 transactions.

### Does Zelle count as a payment app?

The IRS instructions do not name Zelle. The tracker treats it as a bank transfer, which counts toward your 1099-NEC like a check or ACH.

### I paid part by card and part by check. What counts?

Only the check, cash, ACH and wire part. Add those up for the year and compare the total with the $2,000 line.
