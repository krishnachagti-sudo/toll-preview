1099 deadline in 2027

Form 1099-NEC for payments made in 2026 is due Monday, February 1, 2027, both to the IRS and to the payee, because January 31, 2027 is a Sunday and a due date on a weekend moves to the next business day. Form 1099-MISC is due to payees the same day and to the IRS by March 1, 2027 on paper or March 31, 2027 electronically.

When is each 1099 due in 2027?

The IRS instructions say that if a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, it moves to the next business day. The days above are worked out with the federal holiday calendar of the business days calculator. The same dates are on the tax deadline calendar, which you can subscribe to.

Is there an extension for Form 1099-NEC?

No automatic one. The IRS General Instructions for Certain Information Returns say no automatic extension is available for Forms W-2 and 1099-NEC, and point to Form 8809, which has to be filed by the form's due date. An extension of time to furnish the payee copies is a separate request, made by fax.

Do I have to e-file?

Yes if you file 10 or more information returns in total for the year, counting every type, W-2s included. The IRS IRIS Taxpayer Portal takes 1099s free, up to 100 returns per upload; the tracker writes the upload file.

Steps

  1. Collect W-9s. Get a Form W-9 from every payee you paid in 2026 who is not on file yet, so you have the legal name, TIN and tax classification.
  2. Total each payee. Add up what you paid each payee in 2026 by check, cash, ACH or wire. Leave out card and payment-app payments: the processor reports those on Form 1099-K.
  3. Compare with the lines. A payee needs Form 1099-NEC once nonemployee compensation reaches $2,000; the 1099-MISC lines differ by box.
  4. Send the payee copies. Furnish Copy B of Form 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC by February 1, 2027.
  5. File with the IRS. File Form 1099-NEC with the IRS by February 1, 2027. With 10 or more information returns in total for the year you must e-file, for example through the IRS IRIS Taxpayer Portal.

Questions

Is the 1099-NEC due January 31 or February 1, 2027?

Monday, February 1, 2027. The law says January 31, but January 31, 2027 is a Sunday, and the IRS instructions move a due date that falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday to the next business day.

When is Form 1099-MISC due in 2027?

To payees by February 1, 2027 (February 16, 2027 when the form reports only box 8 or box 10). To the IRS by March 1, 2027 on paper or March 31, 2027 electronically.

Can I get an extension to file Form 1099-NEC?

Not an automatic one. The IRS General Instructions say no automatic extension is available for Forms W-2 and 1099-NEC and point to Form 8809, which must be filed by the due date.

What if I miss the February 1, 2027 deadline?

The IRS charges $60 per return up to 30 days late, $130 through August 1 and $340 after that, for returns due in 2027. The payee statement is a separate failure with its own penalty.
