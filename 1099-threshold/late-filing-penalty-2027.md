# 1099 late filing penalties for 2027

For 1099s due in 2027, the IRS penalty is $60 per return filed up to 30 days late, $130 through August 1, $340 after August 1 or not filed, and $690 for intentional disregard. Missing the payee copy is a second penalty, so 12 forms filed 20 days late cost $1,440.

The penalty runs per return. Filing with the IRS (section 6721) and furnishing the payee statement (section 6722) are separate failures, so the worked examples below count both. The 1099-NEC was due February 1, 2027.

## How do I keep the penalty low?

- File as soon as you can: the amount per return steps up after 30 days and again after August 1.
- Send the payee copies even if the IRS filing is late; the statement penalty is counted separately.
- Check who actually needs a form first: payments by card, to most corporations or under the line need none. See [which payments need a 1099](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/which-payments-need-a-1099/).

## Questions

### What is the penalty for filing a 1099 late in 2027?

$60 per return up to 30 days late, $130 from 31 days late through August 1, and $340 after August 1 or if never filed, for returns due in 2027.

### Is the penalty for the payee copy separate?

Yes. Filing with the IRS and giving the payee a statement are two failures, each with its own penalty, so a form that is late on both counts costs twice the per-return amount.

### Did the penalties go up for 2027?

The intentional disregard amount did: $680 per return for returns due in 2026 and $690 for returns due in 2027. The other per-return amounts are the same; the annual maximums rose.
