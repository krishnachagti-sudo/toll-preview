California 1099 threshold for 2026

For 2026 payments, California's line for Form 1099-NEC is $2,000, the same as the IRS's, but California puts gross proceeds paid to an attorney and cash paid for fish at $2,000 where the IRS keeps $600, and lists section 409A deferrals at any amount.

Where it matters

A $900 settlement paid to a law firm in 2026: federally, file Form 1099-MISC and report $900.00 in box 10. For the state: California draws a different line here: 1099-MISC box 10 is not reportable to the state at its $2,000 line (federal: reportable). The Franchise Tax Board says to file separately with the IRS and the state when the amounts differ.

Filing with California

  • California lists its own amounts for 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC by tax year. Filers located in California who file paper information returns with the IRS do not need to send the state a paper copy; when a federal and state amount differ, file separately with the IRS and the FTB.
  • Due to the FTB: February 28 on paper, March 31 electronically. Payee copies: January 31, or February 15 for gross proceeds paid to attorneys and substitute payments.
  • California is on the IRS's Combined Federal/State Filing list in Publication 1220 (code 06). That publication is the specification for the FIRE system, written for tax year 2025; FIRE takes its last filing on November 19, 2026, so check whether IRIS forwards 2026 returns to California before relying on it.

Questions

Does California follow the $2,000 1099 threshold?

Yes for Form 1099-NEC and most 1099-MISC boxes for tax year 2026, per the Franchise Tax Board's guide. California differs on gross proceeds paid to an attorney and cash paid for fish ($2,000 in California, $600 federally) and lists section 409A deferrals at any amount.

Do I have to file 1099s with California separately?

The FTB says filers located in California who file paper information returns with the IRS do not need to send the state a paper copy; when a federal and state amount differ, file separately with the IRS and the FTB.

When are 1099s due to California?

February 28 on paper and March 31 electronically, per the FTB guide; payee copies by January 31, or February 15 for gross proceeds paid to attorneys and substitute payments.
