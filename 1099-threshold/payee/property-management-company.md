# Do I need to issue a 1099 to a property management company?

Not for rent you pay through it: rent paid to a real estate agent or property manager is reported by the manager, not the tenant. A property owner who pays the manager $2,000 or more in fees in 2026 files Form 1099-NEC, unless the company is a corporation.

- 2026 line: $2,000
- Cases needing a form: 2 of 4

## The rule

The IRS instructions list rent paid to real estate agents or property managers among the payments a payer does not report; the agent or manager reports the rent it pays over to the property owner on Form 1099-MISC, box 1. Fees the owner pays the manager for its services are nonemployee compensation.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see [1099 threshold for 2025 payments](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/2025/).
