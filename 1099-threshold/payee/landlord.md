# Do I need to send a 1099 to my landlord?

Yes, if your business paid a landlord that is not a corporation $2,000 or more in rent in 2026: file Form 1099-MISC, box 1. Rent paid to a real estate agent or property manager, or to a corporation, needs no form from you.

- 2026 line: $2,000
- Cases needing a form: 2 of 6

## The rule

Rents include office and commercial space, machines and pasture. The landlord's Form W-9 says whether it is a corporation. If you pay through an agent or property manager, the manager reports the rent it pays over to the owner. Rent for your own home is a personal payment and is not reported.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see [1099 threshold for 2025 payments](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/2025/).
