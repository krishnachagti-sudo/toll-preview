Who gets a 1099

Do I have to issue a 1099 to my house cleaner?

Not for cleaning your home: only payments made in the course of a trade or business are reported. A business that pays a cleaner $2,000 or more in 2026 to clean its premises files Form 1099-NEC.

The rule

The IRS instructions say to report on Form 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC only payments made in the course of your trade or business; personal payments are not reportable. Whether someone who works in your home is your employee is a separate question this page does not answer.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see 1099 threshold for 2025 payments.
