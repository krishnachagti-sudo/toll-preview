# Do I need to issue a 1099 to an LLC?

If you paid an LLC $2,000 or more for services in 2026, file Form 1099-NEC unless the LLC is taxed as a C or S corporation; even then, legal fees and medical payments are reported.

- 2026 line: $2,000
- Cases needing a form: 4 of 6

## The rule

An LLC is reported by how it is taxed, which its Form W-9 says: a single-member LLC that is disregarded is reported like its owner, an LLC taxed as a partnership is reported, and an LLC taxed as a C or S corporation is exempt like any corporation, except for legal fees, medical and health care payments and the other payments reportable for corporations.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see [1099 threshold for 2025 payments](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/2025/).
