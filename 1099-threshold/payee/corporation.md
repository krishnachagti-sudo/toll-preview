# Do I need to issue a 1099 to a corporation?

Generally no: payments to a corporation are not reported, but legal fees of $2,000 or more, medical and health care payments of $2,000 or more and attorney gross proceeds of $600 or more paid in 2026 are reported even to a corporation.

- 2026 line: $2,000

## The rule

The corporate exemption covers C and S corporations, and LLCs taxed as either. It does not cover fees for legal services, medical and health care payments, gross proceeds paid to an attorney, substitute payments in lieu of dividends, or cash paid for fish bought for resale. A federal executive agency also reports services paid to corporations.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see [1099 threshold for 2025 payments](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/2025/).
