# Do I need to send a 1099 to my accountant or CPA?

Yes, if you paid an accountant who is not incorporated $2,000 or more in 2026 for work for your business: file Form 1099-NEC, box 1a. A CPA firm taxed as a corporation needs no form.

- 2026 line: $2,000
- Cases needing a form: 2 of 5

## The rule

The IRS instructions list fees to accountants among the professional service fees reported as nonemployee compensation. The corporate exemption applies to accountants (unlike attorneys), so a firm taxed as a C or S corporation gets no form. A personal tax return is a personal payment.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see [1099 threshold for 2025 payments](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/2025/).
