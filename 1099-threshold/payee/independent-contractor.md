# Do I need to issue a 1099 to an independent contractor?

Yes, if you paid the contractor $2,000 or more in 2026 for services in the course of your business, by check, cash or bank transfer: file Form 1099-NEC, box 1a. Under $2,000, or paid by card or a payment app, no form.

- 2026 line: $2,000
- Cases needing a form: 3 of 6

## The rule

The line is per payee for the whole calendar year, not per payment: add up every payment to the contractor in the year. Once the total reaches the line, the whole total is reported. If you withheld backup withholding because the contractor gave no TIN, the form is required whatever the amount.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see [1099 threshold for 2025 payments](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/2025/).
