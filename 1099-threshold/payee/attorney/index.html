Who gets a 1099

Do I need to send a 1099 to my attorney?

Yes, even if the law firm is a corporation: legal fees of $2,000 or more paid in 2026 go on Form 1099-NEC, box 1a, and gross proceeds of $600 or more (such as a settlement paid to the attorney) go on Form 1099-MISC, box 10.

The rule

The term attorney includes a law firm or other provider of legal services. Fees for the attorney's own services are nonemployee compensation; gross proceeds paid to an attorney in connection with legal services but not for the attorney's services, as in a settlement, go in 1099-MISC box 10. Ask for a W-9: an attorney must give its TIN whether or not it is a corporation.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see 1099 threshold for 2025 payments.
