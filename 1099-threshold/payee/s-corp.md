# Do I need to issue a 1099 to an S corporation?

Usually not: an S corporation is exempt like any corporation, so $9,600 of services paid to one in 2026 needs no form, but legal fees of $2,000 or more and medical payments of $2,000 or more are reported.

## The rule

The IRS instructions treat an S corporation, and an LLC that is taxed as one, like any corporation: exempt, except for legal fees, medical and health care payments and the other payments reportable for corporations. The W-9 says how the payee is taxed.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see [1099 threshold for 2025 payments](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/2025/).
