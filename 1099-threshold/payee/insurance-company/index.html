Who gets a 1099

Do I need to send a 1099 to my insurance company?

Usually not: insurance companies are generally corporations, and payments to a corporation are not reported. An independent insurance agent who is not incorporated and is paid $2,000 or more in 2026 gets Form 1099-NEC.

The rule

Payments to a corporation are not reported, whatever they are for, unless they are legal fees, medical payments or another payment reportable for corporations. The IRS instructions list payments to an insurance salesperson who is not your employee as nonemployee compensation. Check the payee's Form W-9.

Payments made in 2025 use the $600 line instead: see 1099 threshold for 2025 payments.
