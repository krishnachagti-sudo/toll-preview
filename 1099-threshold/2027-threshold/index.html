1099 threshold for 2027 payments

The IRS has not published a 1099 threshold for payments made in 2027 yet. The $2,000 line that applies to 2026 payments may rise with inflation from calendar year 2027: the increase follows the chained consumer price index from a 2025 base and is rounded to the nearest $100, so it can only move in steps of $100.

What does the law say about 2027?

26 USC 6041(h) adjusts the $2,000 amount for calendar years after 2025 by the cost-of-living adjustment of section 1(f)(3), with 2025 as the base year, and rounds any increase to the nearest multiple of $100. The IRS instructions for 2026 payments say the threshold "may be adjusted for inflation beginning in calendar year 2027".

Which boxes move with inflation?

  • 1099-NEC box 1a (nonemployee compensation)
  • 1099-MISC box 1 (rents)
  • 1099-MISC box 3 (other income (prizes, awards, damages))
  • 1099-MISC box 6 (medical and health care payments)
  • 1099-MISC box 9 (crop insurance proceeds)
  • 1099-MISC box 12 (section 409a deferrals)
  • 1099-MISC box 15 (nonqualified deferred compensation)

These stay where they are: 1099-NEC box 2 at $5,000; 1099-NEC box 3 at any amount; 1099-MISC box 2 at $10; 1099-MISC box 5 at any amount; 1099-MISC box 7 at $5,000; 1099-MISC box 8 at $10; 1099-MISC box 10 at $600; 1099-MISC box 11 at $600.

When will the IRS publish it?

The IRS had not published the 2027 figure when this page was checked, and it has not said when it will. Until it does, the tracker checks 2027 payments against the statutory $2,000 and marks the answer as provisional; this page will show the IRS figure once it is out.

For forms you file now, nothing changes: 2026 payments use the 2026 line and 2025 payments the 2025 line.

Questions

What is the 1099 threshold for 2027?

Not published by the IRS yet. The law sets $2,000 and lets it rise with inflation from calendar year 2027, rounded to the nearest $100.

Does the $600 attorney line change in 2027?

No. The inflation rule in 26 USC 6041(h) covers the $2,000 amount only. Gross proceeds paid to an attorney stay at $600, royalties at $10 and direct sales at $5,000.

Which line applies to forms filed in early 2027?

The line for the year of payment. Forms filed in early 2027 report 2026 payments, so the $2,000 line applies; they are due February 1, 2027.
