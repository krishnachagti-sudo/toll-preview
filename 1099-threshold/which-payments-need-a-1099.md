# Which payments need a 1099?

A payment needs a 1099 when five things are true: you paid it in the course of your business, the payee is not exempt (most corporations are), it is a reportable kind of payment, you paid by check, cash or bank transfer rather than card, and the year's total reaches the line ($2,000 for services in 2026). Of the 10 cases worked out below, 4 need a form.

## Where do I find the payee type?

On the payee's Form W-9, where the payee ticks its federal tax classification. An LLC is reported by how it is taxed: see [Do I need to issue a 1099 to an LLC?](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/payee/llc/) and [to an S corporation](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/1099-threshold/payee/s-corp/).

## What if I am not sure?

When the payee type is unknown, the tracker treats the payee as not a corporation, so its answer errs toward filing, and says so. A Form W-9 settles it.

## Steps

1. **Was it a business payment?.** Only payments made in the course of your trade or business are reported. A payment for your own home or family is not.
2. **Who was paid?.** Read the payee's Form W-9. Corporations, and LLCs taxed as corporations, are exempt except for legal fees, medical payments, attorney proceeds and a few others. Tax-exempt organizations, governments and foreign persons with a W-8 get no 1099.
3. **What was it for?.** Services go in 1099-NEC box 1a; rent, royalties, prizes, medical payments and attorney proceeds go in their 1099-MISC boxes. Merchandise, freight, storage and telephone are not reported.
4. **How did you pay?.** Card and payment-app payments are left out: the processor reports them on Form 1099-K.
5. **Did the year's total reach the line?.** Add up the year's reportable payments to the payee. For 2026 the line is $2,000 for services and rent, $10 for royalties and $600 for attorney proceeds.

## Questions

### Do I need to send a 1099 for supplies or merchandise?

No. The IRS instructions list payments for merchandise, telegrams, telephone, freight, storage and similar items among the payments that are not reported.

### Do I need a 1099 for a foreign contractor?

Not a 1099. A foreign person who gives you a Form W-8 is reported on Form 1042-S when reporting applies; the tracker does not cover it.

### Is the 1099 line per payment or per year?

Per payee for the whole calendar year. Add up every reportable payment to the payee in the year; once the total reaches the line, the whole total is reported.
