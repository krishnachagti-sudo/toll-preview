1099 threshold for 2026 payments

From 2027

The $2,000 line is indexed for inflation from calendar year 2027 (26 USC 6041(h)): the increase follows the chained consumer price index and is rounded to the nearest $100. The $10, $600 and $5,000 lines are not indexed. Until the IRS publishes the 2027 figure, check 2027 payments in the tracker.

Questions

What is the 1099-NEC threshold for 2026?

$2,000 or more of nonemployee compensation paid to a payee in 2026, up from $600, per the IRS instructions (Rev. 12/2026).

Is the 1099 threshold still $600?

Only for payments made in 2025, and for two 1099-MISC boxes in 2026: gross proceeds paid to an attorney (box 10) and cash paid for fish bought for resale (box 11) stay at $600. Royalties stay at $10.

When is Form 1099-NEC due for 2026 payments?

February 1, 2027, to the IRS and to the payee, because January 31, 2027 is a Sunday.

Do I have to e-file my 1099s?

Yes if you file 10 or more information returns in total for the year, counting every type, W-2s included.

What is the penalty for a late 1099 due in 2027?

$60 per return up to 30 days late, $130 through August 1, $340 after August 1 or not filed, and $690 for intentional disregard. The payee statement is a separate failure with its own penalty.

Will the $2,000 line change in 2027?

The IRS instructions say the $2,000 may be adjusted for inflation beginning in calendar year 2027. The IRS had not published a 2027 figure when this page was checked.
