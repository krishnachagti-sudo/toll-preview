# Adding up payments to one payee

The 1099 line is for the whole year per payee, so add up every payment you made to the payee in 2026, subtract refunds, leave out card payments, and merge names that share one TIN. A designer paid $1,200 and $900 under two names with one TIN, less a $150 refund, comes to $1,950: under the $2,000 line, so no form.

## The payments

Made-up payments in 2026; both payees are partnerships.

| Date | Payee as written | Amount | Paid by |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| February 10, 2026 | Rivera Design | $1,200 | Check |
| June 18, 2026 | Rivera Design LLC | $900 | Check |
| August 3, 2026 | Rivera Design | -$150 | Check |
| April 2, 2026 | Kim Plumbing | $1,300 | Check |
| November 20, 2026 | Kim Plumbing | $650 | Check |

## The totals, worked out

The tracker's scan of the rows above.

| Payee | Rows | Paid | Counted | Verdict |

| Kim Plumbing | 3 | $2,350 | $1,950 | Near the line |

- Nonemployee compensation: $1,950.00 is under the $2,000 line for 1099-NEC box 1a.
- $400.00 left out. Paid by card or through a payment app: the processor reports it on Form 1099-K, so it is left out here.
- Nonemployee compensation: $1,950.00 is under the $2,000 line for 1099-NEC box 1a.

## What are the rules behind the totals?

- One total per payee per calendar year, by the date the payment was made.
- Refunds and credits from the payee reduce the total.
- Rows with the same TIN are one payee, whatever name was typed.
- Card and payment-app payments are left out: they go on Form 1099-K.
- When the total reaches the line, the whole total is reported, not only the part above it.

## Questions

### Is the $2,000 line per invoice or per year?

Per payee for the calendar year. Two invoices of $1,200 and $900 to the same payee in 2026 add up to $2,100, over the $2,000 line.

### Do I report the whole total or only the part over the line?

The whole total. Once the year's total reaches the line, every reportable dollar paid to that payee goes on the form.

### What if a vendor used two names?

Payments to one taxpayer belong together. The tracker merges rows that share a TIN and lists the names it merged so you can check them.
