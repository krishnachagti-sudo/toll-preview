# 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC?

Use Form 1099-NEC for nonemployee compensation: fees, commissions and other pay for services by someone who is not your employee, $2,000 or more in 2026. Use Form 1099-MISC for rents, royalties, prizes, medical payments, attorney gross proceeds and the other boxes it lists, each with its own line. 1099-NEC is due February 1, 2027; 1099-MISC goes to the IRS later, March 1, 2027 on paper.

## Which payments go on neither form?

- **Rent paid to a real estate agent or property manager**: Rent paid to a real estate agent or property manager is not reported by the tenant; the agent or manager reports the rent it pays over to the owner.
- **Merchandise, freight, storage, telephone and similar items**: Payments for merchandise, telegrams, telephone, freight, storage and similar items are not reported on Form 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC.
- **Wages or allowances to an employee**: Wages, bonuses and travel allowances paid to employees go on Form W-2, not a 1099.
- **A personal payment, not for your business**: Only payments made in the course of your trade or business are reported; personal payments are not.

## Questions

### Are attorney fees reported on 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC?

Fees for the attorney's own legal services go on Form 1099-NEC, box 1a ($2,000 or more in 2026). Gross proceeds paid to an attorney, as in a settlement, go on Form 1099-MISC, box 10 ($600 or more). Both apply even to a law firm that is a corporation.

### Is rent reported on 1099-NEC?

No. Rent goes on Form 1099-MISC, box 1, at $2,000 or more for 2026 payments.

### Do 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC have the same due date?

For the payee copy, yes: February 1, 2027. For the IRS, no: 1099-NEC by February 1, 2027, 1099-MISC by March 1, 2027 on paper or March 31, 2027 electronically.
