1099 forms, per payee

Which payees need a 1099

For payments made in 2026, a payee needs a Form 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC at $2,000 in most boxes, up from $600 for 2025. Royalties stay at $10 and attorney gross proceeds at $600. Check one payee for free, or upload a year of payments and get every payee's form, box and reason, the date each crossed the line, and a file to upload to the IRS's free IRIS portal.

A year-end scan costs $0.10 per payee for the year. Checking one payee is free.

Scan a year of payments

Export payments from your books or bank as a CSV (a payee, an amount and a date in each row; a TIN, category, payment method and state if you have them), drop it here, and see who needs a form. The file is read in this page; it goes to the server only when you run the scan, and is not kept.

Which columns it reads, and exports from QuickBooks Online or Xero

A CSV with a heading row, separated by commas (or tabs, for rows pasted from a spreadsheet). It needs a payee column and an amount column; everything else is optional. Headings are matched without case, spaces or punctuation:

  • Payee: Payee, Vendor, Name, Payee name, Vendor name, Contractor, Recipient, Paid to, Supplier, Contact, Display name or Pay to
  • Amount: Amount, Paid, Payment amount, Amount paid, Total, Debit, Spent or Payment
  • Date: Date, Payment date, Paid date, Transaction date, Txn date or Date paid, written 2026-06-14 or 6/14/2026
  • TIN: TIN, EIN, SSN, Tax ID, Taxpayer ID, Federal ID or FEIN
  • What for: Type, Payment type, Category, Account, Expense category, 1099 box or Box. Words such as "Contract labor", "Legal", "Rent" or "Materials" are read.
  • Paid by: Method, Payment method, Paid by, Paid with or Payment account. Words such as "Check", "ACH", "Zelle", "Card", "PayPal" or "Venmo" are read.
  • Payee type, State, Withheld and the payee's address are read too.

From QuickBooks Online, Xero or another ledger, export a report with one row per payment, and if the preview says no payee or amount column was found, pick the right heading in the Columns list that appears after the file is read, or rename it to Payee or Amount. Delete any total or subtotal rows first: a row with a payee and an amount is read as a payment.

The lines, box by box

A payee needs the form once the year's total in a box reaches its line, and then the whole total is reported. Each box is its own line: $1,500 of services and $1,500 of rent to the same payee needs no form.

What is left out, and why

Corporations

Payments to a C or S corporation, including an LLC taxed as one, are not reported, except fees for legal services, medical and health care payments, gross proceeds paid to an attorney, substitute payments and cash paid for fish. Ask for a Form W-9: it says how the payee is taxed.

Cards and payment apps

Payments by credit, debit or gift card, or through a payment app or online marketplace, are reported by the processor on Form 1099-K, not by you. A payee paid partly by card and partly by check is checked on the check and bank payments alone.

Zelle, Venmo, PayPal or a card: whose form?

The IRS instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC say payments made with a credit card or payment card, and third-party network transactions, "must be reported on Form 1099-K by the payment settlement entity" and are not reported on your 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC. So a contractor you paid by card, or through a payment app such as PayPal or Venmo as a payment for goods or services, gets any Form 1099-K from that company, not a 1099-NEC from you.

Checks, cash, ACH and wire transfers are not card or third-party network payments, so they count toward your 1099-NEC; the Form 1099-K instructions say automated clearing houses do not qualify as third party settlement organizations. The IRS instructions do not name Zelle; this tool reads Zelle as a bank transfer, so those payments count toward your 1099-NEC. If a payment service tells you it files Form 1099-K for a payment, mark that payment as a payment app.

The company files Form 1099-K only above its own line: for payment apps and marketplaces, more than $20,000 and more than 200 payments to that payee in the year (Instructions for Form 1099-K). That line does not move the payment back onto your 1099-NEC.

Goods, rent through an agent, employees

Merchandise, freight, storage and telephone are not reported. Rent paid to a real estate agent or property manager is reported by the agent, not the tenant. Employees' wages go on Form W-2. Personal payments are not reported at all.

Backup withholding

If you withheld federal income tax because a payee gave no TIN, the form is required whatever the amount, with the tax in box 4. Without a TIN, reportable payments are subject to backup withholding at 24%.

After the scan: filing through IRIS

IRIS is the IRS's free website for filing 1099s. To use its Taxpayer Portal you first apply for an IRIS Transmitter Control Code (TCC), a 5-digit code that identifies your business. Then you sign in, choose "Upload CSV with Form Data" and upload the IRIS file from the scan, up to 100 returns at a time; the IRS says the portal also lets you enter returns by hand. Open the file and check it before you upload: it is built on the IRS's tax year 2025 CSV layout, so compare it with the current template in the portal. The portal lets you download payee copies, but sending each payee their copy is still your job, and so is any state filing.
