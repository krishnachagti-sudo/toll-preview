# Unincorporated Los Angeles County rent increase cap

A fully covered unit in unincorporated Los Angeles County may rise 1.919% for an increase between July 1, 2026 and June 30, 2027 ($2,038.38 on $2,000 rent), 2.919% for a qualifying small property landlord and 3.919% for a luxury unit. Give at least 30 days' written notice: by January 30, 2027 for a new rent from March 1, 2027.

- On $2,000: $2,038.38
- Cap in force: 1.919%
- Serve notice by: January 30, 2027

## Which units

Most residential rental units in the unincorporated areas (outside the county's 88 cities); state law keeps local caps off buildings with a certificate of occupancy issued after February 1, 1995 and most single-family homes and condos.

DCBA: "Maximum allowable rent increase for most fully-covered rental units is 1.919% through June 30, 2027\*"

DCBA: "Annual rent increases for fully covered rental units subject to LACC Section 8.52.050 will be limited to sixty percent (60%) of the percentage change in the average consumer price index (CPI) over the previous twelve-month period ending in September, not to exceed a maximum rent increase of three percent (3%)."

DCBA: "Landlords must give at least 30 days' written notice before raising rent."

## Notes

- The 30-day figure is the county's; where the increase is more than 10% in 12 months, state law (Civil Code § 827) asks for 90 days, and the tool applies the longer.
- Small property landlords must also file a self-certification with DCBA each year.
