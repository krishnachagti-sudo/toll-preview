Civ. Code § 1947.12 (San Diego Area)

San Diego rent increase cap

A covered unit in San Diego County may rise at most 8.2% for an increase between August 1, 2026 and July 31, 2027: $2,164 on $2,000 rent. The figure is 5% plus the San Diego Area CPI change from March 2025 to March 2026 (3.223%). Notice: 30 days, served by January 30, 2027 for a new rent from March 1, 2027.

Notes

  • The Attorney General's chart of local rent caps lists no ordinance in San Diego County, so the statewide cap for the San Diego Area applies to covered units.
  • BLS publishes the San Diego index every other month (odd months), so the March figure is used, as the Attorney General's page says.
