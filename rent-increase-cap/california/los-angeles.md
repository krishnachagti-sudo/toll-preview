# City of Los Angeles rent increase cap

An RSO unit in the City of Los Angeles may rise 3% for an increase between July 1, 2026 and June 30, 2027 ($2,060 on $2,000 rent); a city unit outside the RSO follows AB 1482 at 8.7% ($2,174). Notice: 30 days for an increase under 10%, served by January 30, 2027 for a new rent from March 1, 2027.

- RSO on $2,000: $2,060
- AB 1482 on $2,000: $2,174
- Serve notice by: January 30, 2027

## Which units

Rental properties first built on or before October 1, 1978 (apartments, duplexes, two or more houses on a lot, ADUs and others; condos and townhomes are not rent-regulated for tenancies begun after December 31, 1995).

LAHD: "THE CALCULATED ANNUAL INCREASE PERCENTAGE EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2026 THROUGH JUNE 30, 2027 IS THREE PERCENT (3%)."

LAHD: "Effective February 2, 2026, the RSO annual rent increase must not include any additional percentage increase for utilities."

LAHD: "Landlords are required to serve tenants with a written 30-day notice for rent increases that are less than 10% of the tenant's rent, or a 90-day notice for rent increases over 10% of the tenant's rent within a 12-month period."

## Notes

- A unit in the city that is not under the RSO follows the statewide cap for the Los Angeles Area.

## Questions

### How much can rent go up in an LA rent-controlled (RSO) unit in 2026-27?

3% for increases from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027, once every 12 months, with no extra percentage for utilities since February 2, 2026 (LAHD).

### What about a newer Los Angeles building?

A building first built after October 1, 1978 is outside the RSO; most such units follow AB 1482, 8.7% in the Los Angeles Area from August 1, 2026.
