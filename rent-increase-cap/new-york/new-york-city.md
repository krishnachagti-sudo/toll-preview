# New York City rent increase limits

A rent-stabilized lease in New York City starting between October 1, 2026 and September 30, 2027 may rise 0% (one-year and two-year, RGB Order #58), so $2,000 stays $2,000. For other units covered by Good Cause Eviction, the local rent standard is 8.38% ($2,167.60 on $2,000); a larger increase must be justified in the notice.

- Stabilized, $2,000: $2,000
- Good Cause standard: 8.38%
- On $2,000: $2,167.60

## Good Cause local rent standard

Under Real Prop. Law § 211(8) the local rent standard is the inflation index (5% plus the annual CPI change for the region) or 10%, whichever is lower; for New York City the CPI is the New York-Newark-Jersey City index.

## Notice

A landlord who will renew a tenancy with a rent increase of 5% or more, or not renew it, must give written notice: 30 days for occupancy under one year with no lease of at least a year; 60 days for occupancy of one to two years or a lease of one to two years; 90 days for occupancy over two years or a lease of at least two years, whichever is longer.

## Notes

- Rent-stabilized leases follow the Rent Guidelines Board order for the date the lease starts. Good Cause Eviction covers many other New York City units; whether a unit is covered (small landlords, units above 245% of fair market rent, newer buildings and others are excluded) is for you to answer.
- An increase above the Good Cause local rent standard is not barred outright: the notice must justify it (Real Prop. Law § 226-c), and the standard is the figure the law measures reasonableness against.
- The 3.38% CPI is recomputed from BLS annual averages (CUURS12ASA0: 345.511 in 2025 over 334.209 in 2024) and matches DHCR.

## Questions

### How much can rent-stabilized rent go up in NYC in 2026-27?

0% for both one-year and two-year leases starting October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027 (RGB Apartment and Loft Order #58, June 25, 2026). Order #57, for leases starting October 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026, allowed 3% and 4.5%.

### What is the Good Cause rent increase limit in NYC?

The local rent standard is 8.38% (5% plus DHCR's 3.38% CPI for the New York-Newark-Jersey City area); an increase above it must be justified in the notice for a covered unit.
