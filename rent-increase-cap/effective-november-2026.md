# Rent increases starting November 1, 2026

For a rent increase starting Sunday, November 1, 2026, the earliest notice deadline is August 3, 2026 (Oregon, 90 days) and the latest October 2, 2026 (California). Caps in force: California 8.7%, Oregon 9.5%, Washington 9.683%, District of Columbia 4.1% and City of Los Angeles 3%.

- Earliest deadline: August 3, 2026

$2,000 rent rising to $2,100 (5%) on November 1, 2026. California: Los Angeles County; New York: a tenancy of two years or more; Virginia: more than four units, lease ending the day before.
