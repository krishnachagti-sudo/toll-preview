# When each rent cap is published

The caps change on fixed dates: California on August 1 (from April CPI), Oregon and Washington on January 1 (published by September 30 and in mid-July), the District of Columbia on May 1 (published in February), New York City stabilized leases on October 1 (RGB vote in June), and Los Angeles, unincorporated LA County and Montgomery County on July 1.

Publication and effective dates

| Cap | Published | Takes effect | Next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| California (AB 1482) | After BLS publishes April CPI; the Attorney General posts the regional chart | August 1 | August 1, 2027 (from April 2027 CPI) |
| Oregon | By September 30 (ORS 90.324); 2027 figure published September 30, 2026 | January 1 | By September 30, 2027, for 2028 |
| Washington | Shortly after BLS releases June CPI in mid-July; 2027 figure on July 15, 2026 | January 1 | Mid-July 2027, for 2028 |
| District of Columbia | February (Rental Housing Commission); 2026 figure on February 2, 2026 | May 1 | February 2027, for May 1, 2027 |
| New York City stabilized | Rent Guidelines Board vote in June; Order #58 dated June 25, 2026 | October 1 | June 2027, for leases from October 1, 2027 |
| New York Good Cause | DHCR notice by August 1; posted July 16, 2026 | On posting | By August 1, 2027 |
| City of Los Angeles RSO | LAHD bulletin | July 1 | For July 1, 2027 |
| Unincorporated LA County | DCBA, from September CPI | July 1 | For July 1, 2027 |
| Montgomery County, MD | DHCA | July 1 | For July 1, 2027 |

Outside a published window the calculator refuses the date rather than guess the next figure, and says which windows it holds.

## Questions

### When is the 2028 Oregon rent cap announced?

By September 30, 2027: ORS 90.324 has the Department of Administrative Services publish the next calendar year's figure by September 30 each year.
