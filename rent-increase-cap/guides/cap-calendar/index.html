When each rent cap is published

The caps change on fixed dates: California on August 1 (from April CPI), Oregon and Washington on January 1 (published by September 30 and in mid-July), the District of Columbia on May 1 (published in February), New York City stabilized leases on October 1 (RGB vote in June), and Los Angeles, unincorporated LA County and Montgomery County on July 1.

  • Not legal advice. We are not a law firm, and this tool does not review your lease, your building or your case.

  • Local ordinances other than the City of Los Angeles, unincorporated Los Angeles County, New York City and Montgomery County, Maryland were not checked.
  • States without a row are not covered: the tool refuses them rather than guess.

Questions

When is the 2028 Oregon rent cap announced?

By September 30, 2027: ORS 90.324 has the Department of Administrative Services publish the next calendar year's figure by September 30 each year.
