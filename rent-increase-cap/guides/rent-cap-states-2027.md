# Which states cap rent increases in 2027

Four of the jurisdictions read cap rent increases statewide in 2027: Oregon at 10% and Washington at 10% for the calendar year, California at 8.1% to 8.8% by CPI region for increases from August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027, and the District of Columbia at 4.1% for rent-controlled units until April 30, 2027.

- Oregon 2027: 10%
- Washington 2027: 10%
- DC (RCY 2026): 4.1%
