# Is my rent increase legal?

A 12% increase on $2,000 in Los Angeles County starting March 1, 2027 is $66 over the AB 1482 cap of 8.7%, which allows at most $2,174, unless the unit is exempt; and an increase over 10% needs 90 days' written notice, by December 1, 2026.

- Over by: $66

## Steps

1. **Find the place.** State, county or city: a local ordinance can set a lower cap.
2. **Work out the percentage.** Compare the new rent with the lowest rent of the past 12 months (California, Washington) or the current rent.
3. **Compare with the cap.** Use the figure for the day the new rent starts.
4. **Check the notice.** Count the days between the written notice and the start date.
5. **Check exemptions.** Ask the landlord which exemption is claimed; Oregon and Washington require the facts in the notice.

## Questions

### My landlord raised the rent 12% in Los Angeles County. Is that legal?

Not for a covered unit: the AB 1482 cap from August 1, 2026 is 8.7% ($2,174 on $2,000), and a city RSO unit or an unincorporated-county RSTPO unit has a lower cap. An exempt unit has none.
