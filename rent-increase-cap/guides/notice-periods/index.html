How much notice to raise the rent

For a new rent from March 1, 2027, a 5% increase on $2,000 needs notice by January 30 in California, December 1 in Oregon, December 1 in Washington and December 30 in the District of Columbia; a 15% increase stretches California to 90 days (December 1, 2026) and Maine to 75.

Delivery

  • California: the notice must be in writing; a call, text or email is not enough (Attorney General). Mailing adds time under Civil Code § 827, whose text could not be read here.
  • Washington: served as RCW 59.12.040 says, in the RCW 59.18.720 form.
  • Montgomery County: by US mail or in person with a signed receipt (DHCA).
  • The dates count calendar days back from the start date, without the notice day; serving a day or two earlier leaves a margin.

Questions

How long does a landlord have to give notice to raise rent?

In the places read: California 30 days (90 over 10%), Oregon and Washington 90 days, DC to the first rent day more than 60 days out, New York 30 to 90 days by tenancy length for 5% or more, Maine 45 days (75 for 10% or more), Delaware and Virginia 60 days before the lease ends, Montgomery County 90 days.
