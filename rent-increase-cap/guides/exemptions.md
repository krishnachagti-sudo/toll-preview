# Is my building exempt from the rent cap?

A building first occupied June 1, 2013, with the notice dated November 15, 2026, is exempt in California (a unit issued a certificate of occupancy within the past 15 years (rolling)), exempt in Oregon (the first certificate of occupancy was issued less than 15 years before the date of the notice) and covered in Washington (the first certificate of occupancy was issued 12 or less years before the date of the notice).

## California

- A unit issued a certificate of occupancy within the past 15 years (rolling).
- A single-family home not owned or controlled by a corporation or real estate investment trust.
- A duplex where the owner lived in one unit when the tenant moved into the other, while the owner still lives there.
- Housing restricted as affordable by deed, government agreement or other recorded document, or under a subsidy agreement.
- A mobilehome, unless owned and offered for rent by the park's owner or manager.
- A dorm.
- A unit under a more protective local rent control ordinance (that ordinance applies instead).

## Oregon

- The first certificate of occupancy was issued less than 15 years before the date of the notice.
- Regulated or certified affordable housing where the change does not raise the tenant's portion or is required by the program.

## Washington

- The first certificate of occupancy was issued 12 or less years before the date of the notice.
- A unit owned by a public housing authority, public development authority or qualifying nonprofit with regulated rents (or a qualified low-income development they own).
- A low-income housing tax credit development with an enforceable regulatory agreement.
- The tenant shares a bathroom or kitchen with the owner, who lives there.
- A single-family owner-occupied residence renting no more than two units or bedrooms (including an ADU).
- A duplex, triplex or fourplex where the owner lived in one unit when the tenancy began and still does.

## District of Columbia

- A unit in a building whose permit was issued after December 31, 1975 (or a unit added with a certificate of occupancy issued after January 1, 1980).
- A unit in a housing accommodation of 4 or fewer rental units owned by natural persons (conditions apply).
- Federally or District owned or subsidized housing, or a low-income housing tax credit unit.
- A unit in a building owned by a cooperative housing association (conditions apply).

## Questions

### Are new buildings exempt from rent caps?

California: a certificate of occupancy within the past 15 years (rolling). Oregon: a first certificate of occupancy less than 15 years before the notice. Washington: 12 or less years before the notice. DC rent control: a building permit issued after December 31, 1975. Montgomery County: a unit under 23 years old.

### Is a single-family home exempt from AB 1482?

Yes, unless it is owned or controlled by a corporation or real estate investment trust (California Attorney General).
