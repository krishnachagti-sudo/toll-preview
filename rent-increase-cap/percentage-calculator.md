# Rent increase percentage calculator

Raising $1,850 to $1,980 is a 7.03% increase ($130 a month, $1,560 a year): within Oregon's 2027 cap of 10%, within Los Angeles County's 8.7%, and over DC's 4.1% for rent-controlled units.

- Increase: 7.03%
- A month: $130
- A year: $1,560

## Steps

1. **Subtract.** New rent minus current rent is the monthly increase.
2. **Divide.** Divide the monthly increase by the current rent (in California and Washington, by the lowest rent in the past 12 months).
3. **Compare.** Compare the percentage with the cap in force on the day the new rent starts.

## Questions

### How do I calculate a rent increase percentage?

(New rent minus current rent) divided by current rent, times 100. $1,850 to $1,980: $130 / $1,850 = 7.03%.

### Is a 10% rent increase legal?

In Oregon and Washington in 2027 the cap is 10%, so up to 10% is within it; in Los Angeles County (AB 1482) the cap from August 1, 2026 is 8.7%, so 10% is over; in DC rent control it is 4.1%.
