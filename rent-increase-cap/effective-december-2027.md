# Rent increases starting December 1, 2027

For a rent increase starting Wednesday, December 1, 2027, the earliest notice deadline is September 1, 2027 (Virginia, 90 days) and the latest November 1, 2027 (California). Caps in force: Oregon 10% and Washington 10%.

- Earliest deadline: September 1, 2027

$2,000 rent rising to $2,100 (5%) on December 1, 2027. California: Los Angeles County; New York: a tenancy of two years or more; Virginia: more than four units, lease ending the day before.
