# Local rent boards and the state cap

Where a local ordinance covers a unit, its figure applies instead of the state cap: on $2,000 from March 1, 2027, a City of Los Angeles RSO unit may rise 3% ($2,060), an unincorporated LA County unit 1.919% ($2,038.38), and a Los Angeles County unit under neither 8.7% ($2,174).

## When to go to the board

Local boards decide what this calculator does not: banked increases, capital improvement pass-throughs, fair-return petitions and whether a particular unit is registered. San Francisco, Oakland, Berkeley and San Jose run their own boards, which this tool does not cover: their sites could not be read here.

## Please read

- Not legal advice. We are not a law firm, and this tool does not review your lease, your building or your case.
- Figures are as published on the dates shown, for the windows shown; an increase taking effect outside a published window is refused, not guessed.
- Exemptions (building age, owner occupancy, local coverage) are applied from your answers; the tool does not check public records.
- Local ordinances other than the City of Los Angeles, unincorporated Los Angeles County, New York City and Montgomery County, Maryland were not checked.
- States without a row are not covered: the tool refuses them rather than guess.
