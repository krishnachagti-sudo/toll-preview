# Washington's rent cap announcement, and what it leaves to you

Washington's Department of Commerce announced a maximum of 10% for increases between January 1 and December 31, 2027 (9.683% for 2026). The calculator applies it: $2,200 on $2,000 from March 1, 2027 is within the cap, with 90 days' notice due December 1, 2026, in the RCW 59.18.720 form.

## What Commerce gives

- The yearly maximum, announced shortly after BLS releases June CPI (July 15, 2026 for 2027).
- A landlord resource center and a housing policy contact.

## What it leaves to you

- The 12-month total against the lowest rent of the period.
- The 12-year new-building exemption and the owner-occupied ones.
- The statutory notice form, filled in.

## When Commerce is better

For the figure and any guidance it issues, Commerce is the source; the calculator's percentages are read from its announcements.

- Not legal advice. We are not a law firm, and this tool does not review your lease, your building or your case.
- Figures are as published on the dates shown, for the windows shown; an increase taking effect outside a published window is refused, not guessed.
- Exemptions (building age, owner occupancy, local coverage) are applied from your answers; the tool does not check public records.
- Local ordinances other than the City of Los Angeles, unincorporated Los Angeles County, New York City and Montgomery County, Maryland were not checked.
- States without a row are not covered: the tool refuses them rather than guess.
