# Oregon's rent stabilization page, and what it leaves to you

Oregon's Office of Economic Analysis publishes one number a year: 10% for 2027 and 9.5% for 2026, with a workbook showing the CPI (3.116% for 2027). The calculator applies it: on $2,000 from March 1, 2027, at most $2,200, with 90 days' notice due December 1, 2026; a notice dated December 15, 2026 is late.

## What the OEA page gives

- The maximum for the coming calendar year (published by September 30) and the year before.
- The ORS 90.324 formula and a workbook with the West Region CPI data.
- A pointer to Oregon Housing and Community Services for questions on administration.

## What it leaves to you

- The 15-year exemption against your notice date.
- The first-year and once-in-12-months rules.
- The 90-day deadline and the notice content of ORS 90.323(3).

## When the OEA page is better

For the percentage and its method it is the source: the calculator's figure is read from it. For manufactured dwelling and marina tenancies (6% in facilities over 30 spaces), use it; this tool does not cover them.
