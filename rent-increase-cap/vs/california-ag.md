# The Attorney General's rent cap page, and what it leaves to you

The Attorney General's page lists the AB 1482 percentage for five regions and two windows (San Francisco Area 8.8% for increases from August 1, 2026); it does not apply it to a rent or a date. The calculator does: $2,176 on $2,000 in Alameda County from March 1, 2027, with notice by January 30, 2027.

## What the AG page gives

- Regional percentages for increases starting August 1, 2025 to July 31, 2026 and August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027 (checked November 8, 2026).
- How the CPI is chosen: April data, March where April is not published, the state CPI for other counties.
- A chart of local rent ordinances with their caps and windows.

## What it leaves to you

- The arithmetic on your rent and the 12-month base.
- Which window your start date falls in.
- The notice period and the last day to serve it.
- A notice to send.

## When the AG page is better

It is the authority this calculator reads from: for the percentage itself, and for the list of local ordinances this tool does not cover (San Francisco, Oakland, Berkeley, San Jose and others), go to the AG chart and the city.

## Please read

- Not legal advice. We are not a law firm, and this tool does not review your lease, your building or your case.
- Figures are as published on the dates shown, for the windows shown; an increase taking effect outside a published window is refused, not guessed.
- Exemptions (building age, owner occupancy, local coverage) are applied from your answers; the tool does not check public records.
- Local ordinances other than the City of Los Angeles, unincorporated Los Angeles County, New York City and Montgomery County, Maryland were not checked.
- States without a row are not covered: the tool refuses them rather than guess.
