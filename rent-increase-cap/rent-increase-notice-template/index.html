Filled in: the Oregon example

  • This is written notice that the rent for 45 Made-Up Avenue, Unit 2, Portland, OR 97202 will increase from $2,000 to $2,190 a month, an increase of $190 (9.5%), starting March 1, 2027.
  • The limit under ORS 90.323 and 90.324 for an increase taking effect on that date is 10% ($2,200 on this rent); this increase is within it.
  • This notice is given 101 days before the increase takes effect; ORS 90.323 requires 90 days.
  • All other terms of your tenancy stay the same.
  • If you have questions about this notice, please contact me in writing.

Questions

How much notice for a rent increase?

It depends on the place: California 30 days, Oregon 90 days, Washington 90 days, District of Columbia 60 days, New York 90 days, Maine 45 days, Delaware 60 days and Virginia 60 days for a 5% increase on $2,000 from March 1, 2027.

Can I copy the wording for free?

Yes. The wording on this page is free to use. The tool fills it in with the cap and the deadline checked and makes a PDF for $2.50 per unit, tenant and effective date; a watermarked preview is free.

Does Washington require a specific form?

Yes. RCW 59.18.720 requires a notice substantially in its statutory form, with the increase, the new total, whether it is below the maximum, the maximum, or under an exemption, and the exemption facts.
