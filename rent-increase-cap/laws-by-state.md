# Rent increase laws by state

4 of the 8 states read cap rent increases statewide: California (8.1% to 8.8% by region from August 1, 2026), Oregon (10% in 2027), Washington (10% in 2027) and the District of Columbia (4.1% for rent-controlled units). Local caps run from 0% (New York City stabilized leases) to 5.2% (Montgomery County).

- States read: 8

## Questions

### Which states cap rent increases?

Of the states whose rules were read: California (5% plus regional CPI, at most 10%), Oregon and Washington (7% plus CPI, at most 10%) and the District of Columbia for rent-controlled units (CPI-W plus 2%). New York has no statewide cap but limits rent-stabilized apartments and sets a Good Cause standard.

### Why are most states missing?

A state is listed only after its rule was read on its official site. Several state sites refused our reader; the calculator refuses those states rather than guess.
