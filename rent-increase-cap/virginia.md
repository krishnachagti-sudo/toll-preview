# Virginia rent increase rules

Virginia sets no percentage cap in the sections read; a landlord with more than four rental units must give written notice of a rent increase at least 60 days before the lease term ends, and 90 days from July 1, 2027 (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(K)). For a lease ending February 28, 2027, serve it by December 30, 2026.

- Serve notice by: December 30, 2026
- Notice: 60 days
- Cap: None in the sections read

## The rule

Va. Code § 55.1-1204(K): "shall provide written notice to any tenant who has the option to renew a rental agreement or whose rental agreement contains an automatic renewal provision of any increase in rent during the subsequent rental agreement term. Such landlord shall also provide written notice of nonrenewal to any tenant. Such notices shall be provided to the tenant no less than 60 days prior to the end of the rental agreement term."

Va. Code § 55.1-1204(K): "The landlord shall provide the tenant with this written notice of rent increase at least 90 days prior to the end of the rental agreement term."

Va. Code § 55.1-1204(K): "Such notice of rent increase shall include a deadline by which the tenant shall advise the landlord of whether the tenant will renew the rental agreement and shall be no sooner than 30 days after the written notice of rent increase is delivered to the tenant."

Va. Code § 55.1-1204(K): "A landlord who owns more than four rental dwelling units or more than a 10 percent interest in more than four rental dwelling units, whether individually or through a business entity, in the Commonwealth"

## Notes

- The rule does not apply to a periodic tenancy created under § 55.1-1253(C), nor to a landlord with four or fewer units, for whom the section sets no notice period.
- The 90-day version takes effect July 1, 2027; the tool uses it when the 60-day deadline falls on or after that day.
- No percentage cap appears in the sections read.

## Questions

### How much notice to raise rent in Virginia?

A landlord with more than four rental units: at least 60 days before the lease term ends, and from July 1, 2027 at least 90 days, with a deadline for the tenant to answer no sooner than 30 days after delivery (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(K)).
