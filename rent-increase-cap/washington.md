# Washington rent increase cap

Washington caps a rent increase at 10% for 2027 (9.683% in 2026), the lesser of 10% or 7% plus CPI (RCW 59.18.700). On $2,000 rent from March 1, 2027 that is $2,200, with 90 days' written notice served by December 1, 2026; no increase in the first 12 months of a tenancy.

- On $2,000: $2,200
- Cap in force: 10%
- Serve notice by: December 1, 2026

## Units outside the cap

- The first certificate of occupancy was issued 12 or less years before the date of the notice.
- A unit owned by a public housing authority, public development authority or qualifying nonprofit with regulated rents (or a qualified low-income development they own).
- A low-income housing tax credit development with an enforceable regulatory agreement.
- The tenant shares a bathroom or kitchen with the owner, who lives there.
- A single-family owner-occupied residence renting no more than two units or bedrooms (including an ADU).
- A duplex, triplex or fourplex where the owner lived in one unit when the tenancy began and still does.

## What the official source says

RCW 59.18.700: "During any 12-month period of the tenancy, in an amount greater than seven percent plus the consumer price index, or 10 percent, whichever is less."

RCW 59.18.700: "During the first 12 months after the tenancy begins; and"

RCW 59.18.700: ""consumer price index" means the June 12-month percent change in the consumer price index for all urban consumers, all items, for the Seattle area as published by the United States bureau of labor statistics."

RCW 59.18.700: "a landlord shall provide a minimum of 90 days' prior written notice of an increase in the amount of rent to each affected tenant, and any increase in the amount of rent may not become effective prior to the completion of the term of the rental agreement."

RCW 59.18.700: "a landlord shall provide a minimum of 30 days' prior written notice of an increase in the amount of rent to each affected tenant."

RCW 59.18.700: "A tenancy in a dwelling unit for which the first certificate of occupancy was issued 12 or less years before the date of the notice of the rent increase."

RCW 59.18.700: "a landlord must provide a tenant with notice of rent increases in a form that is substantially the same as the form provided in subsection (3) of this section."

RCW 59.18.700: "The maximum annual rent increase percentage allowed between January 1, 2027, and December 31, 2027, is 10%."

RCW 59.18.700: "The Department of Commerce announced today that the maximum allowable rent increase for 2026 is 9.683%."

RCW 59.18.700: "Damages in an amount of up to three months of any unlawful rent, fees, or other costs charged by the landlord; and"

## Notes

- Rent here includes every recurring or periodic charge for the use and occupancy of the unit (RCW 59.18.720 form).
- The owner-occupied exemptions do not apply where the owner is a real estate investment trust, a corporation, or an LLC with a corporate member (RCW 59.18.710(2)).
- The Seattle-area CPI change behind each year is recomputed from BLS series CUURS49DSA0 (June to June): 2.683% for 2026 and 4.530% for 2027, so 2027 hits the 10% ceiling.

## Questions

### What is the Washington rent increase limit for 2027?

10% for increases in calendar year 2027; it was 9.683% in 2026.

### How much notice to raise rent in Washington?

At least 90 days' written notice (30 days for a subsidized tenancy whose rent is based on income), effective no earlier than the end of the lease term (RCW 59.18.140), and no increase in the first 12 months (RCW 59.18.700).

### Is a new building exempt in Washington?

Yes, if its first certificate of occupancy was issued 12 or less years before the date of the notice (RCW 59.18.710).
