# Oregon rent increase cap

Oregon caps a rent increase at 10% for 2027 (9.5% in 2026), the lesser of 10% or 7% plus CPI (ORS 90.323 and 90.324). On $2,000 rent from March 1, 2027 that is $2,200, with 90 days' written notice served by December 1, 2026; no increase in the first year of a tenancy.

- On $2,000: $2,200
- Cap in force: 10%
- Serve notice by: December 1, 2026

## Units outside the cap

- The first certificate of occupancy was issued less than 15 years before the date of the notice.
- Regulated or certified affordable housing where the change does not raise the tenant's portion or is required by the program.

## What the official source says

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "If a tenancy is a week-to-week tenancy, the landlord may not increase the rent without giving the tenant written notice at least seven days prior to the effective date of the rent increase."

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "During the first year after the tenancy begins."

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "At any time after the first year of the tenancy without giving the tenant written notice at least 90 days prior to the effective date of the rent increase."

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "More than once in any 12-month period."

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "The notices required under this section must specify: (a) The amount of the rent increase; (b) The amount of the new rent;"

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "The first certificate of occupancy for the dwelling unit was issued less than 15 years from the date of the notice of the rent increase;"

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "is liable to the tenant in an amount equal to three months' rent plus actual damages suffered by the tenant."

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "for tenancies subject to ORS 90.323, as the lesser of: (A) Ten percent; or (B) Seven percent plus CPI."

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "For 2027, the maximum allowable rent increase percentage for tenancies subject to ORS 90.324(b) is 10%."

ORS 90.323 and 90.324: "The allowable rent increase percentage for the previous year , 2026, was 9.5%."

## Notes

- The statute text is the legislature's ORS chapter 90 page as saved by the Internet Archive on September 23, 2026 (the official site did not answer our reader).

- Manufactured dwelling and marina tenancies under ORS 90.600 are not covered by this tool.

## Questions

### What is the Oregon rent increase limit for 2027?

10% for increases in calendar year 2027; it was 9.5% in 2026.

### How much notice to raise rent in Oregon?

At least 90 days' written notice (7 days for a week-to-week tenancy), no increase in the first year and not more than once in 12 months (ORS 90.323).

### Is a new building exempt in Oregon?

Yes, if its first certificate of occupancy was issued less than 15 years before the date of the notice (ORS 90.323(5)).
