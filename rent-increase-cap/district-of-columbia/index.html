D.C. Code § 42-3502.08

The District of Columbia rent increase cap

A rent-controlled unit in the District of Columbia may rise at most 4.1% (CPI-W plus 2%) in Rent Control Year 2026, May 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027, and 2.1% for a registered elderly tenant or tenant with a disability (D.C. Code § 42-3502.08). On $2,000 rent from March 1, 2027 that is $2,082; the notice must be given by December 30, 2026, so the increase starts on the first rent day more than 60 days later.

Notice deadlines for 2027

Units outside the cap

  • A unit in a building whose permit was issued after December 31, 1975 (or a unit added with a certificate of occupancy issued after January 1, 1980).
  • A unit in a housing accommodation of 4 or fewer rental units owned by natural persons (conditions apply).
  • Federally or District owned or subsidized housing, or a low-income housing tax credit unit.
  • A unit in a building owned by a cooperative housing association (conditions apply).

D.C. Code § 42-3502.08: "the maximum standard rent increase will be 4.1% (CPI-W + 2%)."

D.C. Code § 42-3502.08: "If a rent-controlled unit is occupied by an elderly tenant or a tenant with a disability, the maximum standard rent increase for Rent Control Year 2026 will be 2.1% ."

D.C. Code § 42-3502.08: "Any notice of an adjustment under § 42-3502.06 shall contain a statement of the current rent charged, the increased rent charged, and the utilities covered by the rent charged that justify the adjustment or other justification for the rent charged increase."

Notes

  • The elderly or disability figure applies only once the tenant has registered that status with the Rent Administrator.
  • Rent Control Year 2027 (from May 1, 2027) is published by the Rental Housing Commission each February; until then the tool refuses later effective dates for a rent-controlled unit.
  • For a unit outside rent control there is no cap; the 60-day rule of § 42-3509.04(b) is applied to it too, as its words reach "any" rent increase under the Act.

Questions

What is the DC rent control increase for 2026?

4.1% (CPI-W plus 2%) for increases taking effect May 1, 2026 to April 30, 2027; 2.1% for registered elderly or disabled tenants (Office of the Tenant Advocate, February 2, 2026).

How much notice for a DC rent increase?

The increase takes effect only on the first day rent is normally paid that falls more than 60 calendar days after the notice is given (D.C. Code § 42-3509.04(b)).
