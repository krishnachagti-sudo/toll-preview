When does the IRS announce the next year's mileage rate?

Every year in the table below, in December. The IRS does not publish the date ahead of time. These are the news releases behind each year's January 1 rate, with the dates the IRS lists for them.

Announcement dates from irs.gov's monthly news release lists; rates from the IRS standard mileage rates table.

The latest of these came on December 29, 2025. Nothing on irs.gov says what the 2027 rate will be; any figure you see before the announcement is a guess.

What rates apply until the 2027 rate is announced?

Miles driven in 2026 use the 2026 rates, split at July 1. Miles driven from January 1, 2027 use the 2027 rates once announced.

What a December 2026 trip is worth: 120 business miles on December 14, 2026 is $91.20 (120 x $0.76). The full year, both halves and every rate back to 2011, are on the IRS mileage rate 2026 and rate history pages.

What should I do with January 2027 trips?

  1. Keep logging every trip: date, start, business destination, business purpose and miles. Publication 463 asks for records made at or near the time.
  2. Write down the odometer on December 31, 2026 and January 1, 2027: the year's total miles are part of the record.
  3. Price the January trips once the 2027 rate is out. The calculator refuses a date like January 5, 2027 until the IRS rate for it is in its table; it never guesses a rate.

For the record elements, see IRS mileage log requirements; for a ready file, the mileage log template.

Has the rate ever changed mid-year?

Yes, 3 times in the IRS table: July 1, 2011 (55.5 cents, IR-2011-69); July 1, 2022 (62.5 cents, IR-2022-124); July 1, 2026 (76 cents, IR-2026-29). A mid-year change comes with its own rule for which miles and allowances take the new rate; see the paid-date rule for mileage allowances.

Questions

What is the IRS mileage rate for 2027?

Not announced yet (checked November 6, 2026). The latest rate is 76 cents a business mile for July 1 to December 31, 2026.

When will the IRS announce the 2027 mileage rate?

The IRS does not publish a date in advance. In each of the last 6 years the announcement came in December: December 29, 2025, December 19, 2024, December 14, 2023, December 29, 2022, December 17, 2021, December 22, 2020.

What rate do I use for a trip on January 5, 2027?

The 2027 rate, once the IRS announces it: each mile takes the rate for the date it was driven, and the 76 cent rate covers miles through December 31, 2026 only. Log the trip now and price it when the rate is out.

Can the IRS change the mileage rate in the middle of a year?

Yes. The IRS table shows 3 mid-year changes since 2011: July 1, 2011, July 1, 2022 and July 1, 2026.
