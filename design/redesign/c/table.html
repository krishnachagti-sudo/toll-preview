03The rule

How these dates are set

Federal holidays are set by law in 5 U.S.C. 6103. When a holiday falls on a Saturday, most federal employees get the Friday before off (5 U.S.C. 6103(b)); when it falls on a Sunday, they get the Monday after (Executive Order 11582). That includes New Year's Day, so a Saturday January 1 is observed on December 31 of the year before.

Not included: state and local holidays, and one-off closures of federal offices ordered by the President.

Questions

How many business days are there in 2027 in the US?

249: the 261 weekdays of 2027, less 12 holiday days off on a weekday.

Which federal holidays in 2027 fall on a weekend?

Juneteenth National Independence Day (Saturday, day off Friday, June 18, 2027), Independence Day (Sunday, day off Monday, July 5, 2027) and Christmas Day (Saturday, day off Friday, December 24, 2027).

Which month of 2027 has the fewest business days?

January and February, with 19. March has the most, with 23.
