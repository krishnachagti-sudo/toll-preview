A payroll trip sheet or a year's log

Open the spreadsheet your drivers already send, or the trips a mileage app exported. The totals are worked out in your browser and the file does not leave this page. You pay only to download the results file or the log PDF; those trips are sent once, used for that answer and not kept.

Trip file: CSV, TSV or Excel .xlsx. Two columns are required: one headed Date and one headed Miles. Dates may be written 2026-07-14, 7/14/2026 or 7/14/26. Download a blank template.

Two dates, not one

The trip date

The IRS sets its optional standard mileage rates by period. For 2026: 72.5 cents a business mile, 20.5 cents medical or moving and 14 cents charity from January 1 to June 30 (Notice 2026-10, IR-2025-128); 76, 23.5 and 14 cents from July 1 to December 31 (Announcement 2026-11, IR-2026-29).

Each mile takes the rate of the day it was driven, so a trip that runs over July 1 is entered as two legs.

The date an allowance is paid

Announcement 2026-11 applies the new rates to employer mileage allowances only when they are paid on or after July 1, 2026 for miles driven on or after July 1.

A June 28 trip reimbursed July 5 stays at 72.5 cents; so does an advance paid in June for July miles.

Above the federal rate

Pub 463: when an allowance is more than the federal rate, the employer reports the amount up to the federal rate in W-2 box 12 with code L (not taxable) and the excess in box 1 as wages. The trip file shows both per employee.

Does a log I rebuild later count?

The IRS asks for records made at or near the time. Publication 463 treats a log kept weekly as timely. If your records are incomplete, Pub 463 says you must prove each missing element with your own detailed written or oral statement plus other supporting evidence, such as receipts or paid bills.

The PDF shows the trips you entered, priced by date, and its completeness check lists gaps it can see. It cannot make a log rebuilt from memory into a timely record, and it does not say whether the IRS will accept it.

Limits and sources

This tool computes amounts from the trips you enter. It does not decide which trips are business, medical or charity, or whether a log is adequate. Medical and charity amounts are inputs to Schedule A; deduction limits are not applied. Rates for 2027 are added only once the IRS announces them. Not tax or legal advice.
