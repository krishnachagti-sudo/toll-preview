The rules

Which days it skips, and why.

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday everywhere. What moves is a holiday that lands on one.

United States, federal

11 federal holidays. A Saturday holiday is taken on the Friday, a Sunday holiday on the Monday.

OPM: Federal holidaysread 2026-10-30

Read the full rules

New Year's Day, Birthday of Martin Luther King Jr. (from 1986), Washington's Birthday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth (from 2021), Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

A fixed-date holiday on a Saturday is observed on the Friday, and on a Sunday on the Monday. This includes New Year's Day: when 1 January is a Saturday, the holiday is Friday 31 December. Veterans Day fell on the fourth Monday of October from 1971 to 1977.

England and Wales

8 bank holidays plus one-off royal and national days. A weekend holiday moves to the next free weekday.

GOV.UK: UK bank holidaysread 2026-10-30

Read the full rules

New Year's Day (from 1974), Good Friday, Easter Monday, the Early May bank holiday (from 1978), the Spring bank holiday, the Summer bank holiday, Christmas Day and Boxing Day.

A holiday on a weekend moves to the next weekday that is not already a holiday. One-off royal and national days are included, such as the 2022 Platinum Jubilee days, the State Funeral on 19 September 2022 and the Coronation on 8 May 2023.

India, central government (Delhi)

The closed holidays DoPT declares for Delhi offices: 17 in 2026. A weekend holiday is not moved.

DoPT memorandum for 2026read 2026-11-03

Read the full rules

The closed (gazetted) holidays that the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) declares each year for central government administrative offices in Delhi and New Delhi, from its office memorandum for the year, with any later DoPT change of date. For 2026 that is 17 holidays, among them Holi on 4 March, Id-ul-Zuha (Bakrid) on 28 May (moved from 27 May) and Diwali on 8 November.

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday: central government offices have worked a 5-day week since 1985 (DoPT O.M. No. 13/4/85-JCA). A holiday on a weekend is not moved; the memorandum allows no substitute day. Covered years: 2025 to 2027 only, the years whose memorandum has been read. Other years are refused, not guessed.

Your own days off

Add company closures or state holidays. Each is skipped and named in the answer.

Read the full rules

List them in "Your own days off" (or as daysOff over the API), such as a company holiday, a state holiday or a day your office closed. Each one that falls on a weekday in the range is skipped like a public holiday and named in the answer as your day off. A day off that is already one of the holidays above is listed once, as the holiday.

What it does not cover

State and local holidays, Scotland, Northern Ireland, executive-order days off, and India's restricted and state holidays.

Read the full list

State and local holidays (such as Cesar Chavez Day or Patriots' Day), Scotland's and Northern Ireland's bank holidays, and the extra days off a President sometimes gives federal workers by executive order, such as a Christmas Eve.

For India: the restricted (optional) holidays each employee picks two of, the lists of central government offices outside Delhi, any state's holidays, bank holidays (states and banks differ), paid holidays for voting, and one-off office closures such as Delhi's on 11 September 2026 for the BRICS summit. Add any of these that apply to you as your own days off.

Guides

Same count, in your own sheet or code.

Excel: NETWORKDAYS

=NETWORKDAYS(A2, B2, $H$2:$H$24)

Gives 5 for Jul 1 to Jul 8, 2026; this tool, which does not count the start, gives 4.

Read the guide

Excel: WORKDAY

=WORKDAY(A2, 10, $H$2:$H$24)

10 business days after Jul 1, 2026 is Thu, Jul 16, 2026, the same date this tool gives.

Read the guide

Google Sheets: NETWORKDAYS and WORKDAY

=WORKDAY(DATE(2026,7,1), 10, $H$2:$H$24)

Returns Thursday, July 16, 2026, with US holidays pasted into column H.

Read the guide

REST and MCP API

POST /endwise/api/v1/tools/business-days

Returns 4 business days and names Independence Day (observed). $0.0005 a call.

Read the guide

Python (NumPy)

Returns 2026-07-16, the same date this tool gives.
