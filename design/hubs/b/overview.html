Reading the results

Quality never goes below 35%.

So at small limits the pixels go first. Between neighboring limits the tool can trade one for the other.

Why the busy scene shrinks furthestDetail costs bytes

Photos with detail everywhere need more bytes for the same pixels than photos with plain areas, which is why the busy scene shrinks furthest.

How the tool gets to the limit

Keep the size, lower the quality, shrink only if it must.

Three steps, in this order, every time. Open the details for what happens to the file.

Step by stepFull size, search, shrink

It first saves your photo at its full size at JPEG quality 92%. If that already fits, that is the result.

If not, it tries quality levels between 35% and 92% at full size, halving the gap seven times, and keeps the highest quality that fits.

Only when even 35% quality is too big does it make the picture 15% narrower and shorter and search the quality again, step by step, down to a tenth of the width. Past that it says the limit is too small for the photo.

What happens to the fileAlways a JPEG

The result is always a JPEG. See-through areas of a PNG or WebP become white, a sideways phone photo is turned upright, and camera details such as location are not copied into the new file. A JPEG that is already under your limit is handed back as it is, details included.

Photos over 16 megapixels are first drawn at 16 megapixels, because phones and some browsers refuse larger canvases.

Where it runsIn your browser tab

All of it happens inside your browser tab: the page has no code that sends your photo anywhere.

Steps

Four steps, under a minute.

  1. 1

    Choose the photo

    Open Image to size and drop in a JPEG, PNG or WebP. It stays in your browser.

  2. 2

    Set the limit

    Pick a size such as 50 KB, or type the limit the form gives you in KB.

  3. 3

    Let it search

    It keeps the full size and lowers JPEG quality as far as 35%; only then does it make the picture smaller.

  4. 4

    Download

    The result shows its size, pixels and quality next to the original.
