The holidays and the fine print

New Year's Day, Birthday of Martin Luther King Jr. (from 1986), Washington's Birthday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth (from 2021), Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

A fixed-date holiday on a Saturday is observed on the Friday, and on a Sunday on the Monday. This includes New Year's Day: when 1 January is a Saturday, the holiday is Friday 31 December. Veterans Day fell on the fourth Monday of October from 1971 to 1977.

The holidays and the fine print

New Year's Day (from 1974), Good Friday, Easter Monday, the Early May bank holiday (from 1978), the Spring bank holiday, the Summer bank holiday, Christmas Day and Boxing Day.

A holiday on a weekend moves to the next weekday that is not already a holiday. One-off royal and national days are included, such as the 2022 Platinum Jubilee days, the State Funeral on 19 September 2022 and the Coronation on 8 May 2023.

The holidays and the fine print

The closed (gazetted) holidays that the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT) declares each year for central government administrative offices in Delhi and New Delhi, from its office memorandum for the year, with any later DoPT change of date. For 2026 that is 17 holidays, among them Holi on 4 March, Id-ul-Zuha (Bakrid) on 28 May (moved from 27 May) and Diwali on 8 November.

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday: central government offices have worked a 5-day week since 1985 (DoPT O.M. No. 13/4/85-JCA). Covered years: 2025 to 2027 only, the years whose memorandum has been read. Other years are refused, not guessed.

Your own days off

A company holiday, a state holiday or a day your office closed: each is skipped like a public holiday and named in the answer.

In full

List them in "Your own days off" (or as daysOff over the API). Each one that falls on a weekday in the range is skipped like a public holiday and named in the answer as your day off. A day off that is already one of the holidays above is listed once, as the holiday.

What it does not cover

State and local holidays, Scotland and Northern Ireland, executive-order days off, and India's restricted, state and bank holidays. Add any that apply as your own days off.

In full

State and local holidays (such as Cesar Chavez Day or Patriots' Day), Scotland's and Northern Ireland's bank holidays, and the extra days off a President sometimes gives federal workers by executive order, such as a Christmas Eve.

For India: the restricted (optional) holidays each employee picks two of, the lists of central government offices outside Delhi, any state's holidays, bank holidays (states and banks differ), paid holidays for voting, and one-off office closures such as Delhi's on 11 September 2026 for the BRICS summit.
