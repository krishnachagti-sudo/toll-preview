Liquid design: three options

Same homepage, same content. Scroll each one to see the nav shrink. Try them on your phone too.

A. Liquid glassThe nav melts into a floating frosted capsule with a light that follows your cursor; cards and buttons catch the same light; glass bevels and springy reveals. B. Fluid motionThe warm glow becomes a living WebGL gradient that leans toward your cursor; word-by-word headlines, magnetic buttons, momentum scrolling; the nav springs into a compact bar with a progress line. C. Fluid islandThe nav pinches into a small glass island that springs open on hover or tap; tab highlights slide between states; type and spacing flow with screen width.
