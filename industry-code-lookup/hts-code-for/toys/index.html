HTS code for toys

The USITC HTS (2026 HTS Revision 22) has 13 lines whose text contains "toy" for toys, under 5 six-digit HS codes; the first is 9503.00.00, Tricycles, scooters, pedal cars and similar wheeled toys; dollsʼ carriages; dolls, other toys; reduced-scale (“scaleˮ) models and similar recreational models, working or not; puzzles of all kinds; parts and accessories thereof, general duty Free. The right line depends on the material, use and value of your goods.

How to pick the line

  • Start from the heading whose words describe your goods (General Rule of Interpretation 1), then the subheading at each level.
  • Material, use and value decide many lines: check each "Of cotton", "Valued over" or "Other" step.
  • Use the 10-digit statistical number on an entry, and a Schedule B number for an export.

Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. Only U.S. Customs and Border Protection (by a binding ruling) decides an import classification, and the IRS decides what goes on a return. Each answer names the official table it came from and that table's date; check the official source before you file.

Questions

What is the HTS code for toys?

There are 13 lines in the US HTS that contain "toy"; the right one depends on what the goods are made of and how they are used. 9503.00.00.90 is one: Tricycles, scooters, pedal cars and similar wheeled toys; dollsʼ carriages; dolls, other toys; reduced-scale (“scaleˮ) models and similar recreational models, working or not; puzzles of all kinds; parts and accessories thereof: Other.

Who decides the classification?

You are responsible for it on an import entry; for certainty, ask U.S. Customs and Border Protection for a binding ruling.
