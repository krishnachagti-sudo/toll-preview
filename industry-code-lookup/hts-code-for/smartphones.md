# HTS code for smartphones

The USITC HTS (2026 HTS Revision 22) has 18 lines whose text contains "smartphone" for smartphones, under 9 six-digit HS codes; the first is 8517.79.00.00, Telephone sets, including smartphones and other telephones for cellular networks or for other wireless networks; other apparatus for the transmission or reception of voice, images or other data, including apparatus for communication in a wired or wireless network (such as a local or wide area network), other than transmission or reception apparatus of heading 8443, 8525, 8527 or 8528; parts thereof: Parts: Other, general duty Free. The right line depends on the material, use and value of your goods.

- HTS lines: 18

## How to pick the line

- Start from the heading whose words describe your goods (General Rule of Interpretation 1), then the subheading at each level.
- Material, use and value decide many lines: check each "Of cotton", "Valued over" or "Other" step.
- Use the 10-digit statistical number on an entry, and a Schedule B number for an export.

> Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. Only U.S. Customs and Border Protection (by a binding ruling) decides an import classification, and the IRS decides what goes on a return. Each answer names the official table it came from and that table's date; check the official source before you file.

### What is the HTS code for smartphones?
