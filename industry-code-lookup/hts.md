# HTS code lookup

The US Harmonized Tariff Schedule, 2026 HTS Revision 22 (effective 10/30/2026), has 961 headings, 6,858 eight-digit tariff lines that carry duty rates and 19,949 ten-digit statistical numbers; an import entry needs a 10-digit number. USITC published 23 revisions of the 2026 HTS by the end of October.

- Headings: 961
- Tariff lines: 6,858
- Statistical numbers: 19,949

## Questions

### How many digits is an HTS code?

Ten for an import entry: 6 international HS digits, 2 for the US tariff line that carries the duty rate, and 2 statistical digits.

### How often does the HTS change?

Several times a year: USITC lists 33 releases for 2025 and 23 for 2026 so far, besides the basic edition each January.

### Is an HTS number the same as a Schedule B number?

Often, but not always: the HTS is for imports and Schedule B for exports. Both share the first 6 digits.
