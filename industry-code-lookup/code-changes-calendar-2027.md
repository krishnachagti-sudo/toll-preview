# Code changes, November 2026 to December 2027

From November 2026 to December 2027: NAICS 2027 is proposed for data periods from January 1, 2027; USITC's HTS basic edition has come out on the last days of December (December 31, 2025 for 2026) with 23 revisions in 2026; Schedule B changes each January; and HS 2028 follows on January 1, 2028.

| When | What | Detail | Source |

## Questions

### Do HS codes change every year?

The international HS changes about every five years (next: January 1, 2028). The US HTS changes every year with a basic edition and several revisions, and Schedule B changes in January and often July.

### Does NAICS change in 2027?

OMB has proposed it for data periods beginning on or after January 1, 2027; until a final notice, NAICS 2022 is current.
