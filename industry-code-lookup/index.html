What each answer is checked against

Each code is checked against its official table: NAICS 2022, the US HTS, Schedule B, the six-digit HS level, US SIC 1987, the UK SIC list Companies House accepts, or the IRS Schedule C codes. Retired NAICS 2017 codes come with their replacements, and NAICS codes with their SBA size standard and what the next revision proposes.

Industry codes and product codes, table by table

Industry codes

NAICS 2022 from the Census Bureau's files: 1,012 six-digit codes and every level above them, with the Census definition and index entries. A NAICS 2017 code that 2022 replaced is reported as retired, with its 2022 codes. SBA size standards come from SBA's table effective March 17, 2023, and the 2027 status from OMB's proposal of July 13, 2026. US SIC 1987 is reached by chaining the Census concordances. UK SIC 2007 is the condensed list Companies House accepts. Schedule C codes are the IRS list in the 2025 instructions.

Product codes

The US HTS is the USITC's current revision, every line from 4 to 10 digits, with the general duty rate. Schedule B is the Census 2026 export list. The six-digit HS level is read from the HTS, which carries every HS subheading. HS 2028, in force January 1, 2028, is noted where the WCO has named a change; its correlation tables are not published yet.

Prices
  • One code or one search, on this page: free.
  • Code checks (lists, REST and MCP): $0.002 per code checked for the first 50,000 each calendar month, then $0.0005 per code checked; an invalid code is an answer too.
  • Search over REST or MCP: $0.002 per search.

What it does not cover

  • Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business.
  • Only U.S. Customs and Border Protection (by a binding ruling) decides an import classification, and the IRS decides what goes on a return. Each answer names its table and date; check the official source before you file.
  • India's NIC codes are not included. HS 2028 correlation tables are not published yet.
