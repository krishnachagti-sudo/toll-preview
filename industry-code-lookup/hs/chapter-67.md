# HS chapter 67

HS chapter 67, Prepared feathers and down and articles made of feathers or of down; artificial flowers; articles of human hair, has 2 headings and 8 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 13 US import statistical numbers and 8 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 2
- Subheadings: 8
- US import lines: 13
- Schedule B numbers: 8

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 67; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 67?

Prepared feathers and down and articles made of feathers or of down; artificial flowers; articles of human hair: 2 headings and 8 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 67 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
