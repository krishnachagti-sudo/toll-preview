# HS chapter 44

HS chapter 44, Wood and articles of wood; wood charcoal, has 17 headings and 128 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 556 US import statistical numbers and 272 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 17
- Subheadings: 128
- US import lines: 556
- Schedule B numbers: 272

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 44?

Wood and articles of wood; wood charcoal: 17 headings and 128 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 44 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
