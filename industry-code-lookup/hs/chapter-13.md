# HS chapter 13

HS chapter 13, Lac; gums, resins and other vegetable saps and extracts, has 2 headings and 11 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 24 US import statistical numbers and 11 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 2
- Subheadings: 11
- US import lines: 24
- Schedule B numbers: 11

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 13; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 13?

Lac; gums, resins and other vegetable saps and extracts: 2 headings and 11 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 13 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
