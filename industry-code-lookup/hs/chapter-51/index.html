HS chapter 51

HS chapter 51, Wool, fine or coarse animal hair; horsehair yarn and woven fabric, has 10 headings and 38 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 124 US import statistical numbers and 42 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 51; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. Only U.S. Customs and Border Protection (by a binding ruling) decides an import classification, and the IRS decides what goes on a return. Each answer names the official table it came from and that table's date; check the official source before you file.

Questions

What is HS chapter 51?

Wool, fine or coarse animal hair; horsehair yarn and woven fabric: 10 headings and 38 six-digit subheadings.

How many digits does a chapter 51 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
