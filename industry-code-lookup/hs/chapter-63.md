# HS chapter 63

HS chapter 63, Other made up textile articles; sets; worn clothing and worn textile articles; rags, has 8 headings and 52 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 229 US import statistical numbers and 88 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 8
- Subheadings: 52
- US import lines: 229
- Schedule B numbers: 88

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 63; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 63?

Other made up textile articles; sets; worn clothing and worn textile articles; rags: 8 headings and 52 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 63 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
