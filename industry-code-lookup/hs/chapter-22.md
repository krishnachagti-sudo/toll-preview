# HS chapter 22

HS chapter 22, Beverages, spirits and vinegar, has 6 headings and 24 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 121 US import statistical numbers and 48 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 6
- Subheadings: 24
- US import lines: 121
- Schedule B numbers: 48

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 22; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 22?

Beverages, spirits and vinegar: 6 headings and 24 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 22 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
