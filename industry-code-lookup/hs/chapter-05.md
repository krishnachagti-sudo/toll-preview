# HS chapter 05

HS chapter 05, Products of animal origin, not elsewhere specified or included, has 5 headings and 15 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 38 US import statistical numbers and 28 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 5
- Subheadings: 15
- US import lines: 38
- Schedule B numbers: 28

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 05; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 05?

Products of animal origin, not elsewhere specified or included: 5 headings and 15 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 05 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
