# HS chapter 04

HS chapter 04, Dairy produce; birds eggs; natural honey; edible products of animal origin, not elsewhere specified or included, has 9 headings and 33 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 272 US import statistical numbers and 40 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 9
- Subheadings: 33
- US import lines: 272
- Schedule B numbers: 40
