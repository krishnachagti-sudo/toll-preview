# HS chapter 12

HS chapter 12, Oil seeds and oleaginous fruits; miscellaneous grains, seeds and fruits; industrial or medicinal plants; straw and fodder, has 10 headings and 50 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 167 US import statistical numbers and 121 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 10
- Subheadings: 50
- US import lines: 167
- Schedule B numbers: 121

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 12; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

> Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. ## Questions

### What is HS chapter 12?

Oil seeds and oleaginous fruits; miscellaneous grains, seeds and fruits; industrial or medicinal plants; straw and fodder: 10 headings and 50 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 12 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
