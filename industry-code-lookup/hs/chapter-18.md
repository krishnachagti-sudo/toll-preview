# HS chapter 18

HS chapter 18, Cocoa and cocoa preparations, has 2 headings and 11 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 91 US import statistical numbers and 17 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 2
- Subheadings: 11
- US import lines: 91
- Schedule B numbers: 17

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 18; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 18?

Cocoa and cocoa preparations: 2 headings and 11 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 18 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
