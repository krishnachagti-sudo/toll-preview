# HS chapter 42

HS chapter 42, Articles of leather; saddlery and harness; travel goods, handbags and similar containers; articles of animal gut (other than silkworm gut), has 2 headings and 20 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 121 US import statistical numbers and 27 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 2
- Subheadings: 20
- US import lines: 121
- Schedule B numbers: 27

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 42?

Articles of leather; saddlery and harness; travel goods, handbags and similar containers; articles of animal gut (other than silkworm gut): 2 headings and 20 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 42 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
