# HS chapter 32

HS chapter 32, Tanning or dyeing extracts; dyes, pigments, paints, varnishes, putty and mastics, has 11 headings and 45 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 168 US import statistical numbers and 67 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 11
- Subheadings: 45
- US import lines: 168
- Schedule B numbers: 67

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 32?

Tanning or dyeing extracts; dyes, pigments, paints, varnishes, putty and mastics: 11 headings and 45 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 32 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
