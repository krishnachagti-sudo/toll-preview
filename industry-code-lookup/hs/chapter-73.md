# HS chapter 73

HS chapter 73, Articles of iron or steel, has 20 headings and 124 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 573 US import statistical numbers and 203 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 20
- Subheadings: 124
- US import lines: 573
- Schedule B numbers: 203

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 73?

Articles of iron or steel: 20 headings and 124 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 73 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
