# HS chapter 20

HS chapter 20, Preparations of vegetables, fruit, nuts or other parts of plants, has 8 headings and 52 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 285 US import statistical numbers and 96 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 8
- Subheadings: 52
- US import lines: 285
- Schedule B numbers: 96

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 20; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 20?

Preparations of vegetables, fruit, nuts or other parts of plants: 8 headings and 52 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 20 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
