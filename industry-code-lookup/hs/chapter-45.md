# HS chapter 45

HS chapter 45, Cork and articles of cork, has 3 headings and 7 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 19 US import statistical numbers and 7 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 3
- Subheadings: 7
- US import lines: 19
- Schedule B numbers: 7

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 45; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

> Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. ## Questions

### What is HS chapter 45?

Cork and articles of cork: 3 headings and 7 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 45 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
