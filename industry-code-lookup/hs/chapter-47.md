# HS chapter 47

HS chapter 47, Pulp of wood or of other fibrous cellulosic material; waste and scrap of paper or paperboard, has 4 headings and 21 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 26 US import statistical numbers and 25 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 4
- Subheadings: 21
- US import lines: 26
- Schedule B numbers: 25

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 47; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

> Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. Only U.S. Customs and Border Protection (by a binding ruling) decides an import classification, and the IRS decides what goes on a return. Each answer names the official table it came from and that table's date; check the official source before you file.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 47?

Pulp of wood or of other fibrous cellulosic material; waste and scrap of paper or paperboard: 4 headings and 21 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 47 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
