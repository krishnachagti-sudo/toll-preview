# HS chapter 31

HS chapter 31, Fertilizers, has 4 headings and 24 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 29 US import statistical numbers and 24 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 4
- Subheadings: 24
- US import lines: 29
- Schedule B numbers: 24

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 31?

Fertilizers: 4 headings and 24 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 31 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
