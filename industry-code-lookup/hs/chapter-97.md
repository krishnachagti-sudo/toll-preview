# HS chapter 97

HS chapter 97, Works of art, collectors' pieces and antiques, has 5 headings and 19 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 30 US import statistical numbers and 21 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 5
- Subheadings: 19
- US import lines: 30
- Schedule B numbers: 21

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 97; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 97?

Works of art, collectors' pieces and antiques: 5 headings and 19 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 97 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
