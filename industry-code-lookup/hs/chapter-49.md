# HS chapter 49

HS chapter 49, Printed books, newspapers, pictures and other products of the printing industry; manuscripts, typescripts and plans, has 5 headings and 18 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 50 US import statistical numbers and 36 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 5
- Subheadings: 18
- US import lines: 50
- Schedule B numbers: 36

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 49; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

> Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. ## Questions

### What is HS chapter 49?

Printed books, newspapers, pictures and other products of the printing industry; manuscripts, typescripts and plans: 5 headings and 18 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 49 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
