# HS chapter 01

HS chapter 01, Live animals, has 6 headings and 34 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 78 US import statistical numbers and 39 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 6
- Subheadings: 34
- US import lines: 78
- Schedule B numbers: 39

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 01; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

> Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. ## Questions

### What is HS chapter 01?

Live animals: 6 headings and 34 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 01 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
