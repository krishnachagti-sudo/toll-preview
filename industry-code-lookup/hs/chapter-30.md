# HS chapter 30

HS chapter 30, Pharmaceutical products, has 6 headings and 45 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 198 US import statistical numbers and 117 Schedule B export numbers. HS 2028 changes named by the WCO: Vaccines move out of heading 30.02 into new headings 30.07 (human medicine) and 30.08 (other, including veterinary). New heading 30.07 for vaccines for human medicine, with disease-based subheadings. New heading 30.08 for other vaccines, including veterinary vaccines.

- Headings: 6
- Subheadings: 45
- US import lines: 198
- Schedule B numbers: 117

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 30?

Pharmaceutical products: 6 headings and 45 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 30 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
