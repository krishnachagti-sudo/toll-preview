# HS chapter 19

HS chapter 19, Preparations of cereals, flour, starch or milk; bakers' wares, has 4 headings and 19 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 111 US import statistical numbers and 41 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 4
- Subheadings: 19
- US import lines: 111
- Schedule B numbers: 41

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 19?

Preparations of cereals, flour, starch or milk; bakers' wares: 4 headings and 19 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 19 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
