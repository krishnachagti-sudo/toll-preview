# HS chapter 58

HS chapter 58, Special woven fabrics; tufted textile fabrics; lace, tapestries; trimmings; embroidery, has 7 headings and 37 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 111 US import statistical numbers and 49 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 7
- Subheadings: 37
- US import lines: 111
- Schedule B numbers: 49

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 58?

Special woven fabrics; tufted textile fabrics; lace, tapestries; trimmings; embroidery: 7 headings and 37 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 58 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
