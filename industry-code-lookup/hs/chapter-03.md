# HS chapter 03

HS chapter 03, Fish and crustaceans, molluscs and other aquatic invertebrates, has 9 headings and 225 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 562 US import statistical numbers and 314 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 9
- Subheadings: 225
- US import lines: 562
- Schedule B numbers: 314

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 03?

Fish and crustaceans, molluscs and other aquatic invertebrates: 9 headings and 225 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 03 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
