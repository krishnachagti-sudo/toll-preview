# HS chapter 17

HS chapter 17, Sugars and sugar confectionery, has 4 headings and 17 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 92 US import statistical numbers and 26 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 4
- Subheadings: 17
- US import lines: 92
- Schedule B numbers: 26

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 17; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 17?

Sugars and sugar confectionery: 4 headings and 17 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 17 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
