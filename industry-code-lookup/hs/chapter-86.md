# HS chapter 86

HS chapter 86, Railway or tramway locomotives, rolling-stock and parts thereof; railway or tramway track fixtures and fittings and parts thereof; mechanical (including electro-mechanical) traffic signalling equipment of all kinds, has 5 headings and 23 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 37 US import statistical numbers and 32 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 5
- Subheadings: 23
- US import lines: 37
- Schedule B numbers: 32

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 86; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 86?

Railway or tramway locomotives, rolling-stock and parts thereof; railway or tramway track fixtures and fittings and parts thereof; mechanical (including electro-mechanical) traffic signalling equipment of all kinds: 5 headings and 23 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 86 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
