# HS chapter 21

HS chapter 21, Miscellaneous edible preparations, has 5 headings and 16 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 118 US import statistical numbers and 48 Schedule B export numbers. HS 2028 changes named by the WCO: New heading 21.07 for dietary supplements, with new legal Notes.

- Headings: 5
- Subheadings: 16
- US import lines: 118
- Schedule B numbers: 48

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 21?

Miscellaneous edible preparations: 5 headings and 16 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 21 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
