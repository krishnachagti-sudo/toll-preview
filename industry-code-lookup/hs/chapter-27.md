# HS chapter 27

HS chapter 27, Mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation; bituminous substances; mineral waxes, has 9 headings and 43 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 141 US import statistical numbers and 102 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 9
- Subheadings: 43
- US import lines: 141
- Schedule B numbers: 102

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 27?

Mineral fuels, mineral oils and products of their distillation; bituminous substances; mineral waxes: 9 headings and 43 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 27 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
