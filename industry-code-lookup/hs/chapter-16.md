# HS chapter 16

HS chapter 16, Preparations of meat, of fish or of crustaceans, molluscs or other aquatic invertebrates, has 3 headings and 42 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 179 US import statistical numbers and 90 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 3
- Subheadings: 42
- US import lines: 179
- Schedule B numbers: 90

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 16?

Preparations of meat, of fish or of crustaceans, molluscs or other aquatic invertebrates: 3 headings and 42 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 16 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
