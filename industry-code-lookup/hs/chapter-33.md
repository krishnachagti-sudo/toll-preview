# HS chapter 33

HS chapter 33, Essential oils and resinoids; perfumery, cosmetic or toilet preparations, has 6 headings and 29 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 74 US import statistical numbers and 41 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 6
- Subheadings: 29
- US import lines: 74
- Schedule B numbers: 41

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 33?

Essential oils and resinoids; perfumery, cosmetic or toilet preparations: 6 headings and 29 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 33 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
