# HS chapter 23

HS chapter 23, Residues and waste from the food industries; prepared animal feed, has 5 headings and 23 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 61 US import statistical numbers and 36 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 5
- Subheadings: 23
- US import lines: 61
- Schedule B numbers: 36

## HS 2028

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 23?

Residues and waste from the food industries; prepared animal feed: 5 headings and 23 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 23 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
