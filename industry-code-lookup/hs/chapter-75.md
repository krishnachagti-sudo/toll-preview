# HS chapter 75

HS chapter 75, Nickel and articles thereof, has 6 headings and 17 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 29 US import statistical numbers and 20 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 6
- Subheadings: 17
- US import lines: 29
- Schedule B numbers: 20

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 75; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

> Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. ## Questions

### What is HS chapter 75?

Nickel and articles thereof: 6 headings and 17 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 75 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
