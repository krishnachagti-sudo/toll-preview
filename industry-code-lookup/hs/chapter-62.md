# HS chapter 62

HS chapter 62, Articles of apparel and clothing accessories, not knitted or crocheted, has 16 headings and 104 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 1,247 US import statistical numbers and 175 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 16
- Subheadings: 104
- US import lines: 1,247
- Schedule B numbers: 175

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 62; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

### What is HS chapter 62?

Articles of apparel and clothing accessories, not knitted or crocheted: 16 headings and 104 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 62 code need?
