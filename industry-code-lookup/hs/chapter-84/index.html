HS chapter 84

HS chapter 84, Nuclear reactors, boilers, machinery and mechanical appliances; parts thereof, has 84 headings and 538 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 1,706 US import statistical numbers and 1,331 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 84; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

Questions

What is HS chapter 84?

Nuclear reactors, boilers, machinery and mechanical appliances; parts thereof: 84 headings and 538 six-digit subheadings.

How many digits does a chapter 84 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
