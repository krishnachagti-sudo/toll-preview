# HS chapter 53

HS chapter 53, Other vegetable textile fibers; paper yarn and woven fabric of paper yarn, has 8 headings and 23 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 62 US import statistical numbers and 24 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 8
- Subheadings: 23
- US import lines: 62
- Schedule B numbers: 24

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 53; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 53?

Other vegetable textile fibers; paper yarn and woven fabric of paper yarn: 8 headings and 23 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 53 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
