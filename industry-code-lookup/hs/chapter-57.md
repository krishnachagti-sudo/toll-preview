# HS chapter 57

HS chapter 57, Carpets and other textile floor coverings, has 4 headings and 24 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 74 US import statistical numbers and 26 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 4
- Subheadings: 24
- US import lines: 74
- Schedule B numbers: 26

## HS 2028

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 57?

Carpets and other textile floor coverings: 4 headings and 24 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 57 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
