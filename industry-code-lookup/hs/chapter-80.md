# HS chapter 80

HS chapter 80, Tin and articles thereof, has 1 headings and 5 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 13 US import statistical numbers and 9 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 1
- Subheadings: 5
- US import lines: 13
- Schedule B numbers: 9

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 80; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

> Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. ## Questions

### What is HS chapter 80?

Tin and articles thereof: 1 headings and 5 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 80 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
