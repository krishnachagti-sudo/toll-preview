# HS chapter 66

HS chapter 66, Umbrellas, sun umbrellas, walking sticks, seatsticks, whips, riding-crops and parts thereof, has 2 headings and 6 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 8 US import statistical numbers and 7 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 2
- Subheadings: 6
- US import lines: 8
- Schedule B numbers: 7

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 66; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 66?

Umbrellas, sun umbrellas, walking sticks, seatsticks, whips, riding-crops and parts thereof: 2 headings and 6 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 66 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
