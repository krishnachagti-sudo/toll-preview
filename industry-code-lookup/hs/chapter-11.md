# HS chapter 11

HS chapter 11, Products of the milling industry; malt; starches; inulin; wheat gluten, has 7 headings and 27 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 51 US import statistical numbers and 36 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 7
- Subheadings: 27
- US import lines: 51
- Schedule B numbers: 36

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 11; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 11?

Products of the milling industry; malt; starches; inulin; wheat gluten: 7 headings and 27 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 11 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
