# HS chapter 88

HS chapter 88, Aircraft, spacecraft, and parts thereof, has 4 headings and 26 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 67 US import statistical numbers and 63 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 4
- Subheadings: 26
- US import lines: 67
- Schedule B numbers: 63

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 88; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

> Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. Only U.S. Customs and Border Protection (by a binding ruling) decides an import classification, and the IRS decides what goes on a return. Each answer names the official table it came from and that table's date; check the official source before you file.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 88?

Aircraft, spacecraft, and parts thereof: 4 headings and 26 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 88 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
