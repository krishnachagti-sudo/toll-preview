# HS chapter 08

HS chapter 08, Edible fruit and nuts; peel of citrus fruit or melons, has 13 headings and 70 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 240 US import statistical numbers and 105 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 13
- Subheadings: 70
- US import lines: 240
- Schedule B numbers: 105

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 08; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 08?

Edible fruit and nuts; peel of citrus fruit or melons: 13 headings and 70 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 08 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
