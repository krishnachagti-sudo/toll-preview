# HS chapter 36

HS chapter 36, Explosives; pyrotechnic products; matches; pyrophoric alloys; certain combustible preparations, has 3 headings and 13 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 19 US import statistical numbers and 15 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 3
- Subheadings: 13
- US import lines: 19
- Schedule B numbers: 15

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 36; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 36?

Explosives; pyrotechnic products; matches; pyrophoric alloys; certain combustible preparations: 3 headings and 13 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 36 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
