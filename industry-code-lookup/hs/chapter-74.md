# HS chapter 74

HS chapter 74, Copper and articles thereof, has 11 headings and 49 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 136 US import statistical numbers and 80 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 11
- Subheadings: 49
- US import lines: 136
- Schedule B numbers: 80

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 74?

Copper and articles thereof: 11 headings and 49 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 74 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
