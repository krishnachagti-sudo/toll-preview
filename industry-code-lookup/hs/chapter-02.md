# HS chapter 02

HS chapter 02, Meat and edible meat offal, has 9 headings and 66 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 166 US import statistical numbers and 102 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 9
- Subheadings: 66
- US import lines: 166
- Schedule B numbers: 102

## HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 02; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 02?

Meat and edible meat offal: 9 headings and 66 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 02 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
