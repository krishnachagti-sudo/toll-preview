# HS chapter 59

HS chapter 59, Impregnated, coated, covered or laminated textile fabrics; textile articles of a kind suitable for industrial use, has 6 headings and 24 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 75 US import statistical numbers and 38 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 6
- Subheadings: 24
- US import lines: 75
- Schedule B numbers: 38

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 59?

Impregnated, coated, covered or laminated textile fabrics; textile articles of a kind suitable for industrial use: 6 headings and 24 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 59 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
