HS chapter 90

HS chapter 90, Optical, photographic, cinematographic, measuring, checking, precision, medical or surgical instruments and apparatus; parts and accessories thereof, has 28 headings and 143 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 421 US import statistical numbers and 263 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

Questions

What is HS chapter 90?

Optical, photographic, cinematographic, measuring, checking, precision, medical or surgical instruments and apparatus; parts and accessories thereof: 28 headings and 143 six-digit subheadings.

How many digits does a chapter 90 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
