HS chapter 76

HS chapter 76, Aluminum and articles thereof, has 12 headings and 35 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 124 US import statistical numbers and 87 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

HS 2028

HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. The WCO's announcement names no change in chapter 76; which codes change will be known when it publishes the correlation tables.

Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. Only U.S. Customs and Border Protection (by a binding ruling) decides an import classification, and the IRS decides what goes on a return. Each answer names the official table it came from and that table's date; check the official source before you file.

Questions

What is HS chapter 76?

Aluminum and articles thereof: 12 headings and 35 six-digit subheadings.

How many digits does a chapter 76 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
