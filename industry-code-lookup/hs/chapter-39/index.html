HS chapter 39

HS chapter 39, Plastics and articles thereof, has 24 headings and 131 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 319 US import statistical numbers and 199 Schedule B export numbers. HS 2028 changes named by the WCO: Plastic waste is restructured to align with the Basel Convention (hazardous, prior-informed-consent and other plastic waste); heading 39.15 is where HS 2022 puts plastic waste.

Questions

What is HS chapter 39?

Plastics and articles thereof: 24 headings and 131 six-digit subheadings.

How many digits does a chapter 39 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
