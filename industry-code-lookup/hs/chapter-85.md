# HS chapter 85

HS chapter 85, Electrical machinery and equipment and parts thereof; sound recorders and reproducers, television image and sound recorders and reproducers, and parts and accessories of such articles, has 45 headings and 296 six-digit subheadings (HS 2022 as carried in the US HTS, 2026 HTS Revision 22), under which sit 982 US import statistical numbers and 668 Schedule B export numbers. The WCO has not yet published which of its codes change in HS 2028.

- Headings: 45
- Subheadings: 296
- US import lines: 982
- Schedule B numbers: 668

## Questions

### What is HS chapter 85?

Electrical machinery and equipment and parts thereof; sound recorders and reproducers, television image and sound recorders and reproducers, and parts and accessories of such articles: 45 headings and 296 six-digit subheadings.

### How many digits does a chapter 85 code need?

Six for the international HS code; 10 for a US import entry (HTS) or a US export filing (Schedule B); 8 for the EU's Combined Nomenclature.
