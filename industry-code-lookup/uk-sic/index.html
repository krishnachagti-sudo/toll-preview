UK SIC codes

Companies House accepts 731 five-digit SIC 2007 codes on its condensed list, in 21 sections from A (agriculture) to U (extraterritorial bodies), plus 99999 for a dormant company; filing a code that is not on the list may be rejected.

Questions

What SIC code is a dormant company?

99999, Dormant Company. A non-trading company uses 74990.

Can I use any UK SIC code?

Companies House says to use only codes on its condensed list, or the filing may be rejected.

Is UK SIC the same as US SIC?

No: UK SIC 2007 has 5 digits and follows the EU's NACE; US SIC 1987 has 4 digits and was replaced by NAICS.
