# NAICS 2022 codes list

- Codes: 1,012
- With SBA size standards: 974
- Proposed 2027 change: 53

## The columns

- naics\_2022 and title: the Census 2-6 digit 2022 file.
- sba\_receipts\_million\_usd or sba\_employees, and sba\_footnote: SBA's table effective March 17, 2023 (13 CFR 121.201).
- naics\_2027\_proposed: the change OMB proposed on July 13, 2026 (combined, split, part moves or title), with the 2027 code.
- naics\_2017 and sic\_1987: from the Census concordances.
