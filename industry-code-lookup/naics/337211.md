# NAICS 337211: Wood Office Furniture Manufacturing

337211 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Wood Office Furniture Manufacturing; SBA size standard 1,000 employees; NAICS 2027 proposes combining it into 337210 Office Furniture (including Fixtures) Manufacturing.

- Code: 337211
- SBA size standard: 1,000 employees
- Census index entries: 9
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): combined

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing wood office-type furniture. The furniture may be made on a stock or custom basis and may be assembled or unassembled (i.e., knockdown).

## Census index entries (9)

- Cabinets, wood office-type, freestanding, manufacturing
- Chairs, wood office-type, manufacturing
- Desks, wood office-type, manufacturing

- Modular furniture systems, wood frame office-type, manufacturing
- Office furniture, padded, upholstered, or plain wood, manufacturing
- Tables, wood, office-type, manufacturing
