# NAICS 512110: Motion Picture and Video Production

512110 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Motion Picture and Video Production; SBA size standard $40 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 512110
- SBA size standard: $40 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 21
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in producing, or producing and distributing motion pictures, videos, television programs, or television commercials.
