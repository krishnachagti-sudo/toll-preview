## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing verbatim reporting and stenotype recording of live legal proceedings and transcribing subsequent recorded materials.

## Census index entries (7)

- Closed captioning services, real-time (i.e., simultaneous)
- Court reporting services
- Deposition services
- Public stenography services
- Real-time (i.e., simultaneous) closed captioning of live television performances, meetings, conferences, and so forth
- Stenography services, public
- Stenotype recording services
