# NAICS 712130: Zoos and Botanical Gardens

712130 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Zoos and Botanical Gardens; SBA size standard $34 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 712130
- SBA size standard: $34 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 16
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the preservation and exhibition of live plant and animal life displays.
