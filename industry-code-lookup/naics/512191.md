# NAICS 512191: Teleproduction and Other Postproduction Services

512191 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Teleproduction and Other Postproduction Services; SBA size standard $39 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing specialized motion picture or video postproduction services, such as editing, film/tape transfers, subtitling, credits, closed captioning, and animation and special effects.
