# NAICS 532111: Passenger Car Rental

532111 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Passenger Car Rental; SBA size standard $47 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 532111
- SBA size standard: $47 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 12
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in renting passenger cars without drivers, generally for short periods of time.
