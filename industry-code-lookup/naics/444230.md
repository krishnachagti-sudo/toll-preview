# NAICS 444230: Outdoor Power Equipment Retailers

444230 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Outdoor Power Equipment Retailers; SBA size standard $9.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 444230
- SBA size standard: $9.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 3
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new outdoor power equipment or retailing new outdoor power equipment in combination with activities, such as repair services and selling replacement parts.

## Census index entries (3)

- Garden power equipment stores
- Lawn power equipment stores
- Power equipment stores, outdoor
