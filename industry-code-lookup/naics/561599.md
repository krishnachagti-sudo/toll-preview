561599 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): All Other Travel Arrangement and Reservation Services; SBA size standard $32.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments (except travel agencies, tour operators, and convention and visitors bureaus) primarily engaged in providing travel arrangement and reservation services.
