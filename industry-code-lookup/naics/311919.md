# NAICS 311919: Other Snack Food Manufacturing

311919 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Snack Food Manufacturing; SBA size standard 1,250 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 311919
- SBA size standard: 1,250 employees
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing snack foods (except roasted nuts and peanut butter).

## Census index entries (8)

- Cheese curls and puffs manufacturing
- Corn chips and related corn snacks manufacturing
- Popcorn (except candy covered), popped, manufacturing
- Pork rinds manufacturing
- Potato chips manufacturing
- Potato sticks manufacturing
- Pretzels (except soft) manufacturing
- Tortilla chips manufacturing
