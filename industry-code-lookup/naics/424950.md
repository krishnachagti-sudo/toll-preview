# NAICS 424950: Paint, Varnish, and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

424950 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Paint, Varnish, and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 150 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 424950

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of paints, varnishes, and similar coatings; pigments; wallpaper; and supplies, such as paintbrushes and rollers.
