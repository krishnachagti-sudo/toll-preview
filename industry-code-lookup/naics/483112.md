# NAICS 483112: Deep Sea Passenger Transportation

483112 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Deep Sea Passenger Transportation; SBA size standard 1,500 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 483112
- SBA size standard: 1,500 employees
- Census index entries: 5
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing deep sea transportation of passengers to or from foreign ports.

## Census index entries (5)

- Cruise lines (i.e., deep sea passenger transportation to or from foreign ports)
- Deep sea passenger transportation to or from foreign ports
- Passenger transportation, deep sea, to or from foreign ports
- Ship chartering with crew, deep sea passenger transportation to or from foreign ports
- Transporting passengers to or from foreign ports, deep sea
