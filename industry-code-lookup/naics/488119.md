# NAICS 488119: Other Airport Operations

488119 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Airport Operations; SBA size standard $40 million in average annual receipts; NAICS 2027 proposes the new title "Other Airport and Spaceport Operations".

- Code: 488119
- SBA size standard: $40 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 15

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) operating international, national, or civil airports, or public flying fields or (2) supporting airport operations, such as rental of hangar space, and providing baggage handling and/or cargo handling services.
