# NAICS 532210: Consumer Electronics and Appliances Rental

532210 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Consumer Electronics and Appliances Rental; SBA size standard $47 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 532210

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in renting consumer electronics equipment and appliances, such as televisions, stereos, and refrigerators. Included in this industry are appliance rental centers.
