# NAICS 459110: Sporting Goods Retailers

459110 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Sporting Goods Retailers; SBA size standard $26.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 459110
- SBA size standard: $26.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 20
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new sporting goods, such as bicycles and bicycle parts; camping equipment; exercise and fitness equipment; athletic uniforms; specialty sports footwear; and other sporting goods, equipment, and accessories.
