# NAICS 335991: Carbon and Graphite Product Manufacturing

335991 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Carbon and Graphite Product Manufacturing; SBA size standard 900 employees; NAICS 2027 proposes combining it into 335990 All Other Electrical Equipment and Component Manufacturing.

- Code: 335991
- SBA size standard: 900 employees
- Census index entries: 19
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): combined

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing carbon, graphite, and metal-graphite brushes and brush stock; carbon or graphite electrodes for thermal and electrolytic uses; carbon and graphite fibers; and other carbon, graphite, and metal-graphite products.
