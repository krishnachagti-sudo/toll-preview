311351 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Chocolate and Confectionery Manufacturing from Cacao Beans; SBA size standard 1,250 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in shelling, roasting, and grinding cacao beans and making chocolate cacao products and chocolate confectioneries.
