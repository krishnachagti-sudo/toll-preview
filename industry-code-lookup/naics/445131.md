# NAICS 445131: Convenience Retailers

445131 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Convenience Retailers; SBA size standard $36.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 445131
- SBA size standard: $36.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 1
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing a limited line of groceries that generally includes milk, bread, soda, and snacks, such as convenience stores or food marts (except those operating fuel pumps).

## Census index entries (1)

- Convenience food stores (except those with fuel pumps)
