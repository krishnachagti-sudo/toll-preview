# NAICS 493190: Other Warehousing and Storage

493190 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Warehousing and Storage; SBA size standard $36.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 493190
- SBA size standard: $36.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 9
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in operating warehousing and storage facilities (except general merchandise, refrigerated, and farm product warehousing and storage).

## Census index entries (9)

- Automobile dead storage
- Bonded warehousing (except farm products, general merchandise, refrigerated)
- Bulk petroleum storage
- Document storage and warehousing
- Lumber storage terminals
- Private warehousing and storage (except farm products, general merchandise, refrigerated)
- Public warehousing and storage (except farm products, general merchandise, refrigerated, self-storage)
- Warehousing (except farm products, general merchandise, refrigerated)
- Whiskey warehousing
