# NAICS 111199: All Other Grain Farming

111199 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): All Other Grain Farming; SBA size standard $2.25 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 111199
- SBA size standard: $2.25 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing grains and/or producing grain(s) seeds (except wheat, corn, rice, and oilseed(s) and grain(s) combinations).

## Census index entries (8)

- Barley farming, field and seed production
- Broomcorn farming
- Buckwheat farming
- Milo farming, field and seed production
- Oat farming, field and seed production
- Rye farming, field and seed production
- Sorghum farming, field and seed production
- Wild rice farming, field and seed production
