423740 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Refrigeration Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 125 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of refrigeration equipment (except household-type refrigerators, freezers, and air-conditioners).
