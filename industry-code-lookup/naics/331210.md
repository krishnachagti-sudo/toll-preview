# NAICS 331210: Iron and Steel Pipe and Tube Manufacturing from Purchased Steel

331210 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Iron and Steel Pipe and Tube Manufacturing from Purchased Steel; SBA size standard 1,000 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 331210
- SBA size standard: 1,000 employees
- Census index entries: 6
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing welded, riveted, or seamless pipe and tube from purchased iron or steel.

## Census index entries (6)

- Boiler tubes, wrought, made from purchased iron
- Conduit, welded and lock joint, made from purchased iron or steel
- Pipe (e.g., heavy riveted, lock joint, seamless, welded) made from purchased iron or steel
- Tube (e.g., heavy riveted, lock joint, seamless, welded) made from purchased iron or steel
- Tubing, mechanical and hypodermic sizes, cold-drawn stainless steel, made from purchased steel
- Well casings (e.g., heavy riveted, lock joint, welded, wrought) made from purchased iron or steel
