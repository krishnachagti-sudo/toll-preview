# NAICS 423440: Other Commercial Equipment Merchant Wholesalers

423440 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Commercial Equipment Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 100 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 423440
- SBA size standard: 100 employees
- Census index entries: 36
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of commercial and related machines and equipment (except photographic equipment and supplies; office equipment; and computers and computer peripheral equipment and software) generally used in restaurants and stores.
