# NAICS 424930: Flower, Nursery Stock, and Florists' Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

424930 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Flower, Nursery Stock, and Florists' Supplies Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 100 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 424930
- SBA size standard: 100 employees
- Census index entries: 9

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of flowers, florists' supplies, and/or nursery stock (except plant seeds and plant bulbs).

## Census index entries (9)

- Artificial flowers and plants merchant wholesalers
- Bedding plants merchant wholesalers
- Florist's supplies merchant wholesalers
- Flowers merchant wholesalers
- Nursery stock (except plant bulbs, seeds) merchant wholesalers
- Ornamental plants and flowers merchant wholesalers
- Plants, potted, merchant wholesalers
- Soil, top and potting, merchant wholesalers
- Trees merchant wholesalers
