# NAICS 336370: Motor Vehicle Metal Stamping

336370 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Motor Vehicle Metal Stamping; SBA size standard 1,000 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 336370

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing motor vehicle stampings, such as fenders, tops, body parts, trim, and molding.

## Census index entries (8)

- Job stampings, automotive, metal, manufacturing
- Metal motor vehicle body parts stamping
- Moldings and trim, motor vehicle, stamping
- Motor vehicle metal bumper stampings
- Motor vehicle metal parts stamping
- Motor vehicle metal stampings (e.g., body parts, fenders, hub caps, tops, trim) manufacturing
- Stamping metal motor vehicle body parts
- Stamping metal motor vehicle moldings and trims
