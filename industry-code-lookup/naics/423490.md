# NAICS 423490: Other Professional Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

423490 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Professional Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 150 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 423490
- SBA size standard: 150 employees
- Census index entries: 27
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of professional equipment and supplies (except ophthalmic goods and medical, dental, and hospital equipment and supplies).
