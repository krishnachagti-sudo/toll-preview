# NAICS 336991: Motorcycle, Bicycle, and Parts Manufacturing

336991 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Motorcycle, Bicycle, and Parts Manufacturing; SBA size standard 1,050 employees; NAICS 2027 proposes combining it into 336998 All Other Transportation Equipment Manufacturing.

- Code: 336991
- SBA size standard: 1,050 employees
- Census index entries: 7
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): combined

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing motorcycles, bicycles, tricycles and similar equipment, and parts.

## Census index entries (7)

- Bicycles and parts manufacturing
- Electric bicycles manufacturing
- Mopeds and parts manufacturing
- Motor scooters manufacturing
- Motorcycles and parts manufacturing
- Tricycles, metal, adult and children's, manufacturing
- Vehicle, children's, metal manufacturing
