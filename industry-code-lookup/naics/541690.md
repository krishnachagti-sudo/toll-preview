541690 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Scientific and Technical Consulting Services; SBA size standard $19 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing advice and assistance to businesses and other organizations on scientific and technical issues (except environmental).
