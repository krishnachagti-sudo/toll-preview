# NAICS 424940: Tobacco Product and Electronic Cigarette Merchant Wholesalers

424940 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Tobacco Product and Electronic Cigarette Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 250 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of tobacco products, such as cigarettes, snuff, cigars, pipe tobacco, and electronic cigarettes (including related parts and accessories).

## Census index entries (8)

- Chewing tobacco merchant wholesalers
- Cigarettes merchant wholesalers
- Cigars merchant wholesalers
- Electronic cigarettes merchant wholesalers
- Pipe tobacco merchant wholesalers
- Snuff merchant wholesalers
- Tobacco (except leaf) merchant wholesalers
- Tobacco products merchant wholesalers
