# NAICS 621991: Blood and Organ Banks

621991 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Blood and Organ Banks; SBA size standard $40 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 621991
- SBA size standard: $40 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 11
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in collecting, storing, and distributing blood and blood products and storing and distributing body organs.

## Census index entries (11)

- Blood banks
- Blood donor stations
- Eye banks
- Human egg or ova banks
- Human embryo storage services
- Organ banks, body
- Organ donor centers, body
- Placenta banks
- Plasma collection services
- Plasmapheresis centers
- Sperm banks, human
