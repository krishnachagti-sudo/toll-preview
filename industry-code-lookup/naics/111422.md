# NAICS 111422: Floriculture Production

111422 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Floriculture Production; SBA size standard $3.75 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 111422
- SBA size standard: $3.75 million in receipts

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing and/or producing floriculture products (e.g., cut flowers and roses, cut cultivated greens, potted flowering and foliage plants, and flower seeds) under cover and in open fields.

## Census index entries (14)

- Cut flower growing

- Foliage growing
- Hanging basket plant growing

- Ornamental plant growing
- Plant, ornamental, growing

- Plug (i.e., floriculture products) growing
