# NAICS 523210: Securities and Commodity Exchanges

523210 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Securities and Commodity Exchanges; SBA size standard $47 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 523210
- SBA size standard: $47 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 7
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in furnishing physical or electronic marketplaces for the purpose of facilitating the buying and selling of stocks, stock options, bonds, or commodity contracts.

## Census index entries (7)

- Exchanges, commodity contracts
- Exchanges, securities
- Futures commodity contracts exchanges
- Securities exchanges
- Stock exchanges
- Stock or commodity options exchanges
