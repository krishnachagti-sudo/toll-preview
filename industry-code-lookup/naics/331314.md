# NAICS 331314: Secondary Smelting and Alloying of Aluminum

331314 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Secondary Smelting and Alloying of Aluminum; SBA size standard 750 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 331314
- SBA size standard: 750 employees
- Census index entries: 16
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) recovering aluminum and aluminum alloys from scrap and/or dross (i.e., secondary smelting) and making billet or ingot (except by rolling) and/or (2) manufacturing alloys, powder, paste, or flake from purchased aluminum.
