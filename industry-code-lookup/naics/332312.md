# NAICS 332312: Fabricated Structural Metal Manufacturing

332312 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Fabricated Structural Metal Manufacturing; SBA size standard 500 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in fabricating structural metal products, such as assemblies of concrete reinforcing bars and fabricated bar joists.
