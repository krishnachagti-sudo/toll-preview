# NAICS 424120: Stationery and Office Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

424120 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Stationery and Office Supplies Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 150 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 424120
- SBA size standard: 150 employees
- Census index entries: 44
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of stationery, office supplies, and/or gift wrap.
