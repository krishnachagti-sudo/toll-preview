423920 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Toy and Hobby Goods and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 175 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of games, toys, fireworks, playing cards, hobby goods and supplies, and/or related goods.
