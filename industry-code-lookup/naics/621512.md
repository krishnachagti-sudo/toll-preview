# NAICS 621512: Diagnostic Imaging Centers

621512 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Diagnostic Imaging Centers; SBA size standard $19 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 621512
- SBA size standard: $19 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 22
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments known as diagnostic imaging centers primarily engaged in producing images of the patient generally on referral from a health practitioner.
