# NAICS 423140: Motor Vehicle Parts (Used) Merchant Wholesalers

423140 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Motor Vehicle Parts (Used) Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 125 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 423140

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of used motor vehicle parts (except used tires and tubes) and establishments primarily engaged in dismantling motor vehicles for the purpose of selling the parts.
