# NAICS 541720: Research and Development in the Social Sciences and Humanities

541720 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Research and Development in the Social Sciences and Humanities; SBA size standard $28 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 541720
- SBA size standard: $28 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 14
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in conducting research and analyses in cognitive development, sociology, psychology, language, behavior, economic, and other social science and humanities research.
