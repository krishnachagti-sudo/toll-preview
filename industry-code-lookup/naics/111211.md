# NAICS 111211: Potato Farming

111211 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Potato Farming; SBA size standard $4.25 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 111211
- SBA size standard: $4.25 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 5
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing potatoes and/or producing seed potatoes.

## Census index entries (5)

- Potato farming, field and seed potato production
- Sweet potato farming, field and seed potato production
- Vegetable and melon farming, potato dominant crop, field and seed production
- Vegetable and potato farming, potato dominant crop, field and seed potato production
- Yam farming, field and seed production
