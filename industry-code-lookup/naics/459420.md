# NAICS 459420: Gift, Novelty, and Souvenir Retailers

459420 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Gift, Novelty, and Souvenir Retailers; SBA size standard $13.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 459420
- SBA size standard: $13.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 14
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new gifts, novelty merchandise, souvenirs, greeting cards, seasonal and holiday decorations, and curios.

## Census index entries (14)

- Balloon shops
- Card shops, greeting
- Christmas stores
- Collectible gift shops (e.g., crystal, pewter, porcelain)
- Craft (except craft supply) stores
- Curio shops
- Fruit basket or fruit bouquet stores
- Gift shops
- Gift stands, permanent location
- Greeting card shops
- Novelty shops
- Party goods (e.g., paper supplies, decorations, novelties) stores
- Seasonal and holiday decoration stores
- Souvenir shops
