# NAICS 114119: Other Marine Fishing

114119 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Marine Fishing; SBA size standard $11.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 114119
- SBA size standard: $11.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 5
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the commercial catching or taking of marine animals (except finfish and shellfish).

## Census index entries (5)

- Frog fishing
- Seaweed gathering
- Sponge gathering
- Terrapin fishing
- Turtle fishing
