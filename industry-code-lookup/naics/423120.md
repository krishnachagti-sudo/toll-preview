# NAICS 423120: Motor Vehicle Supplies and New Parts Merchant Wholesalers

423120 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Motor Vehicle Supplies and New Parts Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 200 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 423120
- SBA size standard: 200 employees
- Census index entries: 40
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of motor vehicle supplies, accessories, tools, and equipment; and new motor vehicle parts (except new tires and tubes).
