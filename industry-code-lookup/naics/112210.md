# NAICS 112210: Hog and Pig Farming

112210 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Hog and Pig Farming; SBA size standard $4 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 112210
- SBA size standard: $4 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in raising hogs and pigs. These establishments may include farming activities, such as breeding, farrowing, and the raising of weanling pigs, feeder pigs, or market size hogs.

## Census index entries (8)

- Farrow-to-finish operations
- Feeder pig farming
- Feedlots (except stockyards for transportation), hog
- Hog and pig (including breeding, farrowing, nursery, and finishing activities) farming
- Hog feedlots (except stockyards for transportation)
- Pig farming
- Swine farming
- Weaning pig operations
