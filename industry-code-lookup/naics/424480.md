# NAICS 424480: Fresh Fruit and Vegetable Merchant Wholesalers

424480 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Fresh Fruit and Vegetable Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 100 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 424480
- SBA size standard: 100 employees
- Census index entries: 7
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of fresh fruits and vegetables.

## Census index entries (7)

- Berries, fresh, merchant wholesalers
- Fresh fruits, vegetables, and berries merchant wholesalers

- Health foods, fresh fruits and vegetables, merchant wholesalers
- Produce, fresh, merchant wholesalers
- Salads, prepackaged, merchant wholesalers
- Vegetables, fresh, merchant wholesalers
