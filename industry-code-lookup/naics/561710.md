# NAICS 561710: Exterminating and Pest Control Services

561710 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Exterminating and Pest Control Services; SBA size standard $17.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in exterminating and controlling birds, mosquitoes, rodents, termites, and other insects and pests (except for crop production and forestry production). Establishments providing fumigation services are included in this industry.

## Census index entries (7)

- Bird proofing services
- Exterminating services
- Fumigating services (except crop fumigating)
- Mosquito eradication services
- Pest (e.g., termite) inspection services
- Pest control (except agricultural, forestry) services
- Termite control services
