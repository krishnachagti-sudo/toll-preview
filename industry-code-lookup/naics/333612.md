# NAICS 333612: Speed Changer, Industrial High-Speed Drive, and Gear Manufacturing

333612 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Speed Changer, Industrial High-Speed Drive, and Gear Manufacturing; SBA size standard 750 employees; NAICS 2027 proposes combining it into 333619 Other Engine and Power Transmission Equipment Manufacturing.

- Code: 333612
- SBA size standard: 750 employees
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): combined

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing gears, speed changers, and industrial high-speed drives (except hydrostatic).

## Census index entries (8)

- Drives, high-speed industrial (except hydrostatic), manufacturing
- Gearmotors (i.e., power transmission equipment) manufacturing
- Gears, power transmission (except aircraft, motor vehicle), manufacturing
- Motors, gear, manufacturing
- Reducers, speed, manufacturing
- Reduction gears and gear units (except aircraft power transmission equipment, automotive) manufacturing
- Speed changers (i.e., power transmission equipment) manufacturing
- Speed reducers (i.e., power transmission equipment) manufacturing
