# NAICS 488991: Packing and Crating

488991 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Packing and Crating; SBA size standard $34 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 488991

- Census index entries: 3

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in packing, crating, and otherwise preparing goods for transportation.

## Census index entries (3)

- Crating goods for shipping
- Packing and preparing goods for shipping
- Preparing goods for transportation (i.e., crating, packing)
