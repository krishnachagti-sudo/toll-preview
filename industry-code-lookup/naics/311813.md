# NAICS 311813: Frozen Cakes, Pies, and Other Pastries Manufacturing

311813 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Frozen Cakes, Pies, and Other Pastries Manufacturing; SBA size standard 750 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing frozen bakery products (except bread), such as cakes, pies, and doughnuts.

## Census index entries (10)

- Baked goods (except bread, bread-type rolls), frozen, manufacturing
- Cake, frozen, manufacturing
- Crullers, frozen, made in a commercial bakery
- Desserts, frozen bakery, manufacturing
- Doughnuts, frozen, manufacturing
- Frozen cake manufacturing
- Knishes, frozen, manufacturing
- Pastries (e.g., Danish, French), frozen, manufacturing
- Pies, frozen, manufacturing
- Sweet yeast goods, frozen, manufacturing
