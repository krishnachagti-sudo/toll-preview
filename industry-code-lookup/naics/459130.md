# NAICS 459130: Sewing, Needlework, and Piece Goods Retailers

459130 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Sewing, Needlework, and Piece Goods Retailers; SBA size standard $34 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new sewing supplies, fabrics, patterns, yarns, and other needlework accessories or retailing these products in combination with new sewing machines.

## Census index entries (6)

- Fabric shops
- Needlecraft sewing supply stores
- Piece goods stores
- Quilting supply stores
- Sewing supply stores
- Upholstery materials stores
