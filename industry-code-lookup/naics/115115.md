# NAICS 115115: Farm Labor Contractors and Crew Leaders

115115 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Farm Labor Contractors and Crew Leaders; SBA size standard $19 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 115115
- SBA size standard: $19 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 4
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in supplying labor for agricultural production or harvesting.

## Census index entries (4)

- Agriculture production or harvesting crews
- Crew leaders, farm labor
- Farm labor contractors
- Labor contractors, farm
