# NAICS 445230: Fruit and Vegetable Retailers

445230 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Fruit and Vegetable Retailers; SBA size standard $9 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 445230
- SBA size standard: $9 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing fresh fruits and vegetables.

## Census index entries (8)

- Fruit and vegetable stands, permanent
- Fruit markets
- Fruit stands, permanent
- Fruit stands, temporary
- Produce markets
- Produce stands, permanent
- Produce stands, temporary
- Vegetable markets
