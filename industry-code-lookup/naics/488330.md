# NAICS 488330: Navigational Services to Shipping

488330 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Navigational Services to Shipping; SBA size standard $47 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 488330
- SBA size standard: $47 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing navigational services to shipping. Marine salvage establishments are included in this industry.

## Census index entries (8)

- Cargo salvaging, marine
- Docking and undocking marine vessel services
- Harbor tugboat services
- Marine salvaging services
- Marine vessel traffic reporting services
- Piloting services, water transportation
- Radio beacon (i.e., ship navigation) services
- Tugboat services, harbor operation
