# NAICS 524210: Insurance Agencies and Brokerages

524210 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Insurance Agencies and Brokerages; SBA size standard $15 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 524210
- SBA size standard: $15 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 6
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as agents (i.e., brokers) in selling annuities and insurance policies.

## Census index entries (6)

- Agencies, insurance
- Brokerages, insurance
- Brokers' offices, insurance
- Insurance agencies
- Insurance brokerages
- Life insurance agencies
