# NAICS 813930: Labor Unions and Similar Labor Organizations

813930 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Labor Unions and Similar Labor Organizations; SBA size standard $16.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 813930
- SBA size standard: $16.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 10
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in promoting the interests of organized labor and union employees.
