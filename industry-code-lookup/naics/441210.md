# NAICS 441210: Recreational Vehicle Dealers

441210 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Recreational Vehicle Dealers; SBA size standard $40 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 441210
- SBA size standard: $40 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new and/or used recreational vehicles commonly referred to as RVs or retailing these new vehicles in combination with activities, such as repair services and selling replacement parts and accessories.

## Census index entries (8)

- Camper dealers, recreational
- Camper parts and accessories stores
- Motor home dealers
- Recreational vehicle (RV) dealers
- Recreational vehicle (RV) parts and accessories stores
- RV dealers
- Travel trailer dealers
- Used recreational vehicle (RV) dealers
