# NAICS 523910: Miscellaneous Intermediation

523910 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Miscellaneous Intermediation; SBA size standard $47 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 523910
- SBA size standard: $47 million in receipts

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as principals (except investment bankers, securities dealers, and commodity contracts dealers) in buying or selling financial contracts generally on a spread basis. Principals are investors that buy or sell for their own account.

## Census index entries (9)

- Individuals investing in financial contracts on own account
- Investment clubs
- Mineral royalties or leases dealing (i.e., acting as a principal in dealing royalties or leases to investors)
- Mortgage dealers, buying and selling
- Oil royalty dealing (i.e., acting as a principal in dealing royalties to investors)
- Securities speculators for own account
- Tax liens dealing (i.e., acting as a principal in dealing tax liens to investors)
- Venture capital companies
- Viatical settlement companies
