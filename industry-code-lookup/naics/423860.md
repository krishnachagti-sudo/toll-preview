# NAICS 423860: Transportation Equipment and Supplies (except Motor Vehicle) Merchant Wholesalers

423860 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Transportation Equipment and Supplies (except Motor Vehicle) Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 175 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of transportation equipment and supplies (except marine pleasure craft and motor vehicles).
