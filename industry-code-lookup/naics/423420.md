# NAICS 423420: Office Equipment Merchant Wholesalers

423420 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Office Equipment Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 200 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 423420
- SBA size standard: 200 employees
- Census index entries: 32
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of office machines and related equipment (except computers and computer peripheral equipment).
