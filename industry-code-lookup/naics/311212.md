# NAICS 311212: Rice Milling

311212 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Rice Milling; SBA size standard 750 employees; NAICS 2027 proposes combining it into 311214 Rice Milling and Malt Manufacturing.

- Code: 311212
- SBA size standard: 750 employees
- Census index entries: 16
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): combined

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one of the following: (1) milling rice; (2) cleaning and polishing rice; or (3) milling, cleaning, and polishing rice. The establishments in this industry may package the rice they mill with other ingredients.
