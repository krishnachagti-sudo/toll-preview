# NAICS 541199: All Other Legal Services

541199 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): All Other Legal Services; SBA size standard $20.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 541199
- SBA size standard: $20.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments of legal practitioners (except offices of lawyers and attorneys, settlement offices, and title abstract offices). These establishments are primarily engaged in providing specialized legal or paralegal services.

## Census index entries (8)

- Jury consulting services
- Notary public services
- Notary publics' private practices
- Paralegal services
- Patent agent services (i.e., patent filing and searching services)
- Process server services
- Process serving services
- Trial consulting services
