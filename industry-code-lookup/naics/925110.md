# NAICS 925110: Administration of Housing Programs

925110 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Administration of Housing Programs; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 925110
- SBA size standard: none listed

- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises government establishments primarily engaged in the administration and planning of housing programs.

## Census index entries (3)

- Building standards agencies, government
- Housing authorities, nonoperating
- Housing programs, planning and development, government
