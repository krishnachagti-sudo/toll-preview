# NAICS 423720: Plumbing and Heating Equipment and Supplies (Hydronics) Merchant Wholesalers

423720 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Plumbing and Heating Equipment and Supplies (Hydronics) Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 200 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of plumbing equipment, hydronic heating equipment, household-type water heaters, and/or supplies.
