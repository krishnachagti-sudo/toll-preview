# NAICS 332410: Power Boiler and Heat Exchanger Manufacturing

332410 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Power Boiler and Heat Exchanger Manufacturing; SBA size standard 750 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing power boilers and heat exchangers. Establishments in this industry may perform installation in addition to manufacturing power boilers and heat exchangers.
