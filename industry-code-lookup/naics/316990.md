# NAICS 316990: Other Leather and Allied Product Manufacturing

316990 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Leather and Allied Product Manufacturing; SBA size standard 500 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing leather products (except footwear and apparel) from purchased leather or leather substitutes (e.g., fabric, plastics).
