# NAICS 525920: Trusts, Estates, and Agency Accounts

525920 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Trusts, Estates, and Agency Accounts; SBA size standard $40 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 525920
- SBA size standard: $40 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises legal entities, trusts, estates, or agency accounts, administered on behalf of the beneficiaries under the terms of a trust agreement, will, or agency agreement.

## Census index entries (8)

- Bankruptcy estates
- Personal estates (i.e., managing assets)
- Personal investment trusts
- Personal trusts
- Private estates (i.e., administering on behalf of beneficiaries)
- Settlement trust funds
- Testamentary trusts
- Trusts, estates, and agency accounts
