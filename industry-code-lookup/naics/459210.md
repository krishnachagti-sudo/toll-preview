# NAICS 459210: Book Retailers and News Dealers

459210 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Book Retailers and News Dealers; SBA size standard $36 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 459210
- SBA size standard: $36 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 8
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new books, newspapers, magazines, and other periodicals (without publishing).

## Census index entries (8)

- Book clubs, not publishing, new books, mail-order
- Book stores
- Comic book stores
- Home delivery newspaper routes, direct selling
- Magazine stands (i.e., permanent)
- News dealers
- Newsstands (i.e., permanent)
- Religious book stores
