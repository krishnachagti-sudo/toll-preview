# NAICS 532120: Truck, Utility Trailer, and RV (Recreational Vehicle) Rental and Leasing

532120 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Truck, Utility Trailer, and RV (Recreational Vehicle) Rental and Leasing; SBA size standard $47 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 532120
- SBA size standard: $47 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 16
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in renting or leasing, without drivers, one or more of the following: trucks, truck tractors, buses, semi-trailers, utility trailers, or RVs (recreational vehicles).
