# NAICS 423410: Photographic Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

423410 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Photographic Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 200 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 423410
- SBA size standard: 200 employees
- Census index entries: 22
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of photographic equipment and supplies (except office equipment).
