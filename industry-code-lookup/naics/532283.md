# NAICS 532283: Home Health Equipment Rental

532283 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Home Health Equipment Rental; SBA size standard $41 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in renting home-type health and invalid equipment, such as wheelchairs, hospital beds, oxygen tanks, walkers, and crutches.
