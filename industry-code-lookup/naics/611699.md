# NAICS 611699: All Other Miscellaneous Schools and Instruction

611699 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): All Other Miscellaneous Schools and Instruction; SBA size standard $16.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 611699
- SBA size standard: $16.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 16
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in offering instruction (except academic schools, colleges, and universities; business, computer, and management instruction; technical and trade instruction; and fine arts, sports, recreation, language, exam preparation, tutoring, and automobile driving instruction).
