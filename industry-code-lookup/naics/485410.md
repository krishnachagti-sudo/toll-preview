# NAICS 485410: School and Employee Bus Transportation

485410 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): School and Employee Bus Transportation; SBA size standard $30 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 485410
- SBA size standard: $30 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 4
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing buses and other motor vehicles to transport pupils to and from school or employees to and from work.

## Census index entries (4)

- Bus operation, school and employee
- Employee bus services
- School bus services
- School or employee bus transportation for the disabled or elderly
