# NAICS 488390: Other Support Activities for Water Transportation

488390 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Support Activities for Water Transportation; SBA size standard $47 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 488390
- SBA size standard: $47 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 6
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing services to water transportation (except port and harbor operations; marine cargo handling services; and navigational services to shipping).

## Census index entries (6)

- Cargo checkers, marine
- Cargo surveyors, marine
- Drydocks, floating (i.e., routine repair and maintenance of ships)
- Marine cargo checkers and surveyors
- Ship dismantling at floating drydock
- Ship scaling services not done at a shipyard
