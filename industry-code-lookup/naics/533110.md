# NAICS 533110: Lessors of Nonfinancial Intangible Assets (except Copyrighted Works)

533110 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Lessors of Nonfinancial Intangible Assets (except Copyrighted Works); SBA size standard $47 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 533110
- SBA size standard: $47 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 10
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in assigning rights to assets, such as patents, trademarks, brand names, and/or franchise agreements, for which a royalty payment or licensing fee is paid to the asset holder.

## Census index entries (10)

- Brand name licensing
- Franchise agreements, leasing, selling or licensing, without providing other services
- Industrial design licensing
- Oil royalty companies
- Oil royalty leasing
- Oil royalty traders (except for own account)
- Patent buying and licensing
- Patent leasing
- Performance rights, licensing of
- Trademark licensing
