# NAICS 811111: General Automotive Repair

811111 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): General Automotive Repair; SBA size standard $9 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 811111
- SBA size standard: $9 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 11
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing (1) a wide range of mechanical and electrical repair and maintenance services for automotive vehicles, such as passenger cars, trucks, and vans, and all trailers or (2) engine repair and replacement.
