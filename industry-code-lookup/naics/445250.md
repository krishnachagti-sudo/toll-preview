# NAICS 445250: Fish and Seafood Retailers

445250 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Fish and Seafood Retailers; SBA size standard $9 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 445250
- SBA size standard: $9 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 2
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing fresh, frozen, or cured fish and seafood products not for immediate consumption.

## Census index entries (2)

- Fish markets
- Seafood markets
