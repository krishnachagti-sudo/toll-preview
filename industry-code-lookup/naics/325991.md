# NAICS 325991: Custom Compounding of Purchased Resins

325991 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Custom Compounding of Purchased Resins; SBA size standard 600 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) custom mixing and blending plastics resins made elsewhere or (2) reformulating plastics resins from recycled plastics products.

## Census index entries (4)

- Custom compounding (i.e., blending and mixing) of purchased plastics resins
- Plastics resins compounding from recycled materials
- Plastics resins, custom compounding of purchased
- Reformulating plastics resins from recycled plastics products
