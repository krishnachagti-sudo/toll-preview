# NAICS 532282: Video Tape and Disc Rental

532282 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Video Tape and Disc Rental; SBA size standard $35 million in average annual receipts; NAICS 2027 proposes combining it into 532288 All Other Consumer Goods Rental.

- Code: 532282
- SBA size standard: $35 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 7
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): combined

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in renting prerecorded video tapes and discs for home electronic equipment, including renting through vending machines.

## Census index entries (7)

- Cassette, prerecorded video, rental
- Computer video game rental
- Laser disc, video, rental
- Video disc rental for home electronic equipment (e.g., DVD)
- Video game rental
- Video tape rental for home electronic equipment (e.g., VCR)
- Video tape rental stores
