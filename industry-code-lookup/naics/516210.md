# NAICS 516210: Media Streaming Distribution Services, Social Networks, and Other Media Networks and Content Providers

516210 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Media Streaming Distribution Services, Social Networks, and Other Media Networks and Content Providers; SBA size standard $47 million in average annual receipts; NAICS 2027 proposes splitting it into 516213 Media Streaming Distribution Services and Broadcasting Networks and 516214 Social Networks and Collaborative Media Sites.

- Code: 516210
- SBA size standard: $47 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 49
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): split

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily providing media streaming distribution services, operating social network sites, operating media broadcasting and cable television networks, and supplying information, such as news reports, articles, pictures, and features, to the news media. These establishments distribute textual, audio, and/or video content of general or specific interest.
