# NAICS 721211: RV (Recreational Vehicle) Parks and Campgrounds

721211 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): RV (Recreational Vehicle) Parks and Campgrounds; SBA size standard $10 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 721211
- SBA size standard: $10 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 5
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in operating sites to accommodate campers and their equipment, including tents, tent trailers, travel trailers, and RVs (recreational vehicles). These establishments may provide access to facilities, such as washrooms, laundry rooms, recreation halls, playgrounds, stores, and snack bars.

## Census index entries (5)

- Campgrounds
- Campsites
- Recreational vehicle parks
- RV (recreational vehicle) parks
- Travel trailer campsites
