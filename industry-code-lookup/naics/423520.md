# NAICS 423520: Coal and Other Mineral and Ore Merchant Wholesalers

423520 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Coal and Other Mineral and Ore Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 200 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 423520

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of coal, coke, metal ores, and/or nonmetallic minerals (except precious and semiprecious stones and minerals used in construction, such as sand and gravel).
