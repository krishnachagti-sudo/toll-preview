# NAICS 812922: One-Hour Photofinishing

812922 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): One-Hour Photofinishing; SBA size standard $19 million in average annual receipts; NAICS 2027 proposes combining it into 812920 Photofinishing.

- Code: 812922
- SBA size standard: $19 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 4
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): combined

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments known as "one-hour" photofinishing labs primarily engaged in developing film and/or making photographic slides, prints, and enlargements on a short turnaround or while-you-wait basis.

## Census index entries (4)

- Film developing and printing, one-hour
- One-hour photofinishing services
- Photofinishing labs, one-hour
- Photofinishing services, one-hour
