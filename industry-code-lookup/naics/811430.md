# NAICS 811430: Footwear and Leather Goods Repair

811430 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Footwear and Leather Goods Repair; SBA size standard $9 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 811430
- SBA size standard: $9 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 5
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in repairing footwear and/or repairing other leather or leather-like goods without retailing new footwear and leather or leather-like goods, such as handbags and briefcases.

## Census index entries (5)

- Boot repair shops without retailing new boots
- Leather goods repair shops without retailing new leather goods
- Luggage repair shops without retailing new luggage
- Saddlery repair shops without retailing new saddlery
- Shoe repair shops without retailing new shoes
