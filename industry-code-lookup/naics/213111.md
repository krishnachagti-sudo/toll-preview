# NAICS 213111: Drilling Oil and Gas Wells

213111 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Drilling Oil and Gas Wells; SBA size standard 1,000 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 213111
- SBA size standard: 1,000 employees
- Census index entries: 18
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in drilling oil and gas wells for others on a contract or fee basis. This industry includes contractors that specialize in spudding in, drilling in, redrilling, and directional drilling.
