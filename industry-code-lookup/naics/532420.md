# NAICS 532420: Office Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing

532420 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Office Machinery and Equipment Rental and Leasing; SBA size standard $40 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 532420
- SBA size standard: $40 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 11

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in renting or leasing office machinery and equipment, such as computers, office furniture, duplicating machines (i.e., copiers), or facsimile machines.
