# NAICS 332215: Metal Kitchen Cookware, Utensil, Cutlery, and Flatware (except Precious) Manufacturing

332215 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Metal Kitchen Cookware, Utensil, Cutlery, and Flatware (except Precious) Manufacturing; SBA size standard 1,000 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing metal kitchen cookware (except by casting (e.g., cast iron skillets) or stamped without further fabrication), utensils, and/or nonprecious and precious plated metal cutlery and flatware.
