# NAICS 541890: Other Services Related to Advertising

541890 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Services Related to Advertising; SBA size standard $19 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 541890
- SBA size standard: $19 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 7
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing advertising services (except advertising agency services, public relations agency services, media buying agency services, media representative services, display advertising services, direct mail advertising services, advertising material distribution services, and marketing consulting services).

## Census index entries (7)

- Advertising specialty (e.g., keychain, magnet, pen) distribution services
- Demonstration services, merchandise
- Display lettering services
- Mannequin decorating services
- Sign lettering and painting services
- Welcoming services (i.e., advertising services)
- Window dressing or trimming services, store
