# NAICS 561440: Collection Agencies

561440 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Collection Agencies; SBA size standard $19.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 561440
- SBA size standard: $19.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 9
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in collecting payments for claims and remitting payments collected to their clients.

## Census index entries (9)

- Account collection services
- Adjustment agencies (except insurance)
- Bill collection services
- Collection agencies
- Collection agencies, accounts
- Credit arrears collection services
- Debt collection services
- Delinquent account collection services
- Tax collection services on a contract or fee basis
