# NAICS 811121: Automotive Body, Paint, and Interior Repair and Maintenance

811121 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Automotive Body, Paint, and Interior Repair and Maintenance; SBA size standard $9 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 811121
- SBA size standard: $9 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 13
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in repairing or customizing automotive vehicle and trailer bodies and interiors; and/or painting automotive vehicles and trailer bodies.
