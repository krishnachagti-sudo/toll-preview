541921 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Photography Studios, Portrait; SBA size standard $16 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments known as portrait studios primarily engaged in providing still, video, or digital portrait photography services.
