# NAICS 488410: Motor Vehicle Towing

488410 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Motor Vehicle Towing; SBA size standard $9 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in towing light or heavy motor vehicles, both local and long-distance. These establishments may provide incidental services, such as storage and emergency road repair services.

## Census index entries (5)

- Emergency road services (i.e., tow service)
- Motor vehicle towing services
- Tow truck services
- Towing services, motor vehicle
- Wrecker services (i.e., towing services), motor vehicle
