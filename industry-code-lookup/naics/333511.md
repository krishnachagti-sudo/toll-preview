# NAICS 333511: Industrial Mold Manufacturing

333511 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Industrial Mold Manufacturing; SBA size standard 500 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 333511
- SBA size standard: 500 employees
- Census index entries: 7
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing industrial molds for casting metals or forming other materials, such as plastics, glass, or rubber.

## Census index entries (7)

- Die-casting dies manufacturing
- Foundry casting molds manufacturing
- Industrial molds (except steel ingot) manufacturing
- Molds (except steel ingot), industrial, manufacturing
- Molds for forming materials (e.g., glass, plastics, rubber) manufacturing
- Molds for metal casting (except steel ingot) manufacturing
- Molds for plastics and rubber working machinery manufacturing
