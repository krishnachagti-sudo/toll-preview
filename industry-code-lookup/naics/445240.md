# NAICS 445240: Meat Retailers

445240 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Meat Retailers; SBA size standard $9 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 445240

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing fresh, frozen, or cured meats and poultry not for immediate consumption. Delicatessen-type establishments primarily engaged in retailing fresh meat are included in this industry.

## Census index entries (7)

- Baked ham stores
- Butcher shops
- Delicatessens (except grocery store, restaurants)
- Frozen meat stores
- Locker meat provisioners, direct selling
- Meat markets
- Poultry dealers
