# NAICS 327215: Glass Product Manufacturing Made of Purchased Glass

327215 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Glass Product Manufacturing Made of Purchased Glass; SBA size standard 1,000 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 327215
- SBA size standard: 1,000 employees
- Census index entries: 43
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in coating, laminating, tempering, or shaping purchased glass.
