## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing lime from calcitic limestone, dolomitic limestone, or other calcareous materials, such as coral, chalk, and shells. Lime manufacturing establishments may mine, quarry, collect, or purchase the sources of calcium carbonate.

## Census index entries (8)

- Agricultural lime manufacturing
- Calcium hydroxide (i.e., hydrated lime) manufacturing
- Calcium oxide (i.e., quicklime) manufacturing
- Dolomite, dead-burned, manufacturing
- Dolomitic lime manufacturing
- Hydrated lime (i.e., calcium hydroxide) manufacturing
- Lime production
- Quicklime (i.e., calcium oxide) manufacturing
