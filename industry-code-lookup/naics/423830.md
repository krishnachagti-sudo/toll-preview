# NAICS 423830: Industrial Machinery and Equipment Merchant Wholesalers

423830 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Industrial Machinery and Equipment Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 100 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 423830
- SBA size standard: 100 employees
- Census index entries: 115
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of specialized machinery, equipment, and related parts generally used in manufacturing, oil well, and warehousing activities.
