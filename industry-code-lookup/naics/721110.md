# NAICS 721110: Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels

721110 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels; SBA size standard $40 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 721110
- SBA size standard: $40 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 21
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing short-term lodging in facilities known as hotels, motor hotels, resort hotels, and motels. The establishments in this industry may offer food and beverage services, recreational services, conference rooms, convention services, laundry services, parking, and other services.
