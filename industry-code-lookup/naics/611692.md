# NAICS 611692: Automobile Driving Schools

611692 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Automobile Driving Schools; SBA size standard $10 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 611692
- SBA size standard: $10 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 4

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in offering automobile driving instruction.

## Census index entries (4)

- Automobile driving schools
- Driver education
- Driver training schools (except bus, heavy equipment, truck)
- Motorcycle driving schools
