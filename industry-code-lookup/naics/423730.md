# NAICS 423730: Warm Air Heating and Air-Conditioning Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers

423730 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Warm Air Heating and Air-Conditioning Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 175 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 423730
- SBA size standard: 175 employees
- Census index entries: 19
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of warm air heating and air-conditioning equipment and supplies.
