# NAICS 424110: Printing and Writing Paper Merchant Wholesalers

424110 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Printing and Writing Paper Merchant Wholesalers; SBA size standard 225 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 424110
- SBA size standard: 225 employees
- Census index entries: 6
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of bulk printing and/or writing paper generally on rolls for further processing.

## Census index entries (6)

- Envelope paper, bulk, merchant wholesalers
- Fine paper, bulk, merchant wholesalers
- Groundwood paper, bulk, merchant wholesalers
- Newsprint merchant wholesalers
- Paper (e.g., fine, printing, writing), bulk, merchant wholesalers
- Printing paper, bulk, merchant wholesalers
