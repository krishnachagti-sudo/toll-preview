# NAICS 336415: Guided Missile and Space Vehicle Propulsion Unit and Propulsion Unit Parts Manufacturing

336415 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Guided Missile and Space Vehicle Propulsion Unit and Propulsion Unit Parts Manufacturing; SBA size standard 1,250 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) manufacturing guided missile and/or space vehicle propulsion units and propulsion unit parts and/or (2) developing and making prototypes of guided missile and space vehicle propulsion units and propulsion unit parts.

## Census index entries (4)

- Developing and producing prototypes for guided missile and space vehicle engines
- Guided missile and space vehicle engine manufacturing
- Propulsion units and parts, guided missile and space vehicle, manufacturing
- Rocket engines, guided missile, manufacturing
