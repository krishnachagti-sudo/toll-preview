# NAICS 531190: Lessors of Other Real Estate Property

531190 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Lessors of Other Real Estate Property; SBA size standard $34 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as lessors of real estate (except buildings), such as manufactured home (i.e., mobile home) sites, vacant lots, and grazing land.
