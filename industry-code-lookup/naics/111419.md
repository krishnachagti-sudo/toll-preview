# NAICS 111419: Other Food Crops Grown Under Cover

111419 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Food Crops Grown Under Cover; SBA size standard $4.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 111419
- SBA size standard: $4.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 22
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing food crops (except mushrooms) under glass or protective cover.
