# NAICS 449110: Furniture Retailers

449110 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Furniture Retailers; SBA size standard $25 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 449110
- SBA size standard: $25 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 5
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new furniture, such as household furniture (e.g., baby furniture, box springs, and mattresses) and outdoor furniture; office furniture (except sold in combination with office supplies and equipment); and/or furniture sold in combination with major appliances, home electronics, home furnishings, or floor coverings.

## Census index entries (5)

- Bed stores, retail
- Furniture and appliance stores (i.e., primarily retailing furniture)
- Furniture stores (e.g., household, office, outdoor)
- Mattress stores (including waterbeds)
- Office furniture stores
