# NAICS 311991: Perishable Prepared Food Manufacturing

311991 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Perishable Prepared Food Manufacturing; SBA size standard 700 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 311991

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing perishable prepared foods, such as salads, sandwiches, prepared meals, fresh pizza, fresh pasta, and peeled or cut vegetables.
