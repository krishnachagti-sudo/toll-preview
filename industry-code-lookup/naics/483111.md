# NAICS 483111: Deep Sea Freight Transportation

483111 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Deep Sea Freight Transportation; SBA size standard 1,050 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 483111
- SBA size standard: 1,050 employees
- Census index entries: 5
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing deep sea transportation of cargo to or from foreign ports.

## Census index entries (5)

- Deep sea freight transportation to or from foreign ports
- Freight transportation, deep sea, to or from foreign ports
- Ship chartering with crew, deep sea freight transportation to or from foreign ports
- Shipping freight to or from foreign ports, deep sea
- Transporting freight to or from foreign ports, deep sea
