# NAICS 114112: Shellfish Fishing

114112 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Shellfish Fishing; SBA size standard $14 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 114112
- SBA size standard: $14 million in receipts

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the commercial catching or taking of shellfish (e.g., clams, crabs, lobsters, mussels, oysters, sea urchins, shrimp) from their natural habitat.
