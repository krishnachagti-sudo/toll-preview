# NAICS 212321: Construction Sand and Gravel Mining

212321 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Construction Sand and Gravel Mining; SBA size standard 500 employees; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 212321
- SBA size standard: 500 employees
- Census index entries: 7
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) operating commercial grade (i.e., construction) sand and gravel pits; (2) dredging for commercial grade sand and gravel; and (3) washing, screening, or otherwise preparing commercial grade sand and gravel.

## Census index entries (7)

- Common sand quarrying and/or beneficiating
- Construction sand and gravel beneficiating (e.g., grinding, screening, washing)
- Construction sand or gravel dredging
- Gravel quarrying and/or beneficiating
- Pebbles (except grinding) mining and/or beneficiating
- Sand and gravel quarrying (i.e., construction grade) and/or beneficiating
- Sand, construction grade, quarrying and/or beneficiating
