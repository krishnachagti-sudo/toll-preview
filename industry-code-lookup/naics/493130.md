# NAICS 493130: Farm Product Warehousing and Storage

493130 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Farm Product Warehousing and Storage; SBA size standard $34 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 493130
- SBA size standard: $34 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 6
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in operating bulk farm product warehousing and storage facilities (except refrigerated). Grain elevators primarily engaged in storage are included in this industry.

## Census index entries (6)

- Bonded warehousing, farm products (except refrigerated)
- Farm product warehousing and storage (except refrigerated)
- Grain elevators, storage only
- Private warehousing and storage, farm products (except refrigerated)
- Public warehousing and storage, farm products (except refrigerated)
- Warehousing, farm products (except refrigerated)
