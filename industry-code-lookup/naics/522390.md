# NAICS 522390: Other Activities Related to Credit Intermediation

522390 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Activities Related to Credit Intermediation; SBA size standard $28.5 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 522390
- SBA size standard: $28.5 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 6
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in facilitating credit intermediation (except mortgage and loan brokerage; and financial transactions processing, reserve, and clearinghouse activities).

## Census index entries (6)

- Check cashing services
- Loan servicing
- Money order issuance services
- Money transmission services
- Payday lending services
- Travelers' check issuance services
