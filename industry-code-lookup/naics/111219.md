# NAICS 111219: Other Vegetable (except Potato) and Melon Farming

111219 is a valid NAICS 2022 code (national industry): Other Vegetable (except Potato) and Melon Farming; SBA size standard $3.75 million in average annual receipts; no change is proposed for NAICS 2027.

- Code: 111219
- SBA size standard: $3.75 million in receipts
- Census index entries: 67
- NAICS 2027 (proposed): no change

## The Census definition

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) growing melons and/or vegetables (except potatoes; dry peas; dry beans; field, silage, or seed corn; and sugar beets); (2) producing vegetable and/or melon seeds; and (3) growing vegetable and/or melon bedding plants.
