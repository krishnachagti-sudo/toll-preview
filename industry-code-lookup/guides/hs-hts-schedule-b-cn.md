# HS, HTS, Schedule B and CN: which digits

The first 6 digits are the international HS code everywhere, such as 6109.10 for cotton T-shirts; the US adds 4 more for imports (HTS 6109.10.00.12) and exports (Schedule B 6109.10.0013), and the EU adds 2 for its 8-digit Combined Nomenclature code.

- HS: 6
- US HTS: 10
- Schedule B: 10
- EU CN: 8

## Read one number

1. 61: chapter, Articles of apparel and clothing accessories, knitted or crocheted.
2. 6109: heading, T-shirts, singlets, tank tops and similar garments, knitted or crocheted:
3. 6109.10: subheading, T-shirts, singlets, tank tops and similar garments, knitted or crocheted: Of cotton.
4. 6109.10.00: US tariff line, general duty 16.5%.
5. 6109.10.00.12: US statistical number, Men's (338).

> The EU's page describes a CN code as an 8-digit number followed by a description and a duty rate, updated every year in the Official Journal.

## Questions

### What is the difference between HS and HTS?

HS is the 6-digit international system; the HTS is the US tariff built on it, to 8 digits for the duty rate and 10 for statistics.

### How many digits is an HS code in the EU?

The EU's Combined Nomenclature adds 2 digits to the 6 HS digits: a CN code has 8.
