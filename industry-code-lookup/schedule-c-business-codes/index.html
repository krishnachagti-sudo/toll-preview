Schedule C business codes

Schedule C line B takes one of the 304 six-digit principal business or professional activity codes in the IRS's 2025 instructions, in 20 categories; 160 of them are also a NAICS 2022 code, and the rest group several NAICS industries under codes such as 492000 Couriers & messengers.

999000Unclassified establishments (unable to classify)

The IRS says to pick the category that best describes your primary business activity, then the activity that is the main source of your sales or receipts. Farming income goes on Schedule F instead.

By kind of work: DoorDash, Uber, Lyft, Instacart, Amazon Flex, consulting.

Sources

  • IRS: Instructions for Schedule C (Form 1040) (2025), Principal Business or Professional Activity Codes (page updated April 30, 2026; read November 8, 2026)irs.gov

Reference, not a customs ruling or tax advice: a code that exists may still be the wrong one for your goods or business. Only U.S. Customs and Border Protection (by a binding ruling) decides an import classification, and the IRS decides what goes on a return. Each answer names the official table it came from and that table's date; check the official source before you file.

Questions

Where does the business code go on Schedule C?

Line B, as six digits from the IRS list in the Schedule C instructions.

Is the Schedule C code a NAICS code?

The IRS says the codes are based on NAICS. 160 of the 304 are the same as a NAICS 2022 code; others, such as 492000, group several.

What if no code fits?

The IRS list ends with 999000, Unclassified establishments (unable to classify).
