# HS code lookup

The US HTS (2026 HTS Revision 22) carries 5,612 six-digit HS subheadings under 961 headings in 96 chapters, 01 to 97 with 77 reserved; the first 6 digits of any HTS or Schedule B number are this international HS code. HS 2028, in force January 1, 2028, will have 1,229 headings and 5,852 subheadings (WCO).

- Six-digit subheadings: 5,612
- Headings: 961
- Chapters: 96

## Questions

### Why are HS codes only 6 digits?

The first 6 digits are the international Harmonized System that every member country shares. Countries add digits for their own tariff lines: the US uses 10 for imports (HTS) and exports (Schedule B), and the EU uses 8 (CN).

### How many HS codes are there?

The US HTS carries 5,612 six-digit subheadings. HS 2028 will have 5,852 subheadings and 1,229 headings, the WCO says.

### Do HS codes change?

The WCO revises the HS about every five years; HS 2028 is in force January 1, 2028. National tariffs change more often: the US HTS had several revisions in 2026.
