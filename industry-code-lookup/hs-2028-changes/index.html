HS 2028: what changes

HS 2028 enters into force on January 1, 2028, after the WCO accepted 299 sets of amendments: it will have 1,229 headings and 5,852 six-digit subheadings, with 6 headings and 428 subheadings created and 5 headings and 172 subheadings deleted against HS 2022.

HeadingChangeSix-digit codes today
2107New heading 21.07 for dietary supplements, with new legal Notes.0
3002Vaccines move out of heading 30.02 into new headings 30.07 (human medicine) and 30.08 (other, including veterinary).10
3007New heading 30.07 for vaccines for human medicine, with disease-based subheadings.0
3008New heading 30.08 for other vaccines, including veterinary vaccines.0
3915Plastic waste is restructured to align with the Basel Convention (hazardous, prior-informed-consent and other plastic waste); heading 39.15 is where HS 2022 puts plastic waste.4
  • Health emergencies: new subheadings for ambulances, personal protective equipment, medical ventilators and diagnostic devices.
  • Single-use plastics: new subheadings and legal clarifications for plastic products.
  • Other updates for goods under international conventions and environmentally relevant products.

What to do now

  • List the HS codes you use and check each one: the checker shows the codes the WCO has named so far.
  • Watch for the WCO correlation tables and for the US HTS edition that adopts HS 2028.
  • Plan to update product master data, customs forms and carrier profiles for January 1, 2028.

Questions

When does HS 2028 take effect?

January 1, 2028, the WCO says.

How many HS codes are in HS 2028?

1,229 headings and 5,852 subheadings, after 299 sets of amendments.

Is there a correlation table from HS 2022 to HS 2028?

The WCO is developing the correlation tables during the two-year implementation period; they were not published when this page was written.
