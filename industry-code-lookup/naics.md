## Questions

### How many NAICS codes are there?

NAICS 2022 has 1,012 six-digit national industries and 689 five-digit NAICS industries, under 20 sectors.

### How do I group NAICS codes by two-digit sector?

Take the first two digits, but read 31, 32 and 33 as sector 31-33 (Manufacturing), 44 and 45 as 44-45 (Retail Trade), and 48 and 49 as 48-49 (Transportation and Warehousing).

### When does NAICS change next?

OMB has proposed NAICS 2027 for data periods beginning on or after January 1, 2027; until a final notice, NAICS 2022 is the current edition.
