# The WCO's HS vs national tariffs

The WCO publishes the international HS text and explanatory notes at 6 digits, and HS 2028 (in force January 1, 2028) will have 5,852 subheadings; national tariffs add digits on top, and the US HTS used here carries 5,612 six-digit codes. The WCO classifies and interprets; this checker validates against the US tables.

## Side by side

|  | WCO | This checker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | The international HS legal text, notes and explanatory notes | The US HTS and Schedule B tables, and the 6-digit level they carry |
| Digits | 6 | 6, 8 and 10 (US) |
| Next edition | HS 2028, in force January 1, 2028 | Updated when the US HTS adopts it |
| Correlation tables | Being developed for HS 2028 | Will follow the official tables |

## When the WCO is better

The WCO publishes the international text and explanatory notes; read them to interpret a heading. This checker validates codes; it does not classify goods.
