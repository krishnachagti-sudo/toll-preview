# Schedule B numbers

Census Schedule B (2026) has 9,779 ten-digit export numbers; 6,004 of them are also US HTS import numbers. An export of over $2,500 under one Schedule B number needs Electronic Export Information filed in AES (15 CFR 30.37(a)).

- Schedule B numbers: 9,779
- Also HTS numbers: 6,004
- EEI threshold: $2,500

## Questions

### What is a Schedule B number?

The 10-digit number the Census Bureau uses to classify US exports. Its first 6 digits are the international HS code.

### When do I need a Schedule B number?

For Electronic Export Information in AES: filing is not required for commodities valued at $2,500 or less under one Schedule B number to one consignee, with other exceptions in 15 CFR 30.37.

### Can I use an HTS number instead?

For EEI, the regulation accepts a Schedule B number or an HTSUSA classification code. 6,004 of the 9,779 Schedule B numbers are the same 10 digits as an HTS number.
