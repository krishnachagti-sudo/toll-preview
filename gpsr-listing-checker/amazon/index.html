GPSR for Amazon sellers

Amazon's listings feed carries 4 GPSR attributes: gpsr_manufacturer_reference.gpsr_manufacturer_email_address, dsa_responsible_party_address, gpsr_safety_attestation, compliance_media; the manufacturer's and EU responsible person's contact details must first be entered in Seller Central or Vendor Central. Checked here, the worked example's LED lamp shows nothing missing.

Amazon's GPSR attributes

From Amazon's SP-API changelog (read November 8, 2026)
AttributeWhat it holdsArticle 19
gpsr_manufacturer_reference.gpsr_manufacturer_email_addressManufacturer's email (ASIN level)(a)
dsa_responsible_party_addressResponsible party's email (SKU level)(b)
gpsr_safety_attestationBoolean. True if product is exempt from safety attestation(d)
compliance_mediaDocuments: content_type (for example safety_information, user_manual, instructions_for_use), content_language (for example de_DE) and source_location (a document URL; not Google Drive or Dropbox)(d)

Manufacturer and EU Responsible Person contact information must be entered in Seller Central or Vendor Central before the GPSR attributes are updated.

Compliance documents: content_type is one of patient_fact_sheet, provider_fact_sheet, installation_manual, instructions_for_use, user_guide, user_manual, safety_information. Amazon refuses Google Drive and Dropbox links, and the checker flags them.

A listing checked

An LED desk lamp made in Shenzhen, sold into Germany. Its report carries only the emails, so the manufacturer's name and postal address are given once as shop-wide details:

{
  "item_sku": "LAMP-7",
  "item_name": "LED desk lamp",
  "gpsr_manufacturer_reference#1.gpsr_manufacturer_email_address": "compliance@lumenlamps.example",
  "dsa_responsible_party_address": "rp@dublin-rep.example",
  "responsible person": "Dublin Rep Ltd",
  "responsible person address": "7 Quay St, Dublin 2, D02 X285, Ireland",
  "main_image_url": "https://cdn.example.com/lamp-7.jpg",
  "model_number": "LL-7",
  "external_product_id": "4006381333931",
  "safety information": "Nur in Innenräumen verwenden. Nicht für Kinder unter 3 Jahren geeignet."
}
  • Nothing missing.

The manufacturer is in China, so the responsible person is needed (needed); the warning reads as German, an official language of Germany.

Questions

What does gpsr_safety_attestation mean?

Amazon describes it as: "Boolean. True if product is exempt from safety attestation".
