# Checker vs EU Ready

EU Ready's free plan scans a Shopify store for GPSR with a 0 to 100 score and an issue list, and its Pro plan (about $8.96 a month at the App Store's own conversion on November 8, 2026) assigns the details to products and shows them on product pages; the checker costs $0.01 per SKU for the first 1,000 SKUs in a month and $0.004 after, at most $14.99 a month; one listing on the page is free and reads Amazon, Etsy and eBay exports too.

## Side by side

Facts about the other choice from its own page, read November 8, 2026

|  | GPSR listing checker | The other choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0.01 per SKU for the first 1,000 SKUs in a month and $0.004 after, at most $14.99 a month; one listing on the page is free | Free scan; Pro about $8.96 a month (7-day trial) |
| Channels | Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Shopify, any CSV | Shopify only |
| Shows details on your product pages | No: label and listing text to paste | Yes, on Pro |
| Responsible person outside the EU refused | Yes, with the article | Not described on the listing |
| Warning language per market | Official EU languages of each market | Not described on the listing |

## When the other choice is better

For a Shopify-only store, EU Ready's free scan costs nothing and Pro puts the details on your product pages for you, which the checker does not do.

> This checks what your listing data shows against Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR). It does not certify that a product is safe or compliant, and it cannot confirm that an address is real or that a company is established in the EU. Each Member State decides the language of warnings. The check compares the warning language with that state's official EU languages; national rules can ask for more. Products under other EU product laws (toys, cosmetics, electrical goods and more) can need further warnings and marks that this check does not cover. Rules read from EUR-Lex and the European Commission on the dates shown. Not legal advice.
