# GPSR for Etsy sellers

Etsy keeps GPSR details in 3 places: the "Manufacturers" and "Product safety info" fields of each listing form and an Economic Operator on the account. A US maker's earrings checked here show 3 missing items: the EU responsible person's name, postal and electronic address.

## Etsy's fields

From Etsy Help (read November 8, 2026)

| Field | Where | Article 19 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manufacturers | the manufacturer's details, in the listing form | (a) |
| Economic Operator | added to the account (shop level) | (b) |
| Product safety info | safety information, in the listing form | (d) |

Etsy says GPSR applies to all types of products, both physical and digital, and lets a shop choose whether digital items can be sold in EEA countries.

To stop selling into GPSR states: Shop Manager, Settings, Options: 'Choose how you sell to GPSR states'.

## A handmade listing checked

Brass earrings made by a studio in North Carolina, sold into Germany, with a warning in English:

- Article 19(b): The manufacturer is in the United States, outside the EU, so the listing must show the name of an EU responsible person: an importer, an authorised representative or a fulfilment service provider established in the EU.
- Article 19(b): Add the responsible person's postal address in the EU.
- Article 19(b): Add the responsible person's email address (or the address of a contact form on their website).
- Article 19(d): The warnings are in English (guessed from the text), which is not an official language of Germany (German). Each Member State decides the language (Article 19(d)); add a translation for these markets.
