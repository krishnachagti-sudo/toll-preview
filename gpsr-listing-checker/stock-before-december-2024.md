# Stock from before 13 December 2024

Products placed on the EU market before 13 December 2024 under the old directive may still be sold (Article 51) without the new on-product labels, but the Commission's guidelines say the GPSR applies "to any offer of products made on that date or later", so a listing published or kept up after 13 December 2024 still shows the 4 Article 19 items.

## The texts

Member States shall not impede the making available on the market of products covered by Directive 2001/95/EC which are in conformity with that Directive and which were placed on the market before 13 December 2024.

Commission guidelines, section 2.1: "The obligations of the GPSR apply to products placed or made available on the market as of 13 December 2024, and to any offer of products made on that date or later."

And: "This means that, for example, the new requirements to affix certain traceability and product safety information to the product, or its packaging, under the GPSR do not apply to products that were placed on the market before 13 December 2024."

Placing on the market is decided per unit: a unit first sold into the EU after 13 December 2024 is new for this rule, even if the same model sold before.
