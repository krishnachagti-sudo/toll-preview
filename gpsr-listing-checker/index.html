EU product safety, per SKU

GPSR listing checker

Since 13 December 2024, every online offer of a consumer product to EU buyers must show four things (Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988): the manufacturer, an EU responsible person when the manufacturer is outside the EU, a picture with the type and an identifier, and the warnings in a language each market decides. Paste your export and see which SKUs miss what, with the article and the fix for each, a checklist CSV and a label block per SKU.

  • One listing checked on this page: free.
  • Listing file or API call: $0.01 per SKU for the first 1,000 each calendar month, then $0.004 per SKU.
  • Responsible person check for one manufacturer country: free.
  • The rule set and the Member State table: free.
  • At most $14.99 a calendar month for this tool; after that it is free until the month ends.

Do I need an EU responsible person?

Free. Give the country where the manufacturer is established.

What it checks

  • (a) Manufacturer: name, registered trade name or registered trade mark; postal address; electronic address. An email passes. A web address is flagged to check: the Commission says a website counts only if consumers can contact the manufacturer through it directly.
  • (b) Responsible person: when the manufacturer's country is outside the EU (read from a country column or the end of the address), the listing needs the name, postal and electronic address of an importer, authorised representative or fulfilment service provider established in the EU. A UK or US address is refused.
  • (c) Identification: a picture, and the type or an identifier (model, MPN, batch or serial number, GTIN). Barcodes are checked against their GS1 check digit.
  • (d) Warnings: present, or marked as not needed; their language (declared, or guessed from common words) compared with the official EU languages of each market you tick.
  • Out of scope: a row marked food, feed, medicinal, living plants or animals, animal by-products, plant protection products, aircraft, operated transport equipment or antiques (Article 2(2)).

This checks what your listing data shows against Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR). It does not certify that a product is safe or compliant, and it cannot confirm that an address is real or that a company is established in the EU. Each Member State decides the language of warnings; national rules can ask for more than its official EU languages. Products under other EU product laws (toys, cosmetics, electrical goods and more) can need further warnings and marks that this check does not cover. Not legal advice.

Sources, read November 8, 2026: Regulation (EU) 2023/988 on EUR-Lex; Commission guidelines on the GPSR for businesses (C/2025/6233); Regulation (EU) 2019/1020, Article 4; EU country pages (official EU languages).
