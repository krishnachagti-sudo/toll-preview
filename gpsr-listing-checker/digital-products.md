# GPSR for digital products

Article 3(1) defines a product as "any item", and the Commission's guidelines of 21 November 2025 (section 2.1) say that covers items "whether tangible or non-tangible", naming apps and software products; they do not single out downloads such as printable patterns or e-books.

## What the texts say

‘product’ means any item, whether or not it is interconnected to other items, supplied or made available, whether for consideration or not, including in the context of providing a service, which is intended for consumers or is likely, under reasonably foreseeable conditions, to be used by consumers even if not intended for them;

Commission guidelines, section 2.1: "The GPSR product definition is wide enough to cover “any item”, whether tangible or non-tangible or of a mixed nature. It includes apps and software products, including for example chatbots, and sets requirements for their safety."

Etsy says GPSR applies to all types of products, both physical and digital, and lets a shop choose whether digital items can be sold in EEA countries.

## What is not settled

Neither text says how a downloadable file such as a printable PDF fits Article 19 (it has no packaging, and its "picture" is the file itself). Until a text says so, list the manufacturer, the responsible person where needed, an image and any warnings, as for a physical item, or check with the market surveillance authority of your market.
