# GPSR for print-on-demand sellers

A print-on-demand seller who markets the product under the shop's own name or trademark is the manufacturer under Article 3(8), even when a print partner makes it; a US shop then needs an EU responsible person too, and the worked example below shows nothing missing once all 4 Article 19 items are filled.

## Why you are the manufacturer

‘manufacturer’ means any natural or legal person who manufactures a product or has a product designed or manufactured, and markets that product under that person’s name or trademark;

So the name, postal and electronic address on the listing are your shop's, not the print partner's, unless the partner markets the product under its own name.
