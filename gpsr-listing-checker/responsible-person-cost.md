# GPSR responsible person cost

EAS publishes GPSR authorised representative plans from EUR 199 a year for 1 product type to EUR 1,790 for 16; worked out per number of types, 10 product types cost EUR 1,206 a year on GPSR 6. Sellers on Amazon's forum have called quotes of 650 to 2,000 EUR a year expensive.
