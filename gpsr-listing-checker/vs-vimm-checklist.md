# Checker vs the vimm.be checklist

vimm.be's free GPSR checklist has 9 tick boxes about your shop's process and keeps the answers in your browser; it does not read listings. The checker tests each SKU in an export for the 4 Article 19 items and costs $0.01 per SKU for the first 1,000 SKUs in a month and $0.004 after, at most $14.99 a month; one listing on the page is free.

## Side by side

Facts about the other choice from its own page, read November 8, 2026

| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0.01 per SKU for the first 1,000 SKUs in a month and $0.004 after, at most $14.99 a month; one listing on the page is free | Free |
| What it checks | Each SKU's data | Your answers to 9 questions |
| Bulk | Up to 5,000 rows a file | Not offered (a managed service on a call) |

## When the other choice is better

Before you have an export, or to review your process (supplier imports, required fields, re-checks on update), the vimm.be checklist is free and quick.

> This checks what your listing data shows against Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR). It does not certify that a product is safe or compliant, and it cannot confirm that an address is real or that a company is established in the EU. Each Member State decides the language of warnings. The check compares the warning language with that state's official EU languages; national rules can ask for more. Products under other EU product laws (toys, cosmetics, electrical goods and more) can need further warnings and marks that this check does not cover.
