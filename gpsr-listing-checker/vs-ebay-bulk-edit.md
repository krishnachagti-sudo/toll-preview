# Checker vs eBay's bulk edit

eBay's bulk edit is free and fills up to 4 pictograms, 8 statements and 120 characters of safety text per listing, replacing what was there; it does not tell you which listings miss the manufacturer or responsible person. The checker costs $0.01 per SKU for the first 1,000 SKUs in a month and $0.004 after, at most $14.99 a month; one listing on the page is free.

## Side by side

Facts about the other choice from its own page, read November 8, 2026

| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0.01 per SKU for the first 1,000 SKUs in a month and $0.004 after, at most $14.99 a month; one listing on the page is free | Free, part of Seller Hub |
| Channels | Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Shopify, any CSV | eBay only |

| Responsible person decision | From the manufacturer's country | Not described on the help page |

## When the other choice is better

If you sell only on eBay and already know every listing needs the same safety statement, the bulk edit is free and writes it straight onto the listings. Use it to make the fixes; the checker only tells you what to fix.

> This checks what your listing data shows against Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR). It does not certify that a product is safe or compliant, and it cannot confirm that an address is real or that a company is established in the EU. Each Member State decides the language of warnings. The check compares the warning language with that state's official EU languages; national rules can ask for more. Products under other EU product laws (toys, cosmetics, electrical goods and more) can need further warnings and marks that this check does not cover. Rules read from EUR-Lex and the European Commission on the dates shown. Not legal advice.
