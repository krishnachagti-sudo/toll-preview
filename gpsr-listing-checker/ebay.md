# GPSR for eBay sellers

eBay's item disclosures hold GPSR safety information in up to 4 pictograms, 8 statements and 120 characters of additional information (no URLs), plus up to 10 product documents per listing; a bulk edit there replaces what was before.

## Check your eBay export

[Open the checker](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/gpsr-listing-checker/?platform=ebay&markets=DE%2CFR)

## eBay's fields

From eBay Help (read November 8, 2026)

| Field | Limit | Article 19 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item disclosures: Product safety, Pictograms | up to 4 | (d) |
| Item disclosures: Product safety, Statements | up to 8 | (d) |
| Item disclosures: Product safety, Additional information | 120 characters, no URLs | (d) |
| Item disclosures: Product documents | up to 10 per listing, PDF, JPG or PNG, each with a type and one or more languages | (d) |

Bulk edit: Seller Hub: select listings, Bulk edit, Item disclosures, 'Change to'. A bulk update of product safety information replaces what was there.

Export the listings first and run them through the checker, so you know which ones need the manufacturer and responsible person before you bulk edit.
