# GPSR for books and publishers

Books are not among the 9 exclusions of Article 2(2), so a book offered to EU consumers shows the 4 Article 19 items; a UK publisher's book checked here (its ISBN-13 passes the check digit) misses 3 items, all for the EU responsible person.

## A book checked

A field guide from a publisher in Bath, UK, sold into Ireland, marked as needing no warnings:

- Article 19(b): The manufacturer is in the United Kingdom, outside the EU, so the listing must show the name of an EU responsible person: an importer, an authorised representative or a fulfilment service provider established in the EU.
- Article 19(b): Add the responsible person's postal address in the EU.
- Article 19(b): Add the responsible person's email address (or the address of a contact form on their website).

The ISBN-13 is a GTIN-13, so its last digit is checked like any barcode. A book that can be used safely without warnings needs none (Article 9(7)); mark it so in your file.

## Who the manufacturer is

‘manufacturer’ means any natural or legal person who manufactures a product or has a product designed or manufactured, and markets that product under that person’s name or trademark;
