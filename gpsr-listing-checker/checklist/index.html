GPSR checklist template

What each column checks

PointItemWhat the checker tests
(a)ManufacturerName or registered trade mark present; postal address with a number and two parts; email (a web address is flagged: it must let consumers write directly)
(b)EU responsible personNeeded when the manufacturer's country is outside the EU; name, postal and electronic address; refused when the address is outside the EU
(c)Product identificationA picture URL; a type, model, MPN, batch or serial number, or GTIN; GTIN check digit
(d)Warnings and safety informationPresent or marked not needed; language declared or guessed, compared with each market's official EU languages

Sources

  • EUR-Lex: Regulation (EU) 2023/988 on general product safety (read November 8, 2026)
  • EUR-Lex: Commission guidelines on the GPSR for businesses, C/2025/6233 (read November 8, 2026)

This checks what your listing data shows against Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR). It does not certify that a product is safe or compliant, and it cannot confirm that an address is real or that a company is established in the EU. Each Member State decides the language of warnings. The check compares the warning language with that state's official EU languages; national rules can ask for more. Products under other EU product laws (toys, cosmetics, electrical goods and more) can need further warnings and marks that this check does not cover. Rules read from EUR-Lex and the European Commission on the dates shown. Not legal advice.
