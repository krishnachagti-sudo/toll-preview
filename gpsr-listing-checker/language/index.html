GPSR warning language by EU country

GPSR does not fix one language: Article 9(7) and 19(d) leave it to the Member State where the product is sold. Of the 27 Member States, 5 have more than one official EU language (Belgium, Finland, Ireland, Luxembourg, Malta), and only 2 (Ireland and Malta) list English.

What the law says

any warning or safety information to be affixed to the product or to the packaging or included in an accompanying document in accordance with this Regulation or the applicable Union harmonisation legislation in a language which can be easily understood by consumers, as determined by the Member State in which the product is made available on the market.

7. Manufacturers shall ensure that their product is accompanied by clear instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by consumers, as determined by the Member State in which the product is made available on the market. That requirement shall not apply where the product can be used safely and as intended by the manufacturer without such instructions and safety information.

The Commission's guidelines: "You must make sure that these instructions and the safety information are in a language easily understood by consumers. This is determined by the Member State on whose market you place the product."

How the checker reads the language

  • A warning-language column ("de", "de_DE", "German" or "Deutsch") is used as written.
  • Without one, the language is guessed from common words for 14 languages and from the script for Greek and Bulgarian; a guess needs at least 2 words and a clear lead.
  • Each market you tick is checked against its official EU languages; a gap is "check by hand", never "missing", because the state decides.

This checks what your listing data shows against Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR). It does not certify that a product is safe or compliant, and it cannot confirm that an address is real or that a company is established in the EU. Each Member State decides the language of warnings. The check compares the warning language with that state's official EU languages; national rules can ask for more. Products under other EU product laws (toys, cosmetics, electrical goods and more) can need further warnings and marks that this check does not cover. Rules read from EUR-Lex and the European Commission on the dates shown. Not legal advice.

Questions

Is English enough for GPSR warnings?

English is an official EU language only of Ireland and Malta. For the other 25 Member States the warnings need the language each state decides.

Which article sets the language?

7. Manufacturers shall ensure that their product is accompanied by clear instructions and safety information in a language which can be easily understood by consumers, as determined by the Member State in which the product is made available on the market. That requirement shall not apply where the product can be used safely and as intended by the manufacturer without such instructions and safety information.
