Do you need a sitemap?

Maybe not: Google says a site of 500 or fewer pages, linked well internally and without many media files or news pages, might not need one. If you do have one, check it: a five-page sitemap judged by the validator is valid, 5 URLs.

  • Made by Conyso
  • Exact, no AI
  • Official sources, dated
  • Files you upload are not kept
  • SecurityTerms

You might need a sitemap if

  • Your site is large, so some new pages may not be linked from anywhere yet.
  • Your site is new and has few links from other sites.
  • You have a lot of video or images, or appear in Google News.

You might not if

  • Your site has 500 or fewer pages that you want in search results.
  • Every important page can be reached by following links from the home page.
  • You do not need video, image or news results.

If you have one, keep it right

A sitemap that lists redirects, 404s or noindex pages sends mixed signals and shows up as errors in Search Console. Check it after a redesign, a platform change, a bulk import or a domain move.

Questions

Does a sitemap get my pages indexed?

Not by itself. Google says a sitemap helps discovery but does not guarantee that every item is crawled and indexed.

Does a sitemap improve rankings?

Google's sitemap pages describe it as a way to help discovery and crawling; they do not describe any ranking effect.
