What are the 2026 IRS mileage rates?

The 2026 business rate is 72.5 cents a mile to June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. The IRS rate page for 2026 works out amounts for common mileages, and the history goes back to 2011. Use the mileage calculator for a split year.

GSA rates for federal employee travel

The standard mileage rate is optional: actual car expenses may be used instead.

What are the 2026 trucker per diem rates?

The IRS transportation-industry meals rate is $80 a day in the continental US and $86 outside it for all of 2026. Partial days use three quarters of the rate. See the 2026 per diem page and the rate history, or use the per diem worksheet.

What are the 2026 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC reporting lines?

For payments made in 2026, you report $2,000 or more of nonemployee compensation to a payee on Form 1099-NEC. The lines for each box are below, with the dates they are due in 2027. The 2026 1099 page explains each line; the 1099 tracker checks your payees against them.

Due dates in 2027
Penalties, e-filing and backup withholding

You must file electronically when you file 10 or more information returns in total. Backup withholding is 24%.

What are the 2026 Section 179 limits?

You can expense up to $2,560,000 of equipment under Section 179 for 2026. The limit shrinks dollar for dollar once the equipment you place in service passes $4,090,000, so it is gone at $6,650,000. Work out a full schedule with the depreciation tool.

What is the 2026 federal standard deduction?

For 2026 the standard deduction is $16,100 for a single filer and $32,200 for a married couple filing jointly (Rev. Proc. 2025-32). The tax set-aside calculator uses these figures.

What are the 2026 self-employment tax rate and wage base?

Self-employment tax is 15.3% for 2026: 12.4% for Social Security on the first $184,500 of net earnings and 2.9% for Medicare on all of it. It applies to 92.35% of your net profit. The self-employment tax page shows the arithmetic.

When are 2026 estimated tax payments due?

The four 2026 payments are due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15, 2027. A payment is not owed if you expect to owe less than $1,000. See small business tax deadlines for the full calendar and the quarterly estimated tax page.

Safe harbors and interest rates

What are the 2026 IFTA return dates and late charges?

IFTA fuel tax returns for 2026 are due April 30, July 31, November 2 and February 1, 2027 (4th quarter 2026, filed in 2027). Late returns pay 0.75% interest a month, and a penalty of $50 or 10 percent of the tax due, whichever is greater. The IFTA due dates page has the details, and the IFTA calculator prepares the return.

What are the 2026 to 2027 UK sole trader figures?

For a UK sole trader the Personal Allowance is £12,570 and Class 4 National Insurance is 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270. The Class 4 page works out the amounts.

Questions

What is the IRS business mileage rate for 2026?

72.5 cents a mile to June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. The medical and moving rate is 20.5 cents and then 23.5 cents, and the charity rate is 14 cents.

What is the 1099-NEC reporting threshold for 2026?

$2,000 in nonemployee compensation paid to a payee during 2026 (1099-NEC box 1a), per the Rev. 12/2026 IRS instructions.

What is the Section 179 limit for 2026?

$2,560,000, reduced dollar for dollar once the cost of Section 179 property placed in service in the year passes $4,090,000. The cap for a sport utility vehicle is $32,000.

What is the 2026 standard deduction?

$16,100 for a single filer, $32,200 for married filing jointly and $24,150 for head of household (Rev. Proc. 2025-32).

What is the Social Security wage base for 2026?

$184,500. Self-employment tax is 12.4% Social Security up to that amount and 2.9% Medicare on all of it, on 92.35% of net earnings.
