What it must say

  • The date and the original invoice it refers to. Small Business Commissioner: "You should reference the invoice which has not been paid, or has been paid late, so your customer understands what the interest and compensation invoice refers to."
  • The period, the days and the rate, with the reference date, so the customer can check it.
  • The fixed sum as its own line.
  • A payment date.

GOV.UK: "Send a new invoice if you decide to add interest to the money you're owed."
