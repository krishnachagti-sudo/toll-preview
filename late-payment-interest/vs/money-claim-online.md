# Claim letter first, Money Claim Online next

A court claim costs a fee by the amount claimed, interest included: £4,800 unpaid since August 31, 2026 plus £176.62 statutory interest and compensation on November 8 is a £4,976.62 claim, which GOV.UK's bands put at a £205.00 fee. A claim letter costs nothing to send and often gets paid first.

- Court fee: £205.00

## Court fees by claim size (GOV.UK)

| Claim amount (£) | Fee |
| --- | ---: |
| Up to 300 | £35 |
| 300.01 to 500 | £50 |
| 500.01 to 1,000 | £70 |
| 1,000.01 to 1,500 | £80 |
| 1,500.01 to 3,000 | £115 |
| 3,000.01 to 5,000 | £205 |
| 5,000.01 to 10,000 | £455 |
| 10,000.01 to 200,000 | 5% of the claim |
| More than 200,000 | £10,000 |

GOV.UK: "The fee is based on the amount you're claiming, including any interest." And: "If you're claiming different interest rates for different periods, you'll need to work out the interest yourself and make your claim by post."

## How they fit

- First: the claim letter and interest invoice, with a payment date.
- If unpaid: a court claim, stating the interest; Money Claim Online calculates interest at one rate.
- The statutory rate for a UK business debt is one rate for the life of the debt, so it fits the online service.

## When Money Claim Online is the better route

You have already sent a letter and the debtor has not paid or replied. The court claim is the next step; Endwise only supplies the figure and the letter, not a court process.
