# Late payment interest: Xero and Endwise

Xero has no statutory-interest feature we could find on its own pages: the request "Add interest to late invoices" on Xero's ideas forum has 1,118 votes (read November 8, 2026). Endwise works the figure out, £40.18 plus £70 on a £3,200 invoice 39 days late, and gives an interest invoice you can enter in Xero as a new invoice.

- £3,200, 39 days: £40.18

## What Xero's own pages show

- Xero Product Ideas, "Sales Invoices - Add interest to late invoices (including paid/part-paid Invoices)": "Ability to add interest for the late payment. This includes part-paid too", shared by the Xero team on March 29, 2012, 1,118 votes on November 8, 2026.
- Xero UK plans on its pricing page run from £18 a month (Ignite) to £70 (Ultimate) after a six-month introductory price, excluding VAT.

## Using both

- Keep the original invoice in Xero.
- Work out the claim here (free) and download the letter and interest invoice.
- Enter the interest invoice in Xero as a new invoice with its own number, so your ledger shows it.

## When Xero alone is better

Your customer has agreed a simple fixed fee in the contract and you only need to add it as a line on an invoice that already sits in Xero; a separate tool adds nothing.
