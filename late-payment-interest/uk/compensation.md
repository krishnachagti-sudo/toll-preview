# Late payment compensation: £40, £70 or £100

A business that pays late owes a fixed sum on top of statutory interest: £40 for a debt up to £999.99, £70 from £1,000 to £9,999.99 and £100 from £10,000 (GOV.UK). On a £12,000 invoice paid 60 days late that is £100 plus £231.78 interest at 11.75%: £331.78.

- £12,000, 60 days late: £331.78
- Fixed sum: £100

## The three bands

| Amount of debt | What you can charge |
| --- | ---: |
| Up to £999.99 | £40 |
| £1,000 to £9,999.99 | £70 |
| £10,000 or more | £100 |

GOV.UK: "You can only charge the business once for each payment." The band is set by the amount of the debt, VAT included (Small Business Commissioner).

## Common mistakes

- Leaving VAT out of the amount: the Small Business Commissioner says to use the total including VAT.
- Charging the fixed sum again on the interest invoice: interest and compensation are charged only on the original invoice.
- Charging it on a disputed invoice: not allowed while the delay is due to a dispute.
- Charging a consumer: the fixed sums apply to business debts only.

## Please read

- Not legal advice. The figures follow the official rules and rates quoted, read on the date shown; a court decides any dispute.
- Business-to-business debts only: the UK Act, the Irish regulations, § 288(2) BGB and the EU directive do not cover money owed by consumers.
- Statutory interest does not apply where your contract sets its own late-payment rate (UK: a "substantial remedy"); use your own terms instead.
- No interest or compensation is worked out for an invoice paid late because of a genuine dispute.
- US public-body rules (federal, Texas, Florida local government) apply only to payments by those bodies; between businesses in the US, only the rate in your agreed terms applies, within your state's limits.

## Questions

### How much late payment compensation can I claim?

£40 for a debt up to £999.99, £70 for £1,000 to £9,999.99 and £100 for £10,000 or more, on top of interest (GOV.UK).

### Is the fixed sum per invoice?

GOV.UK: "You can only charge the business once for each payment."

### Can I claim more than the fixed sum?

GOV.UK: "If you're a supplier, you can also claim for reasonable costs each time you try to recover the debt."
