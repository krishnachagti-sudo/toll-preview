# EU late payment interest (Directive 2011/7/EU)

The directive's minimum is simple interest at the ECB main refinancing rate in force on January 1 or July 1 plus 8 points: 10.4% for July to December 2026 (ECB 2.4%), plus at least €40. On €5,000 due May 15, 2026 and paid September 30 that is €195.03 over two half-years, plus €40.

- Minimum, Jul to Dec 2026: 10.4%
- €5,000 example: €195.03

## What the directive says

- Article 2(6): "'statutory interest for late payment' means simple interest for late payment at a rate which is equal to the sum of the reference rate and at least eight percentage points;"
- Article 3(2): "for the first semester of the year concerned shall be the rate in force on 1 January of that year; for the second semester of the year concerned shall be the rate in force on 1 July of that year."
- Article 6(1): "the creditor is entitled to obtain from the debtor, as a minimum, a fixed sum of EUR 40."
- Article 12(3): "Member States may maintain or bring into force provisions which are more favourable to the creditor than the provisions necessary to comply with this Directive."

The rate changes with each half-year of the delay under the directive's wording; national laws decide the details. This calculator splits a delay across half-years for Ireland, Germany and the directive minimum.

## Questions

### What is the EU late payment interest rate?

Under Directive 2011/7/EU, at least the ECB main refinancing rate plus 8 percentage points, as simple interest, with the rate in force on January 1 for the first half-year and July 1 for the second. For July to December 2026: 2.4% + 8 = 10.4%.

### Is EU statutory interest simple or compound?

Simple: the directive defines statutory interest for late payment as simple interest.

### How much is the EU late payment compensation?

At least €40 (Article 6), plus reasonable recovery costs above it.
