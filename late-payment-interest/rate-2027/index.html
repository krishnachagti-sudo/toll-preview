Late payment interest rates for 2027

For UK debts that become late from January 1 to June 30, 2027 the rate is 8% plus the Bank Rate on December 31, 2026. Bank Rate is 4% since November 5, 2026, so it will be 12% unless the December 17, 2026 decision changes it; debts already late keep their rate (11.75% for July to December 2026).

Germany

§ 247 BGB moves the Basiszinssatz on January 1 by the change in the ECB rate since the last change. It is 1.52% from July 1, 2026, when the ECB rate was 2.4%; with the ECB rate at 2.65%, an unchanged ECB rate to the end of the year would give 1.77%, so 10.77% for B2B debts. The Bundesbank publishes the actual figure. "Er verändert sich zum 1. Januar und 1. Juli eines jeden Jahres um die Prozentpunkte, um welche die Bezugsgröße seit der letzten Veränderung des Basiszinssatzes gestiegen oder gefallen ist."

What does not change

A UK debt that became late in 2026 keeps its 2026 rate in 2027. Under the EU directive and Irish law, a delay that runs into 2027 switches to the 2027 rate for the days in 2027. This page is updated when the rates are set; the calculator refuses 2027 reference dates until then rather than guess.
