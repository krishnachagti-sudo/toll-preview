# Ireland late payment interest calculator

In Ireland statutory late payment interest is the ECB main refinancing rate on January 1 or July 1 plus 8 points: 10.4% from July 1, 2026. On a €5,000.00 invoice due May 15, 2026 and paid September 30, 2026 (138 days late), you can claim €195.03 statutory interest at 10.15%, then 10.4% by half-year and €70 fixed compensation: €265.03 in all.

- Rate from July 1, 2026: 10.4%

## What the regulations say

- Regulation 5(1): "the statutory late payment interest payable under Regulation 4 shall be the sum of the interest rate applied by the European Central Bank to its most recent main refinancing operation carried out before 1 January and 1 July in each year (in this Regulation referred to as "the reference rate") and published in the Official Journal of the European Communities plus 8 percentage points."
- Regulation 5(2)(b): "the reference rate in force on 1January in a particular year shall apply for the period commencing on that date and ending on 30 June in that year, and 1July in a particular year shall apply for the period commencing on that date and ending on 31 December in that year."
- Regulation 4(2): "The supplier shall be entitled to statutory late payment interest on the said amount outstanding for the period beginning on the day after the relevant payment date and ending on the date on which the payment of the amount due is made."

## Questions

### What is the late payment interest rate in Ireland?

The ECB main refinancing rate before January 1 or July 1 plus 8 points: 10.4% for July 1 to December 31, 2026 (ECB 2.4%).

### How much compensation can I claim in Ireland?

€40 for a debt not exceeding €1,000, €70 above €1,000 up to €10,000 and €100 above €10,000 (S.I. 580/2012, Schedule).
