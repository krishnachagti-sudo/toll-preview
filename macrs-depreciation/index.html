What each column means

Header row first. Columns: id, description, cost, acquired, placed_in_service, class, business_use_pct, section_179, vehicle, disposed_same_year. Dates are written 2026-03-14. Class is 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, qip, 25, 27.5 or 39.

  • acquired: the date you acquired it, which for a purchase is the binding written contract date. It sets the special allowance: 100% after January 19, 2025. Not needed for buildings or a class you elect out.
  • class: the recovery class you have decided on (Publication 946 Appendix B lists class lives). qip is qualified improvement property, 15-year straight line. 27.5 is residential rental property, 39 nonresidential real property; leave land out of a building's cost.
  • business_use_pct: blank means 100. Section 179 needs more than 50%.
  • section_179: the amount you elect to expense for that asset, in dollars.
  • vehicle: heavy-suv (over 6,000 lb, Section 179 cap applies), heavy-truck (over 6,000 lb with a 6-foot bed or similar), or blank. Passenger automobiles are not covered yet.
  • disposed_same_year: yes for an asset placed in service and disposed of in the same year; it is left out of the test and gets no depreciation.

The rules

How the numbers are made, from Publication 946.

Covered: calendar tax years 2025 and 2026; GDS classes 3, 5, 7, 10, 15 and 20-year, qualified improvement property, 25-year water utility property, 27.5 and 39-year real property; half-year, mid-quarter and mid-month conventions; Section 179 with the limit, phase-out and SUV cap; the special allowance by acquisition date, elections out by class, and the 2025 election of 40%.

What it does not cover

Fiscal and short years, ADS, passenger automobiles, later-year disposals, Form 4562 lines and state depreciation.

Read the full list

Refused with a message: passenger automobiles (Section 280F caps); vehicles used 50% or less for business (ADS); property acquired before January 20, 2025 and placed in service in 2026 with bonus (the published sources disagree on its rate); Section 179 above the business income you give (the carryover is not computed); property acquired before September 28, 2017.

Not covered: fiscal and short tax years, ADS and the 150% or straight-line elections, farm property, long production period property and aircraft, qualified production property, disposals in later years, Form 4562 line amounts and state depreciation.

The 40% test, across every asset

Every asset outside the building classes goes mid-quarter when more than 40% of the year's basis lands in October to December.

Read the full rule

Add up the bases placed in service in October, November and December, and the bases for the whole year. If the last three months are more than 40%, every asset outside the building classes uses the mid-quarter convention; exactly 40% stays half-year. Buildings and same-year disposals are left out. The basis is after Section 179 and personal use, before any bonus, so 100% bonus does not make the test go away.

Section 179, then bonus, then MACRS

Section 179 first, then the special allowance on what is left, then MACRS.

Read the full rule

Section 179 comes off first, within the year's limit ($2,500,000 for 2025 and $2,560,000 for 2026, reduced dollar for dollar above $4,000,000 and $4,090,000 of section 179 property; heavy SUVs capped at $31,300 and $32,000). The special allowance applies to what is left: 100% for property acquired after January 19, 2025, 40% for property acquired earlier and placed in service in 2025.

The IRS table on every row

Each year is the basis times the printed Appendix A percentage.

Read the full rule

The rest is depreciated with the Publication 946 Appendix A percentages: Table A-1 for half-year, A-2 to A-5 for each quarter, A-8 to A-12 for straight-line classes, A-6 and A-7a by month for buildings. Each year is the basis times the printed percentage, rounded to the cent; the last year takes what is left so the years add up to the basis.

  • Computed from IRS Publication 946 (2025, dated March 13, 2026) and Rev. Proc. 2025-32, rules version pub946-2025(2026-03-13)+rp2025-32+read-2026-11-02; not tax or legal advice.
  • Federal MACRS only, for calendar tax years 2025 and 2026. Many states do not follow federal bonus or Section 179; elect out of bonus for every class to get a starting point for those states, and apply your state's own rules.
  • You choose asset classes, acquisition dates and elections; this tool applies the rules to your inputs. It does not decide whether a cost is a repair or an improvement, or which class an asset belongs in.
  • Vehicles: business use is your figure, and at 50% or less the vehicle must use ADS, which is not computed here. Passenger automobiles under the Section 280F caps are not covered yet.
  • Check the result against your tax return software before filing.
