MACRS depreciation table (Table A-1, half-year)

Under MACRS with the half-year convention, 5-year property is depreciated 20.00%, 32.00%, 19.20%, 11.52%, 11.52%, 5.76% of its basis over 6 tax years, and 7-year property 14.29% in year 1 and 24.49% in year 2. These are Table A-1 of IRS Publication 946: apply each year's percentage to the unadjusted basis.

3- to 10-year property uses 200% declining balance, 15- and 20-year property 150% declining balance, each switching to straight line when that gives more. Read from Publication 946 (2025), page 71; every column sums to 100%.

How do I use the table?

  • Find the asset's recovery class (for example, computers are 5-year property and office furniture 7-year, per Publication 946).
  • Check the convention: half-year unless the 40% test puts you on mid-quarter, or the asset is real property.
  • Multiply the basis left after Section 179 and bonus by each year's percentage.

When the 40% test applies, use Tables A-2 to A-5 instead: see the mid-quarter convention.

Questions

What is the MACRS rate for 5-year property in year 1?

20.00% under the half-year convention, from Table A-1. Under mid-quarter it depends on the quarter placed in service.

Why does 5-year property take 6 years?

The half-year convention treats the asset as placed in service in the middle of the first year, so half a year falls into a sixth tax year.

What basis do the percentages apply to?

You must apply the table rates to your property’s unadjusted basis each year of the recovery period.
