# Section 179 limits for 2026, worked out

For tax years beginning in 2026 the Section 179 limit is $2,560,000, reduced by each dollar of Section 179 property placed in service above $4,090,000, so it is gone at $6,650,000; the SUV cap is $32,000. For example, with $4,500,000 of property placed in service, your limit is $2,150,000.

- Limit 2026: $2,560,000
- Phase-out starts: $4,090,000
- SUV cap: $32,000

## What are the 2026 limits?

> For taxable years beginning in 2026, under § 179(b)(1), the aggregate cost of any § 179 property that a taxpayer elects to treat as an expense cannot exceed $2,560,000 and, under § 179(b)(5)(A), the cost of any sport utility vehicle that may be taken into account under § 179 cannot exceed $32,000. Under § 179(b)(2), the $2,560,000 limitation under § 179(b)(1) is reduced (but not below zero) by the amount by which the cost of § 179 property placed in service during the 2026 taxable year exceeds $4,090,000.

## How does the phase-out reduce the limit?

With $4,500,000 of Section 179 property placed in service in 2026, the cost over $4,090,000 is $410,000, so the limit is $2,560,000 less $410,000: $2,150,000. Elect more than that across your assets and the tool refuses the run and says by how much.

## What is the business income limit?

The total cost you can deduct each year after you apply the dollar limit is limited to the taxable income from the active conduct of any trade or business during the year.

A $50,000 machine (7-year property) placed in service in March 2026, bonus elected out for the 7-year class.

| Business income | Section 179 | MACRS year 1 | Year 1 total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $30,000 | $30,000 | $2,858.00 | $32,858.00 |

## How much of an SUV can I expense?

Up to $32,000 of a sport utility vehicle's cost in 2026. A $71,000 SUV over 6,000 pounds used 80% for business has a business basis of $56,800; with $32,000 expensed and bonus elected out for 5-year property, year 1 adds $4,960.00 of MACRS, for $36,960.00 in all.

When you use property for both business and nonbusiness purposes, you can elect the section 179 deduction only if you use the property more than 50% for business in the year you place it in service.

> Passenger automobiles under the Section 280F caps are not covered by the tool. Many states set their own Section 179 limits. Not tax or legal advice.

For tax years beginning in 2027, see [the 2027 Section 179 limits](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/macrs-depreciation/section-179-limit-2027/).

## Questions

### What is the most I can expense under Section 179 in 2026?

$2,560,000, less any phase-out, and no more than your taxable income from the active conduct of a trade or business.

### What happens if my business income is lower than the Section 179 I want?

The deduction is limited to that income. With $30,000 of business income and a $50,000 machine, you can expense $30,000 and depreciate the rest: $2,858 of MACRS in year 1 on the remaining basis. The tool refuses an election above the income you give rather than work the carryover.

### Can I use Section 179 on a vehicle used partly for personal trips?

When you use property for both business and nonbusiness purposes, you can elect the section 179 deduction only if you use the property more than 50% for business in the year you place it in service.
