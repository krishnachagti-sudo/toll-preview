Bonus depreciation in 2026 and 2027

Bonus depreciation (the special depreciation allowance) is 100% in 2026 for qualified property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025, unless you elect out for a class: a $50,000 machine placed in service in March 2026 is deducted in full, $50,000, in year 1. Elect out for 7-year property and year 1 drops to $7,145 of regular MACRS.

What is the bonus depreciation rate for 2026?

100% for qualified property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025. From Publication 946:

Unless you elect out, you must take a 100% special depreciation allowance for certain qualified property (including long production period property and certain aircraft) acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025.

Until Publication 946 (2026) or the Code settles it, this tool does not compute a special allowance for that property.

How do I elect out?

You can elect, for any class of property, not to deduct any special depreciation allowances for all property in such class placed in service during the tax year.

Electing out is common when you expect higher income in later years, or for a state that does not follow federal bonus: the regular MACRS schedule is a starting point for those states.

What does the Code say for 2027?

Section 168(k)(1)(A) of the Code allows "100 percent of the adjusted basis of the qualified property" in the year it is placed in service (26 U.S.C. 168(k)). For calendar 2027, read it with Publication 946 for 2027 once the IRS issues it. For the 2027 Section 179 figures, see Section 179 limit 2027.

Not tax or legal advice. The tool decides nothing about whether property is qualified; you give the acquisition date and it applies the rate.

Questions

Is bonus depreciation 100% in 2026?

Yes, for qualified property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025. Publication 946 (2025): "Unless you elect out, you must take a 100% special depreciation allowance for certain qualified property (including long production period property and certain aircraft) acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025."

What about 2027?

The Code section itself, 26 U.S.C. 168(k)(1)(A), now sets the allowance at 100 percent of the adjusted basis of qualified property, with no phase-down year in its text. Check the Publication 946 edition for the year when the IRS issues it.

Can I take less than 100%?

You can elect out for a whole class of property placed in service in the year. For the first tax year ending after January 19, 2025 (2025 for a calendar year), you could instead elect 40%: on the same $50,000 machine in 2025 that gives $24,287.00 in year 1.

What about property acquired before January 20, 2025 but placed in service in 2026?

Publication 946 (2025) says property acquired before January 20, 2025 "must also be placed in service before January 1, 2026" to be qualified property, while summaries of Notice 2026-11 disagree on whether it gets 20% or nothing when placed in service in 2026. Until Publication 946 (2026) or the Code settles it, this tool does not compute a special allowance for that property.
