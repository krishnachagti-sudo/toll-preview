# Section 179 limits for 2027

For tax years beginning in 2027, you can expense up to $2,640,000 of Section 179 property, reduced dollar for dollar once the property you place in service in the year costs more than $4,230,000, so it reaches zero at $6,870,000. The cap for a sport utility vehicle is $33,100. The IRS set these in Rev. Proc. 2026-35 on November 5, 2026, up from $2,560,000, $4,090,000 and $32,000 for 2026.

- Limit 2027: $2,640,000
- Phase-out starts: $4,230,000
- SUV cap: $33,100

## The Section 179 limits for 2025, 2026 and 2027

| Tax years beginning in | Dollar limit | Phase-out starts | Limit reaches zero | SUV cap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2025 | $2,500,000 | $4,000,000 | $6,500,000 | $31,300 |
| 2026 | $2,560,000 | $4,090,000 | $6,650,000 | $32,000 |
| 2027 | $2,640,000 | $4,230,000 | $6,870,000 | $33,100 |

The IRS text for 2027, from Rev. Proc. 2026-35 section 3.26:

> For taxable years beginning in 2027, under § 179(b)(1), the aggregate cost of any § 179 property that a taxpayer elects to treat as an expense cannot exceed $2,640,000 and, under § 179(b)(5)(A), the cost of any sport utility vehicle that may be taken into account under § 179 cannot exceed $33,100. Under § 179(b)(2), the $2,640,000 limitation under § 179(b)(1) is reduced (but not below zero) by the amount by which the cost of § 179 property placed in service during the 2027 taxable year exceeds $4,230,000.

## How does the 2027 phase-out work?

Add up the cost of all Section 179 property you place in service in the 2027 tax year. Every dollar above $4,230,000 takes a dollar off the $2,640,000 limit:

| Section 179 property placed in service in 2027 | Your 2027 dollar limit |
| ---: | ---: |
| $4,230,000 | $2,640,000 |
| $4,500,000 | $2,370,000 |
| $5,000,000 | $1,870,000 |
| $6,000,000 | $870,000 |
| $6,870,000 | $0 |

The limit is then capped again by your taxable income from the active conduct of a trade or business (the business income limit in [Publication 946](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946)).

## Questions

### What is the Section 179 limit for 2027?

$2,640,000 for tax years beginning in 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2026-35, section 3.26).

### When does the 2027 Section 179 phase-out start?

When the cost of Section 179 property placed in service in the 2027 tax year exceeds $4,230,000. Each dollar over reduces the limit by a dollar, so at $6,870,000 it is zero.

### What is the 2027 Section 179 limit for an SUV?

$33,100 of the cost of a sport utility vehicle can be taken into account under Section 179 for tax years beginning in 2027, up from $32,000 in 2026.

### Does the 2027 limit apply to equipment I buy in December 2026?

No. The limit goes by the tax year the property is placed in service. Property placed in service in a tax year beginning in 2026 uses the 2026 limits.
