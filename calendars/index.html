Holiday calendars to subscribe to

Subscribe once and your calendar keeps itself current. The US federal holidays feed is https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/calendars/us-federal-holidays.ics, and it lists 12 holidays for 2027. There are 3 free calendars below, each a standard .ics feed that works in Google, Outlook and Apple Calendar.

Which holiday calendars can you subscribe to?

Each calendar is one feed address. Paste it into your calendar app and the holidays appear as all-day events that do not block your time. Each feed holds last year, this year and the next two years, as far as the official lists reach.

How do you subscribe in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar?

Copy the feed address (the one ending in .ics) first. Then use the steps for your app.

  • Google Calendar (computer): next to "Other calendars" click Add other calendars, choose From URL, enter the address and click Add calendar.
  • Outlook.com or Outlook on the web: in Calendar click Add calendar, choose Subscribe from web, enter the address and click Import (on a work or school account, then Save).
  • Apple Calendar, Mac: choose File, New Calendar Subscription, enter the address and click Subscribe.
  • Apple Calendar, iPhone or iPad: tap Calendars, Add Calendar, Add Subscription Calendar, enter the address and tap Find.

Google says only a public calendar address works with From URL, and Apple advises you to subscribe only to a source you trust. Menus change between versions, so check the help pages below if a label differs. Steps as of November 2026.

How often does the calendar update?

The feed asks your app to check back once a day. Microsoft says subscribed calendars in Outlook.com update about every 3 hours and in Outlook on the web about every 6 hours, and sometimes take more than 24 hours. Neither Google nor Apple gives a number on the help pages cited here.

What does an observed holiday look like?

It is one all-day event named for the weekday off, such as "Independence Day (observed)" when July 4 falls on a Saturday. The event description names the official source and says it is the observed day.

Does it cost anything?

No. The feeds are free, need no account and do not use your balance. For dates between two days, or to count business days around these holidays, use the Endwise Business days tool, which costs $0.0005 per call after 5 free runs a day.

Questions

How often does a subscribed holiday calendar update?

The feed itself asks calendar apps to check back once a day. How often an app actually does is up to the app. Microsoft says Outlook.com updates should happen about every 3 hours and Outlook on the web about every 6 hours, and can take more than 24 hours. Google and Apple do not state a figure on the help pages used for this guide.

What does "observed" mean on a holiday?

When a holiday falls on a weekend, the day off moves to a nearby weekday. The calendar shows that weekday with "(observed)" or "(substitute day)" in the name, and its description says so.

Does a holiday calendar cost anything?

No. The feeds are free, need no account and are not metered or counted against your balance.

Where do the dates come from?

From the same holiday rules the Business days tool uses, each tied to an official source: OPM and 5 U.S.C. 6103 for the US, GOV.UK for England and Wales, and the Department of Personnel and Training memoranda for India.
