# Late fees for rent due December 1, 2026

For rent due Tuesday, December 1, 2026, a late fee may start December 4, 2026 in Texas (two full days), December 7, 2026 in New York, December 8, 2026 in Colorado, December 9, 2026 in New Jersey (five business days) and December 31, 2026 in Massachusetts; Arizona, California, Hawaii, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, Virginia and Chicago set no waiting period.
