# Rent grace periods by state

13 of the 23 jurisdictions read make a landlord wait before charging a late fee. For rent due December 1, 2026 the first fee day is December 8 in Colorado, December 11 in Connecticut, December 7 in Delaware, December 7 in the District of Columbia, December 17 in Maine, December 31 in Massachusetts, December 9 in New Jersey, December 7 in New York, December 6 in North Carolina, December 5 in Oregon, December 6 in Tennessee, December 4 in Texas and December 7 in Washington; the rest set no waiting period.

- With a waiting period: 13
- Shortest: Texas, 2 full days
- Longest: Massachusetts, 30 days

Rent due on the first of the month. New Jersey counts business days (federal holidays skipped; New Jersey State holidays were not read).

## Questions

### Is there a 5-day grace period for rent by law?

Not everywhere. Of the jurisdictions read, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Maine, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Oregon, Tennessee, Texas and Washington set a waiting period, and they differ; the others set none, so the lease decides.

### Do weekends extend the grace period?

Only where the statute says so. Tennessee excuses a fee when the last grace day is a Sunday or legal holiday and rent is paid the next business day; New Jersey counts business days. The others count calendar days.
