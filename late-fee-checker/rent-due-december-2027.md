# Late fees for rent due December 1, 2027

For rent due Wednesday, December 1, 2027, a late fee may start December 4, 2027 in Texas (two full days), December 7, 2027 in New York, December 8, 2027 in Colorado, December 9, 2027 in New Jersey (five business days) and December 31, 2027 in Massachusetts; Arizona, California, Hawaii, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, Virginia and Chicago set no waiting period.
