# When to ask a lawyer about a late fee

A checker is enough to find the statute's number and first fee day in the 23 jurisdictions read. A lawyer is needed where the statute has no number (Arizona, California, Massachusetts, New Jersey and Washington), where a city or subsidy program adds rules, for a Texas fee above 12% or 10% defended as uncertain damages, and in any dispute or eviction.

- A lawyer: whether a fee is "reasonable", a lease clause's wording, local ordinances, federal subsidy program rules, eviction notices and court.
- The checker: the statute's limit and first fee day for your rent, the conditions quoted, and a notice with the fee checked.
