# Hemlane's late fee laws by state, checked against the statutes

Hemlane's free article covers every state in one line each and was last updated March 18, 2026; checked on November 8, 2026, five of its rows read differently from the statute text: Texas sets its limit by building size, New Mexico caps the fee at 10%, Washington's statute sets no amount, Nevada's sets no waiting period, and Oregon's 5% applies per five-day period.

Hemlane read November 8, 2026; statute rows read November 8, 2026

| State | Hemlane says | The statute read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Texas](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/late-fee-checker/texas/) | Gives the limit by rent: 12% over $500 of rent, 10% at $500 or less. | 12% of the rent for the period in a building of 4 or fewer units, 10% in a building of more than 4 units; only once any part of the rent has stayed unpaid two full days after the due date (Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019). |
| [New Mexico](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/late-fee-checker/new-mexico/) | Says there is no statutory limit. | 10% of the total rent payment for each rental period in default; the statute sets no waiting period (N.M. Stat. § 47-8-15(D)). |
| [Washington](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/late-fee-checker/washington/) | Says $20 or 20% of the monthly rent, whichever is greater, is deemed reasonable. | A waiting period, no amount; no late fee on rent paid within five days of the due date; after that, fees may run from the first day after the due date (Rev. Code Wash. § 59.18.170(2)). |
| [Nevada](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/late-fee-checker/nevada/) | Says a fee may be charged only if rent is more than 3 days late. | 5% of the periodic rent; the statute sets no waiting period (Nev. Rev. Stat. § 118A.210(4)). |
| [Oregon](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/late-fee-checker/oregon/) | Says $5 a day for weekly rentals and 5% of the rent for monthly rentals. | 5% of the periodic rent for each five-day period (or part) the rent is delinquent, from the fifth day of the rental period to its end; only if rent is not received by the fourth day of the rental period, so from the fifth day (Or. Rev. Stat. § 90.260). |
