# Rent software late fees vs a statute check

Rent software applies the late fee you type in. Their pricing pages, read November 8, 2026, do not say the fee is checked against a state limit, so a $200 fee on $1,500 Texas rent in a 12-unit building would be applied though the statute's figure is $150.

From each product's own pricing page, read November 8, 2026

| Product | What the pricing page says |
| --- | --- |
| [Avail](https://www.avail.com/pricing) | Free Unlimited plan, Unlimited Plus $9 a unit a month; lists "auto-assessed late fees (one-time or recurring)". |
| [RentRedi](https://rentredi.com/pricing/) | $29.95 a month, $20 a month on a 6-month plan, $12 a month billed annually. |
| [Baselane](https://www.baselane.com/pricing/) | Core $0 a month; Smart $20 a month on an annual plan. |

## When the software is better

For collecting rent and applying fees every month across many units, software automates what a checker does not. Use the checker to set the lease fee and the first fee day once per state, then enter those in the software.
