Hemlane's late fee laws by state, checked against the statutes

Hemlane's free article covers every state in one line each and was last updated March 18, 2026; checked on November 8, 2026, five of its rows read differently from the statute text: Texas sets its limit by building size, New Mexico caps the fee at 10%, Washington's statute sets no amount, Nevada's sets no waiting period, and Oregon's 5% applies per five-day period.

What each gives you

  • Hemlane: one line per state for all 50, free to read, with links to sources; no figure for your rent.
  • This checker: 21 states, D.C. and Chicago, each quoted from the statute with the date read; the limit and first fee day worked out for your rent, free on the page; other states refused.

When Hemlane is better

When you want management-company style help across the whole rental: Hemlane's own pricing page (read November 8, 2026) lists a free Starter plan and paid plans from $30 a month that include rent collection with late fees. For a state this checker does not cover, a table that covers every state is a place to start, before reading the statute.
