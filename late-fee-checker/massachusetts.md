# Maximum late fee for rent in Massachusetts

In Massachusetts the statute read sets no dollar or percent limit on a rent late fee, only a waiting period (Mass. Gen. Laws ch. 186, § 15B(1)(c)). For rent due December 1, 2026 a fee may start December 31, 2026.

- Fee may start: December 31, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

**In short:** A waiting period, no amount; from day 30 after the due date (Mass. Gen. Laws ch. 186, § 15B(1)(c)).

**The statute's words (1 passage)**

Mass. Gen. Laws ch. 186, § 15B(1)(c): "No lease or other rental agreement shall impose any interest or penalty for failure to pay rent until thirty days after such rent shall have been due."

## Questions

### Is there a limit on rent late fees in Massachusetts?

Not a number: a waiting period, no amount (Mass. Gen. Laws ch. 186, § 15B(1)(c)).

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Massachusetts?

No interest or penalty until 30 days after the rent was due. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 31, 2026.
