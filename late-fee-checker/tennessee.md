# Maximum late fee for rent in Tennessee

In Tennessee a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most 10% of the amount of rent past due (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-201(d)). A fee may start December 6, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (a five-day grace period that counts the due date itself; if its last day is a Sunday or legal holiday, rent paid the next business day carries no fee). On $1,500 rent the limit is $150.

- Limit on $1,500: $150
- Fee may start: December 6, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-201(d): "There shall be a five-day grace period beginning the day the rent was due to the day a fee for the late payment of rent may be charged. The date the rent was due shall be included in the calculation of the five-day grace period. If the last day of the five-day grace period occurs on a Sunday or legal holiday, as defined in § 15-1-101, the landlord shall not impose any charge or fee for the late payment of rent; provided, that the rent is paid on the next business day. Any charge or fee, however described, which is charged by the landlord for the late payment of rent, shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the amount of rent past due."

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Tennessee?

10% of the amount of rent past due (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-201(d)). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $150.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Tennessee?

A five-day grace period that counts the due date itself; if its last day is a Sunday or legal holiday, rent paid the next business day carries no fee. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 6, 2026.
