# Maximum late fee for rent in Arizona

In Arizona the statute read sets no dollar or percent limit on a rent late fee, only a "reasonable" fee set out in the written lease (Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 33-1368(B)). For rent due December 1, 2026 a fee may start December 2, 2026.

- Fee may start: December 2, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 33-1368(B): "Before the filing of a special detainer action the rental agreement shall be reinstated if the tenant tenders all past due and unpaid periodic rent and a reasonable late fee set forth in a written rental agreement."

## Questions

### Is there a limit on rent late fees in Arizona?

Not a number: a "reasonable" fee set out in the written lease, no number (Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 33-1368(B)).

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Arizona?

The statute sets no waiting period. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 2, 2026.

### Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Arizona?

Yes: the fee must be reasonable and set forth in a written rental agreement (Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 33-1368(B)).
