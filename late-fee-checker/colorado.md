# Maximum late fee for rent in Colorado

In Colorado a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most the greater of $50 or 5% of the past-due rent payment (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105). A fee may start December 8, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (only once a rent payment is late by at least seven calendar days). On $1,500 rent the limit is $75.

- Limit on $1,500: $75
- Fee may start: December 8, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

**In short:** The greater of $50 or 5% of the past-due rent payment; from day 7 after the due date (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105).

**The statute's words (8 passages)**

Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105: "Charge a tenant or home owner a late fee unless a rent payment is late by at least seven calendar days;"

Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105: "Charge a tenant or home owner a late fee in an amount that exceeds the greater of: (I) Fifty dollars; or (II) Five percent of the amount of the past due rent payment;"

Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105: "Require a tenant or home owner to pay a late fee unless the late fee is disclosed in the rental agreement;"

Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105: "Remove or exclude a tenant from a dwelling or initiate a court process for the removal or exclusion of a tenant from a dwelling because the tenant fails to pay one or more late fees to the landlord;"

Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105: "Impose a late fee on a tenant or home owner for the late payment or nonpayment of any portion of the rent that a rent subsidy provider, rather than the tenant or home owner, is responsible for paying;"

Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105: "Impose a late fee more than once for each late payment, except that a landlord may impose a late fee more than once for a late payment if the total amount of such late fees does not exceed the amount described in subsection (1)(b) of this section;"

Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105 (in figures; the statute writes the amounts in words, see the source): Written notice of the late fee within 180 days after the rent was due.

Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105: "A landlord who violates subsection (1) of this section shall pay to an aggrieved tenant or home owner a penalty in the amount of fifty dollars for each violation."

## Conditions in the statute

- The late fee must be disclosed in the rental agreement.
- More than one late fee for one late payment only if the total stays within the limit.
- No late fee on the part of the rent a rent subsidy provider pays.
- No eviction, and no court process for removal, because a tenant has not paid late fees.
- Written notice of the late fee within 180 days after the rent was due.

## If a fee breaks the rule

$50 per violation to the tenant; if not cured within seven days of the tenant's notice, $150 to $1,000 per violation plus damages and attorney fees (§ 38-12-105(3) to (5)).

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Colorado?

The greater of $50 or 5% of the past-due rent payment (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $75.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Colorado?

Only once a rent payment is late by at least seven calendar days. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 8, 2026.

### Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Colorado?

Yes: the late fee must be disclosed in the rental agreement (Colo. Rev. Stat. § 38-12-105).
