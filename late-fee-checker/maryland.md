# Maximum late fee for rent in Maryland

In Maryland a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most 5% of the unpaid rent due for the rental period (Md. Code, Real Prop. § 8-208(d)(3)). A fee may start December 2, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (the statute sets no waiting period). On $1,500 rent the limit is $75.

- Limit on $1,500: $75
- Fee may start: December 2, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

Md. Code, Real Prop. § 8-208(d)(3): "Provides for a penalty for the late payment of rent in excess of 5% of the amount of unpaid rent due for the rental period for which the payment was delinquent; or (ii) In the case of leases under which the rent is paid in weekly rental installments, provides for a late penalty of more than $3 per week or a total of no more than $12 per month;"

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Maryland?

5% of the unpaid rent due for the rental period (Md. Code, Real Prop. § 8-208(d)(3)). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $75.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Maryland?

The statute sets no waiting period. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 2, 2026.
