Late fees for rent due May 1, 2027

For rent due Saturday, May 1, 2027, a late fee may start May 4, 2027 in Texas (two full days), May 7, 2027 in New York, May 8, 2027 in Colorado, May 8, 2027 in New Jersey (five business days) and May 31, 2027 in Massachusetts; Arizona, California, Hawaii, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, Virginia and Chicago set no waiting period.

Business days skip weekends and the federal holidays of 5 U.S.C. § 6103(a) (and, to be safe, the Friday or Monday they are observed on). New Jersey State holidays were not read: one in the window moves the first fee day a business day later.
