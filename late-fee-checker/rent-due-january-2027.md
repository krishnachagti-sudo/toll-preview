# Late fees for rent due January 1, 2027

For rent due Friday, January 1, 2027, a late fee may start January 4, 2027 in Texas (two full days), January 7, 2027 in New York, January 8, 2027 in Colorado, January 9, 2027 in New Jersey (five business days) and January 31, 2027 in Massachusetts; Arizona, California, Hawaii, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, Virginia and Chicago set no waiting period.
