Maximum late fee for rent in Minnesota

In Minnesota a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most 8% of the overdue rent payment (Minn. Stat. § 504B.177). A fee may start December 2, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (the written agreement must say when the fee is imposed; the statute sets no waiting period). On $1,500 rent the limit is $120.

What the statute says

In short: 8% of the overdue rent payment; no waiting period (Minn. Stat. § 504B.177).

The statute's words (2 passages)

Minn. Stat. § 504B.177: "A landlord of a residential building may not charge a late fee if the rent is paid after the due date, unless the tenant and landlord have agreed in writing that a late fee may be imposed. The agreement must specify when the late fee will be imposed. In no case may the late fee exceed eight percent of the overdue rent payment."

Minn. Stat. § 504B.177: "A late fee charged by a landlord who has entered into a housing assistance payments contract with the federal, state, or local government must be calculated and assessed only on the portion of rent payable by the tenant."

The limit on sample rents

How the $1,500 figure is worked out: 8% of $1,500 (the past-due rent) = $120.

Conditions in the statute

  • Agreed in writing, and the agreement must say when the late fee will be imposed.
  • With a government housing assistance payments contract, the fee is worked out only on the tenant's portion.

Notes

  • Where a federal subsidy program's rules on late fees conflict with this section, the landlord may follow the federal schedule instead (§ 504B.177(b)); that case is not computed here.

Questions

What is the maximum late fee for rent in Minnesota?

8% of the overdue rent payment (Minn. Stat. § 504B.177). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $120.

When can a landlord charge a late fee in Minnesota?

The written agreement must say when the fee is imposed; the statute sets no waiting period. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 2, 2026.

Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Minnesota?

Yes: agreed in writing, and the agreement must say when the late fee will be imposed (Minn. Stat. § 504B.177).
