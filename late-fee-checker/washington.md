# Maximum late fee for rent in Washington

In Washington the statute read sets no dollar or percent limit on a rent late fee, only a waiting period (Rev. Code Wash. § 59.18.170(2)). For rent due December 1, 2026 a fee may start December 7, 2026.

- Fee may start: December 7, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

Rev. Code Wash. § 59.18.170(2): "The landlord may not charge a late fee for rent that is paid within five days following its due date. If rent is more than five days past due, the landlord may charge late fees commencing from the first day after the due date until paid."

## Notes

- The section read sets no amount.
- A tenant whose main income is a monthly government payment that arrives after the due date may ask in writing to move the due date by up to five days, and the landlord must agree (§ 59.18.170(3)).
- Once that day is reached, Washington's statute lets the fee run from December 2, 2026, the first day after the due date.

## Questions

### Is there a limit on rent late fees in Washington?

Not a number: a waiting period, no amount (Rev. Code Wash. § 59.18.170(2)).

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Washington?

No late fee on rent paid within five days of the due date; after that, fees may run from the first day after the due date. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 7, 2026.
