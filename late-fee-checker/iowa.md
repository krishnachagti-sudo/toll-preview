# Maximum late fee for rent in Iowa

In Iowa a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most rent up to $700 a month: $12 a day, $60 a month at most; rent above $700: $20 a day, $100 a month at most (Iowa Code § 562A.9(4)). A fee may start December 2, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (the statute sets no waiting period). On $1,500 rent the limit is $100.

- Limit on $1,500: $100
- Fee may start: December 2, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Iowa?

Rent up to $700 a month: $12 a day, $60 a month at most; rent above $700: $20 a day, $100 a month at most (Iowa Code § 562A.9(4)). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $100.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Iowa?

The statute sets no waiting period. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 2, 2026.
