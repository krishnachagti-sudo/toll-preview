# Maximum late fee for rent in Nevada

In Nevada a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most 5% of the periodic rent (Nev. Rev. Stat. § 118A.210(4)). A fee may start December 2, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (the statute sets no waiting period). On $1,500 rent the limit is $75.

- Limit on $1,500: $75
- Fee may start: December 2, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

**In short:** 5% of the periodic rent; no waiting period (Nev. Rev. Stat. § 118A.210(4)).

**The statute's words (1 passage)**

Nev. Rev. Stat. § 118A.210(4): "A landlord may charge a reasonable late fee for the late payment of rent as set forth in the rental agreement, but: (a) Such a late fee must not exceed 5 percent of the amount of the periodic rent; and (b) The maximum amount of the late fee must not be increased based upon a late fee that was previously imposed."

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Nevada?

5% of the periodic rent (Nev. Rev. Stat. § 118A.210(4)). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $75.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Nevada?

The statute sets no waiting period. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 2, 2026.

### Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Nevada?

Yes: the fee must be set out in the rental agreement (Nev. Rev. Stat. § 118A.210(4)).
