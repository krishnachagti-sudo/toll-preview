# Maximum late fee for rent in New York

In New York a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most the lesser of $50 or 5% of the monthly rent (N.Y. Real Prop. Law § 238-a(2)). A fee may start December 7, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (only once rent is unpaid more than five days after the due date). On $1,500 rent the limit is $50.

- Limit on $1,500: $50
- Fee may start: December 7, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

N.Y. Real Prop. Law § 238-a(2): "No landlord, lessor, sub-lessor or grantor may demand any payment, fee, or charge for the late payment of rent unless the payment of rent has not been made within five days of the date it was due, and such payment, fee, or charge shall not exceed fifty dollars or five percent of the monthly rent, whichever is less"

## Notes

- A cooperative may charge a shareholder up to 8% of the monthly maintenance fee if the proprietary lease provides for it (§ 238-a(2)); that case is not computed here.

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in New York?

The lesser of $50 or 5% of the monthly rent (N.Y. Real Prop. Law § 238-a(2)). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $50.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in New York?

Only once rent is unpaid more than five days after the due date. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 7, 2026.
