Four checks from the statute

Is my late fee legal?

A rent late fee is lawful in a covered state if the lease provides for it where the statute requires, it is charged no earlier than the statute's first day, and it is no more than the limit. On $1,500 rent due December 1, 2026 the limit runs from $50 (Connecticut and New York) to $450 (Oregon).

A fee that fails

New York, $1,500 rent due December 1, 2026, a $75 fee charged on December 4: For $1,500 monthly rent in New York, the most a landlord may charge as a late fee is $50, and only from Monday, December 7, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (N.Y. Real Prop. Law § 238-a(2)). Rent paid on December 4, 2026 is not late enough for a fee.

  • Paid on Friday, December 4, 2026: no late fee may be charged before Monday, December 7, 2026 (only once rent is unpaid more than five days after the due date).
  • $75 is $25 over the limit of $50 (the lesser of $50 and 5% of $1,500 = $75: $50).

Please read

  • Not legal advice. We are not a law firm, and this tool does not review your lease or your case.
  • Rules are as of the statute reading date shown with each state; a later amendment can change the answer. Rows read from a copy of the statute, not the official site, say so.
  • City and county ordinances (other than Chicago's) and federal subsidy program rules were not checked.
  • Where a statute sets no number, or asks for a "reasonable" fee, a court decides what is allowed; the tool does not.
  • States without a row are not covered: the tool refuses them rather than guess.

Steps

  1. Find the state rule. Find your state in the table. If it is not covered, its statute has not been read here.
  2. Check the lease. Most statutes allow a late fee only if the written lease (or a written notice given at signing) provides for it.
  3. Check the day. Compare the day the fee was charged with the first fee day the statute allows.
  4. Check the amount. Work out the limit on the rent (or the unpaid part, where the statute says so) and compare the total fee, daily fees included.

Questions

Is a $100 late fee legal?

On $1,500 rent it is over the limit in Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Maine, Maryland, Nevada, New York, North Carolina and Chicago, and within it in Hawaii, Iowa, Minnesota, New Mexico, Oregon, Tennessee, Texas and Virginia, if charged on or after the first fee day.

What if my state has no number?

In Arizona, California, Massachusetts, New Jersey and Washington the statute read sets no amount; a court decides whether a fee is reasonable.
