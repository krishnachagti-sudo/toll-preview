# Late fees for rent due November 1, 2026

For rent due Sunday, November 1, 2026, a late fee may start November 4, 2026 in Texas (two full days), November 7, 2026 in New York, November 8, 2026 in Colorado, November 7, 2026 in New Jersey (five business days) and December 1, 2026 in Massachusetts; Arizona, California, Hawaii, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, Virginia and Chicago set no waiting period.
