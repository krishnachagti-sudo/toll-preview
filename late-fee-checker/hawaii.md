# Maximum late fee for rent in Hawaii

In Hawaii a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most 8% of the amount of rent due (Haw. Rev. Stat. § 521-21(f)). A fee may start December 2, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (the statute sets no waiting period). On $1,500 rent the limit is $120.

- Limit on $1,500: $120
- Fee may start: December 2, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

**In short:** 8% of the amount of rent due; no waiting period (Haw. Rev. Stat. § 521-21(f)).

**The statute's words (1 passage)**

Haw. Rev. Stat. § 521-21(f): "Where the rental agreement provides for a late charge payable to the landlord for rent not paid when due, the late charge shall not exceed eight per cent of the amount of rent due."

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Hawaii?

8% of the amount of rent due (Haw. Rev. Stat. § 521-21(f)). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $120.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Hawaii?

The statute sets no waiting period. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 2, 2026.

### Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Hawaii?

Yes: the rental agreement must provide for the late charge (Haw. Rev. Stat. § 521-21(f)).
