# Late fees for rent due April 1, 2027

For rent due Thursday, April 1, 2027, a late fee may start April 4, 2027 in Texas (two full days), April 7, 2027 in New York, April 8, 2027 in Colorado, April 9, 2027 in New Jersey (five business days) and May 1, 2027 in Massachusetts; Arizona, California, Hawaii, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, Virginia and Chicago set no waiting period.
