# Maximum late fee for rent in New Jersey

In New Jersey the statute read sets no dollar or percent limit on a rent late fee, only a waiting period (N.J. Stat. § 2A:42-6.1). For rent due December 1, 2026 a fee may start December 9, 2026.

## What the statute says

**In short:** A waiting period, no amount; after 5 business days (rent due on the 1st) (N.J. Stat. § 2A:42-6.1).

**The statute's words (1 passage)**

N.J. Stat. § 2A:42-6.1: "A person to whom rent is due and payable on the first of the month upon a lease or other agreement shall allow a period of five business days grace in which the rent due shall be paid. No delinquency or other late charge shall be made which includes the grace period of five business days. As used in this section, "business day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or State or federal holiday."

## Notes

- The section read sets no amount.
- Business days skip weekends and the federal holidays of 5 U.S.C. § 6103(a) (and, to be safe, the Friday or Monday they are observed on). New Jersey State holidays were not read: one in the window moves the first fee day a business day later.
- The fifth business day after the due date is Tuesday, December 8, 2026. A New Jersey State holiday in the window (not read) would move it one business day later.

## Questions

### Is there a limit on rent late fees in New Jersey?

Not a number: a waiting period, no amount (N.J. Stat. § 2A:42-6.1).

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in New Jersey?

Rent due on the first of the month has a grace period of five business days, and no late charge may include it. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 9, 2026.
