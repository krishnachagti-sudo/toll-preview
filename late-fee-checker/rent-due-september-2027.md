# Late fees for rent due September 1, 2027

For rent due Wednesday, September 1, 2027, a late fee may start September 4, 2027 in Texas (two full days), September 7, 2027 in New York, September 8, 2027 in Colorado, September 10, 2027 in New Jersey (five business days) and October 1, 2027 in Massachusetts; Arizona, California, Hawaii, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, Virginia and Chicago set no waiting period.
