# Maximum late fee for rent in Delaware

In Delaware a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most 5% of the monthly rent (25 Del. C. § 5501(d)). A fee may start December 7, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (not within 5 days of the due date; 3 days later still if the landlord keeps no office in the county where rent can be paid). On $1,500 rent the limit is $75.

- Limit on $1,500: $75
- Fee may start: December 7, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

25 Del. C. § 5501(d): "Where the rental agreement provides for a late charge payable to the landlord for rent not paid at the agreed time, such late charge shall not exceed 5 percent of the monthly rent. A late charge is considered as additional rent for the purposes of this Code. The late charge shall not be imposed within 5 days of the agreed time for payment of rent. The landlord shall, in the county in which the rental unit is located, maintain an office or other permanent place for receipt of payments, where rent may be timely paid. Failure to maintain such an office, or other permanent place of payment where rent may be timely paid, shall extend the agreed on time for payment of rent by 3 days beyond the due date."

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Delaware?

5% of the monthly rent (25 Del. C. § 5501(d)). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $75.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Delaware?

Not within 5 days of the due date; 3 days later still if the landlord keeps no office in the county where rent can be paid. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 7, 2026.

### Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Delaware?

Yes: the rental agreement must provide for the late charge (25 Del. C. § 5501(d)).
