# Maximum late fee for rent in Texas

In Texas a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most 12% of the rent for the period in a building of 4 or fewer units, 10% in a building of more than 4 units (Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019). A fee may start December 4, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (only once any part of the rent has stayed unpaid two full days after the due date). On $1,500 rent in a building of 4 units the limit is $180.

- Limit on $1,500 (4 units): $180
- Fee may start: December 4, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019: "A landlord may not collect from a tenant a late fee for failing to pay any portion of the tenant's rent unless: (1) notice of the fee is included in a written lease; (2) the fee is reasonable; and (3) any portion of the tenant's rent has remained unpaid two full days after the date the rent was originally due."

Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019: "For purposes of this section, a late fee is considered reasonable if: (1) the late fee is not more than: (A) 12 percent of the amount of rent for the rental period under the lease for a dwelling located in a structure that contains not more than four dwelling units; or (B) 10 percent of the amount of rent for the rental period under the lease for a dwelling located in a structure that contains more than four dwelling units; or (2) the late fee is more than the applicable amount under Subdivision (1), but not more than uncertain damages to the landlord related to the late payment of rent"

Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019: "A late fee under this section may include an initial fee and a daily fee for each day any portion of the tenant's rent continues to remain unpaid, and the combined fees are considered a single late fee for purposes of this section."

Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019: "A landlord who violates this section is liable to the tenant for an amount equal to the sum of $100, three times the amount of the late fee collected in violation of this section, and the tenant's reasonable attorney's fees."

## Conditions in the statute

- Notice of the fee must be in a written lease.
- An initial fee plus a daily fee count as one late fee against the limit.

## If a fee breaks the rule

$100, three times the late fee collected in violation, and the tenant's reasonable attorney's fees (§ 92.019(c)).

## Notes

- The 12% and 10% figures are the amounts the statute treats as reasonable. A higher fee is also reasonable if it is not more than the landlord's uncertain damages from the late payment (§ 92.019(a-1)(2)); the tool cannot judge that, so it checks against the 12% or 10% figure.

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Texas?

12% of the rent for the period in a building of 4 or fewer units, 10% in a building of more than 4 units (Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019). On $1,500 monthly rent in a building of 4 or fewer units that is $180.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Texas?

Only once any part of the rent has stayed unpaid two full days after the due date. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 4, 2026.

### Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Texas?

Yes: notice of the fee must be in a written lease (Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019).
