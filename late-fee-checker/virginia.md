# Maximum late fee for rent in Virginia

In Virginia a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most the lesser of 10% of the periodic rent or 10% of the remaining balance due (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(E)). A fee may start December 2, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (the statute sets no waiting period for a written lease). On $1,500 rent the limit is $150.

- Limit on $1,500: $150
- Fee may start: December 2, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

**In short:** The lesser of 10% of the periodic rent or 10% of the remaining balance due; no waiting period (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(E)).

**The statute's words (2 passages)**

Va. Code § 55.1-1204(E): "A landlord shall not charge a tenant for late payment of rent unless such charge is provided for in the written rental agreement. No such late charge shall exceed the lesser of 10 percent of the periodic rent or 10 percent of the remaining balance due and owed by the tenant."

Va. Code § 55.1-1204(E) (no written lease): "Rent payments shall be due on the first day of each month during the tenancy and shall be considered late if not paid by the fifth of the month;"

## Notes

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Virginia?

The lesser of 10% of the periodic rent or 10% of the remaining balance due (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(E)). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $150.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Virginia?

The statute sets no waiting period for a written lease. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 2, 2026.

### Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Virginia?

Yes: the charge must be provided for in the written rental agreement (Va. Code § 55.1-1204(E)).
