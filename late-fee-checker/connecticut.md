# Maximum late fee for rent in Connecticut

In Connecticut a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most the lesser of $5 a day up to $50, or 5% of the delinquent rent payment (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-15a). A fee may start December 11, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (only on a payment made after the nine-day grace period (four days for a one-week tenancy)). On $1,500 rent the limit is $50.

- Limit on $1,500: $50
- Fee may start: December 11, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-15a: "If rent is unpaid when due and the tenant fails to pay rent within nine days thereafter or, in the case of a one-week tenancy, within four days thereafter, the landlord may terminate the rental agreement in accordance with the provisions of sections 47a-23 to 47a-23b , inclusive. For purposes of this section, "grace period" means the nine-day or four-day time periods identified in this subsection, as applicable."

Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-15a: "If a rental agreement contains a valid written agreement to pay a late charge in accordance with subsection (a) of section 47a-4 a landlord may assess a tenant such a late charge on a rent payment made subsequent to the grace period in accordance with this section. Such late charge may not exceed the lesser of (1) five dollars per day, up to a maximum of fifty dollars, or (2) five per cent of the delinquent rent payment or, in the case of a rental agreement paid in whole or in part by a governmental or charitable entity, five per cent of the tenant's share of the delinquent rent payment. The landlord may not assess more than one late charge upon a delinquent rent payment, regardless of how long the rent remains unpaid."

## Conditions in the statute

- The rental agreement must contain a valid written agreement to pay a late charge.
- One late charge per delinquent payment, however long it stays unpaid.
- Where a government or charitable body pays part of the rent, the 5% is of the tenant's share.

## Notes

- The tool counts the $5 a day from the first day after the grace period, the lower of the readings, so a fee it passes is within either reading.
- With no payment date given, the figure is the most the fee can reach; it builds at $5 a day after the grace period.

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Connecticut?

The lesser of $5 a day up to $50, or 5% of the delinquent rent payment (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-15a). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $50.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Connecticut?

Only on a payment made after the nine-day grace period (four days for a one-week tenancy). For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 11, 2026.

### Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Connecticut?

Yes: the rental agreement must contain a valid written agreement to pay a late charge (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 47a-15a).
