# Is there a 5-day grace period for rent by law?

There is no national grace period. Of the 23 jurisdictions read, 13 make a landlord wait before charging a late fee and 10 set no waiting period. For rent due Wednesday, December 1, 2027, the first fee day runs from Saturday, December 4, 2027 in Texas to Friday, December 31, 2027 in Massachusetts.

## Statute or lease?

A statutory grace period applies whatever the lease says. Where the statute sets none, a grace period exists only if the lease gives one; D.C.'s statute also honors any longer grace period the lease gives.

## Weekends and holidays

Tennessee: rent due December 1, 2027 has a grace period ending Sunday, December 5, so rent paid Monday, December 6 carries no fee and the first fee day is Tuesday, December 7, 2027. "The date the rent was due shall be included in the calculation of the five-day grace period. If the last day of the five-day grace period occurs on a Sunday or legal holiday, as defined in § 15-1-101, the landlord shall not impose any charge or fee for the late payment of rent; provided, that the rent is paid on the next business day."

New Jersey counts five business days for rent due on the first: for rent due Friday, January 1, 2027 the first fee day is Saturday, January 9, 2027. The fifth business day after the due date is Friday, January 8, 2027. A New Jersey State holiday in the window (not read) would move it one business day later.

Everywhere else the statutes read count calendar days and do not move a day that falls on a weekend.

## Questions

### Is a 5-day grace period automatic?

No. Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Maine, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Oregon, Tennessee, Texas and Washington set one in the statutes read, each its own length. In Arizona, California, Hawaii, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, Virginia and Chicago the statute sets none, so the lease decides.

### Does the grace period include the due date?

Only in Tennessee, whose statute says the due date counts as day one. New York, D.C. and Delaware count five days after the due date; Texas two full days after it.
