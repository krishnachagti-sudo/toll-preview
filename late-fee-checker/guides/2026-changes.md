# Late fee law changes, from the statute history notes

In the statutes read on November 8, 2026, the most recent changes shown are Virginia (2026), North Carolina (2025), Minnesota (2024) and Connecticut (2023). Connecticut's 2023 act added its cap; Virginia's 2026 acts and its version from July 1, 2027 leave the late-charge limit in subsection E unchanged.

## Dates to watch

- January 1, 2027 and July 1, 2027: the usual effective dates for new state laws. Every row is read again in January and July.
- July 1, 2027: Virginia's new version of § 55.1-1204 takes effect; subsection E is unchanged.
- Spring 2027: most legislative sessions end; bills are not applied until enacted, read and in force.

## Questions

### Did any late-fee limit change in 2026?

Not in the text read. Virginia's section was amended by 2026 acts and has a version taking effect July 1, 2027, but its late-charge subsection E reads the same in both versions.

### How are pending bills handled?

They are not applied. A row changes only after a person has read the enacted text on the state's site, with the date it takes effect; the checker is re-read every January and July.
