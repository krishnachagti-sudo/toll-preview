The template

[Your name and address]

[Date] / [Tenant name and address]

Notice of late rent and late fee / Re: [rental address]

  • The rent for [rental address] that was due on [due date] has not been paid in full. As of [notice date], [unpaid rent] of rent remains unpaid.
  • Under the late-fee term of your lease, a late fee of [fee] now applies. It is within the limit of [statute] and is charged no earlier than [first fee day].
  • Total now due: [rent plus fee]. Please pay by [date]. Payment: [how to pay].
  • This is a reminder of rent and a late fee owed. It is not a notice to end your tenancy.
  • If you have already paid, or you believe this amount is wrong, please contact me in writing.

Filled in: the Texas example

  • The rent for 45 Made-Up Avenue, Unit 2, Austin, TX 78702 that was due on November 1, 2026 has not been paid in full. As of November 5, 2026, $1,500 of rent remains unpaid.
  • Under the late-fee term of your lease, a late fee of $180 now applies. It is within the limit of Tex. Prop. Code § 92.019 (12% of the rent for the period in a building of 4 or fewer units, 10% in a building of more than 4 units; $180 on this rent), and is charged no earlier than November 4, 2026.
  • Total now due: $1,680 (rent $1,500 plus late fee $180). Please pay by November 10, 2026. Payment: the tenant portal or a check to Sample Landlord LLC.
  • This is a reminder of rent and a late fee owed. It is not a notice to end your tenancy.
  • If you have already paid, or you believe this amount is wrong, please contact me in writing.

The most the fee can be, by state

In Arizona, California, Massachusetts, New Jersey and Washington the statute sets no number; the notice uses the fee in your lease and still checks the first fee day.

Not legal advice. This page quotes the state statutes as read on the date shown; city rules, your lease, subsidy program rules and later changes in the law were not checked and can change the answer.

Please read

  • Not legal advice. We are not a law firm, and this tool does not review your lease or your case.
  • Rules are as of the statute reading date shown with each state; a later amendment can change the answer. Rows read from a copy of the statute, not the official site, say so.
  • City and county ordinances (other than Chicago's) and federal subsidy program rules were not checked.
  • Where a statute sets no number, or asks for a "reasonable" fee, a court decides what is allowed; the tool does not.
  • States without a row are not covered: the tool refuses them rather than guess.

Questions

Is a late rent notice the same as an eviction notice?

No. This notice is a reminder of rent and a late fee owed and says it does not end the tenancy. A notice to pay or quit follows its own state rules.

Can I copy the wording for free?

Yes. The wording on this page is free to use. The tool fills it in with the fee checked against the statute and makes a PDF for $1.50 per tenant and due date; a watermarked preview is free.
