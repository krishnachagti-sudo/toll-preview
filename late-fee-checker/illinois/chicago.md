# Chicago rent late fee calculator

In Chicago a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most $10 a month on the first $500 of rent plus 5% a month of the rent above $500 (Chicago Municipal Code § 5-12-140(h) (RLTO)). A fee may start December 2, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (the summary sets no waiting period). On $1,500 rent the limit is $60.

- Limit on $1,500: $60
- Fee may start: December 2, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

Chicago Municipal Code § 5-12-140(h) (RLTO): "If the tenant fails to pay rent on time, the landlord may charge a late fee of $10.00 per month on rents under $500 plus 5% per month on that part of the rent that exceeds $500.00"

Chicago Municipal Code § 5-12-140(h) (RLTO): "Units in owner occupied buildings with six or fewer units."

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Chicago?

$10 a month on the first $500 of rent plus 5% a month of the rent above $500 (Chicago Municipal Code § 5-12-140(h) (RLTO)). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $60.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Chicago?

The summary sets no waiting period. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 2, 2026.
