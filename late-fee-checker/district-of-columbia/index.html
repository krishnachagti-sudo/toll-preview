Maximum late fee for rent in the District of Columbia

In the District of Columbia a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most 5% of the full amount of rent due (D.C. Code § 42-3505.31). A fee may start December 7, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (only once the full rent is unpaid more than 5 days after the due date (or any longer grace period in the lease)). On $1,500 rent the limit is $75.

What the statute says

D.C. Code § 42-3505.31: "Pursuant to subsection (b) of this section, a housing provider may charge a late fee of no more than 5% of the full amount of rent due by a tenant."

Read the rest: What the statute says

D.C. Code § 42-3505.31: "A housing provider may only charge a late fee: (1) If the written lease agreement between the housing provider and the tenant informs the tenant of the maximum amount of the late fee that may be charged pursuant to this section; and (2) If the tenant has not paid the full amount of rent within 5 days, or any longer grace period that may be provided in the lease, after the day the rent payment is due."

D.C. Code § 42-3505.31: "A housing provider shall not: (1) Charge interest on a late fee; (2) Deduct any amount of a late fee from a subsequent rent payment; (3) Impose a late fee more than one time on each late payment; (4) Evict a tenant on the basis of the nonpayment of a late fee; or (5) Impose a late fee on a tenant for the late payment or nonpayment of any portion of the rent for which a rent subsidy provider, rather that the tenant, is responsible for paying."

Conditions in the statute

  • The written lease must tell the tenant the maximum late fee.
  • One late fee per late payment, no interest on it, not deducted from a later rent payment.
  • No late fee on the part of the rent a rent subsidy provider pays.
  • No eviction for an unpaid late fee.

Notes

  • An unpaid lawful late fee may be invoiced for payment within 30 days and, if still unpaid, taken from the security deposit at the end of the tenancy (§ 42-3505.31(d)).

Questions

What is the maximum late fee for rent in the District of Columbia?

5% of the full amount of rent due (D.C. Code § 42-3505.31). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $75.

When can a landlord charge a late fee in the District of Columbia?

Only once the full rent is unpaid more than 5 days after the due date (or any longer grace period in the lease). For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 7, 2026.

Does the late fee have to be in the lease in the District of Columbia?

Yes: the written lease must tell the tenant the maximum late fee (D.C. Code § 42-3505.31).
