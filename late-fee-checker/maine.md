# Maximum late fee for rent in Maine

In Maine a landlord may charge a rent late fee of at most 4% of the amount due for one month (14 M.R.S. § 6028). A fee may start December 17, 2026 for rent due December 1, 2026 (rent is late only if not paid within 15 days of the due date). On $1,500 rent the limit is $60.

- Limit on $1,500: $60
- Fee may start: December 17, 2026
- Rent due: December 1, 2026

## What the statute says

14 M.R.S. § 6028: "A payment of rent is late if it is not made within 15 days from the time the payment is due."

14 M.R.S. § 6028: "A landlord may not assess a penalty for the late payment of rent which exceeds 4% of the amount due for one month."

14 M.R.S. § 6028: "A landlord may not assess a penalty for the late payment of rent unless the landlord gave the tenant written notice at the time they entered into the rental agreement that a penalty, up to 4% of one month's rent, may be charged for the late payment of rent."

## Questions

### What is the maximum late fee for rent in Maine?

4% of the amount due for one month (14 M.R.S. § 6028). On $1,500 monthly rent that is $60.

### When can a landlord charge a late fee in Maine?

Rent is late only if not paid within 15 days of the due date. For rent due December 1, 2026, that is December 17, 2026.

### Does the late fee have to be in the lease in Maine?

Yes: written notice of the penalty (up to 4% of one month's rent) given when the rental agreement was made (14 M.R.S. § 6028).
