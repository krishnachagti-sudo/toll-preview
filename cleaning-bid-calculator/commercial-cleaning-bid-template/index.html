Commercial cleaning bid template

This commercial cleaning bid template is filled from a worked example: 25,000 sq ft cleaned 5 nights a week is 10.27 hours and $299.74 a visit, $6,494.37 a month and $77,932.40 a year.

What goes in it

  • Your company and the client, the site and the date, and how long the price holds.
  • The cleanable square feet and the service frequency (visits a week).
  • The scope: each task with what it covers (the square feet, the fixtures, the panes), so both sides agree on what one visit includes.
  • The price a visit, a month and a year, and what is not included.
  • Lines for both sides to sign and date.

The example, filled in

The client's copy of the PDF lists the tasks, the square feet or counts they cover and the prices, with signature lines; the estimator's copy adds the hours, the production rates and the cost build-up for your file.

Fill the template with your bid

Opens the calculator with this example; the proposal section below the bid prints the PDF.

Open this bid in the calculator

The bid is free on the calculator page; a proposal PDF costs $3.00 a job, a corrected one for the same job is free for 30 days, and proposals never cost more than $19 in a month.

The production rates, minutes and extras in these examples are made-up samples to show the arithmetic, not industry rates: replace them with your own timed figures. The wages are BLS figures.

Production rates, minutes per fixture or pane, extras and the target range are the figures you enter; this tool supplies no industry production rates or market prices. A bid is a business decision: check it against your own costs, your timed rates and the local market. Not legal, tax or accounting advice.
