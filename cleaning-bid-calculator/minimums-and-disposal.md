# Dumpsters, disposal and minimum charges: the lines that protect the margin

Put extras and minimums on the bid as their own lines: the worked new build's $250.00 haul-away is billed at $350.00 once overhead (12%) and margin (20%) are added, and a 1,800 sq ft touch-up that prices at $15.26 is raised to the $150.00 minimum charge.

- Haul-away billed: $350.00
- Minimum charge: $150.00

## Extras carry overhead and margin

An extra is a cost you pay for the job: a dumpster, a haul-away, a lift rental. Entered at cost, it is marked up by overhead and divided by one minus the margin, like an hour of labor: $250.00 x 1.12 / 0.8 = $350.00.

## A minimum charge for small jobs

The small touch-up, before and after the minimum.

| Item | Hours | Price |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Priced from hours | 0.45 | $15.26 |
| With the $150.00 minimum | 0.45 | $150.00 |

Travel, set-up and the visit itself cost the same on a small job as on a large one; a minimum charge covers them. The proposal shows that the minimum applies.
