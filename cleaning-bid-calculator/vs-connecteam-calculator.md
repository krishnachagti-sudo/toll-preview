# Connecteam post-construction cleaning calculator vs a three-phase bid sheet

Connecteam's free post-construction calculator asks for square feet, a debris level of 1 to 3, wage, payroll tax, overhead and profit and does not show its hours per square foot, while this calculator prices rough, final and touch-up from your own rates (the worked new build is $1,297.35) and counts restrooms and glass as their own lines.

- Worked new build here: $1,297.35

## Side by side

Their facts are from their own pages, read November 8, 2026.

|  | This calculator | Connecteam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free on the page; $3.00 a proposal PDF | Free |
| Hours from | Your production rate per task, fixtures and panes at your minutes | Janitorial: hours per day and days per week. Post-construction: square feet and a debris level; the base hours per square foot are not shown |
| Phases | Rough, final and touch-up priced separately | Not mentioned |
| Glass | A counted line, priced per pane | Only as an "additional tasks" cost |
| Document | Proposal PDF and CSV | Not mentioned on the calculator pages |

## When Connecteam is the better choice

When you already use Connecteam for crew scheduling and want a quick ballpark in the same place, or when you price by hours a day rather than by task.

> Production rates, minutes per fixture or pane, extras and the target range are the figures you enter; this tool supplies no industry production rates or market prices. A bid is a business decision: check it against your own costs, your timed rates and the local market. Not legal, tax or accounting advice.
