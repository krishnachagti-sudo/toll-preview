# ISSA 612 cleaning times explained, and using your own measured rates

ISSA's Cleaning Times is a published book of task times for estimating cleaning labor, listed by ISSA at $150 for non-members ($50 for members) in soft cover; it is copyrighted, so this calculator does not copy its times and works from rates you time yourself, such as 1,500 sq ft in 25 minutes = 3,600 sq ft an hour.

- Soft cover, non-members: $150
- Your timed rate: 3,600

## What ISSA lists

## When ISSA's book is the better choice

When you bid government or large facility contracts that ask for ISSA times, buy the licensed publication rather than relying on tables copied into blog posts. For everything else, your own timed rates describe your crew, your equipment and your buildings.

## Your own rates

[Time three visits](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/cleaning-bid-calculator/production-rates/) and turn the timing into square feet an hour; the calculator then uses it per task.

> Production rates, minutes per fixture or pane, extras and the target range are the figures you enter; this tool supplies no industry production rates or market prices. A bid is a business decision: check it against your own costs, your timed rates and the local market. Not legal, tax or accounting advice.
