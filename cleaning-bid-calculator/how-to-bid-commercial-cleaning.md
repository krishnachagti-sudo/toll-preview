# How to bid commercial cleaning jobs

Hours = cleanable square feet / production rate, plus counted tasks; price = hours x loaded wage + supplies + overhead + margin: this 25,000 sq ft office is 10.27 hours and $299.74 a visit, $6,494.37 a month.

- Per visit: $299.74
- Per month: $6,494.37
- Hours a visit: 10.27

## Steps

1. **Walk the building.** Measure the cleanable square feet by floor type, count restroom fixtures and glass, note trash points and anything special. The example: 25,000 sq ft, 16 fixtures, 6 panes.
2. **Hours from production rates.** Divide each area by your production rate and multiply each count by your minutes each: 10.27 hours a visit here.
3. **Loaded hourly cost.** Wage plus payroll burden: $17.71 plus 14.95% is $20.36 an hour.
4. **Supplies, overhead and margin.** 6% supplies, 15% overhead and a 15% margin bill the hour at $29.20.
5. **Price a visit and a month.** 10.27 hours x $29.20 = $299.74 a visit; x 5 visits a week x 52 / 12 = $6,494.37 a month.
6. **Check the $/sq ft.** $6,494.37 / 25,000 sq ft = $0.260 a month. Compare with your own range; if it is off, check the rates and scope before the price.

## Questions

### How do you bid a commercial cleaning job?

Measure cleanable square feet and fixtures, divide by production rates for hours, multiply by the loaded wage, add supplies, overhead and margin, and check the $/sq ft.

### What is a janitorial bidding calculator?

A tool that turns square feet, frequency, production rates and wages into hours and a contract price. This one also counts restrooms and glass and prints the proposal.
