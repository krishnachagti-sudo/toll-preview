# Janitorial bidding calculator

A janitorial bid is hours a visit times a loaded hourly cost: at the sample production rates below, a 25,000 sq ft office is 10.27 labor hours a visit, $299.74 a visit and $6,494.37 a month at 5 visits a week, $0.260 per sq ft a month.

- Per month: $6,494.37
- Per visit: $299.74
- Hours a visit: 10.27
- Per sq ft a month: $0.260

## What the burden includes

The itemized burden is the employer's 6.2% social security and 1.45% Medicare and the 0.6% FUTA from IRS Publication 15 (2026), plus the state unemployment (2.7%) and workers' comp (4%) entered for the example: those two are samples, and yours depend on your state, class code and claims. Or pick the BLS figure for private service occupations, 5.47 of benefits on 18.26 of wages an hour worked in June 2026.

The bid is free on the calculator page; a proposal PDF costs $3.00 a job, a corrected one for the same job is free for 30 days, and proposals never cost more than $19 in a month.

> Production rates, minutes per fixture or pane, extras and the target range are the figures you enter; this tool supplies no industry production rates or market prices. A bid is a business decision: check it against your own costs, your timed rates and the local market. Not legal, tax or accounting advice.

## Questions

### How do I estimate office cleaning prices?

Add up the hours a visit from your production rates (10.27 here), multiply by the billed rate ($29.20 an hour) and by the visits in a month (21.67 at 5 a week).

### How is a monthly price worked out from a nightly visit?

Visit price times visits a week times 52 weeks over 12 months: $299.74 x 5 x 52 / 12 = $6,494.37.
