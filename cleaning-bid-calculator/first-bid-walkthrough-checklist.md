## Steps

1. **Square feet by floor type.** Carpet, hard floor and other surfaces measured or taken from the plans, less areas not cleaned (mechanical rooms, storage).
2. **Restrooms and fixtures.** Each restroom's toilets, urinals, sinks and partitions counted.
3. **Glass and high work.** Entrance and interior glass counted by pane; anything above reach noted.
4. **Trash and other counted tasks.** Desks and bins, break rooms, kitchens, stairwells, elevators.
5. **Access and hours.** When the crew may work, keys and alarm codes, parking, where supplies can be stored.
6. **Supplies.** Who provides paper, soap and liners, and whether they are billed at cost.
7. **After the first month.** Time the first visits and compare the hours with the bid; adjust the rates you use next time.
