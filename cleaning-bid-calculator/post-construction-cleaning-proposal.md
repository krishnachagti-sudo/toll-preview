# Post-construction cleaning proposal template

This post-construction cleaning proposal prices 12,000 sq ft at $1,297.35 across three phases: rough clean $519.57, final clean $676.04, touch-up clean $101.74.

- Job total: $1,297.35
- Rough clean: $519.57
- Final clean: $676.04
- Touch-up clean: $101.74

## What the proposal holds

- Who it is from and for, the job or site, the date and how long the price holds.
- The cleanable square feet and the phases included.
- A price box per phase and the total, and the $/sq ft it comes to.
- The scope of each phase, line by line: area tasks with their square feet, restrooms with the fixture count, glass with the pane count, and extras such as haul-away.
- Inclusions and exclusions in your own words (high glass, exterior, pressure washing).
- Acceptance lines for the general contractor and for you; each phase billed when complete.
