# Pricing post-construction cleaning in three phases: rough, final and touch-up

Price rough, final and touch-up as three lines built from their own hours: in the worked 12,000 sq ft new build they are $519.57 (40.05%), $676.04 (52.11%), $101.74 (7.84%), $1,297.35 in all, so cancelling the touch-up takes $101.74 off and nothing else moves.

- Rough clean: $519.57
- Final clean: $676.04
- Touch-up clean: $101.74

## The three lines

From the worked example's sample rates.

| Phase | Hours | Price | Share | Per sq ft |

| Rough clean | 5.00 | $519.57 | 40.05% | $0.043 |
| Final clean | 19.93 | $676.04 | 52.11% | $0.056 |
| Touch-up clean | 3.00 | $101.74 | 7.84% | $0.0085 |

## Why separate lines

- The phases happen at different times, often weeks apart, and are invoiced when each is done.
- A general contractor may drop one (the rough clean is sometimes done by laborers) without asking you to re-price the rest.
- Each phase has its own speed: a rough pass covers ground fast, the final detail clean is the slowest.

## Define the final clean in writing

List what the final clean includes and excludes on the proposal: inside and outside glass, high glass above reach, appliance interiors, floor finish, exterior walks. A dispute over "final" is a dispute over scope, not price.

## Questions

### What are the phases of post-construction cleaning?

Rough clean, final clean and touch-up, usually priced as separate line items.

### Which phase costs the most?

The final clean, because it holds the detail work, restrooms and glass: $676.04 of $1,297.35 (52.11%) in the worked example.
