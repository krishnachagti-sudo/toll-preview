## What changes the minutes

- Inside only, or inside and out.
- Stickers, labels, tape, paint overspray and mortar on the glass and frames.
- Height: panes above reach need a ladder, a lift or a water-fed pole, which is slower and may need a rented lift as an extra.
- Frames, tracks and sills cleaned with the glass, or not.

## Questions

### Do I charge windows separately on a new build?

List them as their own line: the pane count changes the hours far more than the floor area does. In the example, 48 panes at 6 min each is $162.79 of the final clean.

### How much to charge for post-construction window cleaning?

Minutes per pane times panes, over 60, times your billed hourly rate. At $33.91 an hour, 6 minutes a pane is $3.39 a pane.
