## Steps

1. **Rough clean.** Remove debris, packaging and offcuts; sweep or vacuum all floors; first dusting of ledges and frames; empty the site bins. Arrange the dumpster or haul-away.
2. **Final clean: surfaces.** Dust every surface top to bottom, wipe cabinets, doors and frames, vents and light fixtures, remove stickers and protective film.
3. **Final clean: restrooms and kitchens.** Fixtures, mirrors and partitions; grout haze and caulk smears; dispensers; appliances inside and out.
4. **Final clean: glass.** Every pane inside (and outside if in scope), with labels, tape and paint specks removed; tracks and sills.
5. **Final clean: floors.** Vacuum carpet, mop hard floors, remove adhesive and paint drips, in the order that keeps finished floors clean.
6. **Touch-up.** After the punch list: dust and marks from the last trades, floors once more, a walk with the general contractor.
