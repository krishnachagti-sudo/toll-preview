# Commercial cleaning price per square foot

Commercial cleaning price per square foot is your price divided by the cleanable square feet: the worked office bid is $299.74 a visit for 25,000 sq ft, $0.012 a visit and $0.260 per sq ft a month; work out the hours first and use $/sq ft as the check.

- Per sq ft a month: $0.260
- Per sq ft a visit: $0.012

## What a rate and an hourly price come to per square foot

Per visit: billed dollars an hour / square feet an hour. A grid of inputs to read across, not market prices.

Read it both ways: a crew covering 2,500 sq ft an hour, billed at $40 an hour, is $0.016 per sq ft a visit. A quote far below what your rate and hourly price give means the hours are short, or the scope is.

## Set your own range

Ranges quoted online come from cleaning companies' blogs and forums, years apart and for different scopes. The calculator's check compares your bid with a range you set yourself, from your past jobs or your market, and shows the break-even $/sq ft (your costs with no profit) beside it.

## Questions

### What is the commercial cleaning price per square foot?

Your bid divided by the cleanable square feet. In the worked example it is $0.012 a visit and $0.260 a month.

### Should I bid cleaning by square foot or by production rate?

Work out the hours from square feet and production rates first, then divide the price by the square feet as a check: $6,494.37 / 25,000 = $0.260 a month.
