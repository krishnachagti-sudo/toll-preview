# Jobber quotes vs a production-rate cleaning bid

Jobber's pricing page lists Core at $29 a month billed annually, $39 on a one-year commitment and $49 with no commitment, with professional quotes included; this calculator works out the price a quote should carry from your production rates (the worked office is 10.27 hours and $299.74 a visit) and prints it for $3.00 a job.

- Jobber Core, a month billed yearly: $29
- Proposal PDF here: $3.00

## Side by side

Their facts are from their own pages, read November 8, 2026.

|  | This calculator | Jobber |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $3.00 a proposal; the bid itself free | Core $29/mo billed annually, $39 on a 1-year commitment, $49 no commitment (1 user) |
| Works out hours | Yes, from your rates, fixtures and panes | Quotes take line items you price |
| Scheduling and invoicing | No | Yes |
| Trial | The bid itself is free | 14-day free trial, no credit card |

## When Jobber is the better choice

When you need quotes, scheduling, invoicing and payments for many small recurring jobs in one app, and you already know your prices.

> Production rates, minutes per fixture or pane, extras and the target range are the figures you enter; this tool supplies no industry production rates or market prices. A bid is a business decision: check it against your own costs, your timed rates and the local market. Not legal, tax or accounting advice.
