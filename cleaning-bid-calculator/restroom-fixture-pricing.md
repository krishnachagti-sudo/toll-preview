# Restroom cleaning: how to bid by fixture instead of by square foot

Count the fixtures and price them by your minutes each: a 6-fixture restroom at 4 minutes a fixture is 0.40 hours, $11.68 a visit ($1.95 a fixture) at $29.20 an hour billed, and $253.07 a month at 5 visits a week.

- 6 fixtures a visit: $11.68
- Per fixture: $1.95

## Why fixtures, not square feet

Two restrooms of the same floor area can hold 3 fixtures or 10. Toilets, urinals, sinks and partitions take the time, so count them on the walk-through and give each its minutes. Consumables (paper, soap, liners) are a supplies cost, or billed at cost if the client asks.
