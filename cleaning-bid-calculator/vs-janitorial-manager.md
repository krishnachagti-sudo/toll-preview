# Janitorial Manager vs a pay-per-bid cleaning bid sheet

Janitorial Manager's pricing page lists its Small Operations plan as starting at $1,000 a month with demos and custom quotes, while this calculator works out a bid free on the page (the worked office is $6,494.37 a month) and charges $3.00 for a proposal PDF.

- Janitorial Manager from, a month: $1,000
- Proposal PDF here: $3.00

## Side by side

Their facts are from their own pages, read November 8, 2026.

|  | This calculator | Janitorial Manager |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Bid free on the page; $3.00 a proposal PDF | Small Operations "$1,000/mo starting"; larger plans by custom quote |
| What it is | A bid calculator and proposal | Operations software for building service contractors |
| Production rates | Yours, entered per task | Bidding tools are not described on the pricing page |
| Trial | The bid itself is free | A personalized demo; no free trial listed |

## When Janitorial Manager is the better choice

When you run many recurring janitorial contracts and need scheduling, inspections, staff and bid history in one system across buildings and sites.

> Production rates, minutes per fixture or pane, extras and the target range are the figures you enter; this tool supplies no industry production rates or market prices. A bid is a business decision: check it against your own costs, your timed rates and the local market. Not legal, tax or accounting advice.
