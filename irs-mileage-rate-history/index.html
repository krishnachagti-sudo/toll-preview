IRS mileage rate history

The IRS business mileage rate is 76 cents a mile for July 1 to December 31, 2026, up from 72.5 cents in January 1 to June 30, 2026. The table below lists every rate since 2011: business, medical and moving, and charity. Mid-year changes: July 1, 2026, July 1, 2022, July 1, 2011.

Business, July 1 to December 31, 2026
76 cents
Medical or moving
23.5 cents
Charity
14 cents

What was the IRS mileage rate each year?

Rates are in cents per mile, newest first. The charity rate is set by law and has not changed in the table. The rate for a year is announced by the IRS the autumn before, and a period that starts mid-year gets its own row. The 2026 rate page works out amounts for common mileages.

Which years had a mid-year change?

  • July 1, 2026: business rate 72.5 to 76 cents, medical and moving 20.5 to 23.5 cents (IR-2026-29, Announcement 2026-11).
  • July 1, 2022: business rate 58.5 to 62.5 cents, medical and moving 18 to 22 cents (IR-2022-124, Announcement 2022-13).
  • July 1, 2011: business rate 51 to 55.5 cents, medical and moving 19 to 23.5 cents (IR-2011-69, Announcement 2011-40).

When the rate changes in July, miles driven on or after the change use the new rate, and an employer allowance is keyed to the date it was paid as the IRS announcement says. The rate page for the year shows the rule.

Is there a per diem history like this?

The trucker per diem rate history is the counterpart for meals: it lists each IRS transportation-industry rate by notice. For a mileage log, use the mileage log template.

Can I use these rates in a calculation?

The mileage reimbursement calculator prices each trip at the rate in force on its date from this table and names the IRS source. The table above can be downloaded as a CSV from the link at the bottom of the page.

  • IRS: Standard mileage rates (Mileage rates for all years) (page updated July 28, 2026, read October 31, 2026)
  • IRS: Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-29 (July 13, 2026), Announcement 2026-11
  • IRS: Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-4 (January 20, 2026), Notice 2026-10

The standard mileage rate is optional: actual car expenses may be used instead. Not tax advice.

Questions

What is the IRS mileage rate for 2026?

76 cents a business mile from July 1, 2026, 23.5 cents for medical and moving and 14 cents for charity (IR-2026-29).

When does the IRS change the mileage rate in the middle of a year?

Rarely. The table records 3 mid-year changes, starting July 1, 2026, July 1, 2022, July 1, 2011. Each is announced by the IRS with a rule for which miles and allowances use the new rate.

What was the highest IRS business mileage rate?

76 cents a mile, for July 1 to December 31, 2026. The lowest in the table was 51 cents, for January 1 to June 30, 2011.

What was the biggest one-step change in the business rate?

4.5 cents up, from 51 to 55.5 cents, starting July 1, 2011.
