# US tariff changes calendar, 2026-2027

The next published US tariff changes after November 8, 2026: the China product exclusions end after November 9, 2026, chassis and gantry cranes from China go to 100% on November 10, polysilicon gets 15% on December 4, 2026, and Nicaragua goes to 10% on January 1, 2027.

Only changes already published in the HTS or the Federal Register; new actions are added when they are read.

| Date | Change | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November 9, 2026 | Last day of the Section 301 China product exclusions in headings 9903.88.69 and 9903.88.70 | USITC HTS |
| November 10, 2026 | Section 301 China: 100% on intermodal chassis and parts (9903.91.12) and on ship-to-shore gantry cranes (9903.91.14) | USITC HTS |
| December 4, 2026 | Section 232: 15% on polysilicon and its derivatives in the proclamation's Annexes | FR 2026-16400 |
| January 1, 2027 | Section 301 Nicaragua rises from 0% to 10% (goods not originating under CAFTA-DR) | FR 2025-22690 |
| October 1, 2027 | Fiscal year 2028 starts: CBP adjusts user fees such as the MPF minimum and maximum each fiscal year (fiscal year 2027: $34.58 and $670.86) | FR 2026-15530 |
| January 1, 2028 | Section 301 Nicaragua rises to 15% | FR 2025-22690 |

> A new HTS revision is published several times a year (the USITC release list names each one); the estimator states the revision it used in every answer.

> Estimate, not a customs ruling: U.S. Customs and Border Protection decides the classification, value, origin and duty at entry, and the importer is responsible for them. This is not legal or customs advice. Rates are from the USITC 2026 HTS Revision 22 (effective 10/30/2026) and the Federal Register actions read on 2026-11-08. A layer we have not read is refused, never guessed: Section 232, the section 338 duties on Canada, Russia, safeguards and quotas are not computed, and antidumping and countervailing duties are never included.
