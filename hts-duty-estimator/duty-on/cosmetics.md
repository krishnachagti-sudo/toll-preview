# US import duty on cosmetics, by origin

Cosmetics under HTS 3304.10.00 pay no base duty; with the additional duties in force on November 8, 2026, a $100 shipment from China pays $37.50 (37.5%), from Vietnam $12.50 (12.5%), and the lowest of the 20 origins below is Nepal at 0%.

- HTS: 3304.10.00
- From China: 37.5%
- From Vietnam: 12.5%

## The lines

- 3304.10.00: Beauty or make-up preparations and preparations for the care of the skin (other than medicaments), including sunscreen or sun tan preparations; manicure or pedicure preparations: Lip make-up preparations (general rate Free).
- 3304.20.00: Beauty or make-up preparations and preparations for the care of the skin (other than medicaments), including sunscreen or sun tan preparations; manicure or pedicure preparations: Eye make-up preparations (general rate Free).
- 3304.91.00: Beauty or make-up preparations and preparations for the care of the skin (other than medicaments), including sunscreen or sun tan preparations; manicure or pedicure preparations: Other: Powders, whether or not compressed (general rate Free).
- 3304.99.50: Beauty or make-up preparations and preparations for the care of the skin (other than medicaments), including sunscreen or sun tan preparations; manicure or pedicure preparations: Other: Other: Other (general rate Free).

> Lines 3304.30.00 (manicure preparations) and 3304.99.50 are named in U.S. note 19 (Section 232, aluminum), so the estimator refuses them.

## Questions

### What is the US duty on cosmetics from China?

37.5% of value under HTS 3304.10.00 on November 8, 2026: 9903.88.03 25% + 9903.05.31 12.5%.

### Who decides the HTS code?

The importer classifies the goods and is responsible for it; U.S. Customs and Border Protection can issue a binding ruling.
