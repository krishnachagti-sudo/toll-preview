# US import duty on hats and knit caps, by origin

Hats and knit caps under HTS 6505.00.15 pay a base duty of 7.9%; with the additional duties in force on November 8, 2026, a $100 shipment from China pays $45.40 (45.4%), from Vietnam $20.40 (20.4%), and the lowest of the 20 origins below is Nepal at 7.9%.

- HTS: 6505.00.15
- From China: 45.4%
- From Vietnam: 20.4%

## The lines

- 6505.00.15: Hats and other headgear, knitted or crocheted, or made up from lace, felt or other textile fabric, in the piece (but not in strips), whether or not lined or trimmed; hair-nets of any material, whether or not lined or trimmed: Other: Other: Of cotton, flax or both: Knitted (general rate 7.9%).
- 6505.00.20: Hats and other headgear, knitted or crocheted, or made up from lace, felt or other textile fabric, in the piece (but not in strips), whether or not lined or trimmed; hair-nets of any material, whether or not lined or trimmed: Other: Other: Of cotton, flax or both: Not knitted: Certified hand-loomed and folklore products; and headwear of cotton (general rate 7.5%).
- 6505.00.50: Hats and other headgear, knitted or crocheted, or made up from lace, felt or other textile fabric, in the piece (but not in strips), whether or not lined or trimmed; hair-nets of any material, whether or not lined or trimmed: Other: Other: Of man-made fibers: Knitted or crocheted or made up from knitted or crocheted fabric: Wholly or in part of braid (general rate 6.8%).

> Hats of wool and of man-made fibers carry a rate per kilogram plus a percentage; the estimator needs the weight for those lines (6505.00.30, .40, .60, .80, .90).

## Questions

### What is the US duty on hats and knit caps from China?

### Who decides the HTS code?

The importer classifies the goods and is responsible for it; U.S. Customs and Border Protection can issue a binding ruling.
