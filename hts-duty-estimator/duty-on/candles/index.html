US import duty on candles, by origin

Candles under HTS 3406.00.00 pay no base duty; with the additional duties in force on November 8, 2026, a $100 shipment from China pays $20.00 (20%), from Vietnam $12.50 (12.5%), and the lowest of the 20 origins below is Nepal at 0%.

Duty by origin on a $100 shipment

Nepal is not one of the 60 economies in the Section 301 forced-labor action, so it pays the base rate only. Trade agreement rates are not claimed in this table; the estimator applies them when you tick "Claim the trade agreement rate".

Estimate, not a customs ruling: U.S. Customs and Border Protection decides the classification, value, origin and duty at entry, and the importer is responsible for them. This is not legal or customs advice. Rates are from the USITC 2026 HTS Revision 22 (effective 10/30/2026) and the Federal Register actions read on 2026-11-08. A layer we have not read is refused, never guessed: Section 232, the section 338 duties on Canada, Russia, safeguards and quotas are not computed, and antidumping and countervailing duties are never included.

Questions

What is the US duty on candles from China?

20% of value under HTS 3406.00.00 on November 8, 2026: 9903.88.15 7.5% + 9903.05.31 12.5%.

Who decides the HTS code?

The importer classifies the goods and is responsible for it; U.S. Customs and Border Protection can issue a binding ruling.
