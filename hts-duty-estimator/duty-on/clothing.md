# US import duty on clothing, by origin

Clothing under HTS 6109.10.00 pay a base duty of 16.5%; with the additional duties in force on November 8, 2026, a $100 shipment from China pays $36.50 (36.5%), from Vietnam $29.00 (29%), and the lowest of the 20 origins below is Germany at 16.5%.

- HTS: 6109.10.00
- From China: 36.5%
- From Vietnam: 29%

## Questions

### What is the US duty on clothing from China?

36.5% of value under HTS 6109.10.00 on November 8, 2026: 9903.88.15 7.5% + 9903.05.31 12.5%.

### Who decides the HTS code?

The importer classifies the goods and is responsible for it; U.S. Customs and Border Protection can issue a binding ruling.
