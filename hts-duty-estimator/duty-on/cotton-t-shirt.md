# US import duty on cotton T-shirts, by origin

Cotton T-shirts under HTS 6109.10.00 pay a base duty of 16.5%; with the additional duties in force on November 8, 2026, a $100 shipment from China pays $36.50 (36.5%), from Vietnam $29.00 (29%), and the lowest of the 20 origins below is Germany at 16.5%.
