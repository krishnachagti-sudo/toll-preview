# IEEPA tariff refunds: what to check

IEEPA duties ended under Executive Order 14389 (February 20, 2026); to get back IEEPA duties already paid, the importer of record (or its customs broker) files a CAPE Declaration, a CSV of up to 9,999 entry numbers, in the ACE Portal, and CBP says valid refunds generally arrive 60 to 90 days after acceptance.

## What CBP processes now

- Phase 1: certain unliquidated entries and certain entries within 80 days of liquidation.
- Phase 2: entries flagged for reconciliation where the reconciliation entry is not yet on file.

Once a declaration is accepted, ACE removes the IEEPA Chapter 99 provision and its duties from each entry summary line; the entries are then liquidated or reliquidated, and over- and under-payments on the whole entry are netted.

> This page does not cover liquidated entries outside the phases, protests or court cases: ask a licensed customs broker or an attorney.

> Estimate, not a customs ruling: U.S. Customs and Border Protection decides the classification, value, origin and duty at entry, and the importer is responsible for them. This is not legal or customs advice. Rates are from the USITC 2026 HTS Revision 22 (effective 10/30/2026) and the Federal Register actions read on 2026-11-08. A layer we have not read is refused, never guessed: Section 232, the section 338 duties on Canada, Russia, safeguards and quotas are not computed, and antidumping and countervailing duties are never included.

## Steps

1. **Check you are the importer of record.** Only the importer of record for the entries, or the customs broker that filed them for it, may file the CAPE Declaration.
2. **Open an ACE Portal account.** Importers and brokers need an ACE Secure Data Portal account; CBP has web forms to apply and to manage sub-accounts.
3. **Add refund bank details.** Give CBP the bank account for ACH refunds in the ACE Portal (the Importer sub-account).
4. **List the entries.** A CAPE Declaration is a CSV of entry numbers, up to 9,999 per declaration, uploaded in the CAPE tab of the ACE Portal; several declarations are allowed.
5. **Wait for the refund.** CBP says valid refunds are generally issued within 60 to 90 days after a declaration is accepted, consolidated by importer and liquidation date, with interest.

## Questions

### Does CBP charge a fee for an IEEPA refund?

No. CBP says it charges no fees to process tariff refunds, and that a request for a fee or financial information from a party claiming to be CBP is a scam.

### Can a seller who did not import claim it?

CBP says only the importer of record for the entries, or its authorized customs broker, may file the CAPE Declaration.
