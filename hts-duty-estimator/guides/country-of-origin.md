# Country of origin for US duty

The country of origin is where goods were manufactured, produced or grown, and further work elsewhere changes it only by a substantial transformation (19 CFR 134.1(b)); it matters because the Section 301 layer follows it: the same $100 of cotton T-shirts pays $36.50 from China and $16.50 from Nepal.

- Made in one country, shipped from another: the origin is where it was made.
- Sewn in Bangladesh from Chinese fabric: Bangladesh, if the sewing is a substantial transformation.
- For goods of Canada or Mexico, CBP's part 102 rules decide the origin.
- Not sure: CBP issues binding origin rulings.

> Estimate, not a customs ruling: U.S. Customs and Border Protection decides the classification, value, origin and duty at entry, and the importer is responsible for them. This is not legal or customs advice. Rates are from the USITC 2026 HTS Revision 22 (effective 10/30/2026) and the Federal Register actions read on 2026-11-08. A layer we have not read is refused, never guessed: Section 232, the section 338 duties on Canada, Russia, safeguards and quotas are not computed, and antidumping and countervailing duties are never included.

## Questions

### What is the country of origin of a handmade item?

Where it was made. If you finish an item in another country, that country becomes the origin only if the work is a substantial transformation (19 CFR 134.1(b)); for goods of Canada and Mexico, the part 102 rules decide.

### Is the origin where I bought the goods?

No: buying or shipping from a country does not make it the origin.
