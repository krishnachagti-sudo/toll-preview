# Tariff calculator: Canada to USA

Goods from Canada pay the HTS base rate plus 10% under Section 301 (heading 9903.05.29), as of November 8, 2026. Goods that qualify for USMCA and are entered under it are exempt from the Section 301 duty (U.S. note 52(g)); the section 338 duties on Canadian goods are not computed, so lines they name get no estimate. For example, candles (HTS 3406.00.00) worth $100 pay no estimate.

- Section 301: 10%
- Heading: 9903.05.29
- As of: November 8, 2026

## Not applied

- IEEPA duties: ended by Executive Order 14389 (February 20, 2026).
- The section 122 surcharge of 10%: imposed for 150 days from February 24, 2026.
- Antidumping and countervailing duties: set per producer, never included.
- Section 338 duties on Canadian alcoholic beverages, dairy and motor vehicles and the other goods in U.S. note 51: refused, and chapters 04, 22 and 87 are refused because the October 30, 2026 scope change was published as images.

> Estimate, not a customs ruling: U.S. Customs and Border Protection decides the classification, value, origin and duty at entry, and the importer is responsible for them. This is not legal or customs advice. Rates are from the USITC 2026 HTS Revision 22 (effective 10/30/2026) and the Federal Register actions read on 2026-11-08. A layer we have not read is refused, never guessed: Section 232, the section 338 duties on Canada, Russia, safeguards and quotas are not computed, and antidumping and countervailing duties are never included.

## Questions

### What is the tariff on goods from Canada in 2026?

The HTS base rate of the goods' line plus 10% under Section 301 (heading 9903.05.29), as of November 8, 2026.

### Are the IEEPA tariffs still collected?

No. Executive Order 14389 of February 20, 2026 ended the IEEPA additional duties; their headings are still printed in the HTS.
