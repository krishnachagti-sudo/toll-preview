What goes into each estimate

The base rate of the HTS line from the USITC's current revision, then each Section 301 duty that applies to the origin, and for a shipment the entry type, the merchandise processing fee and the harbor maintenance fee. A layer we have not verified is refused, never guessed.

What is computed
  • The base rate of the HTS line, from the USITC's current revision: column 1 general, the special rate when you claim a trade agreement the line lists for that origin, or column 2 for Cuba, North Korea, Belarus and Russia.
  • Section 301 duties on goods of 60 economies (10 or 12.5 percent, or topped up to 10 or 12.5 percent for the EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland and Taiwan), with every exemption list in U.S. note 52.
  • Section 301 on goods of China (lists 1, 2, 3 and 4A and the 2024 increases), Brazil (25 percent) and Nicaragua.
  • For a shipment: formal or informal entry, the merchandise processing fee for fiscal year 2027 and, by ocean, the harbor maintenance fee.
Sources, checked each day

Checked each day for changes: the USITC HTS release list and the Federal Register's newest USTR, presidential and CBP documents.

Each answer names the HTS revision and the date each source was read.

Prices
  • One SKU estimated on this page: free.
  • Product list check for missing HTS code, origin, weight and value: free.
  • Duty estimate (per SKU row, every layer with its source and date, results CSV): $0.01 per SKU row for the first 2,000 each calendar month, then $0.004 per SKU row, at least 10 x the price per SKU row for each call; you pay only for what is delivered and the rest is refunded at once; rows we refuse or cannot read are refunded.
  • At most $25.00 a calendar month for this tool; after that it is free until the month ends.

What it does not cover

  • Section 232 duties (steel, aluminum, copper, vehicles and parts, trucks, timber and furniture, semiconductors, pharmaceuticals, drones): a line they name gets no estimate.
  • The section 338 duties on products of Canada, the measures on Russia, safeguards and quotas.
  • A rate per kilogram, unit or dozen without the quantity.
  • Antidumping and countervailing duties are never included: the Department of Commerce sets them per producer.
  • Estimate, not a customs ruling, and not legal or customs advice: U.S. Customs and Border Protection decides the classification, value, origin and duty at entry, and the importer is responsible for them.
