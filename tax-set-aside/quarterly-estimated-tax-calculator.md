## Steps

1. **Work out the year's tax.** Self-employment tax plus income tax on your expected profit (the table below, or the calculator for your own numbers).
2. **Pick the smaller required amount.** 90% of this year's tax, or 100% of last year's (110% if last year's AGI was over $150,000, or $75,000 married filing separately).
3. **Subtract withholding.** Tax withheld from W-2 wages counts as paid evenly through the year.
4. **Pay a quarter on each date.** April 15, 2026, June 15, 2026, September 15, 2026, January 15, 2027. Paying each payment's set-aside into a pot means the money is there on each date.

## Questions

### When are 2026 estimated tax payments due?

April 15, 2026, June 15, 2026, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027. You can skip the January payment if you file your 2026 return by February 1, 2027 and pay the balance with it.

### How do I avoid the underpayment penalty?

Pay at least the smaller of 90% of this year's tax or 100% of last year's (110% if last year's AGI was over $150,000), in four installments by the due dates, or owe under $1,000 after withholding.
