# Small business owner: tax set-aside per payment

A sole proprietor in Illinois with $100,000 of profit so far should set aside $3,509 (35.1%) of a $10,000 payment in 2026, single: $1,413 self-employment tax, $1,636 federal and $460 Illinois income tax.

- Set aside from $10,000: $3,509
- Share: 35.1%
- QBI threshold (single): $201,750

## Set-aside as the QBI deduction phases out

A $10,000 payment, single, Illinois, no employees or business property. Between $201,750 and $276,750 of taxable income the 20% QBI deduction falls to nothing (to the $400 minimum); for a specified service business it falls faster, because its qualified income is cut too.

### How much should a small business owner set aside for taxes?
