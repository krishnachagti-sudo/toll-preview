## Questions

### How much should a single-member LLC set aside for taxes?

The same as a sole proprietor, because a single-member LLC is disregarded for federal income tax unless it elects corporate treatment. A $5,000 payment at $80,000 of profit needs $1,153 (23.1%) single in Florida.
