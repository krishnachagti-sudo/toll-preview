# W-2 job plus 1099 side income: set-aside per payment

With a $60,000 W-2 job, set aside $692 (23.1%) of your first $3,000 of 1099 side income in 2026, single in Texas: $424 self-employment tax and $268 income tax, because the wages already fill the lower brackets.

- Set aside from $3,000: $692
- With no W-2 job: $424
- Bracket: 12%

## Questions

### How much should I set aside for taxes on 1099 income with a W-2 job?

More per dollar than with no job, because your wages use the lower brackets. Single in Texas with $60,000 of wages, the first $3,000 of side income needs $692 (23.1%); with $120,000 of wages it needs $923.

### Can I raise my W-4 withholding instead of paying estimates?

Yes. Withholding from wages counts as paid evenly through the year, so raising it by the set-aside amounts covers the side income without quarterly payments.
