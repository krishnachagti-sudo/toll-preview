# DoorDash: tax set-aside per payout, 2026

A DoorDash driver who has earned $20,000 so far should set aside $79 of a $600 payout after deducting 250 miles at the IRS business rate (72.5 cents January to June 2026, 76 cents July to December), single in Texas with $120 of it in tips.

- Set aside: $79
- Mileage deduction: $190
- Share of payout: 13.1%

## Questions

### How much should a DoorDash driver set aside for taxes?

Less than a flat 25% once mileage comes off. A $600 payout for 250 miles in late 2026 adds $410.00 of profit; with $20,000 earned so far in Texas, single, set aside $79 (13.1% of the payout).

### Do tips lower self-employment tax?

No. Qualified tips (up to $25,000 a year, 2025 to 2028) lower income tax only. On this payout, $120 of tips makes the set-aside $79 instead of $88.
