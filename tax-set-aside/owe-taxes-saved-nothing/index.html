You owe tax and saved nothing: a plan

Start by sizing the bill: a single filer in Texas with $50,000 of 2026 freelance profit owes about $9,732 for the year. Then pay as much as you can by Friday, January 15, 2027, the last 2026 installment, and set aside from every payment from now on.

Can last year's tax set a lower target?

Yes. You avoid the underpayment penalty by paying, in installments, the smaller of 90% of this year's tax or 100% of last year's (110% if last year's AGI was over $150,000). With a $6,000 tax for 2025 and AGI of $45,000, the target is $6,000, or $1,500 an installment. With nothing paid yet, $6,000 is due by January 15, 2027 to be caught up on that target; the earlier installments that were missed still count as late for the days they were unpaid.

What does paying late cost?

Form 1040-ES: the penalty is imposed on each underpayment for the number of days it remains unpaid. It is figured with the IRS underpayment rate for each quarter:

IRS underpayment rate for individuals, 2026.
QuarterRate
2026, quarter 17%
2026, quarter 26%
2026, quarter 37%
2026, quarter 47%

The penalty may be waived under certain conditions; Form 1040-ES points to the Instructions for Form 2210 for details. If income came mostly late in the year, the annualized income installment method (Pub 505, chapter 2) can lower the earlier installments.

What if I cannot pay it all?

Form 1040-ES lists an Online Payment Agreement: if you cannot pay in full by the due date of your return, you can apply for an online monthly installment agreement at IRS.gov/OPA; a user fee is charged.

How do I stop this happening again?

Set aside from each payment as it arrives. A $4,000 payment now, with $46,000 of profit already, needs $922.06 (23.1%) in Texas, single.

Steps

  1. Work out the year's bill. Self-employment tax plus income tax (and state tax) on the year's expected profit.
  2. Find the smaller required amount. 90% of this year's tax, or 100% of last year's (110% above the AGI line). If last year's tax was lower, that can be the cheaper target.
  3. Pay what you can by January 15, 2027. The underpayment penalty runs on each installment for the days it stays unpaid, so paying sooner costs less.
  4. From now on, set aside from each payment. Move the set-aside when each payment arrives, so the next quarter is covered.
  5. If you cannot pay in full when you file. Form 1040-ES points to the Online Payment Agreement at IRS.gov/OPA, a monthly installment agreement with a user fee.

Questions

How much will I owe on $50,000 of 1099 income?

For a single filer in Texas with $50,000 of 2026 profit and no other income: about $9,732, $7,065 self-employment tax and $2,667 federal income tax.

What is the IRS underpayment interest rate in 2026?

7% for 2026 quarter 1, 6% for 2026 quarter 2, 7% for 2026 quarter 3, 7% for 2026 quarter 4, per the IRS quarterly interest rates page.

Is there a way to owe less penalty?

Pay earlier, and check the safe harbor: if your 2025 tax was $6,000, paying $6,000 in total over the four installments meets the 100% rule even if 2026's tax is higher.
