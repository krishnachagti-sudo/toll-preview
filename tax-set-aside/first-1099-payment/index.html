Your first 1099 payment: how much to set aside

If a $2,500 payment is your first and only income of 2026, set aside $353.24 (14.1%): all of it self-employment tax, because the $16,100 standard deduction still covers your income tax. With a $60,000 day job it is $576.28, and later in the year with $30,000 of profit already, $539.11 in Texas and $585.58 in California.

Why is the first payment taxed differently?

Self-employment tax (Social Security and Medicare for the self-employed) applies to profit from the first dollar. Income tax does not: a single filer's first $16,100 of income in 2026 is covered by the standard deduction. So a first payment with no other income carries self-employment tax only, 14.1% of it.

What if I also have a day job?

The wages use up the standard deduction and fill the lower brackets, so freelance profit is taxed at your top rate from the start. W-2 wages also count toward the Social Security wage base. See W-2 job plus 1099 side income.

When is the money due?

Federal estimated tax is paid four times a year. A payment received now falls in the period due January 15, 2027. You may not owe estimated tax at all in your first year: Form 1040-ES says you do not if you had no tax liability for the full 12-month 2025 tax year and were a U.S. citizen or resident alien for all of 2025.

What should I keep track of?

  • Every payment: date, client, amount. The set-aside depends on the profit so far this year.
  • Business expenses: they lower the profit the tax is worked out on.
  • Business miles, if you drive for work: the IRS standard rate is one way to deduct them.

Estimate for planning, not tax advice. It uses the published 2026 federal rates, the standard deduction, no credits and one state, and leaves out local taxes. We do not move money; you decide what to transfer.

Steps

  1. Work out the profit in the payment. The payment less the business expenses that go with it.
  2. Add self-employment tax. 15.3% on 92.35% of net earnings (12.4% Social Security up to $184,500, 2.9% Medicare), from the first dollar once the year's net earnings reach $400.
  3. Add income tax at your rate. Nothing until your income passes the $16,100 standard deduction (single, 2026); then your bracket rate. A day job usually uses the deduction up already.
  4. Add state income tax. Nothing in states without an income tax, such as Texas or Florida; the state's rate elsewhere.
  5. Move it and note the next due date. Move the amount to a separate account when the payment arrives. Federal estimated tax is due four times a year.

Questions

How much should I set aside from my first 1099 payment?

It depends on what else you earn. If a $2,500 payment is your only 2026 income, $353.24 (14.1%), all self-employment tax. With a $60,000 W-2 job, $576.28 (23.1%). Both single, in Texas.

Why is it not a fixed percentage?

Self-employment tax starts with the first dollar of profit, but income tax starts only once the standard deduction is used up, and the rate rises with income. So the same payment needs a different share at different points in the year.

When do I pay it?

Federal estimated tax for 2026 is due April 15, 2026, June 15, 2026, September 15, 2026, January 15, 2027. A payment received on November 6, 2026 falls in the period paid by January 15, 2027.
