# Found or Endwise for a tax set-aside

Use Found if you want a banking app that moves a set percentage of each deposit into a tax pocket for you (its free plan includes it). Use Endwise if you want the amount worked out for each payment from your profit so far: in California, a $3,000 payment needs $423.88 early in the year and **$803.05** at $40,000 of profit.

## How do Found and Endwise differ?

|  | Endwise | Found |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A calculator for the set-aside on each payment, over the web, API or MCP. | A banking app for the self-employed: "Found is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services are provided by Lead Bank, Member FDIC." |
| How the amount is set | Worked out per payment from profit so far, filing status and state, at the rates the payment falls in. | A percentage of each deposit: a smart percentage based on your tax bracket, or your own custom rate. |
| Moves the money | No. You move it. | Yes, into a Taxes Pocket. |
| Pays estimated tax | No; it gives the next due date. | In-app quarterly federal tax payments on Found Plus and Found Pro, for subscribers who file a Schedule C. |
| Price | $0.10 a payment, capped at $2.49 a month. | Found $0 a month; Found Plus $35 a month or $315 a year; Found Pro $80 a month or $720 a year. |

A fixed percentage cannot follow the year: in the example above, the same $3,000 needs 14.1% when it is the first income of the year and 26.8% at $40,000 of profit.

## When is Found the better choice?

- You want the money moved automatically with no step of your own.
- You want a business account, tax estimates and quarterly payments in one app.
- A percentage near your bracket is close enough for you.

## When is Endwise the better choice?

- You want to keep your current bank.
- Your income is uneven, or crosses brackets, and you want the exact amount for each payment.
- You want the working shown, line by line, with sources.

They can work together: work out the amount here, then set it as the transfer in your bank.

## Questions

### How does Found set aside money for taxes?

Found's tax page says it sets aside a percentage of each deposit into a dedicated Taxes Pocket; you choose a smart percentage based on your tax bracket or set your own custom rate.

### Is Found a bank?

Found says it is a financial technology company, not a bank; banking services are provided by Lead Bank, Member FDIC.

### Does Endwise move money?

No. It works out how much of a payment to set aside and when the next payment is due; you move the money yourself, in any bank.
