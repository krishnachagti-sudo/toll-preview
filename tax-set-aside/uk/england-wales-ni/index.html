Sole traders, 2026 to 2027

How much to put aside for tax and NI, self-employed

Put aside £780 (26.0%) of a £3,000 payment in England, Wales or Northern Ireland when your profit so far this 2026 to 2027 tax year is £30,000: £600 Income Tax and £180 Class 4 National Insurance. In your first year of Self Assessment, set aside £1,170 to cover the first payment on account as well.

Questions

How much should I put aside for tax as a self-employed sole trader?

It depends on your profit so far. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland for 2026 to 2027, a £3,000 payment needs £0 at the start of the tax year (inside your Personal Allowance), £780 at £30,000 and £1,260 at £90,000.

Why is my first Self Assessment bill so big?

Because the first 31 January bill holds the whole year's tax plus the first payment on account for the next year, half as much again. At £30,000 profit that makes £1,170 to put aside from a £3,000 payment instead of £780.
