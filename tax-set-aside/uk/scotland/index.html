Scotland: how much to put aside for tax, 2026 to 2027

Put aside £810 (27.0%) of a £3,000 payment in Scotland when your profit so far this 2026 to 2027 tax year is £30,000: £630 Income Tax and £180 Class 4 National Insurance. In your first year of Self Assessment, set aside £1,215 to cover the first payment on account as well.

Questions

How much should I put aside for tax as a self-employed sole trader?

It depends on your profit so far. In Scotland for 2026 to 2027, a £3,000 payment needs £0 at the start of the tax year (inside your Personal Allowance), £810 at £30,000 and £1,410 at £90,000.

Why is my first Self Assessment bill so big?

Because the first 31 January bill holds the whole year's tax plus the first payment on account for the next year, half as much again. At £30,000 profit that makes £1,215 to put aside from a £3,000 payment instead of £810.
