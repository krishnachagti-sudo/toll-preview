# Payments on account calculator

Each payment on account is half of last year's Self Assessment bill, due 31 January and 31 July: a £3,000 bill means two payments of £1,500, and in your first year the 31 January payment is £4,500, the whole £3,000 plus the first £1,500. A sole trader in England with £30,000 profit in 2026 to 2027 owes £4,531.80, so £6,797.70 by 31 January 2028 in a first year.

- Each on a £3,000 bill: £1,500
- First-year January: £4,500
- No payments under: £1,000

## Questions

### When do I have to make payments on account?

When last year's Self Assessment bill was £1,000 or more, unless more than 80% of the tax you owed was collected at source (for example through your tax code).

### How much is each payment on account?

Half of last year's bill, due by midnight on 31 January and 31 July. Student loan and capital gains are not included in them.
