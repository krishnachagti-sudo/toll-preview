# Class 4 National Insurance, 2026 to 2027

For 2026 to 2027 Class 4 National Insurance is 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above £50,270, so £30,000 of profit pays £1,045.80.

- Class 4 on £30,000: £1,045.80
- Main rate: 6%
- Above £50,270: 2%

## Questions

### What is the Class 4 National Insurance threshold for 2026 to 2027?

You pay Class 4 on profit over £12,570: 6% up to £50,270 and 2% above that.

### Do I still pay Class 2 National Insurance?

Not if your profit is £7,105 or more: Class 2 is treated as paid. Below that you can pay it voluntarily at £3.65 a week to protect your National Insurance record.
