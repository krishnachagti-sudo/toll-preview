Self Assessment deadline, 31 January 2027

The 2025 to 2026 Self Assessment online return and payment are due by 11:59pm on Sunday 31 January 2027. Because that is a weekend, GOV.UK says a payment must reach HMRC by the last working day before, Friday 29 January 2027, unless you pay by Faster Payments or card. The same payment holds your first payment on account for 2026 to 2027: on a £3,000 bill, £4,500 in a first year.

What is due on 31 January 2027?

Does the deadline move because 31 January 2027 is a Sunday?

The deadline stays 31 January 2027. What changes is when your money must arrive: GOV.UK says "If the deadline falls on a weekend or bank holiday, make sure your payment reaches HMRC on the last working day before" unless you pay by Faster Payments or by debit or credit card. The last working day before is Friday 29 January 2027, worked out with the England and Wales bank holiday rules.

Why is my first January bill so big?

Payments on account start once a bill reaches £1,000, unless more than 80% of your tax was collected at source. Each is half of the last bill. So in your first year with them, 31 January holds the whole of last year's bill plus half of it again.

The figures for 2025 to 2026 come from your own return; this tool holds the 2026 to 2027 rates. For the year now running, a sole trader in England with £30,000 profit owes £4,531.80 for 2026 to 2027 (£3,486.00 Income Tax and £1,045.80 Class 4), so a first-year bill on Monday 31 January 2028 would be £6,797.70 with the next payment on account.

Setting aside from each invoice spreads that: a £3,000 invoice at £30,000 of profit so far needs £780.00 (26.0%) for 2026 to 2027 tax and National Insurance.

What happens if I miss it?

  • Late return: an initial £100 penalty; after 3 months, £10 per day up to a maximum of £900 (GOV.UK).
  • Late payment: penalties of 5% of the tax unpaid at 30 days, 6 months and 12 months, plus interest on the amount owed (GOV.UK).

Not tax advice. The amounts use GOV.UK's 2026 to 2027 rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland and leave out savings, dividends and the High Income Child Benefit Charge. We do not move money; you decide what to transfer.

Questions

When is the Self Assessment deadline in 2027?

For the 2025 to 2026 tax year: submit your online return by 11:59pm on 31 January 2027 and pay the tax you owe by 11:59pm the same day (GOV.UK).

31 January 2027 is a Sunday. Does the deadline move?

No. GOV.UK gives 31 January 2027. For payments it says: if the deadline falls on a weekend or bank holiday, make sure your payment reaches HMRC on the last working day before (unless you're paying by Faster Payments or by debit or credit card). That is Friday 29 January 2027.

What do I pay on 31 January 2027?

Any tax still owed for 2025 to 2026 (the balancing payment) plus your first payment on account for 2026 to 2027, which is half of your 2025 to 2026 bill. On a £3,000 bill that is £1,500, so £4,500 in a first year with nothing paid on account before.

What is the penalty for filing late?

GOV.UK: an initial £100 penalty, then after 3 months additional daily penalties of £10 per day, up to a maximum of £900. Late payment adds penalties of 5% of the tax unpaid at 30 days, 6 months and 12 months, and interest.
