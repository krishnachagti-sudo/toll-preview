## The rule

```
SE tax = 12.4% x min(0.9235 x profit, 184,500 - W-2 wages) + 2.9% x 0.9235 x profit
```

Nothing is due when net earnings are under $400. Half of the 15.3% is deducted in working out AGI, which lowers income tax but not the self-employment tax itself.

> Estimate for planning, not tax advice. It uses the published 2026 rates (UK: 2026 to 2027), the standard deduction and no credits, and one resident state; it leaves out local taxes. Tips, overtime and senior deductions apply only if you qualify, and never reduce self-employment tax. We do not move money; you decide what to transfer.

## Questions

### What is the self-employment tax rate for 2026?

15.3%: 12.4% for Social Security on net earnings up to $184,500 (less any W-2 wages) and 2.9% for Medicare on all of them. Net earnings are 92.35% of net profit.

### Does a W-2 job lower my self-employment tax?

Only the Social Security part, and only once wages and earnings together pass $184,500. With $150,000 of W-2 wages, $50,000 of profit pays $5,617.08 instead of $7,064.78.
