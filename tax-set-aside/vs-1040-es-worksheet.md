# The 1040-ES worksheet or a per-payment set-aside

The Form 1040-ES worksheet answers "how much estimated tax must I pay this year?": for a $9,000 tax in 2025 the prior-year target is $9,000, $2,250 an installment. A per-payment set-aside answers "how much of this check do I move now?": **$691.53** of a $3,000 payment at $40,000 of profit, single, in Texas.

## What does each answer?

|  | Form 1040-ES worksheet | Endwise set-aside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question | How much estimated tax to pay for the year, and the required annual payment to avoid a penalty. | How much of the payment in hand to set aside, given the profit so far. |
| When you use it | Once at the start of the year, and again if income changes ("Amended estimated tax", Pub 505). | Each time a payment arrives. |
| Safe harbor | Line 12c: the smaller of 90% of this year's tax and 100% (or 110%) of last year's. | The next-due answer works out the same prior-year target and what is due by the next date, free. |
| Uneven income | The annualized income installment method in Pub 505, chapter 2, with Form 2210 Schedule AI. | Each payment is priced at the rate it falls in, so a strong Q4 is covered as it arrives. |
| State tax | Federal only. | The covered states' income tax is included. |
| Cost | Free, from the IRS. | $0.10 a payment, capped at $2.49 a month; next due date and safe harbor free. |

## When is the 1040-ES worksheet the better choice?

- It is the IRS's own method and free.
- You want to work out the whole year at once and pay four equal installments.
- You need the figure for line 12c, the required annual payment, or you file Form 2210 with the annualized method.

## When is Endwise the better choice?

- You are paid irregularly and want the amount to move from each payment.
- You want state income tax in the same answer.
- A program or AI assistant has to work it out the same way every time.

The two fit together: the worksheet sets the year's target; setting aside from each payment means the money is there on each date. With $6,750 paid on a $9,000 target, $2,250 is due by January 15, 2027.

## Questions

### What does the Form 1040-ES worksheet calculate?

Your estimated tax for the year and the required annual payment to avoid a penalty: line 12c is the smaller of line 12a (90% of this year's estimated tax) and line 12b (the payment based on last year's tax).

### Do I have to pay estimated tax?

In most cases, Form 1040-ES says yes if you expect to owe at least $1,000 for 2026 after withholding and refundable credits, and expect withholding and credits to be less than the smaller of 90% of your 2026 tax or 100% of your 2025 tax.

### What is the 110% rule?

If your 2025 AGI was more than $150,000 ($75,000 married filing separately), use 110% of last year's tax instead of 100%. On a $40,000 tax that is $44,000.
