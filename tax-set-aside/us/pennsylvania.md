## Pennsylvania income tax

Pennsylvania taxes net business profit at a flat 3.07%, with no standard deduction or personal exemption. It does not start from federal AGI, so half of self-employment tax is not deducted, and a business loss cannot reduce wages. Local earned income tax set by your municipality and school district comes on top and is not included here.

## What is in the amount

- Self-employment tax: 15.3% of 92.35% of profit (12.4% Social Security up to the $184,500 wage base, 2.9% Medicare on all of it), and nothing under $400 of net earnings.
- Federal income tax: 2026 brackets, the $16,100 standard deduction for a single filer, half of self-employment tax deducted, and the 20% QBI deduction.
- The amount is the tax this payment adds: the year's tax with it, less the year's tax without it.

## Questions

### How much should I set aside for taxes on 1099 income in Pennsylvania?

It depends on what you have earned so far. For a single filer in 2026, a $3,000 payment needs $516 (17.2%) at the start of the year, $784 (26.1%) after $40,000 of profit and $1,007 (33.6%) after $90,000.

### Is 25% enough to set aside for taxes in Pennsylvania?

Not always. A single Pennsylvania filer needs 17.2% of a $3,000 payment at the start of 2026 but 33.6% once profit passes $90,000, so a flat 25% is too much early in the year and too little later.
