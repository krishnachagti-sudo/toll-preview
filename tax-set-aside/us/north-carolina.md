## North Carolina income tax

North Carolina taxes income at a flat 3.99% for 2026, on federal AGI less the NC standard deduction: $12,750 single, $25,500 married filing jointly, $19,125 head of household. There is no extra amount at 65 or for blindness.

## What is in the amount

- Self-employment tax: 15.3% of 92.35% of profit (12.4% Social Security up to the $184,500 wage base, 2.9% Medicare on all of it), and nothing under $400 of net earnings.
- Federal income tax: 2026 brackets, the $16,100 standard deduction for a single filer, half of self-employment tax deducted, and the 20% QBI deduction.
- The amount is the tax this payment adds: the year's tax with it, less the year's tax without it.

## Questions

### How much should I set aside for taxes on 1099 income in North Carolina?

It depends on what you have earned so far. For a single filer in 2026, a $3,000 payment needs $424 (14.1%) at the start of the year, $803 (26.8%) after $40,000 of profit and $1,026 (34.2%) after $90,000.

### Is 25% enough to set aside for taxes in North Carolina?

Not always. A single North Carolina filer needs 14.1% of a $3,000 payment at the start of 2026 but 34.2% once profit passes $90,000, so a flat 25% is too much early in the year and too little later.
