# California: tax set-aside per payment, 2026

In California, a single filer with $40,000 of net profit so far should set aside $803 (26.8%) of a $3,000 payment in 2026: $424 self-employment tax, $268 federal income tax and $112 California income tax.

## California income tax

California taxes self-employment profit at graduated rates from 1% to 12.3%, plus 1% on taxable income over $1 million. FTB's 2026 estimated tax worksheet tells filers to use the 2025 rate schedules, the $5,706 standard deduction ($11,412 joint or head of household) and the $153 personal exemption credit, so those are used here. California allows the federal deduction for half of self-employment tax, but not the QBI deduction.

## What is in the amount

- Self-employment tax: 15.3% of 92.35% of profit (12.4% Social Security up to the $184,500 wage base, 2.9% Medicare on all of it), and nothing under $400 of net earnings.
- Federal income tax: 2026 brackets, the $16,100 standard deduction for a single filer, half of self-employment tax deducted, and the 20% QBI deduction.
- The amount is the tax this payment adds: the year's tax with it, less the year's tax without it.

## Questions

### How much should I set aside for taxes on 1099 income in California?

It depends on what you have earned so far. For a single filer in 2026, a $3,000 payment needs $424 (14.1%) at the start of the year, $803 (26.8%) after $40,000 of profit and $1,174 (39.1%) after $90,000.

### Is 25% enough to set aside for taxes in California?

Not always. A single California filer needs 14.1% of a $3,000 payment at the start of 2026 but 39.1% once profit passes $90,000, so a flat 25% is too much early in the year and too little later.
