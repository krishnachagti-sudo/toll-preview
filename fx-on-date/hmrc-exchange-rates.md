## Can I use the HMRC rate for VAT?

Yes, as one of two routes. VAT Notice 700, section 7.6: you must use the UK market selling rate at the time of the supply, or you may use the period rate of exchange published by HMRC for customs purposes, for all your supplies or for a particular class of supply. Any other commercial rate needs HMRC's agreement in writing, and forward rates are not accepted.

The rate is the one current at the time of supply, so a supply on the last day of a month takes that month's rate. Section 16.4 adds that an invoice in a foreign currency must show the total VAT in sterling. The steps are on [which rate a foreign-currency invoice uses](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/fx-on-date/foreign-currency-invoice-exchange-rate/).

> HMRC also issues weekly amendments when a currency moves sharply; they are not in the monthly files and are not used here. This page quotes HMRC's rules; it is not tax advice.

## Questions

### Can I use HMRC's monthly rate for VAT?

Yes. VAT Notice 700, section 7.6, says you must use the UK market selling rate at the time of supply, or you may use the period rate of exchange published by HMRC for customs purposes, for all supplies or for a class of supply.

### How do I convert dollars to pounds with the November 2026 rate?

Divide by the rate. HMRC lists 1.3396 US dollars per pound for November 2026, so $12,000 is £8,957.90.

### When does HMRC publish next month's rates?

Before the month starts. The November 2026 rates were published on 21 October 2026; the dates for the last 12 months are on this page. HMRC does not promise a date here, so check the Trade Tariff service.
