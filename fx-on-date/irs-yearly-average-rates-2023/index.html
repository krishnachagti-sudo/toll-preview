IRS yearly average exchange rates for 2023

The IRS yearly average exchange rates for 2023 are 0.924 euros, 82.572 Indian rupees and 0.804 British pounds per US dollar; to convert a foreign amount to US dollars, divide it by the rate.

Need the rate for one date instead?

The IRS says to use the rate prevailing when you receive, pay or accrue an item. The tool gives the ECB reference rate for a single day (a different source from the IRS table); this opens US dollar to Euro on 31 December 2023.

A weekend or holiday uses the last fixing before it.

How to use the yearly average

  • Foreign currency to US dollars: divide the foreign amount by the rate.
  • US dollars to foreign currency: multiply the dollar amount by the rate.
  • The IRS also says that, in general, you use the exchange rate prevailing (the spot rate) when you receive, pay or accrue the item, and that it accepts any posted exchange rate used consistently.

Questions

Does the IRS have an official exchange rate?

No. The IRS says it has no official exchange rate and generally accepts any posted exchange rate that is used consistently.

How do I convert euros to US dollars with the 2023 IRS yearly average?

Divide the amount by the rate: 1,000 euros divided by 0.924 is 1,082.25 US dollars. To go from US dollars to the other currency, multiply.

Do these rates apply when paying US tax to the IRS?

No. The IRS says these rates do not apply to payments of US tax; a payment made in a foreign currency is converted at the rate on the day the processing bank converts it.
