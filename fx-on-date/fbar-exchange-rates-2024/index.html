Sources

  • U.S. Treasury Fiscal Data: Treasury Reporting Rates of Exchange (read 31 October 2026)
  • FinCEN: BSA Electronic Filing Requirements for the FBAR (FinCEN Form 114), Step 2

Steps

  1. Find the maximum value. Take the highest value each foreign account reached during 2024, in its own currency.
  2. Convert at the 31 December rate. Divide that value by the Treasury Reporting Rate for 31 December 2024 for the account's currency (the table on this page). If Treasury has no rate for it, FinCEN says to use another verifiable exchange rate and give its source.
  3. Round up. Record the US dollar amount rounded up to the next whole dollar, as the FBAR instructions ask.
  4. Check the threshold. If one account, or the maximum values of all accounts added together, is more than $10,000, an FBAR must be filed.

Questions

What exchange rate do I use for a 2024 FBAR?

The Treasury Reporting Rate of Exchange for the last day of the calendar year, 31 December 2024. If Treasury has no rate for the currency, FinCEN says to use another verifiable exchange rate and give its source.

What is the 2024 FBAR rate for the euro?

0.961 euros per US dollar, so 10,000 euros is 10,406 US dollars rounded up to the next whole dollar.
