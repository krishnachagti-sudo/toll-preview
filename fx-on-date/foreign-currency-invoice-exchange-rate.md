# Which exchange rate a foreign-currency invoice uses

For UK VAT, convert at the UK market selling rate at the time of supply or at HMRC's period rate, and show the VAT total in sterling. With HMRC's November 2026 rate of 1.1930 euros per pound, a net amount of €8,000 is £6,705.78.

## What must be in sterling?

Only the VAT. VAT Notice 700, section 16.3.1: the net amount of each line and the gross total excluding VAT can be expressed in any currency; the total amount of VAT chargeable must be expressed in sterling. Section 16.4 repeats it for invoices in a foreign currency and points to 7.6 for the conversion.

## Which rate may I use?

- **UK market selling rate**: the default, at the time of the supply.
- **HMRC period rate**: the period rate of exchange HMRC publishes for customs purposes, for all supplies or for one class of supply.
- **Anything else**: only with HMRC's agreement in writing; HMRC looks at whether the rate is tied to the UK currency market, can be checked, and is updated often. Forward rates are not accepted.

| Step | Figure |

| Net amount on the invoice | €8,000 |
| HMRC monthly rate, November 2026 | 1.1930 euros per £1 |

## Steps

1. **Price in the customer's currency.** Each line's net amount and the total excluding VAT can be in any currency (VAT Notice 700, 16.3.1).
2. **Pick your rate route.** The UK market selling rate at the time of supply, or HMRC's period rate published for customs purposes. Use the same route for a class of supply.
3. **Convert the VAT.** Show the total VAT chargeable in sterling (16.3.1 and 16.4).
4. **Keep the record.** Note the rate, its source and its date with the invoice, so the sterling figure can be checked later.

## Questions

### Can I invoice a UK customer in euros or dollars?

Yes. VAT Notice 700 says the line amounts and the total excluding VAT can be in any currency, but the total VAT chargeable must be in sterling.

### Which exchange rate does HMRC accept?

The UK market selling rate at the time of the supply, or the period rate of exchange HMRC publishes for customs purposes (VAT Notice 700, 7.6). Another commercial rate needs HMRC's written agreement; forward rates are not accepted.

### Does the ECB rate count as the UK market selling rate?

VAT Notice 700 does not name the ECB rate. If you want to use a rate the notice does not cover, it says to apply in writing to HMRC's VAT Written Enquiries Team.
