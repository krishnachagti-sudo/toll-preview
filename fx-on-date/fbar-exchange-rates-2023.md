# FBAR exchange rates for 2023

For a 2023 FBAR, convert each account's maximum value at the Treasury Reporting Rate for 31 December 2023: 0.905 euros, 83.162 Indian rupees and 0.786 British pounds per US dollar.

- Euro (Treasury): 0.905
- Indian rupee (Treasury): 83.162
- British pound (Treasury): 0.786

## Steps

1. **Find the maximum value.** Take the highest value each foreign account reached during 2023, in its own currency.
2. **Convert at the 31 December rate.** Divide that value by the Treasury Reporting Rate for 31 December 2023 for the account's currency (the table on this page). If Treasury has no rate for it, FinCEN says to use another verifiable exchange rate and give its source.
3. **Round up.** Record the US dollar amount rounded up to the next whole dollar, as the FBAR instructions ask.
4. **Check the threshold.** If one account, or the maximum values of all accounts added together, is more than $10,000, an FBAR must be filed.

## Questions

### What exchange rate do I use for a 2023 FBAR?

The Treasury Reporting Rate of Exchange for the last day of the calendar year, 31 December 2023. If Treasury has no rate for the currency, FinCEN says to use another verifiable exchange rate and give its source.

### What is the 2023 FBAR rate for the euro?

0.905 euros per US dollar, so 10,000 euros is 11,050 US dollars rounded up to the next whole dollar.
