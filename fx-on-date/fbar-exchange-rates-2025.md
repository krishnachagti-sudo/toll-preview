# FBAR exchange rates for 2025

## Steps

1. **Find the maximum value.** Take the highest value each foreign account reached during 2025, in its own currency.
2. **Convert at the 31 December rate.** Divide that value by the Treasury Reporting Rate for 31 December 2025 for the account's currency (the table on this page). If Treasury has no rate for it, FinCEN says to use another verifiable exchange rate and give its source.
3. **Round up.** Record the US dollar amount rounded up to the next whole dollar, as the FBAR instructions ask.
4. **Check the threshold.** If one account, or the maximum values of all accounts added together, is more than $10,000, an FBAR must be filed.

## Questions

### What exchange rate do I use for a 2025 FBAR?

The Treasury Reporting Rate of Exchange for the last day of the calendar year, 31 December 2025. If Treasury has no rate for the currency, FinCEN says to use another verifiable exchange rate and give its source.

### What is the 2025 FBAR rate for the euro?

0.851 euros per US dollar, so 10,000 euros is 11,751 US dollars rounded up to the next whole dollar.
