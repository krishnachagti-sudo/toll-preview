The ECB euro reference rate, explained

The ECB sets its euro reference rates once a working day, from a concertation between central banks around 14:10 CET, and publishes them around 16:00 CET. In 2025 it published 255 fixings; 6 weekdays had none.

Try a day with no fixing

The tool names the fixing it used, so a holiday shows the day before.

Open these dates in the full tool

Is the ECB rate the rate I will get?

No. In the ECB's words the reference rates are published for information purposes only, and using them for transaction purposes is strongly discouraged. Banks, card networks and payment firms quote their own rates, usually with a margin. The reference rate is useful as a neutral, dated figure for records, reports and contracts that name it.

Which weekdays had no rate in 2025?

No rate is published at weekends or on TARGET closing days. In 2025 the weekdays with no fixing in the ECB file were Wednesday 1 January 2025, Friday 18 April 2025, Monday 21 April 2025, Thursday 1 May 2025, Thursday 25 December 2025 and Friday 26 December 2025.

Which date should I use for a past amount?

Use the date your rule names, such as the transaction date. If it falls on a weekend or a closing day, the usual convention, and the one this tool follows, is the last fixing before it. Other rules exist: the US and UK rules are on which rate to use for US taxes and HMRC monthly rates.

Questions

What time does the ECB publish its reference rates?

The ECB says they are usually updated at around 16:00 CET every working day, except on TARGET closing days. They come from a concertation between central banks that normally takes place around 14:10 CET.

Can I buy or sell euros at the ECB rate?

No. The ECB says the reference rates are published for information purposes only and that using them for transaction purposes is strongly discouraged. A bank or card quotes its own rate.

What rate applies on a day with no fixing?

None is published. This tool uses the last fixing before it and names that day: for 25 December 2025 it uses 24 December 2025, when EUR to USD was 1.1787.
