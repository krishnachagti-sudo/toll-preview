Low-inventory fee dates, November 2026 to December 2027

Amazon's 2026 low-inventory rule runs through January 31, 2027; on February 1, 2027 the 2027 rule starts (28 days before and after a weekly check, oversize tiers charged, its own fee line and report), after the size tiers change on January 15, 2027; the first charges under the new rule show about four weeks later, around March 1, 2027.

The dates

From Amazon's help pages and fee announcement, read November 8, 2026. Rows marked "worked out" are computed from Amazon's rules.
DateWhat happensSource
January 14, 2027Last day of the 2026 product size tier definitionsAmazon: Product size tiers
January 15, 2027Product size tiers change: bulky split in four, extra-large becomes six oversize tiers; most 2027 fee changesAmazon: 2027 US Referral and FBA fees; Product size tiers
January 31, 2027Last day of the 2026 low-inventory rule (30- and 90-day historical days of supply) and its rate tableAmazon: Low-inventory-level fee
February 1, 2027The 2027 low-inventory rule starts: trailing and following 28 days, weekly checks, oversize tiers charged, a standalone fee lineAmazon: Low-inventory-level fee 2027
February 1, 2027Low-Inventory-Level Fee Report availableAmazon: Low-inventory-level fee 2027
February 1, 2027Unified monthly storage fee replaces the storage utilization surcharge and the aged inventory surchargeAmazon: Unified monthly storage fee
March 1, 2027About when the first 2027-rule charges show: four weeks after a check in the first week of FebruaryWorked out from Amazon's "four weeks after the inventory check"
March 9, 2027Example: New Selection window ends for a product first received November 9, 2026 (120 days)Worked out by the calculator
March 31, 2027Last day to confirm New Selection Program (2026) enrollment to keep its benefits (incl. no low-inventory fee on the first 200 units) for new ASINsAmazon: New Selection Program - 2026
November 9, 2027Example: new-seller exemption ends for a seller whose first inventory was received November 9, 2026 (365 days)Worked out by the calculator

Amazon has not announced its 2028 fee changes, and it does not publish which weekday it checks inventory, so no later dates are listed. Dates for Prime Big Deal Days and peak inbound cutoffs are not on Amazon's fee pages and are left out.
