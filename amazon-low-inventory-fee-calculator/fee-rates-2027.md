# Low-inventory-level fee rates, 2027

From February 1, 2027 Amazon's low-inventory-level fee is $0.32 to $3.50 a unit shipped in the week after a weekly inventory check, when inventory is under 28 days of supply against both the 28 days before and the 28 days after the check; rates follow the 2027 size tiers, now including oversize. Amazon's example: a large standard item at 24 days pays $0.36.

## Questions

### What changes on February 1, 2027?

Amazon's help: "We only apply the low-inventory level fee if your inventory levels are insufficient based on your actual shipped units in both the trailing and following 28-day periods. The fee rate is applied using the higher of the two days of supply metrics." The fee will now apply to items in all oversize product size tiers.

### When is it charged?

Amazon's help: "The fee applies only to units shipped in the following week (“following week 1”) after the inventory is assessed." It is charged four weeks after the inventory check, as a standalone fee.

### Did the standard-size rates change?

No. The small standard, large standard and bulky rates are the same as in the 2026 table; the bulky rows now cover the 2027 small, medium, large and extra-large bulky tiers, and two oversize rows are new.
