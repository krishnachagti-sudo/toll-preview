The rules it uses

  • The fee applies when days of supply are under 28 in both windows Amazon measures, on every unit shipped that week.
  • 2026 rule (through January 31, 2027): the last 30 and 90 days. 2027 rule: 28 days before and after the weekly check.
  • Bands under 14, 14 to 21 and 21 to 28 days, at Amazon's rate for the size tier and weight.
  • Exempt: new sellers, New Selection, AWD auto-replenished, under 20 units a week, grocery.

What it does not cover

United States only, from the rules and rates Amazon publishes. What Amazon actually charges is in FBA Inventory, SKU Economics and Transaction View. The plan assumes the steady daily sales you give it.

Read the full rules
  • 2026 rule (through January 31, 2027): historical days of supply is average daily units on hand divided by average daily units shipped, over the last 30 and the last 90 days, per FNSKU. Amazon charges only when both are below 28, at the band of the larger one, on every unit shipped that week. Standard-size and small and large bulky products only.
  • 2027 rule (from February 1, 2027): inventory at the weekly check divided by average daily units shipped in the 28 days before and the 28 days after it. Charged only when both are below 28, at the band of the higher one, on the units shipped in the first week after the check, about four weeks later, as its own line. Oversize tiers are added.
  • Bands: under 14 days, 14 to 21, 21 to 28. Rates from Amazon's tables by size tier and shipping weight: $0.32 to $2.09 a unit (2026), $0.32 to $3.50 (2027).
  • Exemptions Amazon lists: new Professional sellers for 365 days; New Selection products (FBA New Selection 180 days; New Selection Program (2026) the first 200 units within 120 days); SKUs 70% or more auto-replenished through AWD over the prior 90 days; products under 20 units in the past 7 days; grocery. No shipments in a window: no fee.
  • The plan: at your daily sales, the day FBA stock would fall under 28 days of sales (plus your safety days), less prep, transit and receiving days, is the send-by date; the units to send bring stock to the days you choose. A late plan shows the weeks under 28 days and, under the 2027 rule, what they cost.
