# Who does not pay the low-inventory-level fee

Amazon does not charge the low-inventory-level fee to new Professional sellers for 365 days, to New Selection products (the first 200 units within 120 days), to SKUs 70% or more auto-replenished through AWD over 90 days, to products under 20 units in the past 7 days, or to grocery: a candle at 13.3 days that shipped 10 units last week pays nothing.

From Amazon's Seller Central help, read November 8, 2026.

| Exemption | 2026 rule (to January 31, 2027) | 2027 rule (from February 1, 2027) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Professional seller | 365 days after the first inventory-received date | 365 days |
| New-to-FBA product | 180 days (FBA New Selection); New Selection Program (2026): first 200 units within 120 days | First 200 units within 120 days |
| AWD auto-replenishment | 70% or more of the SKU over the prior 90 days | 70% or more over the prior 90 days |
| Low volume | Sold under 20 units in the past 7 days | Shipped under 20 units in the past 7 days |
| Grocery | Exempt | Exempt |
| Size tier | Standard, small bulky and large bulky only (extra-large not charged) | All tiers, oversize included |
| No sales in a window | Days of supply above 28: no fee | Days of supply shown as 0 or "--": no fee |

## Two worked checks

- CANDLE-SM is exempt from the low-inventory-level fee even though both windows are under 28 days (30-day 13.3, 90-day 15.0): 10 units in the past 7 days, under Amazon's 20.
- LAMP-NEW is exempt from the low-inventory-level fee even though both windows are under 28 days (30-day 15.0, 90-day 20.0): New Selection Program (2026): day 50 of 120, 150 units since the first receipt (no fee on the first 200 units).

The New Selection window counts from the product's first inventory-received date (here September 20, 2026); the 120 days end January 18, 2027, and only the first 200 units are covered.

## In Amazon's words

2026 rule:

- "New Professional sellers, for the first 365 days after the first inventory-received date."
- "New-to-FBA parent products for the first 180 days after the first inventory-received date. You must be enrolled in FBA New Selection to get this benefit."
- "SKUs for which 70% or more of the inventory was auto-replenished through Amazon Warehousing and Distribution over the prior 90 days, because this program has managed that product’s replenishment into FBA on your behalf."
- "Products that have sold less than 20 units in the past 7 days."
- "Grocery category."

2027 rule:

- "New Professional sellers, for the first 365 days after the first inventory-received date."
- "New-to-FBA parent products for the first 120 days (first 200 units) after the first inventory-received date. You must be enrolled in FBA New Selection to get this benefit."
- "SKUs for which 70% or more of the inventory was auto-replenished through Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) over the prior 90 days, because AWD manages product replenishment into FBA on your behalf."
- "Products that have shipped less than 20 units in the past seven days."
- "Products in the grocery category."

## Questions

### Are new products exempt from the low-inventory fee?

Only through New Selection. Amazon's New Selection Program (2026) page lists "No low-inventory-level fee (first 200 units)." within 120 days of your first fba inbound date. The older FBA New Selection (180 days) ended for new products on July 30, 2026.

### Are slow sellers exempt?

Yes, under 20 units in the past 7 days, in both rules.

### Is a Vine pre-launch product exempt longer?

Amazon's New Selection Program (2026) page: "If your ASIN is enrolled in Vine pre-launch service, benefit windows extend by 45 days."
