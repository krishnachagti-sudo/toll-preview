# InventoryHero's reorder point calculator, compared

InventoryHero's free calculator reorders at 1,550 units for 30 a day, 45 days and 200 units of safety stock, which lands the shipment at about 7 days of supply, inside Amazon's low-inventory fee range; keeping 28 days of supply plus 7 safety days at arrival needs a reorder point of 2,400, which this calculator works out per SKU with the date.

## What InventoryHero says about itself

- The Reorder Point Calculator is free: reorder point = average daily sales x lead time + safety stock.
- Its worked example: 30 a day, 45 days lead time, 200 units of safety stock, reorder point 1,550.
- The paid planner prices by orders a month; Pro (1,001 to 5,000 orders) is $165.83 a month billed annually at $1,990.
- Every plan includes forecasting, stockout detection, supplier management and MCP access, with a 30-day free trial.

## When InventoryHero is better

When you want the planning done for you: forecasts from your sales history, purchase orders, supplier lead times, alerts the day a SKU crosses its point, and several channels in one place.

## When this calculator is better

When you plan yourself and want the fee test, the exact rate and the send date for a list of SKUs each week, for cents, with no account connection.
