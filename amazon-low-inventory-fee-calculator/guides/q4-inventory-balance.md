# Q4 stock: enough for 28 days, not so much it ages

At a peak of 40 a day the low-inventory line is 1,120 units: a gift set with 2,500 on hand on November 9, 2026 should send 700 units by November 28, 2026 (16 days of prep, transit and receiving) for 45 days of stock, and keep under 7,200 units so nothing ages past 180 days.

## The plan

Send 700 units by November 28, 2026 (3 prep + 6 transit + 7 receiving days) so they are in by December 14, 2026, before FBA stock falls under 1,120 units (28 days at 40 a day).

## After the peak

Through January 31, 2027 the 2026 rule applies: when sales drop after the holidays, the 30-day average falls and the 90-day one still carries December, so a sell-down can be charged only while both are under 28 and the SKU ships 20 or more a week. From February 1, 2027 the 2027 rule looks at the 28 days after each check too, which Amazon says reduces charges on end-of-season products.

## Storage changes on the same day

Amazon's 2027 announcement: from February 1, 2027 the storage utilization surcharge ends, and the aged inventory surcharge is folded into a unified monthly storage fee with age-based rates (higher from day 181).
