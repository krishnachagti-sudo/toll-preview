> The reorder plan assumes the steady daily sales you give it. Amazon does not publish which day of the week it checks inventory, so dates are to the day of your own plan.

## Steps

1. **Daily sales.** Take the daily sales you expect over the next weeks (the trailing 28- or 30-day average, or your forecast).
2. **Lead time.** Add prep, transit and Amazon receiving days.
3. **The 28-day line.** Find the day FBA stock would fall under 28 days of sales; the shipment must land by then.
4. **Send-by date.** That day less the lead time is the latest day to send.
5. **Units.** Days of stock you want x daily sales, less the stock left when it lands.
6. **Check the ceiling.** Keep it under about 180 days of sales so nothing ages into Amazon's aged-inventory storage rates.
