How a charge works

You see the price first.
A run that fails is refunded.

  1. 01

    Quote

    Ask what a call would cost before you make it: send the same input to the tool's quote endpoint, or use quote_price over MCP. Nothing runs, and nothing is charged or reserved.

  2. 02

    Reserve

    When a call starts, its full price is taken: on the web from a pass first, then from the free allowance, then from your credit. It is checked against your monthly limits, and a call past a limit is refused, not billed.

  3. 03

    Settle

    The tool runs. A tool that bills only for what it delivers refunds the rest in the same response. Every response says what was charged and your balance after it.

  4. 04

    Refund

    Invalid input is refused before any charge. If a tool fails while it runs, the charge is refunded straight away, to the part of your balance it came from.

FAQ

Questions about paying

How the API charges and refunds is in the developer guide.

Is there a subscription?

No. Passes and credit packs are bought once. Nothing renews, nothing tops itself up, and there is nothing to cancel.

Can I try it without paying?

Yes. Every tool runs on its page: 5 free runs a day without an account, and with an account $0.25 of free use each month, spent before your own money. You see your answer before you buy anything.

What does a pass cover?

Every live tool on its web page, including any tool added while your pass lasts. Each month it pays for up to $25 of runs at the prices listed below (the Practice Pass covers more): for example 2 per diem worksheets, 6 vehicle mileage logs, or 250 payees in a 1099 scan. If you use more than that in a month, the rest is paid from money you have added; with none added, those runs are refused, never billed. Calls from your own code or an AI agent (the API and MCP) are paid from credit, not the pass.

Pass or credit pack?

A pass if you use the tools on their pages, for your own work. Credit if you call them from code or an AI agent, or only need one or two answers. You can have both.

Does credit expire?

Money you add stays in your balance until you spend it, and so do the worksheets in a fleet bundle. The free monthly allowance and a pass's monthly allowance lapse at the end of each month (UTC).

Is there a nonprofit price?

Yes. A US nonprofit recognized as tax-exempt by the IRS pays 50% less for a pass. Ask from your account with the organization's name, its EIN and a contact email. A person checks the EIN in the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search and then emails a code. Nothing is approved automatically. The code works once, for the account with that email, and expires after 60 days.

Can we pay by invoice or check?

Yes, for the Team plan or any order of $250 or more. Ask from your account with what you want, the company name, a billing email and a PO number if you use one. Payment details come on the invoice. Your credit, pass or bundle starts when the payment arrives.

Can I get a refund?

Unused money you added can be refunded within 30 days of adding it, and a call that fails is refunded automatically. The refund policy, linked at the foot of every page, has the details.

Can a bug in my code drain my balance?

Set a hard monthly limit on your account and on each API key. A new key gets a limit of $5 unless you choose another. A call that would go past a limit is refused, not billed.

How do I know what a call will cost?

Every price is listed in words, on this page and on each tool's page. For the exact amount before you run anything, send the same input to the tool's quote endpoint, or ask quote_price over MCP. A quote runs nothing and charges nothing.

Which currency am I charged in?

US dollars. Every price, balance and charge is in US dollars.
