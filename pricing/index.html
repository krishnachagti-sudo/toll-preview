How a charge works

Quoted, reserved, settled.
Refunded if it fails.

  1. 01

    Quote

    Ask what a call would cost before you make it: send the same input to the tool's quote endpoint, or use quote_price over MCP. Nothing runs, and nothing is charged or reserved.

  2. 02

    Reserve

    When a call starts, its full price is taken from your balance, the free allowance first, and checked against your balance and your monthly limits. A call past a limit is refused, not billed.

  3. 03

    Settle

    The tool runs. A tool that bills only for what it delivers refunds the rest in the same response. Every response says what was charged and your balance after it.

  4. 04

    Refund

    Invalid input is refused before any charge. If a tool fails while it runs, the charge is refunded straight away. A refund goes back to the part of your balance it came from.

FAQ

Questions about paying

How the API charges and refunds is in the developer guide.

Is there a subscription?

No. You top up from $20 or ₹200, and each use takes its price from that balance. Nothing renews, and nothing tops itself up.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. Without an account you get 5 free runs a day on the tools' pages. With an account you also get $0.25 (₹20) free each month, spent before your own money.

Does my balance expire?

Money you add never expires. The free monthly allowance is given on the 1st of each month (UTC), and whatever is left of it expires at the end of that month.

Can a bug in my code drain my balance?

Set a hard monthly limit on your account and on each API key. A new key gets a limit of $5 / ₹400 unless you choose another. A call that would go past a limit is refused, not billed.

What happens if a call fails?

Invalid input is refused before any charge. If a tool fails while it runs, the charge is refunded straight away, and the response says so.

How do I know what a call will cost?

Every price is listed in words, on this page and on each tool's page. For the exact amount before you run anything, send the same input to the tool's quote endpoint, or ask quote_price over MCP. A quote runs nothing and charges nothing.

Which currency am I charged in?

Your account has one currency, US dollars or Indian rupees, chosen when you sign up. Every price and charge you see is in that currency.
