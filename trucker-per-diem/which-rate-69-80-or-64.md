# $69, $80 or $64: which per diem number is right

$80 is the current trucker per diem: the IRS rate has been $80 a day since October 1, 2024 (Notice 2026-60 keeps it to September 30, 2027). $69 was the rate from October 1, 2021 to September 30, 2024, and $64.00 is $80 after the 80% limit, a deductible amount, not a rate.

- Rate since October 1, 2024: $80
- Old rate: $69
- After 80%: $64.00

## Which rate do I use for which dates?

Each day takes the rate in force that day, so 2024 has both: $69 through September 30 and $80 from October 1. Every year is on the [trucker per diem rate history](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/trucker-per-diem/rate-history/).

## Why do some sites still say $69?

$69 was the rate for 3 notice years in a row (Notice 2021-52, Notice 2022-44, Notice 2023-68), long enough to be copied into many pages and calculators. Check the date on any page that quotes it: for travel from October 1, 2024 the rate is $80.

## Where does $64 come from?

It is $80 x 80%. Meals during DOT hours-of-service periods are 80% deductible (Publication 463), so $64.00 is the deductible part of a full day, not a rate. Use it only where a form asks for the deductible amount.

## The double cut

Tax software that asks whether you were subject to DOT hours of service takes 80% itself. Give it $64.00 a day and it takes 80% again. For 240 full and 40 departure or return days in 2026: the right deduction is $17,280.00; cut twice it is $13,824.00.

- Tax software that asks for your meal expenses and applies the limit itself (for example by ticking "subject to DOT hours of service"): enter the full per diem, $21,600.00.
- A form line that asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b): enter $17,280.00. Never take 80% of a figure that already had it applied.

## Questions

### Is the trucker per diem $69 or $80?

$80 for travel since October 1, 2024. $69 applied to travel from October 1, 2021 to September 30, 2024, so it is right only for those dates (amended or late returns).

### Why do some spreadsheets use $64 a day?

$64.00 is $80 x 80%, the deductible part for drivers under DOT hours of service. It belongs on Schedule C line 24b, not in tax software that applies the 80% itself.

### What happens if the 80% is taken twice?

The deduction shrinks: for 240 full and 40 partial days in 2026, $17,280.00 becomes $13,824.00.
