Per diem for truck drivers, 2025

The IRS transportation-industry rate for travel in 2025 is $80 a day all year (Notice 2024-68 to September 30, Notice 2025-54 from October 1), or $86 outside the continental US; 240 full days and 40 departure or return days come to $21,600.00 of per diem and a $17,280.00 deduction at the 80% hours-of-service limit.

The 2025 rates by day type

Notice 2024-68 covers travel to September 30, 2025 and Notice 2025-54 travel from October 1; both set $80 and $86, so the whole year has one rate.

2025 deduction by days away

Enter the full per diem in tax software that asks whether you were subject to DOT hours of service; it applies the 80% itself. Where a form asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b), enter the deductible column. See owner-operator per diem on Schedule C.

From October 1, 2025: Notice 2025-54

Notice 2025-54 sets $80 a day in the continental US and $86 outside it for travel on or after October 1, 2025, and keeps the incidental-expenses-only rate at $5. Its section 3 reads: "The special M&IE rates for taxpayers in the transportation industry are $80 for any locality of travel in the continental United States (CONUS) and $86 for any locality of travel outside the continental United States (OCONUS)."

The special transportation rate is used for every CONUS and OCONUS day of 2025; once used, it applies to all trips that calendar year, and this driver cannot switch to federal locality M&IE rates until January 1, 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2019-48 secs. 4.04(3) and 4.06(2); Publication 463).

Please read

  • Figures computed from the IRS notices named on each row, Rev. Proc. 2019-48 and Publication 463; not tax advice. Whether you are away from your tax home, in the transportation industry and subject to DOT hours of service is your call or your preparer's.
  • Partial days use 3/4 of the daily rate, as Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 requires for the self-employed per diem method; Publication 463 also describes other methods, so ask your preparer if you use one.
  • Enter the full per diem in tax software that applies the 80% limit itself; enter the deductible figure only where the form asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b).
  • Company (W-2) drivers cannot deduct unreimbursed meals under current law; days your carrier or employer reimbursed cannot also be deducted.
  • This worksheet counts what you log; keep your ELD records, load sheets and receipts as evidence for at least 3 years (6 if income is under-reported by over 25%).

Questions

What is the per diem rate for truck drivers in 2025?

$80 a day in the continental US and $86 outside it, for all of 2025 (Notice 2024-68 to September 30, Notice 2025-54 from October 1).

How much is a partial day in 2025?

3/4 of the day's rate: $60 in the continental US, on the days you leave and get back.

Is 2025 per diem 80% or 50% deductible for truckers?

80% for meals during periods subject to DOT hours-of-service limits, which covers interstate truck operators under DOT regulations; 50% otherwise. 240 full and 40 partial days are $17,280.00 at 80% and $10,800.00 at 50%.
