Per diem or actual meal costs

Per diem wins unless your real meal spending tops the rate: in 2026, 240 full and 40 departure or return days at $80 are $21,600.00, so actual costs beat it only above $77.14 a day on average across all 280 days, and only with receipts. Both are 80% deductible for drivers under DOT hours of service.

Which gives the bigger deduction?

The one with the bigger total before the 80% limit, since the same limit applies to both. Per diem prices every full day at $80 and every departure or return day at $60.00. Actual cost is what you spent, day by day, with no 3/4 rule.

Break-even: $21,600.00 / 280 days = $77.14 a day. Spend less on average, and per diem gives more. Spend more, and actual costs give more, if every meal has a receipt.

What records does each method need?

Publication 463: with actual costs, "you must keep records of your actual cost." With the standard meal allowance, "you must still keep records to prove the time, place, and business purpose of your travel." For per diem that is a days-away log, not receipts.

Can I mix them?

Not within a year for meals. Publication 463 says that if you use the special transportation rate for any trip, you must use it for all trips that year, and Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 4.06(2) lets you change only from January 1. Lodging is separate: per diem here covers meals and incidentals only, and a motel stay is deducted at its actual cost.

Please read

  • Figures computed from the IRS notices named on each row, Rev. Proc. 2019-48 and Publication 463; not tax advice. Whether you are away from your tax home, in the transportation industry and subject to DOT hours of service is your call or your preparer's.
  • Partial days use 3/4 of the daily rate, as Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 requires for the self-employed per diem method; Publication 463 also describes other methods, so ask your preparer if you use one.
  • Enter the full per diem in tax software that applies the 80% limit itself; enter the deductible figure only where the form asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b).
  • Company (W-2) drivers cannot deduct unreimbursed meals under current law; days your carrier or employer reimbursed cannot also be deducted.
  • This worksheet counts what you log; keep your ELD records, load sheets and receipts as evidence for at least 3 years (6 if income is under-reported by over 25%).

Questions

Is per diem better than actual meal costs for truckers?

For most drivers, yes: it needs no meal receipts and pays $80 a full day whatever you spent. Actual costs win only if you spend more than that on meals on average and keep the receipts.

Do I need receipts for per diem?

Not for the meal amounts. Publication 463: with the standard meal allowance you must still keep records to prove the time, place and business purpose of your travel. Actual costs need records of what you spent.

Can I switch methods during the year?

Not between the special transportation rate and other rates. Publication 463 says if you use the special rate for any trip, you must use it for all trips that year; Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 4.06(2) lets you switch from January 1.
