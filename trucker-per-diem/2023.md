# Per diem for truck drivers, 2023

The IRS transportation-industry rate for travel in 2023 is $69 a day all year (Notice 2022-44 to September 30, Notice 2023-68 from October 1), or $74 outside the continental US; 240 full days and 40 departure or return days come to $18,630.00 of per diem and a $14,904.00 deduction at the 80% hours-of-service limit.

- Deduction, 240 + 40 days: $14,904.00
- Full per diem: $18,630.00
- Rate, continental US: $69
- Rate outside: $74

## From October 1, 2023: Notice 2023-68

Notice 2023-68 sets $69 a day in the continental US and $74 outside it for travel on or after October 1, 2023, and keeps the incidental-expenses-only rate at $5. Its section 3 reads: "The special M&IE rates for taxpayers in the transportation industry are $69 for any locality of travel in the continental United States (CONUS) and $74 for any locality of travel outside the continental United States (OCONUS)."

> The special transportation rate is used for every CONUS and OCONUS day of 2023; once used, it applies to all trips that calendar year, and this driver cannot switch to federal locality M&IE rates until January 1, 2024 (Rev. Proc. 2019-48 secs. 4.04(3) and 4.06(2); Publication 463).

## Please read

- Figures computed from the IRS notices named on each row, Rev. Proc. 2019-48 and Publication 463; not tax advice. Whether you are away from your tax home, in the transportation industry and subject to DOT hours of service is your call or your preparer's.
- Partial days use 3/4 of the daily rate, as Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 requires for the self-employed per diem method; Publication 463 also describes other methods, so ask your preparer if you use one.
- Enter the full per diem in tax software that applies the 80% limit itself; enter the deductible figure only where the form asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b).
- Company (W-2) drivers cannot deduct unreimbursed meals under current law; days your carrier or employer reimbursed cannot also be deducted.
- This worksheet counts what you log; keep your ELD records, load sheets and receipts as evidence for at least 3 years (6 if income is under-reported by over 25%).

## Questions

### What is the per diem rate for truck drivers in 2023?

$69 a day in the continental US and $74 outside it, for all of 2023 (Notice 2022-44 to September 30, Notice 2023-68 from October 1).

### How much is a partial day in 2023?

3/4 of the day's rate: $51.75 in the continental US, on the days you leave and get back.

### Is 2023 per diem 80% or 50% deductible for truckers?

80% for meals during periods subject to DOT hours-of-service limits, which covers interstate truck operators under DOT regulations; 50% otherwise. 240 full and 40 partial days are $14,904.00 at 80% and $9,315.00 at 50%.
