Owner-operator taxes

Trucker per diem worksheet

What you get

Log the days you were out on the road, or just count them. Each day is priced at the IRS transportation-industry rate in force that day, departure and return days at 3/4, then the 80% hours-of-service limit, ending in the number for Schedule C line 24b and the number to type into tax software.

The totals on this page are free. The worksheet for your preparer (PDF and CSV) is $9.00 per driver and tax year, and a later run for the same driver and year is $0.10 for 15 months.

A fleet or a preparer buys one worksheet for each driver and each tax year. Every worksheet names the IRS notice and the daily rate it used on each row, so the preparer can check it against irs.gov.

What this box reads, and ELD exports

One trip per line: the day you left, "to", the day you got back, then "Canada" (or OCONUS) for trips outside the lower 48, then any load number. The first and last days count as departure and return days.

Dates must be written year first, like 2025-03-02 to 2025-03-13 Load 1180; a one-day trip can be a single date. From an ELD or load-board export, copy each trip's leave and return dates into this format. In a spreadsheet with the leave date in column A and the return date in column B, =TEXT(A2,"yyyy-mm-dd")&" to "&TEXT(B2,"yyyy-mm-dd") writes the line for you; paste the column here.

    The rules

    The rate on each day, not one rate for the year

    Each day is priced at the rate of the notice in force that day.

    What it does not cover

    Company (W-2) drivers, days your carrier paid, other per diem methods, and whether you qualify, which is your call or your preparer's.

    Read the full list
    • Figures computed from the IRS notices named on each row, Rev. Proc. 2019-48 and Publication 463; not tax advice. Whether you are away from your tax home, in the transportation industry and subject to DOT hours of service is your call or your preparer's.
    • Partial days use 3/4 of the daily rate, as Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 requires for the self-employed per diem method; Publication 463 also describes other methods, so ask your preparer if you use one.
    • Enter the full per diem in tax software that applies the 80% limit itself; enter the deductible figure only where the form asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b).
    • Company (W-2) drivers cannot deduct unreimbursed meals under current law; days your carrier or employer reimbursed cannot also be deducted.
    • This worksheet counts what you log; keep your ELD records, load sheets and receipts as evidence for at least 3 years (6 if income is under-reported by over 25%).

    A new notice every October 1

    The rate changes each October 1, so a year spans two notices.

    IRS Notice 2026-60

    Read the full rules

    The IRS sets the transportation-industry rate in a notice each fall, for travel on or after October 1. A calendar year spans two notices, and in two years the rate changed partway: 2021 ($66, then $69 from October 1) and 2024 ($69, then $80). Every row of the worksheet names the notice behind its rate.

    3/4 on the days you leave and get back

    Departure and return days are 3/4 of the rate: $60 of $80.

    Rev. Proc. 2019-48

    Read the full rules

    A full day runs from 12:01 a.m. to midnight. Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 makes the self-employed use 3/4 of the rate for departure and return days, so $60 of $80. Publication 463 also describes "any consistent method"; that option is written for employers, so this tool uses 3/4 and says so.

    80% under hours of service, once

    80% deductible under DOT hours of service, applied once.

    IRS Publication 463

    Read the full rules

    Meals during DOT hours-of-service duty are 80% deductible (50% otherwise), and 100% for 2021 and 2022 under Notice 2021-63. Tax software that asks about hours of service applies the 80% itself, so give it the full per diem; Schedule C line 24b takes the deductible amount. Applying 80% twice loses a fifth of it.

    Every rate on file

    Loading the rates.

    Method lock, incidental-only days and record keeping

    Once you use the special rate, use it for every trip that calendar year; you can switch to federal locality rates only from January 1 (Rev. Proc. 2019-48 secs. 4.04(3), 4.06(2)). Incidental-expenses-only days ($5) are for days with no meal costs; they are not limited and go on line 24a. Days your carrier or employer paid per diem cannot also be deducted (sec. 6.06). Carriers need keep duty-status records and ELD back-ups only 6 months (49 CFR 395.8(k), 395.22(i)), so export yours every month or quarter.
