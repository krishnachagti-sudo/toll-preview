# Per diem outside the continental US

Days outside the continental US, including Canada, Alaska and Hawaii, take the outside rate: $86 a day instead of $80, and $64.50 on a departure or return day. 20 full and 4 partial days in Canada in a 2026 year of 240 and 40 add $138.00 of per diem.

- Outside, full day: $86
- Outside, partial day: $64.50
- 2026 with 24 Canada days: $21,738.00

## What rate applies to days in Canada?

The outside rate. Notice 2026-60 section 3 sets the special transportation-industry M&IE rate at $80 for any locality in the continental United States and $86 for any locality outside it. Canada, Mexico, Alaska and Hawaii are all outside. The 3/4 rule for departure and return days applies the same way: $64.50.

## Worked example: a 2026 year with Canada runs

Worked out by the worksheet at Notice 2026-60 rates, 80% hours-of-service limit.

| Days | Continental US | Outside | Per diem | Deductible |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All 280 days in the lower 48 | 240 + 40 | 0 | $21,600.00 | $17,280.00 |
| 24 of them in Canada | 220 + 36 | 20 + 4 | $21,738.00 | $17,390.40 |

The difference is 20 x $6 + 4 x $4.50 = $138.00 of per diem.

## Please read

- Figures computed from the IRS notices named on each row, Rev. Proc. 2019-48 and Publication 463; not tax advice. Whether you are away from your tax home, in the transportation industry and subject to DOT hours of service is your call or your preparer's.
- Partial days use 3/4 of the daily rate, as Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 requires for the self-employed per diem method; Publication 463 also describes other methods, so ask your preparer if you use one.
- Enter the full per diem in tax software that applies the 80% limit itself; enter the deductible figure only where the form asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b).
- Company (W-2) drivers cannot deduct unreimbursed meals under current law; days your carrier or employer reimbursed cannot also be deducted.
- This worksheet counts what you log; keep your ELD records, load sheets and receipts as evidence for at least 3 years (6 if income is under-reported by over 25%).

## Questions

### What is the per diem rate for truck drivers in Canada?

$86 a day under Notice 2026-60, which sets $86 for "any locality of travel outside the continental United States (OCONUS)". A partial day is $64.50.

### Do Alaska and Hawaii count as outside the continental US?

Yes. Publication 463 says the continental standard rates do not apply to travel in Alaska, Hawaii or any other place outside the continental United States.

### Do I need to convert Canadian dollars?

No. The rate is a fixed US-dollar amount per day ($86), not a reimbursement of what you spent, so there is nothing to convert.

### Which rate applies on the day I cross the border?

The IRS notice sets the rate by locality of travel and does not single out border days. Ask your preparer which locality to use for them, and apply the same rule every time.
