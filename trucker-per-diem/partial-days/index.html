Partial days: the 3/4 rule

A departure or return day is 3/4 of the day's rate: $60 of $80 in 2026 ($64.50 of $86 outside the continental US), and $51.75 of $69 for travel before October 1, 2024.

What the revenue procedure says

Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04: "A taxpayer must use the method described in section 6.04(1) of this revenue procedure for purposes of determining the amount deemed substantiated for meal and incidental expenses or for incidental expenses only under section 4.03, 4.05, or 5 of this revenue procedure for partial days of travel away from home." Section 6.04(1) is the Federal Travel Regulations method, "by allocating three-fourths of the applicable rate to each partial day of travel".

Publication 463 lists two methods for departure and return days: 3/4 of the allowance, or any method applied consistently that is in line with reasonable business practice. The revenue procedure keeps the second for payors reimbursing employees under secs. 4.01, 4.02 and 5, so the worksheet uses 3/4 for owner-operators and says so.

A trip from Monday to Friday is three full days and two partial days: in 2026, 3 x $80 + 2 x $60 = $360 of per diem, $288 at the 80% limit.

Incidental-expenses-only days ($5) are prorated the same way (Publication 463) and are not subject to the 80% limit.

Please read

  • Figures computed from the IRS notices named on each row, Rev. Proc. 2019-48 and Publication 463; not tax advice. Whether you are away from your tax home, in the transportation industry and subject to DOT hours of service is your call or your preparer's.
  • Partial days use 3/4 of the daily rate, as Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 requires for the self-employed per diem method; Publication 463 also describes other methods, so ask your preparer if you use one.
  • Enter the full per diem in tax software that applies the 80% limit itself; enter the deductible figure only where the form asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b).
  • Company (W-2) drivers cannot deduct unreimbursed meals under current law; days your carrier or employer reimbursed cannot also be deducted.
  • This worksheet counts what you log; keep your ELD records, load sheets and receipts as evidence for at least 3 years (6 if income is under-reported by over 25%).

Questions

How much is a partial day of per diem for truckers?

3/4 of the day's rate: $60 of $80 in the continental US and $64.50 of $86 outside it, for travel since October 1, 2024.

Can an owner-operator use another way to prorate partial days?

Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 says a taxpayer must use the 3/4 method for the self-employed M&IE method; the "any consistent method" option in sec. 6.04(2) is written for payors reimbursing employees. Publication 463 describes both methods, so ask your preparer if you use another.

Is a day I leave after midnight a full day?

A full day of travel runs from 12:01 a.m. to midnight (Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04). The day you leave home and the day you get back are partial days.
