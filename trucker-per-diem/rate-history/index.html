Trucker per diem rate history

The IRS transportation-industry M&IE rate was $66 in 2019, $66 in 2020, $66 then $69 in 2021 and $69 in 2022 (continental US), set each October 1 by notice; it rose from $69 to $80 on October 1, 2024, and per diem was 100% deductible in 2021 and 2022.

2022

$69 a day all year (Notice 2021-52 and Notice 2022-44), $74 outside the continental US. The meal portion of 2022 per diem was 100% deductible: Notice 2021-63 lets it be treated as restaurant food under the temporary IRC 274(n)(2)(D) exception. 240 full and 40 partial days come to $18,630.00 of per diem and a $18,630.00 deduction.

2021

$66 a day through September 30 (Notice 2020-71) and $69 from October 1 (Notice 2021-52), $71 then $74 outside the continental US. The meal portion of 2021 per diem was 100% deductible: Notice 2021-63 lets it be treated as restaurant food under the temporary IRC 274(n)(2)(D) exception. 240 full and 40 partial days come to $18,022.50 of per diem and a $18,022.50 deduction.

2020

$66 a day all year (Notice 2019-55 and Notice 2020-71), $71 outside the continental US. Meals during DOT hours-of-service periods were 80% deductible. 240 full and 40 partial days come to $17,820.00 of per diem and a $14,256.00 deduction.

2019

$66 a day all year (Notice 2018-77 and Notice 2019-55), $71 outside the continental US. Meals during DOT hours-of-service periods were 80% deductible. 240 full and 40 partial days come to $17,820.00 of per diem and a $14,256.00 deduction.

Travel before November 26, 2019 falls under Rev. Proc. 2011-47, which already required the 3/4 method for partial days; Rev. Proc. 2019-48 applies after that date.

Please read

  • Figures computed from the IRS notices named on each row, Rev. Proc. 2019-48 and Publication 463; not tax advice. Whether you are away from your tax home, in the transportation industry and subject to DOT hours of service is your call or your preparer's.
  • Partial days use 3/4 of the daily rate, as Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 requires for the self-employed per diem method; Publication 463 also describes other methods, so ask your preparer if you use one.
  • Enter the full per diem in tax software that applies the 80% limit itself; enter the deductible figure only where the form asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b).
  • Company (W-2) drivers cannot deduct unreimbursed meals under current law; days your carrier or employer reimbursed cannot also be deducted.
  • This worksheet counts what you log; keep your ELD records, load sheets and receipts as evidence for at least 3 years (6 if income is under-reported by over 25%).

Questions

What was the per diem rate for truck drivers in 2022?

$69 a day in the continental US and $74 outside it (Notice 2021-52 to September 30, Notice 2022-44 from October 1), and the meal portion was 100% deductible in 2022 under Notice 2021-63.

What was the trucker per diem rate in 2021?

$66 a day through September 30, 2021 (Notice 2020-71) and $69 from October 1 (Notice 2021-52), 100% deductible under Notice 2021-63.

What were the 2019 and 2020 per diem rates for truck drivers?

$66 a day in the continental US and $71 outside it, all of 2019 and 2020 (Notices 2018-77, 2019-55 and 2020-71), 80% deductible under DOT hours of service.

When did the trucker per diem go to $80?

For travel on or after October 1, 2024 (Notice 2024-68). Notices 2025-54 and 2026-60 kept $80.
