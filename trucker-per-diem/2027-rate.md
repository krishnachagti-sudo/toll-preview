# Trucker per diem rate for 2027

The IRS transportation-industry per diem for 2027 is $80 a day in the continental US and $86 outside it for travel through September 30, 2027 (Notice 2026-60); the rate for October 1, 2027 on is set by the next annual notice and is not published yet. 180 full days and 30 departure or return days from January to September 2027 come to $16,200.00, a $12,960.00 deduction at 80%.

- 180 + 30 days, January to September 2027: $16,200.00
- Deduction at 80%: $12,960.00
- Continental US: $80
- Outside: $86

## What is the per diem for truck drivers in 2027?

Notice 2026-60, section 3: "The special M&IE rates for taxpayers in the transportation industry are $80 for any locality of travel in the continental United States (CONUS) and $86 for any locality of travel outside the continental United States (OCONUS)." It covers travel from October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027. Departure and return days are 3/4 of the day: $60.00.

## When does the rate change?

Every October 1. Each notice covers October 1 to September 30, so a calendar tax year uses two notices: one for January to September and one for October to December. When the two rates differ, each day takes the rate in force that day; 2024 was such a year ($69, then $80).

## Please read

- Figures computed from the IRS notices named on each row, Rev. Proc. 2019-48 and Publication 463; not tax advice. Whether you are away from your tax home, in the transportation industry and subject to DOT hours of service is your call or your preparer's.
- Partial days use 3/4 of the daily rate, as Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 6.04 requires for the self-employed per diem method; Publication 463 also describes other methods, so ask your preparer if you use one.
- Enter the full per diem in tax software that applies the 80% limit itself; enter the deductible figure only where the form asks for the deductible amount (Schedule C line 24b).
- Company (W-2) drivers cannot deduct unreimbursed meals under current law; days your carrier or employer reimbursed cannot also be deducted.
- This worksheet counts what you log; keep your ELD records, load sheets and receipts as evidence for at least 3 years (6 if income is under-reported by over 25%).

## Questions

### What is the trucker per diem rate for 2027?

$80 a day in the continental US and $86 outside it, for travel from January 1 through September 30, 2027 (Notice 2026-60). The rate from October 1, 2027 is not published yet.

### When does the trucker per diem rate change?

On October 1. Each annual IRS notice sets the special transportation-industry rates for travel from October 1 of one year to September 30 of the next.

### When is the new rate announced?

In an IRS notice before or around October 1. Notice 2023-68 was printed in Internal Revenue Bulletin 2023-41 (October 10, 2023); Notice 2024-68 was printed in Internal Revenue Bulletin 2024-41 (October 7, 2024); Notice 2025-54 was printed in Internal Revenue Bulletin 2025-41 (October 6, 2025); Notice 2026-60 was printed in Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-43 (October 19, 2026).

### Is 2027 per diem still 80% deductible?

Yes for meals during DOT hours-of-service periods, which covers interstate truck drivers under DOT rules (Publication 463). Otherwise 50%.
