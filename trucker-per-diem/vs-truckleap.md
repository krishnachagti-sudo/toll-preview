# Truckleap per diem calculator and this worksheet

Truckleap's free per diem calculator asks for work days, a daily rate you type in and your tax bracket, with no sign-up; the Truckleap page listed $69 a day for 2024 to 2026 when checked on November 6, 2026, while the IRS rate is $80 since October 1, 2024. The worksheet here prices each day at the rate in force, 3/4 on partial days: 240 full and 40 partial days in 2026 are $17,280.00 deductible.

- IRS rate since October 1, 2024: $80
- Deduction, 240 + 40 days in 2026: $17,280.00

## How do they differ?

Facts about Truckleap come from its per diem calculator page, checked on November 6, 2026. Anything not listed was not checked.

|  | Truckleap per diem calculator | Endwise trucker per diem worksheet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inputs | Work days, a per diem rate per day, your tax bracket | Full and partial days, or trips by date with a locality |
| Rate | Typed in; the page listed $69 a day for 2024 to 2026 | IRS rate in force each day, from the notices ($80 since October 1, 2024) |
| Partial days at 3/4 | Mentioned in its guide; no partial-day input | Yes |
| 80% hours-of-service limit | Explained on the page | Applied, with 50% for drivers not under hours of service |
| Days outside the continental US | Not addressed on the page | Yes, at $86 |
| October 1 rate change | Not mentioned | Split automatically by date |
| Days log and checks | Suggests keeping a spreadsheet of nights away | Log mode with checks, monthly table, PDF and CSV worksheet |
| Price | Free, no sign-up | Quick totals free on the page (5 free runs a day); a paid worksheet over the web, REST API or MCP |

## When is Truckleap the better choice?

- You want an estimate of tax saved, from your bracket, in a few seconds.
- You know your rate and day count and do not need a log or a worksheet for a preparer.

## When is the worksheet the better choice?

- You need the right rate for each day without looking it up, including the October 1 change in a split year.
- You have departure and return days, or days in Canada.
- You want a log your preparer can rely on, with both numbers: the full per diem for software and the 80% amount for Schedule C line 24b.

## Questions

### Is the Truckleap per diem calculator free?

Its site says "No signup. No paywalls." (checked November 6, 2026).

### Which rate does the Truckleap calculator use?

You type the rate in. When checked on November 6, 2026 the page listed "$69/day (M&IE, DOT)" for 2024 to 2026; IRS Notice 2024-68 set $80 for travel from October 1, 2024, and Notices 2025-54 and 2026-60 kept it.

### When is Truckleap the better choice?

When you want a quick estimate of the tax a per diem deduction saves, from work days and your tax bracket, without logging any days.
