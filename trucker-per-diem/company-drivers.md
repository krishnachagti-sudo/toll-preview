# Company drivers and per diem

Company (W-2) drivers cannot deduct unreimbursed meals: Publication 463 calls it a miscellaneous itemized deduction that is no longer allowable. An employer can pay per diem free of tax up to the transportation rate under an accountable plan: for 240 full and 40 departure or return days in 2026, up to $21,600.00 that is not wages.

- Tax-free ceiling, 240 + 40 days: $21,600.00
- 22 days in one month: $1,760.00

## Why a company driver cannot deduct meals

Publication 463 says a deduction for unreimbursed employee travel is a miscellaneous itemized deduction that is no longer allowable, and that an employee entitled to a reimbursement who does not claim it cannot deduct the expense either. The per diem worksheet is for owner-operators filing Schedule C.

## Is employer per diem taxable?

Under an accountable plan, the part of a meal per diem up to the amount deemed substantiated is not wages and is not on the W-2 (Rev. Proc. 2019-48 sec. 7.03). Any excess is wages, subject to withholding and employment taxes (secs. 7.04 and 8.01).

For a driver in the transportation industry, the carrier may use the special rate for every day away and compare at least monthly (sec. 4.04(4)): 22 full days away in a month of 2026 allows up to $1,760.00. A per diem figured from miles driven is treated as a meal allowance (sec. 4.02).

> A carrier reimbursing employees may prorate departure and return days at 3/4 or by another consistent method (sec. 6.04(2)); the figures here use 3/4.

## Questions

### Can a company driver claim per diem?

Not as a deduction: Publication 463 says unreimbursed employee travel expenses are a miscellaneous itemized deduction that is no longer allowable. Company drivers benefit when the employer pays per diem.

### Is per diem pay taxable for company drivers?

Per diem up to the amount deemed substantiated (the transportation rate for each day away) is not reported as wages under an accountable plan; any excess is wages, subject to withholding (Rev. Proc. 2019-48 secs. 7.03, 7.04 and 8.01).
