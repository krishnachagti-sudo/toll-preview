# We forgot the disclosure

Send each buyer a corrected receipt with the good faith estimate and the deductible amount (for a $150.00 ticket with a $60.00 dinner, $90.00); the penalty for the missed disclosures is $10 each, at most $5,000 for the event, and is avoided if the failure was due to reasonable cause.

## The corrected receipt

**This corrects our receipt dated November 16, 2026 for your payment of $150.00 for the Harvest Gala. Thank you for your payment of $150.00 to Riverside Food Bank for the Harvest Gala. In exchange for your payment you received dinner and dance, with an estimated fair market value of $60.00. The amount of your contribution that is deductible for federal income tax purposes is limited to the excess of your payment over the value of the goods and services we provided: $90.00. Please keep this corrected receipt with your tax records in place of the earlier one. Riverside Food Bank**

## What it could cost

Worked out by the checker: $10 each, capped per event or mailing.

| Receipts without the disclosure | Penalty |
| ---: | ---: |
| 120 | $1,200.00 |
| 300 | $3,000.00 |
| 600 | $5,000.00 |

The penalty is $10 per contribution, not to exceed $5,000 per fundraising event or mailing. The charity can avoid the penalty if it can show that the failure was due to reasonable cause.

## Steps

1. **List who paid what.** Export the event's payments with the value of what each buyer received.
2. **Find the receipts that needed it.** Every payment over $75 with a benefit that is not of insubstantial value. The checker's list check marks them.
3. **Send corrected receipts.** One per payment, stating the good faith estimate and the deductible limit, saying it replaces the earlier receipt.
4. **Keep the record.** Keep the list, the method behind your estimate and the dates the corrections went out.
5. **Fix the template.** Put the disclosure on the invitation or ticket page and on the receipt for the next event.

## Questions

### Can we send the disclosure after the event?

An organization must furnish a disclosure statement in connection with either the solicitation or the receipt of the quid pro quo contribution. A correction after the event does not change when it was due, but it gives donors the right figures before they file.

### Can the penalty be avoided?

The charity can avoid the penalty if it can show that the failure was due to reasonable cause.
