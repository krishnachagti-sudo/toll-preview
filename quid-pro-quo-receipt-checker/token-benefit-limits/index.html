Token benefit limits, every year

The IRS token limits rise each year with inflation: for 2027 the low-cost article limit is $14.33, the minimum gift $71.65 and the cap $143.30, up from $13.90, $69.50 and $139 in 2026; the 1990 base figures were $5, $25 and $50.

How to use them

  • the fair market value of the goods and services the donor receives does not exceed the lesser of 2 percent of the donor's payment or the year's cap.
  • donor's payment is at least the year's minimum, the only goods or services the charitable organization provides bear the organization's name or logo (for example, calendars, mugs, or posters), and the cost of these items, in the aggregate, is within the limit for "low-cost articles," which is the year's low-cost article limit.
  • An annual membership benefit is also considered to be insubstantial if it is provided in exchange for an annual payment of $75 or less and consists of annual recurring rights or privileges.
  • Admission to events that are open only to members and the cost per person of which is within the limits for low-cost articles described in Revenue Procedures 90-12 and 92-49 (as adjusted for inflation).

*The dollar amounts are for 2023. Guideline amounts are adjusted for inflation. Publication 1771 still prints the 2023 figures; the table above has the current ones.

Questions

What are the 2027 token limits?

$14.33 low-cost article, $71.65 minimum gift, $143.30 cap (Rev. Proc. 2026-35).

Which year's limits apply?

The year of the payment: each revenue procedure gives the figures "for taxable years beginning in" that year.
