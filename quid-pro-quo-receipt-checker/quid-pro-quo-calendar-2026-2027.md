# Receipt calendar, November 2026 to December 2027

Payments through Thursday, December 31, 2026 use the 2026 limits; from Friday, January 1, 2027 the 2027 limits from Rev. Proc. 2026-35 apply ($14.33, $71.65, $143.30). GivingTuesday is Tuesday, December 1, 2026, and donors need their 2026 acknowledgments by Thursday, April 15, 2027 unless they extend.

## The calendar

Weekdays and business days computed with the US federal holiday calendar; GivingTuesday is the Tuesday after the fourth Thursday of November.

| Date | What | The rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thursday, November 5, 2026 | Rev. Proc. 2026-35 dated | The 2027 limits are out: $14.33, $71.65 and $143.30. |
| Tuesday, December 1, 2026 | GivingTuesday 2026 | Gifts that day are 2026 gifts; event tickets sold that day follow the 2026 limits. |
| Thursday, December 31, 2026 | Last day of the 2026 limits | Premiums for 2026 payments are tested at $13.90, $69.50 and $139.00; raffle prizes paid in 2026 are reported from $2,000. |
| Friday, January 1, 2027 | The 2027 limits apply | Payments in 2027: $14.33 low-cost article, $71.65 minimum gift, $143.30 cap; the section 6041(a) reporting threshold is $2,100. |
| Sunday, January 31, 2027 | January 31, 2027 | IRS Pub 1771: "Charities typically send written acknowledgments to donors no later than January 31 of the year following the donation." |
| Thursday, April 15, 2027 | Usual due date of a 2026 return | Donors need their acknowledgments for 2026 gifts before filing, and by this date at the latest without an extension. |
| Friday, October 15, 2027 | Extended due date of a 2026 return | The outer limit for a donor who extended. |
| Tuesday, November 30, 2027 | GivingTuesday 2027 | Gifts that day are 2027 gifts. |
| Friday, December 31, 2027 | Last day of the 2027 limits | The 2028 figures come in the next revenue procedure. |

Gala and auction season runs mostly October to December and March to May: check each event's receipt wording before the invitations go out, since the disclosure can be given with the solicitation.

## Questions

### Which limits apply to a gala on December 31, 2026?

The 2026 limits: the payment's year decides.

### When will the 2028 limits come out?

In the IRS revenue procedure for 2028. The PDFs of the last three are dated October 22, 2024, October 17, 2025 and November 5, 2026.
