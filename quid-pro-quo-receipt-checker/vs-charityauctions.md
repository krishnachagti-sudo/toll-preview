# Auction software receipts or the checker

CharityAuctions.com runs the whole auction for a 5% success fee ($0 to start, capped at $100 an item) or $499 plus 3% (capped at $75 an item), and its article describes FMV-aware receipts; for an event run on paper or another tool, the checker words and checks the receipts for $2.00 an event or $0.01 a row.

## What CharityAuctions.com offers

- Nothing Out of Pocket: 5% success fee only after you raise money, $0 to start; success fee capped at $100 per item.
- Prepay and Save: $499 plus 3%, success fee capped at $75 per item, event setup and Message Blast.
- Modules on every plan: online auction, ticket sales, paddle raise, raffle items, sponsor logos, golf outing management.
- Its silent auction article says winners "need FMV on the receipt" and describes automated FMV-aware receipts.

> From CharityAuctions.com's own page, read November 8, 2026. Plans and features change; check their page.

## What Endwise's checker does

- Checks the receipt text you already send: the estimate, the deductible limit, and a wrong "no goods or services" or "fully deductible" line.
- Applies the token, membership, religious and sponsorship exceptions with each year's IRS limits (2020 to 2027).
- Free single checks; $2.00 for an event's wording at every ticket level; $0.01 a row to check a list, with the penalty exposure; at most $9.00 a month.

## When CharityAuctions.com is the better choice

- You want mobile bidding, checkout and receipts in one place.
- The event is big enough that the platform's features are worth its fee.

## When the checker is the better choice

- The auction ran on paper bid sheets or another tool, and only the receipts need doing.
- You want the token, raffle and sponsorship rules checked, not only auction lots.
- A 5% fee on a $20,000 auction is $1,000; a list check of 300 winners is $3.00.
