# A silent auction receipt spreadsheet or the checker

Spreadsheet Daddy's silent auction receipt template is free in Google Sheets, Excel, Word, Google Docs and PDF and totals items, quantities, unit costs and a tax rate, but its page lists no fair market value or deductible field; a $450.00 winning bid on a $300.00 item needs the $150.00 deductible part and the disclosure, which the checker writes.

## What Spreadsheet Daddy's silent auction receipt template offers

- Free download in Google Sheets, Excel, Word, Google Docs and PDF (page updated September 25, 2026).
- Organization and recipient details, receipt date and number, payment method.
- An item table with Description, Quantity, Unit Cost and Amount; a subtotal, a tax rate and tax amount, and a total.
- A notes field for "donation acknowledgments" and pickup details.

> From Spreadsheet Daddy's silent auction receipt template's own page, read November 8, 2026. Plans and features change; check their page.

## What Endwise's checker does

- Checks the receipt text you already send: the estimate, the deductible limit, and a wrong "no goods or services" or "fully deductible" line.
- Applies the token, membership, religious and sponsorship exceptions with each year's IRS limits (2020 to 2027).
- Free single checks; $2.00 for an event's wording at every ticket level; $0.01 a row to check a list, with the penalty exposure; at most $9.00 a month.

## When Spreadsheet Daddy's silent auction receipt template is the better choice

- You want an editable layout for checkout at the event and already work in Excel or Sheets.
- You need a sales-style receipt with quantities and a tax line.

## When the checker is the better choice

- The receipt must carry the item's value and the deductible part (a bid over $75 above the value needs the disclosure).
- A bid at or below value must say nothing is deductible.
- You have a winners list to check at once.
