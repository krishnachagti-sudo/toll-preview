How long is 2 business days?

2 business days from today, Sunday, November 8, 2026 (the date in Washington, DC), is Tuesday, November 10, 2026 in the US, 2 calendar days away. In England and Wales it is Tuesday 10 November 2026.

A start on a Saturday or Sunday counts from the Monday, so it ends on the same day as a Friday start. Each weekday holiday on the way adds one more business day to find.

How this is counted

The start day is never counted: day 1 is the next business day, as in Excel's WORKDAY. Weekends are Saturday and Sunday. US dates skip the federal holidays of 5 U.S.C. 6103 on their observed days; England and Wales dates skip the bank holidays GOV.UK publishes. The figures here leave out state, company and carrier holidays: add those as your own days off in the calculator, and check the terms your deadline comes from.

"Today" is the date in Washington, DC; this page turns over at midnight there.

Questions

How many calendar days is 2 business days?

With no holidays in the way, 2 business days take 2 to 4 calendar days from a weekday (2 from a Monday, 2 from a Tuesday, 2 from a Wednesday, 4 from a Thursday and 4 from a Friday). A weekday holiday on the way pushes the end date later.

Does 2 business days include today?

Not here: day 1 is the first business day after today, the way WORKDAY counts in Excel and Google Sheets. If your terms count today as day 1 and today is a business day, the deadline is one business day earlier.
