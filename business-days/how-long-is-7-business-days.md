- US date: November 10, 2026

## 7 business days from each weekday, with no holidays

| Start day | Ends on | Calendar days |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Monday | Wednesday | 9 |
| Tuesday | Thursday | 9 |
| Wednesday | Friday | 9 |
| Thursday | Monday | 11 |
| Friday | Tuesday | 11 |
| Saturday | Tuesday | 10 |
| Sunday | Tuesday | 9 |

A start on a Saturday or Sunday counts from the Monday, so it ends on the same day as a Friday start. Each weekday holiday on the way adds one more business day to find.

## How this is counted

The start day is never counted: day 1 is the next business day, as in [Excel's WORKDAY](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/excel-workday/). Weekends are Saturday and Sunday. US dates skip the federal holidays of 5 U.S.C. 6103 on their observed days; England and Wales dates skip the bank holidays GOV.UK publishes. Neither knows state, company or carrier holidays, so check the terms your deadline comes from.

## Questions

### How many calendar days is 7 business days?

With no holidays in the way, 7 business days take 9 to 11 calendar days from a weekday (9 from a Monday, 9 from a Tuesday, 9 from a Wednesday, 11 from a Thursday and 11 from a Friday). A weekday holiday on the way pushes the end date later.

### Does 7 business days include today?

Not here: day 1 is the first business day after today, the way WORKDAY counts in Excel and Google Sheets. If your terms count today as day 1 and today is a business day, the deadline is one business day earlier.
