## How this is counted

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday. Bank holidays are the ones GOV.UK publishes for England and Wales; one that falls on a weekend moves to the next weekday that is not already a holiday. Scotland and Northern Ireland differ. Every holiday of the year is on [the England and Wales bank holidays 2026 page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/england-wales-bank-holidays-2026/).
