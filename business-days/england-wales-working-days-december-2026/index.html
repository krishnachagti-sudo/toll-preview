There are 21 working days in December 2026 in England and Wales: 23 weekdays less 2 bank holidays, Christmas Day on Friday 25 December 2026 and Boxing Day (substitute day) on Monday 28 December 2026.

The first working day of December 2026 is Tuesday 1 December 2026; the last is Thursday 31 December 2026. That ends the year: 2026 has 253 working days in England and Wales.

How this is counted

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday. Bank holidays are the ones GOV.UK publishes for England and Wales; one that falls on a weekend moves to the next weekday that is not already a holiday. Scotland and Northern Ireland differ. Every holiday of the year is on the England and Wales bank holidays 2026 page.
