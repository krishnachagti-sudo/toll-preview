How these dates are set

Federal holidays are set by law in 5 U.S.C. 6103. When a holiday falls on a Saturday, most federal employees get the Friday before off (5 U.S.C. 6103(b)); when it falls on a Sunday, they get the Monday after (Executive Order 11582). That includes New Year's Day, so a Saturday 1 January is observed on 31 December of the year before.

Juneteenth National Independence Day became a federal holiday in 2021, so 2019 has 10.

Not included: state and local holidays, and one-off closures of federal offices ordered by the President.

Questions

How many business days are there in 2019 in the US?

251: the 261 weekdays of 2019, less 10 holiday days off on a weekday.

Which federal holidays in 2019 fall on a weekend?

No federal holiday in 2019 falls on a weekend, so every one is a day off on its own date.

Which month of 2019 has the fewest business days?

February and November, with 19. April, May, July, August and October have the most, with 22.
