What counts as a working day here

Monday to Friday, except the bank holidays GOV.UK publishes for England and Wales. A holiday that falls on a weekend moves to the next weekday that is not already a holiday, the substitute day. Scotland and Northern Ireland have different bank holidays.

"Today" is the date in London; this page turns over at midnight there. For any other date, use the tool.

Questions

What happens when a bank holiday falls on a weekend in England and Wales?

A substitute bank holiday is given on the next weekday that is not already a holiday. In 2027 Christmas Day is a Saturday and Boxing Day a Sunday, so the substitute days are Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December 2027.
