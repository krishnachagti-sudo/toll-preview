## How this is counted

Today is not counted, so on a working day the figure "counting today" is one more. Weekends are Saturday and Sunday, and the holidays are those on [the England and Wales bank holidays 2026 page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/england-wales-bank-holidays-2026/).

> "Today" is the date in London; this page turns over at midnight there.
