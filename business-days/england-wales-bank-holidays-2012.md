# England and Wales bank holidays 2012

There are 9 bank holidays in England and Wales in 2012; with weekends and those holidays off, 2012 has 252 working days. One is a substitute day for a holiday on a weekend. One is a one-off: Diamond Jubilee bank holiday.

- Bank holidays: 9
- Working days: 252
- Weekdays: 261
- On a weekend: 1

## How these dates are set

Bank holidays in England and Wales come from the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 and royal proclamations. When New Year's Day, Christmas Day or Boxing Day falls on a weekend, the next weekday that is not already a holiday becomes a substitute day.

2012 has changes for a royal or national occasion, shown in the notes above.

> Scotland and Northern Ireland have different bank holidays and are not included.
