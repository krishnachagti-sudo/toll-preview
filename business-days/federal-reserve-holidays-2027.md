# Federal Reserve bank holidays 2027

The Federal Reserve Banks close on 9 weekdays in 2027, so 2027 has 252 banking days for ACH and wire settlement. That is 3 more than the 249 federal business days, because the Reserve Banks stay open on Friday, June 18, 2027, Friday, December 24, 2027 and Friday, December 31, 2027: each is the Friday that federal offices take off for a holiday falling on a Saturday.

- Banking days: 252
- Federal business days: 249
- Fridays the Fed opens: 3

## Why are the Reserve Banks open when federal offices are closed?

The Federal Reserve keeps the same 11 holidays as federal offices, with one difference in its weekend rule. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, all Federal Reserve offices close the Monday after. When it falls on a Saturday, the Reserve Banks are open on the Friday before and only the Board of Governors closes ([K.8](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/k8.htm)). Federal offices follow [5 U.S.C. 6103](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/6103) and take that Friday off.

In 2027 that happens 3 times, more than in most years, because Juneteenth, Christmas Day and New Year's Day 2028 all fall on a Saturday.

## What Federal Reserve holidays are left in 2026?

Veterans Day on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, November 26, 2026 and Christmas Day on Friday, December 25, 2026. None of them falls on a weekend, so federal offices and the Reserve Banks close on the same days.

## What this means for payments

- An ACH payment or wire that would settle on a closed day settles on the next banking day instead.
- On the open Fridays (Fri, Jun 18, 2027, Fri, Dec 24, 2027 and Fri, Dec 31, 2027), payments can settle even though federal offices, and possibly your bank's branches, are closed.
- To count banking days for a payment, see [how many business days ACH and wires take](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/ach-wire-transfer-business-days/).

> Your bank, card network or payment app may set its own cutoffs and closures. This page covers the Federal Reserve Banks only.

## Questions

### Are banks closed on June 18, 2027?

The Federal Reserve Banks are open on Friday, June 18, 2027. Juneteenth falls on Saturday, June 19, 2027, so federal offices take the Friday off, but the Reserve Banks follow the Saturday date and stay open, so ACH and wires can settle that Friday. Your own bank decides its branch hours.

### Is December 31, 2027 a bank holiday?

Not for the Federal Reserve Banks. New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so federal offices close on Friday, December 31, 2027, but the K.8 schedule lists the Reserve Banks as open that day.

### When July 4, 2027 falls on a Sunday, which day is the bank holiday?

Monday, July 5, 2027. For a holiday on a Sunday, every Federal Reserve office closes the Monday after.

### How many banking days are in 2027?

252: the 249 federal business days plus the 3 Fridays the Reserve Banks open for a Saturday holiday.
