# How many business days until the end of the year?

37 business days from today, Thursday, November 5, 2026 (the date in Washington, DC), until the end of the year on Thursday, December 31, 2026. That is 56 calendar days, with 3 federal holidays skipped. Today is not counted; the date itself is counted when it is a business day.

- Business days: 37
- Calendar days: 56
- Holidays skipped: 3

## How this is counted

The count runs to December 31 and includes it when it is a business day. It does not include today. It is the same figure as on the page for business days left this year.

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday, and the holidays are those on [the US federal holidays 2026 page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/us-federal-holidays-2026/). State, company and carrier holidays are not included: add them as your own days off in the [calculator](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/).

> "Today" is the date in Washington, DC; this page turns over at midnight there.

## Questions

### Is December 31 counted?

Yes, when it is a business day. When it is a Saturday, Sunday or the observed day off for a Saturday New Year's Day, it is skipped.

### Does the count include today?

No. Day 1 is the next business day after today, the way WORKDAY and NETWORKDAYS count from a start date. The date counted to is included when it is a business day.
