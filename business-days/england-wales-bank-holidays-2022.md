# England and Wales bank holidays 2022

There are 10 bank holidays in England and Wales in 2022; with weekends and those holidays off, 2022 has 250 working days. Two are substitute days for holidays on a weekend. Two are one-offs: Platinum Jubilee bank holiday and State Funeral of Queen Elizabeth II.

- Bank holidays: 10
- Working days: 250
- Weekdays: 260
- On a weekend: 2

## How these dates are set

Bank holidays in England and Wales come from the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 and royal proclamations. When New Year's Day, Christmas Day or Boxing Day falls on a weekend, the next weekday that is not already a holiday becomes a substitute day.

2022 has changes for a royal or national occasion, shown in the notes above.

> Scotland and Northern Ireland have different bank holidays and are not included.
