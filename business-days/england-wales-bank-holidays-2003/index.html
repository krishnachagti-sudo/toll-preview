How these dates are set

Bank holidays in England and Wales come from the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 and royal proclamations. When New Year's Day, Christmas Day or Boxing Day falls on a weekend, the next weekday that is not already a holiday becomes a substitute day.

Scotland and Northern Ireland have different bank holidays and are not included.

Questions

How many working days are there in 2003 in England and Wales?

253: the 261 weekdays of 2003, less 8 holiday days off on a weekday.

Which bank holidays in 2003 fall on a weekend?

No bank holiday in 2003 falls on a weekend, so every one is a day off on its own date.

Which month of 2003 has the fewest working days?

February, April, May, August and November, with 20. July and October have the most, with 23.
