# India central government holidays (Delhi) 2026

There are 17 closed holidays in 2026 for central government offices in Delhi and New Delhi; with weekends and those holidays off, 2026 has 247 business days. Three fall on a Saturday or Sunday and are not moved.

- Closed holidays: 17
- Business days: 247
- Weekdays: 261
- On a weekend: 3

## Which list this is

These are the closed (gazetted) holidays that the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, declares for the administrative offices of the central government located in Delhi and New Delhi, in its office memorandum for 2026, with any later DoPT change of date. A holiday that falls on a weekend is not moved: the memorandum allows no substitute day.

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday: central government administrative offices have worked a 5-day week, Monday to Friday, since 3 June 1985 (DoPT O.M. No. 13/4/85-JCA).

Dates of Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Muharram and Id-e-Milad can be changed by DoPT on the sighting of the moon. A change is added here once DoPT publishes it.

> Not included: the restricted (optional) holidays, of which each employee picks two; the lists for central government offices outside Delhi; any state's holidays; bank holidays; paid holidays for voting; and one-off office closures. States and banks differ from this list.

## Questions

### How many business days are there in 2026 at central government offices in Delhi and New Delhi?

247: the 261 weekdays of 2026, less 14 weekdays taken by the holidays. Weekends are Saturday and Sunday.

### When is the Diwali holiday in 2026?

Sunday 8 November 2026. It falls on a weekend, so it takes no business day.

### Which month of 2026 has the fewest business days?

March and May, with 19.

### Does this list apply to banks or to a state government?

No. It is the central government's list for its administrative offices in Delhi and New Delhi. States, banks and central government offices elsewhere in India keep their own lists.
