The first working day of March 2027 is Monday 1 March 2027; the last is Wednesday 31 March 2027. After March, 192 working days are left in 2027.

How this is counted

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday. Bank holidays are the ones GOV.UK publishes for England and Wales; one that falls on a weekend moves to the next weekday that is not already a holiday. Scotland and Northern Ireland differ. Every holiday of the year is on the England and Wales bank holidays 2027 page.
