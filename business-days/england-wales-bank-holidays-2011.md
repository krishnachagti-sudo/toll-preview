# England and Wales bank holidays 2011

There are 9 bank holidays in England and Wales in 2011; with weekends and those holidays off, 2011 has 251 working days. Two are substitute days for holidays on a weekend. One is a one-off: Wedding of Prince William and Catherine Middleton.

- Bank holidays: 9
- Working days: 251
- Weekdays: 260
- On a weekend: 2

## How these dates are set

Bank holidays in England and Wales come from the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 and royal proclamations. When New Year's Day, Christmas Day or Boxing Day falls on a weekend, the next weekday that is not already a holiday becomes a substitute day.

2011 has changes for a royal or national occasion, shown in the notes above.

> Scotland and Northern Ireland have different bank holidays and are not included.
