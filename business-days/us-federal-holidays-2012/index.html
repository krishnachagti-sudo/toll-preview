How these dates are set

Federal holidays are set by law in 5 U.S.C. 6103. When a holiday falls on a Saturday, most federal employees get the Friday before off (5 U.S.C. 6103(b)); when it falls on a Sunday, they get the Monday after (Executive Order 11582). That includes New Year's Day, so a Saturday 1 January is observed on 31 December of the year before.

Juneteenth National Independence Day became a federal holiday in 2021, so 2012 has 10.

Not included: state and local holidays, and one-off closures of federal offices ordered by the President.

Questions

How many business days are there in 2012 in the US?

251: the 261 weekdays of 2012, less 10 holiday days off on a weekday.

Which federal holidays in 2012 fall on a weekend?

Two federal holidays in 2012 fall on a weekend: New Year's Day (Sunday, day off Monday, January 2, 2012) and Veterans Day (Sunday, day off Monday, November 12, 2012).

Which month of 2012 has the fewest business days?

September, with 19. August has the most, with 23.
