# US federal holidays 2023

There are 11 US federal holidays in 2023; with weekends and those holidays off, 2023 has 249 business days.

- Federal holidays: 11

## How these dates are set

Federal holidays are set by law in 5 U.S.C. 6103. When a holiday falls on a Saturday, most federal employees get the Friday before off (5 U.S.C. 6103(b)); when it falls on a Sunday, they get the Monday after (Executive Order 11582). That includes New Year's Day, so a Saturday 1 January is observed on 31 December of the year before.

> Not included: state and local holidays, and one-off closures of federal offices ordered by the President.

## Questions

### How many business days are there in 2023 in the US?

249: the 260 weekdays of 2023, less 11 holiday days off on a weekday.

### Which federal holidays in 2023 fall on a weekend?

Two federal holidays in 2023 fall on a weekend: New Year's Day (Sunday, day off Monday, January 2, 2023) and Veterans Day (Saturday, day off Friday, November 10, 2023).

### Which month of 2023 has the fewest business days?

February, with 19. March and August have the most, with 23.
