# How many business days until the end of the quarter?

35 business days from today, Monday, November 9, 2026 (the date in Washington, DC), until the end of the quarter on Thursday, December 31, 2026. That is 52 calendar days, with 3 federal holidays skipped. Today is not counted; the date itself is counted when it is a business day.

- Business days: 35

## How this is counted

Calendar quarters end on March 31, June 30, September 30 and December 31. The count runs to that date and includes it when it is a business day. It does not include today. Companies with a fiscal year that does not follow the calendar have different quarter ends: use the calculator for those dates.

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday, and the holidays are those on [the US federal holidays 2026 page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/us-federal-holidays-2026/). State, company and carrier holidays are not included: add them as your own days off in the [calculator](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/).

> "Today" is the date in Washington, DC; this page turns over at midnight there.

## Questions

### Does this use my fiscal quarter?

No. It uses calendar quarters ending March 31, June 30, September 30 and December 31. For another quarter end, set the date in the calculator.

### Does the count include today?

No. Day 1 is the next business day after today, the way WORKDAY and NETWORKDAYS count from a start date. The date counted to is included when it is a business day.
