# Business days in December 2027, US

The first business day of December 2027 is Wednesday, December 1, 2027; the last is Thursday, December 30, 2027. That ends the year: 2027 has 249 business days in the US.

> Banks: the Federal Reserve Banks are open on Friday, December 24, 2027 and Friday, December 31, 2027, because those holidays fall on a Saturday ([Federal Reserve K.8](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/k8.htm)). On the Reserve Banks' calendar December 2027 has 23 business days.

## How this is counted

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday. Federal holidays are the eleven set by [5 U.S.C. 6103](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/6103), on the days federal employees get off: a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday before and a Sunday one on the Monday after. State and local holidays are not included. Every holiday of the year is on [the US federal holidays 2027 page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/us-federal-holidays-2027/).
