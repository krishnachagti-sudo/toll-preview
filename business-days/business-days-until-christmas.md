# How many business days until Christmas?

34 business days from today, Wednesday, November 4, 2026 (the date in Washington, DC), until Christmas on Friday, December 25, 2026. That is 51 calendar days, with 3 federal holidays skipped. Today is not counted; the date itself is counted when it is a business day.

- Business days: 34
- Calendar days: 51
- Holidays skipped: 3
- Date: December 25, 2026

## How this is counted

Christmas Day, December 25, is a federal holiday under 5 U.S.C. 6103, so it is never a business day itself. The count runs to the date and includes it only when it is a business day, which Christmas never is, so the last business day counted is the one before.

When December 25 falls on a Saturday, federal offices close on Friday, December 24, and that Friday is not a business day either. The Federal Reserve Banks stay open that Friday, but this count follows federal offices.

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday, and the holidays are those on [the US federal holidays 2026 page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/us-federal-holidays-2026/). State, company and carrier holidays are not included: add them as your own days off in the [calculator](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/).

## Questions

### Is Christmas Day counted as a business day?

No. It is a federal holiday, so it is skipped. The count stops at the last business day before it.

### Does the count include today?

No. Day 1 is the next business day after today, the way WORKDAY and NETWORKDAYS count from a start date. The date counted to is included when it is a business day.
