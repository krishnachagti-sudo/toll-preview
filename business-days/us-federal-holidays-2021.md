# US federal holidays 2021

There are 11 US federal holidays in 2021; with weekends and those holidays off, 2021 has 249 business days. New Year's Day 2022 falls on a Saturday, so Friday, December 31, 2021, is a day off too.

- Federal holidays: 11
- Business days: 249
- Weekdays: 261
- On a weekend: 3

## How these dates are set

Federal holidays are set by law in 5 U.S.C. 6103. When a holiday falls on a Saturday, most federal employees get the Friday before off (5 U.S.C. 6103(b)); when it falls on a Sunday, they get the Monday after (Executive Order 11582). That includes New Year's Day, so a Saturday 1 January is observed on 31 December of the year before.

Juneteenth National Independence Day became a federal holiday in June 2021; it was first observed on Friday, June 18, 2021.

2021 is an inauguration year. Inauguration Day is a holiday only for federal employees in the Washington, DC, area (5 U.S.C. 6103(c)), so it is not in this list or the counts.

> Not included: state and local holidays, and one-off closures of federal offices ordered by the President.

## Questions

### How many business days are there in 2021 in the US?

249: the 261 weekdays of 2021, less 12 holiday days off on a weekday.

### Which federal holidays in 2021 fall on a weekend?

Three federal holidays in 2021 fall on a weekend: Juneteenth National Independence Day (Saturday, day off Friday, June 18, 2021), Independence Day (Sunday, day off Monday, July 5, 2021) and Christmas Day (Saturday, day off Friday, December 24, 2021).

### Which month of 2021 has the fewest business days?

January and February, with 19. March has the most, with 23.
