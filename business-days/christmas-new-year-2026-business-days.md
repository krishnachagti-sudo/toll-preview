# Business days over Christmas and New Year 2026

Between Christmas and New Year 2026, the US has 4 business days (Mon, Dec 28, 2026 to Thu, Dec 31, 2026) and England and Wales has 3 working days (Tue 29 Dec 2026 to Thu 31 Dec 2026), because Boxing Day falls on a Saturday and its substitute bank holiday is Monday 28 December 2026. Christmas Day is Friday, December 25, 2026 and New Year's Day Friday, January 1, 2027, days off in both.

- US business days, Dec 28 to 31: 4
- England and Wales working days, Dec 28 to 31: 3

## Why does the UK have one fewer working day that week?

In England and Wales, when Christmas Day or Boxing Day falls on a weekend, the next weekday that is not already a holiday becomes a substitute day. Boxing Day 2026 is a Saturday, so the substitute is Monday 28 December ([GOV.UK bank holidays](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays)). The US has no Boxing Day, and Christmas Day 2026 falls on a Friday, so nothing moves.

## Questions

### Is December 28, 2026 a bank holiday in the UK?

Yes, in England and Wales. Boxing Day, Saturday 26 December 2026, falls on a Saturday, so Monday 28 December 2026 is the substitute bank holiday. In the US it is a normal business day.

### Is Christmas Eve 2026 a business day?

Yes. Thursday, December 24, 2026 is not a federal holiday or a bank holiday, so the tool counts it as a business day in the US and a working day in England and Wales. A one-off closure of federal offices ordered by the President is not in the tool's calendar.

### If I add 5 business days to December 23, 2026, what date do I get?

Thursday, December 31, 2026 in the US, skipping Christmas Day; Monday 4 January 2027 in England and Wales, skipping Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day.

### How many business days are there from December 23, 2026 to January 4, 2027?

6 in the US and 5 in England and Wales, not counting December 23 itself.
