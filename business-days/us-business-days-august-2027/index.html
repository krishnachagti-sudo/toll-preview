The first business day of August 2027 is Monday, August 2, 2027; the last is Tuesday, August 31, 2027. After August, 82 business days are left in 2027.

How this is counted

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday. Federal holidays are the eleven set by 5 U.S.C. 6103, on the days federal employees get off: a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday before and a Sunday one on the Monday after. State and local holidays are not included. Every holiday of the year is on the US federal holidays 2027 page.
