Thanksgiving week 2026

Is Black Friday 2026 a business day?

Yes. Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26, 2026), and it is not a federal holiday, so it is a business day in the US and the Federal Reserve Banks are open. Thanksgiving week has 4 business days, and 5 business days after Wednesday, November 25, 2026 is Thursday, December 3, 2026.

Why is Black Friday not a holiday?

Federal holidays are the 11 days in 5 U.S.C. 6103. Thanksgiving Day, the fourth Thursday in November, is one; the Friday after is not. An employer or a state government may give it as a day off, but that is its own day off, not a federal or bank holiday.

The tool counts statutory federal holidays only. It does not include a one-off closure of federal offices ordered by the President.

Questions

Is the day after Thanksgiving a federal holiday?

No. The federal holidays in 5 U.S.C. 6103 include Thanksgiving Day but not the day after, so Friday, November 27, 2026 counts as a business day.

Are banks open on Black Friday 2026?

The Federal Reserve Banks are open, so ACH and wires settle that day. Your own bank sets its branch hours.

Is Cyber Monday a business day?

Yes. Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30, 2026, a normal US business day.

My state gives the day after Thanksgiving off. How do I count it?

State holidays are not in the federal calendar. Add the date in the calculator's "Your own days off" box, or as daysOff over the API, and the tool skips it like a holiday.
