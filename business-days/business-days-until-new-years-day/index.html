How many business days until New Year's Day?

36 business days from today, Saturday, November 7, 2026 (the date in Washington, DC), until New Year's Day on Friday, January 1, 2027. That is 55 calendar days, with 4 federal holidays skipped. Today is not counted; the date itself is counted when it is a business day.

How this is counted

New Year's Day, January 1, is a federal holiday under 5 U.S.C. 6103. When it falls on a Saturday, federal employees get the Friday before (December 31) off instead, and when it falls on a Sunday they get the Monday after. The count skips whichever day is the day off.

The date counted to is January 1 itself, so it adds nothing to the count: it is a holiday, or a weekend day.

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday, and the holidays are those on the US federal holidays 2026 page. State, company and carrier holidays are not included: add them as your own days off in the calculator.

"Today" is the date in Washington, DC; this page turns over at midnight there.

Questions

What if New Year's Day falls on a weekend?

A Saturday January 1 is observed on the Friday before, December 31, and a Sunday January 1 on the Monday after. Those observed days are skipped in the count.

Does the count include today?

No. Day 1 is the next business day after today, the way WORKDAY and NETWORKDAYS count from a start date. The date counted to is included when it is a business day.
