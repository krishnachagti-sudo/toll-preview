## What counts as a business day here

Monday to Friday, except the federal holidays set by [5 U.S.C. 6103](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/6103), on the days federal employees get off: a holiday on a Saturday is observed on the Friday before, and one on a Sunday on the Monday after.

The Federal Reserve Banks close on the same holidays with one difference: when a holiday falls on a Saturday they stay open on the Friday before ([K.8](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/k8.htm)). The Reserve Banks column follows that rule. Your own bank, state offices and delivery companies such as USPS, UPS and FedEx set their own schedules.

## Questions

### Are banks open on the Friday before a Saturday federal holiday?

The Federal Reserve Banks and Branches are: when a holiday falls on a Saturday they open on the Friday before, while the Board of Governors and most federal offices close. From 2026 to 2030 that happens on July 3, 2026, June 18, 2027, December 24, 2027, December 31, 2027 and November 10, 2028. For a Sunday holiday all Federal Reserve offices close on the Monday after.

### What happens when a federal holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday?

For most federal employees a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday before and a Sunday holiday on the Monday after. That Friday or Monday is then not a business day on this page.
