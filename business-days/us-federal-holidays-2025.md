## How these dates are set

Federal holidays are set by law in 5 U.S.C. 6103. When a holiday falls on a Saturday, most federal employees get the Friday before off (5 U.S.C. 6103(b)); when it falls on a Sunday, they get the Monday after (Executive Order 11582). That includes New Year's Day, so a Saturday 1 January is observed on 31 December of the year before.

2025 is an inauguration year. Inauguration Day is a holiday only for federal employees in the Washington, DC, area (5 U.S.C. 6103(c)), so it is not in this list or the counts.

> Not included: state and local holidays, and one-off closures of federal offices ordered by the President.
