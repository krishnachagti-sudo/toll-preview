India central government holidays (Delhi) 2027

There are 17 closed holidays in 2027 for central government offices in Delhi and New Delhi; with weekends and those holidays off, 2027 has 250 business days. Six fall on a Saturday or Sunday and are not moved.

Which list this is

These are the closed (gazetted) holidays that the Department of Personnel and Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, declares for the administrative offices of the central government located in Delhi and New Delhi, in its office memorandum for 2027, with any later DoPT change of date. A holiday that falls on a weekend is not moved: the memorandum allows no substitute day.

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday: central government administrative offices have worked a 5-day week, Monday to Friday, since 3 June 1985 (DoPT O.M. No. 13/4/85-JCA).

Dates of Id-ul-Fitr, Id-ul-Zuha, Muharram and Id-e-Milad can be changed by DoPT on the sighting of the moon. A change is added here once DoPT publishes it.

Not included: the restricted (optional) holidays, of which each employee picks two; the lists for central government offices outside Delhi; any state's holidays; bank holidays; paid holidays for voting; and one-off office closures. States and banks differ from this list.

Questions

How many business days are there in 2027 at central government offices in Delhi and New Delhi?

250: the 261 weekdays of 2027, less 11 weekdays taken by the holidays. Weekends are Saturday and Sunday.

When is the Diwali holiday in 2027?

Friday 29 October 2027.

Which month of 2027 has the fewest business days?

May, with 19.

Does this list apply to banks or to a state government?

No. It is the central government's list for its administrative offices in Delhi and New Delhi. States, banks and central government offices elsewhere in India keep their own lists.
