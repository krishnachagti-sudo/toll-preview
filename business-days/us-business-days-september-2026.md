## How this is counted

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday. Federal holidays are the eleven set by [5 U.S.C. 6103](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/6103), on the days federal employees get off: a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday before and a Sunday one on the Monday after. State and local holidays are not included. Every holiday of the year is on [the US federal holidays 2026 page](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/business-days/us-federal-holidays-2026/).
