The first business day of December 2026 is Tuesday, December 1, 2026; the last is Thursday, December 31, 2026. That ends the year: 2026 has 250 business days in the US.

How this is counted

Weekends are Saturday and Sunday. Federal holidays are the eleven set by 5 U.S.C. 6103, on the days federal employees get off: a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday before and a Sunday one on the Monday after. State and local holidays are not included. Every holiday of the year is on the US federal holidays 2026 page.
