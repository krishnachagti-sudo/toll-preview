# England and Wales bank holidays 2005

There are 8 bank holidays in England and Wales in 2005; with weekends and those holidays off, 2005 has 252 working days. Two are substitute days for holidays on a weekend.

- Bank holidays: 8
- Working days: 252
- Weekdays: 260
- On a weekend: 2

## How these dates are set

Bank holidays in England and Wales come from the Banking and Financial Dealings Act 1971 and royal proclamations. When New Year's Day, Christmas Day or Boxing Day falls on a weekend, the next weekday that is not already a holiday becomes a substitute day.

> Scotland and Northern Ireland have different bank holidays and are not included.

## Questions

### How many working days are there in 2005 in England and Wales?

252: the 260 weekdays of 2005, less 8 holiday days off on a weekday.

### Which bank holidays in 2005 fall on a weekend?

Two bank holidays in 2005 fall on a weekend: New Year's Day (Saturday, day off Monday 3 January 2005) and Christmas Day (Sunday, day off Tuesday 27 December 2005).

### Which month of 2005 has the fewest working days?

January, February, May and December, with 20. June, August, September and November have the most, with 22.
