# US federal holidays 2010

There are 10 US federal holidays in 2010; with weekends and those holidays off, 2010 has 250 business days. New Year's Day 2011 falls on a Saturday, so Friday, December 31, 2010, is a day off too.

- Federal holidays: 10
- Business days: 250
- Weekdays: 261
- On a weekend: 2

## How these dates are set

Federal holidays are set by law in 5 U.S.C. 6103. When a holiday falls on a Saturday, most federal employees get the Friday before off (5 U.S.C. 6103(b)); when it falls on a Sunday, they get the Monday after (Executive Order 11582). That includes New Year's Day, so a Saturday 1 January is observed on 31 December of the year before.

Juneteenth National Independence Day became a federal holiday in 2021, so 2010 has 10.

> Not included: state and local holidays, and one-off closures of federal offices ordered by the President.

### How many business days are there in 2010 in the US?

### Which federal holidays in 2010 fall on a weekend?

Two federal holidays in 2010 fall on a weekend: Independence Day (Sunday, day off Monday, July 5, 2010) and Christmas Day (Saturday, day off Friday, December 24, 2010).
