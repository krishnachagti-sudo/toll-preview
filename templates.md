# Free spreadsheet templates for logs and records

There are 3 free spreadsheet templates (.xlsx) here, for a trucker per diem log, an IFTA mileage and fuel log and a 1099 payee tracker. They open in Excel and import into Google Sheets, and none of them has a rate typed in: you fill the rate or reporting line from the official notice.

## Which templates can you download?

Each template is a clean, printable sheet with a frozen header row, drop-down lists, date and number checks, totals that add themselves up, and a "How to use" sheet. Each names the official rule it follows and the tool that works the figures out exactly.

## Trucker per diem log

Who it is for: owner-operators and drivers who keep their own days-away record. One row per day: full day, departure or return day (counted at 3/4), or not away. You type the daily meals and incidental expenses rate for the continental US and for travel outside it; the sheet multiplies and totals.

Rule it follows: the special rates for transportation workers in [IRS Revenue Procedure 2019-48](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-19-48.pdf) and [IRS Publication 463, Travel, Gift, and Car Expenses](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463). The sheet does not apply the 80% or 50% meals limit, because that depends on your hours-of-service periods.

## IFTA mileage and fuel log

Who it is for: truckers and fleets filing an IFTA return each quarter. A trip log (dates, origin, destination, route, jurisdiction, miles, optional odometer readings), a fuel purchase log (date, seller, jurisdiction, fuel type, gallons), and a summary that adds miles and gallons for every jurisdiction and works out the fleet miles per gallon.

Rule it follows: the IFTA record requirements as published by the [Idaho State Tax Commission: IFTA records](https://tax.idaho.gov/?p=1239). No tax rate is stored in the sheet, because rates change every quarter.

[Download IFTA mileage and fuel log (ifta-mileage-fuel-log.xlsx)](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/templates/ifta-mileage-fuel-log.xlsx). Sheets inside: Trip log, Fuel purchases, By jurisdiction, How to use.

## 1099 payee tracker

Who it is for: small businesses that pay contractors and need to know, before January, who is over the reporting line and whose W-9 is missing. One row per payee with entity type, whether a TIN is on file, the payment method, payments by month and a year total. You type the reporting line for the year; the status column compares each payee with it.

Rule it follows: the [IRS: Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec). For most boxes the line is **$600** for 2025 payments and **$2,000** for 2026 payments, as the tool holds them; some boxes differ, so check yours. Payments by card or payment app are reported by the processor on Form 1099-K, so the sheet marks them as card payments.

## How do you use a template in Excel or Google Sheets?

Download the file, open it in Excel, or import it into Google Sheets with File, Import, Upload. Then fill in the yellow cells. Grey cells are formulas, so leave them alone.

1. Download the file: Click the download link for the template. It is an .xlsx file and needs no account.
2. Open it in Excel: Double-click the file. Excel opens it with the drop-down lists and formulas working.
3. Or import it into Google Sheets: In Google Sheets choose File, then Import, then Upload, drop the file in, and choose Replace spreadsheet or Insert new sheet(s).
4. Fill in the yellow cells: Yellow cells are for you, grey cells are formulas. The rate or reporting-line cell at the top is empty on purpose: type the figure for your tax year.

## Questions

### Are the templates really free?

Yes. Each file is a free download with no account and no limit, and downloads are not counted against your balance.

### Do the templates work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Use File, Import, Upload in Google Sheets and pick the .xlsx file. Google documents that it converts uploaded Excel files; formulas and drop-down lists carry over, but check the first rows after importing.

### Why is the rate cell empty?

Rates and reporting lines change by year, and sometimes part-way through a year. A template with a rate typed into its formulas goes quietly wrong. The cell is yours to fill from the official notice, and the page for each template names the rule.

### What is the difference between a template and the tool?

A template is a clean sheet that holds your records and adds them up. The tool applies the official rules exactly, with the right rate for each date, and is paid per use. Many people keep the log in the template and run the tool once at the end.

### Is a template tax advice?

No. They are record-keeping aids that follow the named rule. Your preparer decides what applies to you.
