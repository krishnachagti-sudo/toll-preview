# Wyoming final paycheck deadline

In Wyoming, final wages are due by the next regular payday whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (W.S. 27-4-104). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.
