# Arizona final paycheck deadline

In Arizona, a fired employee must be paid within 7 working days or by the end of the next regular pay period, whichever is sooner (A.R.S. 23-353(A)); one who quits, by the regular payday for the pay period in which the termination occurred (A.R.S. 23-353(B)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Saturday, November 28, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

## Arizona in words

- "When an employee is discharged from the service of an employer, he shall be paid wages due him within seven working days or the end of the next regular pay period, whichever is sooner." [A.R.S. 23-353, Payment of wages of discharged employee](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00353.htm)
- "When an employee quits the service of an employer he shall be paid in the usual manner all wages due him no later than the regular payday for the pay period during which the termination occurred. If requested by the employee, such wages shall be paid by mail." [A.R.S. 23-353, Payment of wages of discharged employee](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00353.htm)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## If the check is late

In a civil action, treble the unpaid wages. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).

## Deductions from the final check

Only if required or empowered by law, with the employee's prior written authorization, or where there is a reasonable good faith dispute over the amount due (including a set-off the employer claims). (A.R.S. 23-352). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
