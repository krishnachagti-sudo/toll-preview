# New York final paycheck deadline

In New York, final wages are due by the payday on which the next pay would have been due whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (N.Y. Labor Law 191(3)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## New York in words

- "Employees whose employment has ended are entitled to be paid no later than the pay day on which their next pay would have been due had they still been employed." [New York State Department of Labor, Frequency of Pay FAQ (Labor Law 191)](https://dol.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2021/03/frequency-of-pay-frequently-asked-questions.pdf)
- "Upon request of the employee, employers are required to mail the employee his or her wages." [New York State Department of Labor, Frequency of Pay FAQ (Labor Law 191)](https://dol.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2021/03/frequency-of-pay-frequently-asked-questions.pdf)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.
