# Illinois final paycheck deadline

In Illinois, final wages are due by the next regular payday whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (820 ILCS 115/5). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Illinois in words

- "All final compensation, including bonus payments, vacation pay, wages and commissions must be paid on your next regularly scheduled payday. 820 ILCS 115 /5." [Illinois Department of Labor, Wage Payment and Collection Act FAQ (820 ILCS 115/5)](https://labor.illinois.gov/faqs/wage-payment-faq.html)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## Deductions from the final check

Only with the employee's express written consent given freely at the time the deduction is made. (820 ILCS 115/9; 56 Ill. Adm. Code 300). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
