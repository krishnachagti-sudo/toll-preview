# West Virginia final paycheck deadline

In West Virginia, final wages are due by the next regular payday on which the wages would otherwise be due whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (W. Va. Code 21-5-4(b)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

## West Virginia in words

- "Whenever a person, firm, or corporation discharges an employee, or whenever an employee quits or resigns from employment, the person, firm or corporation shall pay the employee's wages due for work that the employee performed prior to the separation of employment on or before the next regular payday on which the wages would otherwise be due and payable" [West Virginia Division of Labor, W. Va. Code chapter 21 article 5, Wage Payment and Collection](https://labor.wv.gov/media/164/download?inline=)
- "when an employee for any reason whatsoever is laid off, the person, firm or corporation shall pay in full to the employee not later than the next regular payday" [West Virginia Division of Labor, W. Va. Code chapter 21 article 5, Wage Payment and Collection](https://labor.wv.gov/media/164/download?inline=)

## If the check is late

The unpaid amount plus two times that amount as liquidated damages. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).

## Deductions from the final check

Deductions are amounts required by law and authorized amounts for dues, pensions, savings, credit unions, charities and insurance. Unreturned employer property over $100 can be deducted from final wages at its replacement cost only under a signed itemized agreement and a written notice and demand. (W. Va. Code 21-5-1(g), 21-5-4(f)). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
