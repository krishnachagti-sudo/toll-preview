# Colorado final paycheck deadline

In Colorado, a fired employee must be paid immediately (C.R.S. 8-4-109); one who quits, by the next regular payday (C.R.S. 8-4-109). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## The rule

Colorado: when final wages are due

| Separation | Due | Law |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fired (discharged) | immediately | C.R.S. 8-4-109 |
| laid off | immediately | C.R.S. 8-4-109 |
| quit (resigned) | by the next regular payday | C.R.S. 8-4-109 |

- If the payroll unit is not regularly scheduled to be working, by six hours after the start of its next regular workday, or 24 hours if the payroll unit is off-site (CDLE INFO #3A).

## Colorado in words

- "When an employer fires or lays off an employee, the employer must immediately pay all wages owed." [Colorado Department of Labor and Employment, INFO #3A: Timing of Wage Payments (C.R.S. 8-4-109)](https://cdle.colorado.gov/sites/cdle/files/INFO%20%233A%20Timing%20of%20Wage%20Payments,%20&%20Required%20Record-Keeping%2007.11.2023%20accessible.pdf)
- "When an employee quits or resigns, the employer must pay all wages owed to the employee by or on the next regular payday" [Colorado Department of Labor and Employment, INFO #3A: Timing of Wage Payments (C.R.S. 8-4-109)](https://cdle.colorado.gov/sites/cdle/files/INFO%20%233A%20Timing%20of%20Wage%20Payments,%20&%20Required%20Record-Keeping%2007.11.2023%20accessible.pdf)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.
