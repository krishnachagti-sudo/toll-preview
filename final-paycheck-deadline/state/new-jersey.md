# New Jersey final paycheck deadline

In New Jersey, final wages are due by the regular payday for the pay period in which employment ended whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (N.J.S.A. 34:11-4.3). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

## New Jersey in words

- "the employer shall pay the employee all wages due not later than the regular payday for the pay period during which the employee's termination, suspension or cessation of employment (whether temporary or permanent) took place" [New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, selected state labor laws (N.J.S.A. 34:11-4.3 and 34:11-4.4)](https://www.nj.gov/labor/wageandhour/tools-resources/laws/selectedstatelaborlaws.shtml)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## Deductions from the final check

Only deductions required by law or on the statute's list: benefit and retirement contributions authorized in writing, savings, employer loans under the original loan agreement's schedule, payroll error corrections, safety equipment, company products on a payment schedule, charities and similar items. Unreturned equipment is not on the list. (N.J.S.A. 34:11-4.4). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
