# District of Columbia final paycheck deadline

In District of Columbia, a fired employee must be paid by the next working day (D.C. Code 32-1303(1)); one who quits, by the next regular payday or within 7 days, whichever is earlier (D.C. Code 32-1303(2)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Thursday, November 19, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## District of Columbia in words

- "Whenever an employer discharges an employee, the employer shall pay the employee's wages earned not later than the working day following such discharge" [D.C. Code 32-1303, Payment of wages upon discharge or resignation](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-1303)
- "quits or resigns, the employer shall pay the employee's wages due upon the next regular payday or within 7 days from the date of quitting or resigning, whichever is earlier." [D.C. Code 32-1303, Payment of wages upon discharge or resignation](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/32-1303)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## If the check is late

Liquidated damages of 10% of the unpaid wages for each working day the failure continues, or treble the unpaid wages, whichever is smaller. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).
