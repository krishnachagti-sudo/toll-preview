# Iowa final paycheck deadline

In Iowa, final wages are due by the next regular payday for the pay period in which the wages were earned whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (Iowa Code 91A.4). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

## Iowa in words

- "When the employment of an employee is suspended or terminated, the employer shall pay all wages earned, less any lawful deductions specified in section 91A.5 by the employee up to the time of the suspension or termination not later than the next regular payday for the pay period in which the wages were earned" [Iowa Code 91A.4 (2026), Employment suspension or termination](https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/code/91A.4.pdf)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## Deductions from the final check

Only if required or permitted by law or a court, or with written authorization for a lawful purpose that benefits the employee. Some items can never be deducted (shared-till shortages, most breakage and damage, lost or stolen property unless it was assigned equipment the employee acknowledged in writing). (Iowa Code 91A.5).
