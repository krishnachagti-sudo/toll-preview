# Maryland final paycheck deadline

In Maryland, final wages are due on or before the day the wages would have been paid had employment continued whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (Md. Code, Lab. & Empl. 3-505(a)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

## Maryland in words

- "each employer shall pay an employee or the authorized representative of an employee all wages due for work that the employee performed before the termination of employment, on or before the day on which the employee would have been paid the wages if the employment had not been terminated." [Md. Code, Labor and Employment 3-505](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gle&section=3-505&enactments=false)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## Deductions from the final check

Only if ordered by a court, authorized expressly in writing by the employee, allowed by the Commissioner because the employee received full consideration, or made under another law. (Md. Code, Lab. & Empl. 3-503). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
