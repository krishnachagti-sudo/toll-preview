# Kansas final paycheck deadline

In Kansas, final wages are due by the next regular payday on which the employee would have been paid whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (K.S.A. 44-315(a)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

## Kansas in words

- "Whenever an employer discharges an employee or whenever an employee quits or resigns, the employer shall pay the employee's earned wages not later than the next regular payday upon which he or she would have been paid if still employed" [K.S.A. 44-315, Payment of wages to discharged or quitting employee; penalty](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch44/044_003_0015.html)

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## If the check is late

If willful, 1% of the unpaid wages for each day (except Sundays and legal holidays) after the eighth day, or 100% of the unpaid wages, whichever is less. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).

## Deductions from the final check

Only if required by law, for medical care without financial benefit to the employer, with a signed authorization for a lawful purpose benefiting the employee, or for automatic retirement enrollment. From final wages, after written notice and explanation, an employer may also withhold for unreturned property (until it is returned), loans or advances, payroll overpayments and merchandise or uniforms the employee bought. Never below minimum wage. (K.S.A. 44-319). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
