# Vermont final paycheck deadline

In Vermont, a fired employee must be paid within 72 hours of the discharge (21 V.S.A. 342(c)); one who quits, on the regular payday, or the following Friday if there is none (21 V.S.A. 342(c)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by 4:00 pm on Saturday, November 21, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

Vermont: when final wages are due

| Separation | Due | Law |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fired (discharged) | within 72 hours of the discharge | 21 V.S.A. 342(c) |
| laid off | within 72 hours of the discharge | 21 V.S.A. 342(c) |
| quit (resigned) | on the regular payday, or the following Friday if there is none | 21 V.S.A. 342(c) |

> Vermont's text names a discharge (the employer ending the job); a layoff is shown under the same rule.

## Vermont in words

- "If the employer discharges an employee, the employee must be paid within 72 hours from the time of discharge." [Vermont Department of Labor, A Summary of Vermont Wage and Hour Laws (WH-13, 2019; 21 V.S.A. 342)](https://labor.vermont.gov/sites/labor/files/doc_library/WH-13-Wage-and-Hour-Laws-2019%20.pdf)
- "An employee who voluntarily leaves an employment position, shall be paid on the last regular payday, or if there is no regular payday, on the following Friday." [Vermont Department of Labor, A Summary of Vermont Wage and Hour Laws (WH-13, 2019; 21 V.S.A. 342)](https://labor.vermont.gov/sites/labor/files/doc_library/WH-13-Wage-and-Hour-Laws-2019%20.pdf)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.
