# Virginia final paycheck deadline

In Virginia, final wages are due on or before the payday the wages would have been paid had employment continued whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (Va. Code 40.1-29(A)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

## Virginia in words

- "Upon termination of employment an employee shall be paid all wages due him for work performed prior thereto; such payment shall be made on or before the date on which he would have been paid for such work had his employment not been terminated." [Va. Code 40.1-29, Time and medium of payment; withholding wages](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title40.1/chapter3/section40.1-29/)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## Deductions from the final check

Only taxes, deductions in accordance with law, or with the employee's written and signed authorization. (Va. Code 40.1-29(C)). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
