# South Dakota final paycheck deadline

In South Dakota, a fired employee must be paid by the next regular payday (SDCL 60-11-10); one who quits, by the next regular payday (SDCL 60-11-11). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## South Dakota in words

- "All wages and compensation earned are due on the next regular payday after the employee is terminated." [South Dakota Department of Labor and Regulation, What you need to know about South Dakota labor laws (rev. 01/2026)](https://dlr.sd.gov/employment_laws/publications/what_need_know_laws.pdf)
- "The law requires that all wages be paid on the next regular payday after an employee quits." [South Dakota Department of Labor and Regulation, What you need to know about South Dakota labor laws (rev. 01/2026)](https://dlr.sd.gov/employment_laws/publications/what_need_know_laws.pdf)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## Deductions from the final check

The employer may withhold the final paycheck until the employee returns property that belongs to the employer. (SDCL 60-11-10, 60-11-11). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
