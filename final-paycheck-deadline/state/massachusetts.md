# Massachusetts final paycheck deadline

In Massachusetts, a fired employee must be paid in full on the day of discharge (G.L. c. 149, s. 148); one who quits, in full on the following regular payday, or the following Saturday if there is none (G.L. c. 149, s. 148). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

> Massachusetts's text names a discharge (the employer ending the job); a layoff is shown under the same rule.

## Massachusetts in words

- "any employee discharged \[or terminated\] from such employment shall be paid in full on the day of his discharge" [Massachusetts Trial Court Law Libraries, Massachusetts law about wages (quoting G.L. c. 149, s. 148)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massachusetts-law-about-wages)
- "any employee \[voluntarily\] leaving his employment shall be paid in full on the following regular pay day, and, in the absence of a regular pay day, on the following Saturday" [Massachusetts Trial Court Law Libraries, Massachusetts law about wages (quoting G.L. c. 149, s. 148)](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massachusetts-law-about-wages)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.
