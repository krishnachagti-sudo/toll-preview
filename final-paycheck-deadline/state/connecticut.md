# Connecticut final paycheck deadline

In Connecticut, a fired employee must be paid by the next business day (C.G.S. 31-71c(b)); one who quits, by the next regular payday (C.G.S. 31-71c(a)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Thursday, November 19, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Connecticut in words

- "Whenever an employer discharges an Employee, the employer shall pay the employee's wages in full no later than the business day next succeeding the date of such discharge." [Connecticut Department of Labor, Wage Payment Laws (C.G.S. 31-71a to 31-72), update December 2023](https://portal.ct.gov/dol/-/media/DOL/2022-New-Design-System/Divisions/wage-and-workplace-standards/WagePaymentLaws_Update12-2023.pdf)
- "Whenever an employee voluntarily terminates his employment, the employer shall pay the employee's wages in full not later than the next regular pay day" [Connecticut Department of Labor, Wage Payment Laws (C.G.S. 31-71a to 31-72), update December 2023](https://portal.ct.gov/dol/-/media/DOL/2022-New-Design-System/Divisions/wage-and-workplace-standards/WagePaymentLaws_Update12-2023.pdf)
- "when an employee for any reason is laid off, the employer shall pay in full to such employee the wages earned by him not later than the next regular pay day" [Connecticut Department of Labor, Wage Payment Laws (C.G.S. 31-71a to 31-72), update December 2023](https://portal.ct.gov/dol/-/media/DOL/2022-New-Design-System/Divisions/wage-and-workplace-standards/WagePaymentLaws_Update12-2023.pdf)

## If the check is late

In a civil action, twice the full amount of the wages. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).

## Deductions from the final check

Only if required or empowered by law, or with the employee's written authorization on a form approved by the labor commissioner (plus medical deductions without financial benefit to the employer, and automatic retirement enrollment). (C.G.S. 31-71e). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
