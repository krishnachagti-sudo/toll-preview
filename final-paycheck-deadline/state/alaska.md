# Alaska final paycheck deadline

In Alaska, a fired employee must be paid within 3 working days after the day of termination (AS 23.05.140); one who quits, the next regular payday at least 3 days after the employer received notice (AS 23.05.140). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Monday, November 23, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

> Alaska's text names a discharge (the employer ending the job); a layoff is shown under the same rule.

## Alaska in words

- "If employment is terminated by the employer, regardless of the cause for the termination, payment is due within three working days after the day of termination." [Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Wage and Hour: letter to employers and employees (AS 23.05.140)](https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/forms/FISH_PROCESSORS_LETTER_2020.pdf)
- "The day of termination, weekends, and bank or state holidays, are not included in these three days." [Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Wage and Hour: letter to employers and employees (AS 23.05.140)](https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/forms/FISH_PROCESSORS_LETTER_2020.pdf)
- "If the employment is terminated by the employee, payment is due at the next regular payday that is at least three days after the employer received notice of the employee's termination of services." [Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Wage and Hour: letter to employers and employees (AS 23.05.140)](https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/forms/FISH_PROCESSORS_LETTER_2020.pdf)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## If the check is late

The regular wage from the time of demand to the time of payment, or for 90 working days, whichever is less. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).
