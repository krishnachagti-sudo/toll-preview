# Oregon final paycheck deadline

In Oregon, a fired employee must be paid by the end of the next business day (ORS 652.140(1)); one who quits, with at least 48 hours' notice (weekends and holidays not counted): on the last day, or the next business day if that is a weekend or holiday; with less: within 5 business days or by the next regular payday, whichever comes first (ORS 652.140(2)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Thursday, November 19, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Oregon in words

- "If you are let go or fired, your final paycheck is due by the end of the next business day." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Paychecks: final pay (ORS 652.140, 652.150)](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/paychecks.aspx)
- "If you quit with less than 48 hours' notice (not including weekends and holidays) your paycheck and any wages owed are due within five business days or on the next regular payday, whichever comes first." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Paychecks: final pay (ORS 652.140, 652.150)](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/paychecks.aspx)
- "If you quit with at least 48 hours notice, your final check is due on your last day of employment, unless that day is a weekend or a holiday. In that case, your check is due on the next business day." [Oregon Bureau of Labor and Industries, Paychecks: final pay (ORS 652.140, 652.150)](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/workers/pages/paychecks.aspx)

## If the check is late

Penalty wages of eight times the regular hourly rate for each day unpaid, up to 30 days; plus a possible $1,000 civil penalty for willful failure. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).

## Deductions from the final check

Only if legally required, or the employee voluntarily agrees in writing and the deduction is for the employee's benefit. (ORS 652.610). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
