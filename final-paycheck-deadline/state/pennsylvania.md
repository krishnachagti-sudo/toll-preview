# Pennsylvania final paycheck deadline

In Pennsylvania, final wages are due by the next regular payday on which the wages would otherwise be due whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (43 P.S. 260.5(a)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

## Pennsylvania in words

- "Whenever an employer separates an employe from the payroll, or when- ever an employe quits or resigns his employment, the wages or compensation earned shall become due and payable not later than the next regular payday of his employer on which such wages would otherwise be due and payable." [Pennsylvania Department of Labor and Industry, Wage Payment and Collection Law (Act 329 of 1961) text](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dli/documents/documents/regulations/llc/wpclaw.pdf)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## If the check is late

Wages unpaid 30 days after the regular payday (with no good-faith dispute): liquidated damages of 25% of the wages due or $500, whichever is greater. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).
