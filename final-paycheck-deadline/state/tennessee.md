# Tennessee final paycheck deadline

In Tennessee, final wages are due by the next regular payday or 21 days after separation, whichever is later whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (Tenn. Code Ann. 50-2-103(g)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Wednesday, December 9, 2026.

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.
