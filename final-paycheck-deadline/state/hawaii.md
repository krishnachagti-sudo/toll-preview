# Hawaii final paycheck deadline

In Hawaii, a fired employee must be paid at the time of discharge, or not later than the next working day (HRS 388-3(a)); one who quits, by the next regular payday; at the time of quitting if the employee gave at least one pay period's notice (HRS 388-3(b)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Thursday, November 19, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## The rule

Hawaii: when final wages are due

| Separation | Due | Law |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fired (discharged) | at the time of discharge, or not later than the next working day | HRS 388-3(a) |
| laid off | at the time of discharge, or not later than the next working day | HRS 388-3(a) |
| quit (resigned) | by the next regular payday; at the time of quitting if the employee gave at least one pay period's notice | HRS 388-3(b) |

- If the employee gave at least one pay period's notice, all wages are due at the time of quitting; the date shown is for a quit without that notice.

## Hawaii in words

- "Under Section 388-3(a), HRS, whenever an employer discharges an employee, the employer is required to pay all earned wages in full at the time of discharge, or not later than the next working day." [Hawaii Department of Labor and Industrial Relations, Wage Standards Division: Unpaid Wages (HRS 388-3)](https://labor.hawaii.gov/wsd/unpaid-wages/)
- "Under Section 388-3(b), HRS, when an employee quits or resigns, the employer shall pay the employee’s wages in full no later than the next regular pay day, except that if the employee gives at least one pay period’s notice of intention to quit, the employer shall pay all wages earned at the time of quitting." [Hawaii Department of Labor and Industrial Relations, Wage Standards Division: Unpaid Wages (HRS 388-3)](https://labor.hawaii.gov/wsd/unpaid-wages/)
