# Washington final paycheck deadline

In Washington, final wages are due at the end of the established pay period whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (RCW 49.48.010(2)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Saturday, November 28, 2026.

## Washington in words

- "When any employee shall cease to work for an employer, whether by discharge or by voluntary withdrawal, the wages due him or her on account of his or her employment shall be paid to him or her at the end of the established pay period" [RCW 49.48.010, Payment of wages due to employee ceasing work](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=49.48.010)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## Deductions from the final check

Only if required by law, specifically agreed orally or in writing by the employee and employer (except as RCW 49.48.160 prohibits), or for medical care recorded in the books. (RCW 49.48.010(3)).
