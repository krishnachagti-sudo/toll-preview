# Montana final paycheck deadline

In Montana, a fired employee must be paid immediately when separated for cause or laid off, unless a written personnel policy extends it to the next regular payday or 15 days, whichever is first (Mont. Code Ann. 39-3-205(2)); one who quits, by the next regular payday for the pay period, or 15 days, whichever is first (Mont. Code Ann. 39-3-205(1)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Thursday, December 3, 2026.

- A discharge not for cause falls under the general rule: the next regular payday for the pay period or 15 days, whichever is first.

## Montana in words

- "when an employee is separated for cause or laid off from employment by the employer, all the unpaid wages of the employee are due and payable immediately upon separation unless the employer has a written personnel policy governing the employment that extends the time for payment of final wages to the employee's next regular payday for the pay period or to within 15 days from the separation, whichever occurs first." [Mont. Code Ann. 39-3-205, Payment of wages when employee separated](https://archive.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0390/chapter_0030/part_0020/section_0050/0390-0030-0020-0050.html)
- "all the unpaid wages of the employee are due and payable on the next regular payday for the pay period during which the employee was separated from employment or 15 days from the date of separation from employment, whichever occurs first" [Mont. ## Deductions from the final check

Reasonable deductions for board, room and other incidentals that are a condition of employment, or as otherwise provided by law. When an employee is discharged over an alleged theft connected to the work, the employer may withhold the value of the theft from the final paycheck if the conditions of 39-3-205(3) are met. (Mont.
