# Michigan final paycheck deadline

In Michigan, a fired employee must be paid on the regular scheduled payday for the period in which the employee is discharged (Payment of Wages and Fringe Benefits Act (1978 PA 390)); one who quits, on the regular scheduled payday for the period in which the employee quits (Payment of Wages and Fringe Benefits Act (1978 PA 390)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, December 4, 2026.

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.
