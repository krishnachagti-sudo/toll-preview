# Maine final paycheck deadline

In Maine, final wages are due by the next established payday whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (26 M.R.S. 626). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Maine in words

- "An employee leaving employment must be paid in full no later than the employee's next established payday." [26 M.R.S. 626, Cessation of employment](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/26/title26sec626.html)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## If the check is late

In a court action, the unpaid wages plus twice the unpaid wages as liquidated damages, interest, costs and fees; also a fine of $100 to $500 per violation. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).

## Deductions from the final check

From the final pay, an overpayment may be withheld if authorized under section 635, and a loan or advance against future wages may be deducted if evidenced by a statement in writing signed by the employee. (26 M.R.S. 626, 629). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).

## Unused vacation

Unused paid vacation accrued on and after January 1, 2023 must be paid on cessation of employment, unless the employer has 10 or fewer employees or is a public employer (a collective bargaining agreement can override).
