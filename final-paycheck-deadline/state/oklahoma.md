# Oklahoma final paycheck deadline

In Oklahoma, final wages are due by the next scheduled payday whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (40 O.S. 165.3). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.
