# North Dakota final paycheck deadline

In North Dakota, final wages are due at the regular payday for the periods worked whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (N.D.C.C. 34-14-03). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

- When the employer ends the employment, payment by certified mail to the address the employee designates (or as agreed).

## North Dakota in words

- "the employee's unpaid wages or compensation becomes due and payable at the regular paydays established in advance by the employer for the periods worked by the employee." [N.D. Cent. Code chapter 34-14, Wage Collection](https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t34c14.pdf)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## If the check is late

The agreed wage for each day in default, ending 30 days after the default. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).

## Deductions from the final check

Besides deductions required by law or a court, only: advances paid to employees (other than undocumented cash), a recurring deduction authorized in writing, a nonrecurring deduction authorized in writing that names its source, and a deduction for damage, breakage, shortage or negligence authorized by the employee at the time of the deduction. (N.D.C.C. 34-14-04.1). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).

## Unused vacation

A private employer may withhold accrued PTO on a voluntary quit only if it gave written notice of the limit at hiring, the employee worked under one year, and gave under five days' notice; and may withhold awarded but unearned PTO after written notice.
