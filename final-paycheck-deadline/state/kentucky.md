# Kentucky final paycheck deadline

In Kentucky, final wages are due by the next normal pay period or 14 days after the separation, whichever is later whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (KRS 337.055). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Wednesday, December 2, 2026.

- The statute says "the next normal pay period"; the next regular payday is used for it.

## Kentucky in words

- "Any employee who leaves or is discharged from his employment shall be paid in full all wages or salary earned by him; not later than the next normal pay period following the date of dismissal or voluntary leaving or fourteen (14) days following such date of dismissal or voluntary leaving whichever last occurs." [KRS 337.055, Payment of all wages or salary upon dismissal or voluntary leaving required](https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=32050)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.
