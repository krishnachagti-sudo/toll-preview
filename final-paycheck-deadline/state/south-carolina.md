# South Carolina final paycheck deadline

In South Carolina, final wages are due within 48 hours of separation or by the next regular payday, which may not be more than 30 days away whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (S.C. Code 41-10-50). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## South Carolina in words

- "When an employer separates an employee from the payroll for any reason, the employer shall pay all wages due to the employee within forty-eight hours of the time of separation or the next regular payday which may not exceed thirty days." [S.C. Code of Laws Title 41 chapter 10 (2019 session version, the newest copy the legislature's site served us)](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/Archives/CodeofLaws2019/t41c010.php)

## If the check is late

In a civil action, three times the unpaid wages, plus costs and attorney's fees. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).

## Deductions from the final check

Only if required or permitted by law, or the employer gave the written notice of the deductions required at hiring (with seven days' written notice of changes). (S.C. Code 41-10-40(C)). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
