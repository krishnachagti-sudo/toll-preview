# North Carolina final paycheck deadline

In North Carolina, final wages are due by the next regular payday whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (G.S. 95-25.7). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

- Bonuses and commissions: on the first regular payday after the amount can be calculated.

## North Carolina in words

- "Employees whose employment is discontinued for any reason shall be paid all wages due on or before the next regular payday either through the regular pay channels or by mail if requested by the employee." [North Carolina Department of Labor, Payment of Final Wages to Separated Employees (G.S. 95-25.7)](https://www.labor.nc.gov/workplace-rights/employee-rights-regarding-time-worked-and-wages-earned/payment-final-wages)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## Deductions from the final check

With a written authorization signed on or before the payday that gives the reason and the dollar amount or percentage; if the amount is not known in advance, a written authorization with the reason, then written notice of the amount and of the right to withdraw. Deductions for the employer's benefit may not cut into minimum wage or overtime. (G.S. 95-25.8).
