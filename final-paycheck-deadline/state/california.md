# California final paycheck deadline

In California, a fired employee must be paid immediately, at the time of discharge (Cal. Lab. Code 201(a)); one who quits, with at least 72 hours' notice: at the time of quitting; with less: within 72 hours (Cal. Lab. Code 202(a)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026; after a resignation that day, by 4:00 pm on Saturday, November 21, 2026.

## The rule

California: when final wages are due

| Separation | Due | Law |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fired (discharged) | immediately, at the time of discharge | Cal. Lab. Code 201(a) |
| laid off | immediately, at the time of discharge | Cal. Lab. Code 201(a) |
| quit (resigned) | with at least 72 hours' notice: at the time of quitting; with less: within 72 hours | Cal. Lab. Code 202(a) |

> California's text names a discharge (the employer ending the job); a layoff is shown under the same rule.

- This applies to an employee without a written contract for a definite period.

## California in words

- "If an employer discharges an employee, the wages earned and unpaid at the time of discharge are due and payable immediately." [Cal. Labor Code 201, payment of wages on discharge (California Legislative Information)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=201.)
- "If an employee not having a written contract for a definite period quits his or her employment, his or her wages shall become due and payable not later than 72 hours thereafter, unless the employee has given 72 hours previous notice of his or her intention to quit, in which case the employee is entitled to his or her wages at the time of quitting." [Cal. Labor Code 202, payment of wages on quitting (California Legislative Information)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=202.)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## If the check is late

If the employer willfully fails to pay, the wages continue as a penalty at the same daily rate until paid, for up to 30 days. [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).
