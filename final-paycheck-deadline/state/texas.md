# Texas final paycheck deadline

In Texas, a fired employee must be paid by the sixth day after the discharge (Tex. Lab. Code 61.014(a)); one who quits, by the next regularly scheduled payday (Tex. Lab. Code 61.014(b)). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Tuesday, November 24, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Texas in words

- "An employer shall pay in full an employee who is discharged from employment not later than the sixth day after the date the employee is discharged." [Texas Labor Code chapter 61, Payment of Wages (sections 61.014 and 61.018)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/LA/htm/LA.61.htm)
- "An employer shall pay in full an employee who leaves employment other than by discharge not later than the next regularly scheduled payday." [Texas Labor Code chapter 61, Payment of Wages (sections 61.014 and 61.018)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/LA/htm/LA.61.htm)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.

## Deductions from the final check

Only if ordered by a court, authorized by law, or with the employee's written authorization for a lawful purpose. (Tex. Lab. Code 61.018). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
