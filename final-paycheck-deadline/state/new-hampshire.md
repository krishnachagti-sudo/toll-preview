# New Hampshire final paycheck deadline

In New Hampshire, a fired employee must be paid within 72 hours of the termination (RSA 275:44); one who quits, by the next regular payday; within 72 hours if the employee gave one pay period's notice (RSA 275:44). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by 4:00 pm on Saturday, November 21, 2026; after a resignation that day, by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## The rule

New Hampshire: when final wages are due

| Separation | Due | Law |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fired (discharged) | within 72 hours of the termination | RSA 275:44 |
| laid off | within 72 hours of the termination | RSA 275:44 |
| quit (resigned) | by the next regular payday; within 72 hours if the employee gave one pay period's notice | RSA 275:44 |

> New Hampshire's text names a discharge (the employer ending the job); a layoff is shown under the same rule.

- If the employee gave one pay period's notice, all wages are due within 72 hours; the date shown is for a quit without that notice.

## New Hampshire in words

- "If fired, the wages are due within 72 hours from the time of the termination (RSA 275:44)." [New Hampshire Department of Labor, Wages and Work Hours FAQs (RSA 275:44)](https://www.dol.nh.gov/resource-center/frequently-asked-questions/wages-and-work-hours-faqs)
- "If an employee quits or resigns, the wages are due by the next regular payday, except if the employee gives one pay notice to quit the employer shall pay all wages due within 72 hours." [New Hampshire Department of Labor, Wages and Work Hours FAQs (RSA 275:44)](https://www.dol.nh.gov/resource-center/frequently-asked-questions/wages-and-work-hours-faqs)

## Worked dates

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Each date is the engine's answer for that last day.
