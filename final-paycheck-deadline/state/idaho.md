# Idaho final paycheck deadline

In Idaho, final wages are due by the next regular payday or within 10 days (weekends and holidays excluded), whichever is sooner; within 48 hours (weekends and holidays excluded) of a written request whether the employee is fired, laid off or quits (Idaho Code 45-606). Fired at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with paydays every other Friday, the final paycheck is due by the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Idaho in words

- "all wages then due must be paid the sooner of the next regularly scheduled payday or within 10 days of the separation (weekends and holidays excluded)." [Idaho Department of Labor, Labor Laws FAQ (Idaho Code 45-606)](https://www.labor.idaho.gov/dnn/Businesses/Idaho-Labor-Laws/Labor-Laws-FAQ)
- "If, after separation occurs, the employee gives the employer a written request for earlier payment of all wages, the employee must be paid within 48 hours of the employer's receipt of the written request (weekends and holidays excluded)." [Idaho Department of Labor, Labor Laws FAQ (Idaho Code 45-606)](https://www.labor.idaho.gov/dnn/Businesses/Idaho-Labor-Laws/Labor-Laws-FAQ)

## If the check is late

Administrative penalties of up to $750 ($500 if paid before the department files a state lien). [Work out the amount](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/waiting-time-penalty/).

## Deductions from the final check

Only if required by law or with written authorization; even then usually not below minimum wage. A proven advance or draw can be withheld in full. (Idaho Code 45-609). [Check one deduction](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/deductions/).
