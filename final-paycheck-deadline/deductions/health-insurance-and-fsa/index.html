Health insurance and FSA deductions on the last check

No covered state has a final-pay rule just for benefit deductions; the general deduction rule applies, and several name medical or insurance deductions in it. In Delaware, a $180.00 deduction for health insurance, FSA or other benefit contributions: the general deduction rule applies; after it, the final week pays $1,020.00 for 40 hours ($25.50 an hour), at or above $15.00 an hour (Delaware's basic minimum wage (DOL table)).
