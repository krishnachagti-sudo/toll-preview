# Unreturned equipment and the final paycheck

10 of the 36 states read here have a rule for it in so many words; the rest apply their general deduction rule. In Kansas, a $900.00 deduction for unreturned equipment (laptop, phone, tools, uniform): allowed after written notice; after it, the final week pays -$300.00 for 40 hours (-$7.50 an hour), below $7.25 an hour (the federal minimum wage): at most $310.00 can come out of that week.

## The minimum-wage floor

A deduction for the employer's benefit may not take the final week below the minimum wage or into overtime (29 CFR 531.35). The checker works out the most that can come out of the week.

> Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it. Not legal advice. Business days skip weekends and US federal holidays; add your state's holidays and closed days if they differ. Local ordinances, union contracts, employment contracts and industry rules can be stricter.
