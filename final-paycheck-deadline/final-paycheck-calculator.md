# Final paycheck calculator

A final week of 40 hours at $18.00 is $720.00 gross; taking a $250.00 advance out leaves $470.00 ($11.75 an hour) against a minimum of $7.25 in Texas, so at most $430.00 of it can come out that week, and for a firing on Wednesday, November 18, 2026 the check is due by the end of Tuesday, November 24, 2026.

- Gross: $720.00
- Most deductible: $430.00
