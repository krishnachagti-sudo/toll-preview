# Late final paycheck penalties

Final wages of $1,000.00 due on November 20, 2026 and paid 10 days late (November 30, 2026) can carry up to $2,000.00 on top in Arizona, the highest of the 15 states whose penalty we read; at $200 a day or $25 an hour, Oregon's penalty wages come to $2,000.00 and Minnesota's $2,000.00.

## One late check, state by state

$1,000.00 of final wages due Friday, November 20, 2026, paid Monday, November 30, 2026; $200 a day or $25 an hour. Penalties that depend on willfulness, a written demand or a court are shown as the most the formula allows; Minnesota counts from 24 hours after a written demand.

Penalty on $1,000 paid 10 days late

| State | On top of the wages | How |

| [Arizona](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/arizona/) | $2,000.00 | 3 x $1,000.00 recoverable in all, so $2,000.00 on top of the wages |
| [California](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/california/) | $2,000.00 | $200.00 a day x 10 days (at most 30) |
| [Connecticut](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/connecticut/) | $1,000.00 | 2 x $1,000.00 recoverable in all, so $1,000.00 on top of the wages |
| [Delaware](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/delaware/) | $700.00 | 10% x $1,000.00 x 7 days, Sundays and holidays not counted = $700.00; capped at the unpaid wages ($1,000.00) |
| [District of Columbia](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/district-of-columbia/) | $500.00 | 10% x $1,000.00 x 5 days, working days only = $500.00; capped at 3 x the unpaid wages ($3,000.00) |
| [Idaho](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/idaho/) | set by the agency | administrative penalties of up to $750 ($500 if paid before the department files a state lien) |
| [Kansas](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/kansas/) | $10.00 | 1% x $1,000.00 x 1 day (after the first 8), Sundays and holidays not counted = $10.00; capped at the unpaid wages ($1,000.00) |
| [Maine](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/maine/) | $2,000.00 | 2 x $1,000.00 on top of the wages |
| [Minnesota](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/minnesota/) | $2,000.00 | $200.00 a day x 10 days (at most 15) |
| [North Dakota](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/north-dakota/) | $2,000.00 | $200.00 a day x 10 days (at most 30) |
| [Oregon](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/oregon/) | $2,000.00 | 8 x $25.00 an hour x 10 days (at most 30) |
| [Pennsylvania](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/pennsylvania/) | $0.00 | 10 days late: liquidated damages start once wages are unpaid 30 days beyond the regular payday |
| [South Carolina](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/south-carolina/) | $2,000.00 | 3 x $1,000.00 recoverable in all, so $2,000.00 on top of the wages |
| [West Virginia](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/west-virginia/) | $2,000.00 | 2 x $1,000.00 on top of the wages |

> Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it. Not legal advice. Business days skip weekends and US federal holidays; add your state's holidays and closed days if they differ. Local ordinances, union contracts, employment contracts and industry rules can be stricter.
