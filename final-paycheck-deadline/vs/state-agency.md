# State agency pages and this lookup

A state's own statute or labor department page is the legal source, and this lookup quotes it word for word: for 16 of the 30 jurisdictions covered the words come from the labor department's page, the oldest dated one being West Virginia's (2016-07); the lookup adds the computed date, the deduction floor and the other states side by side.

A dated summary can lag the statute. Where a state's statute site could be read, this lookup quotes the statute; where only the agency's summary could be read, it quotes that and shows its date.

## When the agency page is better

When you need the legal source for one state, the official wording in full, or the agency's contact to file or answer a wage claim. Start there for a dispute.

> Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it. Not legal advice. Business days skip weekends and US federal holidays; add your state's holidays and closed days if they differ. Local ordinances, union contracts, employment contracts and industry rules can be stricter.
