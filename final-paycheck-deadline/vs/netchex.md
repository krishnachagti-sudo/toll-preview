Netchex's chart (last updated August 2026) shows Delaware's final pay as "Next regular payday", but the Delaware Code we read on November 8, 2026 makes it the later of the next payday and 3 business days: a layoff on December 31, 2026 with the payday on January 1 is due by the end of Wednesday, January 6, 2027.

## Delaware, worked

Their row for Delaware reads: "Next regular payday". The Delaware Code says:

- "Whenever an employee quits, resigns, is discharged, suspended, or laid off, the wages earned by the employee become due and payable by the employer on the later of the following:"
- "The next date the wages would be paid through the last day worked under the employer's regular pay cycle as if the employment had not stopped."
- "Three business days after the last day worked."
