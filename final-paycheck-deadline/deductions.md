# Deductions from a final paycheck

Every covered state limits deductions from final pay, usually to amounts the law requires or the employee authorized in writing, and federal law adds a floor: a $400 deduction from a $580, 40-hour final week leaves $180.00, $4.50 an hour, below the $7.25 federal minimum, so at most $290.00 can come out that week. Illinois and New Jersey do not allow a deduction for unreturned equipment at all.

- Final week left: $180.00
- Most deductible: $290.00

## The federal floor

Wages must be paid "free and clear": under 29 CFR 531.35 a cost for the employer's benefit, such as tools of the trade, may not cut into the minimum wage or overtime due for the workweek. The check here uses the higher of the federal $7.25 and the state's basic rate in DOL's state table, or the rate you type.
