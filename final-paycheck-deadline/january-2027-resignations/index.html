Final pay for a resignation on January 8, 2027

Resigning without notice at 4:00 pm on Friday, January 8, 2027, with paydays every other Friday (the next on January 15, 2027), the final paycheck is due as early as the end of Saturday, January 9, 2027 in Washington and as late as the end of Friday, January 29, 2027 in Tennessee.

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Holidays on the way: Christmas Day (December 25, 2026), New Year's Day (January 1, 2027) and Martin Luther King Jr. Day (January 18, 2027) where they fall in the count.

Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it. Not legal advice. Business days skip weekends and US federal holidays; add your state's holidays and closed days if they differ. Local ordinances, union contracts, employment contracts and industry rules can be stricter.
