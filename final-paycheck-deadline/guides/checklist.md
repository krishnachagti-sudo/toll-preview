# Separation-day final pay checklist

Six steps on the day an employee leaves, starting with the work state and the deadline: a Connecticut firing on December 24, 2026 is due by the end of Monday, December 28, 2026.

> Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it. Not legal advice. Business days skip weekends and US federal holidays; add your state's holidays and closed days if they differ. Local ordinances, union contracts, employment contracts and industry rules can be stricter.

## Steps

1. **Name the work state.** Use the state where the employee works, not headquarters. Rules differ by state and by how the job ended.
2. **Work out the deadline.** Enter the last day, the time and your next payday. A Connecticut firing on Thursday, December 24, 2026 is due by the end of Monday, December 28, 2026: the next business day, after Christmas.
3. **Add what the final pay must include.** Wages to the last minute worked, and unused vacation where the state or your policy requires it.
4. **Check each deduction.** Equipment, advances and negative PTO, shortages: check the state rule and the written authorization it needs, and keep the final week at or above the minimum wage.
5. **Choose delivery.** Pay through the usual channel or by mail if the employee asks; several states count the mailing date as the payment date.
6. **Keep the record.** Save the answer with the statute quoted and the date it was paid.
