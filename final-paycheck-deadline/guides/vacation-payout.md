# Unused vacation in the final paycheck

Of the 9 states whose own pages we read with a rule on unused vacation at separation, Illinois and Maine require it in the final pay (Maine for vacation accrued from January 1, 2023, unless the employer has 10 or fewer employees); the others leave it to the employer's written policy or agreement.

Vacation at separation, quoted from each state's own site (read November 8, 2026)

| State | Rule | In the state's words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Illinois](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/illinois/) | Final compensation includes vacation pay. | "All final compensation, including bonus payments, vacation pay, wages and commissions must be paid on your next regularly scheduled payday." |
| [Iowa](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/iowa/) | If vacation is due under an agreement or policy with pro rata accrual, the amount is in proportion to the part of the year worked. | "If vacations are due an employee under an agreement with the employer or a policy of the employer establishing pro rata vacation accrued, the increment shall be in proportion to the fraction of the year which the employee was actually employed." |
| [Maine](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/maine/) | Unused paid vacation accrued on and after January 1, 2023 must be paid on cessation of employment, unless the employer has 10 or fewer employees or is a public employer (a collective bargaining agreement can override). | "All unused paid vacation accrued pursuant to the employer's vacation policy on and after January 1, 2023 must be paid to the employee on cessation of employment unless the employee is employed by an employer with 10 or fewer employees or by a public employer." |
| [Maryland](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/maryland/) | Accrued leave must be paid unless the employer has a written policy limiting it, told the employee about it, and the policy denies payment. | "An employer is not required to pay accrued leave to an employee if:" |
| [North Dakota](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/north-dakota/) | A private employer may withhold accrued PTO on a voluntary quit only if it gave written notice of the limit at hiring, the employee worked under one year, and gave under five days' notice; and may withhold awarded but unearned PTO after written notice. | "If an employee separates from employment voluntarily, a private employer may withhold payment for accrued paid time off if:" |
| [Oklahoma](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/oklahoma/) | A written policy that promises payout of vacation, sick leave or bonuses makes them wages. | "If your employer has a written policy (like in an employee handbook) that promises payout of vacation, sick leave, or bonuses" |
| [Tennessee](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/tennessee/) | Only if the employer's policy or labor agreement requires it. | "Unless the employer's policy or its labor agreement specifically requires compensation of unused fringe benefits to an employee upon his or her separation of employment. Tennessee law does not require that an employee's final wages include such compensation." |
| [West Virginia](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/west-virginia/) | Fringe benefits earned and payable under a written policy are normally part of final wages unless the policy sets a later date. | "Fringe benefits that are earned and payable upon separation of employment based on the terms and conditions of written company policy are normally considered as part of "final wages" unless the policy specifically designates them payable at a later date." |
| [Wyoming](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/state/wyoming/) | Vacation payout can be denied only if a written policy clearly says so and the employee acknowledged it. | "Vacation: You can deny payout only if your written policy clearly states it and the employee acknowledged it." |

Other covered states: we did not read a vacation payout rule on their own sites, so none is shown. Sick leave rules are separate and not covered here.

## In the final paycheck

Where payout is owed, it is part of the final wages and due on the same deadline. [Work out the deadline](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/fired/).

> Worked out from each state's own published text, quoted with the date we read it. Not legal advice. Business days skip weekends and US federal holidays; add your state's holidays and closed days if they differ. Local ordinances, union contracts, employment contracts and industry rules can be stricter.

## Questions

### Does unused vacation have to be paid out when an employee leaves?

It depends on the state and the policy. Of the states read here, Illinois and Maine require it; the rest follow the written policy or agreement.
