# Final pay for a layoff on December 15, 2026

Laid off at 4:00 pm on Tuesday, December 15, 2026, with paydays every other Friday (the next on December 18, 2026), the final paycheck is due as early as 4:00 pm on Tuesday, December 15, 2026 in California and as late as the end of Tuesday, January 5, 2027 in Tennessee.

## Work out your own date

[Work out the deadline](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/final-paycheck-deadline/?state=CA&sep=layoff&date=2026-12-15&time=16%3A00&payday=2026-12-18)

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays. Holidays on the way: Christmas Day (December 25, 2026), New Year's Day (January 1, 2027) and Martin Luther King Jr. Day (January 18, 2027) where they fall in the count.
