# Final pay after a resignation, state by state

When an employee quits, 24 of the 36 jurisdictions read here require final pay by the next regular payday (or the payday for the final pay period); the rest set days, hours or notice rules. For a resignation at 4:00 pm on Wednesday, November 18, 2026 with paydays every other Friday, the earliest deadline is Colorado's, the end of Friday, November 20, 2026.

## Sorted by the due date

Two-week pay periods from Sunday to Saturday, each paid on the Friday six days after it ends (November 1 to 14, 2026 is paid on Friday, November 20), the last day ending at 4:00 pm, US federal holidays as the holidays; the last day is Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with no notice.
