# Delaware WARN Act

The Delaware Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act covers 100 or more employees in the company; at a 120-person site 50 full-time losses in 30 days trigger it, against 50 for federal WARN, and 60 days' notice is due by November 16, 2026 for a last day of January 15, 2027.

- Notice: 60 days
- Losses at 120: 50

## The rules

- 100 or more employees excluding part-time, or 100 or more who work at least 2,000 hours a week in all (1903(a)(4)).
- Trigger, plant closing: 50 or more.
- Trigger, mass layoff: 50 or more who are at least 33%, or 500 or more.
- Trigger, relocation of 50 miles or more, any number.
- The notice must carry the federal elements plus each dislocated worker's name, job title, home address, telephone number and email, general information on payouts, severance, relocation and retirement options, and whether the employer self-insures workers' compensation (1904(b)).
- Small rounds within any 90 days add up unless the employer shows separate and distinct causes (1907).

## Who gets the notice

- Each affected employee and their representatives
- The Delaware Department of Labor Division of Employment and Training, WARN Act Administrator
- The Delaware Workforce Development Board for the locality

## Questions

### How much notice does Delaware require?

60 days under Delaware WARN (19 Del. C. 1901 to 1911), the same as federal WARN.

### Which employers does Delaware WARN cover?

100 or more employees excluding part-time, or 100 or more who work at least 2,000 hours a week in all (1903(a)(4)).

### Where does the Delaware notice go?

Each affected employee and their representatives; The Delaware Department of Labor Division of Employment and Training, WARN Act Administrator; The Delaware Workforce Development Board for the locality.
