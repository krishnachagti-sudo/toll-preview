# Iowa WARN Act

The Iowa Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act covers 25 or more employees in the company; at a 120-person site 25 full-time losses in 30 days trigger it, against 50 for federal WARN, and 30 days' notice is due by December 16, 2026 for a last day of January 15, 2027.

- Notice: 30 days
- Losses at 120: 25

## The rules

- A person who employs 25 or more employees, excluding part-time employees (84C.2(5)).
- Trigger, business closing: 25 or more.
- Trigger, mass layoff: 25 or more.
- A business closing or mass layoff is an employment loss for 25 or more employees, part-time excluded, at a single site; a mass layoff counts any 30-day period (84C.2).
- Notice 30 days before; a collective bargaining agreement may set a different period (84C.3(1)).

## Who gets the notice

- Each affected employee, or their representative
- The Iowa Department of Workforce Development (this notice also lists the affected employees' addresses)

## Questions

### How much notice does Iowa require?

30 days under Iowa WARN (Iowa Code chapter 84C), against 60 under federal WARN.

### Which employers does Iowa WARN cover?

A person who employs 25 or more employees, excluding part-time employees (84C.2(5)).

### Where does the Iowa notice go?

Each affected employee, or their representative; The Iowa Department of Workforce Development (this notice also lists the affected employees' addresses).
