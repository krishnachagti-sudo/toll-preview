The rules

  • Federal: back pay and benefits for the period of violation, up to 60 days, reduced by wages paid over the notice period and voluntary unconditional payments; up to $500 a day for not notifying local government, avoided by paying each employee within three weeks (DOL Employer's Guide).
  • California and New York: up to 60 days or half the days the employee was employed, whichever is smaller (EDD; 12 NYCRR 921-7).
  • California and Washington: at the higher of the final rate or the 3-year average rate (EDD; RCW 49.45.040).
  • New Jersey: one week of severance per year of service, on top (Business.NJ.gov).
  • Maine: one week's pay per year, with a partial year pro rata, for employees with 3 years or more, after a closing or mass layoff (26 M.R.S. 625-B(2)).

Questions

Can we pay 60 days of wages instead of giving WARN notice?

Neither the Act nor the regulations recognize pay in lieu of notice, but because liability is capped at back pay and benefits for the period of violation up to 60 days, full pay and benefits for 60 days effectively precludes any relief (DOL Employer's Guide).

Does severance pay offset WARN damages?

Only voluntary and unconditional payments not required by any legal obligation; severance owed under a contract, policy or handbook does not (DOL Employer's Guide).

How much is New Jersey WARN severance?

One week of severance pay for each year of service (Business.NJ.gov, effective April 10, 2023).
