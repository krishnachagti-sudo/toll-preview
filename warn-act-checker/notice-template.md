# WARN notice template

Your notice to the state dislocated worker unit must go out by November 16, 2026, 60 days before the first separation on January 15, 2027, and list 6 elements; the notice to each employee without a union lists 4, including the date that employee will be separated (20 CFR 639.7).

- Send by: November 16, 2026

## Each affected employee without a union

1. Whether the action is expected to be permanent or temporary and, if the whole plant will close, a statement saying so
2. The expected date the plant closing or mass layoff will start and the expected date this employee will be separated
3. Whether bumping rights exist
4. The name and telephone number of a company official to contact

> 20 CFR 639.7(d).

## The union (chief elected officer of each representative)

1. The name and address of the employment site, and the name and telephone number of a company official to contact
2. Whether the action is expected to be permanent or temporary and, if the whole plant will close, a statement saying so
3. The expected date of the first separation and the schedule of separations
4. The job titles of the positions affected and the names of the workers in them

> 20 CFR 639.7(c).

## The state dislocated worker unit and the local chief elected official

1. The name and address of the employment site, and the name and telephone number of a company official to contact
2. Whether the action is expected to be permanent or temporary and, if the whole plant will close, a statement saying so
3. The expected date of the first separation and the schedule of separations
4. The job titles of the positions affected and the number of affected employees in each
5. Whether bumping rights exist
6. The name of each union representing affected employees, and the name and address of its chief elected officer

> 20 CFR 639.7(e).

## A fill-in letter

Filled from the worked example's Fresno plant. Replace the brackets; keep every element for the recipient.

```text
[Company letterhead]
[Date: on or before November 16, 2026]

To: [State dislocated worker unit / chief elected official / employee name]

Notice under the Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act

[Company] will carry out a [permanent / temporary] mass layoff at Fresno plant, [street address, city, state]. [The entire plant will / will not close.]
The first separations are expected on January 15, 2027 (45 employees), followed by February 5, 2027 (15).
Affected job titles and the number in each: [title: number].
Bumping rights [do / do not] exist.
Union: [name of each union and the name and address of its chief elected officer, or "none"].
Contact: [name, title, telephone] for more information.
```

## State extras

- California, from 2026 (SB 617): whether you will coordinate services through the local workforce board, the board's contact details, a description of its services, and CalFresh with its helpline (EDD).
- Delaware: each dislocated worker's name, job title, home address, telephone and email; payouts, severance, relocation and retirement options; whether you self-insure workers' compensation (19 Del. C. 1904(b)).
- Washington: whether the action results from relocating or contracting out operations or positions, and whether a temporary action is expected to last more or less than 3 months (RCW 49.45.020(2)).
- Iowa: the notice to the department also lists the affected employees' addresses (Iowa Code 84C.3(2)).

> Worked out from the counts and dates shown under 20 CFR Part 639 and the state sources listed. Whether locations are one single site, whether an exception applies and who the employer is are facts for you and your counsel. Not legal advice.

## Questions

### What must a WARN notice to employees say?

Whether the action is permanent or temporary and whether the whole plant will close; the expected start date and this employee's separation date; whether bumping rights exist; and a company contact's name and telephone number (20 CFR 639.7(d)).

### Can a WARN notice give a range of dates?

Yes: a specific date or a 14-day period in which separations are expected; notice must be given at least 60 days before the first day of the period (20 CFR 639.7(b)).

### Does a notice in the paycheck count?

Inserting the notice in pay envelopes is acceptable; a preprinted notice regularly included in every paycheck is not (20 CFR 639.8).
