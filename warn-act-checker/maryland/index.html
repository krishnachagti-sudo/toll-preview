State WARN law

Maryland WARN Act

The Maryland Economic Stabilization Act covers 50 or more employees in Maryland; at a 120-person site 30 full-time losses in 3 months trigger it, against 50 for federal WARN, and 60 days' notice is due by November 16, 2026 for a last day of January 15, 2027.

The rules

  • At least 50 employees in Maryland, operating for at least a year. An "employee" works 20 or more hours a week on average and has worked at least 6 of the last 12 months.
  • Trigger, reduction in operations: 15 or more who are at least 25%.
  • A reduction in operations is a shutdown of a workplace or part of it, or a relocation of part of the operation, that reduces employees at the workplace by at least 25% or 15 employees, whichever is greater, over any 3-month period.
  • For remote workers the whole State of Maryland is one workplace; for teleworkers it is their official duty station.
  • Not covered: labor disputes, construction sites and other temporary workplaces, customary seasonal factors, and bankruptcy filings.

Who gets the notice

  • Every employee at the workplace subject to the reduction, part-time and recently hired staff included
  • Each exclusive representative or bargaining agent of those employees
  • The Maryland Department of Labor Dislocation Services Unit, 100 S. Charles Street, Tower 1, Suite 2000, Baltimore, MD 21201
  • The chief elected official (where the workplace is in more than one political subdivision, the one paid the most taxes the year before)

Worked out from the counts and dates shown under 20 CFR Part 639 and the state sources listed. Whether locations are one single site, whether an exception applies and who the employer is are facts for you and your counsel. Not legal advice.

Questions

How much notice does Maryland require?

60 days under Maryland ESA (Md. Code Ann., Lab. & Empl. 11-301 et seq.), the same as federal WARN.

Which employers does Maryland ESA cover?

At least 50 employees in Maryland, operating for at least a year. An "employee" works 20 or more hours a week on average and has worked at least 6 of the last 12 months.

Where does the Maryland notice go?

Every employee at the workplace subject to the reduction, part-time and recently hired staff included; Each exclusive representative or bargaining agent of those employees; The Maryland Department of Labor Dislocation Services Unit, 100 S. Charles Street, Tower 1, Suite 2000, Baltimore, MD 21201; The chief elected official (where the workplace is in more than one political subdivision, the one paid the most taxes the year before).
