# New York WARN Act

The New York WARN Act covers 50 or more employees in New York; at a 120-person site 40 full-time losses in 30 days trigger it, against 50 for federal WARN, and 90 days' notice is due by October 17, 2026 for a last day of January 15, 2027.

- Notice: 90 days
- Losses at 120: 40

## The rules

- 50 or more employees in New York State not counting part-time employees, or 50 or more including part-time who work at least 2,000 hours a week in all (12 NYCRR 921-1.1(e)). Remote workers based at a site count at that site (921-1.1(e)(7)(i)).
- Trigger, plant closing: 25 or more.
- Trigger, mass layoff: 25 or more who are at least 33%, or 250 or more.
- Trigger, relocation of 50 miles or more, 25 or more.
- A plant closing affecting 25 or more full-time employees; a mass layoff of 25 or more full-time employees who are at least 33% of the site, or 250 or more; a relocation of 50 miles or more affecting 25 or more; a covered cut in hours (12 NYCRR 921-1.1).
- Look 30 days ahead and behind, and 90 days ahead and behind for rounds that are each too small (921-2.1(e)).
- Notice 90 calendar days before; by first class mail it must be postmarked at least 90 days before separation (921-2.1(a), 921-2.2(a)).
- Back pay and benefits for the period of violation up to 60 days or half the days employed, whichever is smaller; a civil penalty of up to $500 a day, avoided only by paying each employee in full within 3 weeks of the layoff (921-7.2).

## Who gets the notice

- Each affected employee, full- or part-time
- Each employee representative (every bargaining unit with affected employees)
- The Commissioner of Labor (New York State DOL)
- The local workforce development board where the site is
- The chief elected official of each unit of local government where the site is
- Each school district where the site is
- Each locality that provides police, fire, emergency medical or ambulance services to the site

## Questions

### How much notice does New York require?

90 days under NY WARN (N.Y. Labor Law Article 25-A (860 et seq.); 12 NYCRR Part 921), against 60 under federal WARN.

### Which employers does NY WARN cover?

50 or more employees in New York State not counting part-time employees, or 50 or more including part-time who work at least 2,000 hours a week in all (12 NYCRR 921-1.1(e)). Remote workers based at a site count at that site (921-1.1(e)(7)(i)).

### Where does the New York notice go?

Each affected employee, full- or part-time; Each employee representative (every bargaining unit with affected employees); The Commissioner of Labor (New York State DOL); The local workforce development board where the site is; The chief elected official of each unit of local government where the site is; Each school district where the site is; Each locality that provides police, fire, emergency medical or ambulance services to the site.
