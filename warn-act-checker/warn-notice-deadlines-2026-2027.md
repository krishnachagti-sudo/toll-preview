# WARN notice deadlines, November 2026 to December 2027

To lay people off on January 15, 2027, federal WARN notice must be received by November 16, 2026 and New York or New Jersey notice by October 17, 2026; for a year-end last day of December 31, 2026 the federal date is November 1, 2026, and Iowa's 30 days run to December 1, 2026.

- January 15, 2027 last day: November 16, 2026

## Questions

### When must WARN notice go out for a January 15, 2027 layoff?

Received by November 16, 2026 for federal WARN (60 days), October 17, 2026 for New York or New Jersey (90 days).

### When should we decide whether WARN applies?

About 65 to 70 days before the action, so there is time to give notice (DOL Employer's Guide).
