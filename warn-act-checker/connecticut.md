# Connecticut WARN notice

Connecticut's Department of Labor WARN page describes only the federal WARN Act: 100 or more full-time employees, and for a 120-person site a layoff of 50 full-time employees in 30 days triggers 60 days' notice, due by November 16, 2026 for a last day of January 15, 2027; send the state's copy to the Rapid Response Unit in Wethersfield.

- Notice: 60 days
- Losses at 120: 50

## The rules

- The Connecticut DOL WARN page describes the federal Act (100 or more full-time workers, 60 days) and where to send the notice. No other notice law is described there.

## Who gets the notice

- Send the federal notice to the Rapid Response Unit, Connecticut Department of Labor, 200 Folly Brook Boulevard, Wethersfield, CT 06109-1114, or dol.rapidresponse@ct.gov

The federal recipients also apply: each affected employee or their union, and the chief elected official of the local government (20 CFR 639.6).

## Questions

### Does Connecticut have its own WARN Act?

The Connecticut Department of Labor WARN page describes the federal Act and where to send the notice; it describes no separate state notice law.

### Where does the Connecticut notice go?

Send the federal notice to the Rapid Response Unit, Connecticut Department of Labor, 200 Folly Brook Boulevard, Wethersfield, CT 06109-1114, or dol.rapidresponse@ct.gov.
