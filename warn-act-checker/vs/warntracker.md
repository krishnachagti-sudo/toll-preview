# WARNTracker vs this checker

WARNTracker sells the WARN notices other employers have filed, $250 a month for a weekly CSV covering 45 states and DC or $2,000 for the history (read November 8, 2026); it does not work out whether your own layoff needs notice, which this checker does free per site and at $0.05 per affected employee for the full employee list.

## When WARNTracker is better

When you want to see filed notices by company, state or date: for job seekers, investors, researchers or anyone tracking other employers' layoffs. This checker holds no filed notices.

> Worked out from the counts and dates shown under 20 CFR Part 639 and the state sources listed. Whether locations are one single site, whether an exception applies and who the employer is are facts for you and your counsel. Not legal advice.
