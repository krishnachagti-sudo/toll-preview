## When the DOL is better

When you need the official text and the DOL's own reading, or to learn the rules: the guide is plain, free and in the public domain. Read it with this checker, not instead of it.

## Questions

### Does the Department of Labor enforce WARN?

No. WARN is enforced through the federal courts; the DOL has no standing in enforcement and cannot give binding advice on individual cases (20 CFR 639.1(d); DOL Employer's Guide).
