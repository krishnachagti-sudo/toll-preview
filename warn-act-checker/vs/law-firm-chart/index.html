Law-firm mini-WARN charts vs this checker

Barnes & Thornburg's June 4, 2025 post lists 13 states with mini-WARN acts; Washington's RCW 49.45 took effect on July 27, 2025, after it, so a dated chart can miss a law, while this checker applies 10 state laws each read on the state's own site on November 8, 2026 and computes the notice for your sites.

When a law-firm chart is better

When counsel is writing the notice, or a state this checker does not cover is involved (Hawaii, New Hampshire, Vermont and Wisconsin are in the firm's list and not read here). A lawyer also judges single-site and exception questions, which this tool leaves to you.

Worked out from the counts and dates shown under 20 CFR Part 639 and the state sources listed. Whether locations are one single site, whether an exception applies and who the employer is are facts for you and your counsel. Not legal advice.
