# Tennessee WARN Act

The Tennessee Plant Closing and Reduction in Operations Act covers 50 to 99 employees at a site; at an 80-person site 50 full-time losses in 3 months trigger it, against 50 for federal WARN, and the Commissioner must be phoned when employees are told for a last day of January 15, 2027.

- Notice: when employees are told
- Losses at 80: 50

## The rules

- At least 50 but not more than 99 full-time employees at a workplace in Tennessee (50-1-601(1)). Employers with 100 or more fall under the federal Act.
- Trigger, reduction in operations: 50 or more.
- A closure, modernization, relocation of more than 50 miles or management policy that cuts the workplace by 50 or more employees, permanently or indefinitely, in any 3-month period (50-1-601(2)).
- When the employer tells the affected employees, it must then telephone the Commissioner with the circumstances and the number affected (50-1-602(a)). The law sets no number of days.
- Not covered: labor disputes, construction sites and other temporary workplaces, customary seasonal factors (50-1-603).

## Questions

### How much notice does Tennessee require?

Tennessee 50-1-601 sets no number of days: the employer telephones the Commissioner when it tells the affected employees (Tenn. Code Ann. 50-1-602(a)).

### Which employers does Tennessee 50-1-601 cover?

At least 50 but not more than 99 full-time employees at a workplace in Tennessee (50-1-601(1)). Employers with 100 or more fall under the federal Act.

### Where does the Tennessee notice go?

The affected employees first, then the Commissioner of Labor and Workforce Development by telephone: Dislocated Worker Unit, 220 French Landing Drive, Nashville, TN 37243, (615) 210-2724 or toll free (800) 255-5872.
