# California WARN Act

The California WARN Act (Cal-WARN) covers 75 or more employees at a site; at a 120-person site 50 full-time losses in 30 days trigger it, against 50 for federal WARN, and 60 days' notice is due by November 16, 2026 for a last day of January 15, 2027.

- Notice: 60 days
- Losses at 120: 50

## The rules

- A "covered establishment" employs, or employed in the 12 months before, 75 or more full- and part-time employees; each must have been employed at least 6 of the 12 months before notice is due (Labor Code 1400.5(a), (h)).
- Trigger, closure (termination): any number.
- Trigger, mass layoff: 50 or more.
- Trigger, relocation of 100 miles or more, any number.
- Covers a closure of any size, a layoff of 50 or more in 30 days whatever the percentage, and a relocation of 100 miles or more (Labor Code 1400.5(d) to (f)).
- From 2026 (SB 617) the notice must say whether the employer will coordinate services with the local workforce board, another organization, or not at all, give the board's contact details, describe the services, and describe CalFresh with its helpline 1-877-847-3663.
- Liability: back pay at the higher of the final rate or the 3-year average rate, and benefits, for the period of violation up to 60 days or half the days employed, whichever is smaller; a civil penalty of up to $500 a day (Labor Code 1403).
- Exempt: seasonal employees hired knowing the work was seasonal; project work under Wage Orders 11, 12 or 16 (film, construction, drilling, logging, mining); a physical calamity or act of war (1400.5(g), 1401(c)).

## Who gets the notice

- Each affected employee
- The Employment Development Department (email EDDWarnNotice@edd.ca.gov, employer name in the subject line)
- The Local Workforce Development Board
- The chief elected official of each city and county government where the closure, relocation or layoff happens

## Questions

### How much notice does California require?

60 days under Cal-WARN (Cal. Labor Code 1400 to 1408 (1400.5, 1401, 1403)), the same as federal WARN.

### Which employers does Cal-WARN cover?

A "covered establishment" employs, or employed in the 12 months before, 75 or more full- and part-time employees; each must have been employed at least 6 of the 12 months before notice is due (Labor Code 1400.5(a), (h)).

### Where does the California notice go?

Each affected employee; The Employment Development Department (email EDDWarnNotice@edd.ca.gov, employer name in the subject line); The Local Workforce Development Board; The chief elected official of each city and county government where the closure, relocation or layoff happens.
