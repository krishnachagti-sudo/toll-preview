# California WARN notices in 2026

A California WARN notice for a layoff on February 26, 2027 must be received by December 28, 2026, 60 days before, and from 2026 must say whether you will coordinate services with the local workforce board and describe those services and CalFresh; this 52-person layoff at a 102-person office triggers Cal-WARN while federal WARN is not (44 full-time losses).

- Send by: December 28, 2026

## What the notice must contain

- Employer and contact: name and address of the site; name, phone and email of a company contact.
- The action: permanent or temporary; whether the whole plant closes; the first separation date and the schedule after it.
- Employees: job titles affected and the number in each, by location where there are several.
- Coordination (from 2026): whether you plan to coordinate rapid response services, the local board's contact details and the required description of its services.
- CalFresh: a description of the program and its helpline, 1-877-847-3663.
- Where they apply: bumping rights; each union's name and address and its chief elected officer.

## Who receives it

- Each affected employee
- The Employment Development Department (email EDDWarnNotice@edd.ca.gov, employer name in the subject line)
- The Local Workforce Development Board
- The chief elected official of each city and county government where the closure, relocation or layoff happens

## Steps

1. **Covered?.** 75 or more full- and part-time employees at the establishment in the last 12 months, each with 6 of 12 months (Labor Code 1400.5).
2. **Trigger.** A closure of any size, a layoff of 50 or more in 30 days, or a relocation of 100 miles or more.
3. **Content.** The federal elements plus, from 2026, rapid response coordination, the board's contacts, the services and CalFresh.
4. **Send.** Received 60 days before: employees, EDD by email, the Local Workforce Development Board, and the chief elected official of each city and county.

## Questions

### What changed in California WARN notices for 2026?

Under SB 617 a notice must explain how you will support laid-off workers: whether you plan to work with the local workforce board, another organization, or not coordinate services, with contact information and a short description of the services (EDD).

### How do you file a California WARN notice?

Email it to EDDWarnNotice@edd.ca.gov with the employer name in the subject line, as a DOC, DOCX or PDF, and send it to the Local Workforce Development Area and the chief elected official (EDD).
