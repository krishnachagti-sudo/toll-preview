# WARN recipients and deadlines

For a last day of January 15, 2027, federal WARN notice must reach each affected employee or their union, the state dislocated worker unit and the local chief elected official by November 16, 2026; New York and New Jersey need it by October 17, 2026, and New York names 7 kinds of recipient, including school districts and emergency services.

## Federal recipients

- Each affected employee, part-time employees included, or the chief elected officer of their union if they are represented (20 CFR 639.6(a), (b))
- The state dislocated worker unit, the state's rapid response team (20 CFR 639.6(c))
- The chief elected official of the local government where the closing or layoff happens; where the site is in more than one, the one the employer paid the most taxes the year before (20 CFR 639.3(g), 639.6(d))

## What each must be told

- Each affected employee without a union: 4 elements (20 CFR 639.7(d)).
- The union (chief elected officer of each representative): 4 elements (20 CFR 639.7(c)).
- The state dislocated worker unit and the local chief elected official: 6 elements (20 CFR 639.7(e)).

## Questions

### Who must receive a WARN notice?

Each affected employee (part-time included) or, if represented, the chief elected officer of the union; the state dislocated worker unit; and the chief elected official of the local government (20 CFR 639.6).

### Does the 60 days run from mailing or receipt?

From receipt: workers must receive the notice at least 60 days before separation (DOL Employer's Guide). New York accepts first class mail postmarked at least 90 days before (12 NYCRR 921-2.2(a)).
