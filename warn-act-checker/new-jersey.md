# New Jersey WARN Act

The New Jersey WARN Act covers 100 or more employees in the company; at a 120-person site 50 full-time losses at the site trigger it, against 50 for federal WARN, and 90 days' notice is due by October 17, 2026 for a last day of January 15, 2027.

- Notice: 90 days
- Losses at 120: 50

## The rules

- Employers with at least 100 employees; part-time employees are counted.
- Trigger, closure: 50 or more.
- Trigger, mass layoff: 50 or more.
- Trigger, relocation: 50 or more.
- Notice is due whenever a mass layoff affects at least 50 workers at a worksite; layoffs, closures and relocations all need 90 days' notice.
- Part-time employees have the same protections as full-time employees and are counted.
- Employers must give discharged employees one week of severance pay for each year of service.
- The summary read gives no period for counting the 50, so this tool counts every loss at the worksite in your list.

## Questions

### How much notice does New Jersey require?

90 days under NJ WARN (N.J.S.A. 34:21-1 et seq., as amended effective April 10, 2023), against 60 under federal WARN.

### Which employers does NJ WARN cover?

Employers with at least 100 employees; part-time employees are counted.

### Where does the New Jersey notice go?

Each affected employee (the state summary read names employees; the full recipient list is in N.J.S.A. 34:21-2, which was not read).
