# Washington WARN Act

The Washington Securing Timely Notification and Benefits for Laid-Off Employees Act covers 50 or more employees in Washington; at a 120-person site 50 full-time losses in 30 days trigger it, against 50 for federal WARN, and 60 days' notice is due by November 16, 2026 for a last day of January 15, 2027.

- Notice: 60 days
- Losses at 120: 50

## The rules

- A person who employs 50 or more employees in Washington, excluding part-time employees (RCW 49.45.010(8)).
- Trigger, business closing: 50 or more.
- Trigger, mass layoff (counted statewide): 50 or more.
- A business closing is an employment loss for 50 or more employees, excluding part-time, at a single site. A mass layoff is an employment loss for 50 or more employees, excluding part-time, in any 30-day period; the definition has no single-site words, so this tool counts all your Washington sites together (RCW 49.45.010).
- A short-term layoff of 3 months or less that is extended needs notice when the extension becomes foreseeable (49.45.020(1)(b)).
- The notice must say whether the action is the result of relocating or contracting out operations or positions (49.45.020(2)(e)).
- Back pay for each day of violation, up to 60 days, at the higher of the 3-year average or the final rate, plus benefits (49.45.040); a penalty of up to $500 a day (ESD).

## Who gets the notice

- The Employment Security Department (email ESDGPLayoffAssistance@esd.wa.gov with "WARN" in the subject, or P.O. Box 9046, Olympia, WA 98507-9046)
- Each affected employee, or their bargaining representative if they are represented
- The chief elected official of the community (named on ESD's page; not in RCW 49.45.020)

## Questions

### How much notice does Washington require?

60 days under Washington WARN (RCW 49.45 (2025 c 277; 2026 c 86)), the same as federal WARN.

### Which employers does Washington WARN cover?

A person who employs 50 or more employees in Washington, excluding part-time employees (RCW 49.45.010(8)).

### Where does the Washington notice go?

The Employment Security Department (email ESDGPLayoffAssistance@esd.wa.gov with "WARN" in the subject, or P.O. Box 9046, Olympia, WA 98507-9046); Each affected employee, or their bargaining representative if they are represented; The chief elected official of the community (named on ESD's page; not in RCW 49.45.020).
