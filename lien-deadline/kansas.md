# Kansas lien deadlines

For a job you first furnish on Monday, November 2, 2026, last furnish on Friday, January 29, 2027, with the project complete on Friday, February 26, 2027: as a subcontractor on a commercial project in Kansas, no preliminary notice applies to you here, the lien statement must be filed by April 29, 2027 and the suit to enforce it must be filed by April 29, 2028 (if recorded on the last day, April 29, 2027) (K.S.A. 60-1103(a)(1), (e); K.S.A. 60-1105(a)).

## The rules

Kansas lien law, read November 8, 2026

| Step | Who | When | Law |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| File the verified lien statement with the clerk of the district court | original (prime) contractor | Within four months after you last furnished material, equipment or supplies or last performed labor (five months on property other than residential property, if you file a notice of extension within the four months). | K.S.A. 60-1102(a), (c) |
| File the lien statement with the clerk of the district court | subcontractor, sub-subcontractor, supplier to the prime contractor, supplier to a subcontractor, laborer paid wages | Within three months after you last furnished supplies, material or equipment or last performed labor (five months on property other than residential property, if you file a notice of extension within the three months). | K.S.A. 60-1103(a)(1), (e) |
| Sue to foreclose the lien | everyone with a lien | Within one year from filing the lien statement. | K.S.A. 60-1105(a) |

Weekends and holidays: the Kansas text we read does not move a deadline that falls on a weekend or holiday, so the calculator keeps the statute's date and flags it with the business day before.

- Kansas has no general preliminary notice. On residential work, 60-1103 also requires the 60-1103a warning statement where it applies and, on new residential property sold to a good faith purchaser, a notice of intent to perform filed before the deed is recorded (60-1103b). We have not modeled 60-1103a.

## Kansas in its own words

- "within four months after the date material, equipment or supplies, used or consumed was last furnished or last labor performed under the contract a verified statement showing:" [K.S.A. 60-1102, Lien statement](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch60/060_011_0002.html)
- "a lien for the furnishing of labor, equipment, materials or supplies on property other than residential property may be claimed pursuant to this section within five months only if the claimant has filed a notice of extension within four months since last furnishing labor, equipment, materials or supplies to the job site." [K.S.A. 60-1102, Lien statement](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch60/060_011_0002.html)
- "The lien statement must state the name of the contractor and be filed within three months after the date supplies, material or equipment was last furnished or labor performed by the claimant;" [K.S.A. 60-1103, Liens of suppliers and subcontractors](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch60/060_011_0003.html)
- "a lien for the furnishing of labor, equipment, materials or supplies on property other than residential property may be claimed pursuant to this section, and amendments thereto, within five months only if the claimant has filed a notice of extension within three months since last furnishing labor, equipment, materials or supplies to the job site." [K.S.A. 60-1103, Liens of suppliers and subcontractors](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch60/060_011_0003.html)
- "An action to foreclose a lien under this article shall be brought within one year from the time of filing the lien statement" [K.S.A. 60-1105, Action to foreclose](https://www.ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch60/060_011_0005.html)

## Questions

### How long do I have to file a mechanics lien in Kansas?

Within four months after you last furnished material, equipment or supplies or last performed labor (five months on property other than residential property, if you file a notice of extension within the four months) (K.S.A. 60-1102(a), (c)).

### How long do I have to sue to enforce a Kansas lien?

Within one year from filing the lien statement (K.S.A. 60-1105(a)).
