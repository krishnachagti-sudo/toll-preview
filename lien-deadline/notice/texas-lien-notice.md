# Texas notice of claim for unpaid labor or materials

Your Texas notice of claim for unpaid labor or materials is due by the 15th day of the third month after each month in which the unpaid labor or materials were provided (Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1)(1)). The form below is the statute's; the PDF fills it in with your job and adds a proof-of-service log.

- [Worked example](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/lien-deadline/?state=TX&role=sub&project=commercial&first=2026-11-02): If you first furnished on November 2, 2026, send it for work in November 2026, by Tuesday, February 16, 2027; for December 2026, by Monday, March 15, 2027; and so on, one notice for each month (Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1)(1)).

## Who sends it, to whom, and how

- Who: Subcontractors and the others the statute names: subcontractor, sub-subcontractor, supplier to the prime contractor, supplier to a subcontractor, laborer paid wages.
- To: the owner or reputed owner and the original contractor.
- How: in person, by certified mail, or by a traceable delivery service that confirms receipt; a certified-mail notice counts when mailed (53.003(b), (c)).
- Late: Each month has its own notice. A month whose notice day has passed cannot be added to the lien; months still open can (53.056(a-1)).

Notice dates for the months of one job (November 2, 2026 to January 29, 2027)

| Work in | Notice by |
| --- | --- |
| November 2026 | Tuesday, February 16, 2027 (moved off a weekend or holiday, 53.003(e)) |
| December 2026 | Monday, March 15, 2027 |
| January 2027 | Thursday, April 15, 2027 |

## Steps

1. **Find your date.** By the 15th day of the third month after each month in which the unpaid labor or materials were provided (Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1)(1)).
2. **Fill in the form.** Use the statute's words (Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-2) asks for substantially the following form): the parties, the property and what you furnish.
3. **Serve it.** On the owner or reputed owner and the original contractor, by in person, by certified mail, or by a traceable delivery service that confirms receipt; a certified-mail notice counts when mailed (53.003(b), (c)).
4. **Keep proof.** Keep the mailing receipt or signed acknowledgment with a copy of the notice; you will need the date if you record a lien.
