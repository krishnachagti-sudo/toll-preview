# Texas lien deadline chart

For work in October 2026, a Texas subcontractor or supplier on a commercial job must send the notice of claim by January 15, 2027 and file the lien affidavit by February 16, 2027 if that was the last month of work; on a residential project, by December 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027 (Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1), 53.052(b), (c); 53.003(e) moves a weekend or holiday to the next business day).

- The notice dates are for claimants other than the original contractor; the original contractor files its affidavit by the 15th day of the fourth month (third, residential) after the month its work was completed, terminated or abandoned (53.052(a)).
- Residential means a home the owner lives in or will live in (53.001(8), (10)).
- Weekends and federal holidays are moved under 53.003(e); Texas-only state holidays we have not read, so check those yourself.

> Not legal advice and not a law firm. Dates are worked out from each state's own statute text, quoted with the date we read it. Deadlines are strict: a lien recorded one day late is usually lost. Act before the date shown, and earlier when it falls on a weekend or holiday. For disputes, large amounts or anything unusual about your job, ask a construction attorney licensed in the project's state.
