# Direct contractors and preliminary notices

Mostly no: of the 10 jurisdictions read on their own statute sites, a contractor in a direct contract with the owner sends a preliminary notice only in Arizona (the 20-day notice, A.R.S. 33-992.01(B)) and Minnesota (the contractor's notice in the contract or within ten days, 514.011, subd. 1); Florida, Washington and Montana exempt it by name (713.06(2)(a); RCW 60.04.031(2)(a); 71-3-531(1)(a)).

## Questions

### Do general contractors need to send a preliminary notice?

Usually not among the states read here. Arizona is the exception: every claimant except wage laborers serves the 20-day notice (A.R.S. 33-992.01(B)); and in Minnesota the contractor puts the pre-lien notice in its contract or delivers it within ten days (514.011, subd. 1).
