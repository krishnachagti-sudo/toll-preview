# Texas monthly notice dates, 2026 to 2027

In Texas the notice of claim for a month's unpaid work is due on the 15th day of the third month after it (second month on a residential project); from November 2026 to December 2027 that day moves 4 times because the 15th falls on a weekend or federal holiday, for example to Monday, November 16, 2026 for August 2026 commercial work (Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1), 53.003(e)).

## The calendar

| Monday, November 16, 2026 (moved from the 15th) | August 2026 | September 2026 |
| Tuesday, December 15, 2026 | September 2026 | October 2026 |
| Friday, January 15, 2027 | October 2026 | November 2026 |
| Tuesday, February 16, 2027 (moved from the 15th) | November 2026 | December 2026 |
| Monday, March 15, 2027 | December 2026 | January 2027 |

| Monday, May 17, 2027 (moved from the 15th) | February 2027 | March 2027 |
| Tuesday, June 15, 2027 | March 2027 | April 2027 |
| Thursday, July 15, 2027 | April 2027 | May 2027 |
| Monday, August 16, 2027 (moved from the 15th) | May 2027 | June 2027 |
| Wednesday, September 15, 2027 | June 2027 | July 2027 |
| Friday, October 15, 2027 | July 2027 | August 2027 |
| Monday, November 15, 2027 | August 2027 | September 2027 |
| Wednesday, December 15, 2027 | September 2027 | October 2027 |

- The original contractor sends no monthly notice (53.056(a)).
- The lien affidavit has its own date, the 15th day of the fourth month (third, residential) after your last month of work (53.052).
- Only weekends and US federal holidays are moved here; check Texas-only state holidays yourself.
