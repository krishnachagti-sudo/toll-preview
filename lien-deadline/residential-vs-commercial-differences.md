# Residential vs commercial lien deadlines

The same job can have different clocks on a home: in Texas a subcontractor's lien affidavit on a commercial job last worked January 29, 2027 is due May 17, 2027, but April 15, 2027 on a residential project the owner lives in; Arizona allows no lien by a subcontractor on an owner-occupant's dwelling; Montana's notice applies only to homes (Tex. Prop. Code 53.052(b), (c); A.R.S. 33-1002(B); Mont. Code 71-3-531(1)).

## What counts as residential

- Texas: a single-family house, duplex, triplex or quadruplex (or a condominium or co-op unit) that one of the owners uses or will use as a dwelling (53.001(8), (10)).
- Kansas: a structure built for use as a residence by no more than two families (60-1102(d)).
- Arizona: a one- or two-family dwelling of an owner-occupant who held title before the work began and lives there (33-1002(A)).
- Montana: the notice applies to dwellings of up to four families and not to commercial work (71-3-531(1)).
