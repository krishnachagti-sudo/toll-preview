# Montana lien deadlines

In Montana, a subcontractor records the lien not later than 90 days after your final furnishing of services or materials, or after the owner files a notice of completion (we show the earlier date when you enter one) (Mont. Code 71-3-535(1)).

- [Worked example](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/lien-deadline/?state=MT&role=sub&project=new-home&first=2026-11-02&last=2027-01-29&completion=2027-02-26): For a job you first furnish on Monday, November 2, 2026, last furnish on Friday, January 29, 2027, with the project complete on Friday, February 26, 2027: as a subcontractor on a new home in Montana, your notice is due November 22, 2026, the lien must be filed by April 29, 2027 and the suit to enforce it must be filed by April 29, 2029 (if recorded on the last day, April 29, 2027) (Mont. Code 71-3-531(2), (3); Mont. Code 71-3-535(1); Mont. Code 71-3-562).

## The rules

Montana lien law, read November 8, 2026

| Step | Who | When | Law |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Give the contracting owner notice of the right to claim a lien | subcontractor, sub-subcontractor, supplier to the prime contractor, supplier to a subcontractor | Not later than 20 days after you first furnish services or materials (45 days where 71-3-531(4) applies: lender-financed work that is not on an owner-occupied residence); then file a copy with the county clerk and recorder within 5 business days. | Mont. Code 71-3-531(2), (3) |
| File the lien with the county clerk and recorder | everyone with a lien | Not later than 90 days after your final furnishing of services or materials, or after the owner files a notice of completion (we show the earlier date when you enter one). | Mont. Code 71-3-535(1) |
| Commence the action | everyone with a lien | Within 2 years from the date the lien is filed. | Mont. Code 71-3-562 |

Weekends and holidays: the Montana text we read does not move a deadline that falls on a weekend or holiday, so the calculator keeps the statute's date and flags it with the business day before.

- No notice is required from an original contractor dealing directly with the owner, a wage earner or laborer, or anyone on a dwelling for five or more families or on partly or wholly commercial work (71-3-531(1)). Choose "new home" or "home improvement" only for a dwelling of up to four families.
- The copy filed with the clerk and recorder lapses after 1 year unless continued (71-3-531(6)).

## Questions

### When is the preliminary notice due in Montana?

Not later than 20 days after you first furnish services or materials (45 days where 71-3-531(4) applies: lender-financed work that is not on an owner-occupied residence); then file a copy with the county clerk and recorder within 5 business days (Mont. Code 71-3-531(2), (3)).

### How long do I have to file a mechanics lien in Montana?

Not later than 90 days after your final furnishing of services or materials, or after the owner files a notice of completion (we show the earlier date when you enter one) (Mont. Code 71-3-535(1)).

### How long do I have to sue to enforce a Montana lien?

Within 2 years from the date the lien is filed (Mont. Code 71-3-562).
