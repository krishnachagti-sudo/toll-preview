# Miller Act bond claim deadlines

On a federal project, a second-tier subcontractor or supplier whose last work was on January 29, 2027 must give the prime contractor written notice by April 29, 2027 and sue on the payment bond by January 29, 2028; a first-tier subcontractor gives no notice, may sue from April 29, 2027 and must sue by January 29, 2028 (40 U.S.C. 3133(b)(1), (2), (4)).

## The statute

- "A person having a direct contractual relationship with a subcontractor but no contractual relationship, express or implied, with the contractor furnishing the payment bond may bring a civil action on the payment bond on giving written notice to the contractor within 90 days from the date on which the person did or performed the last of the labor or furnished or supplied the last of the material for which the claim is made." [40 U.S.C. 3133, Rights of persons furnishing labor or material (Miller Act)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title40/html/USCODE-2023-title40-subtitleII-partA-chap31-subchapIII-sec3133.htm)
- "has not been paid in full within 90 days after the day on which the person did or performed the last of the labor or furnished or supplied the material for which the claim is made may bring a civil action on the payment bond" [40 U.S.C. 3133, Rights of persons furnishing labor or material (Miller Act)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title40/html/USCODE-2023-title40-subtitleII-partA-chap31-subchapIII-sec3133.htm)
- "An action brought under this subsection must be brought no later than one year after the day on which the last of the labor was performed or material was supplied by the person bringing the action." [40 U.S.C. 3133, Rights of persons furnishing labor or material (Miller Act)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title40/html/USCODE-2023-title40-subtitleII-partA-chap31-subchapIII-sec3133.htm)

## Questions

### What is the Miller Act notice deadline?

A claimant with a contract with a subcontractor but none with the prime contractor gives the prime written notice within 90 days from the last labor or material (40 U.S.C. 3133(b)(2)).

### How long do I have to sue on a Miller Act bond?

No later than one year after the day the last labor was performed or material supplied by the person suing (40 U.S.C. 3133(b)(4)).
