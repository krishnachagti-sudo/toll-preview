Supplier notice checklist

On every new job a supplier collects the owner, prime contractor and property details at the order and calendars the first notice from the first delivery: for a first delivery on November 2, 2026 to a subcontractor, by November 22, 2026 in Arizona, December 17, 2026 in Florida and February 16, 2027 for the Texas notice covering November's deliveries.

Not legal advice and not a law firm. Dates are worked out from each state's own statute text, quoted with the date we read it. Deadlines are strict: a lien recorded one day late is usually lost. Act before the date shown, and earlier when it falls on a weekend or holiday. For disputes, large amounts or anything unusual about your job, ask a construction attorney licensed in the project's state.

Steps

  1. At the order. Get the owner's name and address, the prime contractor's, the lender's (if any), the property address and legal description, and who is ordering.
  2. At the first delivery. Enter the first delivery date in the calculator and calendar the notice date (or each Texas month).
  3. Serve the notice. Serve the state's notice the way its statute says (certified mail in most states read here) and keep the receipt.
  4. At each delivery. Keep delivery tickets: the last one sets the lien date.
  5. When paid. Exchange lien waivers for the payments you receive.
  6. If unpaid. Calendar the lien and suit dates from your last delivery.
