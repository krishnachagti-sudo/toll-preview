January 28, 2027last day to record the lien

As a subcontractor on a commercial project in Arizona, your preliminary twenty day notice is due March 23, 2026, the lien must be recorded by January 28, 2027 and the suit to enforce it must be filed by July 28, 2027 (if recorded on the last day, January 28, 2027) (A.R.S. 33-992.01(B), (C); A.R.S. 33-993(A); A.R.S. 33-998(A)).

  1. 1Serve the preliminary twenty day noticeMar 23, 2026A.R.S. 33-992.01(B), (C): not later than 20 days after you first furnish labor, services, materials or equipment to the jobsite
  2. 2Record the notice and claim of lienJan 28, 2027A.R.S. 33-993(A): within 120 days after completion of the work, or within 60 days after a notice of completion is recorded
  3. 3Sue to foreclose and record a notice of pendency of actionJul 28, 2027A.R.S. 33-998(A): within six months after the lien is recorded (if recorded on the last day, January 28, 2027)

A late notice still counts, but only for what you furnished within the 20 days before you served it and after (33-992.01(E)).

The statute's words, who gets each notice and how

Notice: to the owner, the original contractor, the construction lender (if any) and the person you contracted with, by first class mail with a certificate of mailing, or registered or certified mail; service is complete when mailed (33-992.01(F)).

shall be given not later than twenty days after the claimant has first furnished labor, professional services, materials, machinery, fixtures or tools to the jobsite A.R.S. 33-992.01, read November 8, 2026

Lien: record with the county recorder, then serve a copy on the owner within a reasonable time; attach the 20-day notice and its proof of mailing (33-993(A)).

Within 120 days after completion of the work, or within 60 days after a notice of completion is recorded: A.R.S. 33-993(A), read November 8, 2026. Change anything above to see the statute's own words.

Suit: A lien granted under the provisions of this article shall not continue for a longer period than six months after it is recorded, unless action is brought within that period to enforce the lien and a notice of pendency of action is recorded pursuant to section 12-1191 in the office of the county recorder in the county where the property is located. Deadlines are strict

  • Not legal advice and not a law firm. Dates are worked out from each state's own statute text, quoted with the date we read it.
  • Deadlines are strict: a lien recorded one day late is usually lost. Act before the date shown, and earlier when it falls on a weekend or holiday. Only Texas's statute moves a weekend deadline (53.003(e)); elsewhere we keep the statute's date and flag a weekend or federal holiday.

The states whose own statute site we have read, and federal projects under the Miller Act.
