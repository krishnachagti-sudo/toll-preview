# Virginia lien deadlines

In Virginia, a subcontractor records the lien not later than 90 days from the last day of the month in which you last performed labor or furnished material, and in no event later than 90 days after the building is completed or the work otherwise ends (Va. Code 43-4).

Code 43-4; Va. Code 43-17).

## The rules

Virginia lien law, read November 8, 2026

| Step | Who | When | Law |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Give the owner written notice of the amount and character of your claim | subcontractor | Required to perfect a subcontractor's lien, in addition to filing the memorandum; 43-7 sets no separate day, so give it no later than the memorandum. | Va. Code 43-7(A) |
| Give the owner and the general contractor written notice of the amount and character of your claim | sub-subcontractor, supplier to a subcontractor | Required to perfect a lien for work or materials furnished to a subcontractor, in addition to filing the memorandum; 43-9 sets no separate day, so give it no later than the memorandum. | Va. Code 43-9 |
| File the memorandum of lien | everyone with a lien | Not later than 90 days from the last day of the month in which you last performed labor or furnished material, and in no event later than 90 days after the building is completed or the work otherwise ends. | Va. Code 43-4 |
| Sue to enforce the lien | everyone with a lien | Within six months after the memorandum is recorded or 60 days after the building is completed or the work otherwise ends, whichever is later. | Va. Code 43-17 |

Weekends and holidays: the Virginia text we read does not move a deadline that falls on a weekend or holiday, so the calculator keeps the statute's date and flags it with the business day before.

- A subcontractor's lien cannot exceed what the owner owes the general contractor when the notice is given or later comes to owe (43-7(A)).
- Not modeled here: 43-4.01 (the mechanics' lien agent named on a building permit for a one- or two-family home, and its notice). If the permit names a lien agent, read 43-4.01 before relying on these dates.

## Virginia in its own words

**Read the statute's words (4 quotations)**

- "Any subcontractor, in order to perfect the lien given him by § 43-3 shall comply with § 43-4, and in addition give notice in writing to the owner of the property or his agent of the amount and character of his claim." [Code of Virginia 43-7, Perfection of lien by subcontractor](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title43/chapter1/section43-7/)
- "Any person performing labor or furnishing materials for a subcontractor, in order to perfect the lien given him by § 43-3, shall comply with the provisions of § 43-4, and in addition thereto give notice in writing to the owner of the property, or his agent, and to the general contractor, or his agent, of the amount and character of his claim." [Code of Virginia 43-9, Perfection of lien by person furnishing for a subcontractor](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title43/chapter1/section43-9/)
- "shall file a memorandum of lien at any time after the work is commenced or material furnished, but not later than 90 days from the last day of the month in which he last performs labor or furnishes material, and in no event later than 90 days from the time such building, structure, or railroad is completed, or the work thereon otherwise terminated." [Code of Virginia 43-4, Perfection of lien by general contractor](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title43/chapter1/section43-4/)
- "No suit to enforce any lien perfected under §§ 43-4, 43-5 and 43-7 to 43-10 shall be brought after six months from the time when the memorandum of lien was recorded or after sixty days from the time the building, structure or railroad was completed or the work thereon otherwise terminated, whichever time shall last occur" [Code of Virginia 43-17, Limitation on suit to enforce lien](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title43/chapter1/section43-17/)

## Questions

### How long do I have to file a mechanics lien in Virginia?

Not later than 90 days from the last day of the month in which you last performed labor or furnished material, and in no event later than 90 days after the building is completed or the work otherwise ends (Va. Code 43-4).

### How long do I have to sue to enforce a Virginia lien?

Within six months after the memorandum is recorded or 60 days after the building is completed or the work otherwise ends, whichever is later (Va. Code 43-17).
