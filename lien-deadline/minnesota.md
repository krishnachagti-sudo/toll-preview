# Minnesota lien deadlines

In Minnesota, a subcontractor records the lien within 120 days after doing the last of the work or furnishing the last item: record it with the county recorder (or registrar of titles) and serve a copy on the owner (Minn. Stat. 514.08, subd. 1); the notice comes first, not later than 45 days after you first furnish labor, skill or materials for the improvement (Minn. Stat. 514.011, subd. 2(a)).

Stat. 514.011, subd. 2(a); Minn. Stat. 514.08, subd. 1; Minn. Stat. 514.12, subd. 3).

## The rules

Minnesota lien law, read November 8, 2026

| Step | Who | When | Law |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Give the owner the contractor's pre-lien notice | original (prime) contractor | In the written contract with the owner; with no written contract, delivered personally or by certified mail within ten days after the work is agreed upon (only if you use subcontractors or suppliers). | Minn. Stat. 514.011, subd. 1 |
| Give the owner the pre-lien notice | subcontractor, sub-subcontractor, supplier to the prime contractor, supplier to a subcontractor, laborer paid wages | Not later than 45 days after you first furnish labor, skill or materials for the improvement. | Minn. Stat. 514.011, subd. 2(a) |
| Record the lien statement and serve a copy | everyone with a lien | Within 120 days after doing the last of the work or furnishing the last item: record it with the county recorder (or registrar of titles) and serve a copy on the owner. | Minn. Stat. 514.08, subd. 1 |
| File the complaint (or answer) to enforce the lien | everyone with a lien | Within one year after the date of the last item of the claim as set forth in the recorded lien statement. | Minn. Stat. 514.12, subd. 3 |

Weekends and holidays: the Minnesota text we read does not move a deadline that falls on a weekend or holiday, so the calculator keeps the statute's date and flags it with the business day before.

## Minnesota in its own words

**Read the statute's words (5 quotations)**

- "If no written contract for the improvement is entered into, the notice must be prepared separately and delivered personally or by certified mail to the owner or the owner's authorized agent within ten days after the work of improvement is agreed upon." [Minn. Stat. 514.011, Notices](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/514.011)
- "A person who fails to provide the notice shall not have the lien and remedy provided by this chapter." [Minn. Stat. 514.011, Notices](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/514.011)
- "Every person who contributes to the improvement of real property so as to be entitled to a lien pursuant to section 514.01, except a party under direct contract with the owner must, as a necessary prerequisite to the validity of any claim or lien, cause to be given to the owner or the owner's authorized agent, either by personal delivery or by certified mail, not later than 45 days after the lien claimant has first furnished labor, skill or materials for the improvement, a written notice" [Minn. Stat. 514.011, Notices](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/514.011)
- "The lien ceases at the end of 120 days after doing the last of the work, or furnishing the last item of skill, material, or machinery, unless within this period:" [Minn. Stat. 514.08, Lien statement](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/514.08)
- "No lien shall be enforced in any case unless the holder thereof shall assert the same, either by filing a complaint or answer with the court administrator, within one year after the date of the last item of the claim as set forth in the recorded lien statement" [Minn. Stat. 514.12, Enforcement action](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/514.12)

## Questions

### When is the pre-lien notice due in Minnesota?

In the written contract with the owner; with no written contract, delivered personally or by certified mail within ten days after the work is agreed upon (only if you use subcontractors or suppliers) (Minn. Stat. 514.011, subd. 1).

### How long do I have to file a mechanics lien in Minnesota?

Within 120 days after doing the last of the work or furnishing the last item: record it with the county recorder (or registrar of titles) and serve a copy on the owner (Minn. Stat. 514.08, subd. 1).

### How long do I have to sue to enforce a Minnesota lien?

Within one year after the date of the last item of the claim as set forth in the recorded lien statement (Minn. Stat. 514.12, subd. 3).
