# Missed the preliminary notice?

Sometimes. For work begun November 2, 2026 and a notice sent late on January 15, 2027: in Arizona it still protects what you furnished from December 26, 2026 on, in Washington from November 17, 2026, in Texas the months whose notice day has not passed; in Florida a late notice to owner is a complete defense to the lien (A.R.S. 33-992.01(E); RCW 60.04.031(1); Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1); Fla. Stat. 713.06(2)(a)).

## One late notice in five states

A job you first furnish on Monday, November 2, 2026, last furnish on Friday, January 29, 2027, with the project complete on Friday, February 26, 2027; the notice goes out on Friday, January 15, 2027.

What a late notice still protects

| State | Outcome | Law |

A late notice still counts, but only for what you furnished within the 20 days before you served it and after (33-992.01(E)). | A.R.S. 33-992.01(E) |
| [Montana](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/lien-deadline/montana/) | Sent on January 15, 2027, after the November 22, 2026 deadline, the notice protects only what you furnished from December 26, 2026 on. A late notice still counts, but the lien covers only what you furnished within the 20 days before it was given (71-3-531(3)). | Mont. Code 71-3-531(3) |
| [Washington](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/lien-deadline/washington/) | Sent on January 15, 2027, after the December 31, 2026 deadline, the notice protects only what you furnished from November 17, 2026 on. A later notice still counts, but only for what you supplied after the date 60 days (10 days on a new single-family home) before it was mailed or delivered (60.04.031(1)). | RCW 60.04.031(1) |
| [Texas](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/lien-deadline/texas/) | A notice sent on January 15, 2027 is in time for every month entered. | Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1) |
| [Florida](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/lien-deadline/florida/) | Sent on January 15, 2027, after the December 17, 2026 deadline. Florida is strict: failing to serve the notice, or serving it late, is a complete defense to enforcing the lien (713.06(2)(a)). | Fla. Stat. 713.06(2)(a) |

## What to do now

- Send the notice today: in Arizona, Montana and Washington the protected period starts a fixed number of days before the notice, so each day of delay costs a day of work.
- Write down the lien recording date: it runs from your last furnishing or the completion, not from the notice.
- Keep proof of mailing; the date of service is what counts.

> Not legal advice and not a law firm. Dates are worked out from each state's own statute text, quoted with the date we read it. Deadlines are strict: a lien recorded one day late is usually lost. Act before the date shown, and earlier when it falls on a weekend or holiday. For disputes, large amounts or anything unusual about your job, ask a construction attorney licensed in the project's state.

## Questions

### Can I file a mechanics lien if I missed the preliminary notice?

It depends on the state: Arizona, Montana and Washington count a late notice for recent work only; Texas loses the months whose notice day has passed; Florida treats a late notice to owner as a complete defense. The lien recording deadline is a separate clock either way.

### What should I do today if I am late?

Send the notice now: in the lookback states every day of delay moves the protected-from date later. Then check the lien recording date, which has its own clock.
