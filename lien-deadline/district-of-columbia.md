# District of Columbia lien deadlines

In District of Columbia, a subcontractor records the lien during construction or within 90 days after the earlier of the completion or the termination of the project (D.C. Code 40-301.02(a)(1); 40-303.01).

- [Worked example](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/lien-deadline/?state=DC&role=sub&project=commercial&first=2026-11-02&last=2027-01-29&completion=2027-02-26): For a job you first furnish on Monday, November 2, 2026, last furnish on Friday, January 29, 2027, with the project complete on Friday, February 26, 2027: as a subcontractor on a commercial project in District of Columbia, no preliminary notice applies to you here, the notice of intent (the lien) must be recorded by May 27, 2027 and the suit to enforce it must be filed by November 23, 2027 (if recorded on the last day, May 27, 2027) (D.C. Code 40-301.02(a)(1); 40-303.01; D.C. Code 40-303.13(a)).

## The rules

District of Columbia lien law, read November 8, 2026

| Step | Who | When | Law |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Record the notice of intent in the land records | original (prime) contractor, subcontractor, supplier to the prime contractor, laborer paid wages | During construction or within 90 days after the earlier of the completion or the termination of the project. | D.C. Code 40-301.02(a)(1); 40-303.01 |
| Send the owner a copy of the recorded notice of intent | original (prime) contractor | By certified mail within 5 business days after it is recorded (post it on the property if the mail comes back). | D.C. Code 40-301.02(a)(2) |
| Sue to enforce the lien and record a notice of pendency | original (prime) contractor, subcontractor, supplier to the prime contractor, laborer paid wages | Within 180 days after the notice of intent is recorded, and record a notice of pendency of action within 10 days of filing suit. | D.C. Code 40-303.13(a) |

- The D.C. Code we read gives the lien to the original contractor and to persons directly employed by the original contractor. It does not name sub-subcontractors or suppliers to a subcontractor, so we give no dates for them. (D.C. Code 40-301.01; 40-303.01)

Weekends and holidays: the District of Columbia text we read does not move a deadline that falls on a weekend or holiday, so the calculator keeps the statute's date and flags it with the business day before.

- Business days for the 5-day copy skip weekends and US federal holidays; District holidays we have not read.

## District of Columbia in its own words

**Read the statute's words (4 quotations)**

- "The notice of intent shall be recorded during the construction or within 90 days after the earlier of the completion or termination of the project." [D.C. Code 40-301.02, Notice of intent](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/40-301.02)
- "Any person directly employed by a contractor described by § 40-301.01 (any such contractor also referred to herein as original contractor), whether the person is a subcontractor, materialman, or laborer, to furnish work or materials for the completion of the project, shall be entitled to the same rights and subject to the same obligations as the original contractor under this chapter" [D.C. Code 40-303.01, Subcontractors, materialmen and laborers](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/40-303.01)
- "shall send to the owner, by certified mail to the current address (or if not available in the local public records, the last known address) of the owner, a copy of the notice of intent within 5 business days after the date of its recordation in the land records." [D.C. Code 40-301.02, Notice of intent](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/40-301.02)
- "Filing suit under § 40-303.08 to enforce the lien at anytime within 180 days after the date that the notice of intent is recorded in the land records; and" [D.C. Code 40-303.13, Suit to enforce](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/40-303.13)

## Questions

### How long do I have to file a mechanics lien in District of Columbia?

During construction or within 90 days after the earlier of the completion or the termination of the project (D.C. Code 40-301.02(a)(1); 40-303.01).

### How long do I have to sue to enforce a District of Columbia lien?

Within 180 days after the notice of intent is recorded, and record a notice of pendency of action within 10 days of filing suit (D.C. Code 40-303.13(a)).
