# Siteline vs this calculator

Siteline sells lien rights management as an add-on to its billing software for subcontractors, with pricing through a demo; this calculator is free for the dates, with the statute quoted (for an Arizona subcontractor who last furnished on January 29, 2027, a project complete on February 26, 2027: lien by June 26, 2027), and $2.90 for a filled statutory notice.

## Side by side

Siteline facts from its own site, read November 8, 2026

| What | Siteline | This calculator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Pay app and billing software for subcontractors; "Add-On: Lien Rights Management" | A single calculator and notice form, no account needed for dates |
| Price | No prices on its pricing page; "Book a Demo" | Dates free; $2.90 per notice PDF; $0.02 per API lookup |
| Deadlines | Tracks "critical deadlines and milestones based on state-specific lien laws" | Notice, lien and suit dates with the statute quoted and dated |
| Notices | Generates required notices and sends them (pricing page) | Fills the statutory form; you serve it |

## When Siteline is the better choice

- When you already bill through Siteline's pay app software and want lien rights inside that workflow.
- When you want notices sent for you from your billing data.

> Not legal advice and not a law firm. Dates are worked out from each state's own statute text, quoted with the date we read it. Deadlines are strict: a lien recorded one day late is usually lost. Act before the date shown, and earlier when it falls on a weekend or holiday. For disputes, large amounts or anything unusual about your job, ask a construction attorney licensed in the project's state.
