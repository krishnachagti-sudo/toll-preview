Lien deadlines by state

Maryland lien deadlines

For a job you first furnish on Monday, November 2, 2026, last furnish on Friday, January 29, 2027, with the project complete on Friday, February 26, 2027: as a subcontractor on a commercial project in Maryland, your notice to owner of intention to claim a lien is due May 29, 2027, the petition to establish the lien must be filed by July 28, 2027 and the suit to enforce it must be filed by July 28, 2028 (if recorded on the last day, July 28, 2027) (Md. Code, Real Prop. 9-104(a); Md. Code, Real Prop. 9-105(a); Md. Code, Real Prop. 9-109).

Weekends and holidays: the Maryland text we read does not move a deadline that falls on a weekend or holiday, so the calculator keeps the statute's date and flags it with the business day before.

  • Maryland counts the 120 days from "doing the work or furnishing the materials". We count from your last day; work done more than 120 days before the notice may fall outside it, so the safest notice day is 120 days after your first day.
  • On a single family dwelling being built on the owner's land for the owner's own residence, the notice must also reach the owner before the owner has paid the contractor in full, and the lien is limited to what the owner still owes (9-104(a)(2), (f)(3)).

Maryland in its own words

  • "A subcontractor doing work or furnishing materials or both for or about a building other than a single family dwelling being erected on the owner’s land for his own residence is not entitled to a lien under this subtitle unless, within 120 days after doing the work or furnishing the materials, the subcontractor gives written notice of an intention to claim a lien substantially in the form specified in subsection (b) of this section." Md. Code, Real Property 9-104, Notice by subcontractor
  • "“Subcontractor” means a person who has a contract with anyone except the owner or his agent." Md. Code, Real Property 9-101, Definitions
  • "In order to establish a lien under this subtitle, a person entitled to a lien shall file proceedings in the circuit court for the county where the land or any part of the land is located within 180 days after the work has been finished or the materials furnished." Md. Code, Real Property 9-105, Petition to establish lien
  • "The right to enforce any lien established under this subtitle expires at the end of one year from the day on which the petition to establish the lien was first filed." Md. Code, Real Property 9-109, Enforcement

Not legal advice and not a law firm. Dates are worked out from each state's own statute text, quoted with the date we read it. Deadlines are strict: a lien recorded one day late is usually lost. Act before the date shown, and earlier when it falls on a weekend or holiday. For disputes, large amounts or anything unusual about your job, ask a construction attorney licensed in the project's state.

Questions

When is the notice of intention to claim a lien due in Maryland?

Within 120 days after doing the work or furnishing the materials (Md. Code, Real Prop. 9-104(a)).

How long do I have to file a mechanics lien in Maryland?

Within 180 days after the work has been finished or the materials furnished (Md. Code, Real Prop. 9-105(a)).

How long do I have to sue to enforce a Maryland lien?

Within one year from the day the petition to establish the lien was first filed (Md. Code, Real Prop. 9-109).
