# Texas lien deadlines

In Texas, a subcontractor records the lien by the 15th day of the fourth month after the month you last provided labor or materials (Tex. Prop. Code 53.052(b)); the notice comes first, by the 15th day of the third month after each month in which the unpaid labor or materials were provided (Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1)(1)).

- [Worked example](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/lien-deadline/?state=TX&role=sub&project=commercial&first=2026-11-02&last=2027-01-29&completion=2027-02-26): For a job you first furnish on Monday, November 2, 2026, last furnish on Friday, January 29, 2027, with the project complete on Friday, February 26, 2027: as a subcontractor on a commercial project in Texas, your first open monthly notice (for November 2026) is due February 16, 2027, the lien affidavit must be filed by May 17, 2027 and the suit to enforce it must be filed by May 15, 2028 (Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1)(1); Tex. Prop. Code 53.052(b); Tex. Prop. Code 53.158(a)).

Weekends and holidays: Tex. Prop. Code 53.003(e) moves a deadline that falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday to the next day that is not one.

- Weekends and holidays: Texas moves a deadline on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday to the next day that is none of these (53.003(e)). We skip US federal holidays; Texas state holidays we have not read, so if the next day is a Texas-only holiday, count it yourself.
- A lien on a homestead needs a written contract signed before the work starts (by both spouses if married) and filed with the county clerk, and the affidavit must carry the 53.254(f) notice (53.254).
- The original contractor gives no monthly notice; the notices are for claimants other than the original contractor (53.056(a)).

## Questions

### When is the monthly notice of claim due in Texas?

By the 15th day of the third month after each month in which the unpaid labor or materials were provided (Tex. Prop. Code 53.056(a-1)(1)).

### How long do I have to file a mechanics lien in Texas?

By the 15th day of the fourth month after the month in which your original contract work was completed, terminated or abandoned (Tex. Prop. Code 53.052(a)(1)).

### How long do I have to sue to enforce a Texas lien?

Not later than the first anniversary of the last day you could file the lien affidavit (extendable to two years after filing by a recorded written agreement with the owner) (Tex. Prop. Code 53.158(a)).
