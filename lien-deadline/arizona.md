# Arizona lien deadlines

In Arizona, a subcontractor records the lien within 120 days after completion of the work, or within 60 days after a notice of completion is recorded (A.R.S. 33-993(A)); the notice comes first, not later than 20 days after you first furnish labor, services, materials or equipment to the jobsite (A.R.S. 33-992.01(B), (C)).

- [Worked example](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/lien-deadline/?state=AZ&role=sub&project=commercial&first=2026-11-02&last=2027-01-29&completion=2027-02-26): For a job you first furnish on Monday, November 2, 2026, last furnish on Friday, January 29, 2027, with the project complete on Friday, February 26, 2027: as a subcontractor on a commercial project in Arizona, your preliminary twenty day notice is due November 22, 2026, the lien must be recorded by June 26, 2027 and the suit to enforce it must be filed by December 26, 2027 (if recorded on the last day, June 26, 2027) (A.R.S. 33-992.01(B), (C); A.R.S. 33-993(A); A.R.S. 33-998(A)).

## The rules

Arizona lien law, read November 8, 2026

| Step | Who | When | Law |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Serve the preliminary twenty day notice | original (prime) contractor, subcontractor, sub-subcontractor, supplier to the prime contractor, supplier to a subcontractor | Not later than 20 days after you first furnish labor, services, materials or equipment to the jobsite. | A.R.S. 33-992.01(B), (C) |
| Record the notice and claim of lien | everyone with a lien | Within 120 days after completion of the work, or within 60 days after a notice of completion is recorded. | A.R.S. 33-993(A) |
| Sue to foreclose and record a notice of pendency of action | everyone with a lien | Within six months after the lien is recorded. | A.R.S. 33-998(A) |

- No lien against the dwelling of an owner-occupant, except by someone with a written contract directly with the owner-occupant. (A.R.S. 33-1002(B))

Weekends and holidays: the Arizona text we read does not move a deadline that falls on a weekend or holiday, so the calculator keeps the statute's date and flags it with the business day before.

- "Completion" is the earliest of 30 days after final inspection and written final acceptance by the permit authority, or 60 consecutive days without labor (33-993(C)); without permit inspections, it is the last day anything was furnished (33-993(D)). Enter that date as the completion date.
- Wage laborers do not serve the 20-day notice (33-992.01(B)).

## Arizona in its own words

**Read the statute's words (6 quotations)**

- "Except for a person performing actual labor for wages, every person who furnishes labor, professional services, materials, machinery, fixtures or tools for which a lien otherwise may be claimed under this article shall, as a necessary prerequisite to the validity of any claim of lien, serve the owner or reputed owner, the original contractor or reputed contractor, the construction lender, if any, or reputed construction lender, if any, and the person with whom the claimant has contracted for the purchase of those items with a written preliminary twenty day notice as prescribed by this section." [A.R.S. 33-992.01, Preliminary twenty day notice](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/00992-01.htm)
- "shall be given not later than twenty days after the claimant has first furnished labor, professional services, materials, machinery, fixtures or tools to the jobsite" [A.R.S. 33-992.01, Preliminary twenty day notice](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/00992-01.htm)
- "The person, however, is entitled to claim a lien only for such labor, professional services, materials, machinery, fixtures or tools furnished within twenty days before the service of the notice and at any time thereafter." [A.R.S. 33-992.01, Preliminary twenty day notice](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/00992-01.htm)
- "within \[120\] days after completion of a building, structure or improvement, or any alteration or repair of such building, structure or improvement, or if a notice of completion has been recorded, within sixty days after recordation of such notice, shall make duplicate copies of a notice and claim of lien and record one copy with the county recorder" [A.R.S. 33-993, Procedure to perfect lien](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/00993.htm)
- ""completion" means the earliest of the following events:" [A.R.S. 33-993, Procedure to perfect lien](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/00993.htm)
- "A lien granted under the provisions of this article shall not continue for a longer period than six months after it is recorded, unless action is brought within that period to enforce the lien and a notice of pendency of action is recorded pursuant to section 12-1191 in the office of the county recorder in the county where the property is located." [A.R.S. 33-998, Limitation of action to foreclose lien](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/33/00998.htm)

## Questions

### When is the 20-day notice due in Arizona?

Not later than 20 days after you first furnish labor, services, materials or equipment to the jobsite (A.R.S. 33-992.01(B), (C)).

### How long do I have to file a mechanics lien in Arizona?

Within 120 days after completion of the work, or within 60 days after a notice of completion is recorded (A.R.S. 33-993(A)).

### How long do I have to sue to enforce an Arizona lien?

Within six months after the lien is recorded (A.R.S. 33-998(A)).
