# The client paid the deposit, then went silent

If the $1,500.00 midpoint invoice on a $6,000 project falls due on February 8, 2027 unpaid, a calm sequence is a reminder on February 9, 2027, a second on February 16, 2027, a written pause on February 22, 2027 and a final notice on March 10, 2027.

## First, read the contract

- What the deposit covers: booking your time, work already done, or materials.
- The payment schedule and what happens when a payment is late.
- A pause or termination clause, and any notice period.

## A dated reminder timeline

A suggested cadence, not a legal requirement; dates that fall on a weekend or US federal holiday move to the next business day.

| When | Date | What to send or do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Due date | February 8, 2027 | The midpoint invoice is due. Nothing to do yet. |
| 1 business day later | February 9, 2027 | A friendly reminder with the invoice attached and the amount and due date in the message. |
| 7 days later | February 16, 2027 | A second reminder: say that work on the next stage starts when this payment arrives. |
| 14 days later | February 22, 2027 | Pause the work in writing, as your contract allows, with a date by which payment restarts it. |
| 30 days later | March 10, 2027 | A final notice citing the contract's payment and termination clauses; decide whether to end the contract or take advice. |

## The deposit

Whether you keep the deposit depends on what the contract says and on the law where you work; this is not legal advice. [Can I keep the deposit if the client cancels?](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/guides/keep-deposit-if-client-cancels/) sets out the usual clause wording.

## Prevent it next time

- Smaller stages: the work you do ahead of a payment is never more than one stage.
- Work on each stage starts when the payment before it has arrived (write it into the terms).
- Approvals with dates, and a clause that a missed approval ends or pauses the project after a stated period.

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.
