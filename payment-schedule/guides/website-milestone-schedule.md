# A website milestone schedule, in dates

A $6,000 website at 50/25/25 starting November 16, 2026: $3,000.00 invoiced November 16, 2026, $1,500.00 invoiced December 8, 2026 and $1,500.00 invoiced January 7, 2027, with designs due 15 business days in and launch 20 business days after approval.

## Plan your own website schedule

[Make the schedule](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/?total=6000&start=2026-11-16&split=50-25-25&weeks=8&net=7)

## The milestones and what "done" means

| Milestone | Accepted when |
| --- | --- |
| Deposit | The contract is signed; the deposit books your time and starts discovery. |
| Designs approved | The client approves the page designs in writing (an email is enough if the contract says so). |
| Launch | The site is live on the client's domain, or the files are handed over. |

## The dates, in business days

Offsets are counted in business days: weekends and US federal holidays (Thanksgiving, November 26, 2026; Christmas, December 25, 2026; New Year's Day, January 1, 2027) are skipped.

Net 7, due dates moved off weekends and holidays

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 50% | $3,000.00 | November 16, 2026 | Net 7 | November 23, 2026 | $3,000.00 |
| 2. Designs approved | 25% | $1,500.00 | December 8, 2026 | Net 7 | December 15, 2026 | $1,500.00 |
| 3. Launch | 25% | $1,500.00 | January 7, 2027 | Net 7 | January 14, 2027 | $0.00 |

## If the client approves the designs late

Write into the contract that the next invoice dates move with a late approval. Five business days late, the designs invoice moves to December 15, 2026 and launch to January 14, 2027:

Designs approved 5 business days late

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 50% | $3,000.00 | November 16, 2026 | Net 7 | November 23, 2026 | $3,000.00 |
| 2. Designs approved | 25% | $1,500.00 | December 15, 2026 | Net 7 | December 22, 2026 | $1,500.00 |
| 3. Launch | 25% | $1,500.00 | January 14, 2027 | Net 7 | January 21, 2027 | $0.00 |

## The three invoices

Invoice INV-1001, issued November 16, 2026, net 7 (due November 23, 2026)

| Line | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Deposit: 50% of Website (contract price $6,000.00) | $3,000.00 |
| **Total due** | **$3,000.00** |

Invoice INV-1002, issued December 8, 2026, net 7 (due December 15, 2026)

| Line | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Designs approved: 25% of Website (contract price $6,000.00) | $1,500.00 |
| **Total due** | **$1,500.00** |

Invoice INV-1003, issued January 7, 2027, net 7 (due January 14, 2027)

| Line | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Project total: Website (contract price) | $6,000.00 |
| Less: Deposit, invoice INV-1001 dated November 16, 2026 | -$3,000.00 |
| Less: Designs approved, invoice INV-1002 dated December 8, 2026 | -$1,500.00 |
| **Total due** | **$1,500.00** |

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.
