# The final invoice after a deposit

Bill the total of $6,000.00, subtract the $4,500.00 already received, and the balance due is $1,500.00. On a $6,000 project with a $3,000.00 deposit and a $1,500.00 milestone paid, the final invoice is for $1,500.00.

## Make the whole schedule, final invoice included

[Make the schedule](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/?total=6000&start=2027-01-04&split=50-25-25&weeks=8&net=7)

## The worked final invoice

Issued March 1, 2027, net 7 (due March 8, 2027)

| Line | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Project total: Website build (contract price) | $6,000.00 |
| Less: Deposit received, invoice INV-1001 on January 4, 2027 | -$3,000.00 |
| Less: Design approved received, invoice INV-1002 on February 2, 2027 | -$1,500.00 |
| **Balance due** | **$1,500.00** |

## With tax

Charge tax on the whole price on the final invoice and subtract what was received including its tax, so the tax on the balance is never worked out twice. A $6,000 project with 20% VAT and a $3,600 deposit received (VAT included):

| Line | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Project total: Website build (contract price) | $6,000.00 |
| VAT at 20% on the project total | $1,200.00 |
| Less: Deposit received on January 4, 2027 | -$3,600.00 |
| **Balance due** | **$3,600.00** |

Whether the deposit invoice itself had to carry tax depends on where you are: see [tax on deposits](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/guides/tax-on-deposits/).

## Rounding and currency

- Round each invoice to the cent once, then subtract the exact amounts billed: the final invoice takes whatever cent the split left over, so the invoices add up to the price exactly.
- If a deposit was paid in another currency, subtract the amount in the invoice currency that you agreed or that your books record for it, and say which rate on the line.
- If an earlier invoice was not paid in full, subtract only what was received and say so.

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.

## Steps

1. **Start from the total.** Put the full project price on the first line, and tax on the whole price if you charge it.
2. **Subtract each payment received.** Add one "Less" line per payment, with its invoice number and the date it arrived.
3. **Show the balance due.** The total due is the price (with tax) minus the payments; it should equal the last stage of your schedule.
