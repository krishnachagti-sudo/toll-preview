# Stage payments for contractors

On a $40,000 California remodel the down payment can be at most $1,000.00 (the lesser of $1,000 or 10%); a stage schedule then bills $15,000.00, $10,000.00, $10,000.00, $2,000.00 and $2,000.00 as the work passes each stage.

## Deposit rules we cover

| State | Applies to | Rule | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| California | Home improvement and swimming pool contracts | $1,000 or 10% of the contract price, whichever is less (finance charges excluded) | [Contractors State License Board (CSLB), Learn About Home Improvement Contracts (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code 7159.5)](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Consumers/Hire_A_Contractor/Home_Improvement_Contracts/What_Is_A_Contract.aspx) |
| Maryland | Home improvement contracts | one-third of the contract price, before or when the contract is signed; no payment at all before it is signed | [Md. Code, Business Regulation 8-617(b)](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gbr&section=8-617&enactments=false) |
| Maine | Home construction contracts for more than $3,000 in materials or labor | one-third of the total contract price, on contracts over $3,000 | [10 M.R.S. 1487(5)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/10/title10sec1487.html) |
| Florida | Repair, restoration, improvement or construction to residential real property | no cap; above 10% of the contract price, the contractor must apply for permits within 30 days and start work within 90 days after the permits are issued | [Fla. Stat. 489.126(2)(a)](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0400-0499/0489/Sections/0489.126.html) |

What a 50% deposit on $40,000 ($20,000.00) meets in each: The deposit of $20,000.00 is over California's limit of $1,000.00 ($1,000 or 10% of the contract price, whichever is less (finance charges excluded)). The deposit of $20,000.00 is over Maryland's limit of $13,333.33 (one-third of the contract price, before or when the contract is signed; no payment at all before it is signed). The deposit of $20,000.00 is over Maine's limit of $13,333.33 (one-third of the total contract price, on contracts over $3,000). The deposit of $20,000.00 is more than 10% of the price ($4,000.00): Florida then requires the contractor to apply for permits within 30 days of payment (by February 10, 2027 if it is paid on its due date) and start work within 90 days after the permits are issued.

> Only these four states are covered: each was read on the state's own statute site or its contractors' board on November 8, 2026. Other states are not covered here, which does not mean they have no rule.

## A stage schedule for a $40,000 remodel in California

Down payment lowered to the CSLB limit; the rest moved to the next stage. Due on receipt.

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Down payment | 2.5% | $1,000.00 | January 4, 2027 | Due on receipt | January 4, 2027 | $39,000.00 |
| 2. Demolition and rough-in done | 37.5% | $15,000.00 | January 25, 2027 | Due on receipt | January 25, 2027 | $24,000.00 |
| 3. Rough inspection passed | 25% | $10,000.00 | February 8, 2027 | Due on receipt | February 8, 2027 | $14,000.00 |
| 4. Cabinets and fixtures installed | 25% | $10,000.00 | March 1, 2027 | Due on receipt | March 1, 2027 | $4,000.00 |
| 5. Final walkthrough | 5% | $2,000.00 | March 15, 2027 | Due on receipt | March 15, 2027 | $2,000.00 |
| 6. Retention release | 5% | $2,000.00 | April 14, 2027 | Due on receipt | April 14, 2027 | $0.00 |

The deposit was lowered to $1,000.00, California's limit, and the other $3,000.00 moved to "Demolition and rough-in done".

CSLB's page adds that payments cannot exceed the value of the work performed, the down payment excepted: time each stage to work that is done, not to dates alone.

## Inspections, retention and changes

- Inspections: tie a stage to a passed inspection, so the payment follows a fact both sides can see.
- Retention: a percentage held back until the job is complete, then released (5% above, 30 days after the walkthrough). Whether and how much retention is allowed is a matter of contract and local law.
- Variations (change orders): price and approve every change in writing before the work, and add it as its own stage or line.
- Lien and waiver rules vary by state: check yours before the first stage payment.
