# Tax on deposits

Where tax is due when a deposit is received, as HMRC says it usually is for VAT in the UK, each stage invoice carries its own tax: on £10,000 at 20% in a 50/25/25 schedule that is £1,000.00, £500.00 and £500.00, adding up to £2,000.00.

## Put a tax rate on every stage

## The principle varies by country

- UK VAT: HMRC's VAT Notice 700 says most deposits serve primarily as advance payments and create a tax point when you receive them. A deposit taken only as security (refunded when hired goods come back safely) does not.
- US sales tax is set state by state, and whether a service is taxed at all differs; we have not read those rules for this page. Ask the state's revenue department.
- Elsewhere, check the tax authority's guidance on advance payments, or ask your accountant.

## How to show it on the invoices

VAT at 20% on each stage of £10,000 from January 4, 2027, net 7

| Stage | Share | Amount | VAT | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 50% | £5,000.00 | £1,000.00 | January 4, 2027 | Net 7 | January 11, 2027 | £6,000.00 |
| 2. Midpoint milestone | 25% | £2,500.00 | £500.00 | February 1, 2027 | Net 7 | February 8, 2027 | £3,000.00 |
| 3. Final delivery | 25% | £2,500.00 | £500.00 | March 1, 2027 | Net 7 | March 8, 2027 | £0.00 |

- Round the tax on each invoice to the penny or cent; the last invoice takes any leftover so the stages' tax equals tax on the whole price.
- On the final invoice, charge tax on the whole price and subtract what was received including its tax: see [the final invoice after a deposit](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/guides/final-invoice-after-deposit/).

## Checklist

- Does receiving a deposit create a tax point where you are?
- Must the deposit invoice be a full tax invoice (with your tax number and the tax shown)?
- Is the deposit a payment for the supply, or a refundable security?
- Ask an accountant about your own jurisdiction before the first deposit invoice.
