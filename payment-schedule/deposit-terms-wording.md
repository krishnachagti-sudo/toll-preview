# Deposit and payment terms wording

"A 50% deposit of $2,500.00 is required before work begins; the balance of $2,500.00 is due on delivery." On a $5,000.00 project that line goes on the deposit invoice, and the longer version below goes in the contract.

## 50/50 on $5,000.00

The total fee is $5,000.00. A deposit of $2,500.00 (50%) is invoiced on signing (January 4, 2027) and due within 7 days of the invoice date; and the final payment of $2,500.00 (50%) is invoiced on March 1, 2027 (balance on delivery) and due within 7 days of the invoice date. Work on each later stage starts when the payment before it has been received.

## 50/25/25 on $6,000.00

The total fee is $6,000.00. A deposit of $3,000.00 (50%) is invoiced on signing (January 4, 2027) and due on receipt; a further payment of $1,500.00 (25%) is invoiced on February 1, 2027 (midpoint milestone) and due on receipt; and the final payment of $1,500.00 (25%) is invoiced on March 1, 2027 (final delivery) and due on receipt. Work on each later stage starts when the payment before it has been received.

## 30/30/40 on $12,000.00

The total fee is $12,000.00. A deposit of $3,600.00 (30%) is invoiced on signing (January 4, 2027) and due within 14 days of the invoice date; a further payment of $3,600.00 (30%) is invoiced on February 8, 2027 (midpoint milestone) and due within 14 days of the invoice date; and the final payment of $4,800.00 (40%) is invoiced on March 15, 2027 (final delivery) and due within 14 days of the invoice date. Work on each later stage starts when the payment before it has been received.

## The short line for the invoice

- "50% deposit required before work begins. Balance due on delivery."
- "This invoice is the deposit (50% of $5,000.00). Work starts when it is paid."
- "Final invoice. Deposit and milestone payments received are shown as deductions."

## Add what your contract must say

- Whether the deposit is refundable, and in what cases: see the guide on cancellations.
- What happens if an approval is late: the next invoice date moves, or not.
- Home improvement in California, Maryland or Maine: the deposit has a legal limit; see stage payments for contractors.

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.
