# 50/50 payment terms

A 50/50 schedule on a $5,000 project bills $2,500.00 on January 4, 2027 (deposit) and $2,500.00 on March 1, 2027 (balance on delivery); the final invoice nets off the $2,500.00 already paid.

## The schedule on a $5,000 project

Half of the fee is invoiced before work starts and half when the work is delivered. The project starts Monday, January 4, 2027 and runs 8 weeks; each invoice is due within 7 days (net 7), moved to the next business day when that falls on a weekend or a US federal holiday.

50/50 on $5,000, starting January 4, 2027

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 50% | $2,500.00 | January 4, 2027 | Net 7 | January 11, 2027 | $2,500.00 |
| 2. Balance on delivery | 50% | $2,500.00 | March 1, 2027 | Net 7 | March 8, 2027 | $0.00 |

## The final invoice, netting the deposit

The last invoice shows the whole project and subtracts each invoice already paid, so the client sees how the balance was reached.

Invoice INV-1002, issued March 1, 2027, net 7 (due March 8, 2027)

| Line | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Project total: Sample project (contract price) | $5,000.00 |
| Less: Deposit, invoice INV-1001 dated January 4, 2027 | -$2,500.00 |
| **Total due** | **$2,500.00** |

## Wording for the contract

The total fee is $5,000.00. A deposit of $2,500.00 (50%) is invoiced on signing (January 4, 2027) and due within 7 days of the invoice date; and the final payment of $2,500.00 (50%) is invoiced on March 1, 2027 (balance on delivery) and due within 7 days of the invoice date. Work on each later stage starts when the payment before it has been received.

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.

## Questions

### What does 50/50 mean in payment terms?

Half of the fee is invoiced before work starts and half when the work is delivered. On a $5,000 project that is $2,500.00 and $2,500.00.

### How do I write the final invoice on a 50/50 schedule?

Bill the project total of $5,000.00 and subtract every invoice already paid ($2,500.00 here), so the balance due is $2,500.00.
