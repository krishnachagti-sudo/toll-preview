# Post-production payment schedule

Under the AICP's 50-25-25 plan, a $40,000 post-production job pays $20,000.00 at award of the job, $10,000.00 at rough cut approved and $10,000.00 at master delivered; each invoice is due 30 days after its date (February 3, 2027, February 24, 2027 and March 17, 2027 in this example).

## What the AICP guidelines say

- Unless other terms are agreed in advance, invoices are paid in full within 30 days of the invoice date.
- Unpaid invoices bear interest at 1 1/2% per month on the unpaid balance from the invoice date.
- The first payment is made on booking: on delivery of dailies by the production company, or award of the job to the post-production company.
- The middle payment is due on the agency's approval of the rough cut, the last on delivery of the master; overages are due on approval of final actuals.

| 2. Rough cut approved | 25% | $10,000.00 | January 25, 2027 | Net 30 | February 24, 2027 | $10,000.00 |
| 3. Master delivered | 25% | $10,000.00 | February 15, 2027 | Net 30 | March 17, 2027 | $0.00 |
