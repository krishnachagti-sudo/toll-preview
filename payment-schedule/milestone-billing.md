# Milestone billing

Milestone billing invoices fixed amounts when named deliverables are reached; for example 30/30/40 on $20,000 bills $6,000.00 on January 4, 2027, $6,000.00 on February 15, 2027 and $8,000.00 on March 29, 2027.

| 2. Midpoint milestone | 30% | $6,000.00 | February 15, 2027 | Net 7 | February 22, 2027 | $8,000.00 |
| 3. Final delivery | 40% | $8,000.00 | March 29, 2027 | Net 7 | April 5, 2027 | $0.00 |

## How it differs

- Hourly or time-and-materials billing invoices the time spent, usually monthly; the total is not fixed.
- Milestone billing splits a fixed price into parts tied to deliverables (wireframes approved, beta delivered).
- Progress billing bills the share of the work completed, common in construction, often with retention held back.
- A retainer pays for availability or a block of work over a period.

## What makes a good milestone

- Something the client can check and approve, with how approval is given (an email is enough if the contract says so).
- A date, and what happens to later dates if an approval is late.
- An amount fixed in the contract, so the invoice needs no discussion.

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.

## Questions

### What is a milestone payment?

A fixed part of the fee invoiced when a named deliverable is reached. At 30/30/40 on $20,000 the payments are $6,000.00, $6,000.00 and $8,000.00.
