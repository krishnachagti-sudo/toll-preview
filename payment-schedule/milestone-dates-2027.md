# Milestone payment dates, November 2026 to December 2027

A $6,000, 8-week project at 50/25/25 starting December 1, 2026 invoices $3,000.00 on December 1, 2026, $1,500.00 on December 29, 2026 and $1,500.00 on January 26, 2027; with net 7 the deposit is due December 8, 2026. Every month to December 2027 is below.

Each project starts on the first business day of its month (New Year's Day and weekends skipped). The midpoint is 4 weeks in, the final payment at 8 weeks; each is due 7 days after its invoice, moved to the next business day when that is a weekend or a US federal holiday.

## Seasonal notes

- December: year-end closes and holidays move due dates; send final invoices before the last week of the month if you want them paid in the year.
- January: new projects and new budgets; a good month to settle deposit terms for the year.
