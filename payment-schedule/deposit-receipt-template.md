# Deposit receipt template

A deposit receipt for $4,000.00 (50% of an $8,000.00 project) states who paid, when, what it is for and the balance of $4,000.00 still due.

## The receipt, filled in

**Deposit receipt**

Received from Riverside Dental on January 11, 2027 the sum of $4,000.00 as a 50% deposit toward Brand identity (total project price $8,000.00). Balance remaining: $4,000.00, payable as $2,000.00 invoiced February 1, 2027 and $2,000.00 invoiced March 1, 2027.

Signed: (your name), (your business), (date).

## The schedule it refers to

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 50% | $4,000.00 | January 4, 2027 | Net 7 | January 11, 2027 | $4,000.00 |
| 2. Midpoint milestone | 25% | $2,000.00 | February 1, 2027 | Net 7 | February 8, 2027 | $2,000.00 |
| 3. Final delivery | 25% | $2,000.00 | March 1, 2027 | Net 7 | March 8, 2027 | $0.00 |

## What to put on a deposit receipt

- Who paid and who received it.
- The amount and the date it arrived (not the invoice date).
- What it is for: the project, and that it is a deposit toward a stated total.
- The balance still due and when.
- Whether the deposit is refundable, in the same words as your contract.

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.
