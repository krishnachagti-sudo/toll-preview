## What FreshBooks and QuickBooks Online offers, from its own pages

- FreshBooks: Lite $23 a month (invoices to 5 clients), Plus $43 (50 clients, adds proposals and client retainers); "Accept Deposits" is ticked on every plan in its comparison table.
- QuickBooks Online: progress invoicing turns one accepted estimate into several partial invoices, by percentage, line items or the whole; it will not invoice more than 100% of the estimate.
- Both post the invoices and payments to your books.

## What this tool does

- Splits the fee to the cent (any split, percentages, amounts or a balance) and shows where the rounding cent went.
- Puts an invoice date and a due date on every payment, moved off weekends and US or England-and-Wales public holidays.
- Writes every invoice's lines, with a final invoice that subtracts the deposit and milestones already billed.
- Gives a calendar file (.ics) and a CSV, and checks the California, Maryland, Maine and Florida home-improvement deposit rules.
- Charges per project: the schedule is free on the page; the invoice pack is $0.49 per project, with edits free for 90 days.
- Does not send invoices or collect payments: you send them from your own invoicing app or email.

## When it is the better choice

- Your books already live in the app: native deposits and progress invoices post correctly to the ledger.
- You want the app to email the invoices and track what is paid.
- Use the schedule here to plan the dates and check the amounts, then enter them; see the QuickBooks steps.
