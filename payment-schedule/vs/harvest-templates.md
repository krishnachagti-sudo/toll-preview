# Harvest's invoice templates and a dated schedule

Harvest's milestone and deposit templates are free and make one invoice at a time; a 50/25/25 schedule on $6,000 needs three (INV-1001 for $3,000.00, INV-1002 for $1,500.00 and INV-1003 for $1,500.00), with the last subtracting the first two.

## What Harvest offers, from its own pages

- Free online invoice editors on its milestone-based and deposit invoice template pages: logo, invoice number, dates, line items, up to two tax rates, a discount and currency.
- Written advice: tie payments to verifiable milestones, agree approvals in writing, invoice within 24 to 48 hours of approval, and state terms such as "Net 30".
- The Harvest app: Free for 1 seat and 2 projects; Teams from $11 a seat a month ($9 billed annually); Enterprise from $17.50 ($14).

## What this tool does

- Splits the fee to the cent (any split, percentages, amounts or a balance) and shows where the rounding cent went.
- Puts an invoice date and a due date on every payment, moved off weekends and US or England-and-Wales public holidays.
- Writes every invoice's lines, with a final invoice that subtracts the deposit and milestones already billed.
- Gives a calendar file (.ics) and a CSV, and checks the California, Maryland, Maine and Florida home-improvement deposit rules.
- Charges per project: the schedule is free on the page; the invoice pack is $0.49 per project, with edits free for 90 days.
- Does not send invoices or collect payments: you send them from your own invoicing app or email.

## When it is the better choice

- You need one invoice now and already know its amount.
- You track time in Harvest and want invoices from those hours, in the same app.
