# Endwise vs HoneyBook payment plans

HoneyBook's plans start at $29 a month billed annually ($36 monthly) and include payment plans, autopay and reminders; here the schedule for a $6,000 project ($3,000.00, $1,500.00 and $1,500.00) is free to work out and its invoice pack is $0.49.

## What HoneyBook offers, from its own pages

- Plans: Starter $29 a month billed annually ($36 billed monthly), Essentials $49 ($59), Premium $109 ($129); every plan starts with a free trial.
- Payments on every plan: card and ACH, payment tracking, invoices "on one-time, recurring, or custom schedules", autopay, payment reminders, payment plans and late fees.
- Card payments cost 2.9% + 25 cents; ACH 1.5%.
- Proposals, contracts, a client portal and a scheduler in the same app; free milestone and retainer invoice templates.

## What this tool does

- Splits the fee to the cent (any split, percentages, amounts or a balance) and shows where the rounding cent went.
- Puts an invoice date and a due date on every payment, moved off weekends and US or England-and-Wales public holidays.
- Writes every invoice's lines, with a final invoice that subtracts the deposit and milestones already billed.
- Gives a calendar file (.ics) and a CSV, and checks the California, Maryland, Maine and Florida home-improvement deposit rules.
- Charges per project: the schedule is free on the page; the invoice pack is $0.49 per project, with edits free for 90 days.
- Does not send invoices or collect payments: you send them from your own invoicing app or email.

## When it is the better choice

- You want the proposal, contract, invoices and payment collection in one client portal.
- You want autopay and automatic reminders sent to the client: this tool sends nothing.
- You run enough projects a month that one subscription is simpler than paying per project.
