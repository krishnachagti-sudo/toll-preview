# Endwise vs Bonsai

Bonsai includes invoices and payments from its Essentials plan at $19 per user a month billed annually ($25 monthly); here the schedule for a $6,000 project ($3,000.00, $1,500.00 and $1,500.00) is free to work out and its invoice pack is $0.49.

## What Bonsai offers, from its own pages

- Basic $9 per user a month billed annually ($15 monthly): time tracking, tasks, projects and CRM, without invoices.
- Essentials $19 ($25 monthly): adds invoices and payments, proposals and contracts, templates, forms, scheduling and a client portal.
- Premium $29 ($39) and Elite $49 ($59), with integrations including QuickBooks and Xero from Premium.
- The pricing page does not describe how milestone or deposit invoices are scheduled; we have not checked inside the app.

## What this tool does

- Splits the fee to the cent (any split, percentages, amounts or a balance) and shows where the rounding cent went.
- Puts an invoice date and a due date on every payment, moved off weekends and US or England-and-Wales public holidays.
- Writes every invoice's lines, with a final invoice that subtracts the deposit and milestones already billed.
- Gives a calendar file (.ics) and a CSV, and checks the California, Maryland, Maine and Florida home-improvement deposit rules.
- Charges per project: the schedule is free on the page; the invoice pack is $0.49 per project, with edits free for 90 days.
- Does not send invoices or collect payments: you send them from your own invoicing app or email.

## When it is the better choice

- You want projects, time, proposals, contracts and invoices in one workspace.
- You bill hourly as well as by milestone.
- You want the app to collect the payment.
