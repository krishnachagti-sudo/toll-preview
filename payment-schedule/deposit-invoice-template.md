## What a deposit invoice needs

- Your name, address and contact details, and the client's.
- A unique invoice number and the date it is issued.
- The due date, or the terms ("due on receipt", "net 7").
- What the deposit is for: the project, and the deposit as a share of the total ("50% of $5,000.00").
- The total project price and the balance still to pay after this invoice.
- Tax, if you charge it: see the guide on tax on deposits.

Harvest's deposit template page lists the same essentials: business and client details, invoice number, issue and due dates, the deposit as a sum or a percentage, the total project cost and the remaining balance. [Tax on deposits](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/guides/tax-on-deposits/) explains why the tax line can matter more on a deposit than on a normal invoice.
