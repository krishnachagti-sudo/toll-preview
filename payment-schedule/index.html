Every invoice's lines ready to paste into your invoicing or accounting app, the final invoice with the deposit and milestones shown as deductions, a calendar file with each invoice and due date, and a CSV. Nothing is sent to your client: you send the invoices yourself.

$0.49 per project; edits to the same project are free for 90 days.

The rules

Home-improvement deposit rules we cover, quoted.

Each rule is quoted from the state's own statute site or its contractors' licensing board, read November 8, 2026.

What it does not cover

States other than California, Florida, Maine and Maryland, local closures, and legal or tax advice.

Read the full notes
  • Each rule is quoted from the state's own statute site or its contractors' licensing board, read November 8, 2026. Only California, Florida, Maine and Maryland are covered; any other state is not checked, which does not mean it has no rule.
  • Holidays: US federal holidays as OPM observes them, or England and Wales bank holidays as GOV.UK lists them. Your client's state, bank or office closures are not included.
  • Not legal or tax advice. Whether a deposit is refundable and when tax is due on it depend on your contract and where you work.
