# Progress billing invoice template

Progress billing invoices a project in parts as the work advances: on $12,000 at 30/30/40 the invoices are $3,600.00, $3,600.00 and $4,800.00, and the second shows $7,200.00 billed to date and $4,800.00 left to bill.

Paid before this invoice: $3,600.00. Balance after it: $4,800.00.

## Progress billing or milestone billing?

The words overlap. Milestone billing ties each amount to a named deliverable; progress billing (common in construction) bills the share of the work done, often with a percentage held back as retention. Both need the running totals above. See [milestone billing](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/milestone-billing/) and [stage payments for contractors](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/guides/stage-payments-contractors/).

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.
