Progress billing or milestone billing?

The words overlap. Milestone billing ties each amount to a named deliverable; progress billing (common in construction) bills the share of the work done, often with a percentage held back as retention. Both need the running totals above. See milestone billing and stage payments for contractors.

Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.
