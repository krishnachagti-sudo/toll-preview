# 50/25/25 payment schedule

A 50/25/25 schedule on a $5,000 project bills $2,500.00 on January 4, 2027 (deposit), $1,250.00 on February 1, 2027 (midpoint milestone) and $1,250.00 on March 1, 2027 (final delivery); the final invoice nets off the $3,750.00 already paid.

## The schedule on a $5,000 project

Half of the fee is the deposit, a quarter is due at a midpoint milestone and the last quarter on delivery. The project starts Monday, January 4, 2027 and runs 8 weeks; each invoice is due within 7 days (net 7), moved to the next business day when that falls on a weekend or a US federal holiday.

50/25/25 on $5,000, starting January 4, 2027

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 50% | $2,500.00 | January 4, 2027 | Net 7 | January 11, 2027 | $2,500.00 |
| 2. Midpoint milestone | 25% | $1,250.00 | February 1, 2027 | Net 7 | February 8, 2027 | $1,250.00 |
| 3. Final delivery | 25% | $1,250.00 | March 1, 2027 | Net 7 | March 8, 2027 | $0.00 |

## The final invoice, netting the deposit

The last invoice shows the whole project and subtracts each invoice already paid, so the client sees how the balance was reached.

## Wording for the contract

The total fee is $5,000.00. A deposit of $2,500.00 (50%) is invoiced on signing (January 4, 2027) and due within 7 days of the invoice date; a further payment of $1,250.00 (25%) is invoiced on February 1, 2027 (midpoint milestone) and due within 7 days of the invoice date; and the final payment of $1,250.00 (25%) is invoiced on March 1, 2027 (final delivery) and due within 7 days of the invoice date. Work on each later stage starts when the payment before it has been received.

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.

## Questions

### What does 50/25/25 mean in payment terms?

Half of the fee is the deposit, a quarter is due at a midpoint milestone and the last quarter on delivery. On a $5,000 project that is $2,500.00, $1,250.00 and $1,250.00.

### How do I write the final invoice on a 50/25/25 schedule?

Bill the project total of $5,000.00 and subtract every invoice already paid ($3,750.00 here), so the balance due is $1,250.00.
