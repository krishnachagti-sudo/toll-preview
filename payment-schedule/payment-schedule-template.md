# Payment schedule template

A payment schedule lists each payment's share, amount, invoice date and due date: a $6,000 website at 50/25/25 is $3,000.00 due January 11, 2027, $1,500.00 due February 8, 2027 and $1,500.00 due March 8, 2027.

## Fill the template for your project

[Make the schedule](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/?total=6000&start=2027-01-04&split=50-25-25&weeks=8&net=7)

## Example 1: $6,000 website, 50/25/25 over 8 weeks

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 50% | $3,000.00 | January 4, 2027 | Net 7 | January 11, 2027 | $3,000.00 |
| 2. Midpoint milestone | 25% | $1,500.00 | February 1, 2027 | Net 7 | February 8, 2027 | $1,500.00 |
| 3. Final delivery | 25% | $1,500.00 | March 1, 2027 | Net 7 | March 8, 2027 | $0.00 |

## Example 2: $12,000 brand refresh, 30/30/40 over 10 weeks

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 30% | $3,600.00 | January 4, 2027 | Net 7 | January 11, 2027 | $8,400.00 |
| 2. Midpoint milestone | 30% | $3,600.00 | February 8, 2027 | Net 7 | February 16, 2027 (moved from February 15, 2027, Washington's Birthday) | $4,800.00 |
| 3. Final delivery | 40% | $4,800.00 | March 15, 2027 | Net 7 | March 22, 2027 | $0.00 |

## The columns

- Stage and name: what the payment is for (deposit, a named milestone, final delivery).
- Share and amount: the amounts add up to the total exactly; any rounding cent goes on one stage.
- Invoice number and date, terms and due date.
- Paid before and balance after: the running position after each payment.

The CSV link on this page has both examples in these columns; open it in Excel or Google Sheets and replace the values, or make a filled one with the tool.

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.
