# Deposit and milestone invoices in QuickBooks Online

In QuickBooks Online, turn on progress invoicing and invoice an accepted estimate in parts; for a $10,000.00 job at 50/25/25 the amounts to enter are $5,000.00, $2,500.00 and $2,500.00, invoiced January 4, 2027, February 1, 2027 and March 1, 2027.

## The amounts and dates for a $10,000 job

50/25/25 on $10,000, starting January 4, 2027, net 7

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 50% | $5,000.00 | January 4, 2027 | Net 7 | January 11, 2027 | $5,000.00 |
| 2. Midpoint milestone | 25% | $2,500.00 | February 1, 2027 | Net 7 | February 8, 2027 | $2,500.00 |
| 3. Final delivery | 25% | $2,500.00 | March 1, 2027 | Net 7 | March 8, 2027 | $0.00 |

## What Intuit's help page says

- You can invoice the entire estimate, a percentage of it, or selected line items.
- The original estimate stays in your records, and you can create several progress invoices from it.
- You cannot invoice more than 100% of the estimate; a fully invoiced estimate shows as Converted.
- The page lists QuickBooks Online Advanced, Essentials, Plus, Simple Start, Solopreneur and Ledger, but does not say which of them include progress invoicing: check your plan.

A percentage in QuickBooks is taken of the estimate as a whole. On a total that does not split evenly, compare its amounts with the schedule here, which puts the rounding cent on the last stage so the invoices add up exactly.

## Steps

1. **Turn on progress invoicing.** Settings (gear) \> Account and settings \> Sales tab \> Progress Invoicing \> Edit, turn on "Create multiple partial invoices from a single estimate", then Done.
2. **Create the estimate for the whole job.** Create an estimate for the full price ($10,000.00 here) and mark it accepted.
3. **Invoice the deposit.** Create \> Invoice for the customer, open the Manage drawer, Suggestions \> Suggested Transactions, Add the estimate, choose a percentage, enter 50 and Copy to invoice: $5,000.00.
4. **Invoice each milestone.** When the milestone is reached, add the same estimate to a new invoice at 25%: $2,500.00.
5. **Invoice the rest.** For the last invoice, invoice the remaining balance: $2,500.00. QuickBooks will not invoice more than 100% of the estimate; once it is fully invoiced its status changes to Converted.
