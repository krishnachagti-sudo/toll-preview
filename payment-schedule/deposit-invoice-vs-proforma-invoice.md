# Deposit invoice vs proforma invoice

A deposit invoice requests part payment now; a proforma invoice is a quote-like preview of an invoice to come. For a $7,500.00 job with a 40% deposit, send a deposit invoice for $3,000.00.

## Side by side

|  | Deposit invoice | Proforma invoice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A real invoice for part of the price, payable now | A preview of an invoice: what will be billed, before it is |
| Asks for payment | Yes, by its due date | Not as a bill; some sellers ask for payment against it |
| Numbered with your invoices | Yes | Usually not |
| Goes in your books | Yes, as money received in advance | No |
| Typical use | Securing the start date on a project | Customs, a buyer's purchase approval, or a quote in invoice form |

## Where the tax invoice comes in

In VAT countries, the tax invoice (VAT invoice) is the one that lets a business client reclaim the tax. In the UK, HMRC says most deposits are advance payments that create a tax point when you receive them, so VAT on a deposit is usually due then, not at the end. Rules differ by country: see [tax on deposits](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/guides/tax-on-deposits/).
