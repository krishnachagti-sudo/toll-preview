# Web design payment schedule

A typical web design schedule on an $8,000.00 site is 50/25/25: $4,000.00 at deposit (January 4, 2027), $2,000.00 at design approved (February 1, 2027) and $2,000.00 at launch (March 1, 2027).

## Milestones that work for websites

- Deposit on signing: it books your time and pays for discovery.
- Design approved: the client signs off the designs before you build.
- Launch, or handover of the site: the balance before the files or the domain transfer.

These splits are examples, not a rule: a larger site can have more stages. Whatever you choose, write the approvals and what a late approval does to the dates into the contract.

> Not legal or tax advice. The schedule is arithmetic and calendar dates; whether a deposit is refundable, and when tax is due on it, depend on your contract and where you and your client are.
