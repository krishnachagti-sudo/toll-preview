# Milestone invoice template

This milestone invoice bills 25% ($2,500.00) of a $10,000 project on February 1, 2027, due February 8, 2027; $5,000.00 was paid before it and $2,500.00 is left after it.

## The milestone invoice

Invoice LH-22, issued February 1, 2027, net 7 (due February 8, 2027)

| Line | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Midpoint milestone: 25% of Mobile app MVP (contract price $10,000.00) | $2,500.00 |
| **Total due** | **$2,500.00** |

Paid before this invoice: $5,000.00. Balance after it: $2,500.00.

## The three invoices together

50/25/25 on $10,000, starting January 4, 2027, net 7

| Stage | Share | Amount | Invoice date | Terms | Due date | Balance after |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Deposit | 50% | $5,000.00 | January 4, 2027 | Net 7 | January 11, 2027 | $5,000.00 |
| 2. Midpoint milestone | 25% | $2,500.00 | February 1, 2027 | Net 7 | February 8, 2027 | $2,500.00 |
| 3. Final delivery | 25% | $2,500.00 | March 1, 2027 | Net 7 | March 8, 2027 | $0.00 |

Each invoice names the milestone it is for and shows what was paid before and what is left, so no one has to work it out. The last one is a [final invoice that nets off the earlier payments](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/payment-schedule/guides/final-invoice-after-deposit/).

## Good practice from Harvest's template page

- Tie each payment to a milestone the client can verify, and agree how it is approved before the work starts.
- Invoice promptly once the milestone is approved: Harvest suggests within 24 to 48 hours.
- State the payment terms on the invoice, such as "Net 30" or "Due upon receipt".
