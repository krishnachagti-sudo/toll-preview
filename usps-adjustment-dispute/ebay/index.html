eBay Labels

eBay USPS shipping cost adjustments: check and dispute

On eBay, USPS adjustments are under Manage Shipping Labels, in Shipping Cost Adjustments; you dispute them with USPS within 60 days of the notice, with the tracking number and the Revenue Assurance ID. For example, a Priority Mail label for 4 lb at 14 x 10 x 6 in to zone 6 that USPS measured at 16 x 12 x 10 in is priced at 14 lb instead of 4 lb, so the adjustment should be $17.61, or $20.61 with the $3.00 Dimension Noncompliance Fee; a notice dated October 22, 2026 can be disputed until December 21, 2026.

Where eBay shows it

  • USPS labels: your Manage Shipping Labels dashboard, in the section for Shipping Cost Adjustments.
  • FedEx and UPS labels: the Shipping Labels view in Seller Hub or My eBay; the Shipment details or Cost column shows the adjustment, and the service name link shows the input and carrier-audited weights and dimensions.
  • eBay Labels already include USPS nonstandard fees and Dimension Noncompliance fees worked out from the weight and dimensions you enter; choose the irregular package indicator for nonstandard characteristics.

eBay says USPS responds within 5 to 15 business days. USPS says most answers take 2-5 business days for most responses, at most 15 business days, and that the status is with your PC Postage provider (eBay).

The worked example

Notice 123 commercial prices, Time-limited prices, October 4, 2026 to January 17, 2027
VersionPriced atPrice
As labelled, 14 x 10 x 6 in, 4 lb4 lb$26.26
USPS measured, 16 x 12 x 10 in14 lb$43.87
Adjustment$17.61, or $20.61 with the $3.00 Dimension Noncompliance Fee
  • Measure: 16 x 12 x 10 in.
  • Round up each side to the whole inch: 16 x 12 x 10 in.
  • Multiply: 1,920 cubic inches.
  • Over 1,728 cubic inches, so divide by 139 and round up: 14 lb.
  • Dimensional weight 14 lb is more than the actual weight (4 lb, the 4 lb row): priced at 14 lb.
  • Priority Mail commercial, 14 lb, zone 6: $43.87.

eBay's own label prices can differ from Notice 123 commercial prices, so compare the adjustment (the difference). See how to check an adjustment and the dispute packet.

Questions

Where does eBay show a USPS adjustment?

On your Manage Shipping Labels dashboard, in the section for Shipping Cost Adjustments (eBay Seller Center shipping FAQs).

Who decides a USPS adjustment dispute on an eBay label?

USPS. eBay says to send USPS disputes to USPS with the tracking number and the USPS Revenue Assurance ID; UPS and FedEx disputes go to eBay Customer Service.

How long do I have?

60 days from the date of the notification (USPS). USPS's webform refuses disputes over 60 days.
