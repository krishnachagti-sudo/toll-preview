USPS Revenue Assurance ID (USPS Adjustment ID)

The Revenue Assurance ID is the USPS Adjustment ID on the adjustment: a 15-digit number, one per tracking number, with up to 5 leading zeros that you leave out on USPS's webform (000001234567890 is entered as 1234567890), together with the last 4 digits of the tracking number.

Click-N-Ship and other PC Postage vendorsOn the notice the vendor sends; USPS says to ask your PC Postage vendor if you need help getting the information.USPS FAQ

USPS says it has no access to the PC Postage platforms' systems (except Click-N-Ship), so only your platform can give you a missing ID.

What the webform wants

  • Shipper's first and last name
  • Shipper's email address
  • Shipper's telephone number with area code
  • Revenue Assurance ID / USPS Adjustment ID number
  • USPS tracking number / IMpb (the webform asks for the last 4 digits of the IMpb)
  • Reason (weight, package, zone, dimensions)
  • Description of dispute

The webform gives an error for a wrong ID and tracking combination, an existing dispute for the same ID, an adjustment over 60 days old, or an attempt to dispute an overpaid refund (USPS Industry Alert, updated October 25, 2024).

Questions

What is a USPS Revenue Assurance ID?

The USPS Adjustment ID on an APV adjustment: a unique 15-digit number for each tracking number. USPS's dispute webform asks for it with the last 4 digits of the tracking number.

Do I type the leading zeros?

No. USPS says the number currently has up to 5 leading zeros and not to include them on the website: 000001234567890 is entered as 1234567890.

Where is it on Etsy?

At the top of the adjustment receipt in your Etsy Payment account.
