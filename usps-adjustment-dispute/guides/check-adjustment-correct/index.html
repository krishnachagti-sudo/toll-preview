Is my USPS adjustment correct?

Price the label and USPS's measurements from the same Notice 123 list and compare the difference with the charge. The label was priced as Ground Advantage 3 lb to zone 5 ($12.12 at Notice 123 commercial prices); at USPS's measurements (14 x 12 x 11 in, 3 lb) it is 14 lb, $22.13, so the adjustment should be $10.01, or $13.01 with the $3.00 Dimension Noncompliance Fee. The $13.01 charged matches USPS's own measurements. At your measurements (12 x 10 x 8 in, 3 lb) nothing extra is owed, so dispute the full $13.01. File by December 27, 2026.

  • Round up each side to the whole inch: 14 x 12 x 11 in.
  • Multiply: 1,848 cubic inches.
  • Over 1,728 cubic inches, so divide by 139 and round up: 14 lb.

  • USPS Ground Advantage commercial, 14 lb, zone 5: $22.13.

Dimension Noncompliance Fee: The label's dimensions were not the measured ones and the parcel is over 1 cubic foot: USPS can add the $3.00 Dimension Noncompliance Fee.

Please read

  • Not legal advice. USPS decides every APV dispute; a packet does not guarantee a refund.
  • Prices are USPS Notice 123 commercial prices for the label's ship date. eBay, Etsy, Pirate Ship and other platforms may charge different rates, so the tool compares the adjustment (the difference), not label totals.
  • The zone and USPS's measurements come from your adjustment notice; the tool does not read your account or USPS's scan data.
  • Photos you add are processed in memory to build the PDF and are not kept.
  • Rules and prices are as read on the dates shown. USPS changes prices next on January 17, 2027; labels from that date are refused until the new Notice 123 is loaded.

Steps

  1. Gather. From the label: service, zone, ship date, weight and dimensions. From the notice: what USPS measured, the amount and the date.
  2. Billable weight. Work out the billable weight both ways: the weight row, and the dimensional weight over 1,728 cubic inches.
  3. Price both. Look up both in Notice 123 for the service, zone and ship date, and add any nonstandard fee.
  4. Add the fee. If the dimensions were omitted or wrong and the parcel is over 1 cubic foot or 22 inches long, USPS can add the $3.00 Dimension Noncompliance Fee.
  5. Compare. Compare the difference with the amount charged; your platform's own rates can differ from Notice 123, so compare differences, not totals.
