What is USPS Automated Package Verification (APV)?

USPS's Automated Package Verification compares the label's weight, dimensions, zone and service with what its machines measure, and debits or credits the difference through your label platform; you have 60 days from the notification to dispute, and most answers take 2-5 business days for most responses, at most 15 business days.

How it works

USPS says APV catches postage differences during processing and credits or debits the shipper directly, instead of returning the parcel or making the recipient pay postage due. You are told through Click-N-Ship or your PC Postage vendor, and the payment goes through that platform.

USPS names these platforms: Click-N-Ship, Endicia, Stamps.com, Pitney Bowes, eBay, PayPal and EasyPost.

More adjustments since June 2026

USPS's Office of Inspector General found that from March through May 2026 APV ignored 30 to 50 million scans a week, missing $22.6 million of underpaid and $6.1 million of overpaid postage; USPS fixed the programming in June 2026. The OIG estimates the fix lets USPS collect about $96.9 million and refund about $36.6 million from June 2026 through February 2027 (report 25-130-2-R26, July 29, 2026).

Disputing

  • File on USPS's APV dispute webform (https://apvdisputes.usps.com/) within 60 days of the notification.
  • You need the Revenue Assurance ID and the last 4 digits of the tracking number (IMpb), your name, email and phone, the reason and a description.
  • USPS may review all scan data, including images. Most answers take 2-5 business days for most responses, at most 15 business days; the status is with your label platform.
  • Questions: apvsupport@usps.gov or 1-844-819-5187 (Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. CT; closed weekends and holidays).

Questions

What is a USPS APV adjustment?

A charge (or credit) USPS makes through your label platform when its machines find the package's weight, dimensions, zone or service differ from the label.

Which platforms does APV cover?

USPS names Click-N-Ship and the PC Postage platforms of Endicia, Stamps.com, Pitney Bowes, eBay, PayPal and EasyPost.

How long do I have to dispute?

60 days from the date of the notification.
