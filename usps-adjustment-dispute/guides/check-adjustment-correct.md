## Steps

1. **Gather.** From the label: service, zone, ship date, weight and dimensions. From the notice: what USPS measured, the amount and the date.
2. **Billable weight.** Work out the billable weight both ways: the weight row, and the dimensional weight over 1,728 cubic inches.
3. **Price both.** Look up both in Notice 123 for the service, zone and ship date, and add any nonstandard fee.
4. **Add the fee.** If the dimensions were omitted or wrong and the parcel is over 1 cubic foot or 22 inches long, USPS can add the $3.00 Dimension Noncompliance Fee.
5. **Compare.** Compare the difference with the amount charged; your platform's own rates can differ from Notice 123, so compare differences, not totals.
