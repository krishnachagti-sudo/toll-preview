# Who handles a USPS adjustment dispute

USPS decides every USPS adjustment dispute, filed on its webform within 60 days, and pays any refund through your label platform; for a UPS or FedEx adjustment on an eBay label you go to eBay Customer Service instead. USPS's APV help desk is 1-844-819-5187.

## Contact matrix

| Platform | USPS adjustment | Refund lands | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| eBay | USPS, with the tracking number and Revenue Assurance ID (UPS and FedEx: eBay Customer Service) | Your eBay account | eBay Seller Center FAQs |
| Etsy | USPS (Etsy gives 844-819-5187 and verifypostagehelp@usps.gov) | Your Etsy Payment account | Etsy Help |
| Thryft Ship | Thryft Ship files it for you | Reported back by Thryft Ship | Thryft Ship support |
| Click-N-Ship | USPS webform; account questions 1-800-344-7779 | Your Click-N-Ship account | USPS FAQ |
| Other PC Postage (Endicia, Stamps.com, Pitney Bowes, PayPal, EasyPost) | USPS webform; the vendor for the ID and status | The vendor account | USPS FAQ |

USPS says that, except for Click-N-Ship, it has no access to the platforms' systems or accounts, so the status and the refund come from your platform. USPS lists these vendor contacts (USPS FAQ: Automated Package Verification Program, read November 8, 2026): eBay 1-866-540-3229, EasyPost Support@EasyPost.com, Endicia 1-800-576-3279, PayPal 1-888-221-1161, Pitney Bowes 1-844-256-6444, Stamps.com 1-855-889-7867 or 1-888-434-0055.

## Escalating

- No answer after 15 business days: ask your platform for the status (USPS says most answers take 2-5 business days for most responses, at most 15 business days).
- More evidence or questions: apvsupport@usps.gov or 1-844-819-5187, Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. CT; closed weekends and holidays.
