# USPS time-limited prices until January 17, 2027

USPS's time-limited commercial prices apply from October 4, 2026 through January 17, 2027, when the January 2027 price change takes effect; a 5 lb USPS Ground Advantage parcel to zone 5 went from $13.48 to $14.53 (7.8%). An adjustment is priced at the list for the label's ship date.

## What it means for adjustments

- An adjustment is the price at USPS's measurements minus the price of the label, both from the same list: a label bought on October 3 is checked against July prices, one bought on October 4 against October prices.
- Larger parcels rose more in dollars, so a dimensional weight adjustment on a time-limited label is larger than the same one in September.
- In January, check the label date before comparing: from January 17, 2027 new prices apply.

## Please read

- Not legal advice. USPS decides every APV dispute; a packet does not guarantee a refund.
- Prices are USPS Notice 123 commercial prices for the label's ship date. eBay, Etsy, Pirate Ship and other platforms may charge different rates, so the tool compares the adjustment (the difference), not label totals.
- The zone and USPS's measurements come from your adjustment notice; the tool does not read your account or USPS's scan data.
- Photos you add are processed in memory to build the PDF and are not kept.
- Rules and prices are as read on the dates shown. USPS changes prices next on January 17, 2027; labels from that date are refused until the new Notice 123 is loaded.
