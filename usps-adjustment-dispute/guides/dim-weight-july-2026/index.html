Rule change

USPS dimensional weight since July 12, 2026

Since July 12, 2026 an 18 x 14 x 10 in box has a dimensional weight of 19 lb (2,520 cubic inches divided by 139, rounded up); under the old rule (rounded off, divided by 166) it was 16 lb.

What it means for adjustments

  • A box just over a whole inch now counts the whole next inch: 12.1 x 12 x 12 in is 13 x 12 x 12 = 1,872 cubic inches, over the line.
  • Labels bought with the old habits (rounded off, or dimensions left out) are more likely to be adjusted, and the $3.00 Dimension Noncompliance Fee can be added.
  • Right-size boxes: under 1,728 cubic inches (after rounding up) there is no dimensional weight at all.

Questions

Did USPS change dimensional weight in 2026?

Yes. From July 12, 2026 the DMM rounds each side up to the whole inch and divides by 139; before, it rounded each side off and divided by 166.

Did the change apply to every service?

To Priority Mail Express, USPS Ground Advantage and Parcel Select for all parcels, and to rectangular Priority Mail. Nonrectangular Priority Mail still rounds off and divides by 166.
