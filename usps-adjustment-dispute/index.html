Build the dispute packet ($0.49)

The text for the webform's Description of Dispute box, the filing steps, and a PDF with the worksheet and up to 6 photos (a ruler on the box, the parcel on a scale). $0.49 per adjustment; changes to the same adjustment are free for 60 days.

The USPS Adjustment ID on the notice.

The rules

How the charge is checked, and by when.

Both sets of measurements are priced at USPS's published commercial prices with the Domestic Mail Manual rules for the label date. The difference is what the adjustment should be.

What it does not cover

Platform label rates, your USPS account or scan data, and labels from January 17, 2027. Not legal advice.

Please read
  • Not legal advice. USPS decides every APV dispute; a packet does not guarantee a refund.
  • Prices are USPS Notice 123 commercial prices for the label's ship date. eBay, Etsy, Pirate Ship and other platforms may charge different rates, so the tool compares the adjustment (the difference), not label totals.
  • The zone and USPS's measurements come from your adjustment notice; the tool does not read your account or USPS's scan data.
  • Photos you add are processed in memory to build the PDF and are not kept.
  • Rules and prices are as read on the dates shown. USPS changes prices next on January 17, 2027; labels from that date are refused until the new Notice 123 is loaded.

Dimensional weight

For labels from July 12, 2026, each side is rounded up to the whole inch, and a parcel over 1 cubic foot is divided by 139.

The 60-day window

A dispute is filed within 60 days of the adjustment notice, through USPS's webform.

The check, dimensional weight and deadline are free on this page. The packet (dispute text and PDF) is $0.49 per adjustment, and changes to the same adjustment are free for 60 days. Over the API or MCP a check is $0.01.
