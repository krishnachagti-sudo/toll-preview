# Etsy's USPS label adjustment help vs a prepared packet

Etsy's help page tells you the USPS Adjustment ID is at the top of the adjustment receipt and that you have 60 days to contact USPS (844-819-5187 or verifypostagehelp@usps.gov); it does not work out whether the amount is right. Since October 23, 2024 that USPS address answers with a link to the dispute webform.

|  | Etsy Help | This checker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where the ID is | Top of the adjustment receipt | Points to Etsy's receipt |
| Why it happened | Weight, dimensions, drop-off ZIP code or package type differ | Prices each version step by step |
| How to dispute | Phone or email USPS with tracking, Adjustment ID, reason, contact | Text for USPS's webform, the filing steps, a PDF |
| Window | 60 days from the notification; most answers in 2 to 5 business days | The last day for your notice date |
| Price | Free | Check free; packet $0.49 |

## When Etsy's page is better

For the Etsy-specific path (the email link, the Payment account, where a refund lands) Etsy's own page is the place to read. Use the checker when you need to know whether the amount is right and what to write.
