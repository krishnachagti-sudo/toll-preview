# Etsy USPS shipping label adjustments: check and dispute

On Etsy, the USPS Adjustment ID is at the top of the adjustment receipt in your Payment account, and you have 60 days from the notice to dispute with USPS. For example, a USPS Ground Advantage label bought at 1.5 lb to zone 4 that USPS weighed at 2.4 lb is priced at 3 lb instead of 2 lb, an adjustment of $1.16 at Notice 123 commercial prices; a notice dated October 16, 2026 can be disputed until December 15, 2026.

## Why Etsy shows an adjustment

Etsy says USPS may adjust the price of a label it sold once USPS weighs and measures the package and finds any of these different from what you entered:

- The package weight
- The package dimensions
- The post office ZIP code where you dropped off the package
- The package type

Etsy emails you, and the adjustment is charged to (or, for an overpayment, credited to) your Etsy Payment account. Etsy says the package should still be delivered and the buyer should not get a Postage Due notice.

## Find the USPS Adjustment ID

1. Click See my adjustments in Etsy's email.
2. Click the label ID in your Payment account.
3. The adjustment receipt shows why the label was adjusted; the USPS Adjustment ID is at the top.

USPS calls the same number the Revenue Assurance ID. Its webform wants it without leading zeros, with the last 4 digits of the tracking number: [more on the ID](https://krishnachagti-sudo.github.io/toll-preview/usps-adjustment-dispute/guides/revenue-assurance-id/).

## Dispute it

Etsy's page gives USPS's phone (844-819-5187) and email (verifypostagehelp@usps.gov) and asks for the tracking number, the USPS Adjustment ID, a detailed reason and your contact details. Since October 23, 2024 USPS answers that email with a link to its dispute webform (https://apvdisputes.usps.com/), so you can go straight there. Etsy says most responses come in 2 to 5 business days, and a correction is credited to your Etsy Payment account.

## The worked example

- Actual weight 2.4 lb is priced at the 3 lb row.
- USPS Ground Advantage commercial, 3 lb, zone 4: $10.07.

Etsy's label prices can differ from Notice 123 commercial prices, so compare the adjustment, not the label totals.

## Please read

- Not legal advice. USPS decides every APV dispute; a packet does not guarantee a refund.
- Prices are USPS Notice 123 commercial prices for the label's ship date. eBay, Etsy, Pirate Ship and other platforms may charge different rates, so the tool compares the adjustment (the difference), not label totals.

- Rules and prices are as read on the dates shown. USPS changes prices next on January 17, 2027; labels from that date are refused until the new Notice 123 is loaded.

## Questions

### Where is the USPS Adjustment ID on Etsy?

At the top of the adjustment receipt: click See my adjustments in the email, then the label ID in your Payment account (Etsy Help).

### Why did USPS adjust my Etsy label?

Because the weight, the dimensions, the post office ZIP code where you dropped it off or the package type USPS found differ from what you entered (Etsy Help).

### How long do I have to dispute it?

60 days from the date of the notification of the adjustment (Etsy Help and USPS).
